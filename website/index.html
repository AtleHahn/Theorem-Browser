Select mathematical fields to filter documents.

The list is based on the 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification (MSC2020), with a few fields removed and High School Mathematics added.

The choices include fields used in statements or under Fields Used Beyond Statement in proofs.

In Settings, ‘Search item in Mathematical Fields’ controls matching.

‘appears in statement’ requires at least one selected field in the statement.

‘appears exclusively in statement’ requires at least one statement field and all statement fields to belong to the selected set.

‘does NOT appear in statement but in at least one proof’ requires none of the selected fields in the statement and at least one selected field under Fields Used Beyond Statement in a proof.

With no fields selected, this filter imposes no restriction.

Statement level measures understanding the complete statement, not its proof.

High school only uses standard US high-school mathematics.

Undergraduate level assumes standard US undergraduate mathematics: calculus, linear algebra, introductory real analysis, abstract algebra, and basic point-set topology.

Approximately 30 minutes of focused reading may cover a small extension of that background, not several interdependent concepts or a new theory.

The student must understand the objects, hypotheses, quantifiers and conclusion, and make sense of a basic example.

In doubt, choose Beyond undergraduate level.

Undergraduate level excludes high school; Within undergraduate level includes it.
