Statement

Let G be a real Lie group and H\subseteq G a closed subgroup. The left coset space G/H has a unique real-analytic manifold structure for which \pi:G\to G/H is an analytic submersion. The action g_1\cdot(g_2H)=(g_1g_2)H makes it a homogeneous G-space.

Level

Level justification

The assertion involves Lie-group quotients or actions and manifold structures, beyond calculus and basic linear algebra.

Proof 1: Remove the Lie-subgroup hypothesis by the closed-subgroup theorem

Use the homogeneous space construction theorem:

If H ⊂ G is a closed Lie subgroup, then G/H, the left coset space, has a unique real-analytic manifold structure such that the quotient map π:G → G/H is an analytic submersion. The left action given by g1 ⋅ (g2H) = (g1g2)H turns G/H into a homogeneous G-space.

The closed-subgroup theorem now simplifies the hypotheses considerably, a priori widening the class of homogeneous spaces. Every closed subgroup yields a homogeneous space.
