Statement

Let a,b,c>0 be the side lengths of a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle, opposite angles \alpha,\beta,\gamma\in(0,\pi), and let R be its circumradius. If T is the triangle area, then T=abc/(4R).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses elementary Euclidean geometry and real trigonometry.

Review notes

Proof 1: Substitute the circumdiameter sine ratio

The area formula is T=\tfrac12ab\sin\gamma. By the extended sine law, \sin\gamma=c/(2R). Therefore T=\tfrac12ab\,c/(2R)=abc/(4R).

Level
High school only
Level justification

Insert the extended sine-law value for sine gamma into the two-side area formula. School trigonometry and fractions suffice.
