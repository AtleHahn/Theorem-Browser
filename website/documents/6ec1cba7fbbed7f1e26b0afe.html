Statement

Let A(x)=1+\sum_{j\ge1}a_jx^j be a real or complex formal series. Its reciprocal is 1+\sum_{n\ge1}c_nx^n, where c_n=\sum_{k=1}^n(-1)^k\sum_{\substack{i_1+\cdots+i_k=n\\i_j\ge1}}a_{i_1}\cdots a_{i_k}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Power-series coefficients, convergence radii and complex exponentials extend familiar calculus. A circle integral is parametrized by a real angle, and formal reciprocals use coefficient multiplication. These direct definitions suffice without abstract continuation theory.

Proof 1: Compose the geometric series with the negative tail — outline

Use the source substitution F(x)=1/(1-x) and G(x)=-\sum_{j\ge1}a_jx^j in the composition coefficient formula. The composite is 1/A(x); every ordered k-part product contributes the factor (-1)^k.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Substitute the negative tail into the formal geometric series and collect ordered compositions of each degree. Undergraduate polynomial algebra extends to formal series by coefficientwise finite sums; no analytic convergence or advanced algebra is required.
