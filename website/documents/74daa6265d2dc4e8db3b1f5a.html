Statement

In the Euclidean plane, any finite composition of rotations about the origin and reflections in lines through the origin can be expressed as a composition of at most two such reflections.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement concerns elementary plane transformations and their compositions.

Proof 1: Orthogonal closure and planar classification

Use the stated planar classification: every orthogonal transformation is a reflection through an origin line or a rotation about the origin, and a rotation is a composition of two reflections. Rotations and reflections are orthogonal, and orthogonal transformations form a group under composition. Therefore their finite composition is again in the classified family and needs at most two reflections.

Level justification
