Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate plane triangle with area Δ and side lengths a,b,c. For each choice, all inward or all outward, erect equilateral triangles on its three sides and join their centroids. Denote the nonnegative areas of these inner and outer centroid triangles by A_in and A_out (zero area is allowed). Then A_{out}-A_{in}=\Delta.

Level
High school only

Proof 1: Subtract the two area formulas

Use the source-stated area formulas A_{in}=-\Delta/2+\sqrt3(a^2+b^2+c^2)/24 and A_{out}=\Delta/2+\sqrt3(a^2+b^2+c^2)/24. Subtracting cancels the common squared-side term and leaves Δ.
