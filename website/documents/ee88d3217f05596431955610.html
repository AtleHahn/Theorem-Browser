Statement

Let k be a field and let a k-linear endomorphism t:V\to V make V a k[t]-module. Regard k as k[t]/(t). Then V\otimes_{k[t]}k\cong V/tV=\operatorname{coker}(t).

Proof 1: Apply the tensor-quotient formula

The vector space V is a k[t]-module by t:V\to V being a k-linear transformation, so we can tensor V and k over k[t]. Using the tensor-quotient identification,

V\otimes_{k[t]}k =V\otimes_{k[t]}(k[t]/(t)) \cong V/tV =\operatorname{coker}(t).

Level justification

The cokernel itself is elementary linear algebra, but interpreting V as a polynomial-ring module and a balanced tensor product with a residue module adds several related constructions. A confident small-extension justification is lacking.
