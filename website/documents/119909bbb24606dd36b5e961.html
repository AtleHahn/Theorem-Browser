Statement

Let N and M be second-countable smooth manifolds without boundary of dimensions n and m\geq1. If f:N\to M is C^k with k\geq\max\{n-m+1,1\}, then its critical values form a null set in every target coordinate chart; a point is critical when df has rank less than m.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The manifold and coordinate-invariant null-set notions require differential topology and measure theory.

Proof 1: Pass to countably many coordinate charts — outline

This formulation of the result follows from Sard’s theorem for Euclidean spaces by taking a countable set of coordinate patches. The conclusion of the theorem is a local statement, since a countable union of sets of measure zero is a set of measure zero, and the property of a subset of a coordinate patch having zero measure is invariant under diffeomorphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Transfer a Euclidean critical-value theorem through countably many manifold charts and coordinate changes. Smooth manifold differentials, atlases and chartwise nullity constitute a substantial geometric framework.
