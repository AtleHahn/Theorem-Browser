Statement

Let X be an infinite-dimensional complex Banach space. If T:X\to X is a nonzero compact linear operator and S:X\to X is a bounded linear operator with ST=TS, then there is a closed linear subspace M such that

\{0\}\subsetneq M\subsetneq X,\qquad S(M)\subseteq M.

A Banach space is a complete normed vector space. Bounded means \|Sx\|\le C\|x\| for a fixed finite C. Compact means that the image under T of the unit ball has compact closure. Thus M is a nonzero proper closed invariant subspace for S.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Compactness and completeness belong to undergraduate analysis/topology; compact operators are a short explicitly defined extension of linear maps.
