Statement

Let D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\} and let U_1,\ldots,U_n be open connected subsets of \mathbb C covering D\setminus\{0\}. Suppose each f_j:U_j\to\mathbb C is injective and holomorphic and f_j'=f_k' on every intersection U_j\cap U_k. Then there is a unique holomorphic function g on D\setminus\{0\} such that g=f_j' on U_j\cap(D\setminus\{0\}). Consequently the local differentials df_j glue to the holomorphic one-form g(z)\,dz on the punctured disk.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Holomorphic means complex differentiable locally, a short extension of undergraduate derivative limits. Open covers are basic topology, and here the one-form is explicitly g(z) dz in a single global coordinate, requiring no manifold or bundle construction. The injectivity assumption is ordinary set-theoretic injectivity.

Proof 1: Use the agreeing derivative on each covering set — outline

Define g(z)=f_j'(z) wherever z\in U_j\cap(D\setminus\{0\}). The agreement on intersections makes this definition independent of the chosen index. It is holomorphic locally because each derivative is holomorphic, and the cover also makes it unique. Thus these local differentials give g(z)\,dz on the punctured disk.

Level justification

Define g by each derivative and use overlap agreement for well-definedness and uniqueness. Undergraduate open sets and complex differentiation suffice; the one-form is explicitly just g(z)dz in the single global coordinate, not a bundle construction.
