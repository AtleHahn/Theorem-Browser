Statement

Let A(x)=\sum_{j\ge1}a_jx^j/j! be a real or complex formal series. Then \exp A(x)=1+\sum_{n\ge1}\left(\sum_{\substack{m_1,\ldots,m_n\ge0\\\sum jm_j=n}}\prod_{j=1}^n\frac{(a_j/j!)^{m_j}}{m_j!}\right)x^n.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Formal power series use coefficientwise polynomial arithmetic without a convergence requirement. The finite partition or coefficient sums are explicitly specified, making this a small extension of undergraduate algebra and calculus series.

Proof 1: Specialize composition to the exponential — outline

Apply the formal composition coefficient formula with outer series F(x)=e^x and inner series G(x)=\sum_{j\ge1}a_jx^j/j!, as specified in the source. The outer coefficient 1/k! cancels the multinomial numerator k! in each group with \sum m_j=k.

Level justification

Apply the supplied coefficient formula and cancel the outer exponential factorial against the multinomial numerator. Formal series are coefficient arrays with finitely many contributions to each coefficient, a small extension of polynomial algebra and finite counting.
