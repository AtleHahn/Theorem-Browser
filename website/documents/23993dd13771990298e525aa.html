Statement

Let (M,g) be a smooth pseudo-Riemannian manifold of dimension n\ge3. It is locally conformally flat if every point has a neighborhood on which a positive smooth multiple of g is a flat metric. Let W be its Weyl curvature tensor and put

S=\frac{2}{n-2}\operatorname{Ric}-\frac{\operatorname{Scal}}{(n-1)(n-2)}g.

Then:

  • If n\ge4, g is locally conformally flat if and only if W=0.
  • If n=3, g is locally conformally flat if and only if S is a Codazzi tensor, meaning \nabla_kS_{ij}=\nabla_iS_{kj} for the Levi-Civita connection \nabla.

Here \operatorname{Ric} and \operatorname{Scal} are the Ricci and scalar curvatures. No global topological hypothesis is required.

Proof 1: Conformal curvature and an integrable one-form system (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Theorem4.4.2 and Lemma4.4.3, printed pp.50–53; Sections3.2–3.4 and4.2).

Write P=S/2 for the source’s Schouten tensor. The equation to solve is \nabla_i\omega_j=P_{ij}+\omega_i\omega_j-\tfrac12g^{ab}\omega_a\omega_b g_{ij}. In the curvature expansion on printed p.53, the term P_{ik}\delta^l_k must read P_{ik}\delta^l_j, as the next contracted line already indicates. The resulting compatibility identity holds for every covector value, not only along a proposed solution. Thus the corresponding horizontal vector fields on T^*M commute; Frobenius gives a local section solving the equation. Its symmetric right-hand side makes \omega closed, so locally \omega=d\sigma and e^{2\sigma}g is flat. In dimension three W vanishes identically and the Codazzi condition supplies compatibility; for n\geq4 the contracted Bianchi identity supplies it from W=0.
