Statement

Let S\subset\mathbb R^3 be a compact smooth oriented surface with smooth boundary, induced boundary orientation and unit normal n. Let H,J,D be vector fields smooth in space and differentiable in time near S. At each time where \nabla\times H=J+\partial_tD, \int_{\partial S}H\cdot dl=\int_S J\cdot n\,dS+\int_S\partial_tD\cdot n\,dS.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Time-dependent vector fields; Curl; Line and surface integrals. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Substitute the curl equation into Stokes

The differential and integral forms are related by the special 3-dimensional (vector calculus) case of Stokes' theorem. Put C=\partial S and d\mathbf A=n\,dS. At the fixed time,

\begin{aligned} \oint_C\mathbf H\cdot d\mathbf l &=\iint_S\nabla\times\mathbf H\cdot d\mathbf A\\ &=\iint_S\mathbf J\cdot d\mathbf A+\iint_S\frac{\partial\mathbf D}{\partial t}\cdot d\mathbf A. \end{aligned}

At a fixed time the proof applies the vector-calculus Stokes theorem to H and substitutes the supplied curl equation. Linearity splits the surface integral. All objects are smooth fields and oriented surfaces in R^3 from undergraduate vector calculus; no general differential-form Stokes theorem or electromagnetic theory is required.

Statement used:

For a smooth vector field F near a compact smooth oriented surface S with its induced boundary orientation, the line integral of F around the boundary equals the surface integral of curl F dotted with the unit normal.

Application in proof:
