Statement

In the free group on a,b, the subgroup H consisting of reduced words of even length is free of rank three.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A free group is the reduced-word group on the displayed generators, with adjacent inverse cancellation. The subgroup is specified by word-length parity and free rank counts generators in a free basis. These concrete word definitions are a small extension of group theory.

Proof 1: Contract one edge of the two-vertex covering graph — outline

Represent the free group G as \pi_1(X), where X has one vertex P and two loop-edges a,b. The free group G=\pi_1(X) has n=2 generators corresponding to loops a,b from the base point P in X. The subgroup H of even-length words, with index e=[G:H]=2, corresponds to the covering graph Y with two vertices corresponding to the cosets H and H'=aH=bH=a^{-1}H=b^{-1}H, and two lifted edges for each of the original loop-edges a,b. Contracting one of the edges of Y gives a homotopy equivalence to a bouquet of three circles, so that H=\pi_1(Y) is a free group on three generators.

Level justification

The actual proof passes through fundamental groups, subgroup-cover correspondence and homotopy contractions of covering graphs. This linked algebraic-topology framework exceeds a small extension of introductory group theory and topology.
