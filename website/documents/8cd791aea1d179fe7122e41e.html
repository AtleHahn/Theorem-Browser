Statement

If R is a finite-dimensional semisimple unital algebra over an algebraically closed field k, then R\cong\prod_{i=1}^r M_{n_i}(k) for positive integers n_i (with the empty product allowed for the zero algebra).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Semisimplicity and algebraic closedness are substantive abstract-algebraic notions beyond calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Specializing the division factors

Use the k-algebra Wedderburn–Artin decomposition: R\cong\prod_iM_{n_i}(D_i) with D_i finite-dimensional division algebras over k. Use the fact that a finite-dimensional division algebra over an algebraically closed field is that field itself. Thus every D_i=k, giving the product stated.

Level justification

Apply Wedderburn-Artin and eliminate its division-algebra factors. Semisimplicity, finite-dimensional division algebras and the structural decomposition require a linked advanced algebra framework.
