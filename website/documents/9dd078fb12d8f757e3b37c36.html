Statement

Let X be a connected affine scheme of finite type over a field K, of dimension n. Let f,g:X\to\mathbb A_K^n be finite Noether-normalization morphisms. The fibers of f all have the same degree if and only if the fibers of g all have the same degree. Here fiber degree means the dimension of the finite fiber algebra over the residue field of the point in \mathbb A_K^n.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Affine schemes of finite type; Finite normalization morphisms; Degrees of finite fibers. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Compare both fiber conditions with Cohen–Macaulayness

Use the geometric miracle-flatness criterion. Let X be a connected affine scheme of finite type over a field K (for example, an affine variety). Let n be the dimension of X. By Noether normalization, there is a finite morphism f from X to affine space An over K. Then X is Cohen–Macaulay if and only if all fibers of f have the same degree. It is striking that this property is independent of the choice of f.
