Statement

Over Zermelo–Fraenkel set theory without the axiom of choice (ZF), the following statements are equivalent:

  1. The axiom of choice: every family of nonempty sets has a function selecting an element of each set.
  2. For every infinite set A, there exists a bijection A\to A\times A.
