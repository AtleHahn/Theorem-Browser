Proof 1: Integrate the power derivative

The power rule gives (x^n)'=nx^{n-1}. Integrating, which corresponds to applying the fundamental theorem of calculus, one obtains Cavalieri's quadrature formula, the integral

\int x^{n-1}\,dx=\tfrac1n x^n+C.

Level justification
