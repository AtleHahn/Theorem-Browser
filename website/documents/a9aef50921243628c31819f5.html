Statement

For a finite group G and prime p, the number n_p of Sylow p-subgroups divides |G|.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group/ring theory and linear algebra supply the objects. The extra Sylow, normalizer, invariant-polynomial or pseudoreflection condition is explicitly defined in subgroup, coordinate or polynomial terms; no specialist extension or representation theory is required.

Proof 1: The normalizer index formula

The Sylow counting formula gives n_p=[G:N_G(P)] for a Sylow subgroup P. Since |G|=[G:N_G(P)]|N_G(P)|, the divisibility follows.
