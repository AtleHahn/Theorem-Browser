Statement

Let K\subset\mathbb C be compact, let \Gamma be a finite closed polygonal contour disjoint from K, and suppose a function f:K\cup\Gamma\to\mathbb C continuous on \Gamma satisfies f(w)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\int_\Gamma f(z)/(z-w)\,dz for w\in K. Then f|_K is a uniform limit of rational functions with poles on \Gamma.

Proof 1: Take uniform Riemann sums — outline

Riemann approximating sums can be used to approximate the contour integral uniformly over K, since the contour and K are compact and disjoint. Each term in the sum is a scalar multiple of (z-w)^{-1} for some point z on the contour. This gives a uniform approximation by a rational function with poles on \Gamma.
