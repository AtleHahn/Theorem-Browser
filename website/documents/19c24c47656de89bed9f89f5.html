Statement

Every finite-dimensional real Lie algebra \mathfrak g is isomorphic to the Lie algebra of a finite-dimensional real Lie group.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns Lie groups and their Lie algebras, beyond calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Reduce by Ado to matrix Lie algebra integration — outline

By Ado’s theorem, choose a faithful finite-dimensional real matrix representation of \mathfrak g, identifying it with a Lie subalgebra \mathfrak h\subseteq\mathfrak{gl}_N(\mathbb R). Use the matrix-Lie-algebra integration result invoked by the source: a matrix Lie subalgebra integrates, via the matrix exponential, to a Lie group with that Lie algebra. Applied to \mathfrak h, this gives a Lie group whose Lie algebra is isomorphic to \mathfrak g. The matrix integration result is used here as an external prerequisite; this paragraph does not construct its group structure.

Level justification

Use a faithful matrix representation and the external integration theorem. Lie groups, their tangent Lie algebras and integration of matrix subalgebras require graduate differential geometry.
