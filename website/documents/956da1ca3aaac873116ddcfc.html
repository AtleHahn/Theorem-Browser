Statement

For integers \ell,\ell'\ge0, let D^\ell and D^{\ell'} be the standard unitary Wigner matrix representations of SO(3), with indices n,m\in\{-\ell,\ldots,\ell\} and n',m'\in\{-\ell',\ldots,\ell'\}. Then \int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi\!\int_0^{2\pi}\overline{D^\ell(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)_{nm}}D^{\ell'}(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)_{n' m'}\sin\beta\,d\gamma\,d\beta\,d\alpha=\delta_{\ell\ell'}\delta_{nn'}\delta_{mm'}\frac{8\pi^2}{2\ell+1}. Here each Kronecker delta is one for equal indices and zero otherwise.

Proof 1: Evaluate Euler-angle volume and substitute the dimension

Use compact-group matrix orthogonality: the integral of a conjugate matrix coefficient times another is zero unless the irreducible representations and both indices match, and in the matching case it is the Haar volume divided by the representation dimension.

The Euler angle parametrization of SO(3) gives the weight \omega(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=\sin\beta. The irreducible representations of SO(3) are Wigner D-matrices D^\ell(\alpha,\beta,\gamma), which are of dimension 2\ell+1. Since

|SO(3)|=\int_0^{2\pi}d\alpha\int_0^\pi\sin\beta\,d\beta\int_0^{2\pi}d\gamma=8\pi^2,

they satisfy

\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi\int_0^{2\pi} \overline{D^\ell(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)_{nm}} D^{\ell'}(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)_{n'm'}\sin\beta\,d\gamma\,d\beta\,d\alpha =\delta_{\ell\ell'}\delta_{nn'}\delta_{mm'}\frac{8\pi^2}{2\ell+1}.
