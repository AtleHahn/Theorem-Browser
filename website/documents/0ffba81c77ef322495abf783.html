Statement

Let B,C be exact categories and F^{\prime}\rightarrowtail F\twoheadrightarrow F^{\prime\prime} a short exact sequence of exact functors from B to C. For every integer i\ge0, the induced homomorphisms on algebraic K-groups satisfy F_*=F^{\prime}_*+F^{\prime\prime}_*:K_i(B)\to K_i(C).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Exact categories and their higher algebraic K-groups require a substantial categorical and homotopical framework. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Pass homotopy additivity to homotopy groups — outline

The additivity theorem gives F_*\simeq F^{\prime}_*+F^{\prime\prime}_* as maps of K-theory H-spaces. Consequently the induced maps on their homotopy groups satisfy F_*=F^{\prime}_*+F^{\prime\prime}_*:K_i(B)\to K_i(C).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Exact categories, K-theory H-spaces and their induced homotopy-group maps require advanced algebraic topology and categorical algebra. Passing a homotopy to homotopy groups does not remove that prerequisite burden.
