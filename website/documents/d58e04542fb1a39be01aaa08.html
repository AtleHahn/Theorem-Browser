Statement

In the standard first-order sequent calculus LK, replace identity A\vdash A by the contextual identity axioms \Gamma,A\vdash A,\Delta and omit the explicit left and right weakening rules. Then every derivation using weakening can be transformed into a derivation of the same endsequent without explicit weakening.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The claim concerns transformation of arbitrary derivations in the named first-order LK system, whose rules exceed the elementary or calculus/linear-algebra backgrounds.

Proof 1: Move weakening to the contextual axioms — outline

Move any weakening occurring in a derivation to the beginning of the proof. It is then absorbed by the contextual identity axioms \Gamma,A\vdash A,\Delta.
