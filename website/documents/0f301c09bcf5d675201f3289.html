Statement

Let S\subseteq2^\omega be a nonempty effectively closed (lightface \Pi^0_1) class. There is X\in S with X'\equiv_T\emptyset', where X' is the halting set for oracle X, \emptyset' is the ordinary halting set, and \equiv_T denotes mutual Turing computability. Such an X is called low.
