Statement

Every complete connected Riemannian manifold of constant sectional curvature is isometric to a quotient of a round sphere, Euclidean space, or hyperbolic space by a group of isometries acting freely and properly discontinuously. The model has the same dimension as the manifold. In dimension at least two its curvature is the same: for curvature c>0 it is the sphere of radius 1/\sqrt c, for c=0 Euclidean space, and for c<0 the hyperbolic model scaled to curvature c. Sectional curvature assigns curvature to each two-dimensional tangent plane; constant means the same value at every such plane. Completeness refers to the distance induced by the Riemannian metric. Free means no nonidentity group element fixes a point; proper discontinuity here means that for every compact set C only finitely many group elements g satisfy gC\cap C\ne\varnothing. The quotient carries the metric locally induced by the model.
