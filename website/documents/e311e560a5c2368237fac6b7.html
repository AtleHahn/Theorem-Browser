Statement

Let D_1,D_2 be sound and complete deductive systems for the same first-order language and semantics. A formula is provable without nonlogical axioms in D_1 if and only if it is provable without nonlogical axioms in D_2. Sound means each provable formula is true in every structure under every assignment; complete means each such valid formula is provable.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Soundness and completeness are defined, but first-order languages and their structures/assignments are additional semantic prerequisites. Their cumulative preparation is not confidently a small baseline extension. The cumulative prerequisite preparation cannot confidently fit approximately 30 minutes beyond the stipulated undergraduate baseline. Under the conservative threshold rule, the classification is therefore assessed as Beyond undergraduate level. Uncertainty about the exact preparation time is retained in this justification but no longer leaves the educational assessment provisional.

Proof 1: Pass through validity in both directions

If a deductive system of first-order logic is sound and complete, then a formula is provable if and only if it is logically valid. Thus it is equivalent to any other deductive system with the same quality: both prove exactly the logically valid formulas.
