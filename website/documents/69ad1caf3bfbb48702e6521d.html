Statement

Put \omega=(-1+i\sqrt3)/2 and E=\mathbb Z[\omega]=\{a+b\omega:a,b\in\mathbb Z\}. For z=a+b\omega put N(z)=a^2-ab+b^2. Congruence modulo \lambda\in E means that the difference is divisible by \lambda in E. An element is called primary if it is coprime to 3 and congruent to an ordinary integer modulo (1-\omega)^2 (equivalently, congruent to 1 or -1 modulo 3 in E).

For a prime element \pi\in E with N(\pi)\ne3 and \pi\nmid\gamma, define (\gamma/\pi)_3 to be the unique element of \{1,\omega,\omega^2\} satisfying

(\gamma/\pi)_3\equiv\gamma^{(N(\pi)-1)/3}\pmod\pi.

Extend the symbol multiplicatively in a nonzero denominator coprime to 3: if \lambda=u\prod_j\pi_j^{e_j} with u a unit and \gamma coprime to \lambda, put

(\gamma/\lambda)_3=\prod_j(\gamma/\pi_j)_3^{e_j},

with the empty product equal to 1.

For every pair of coprime primary elements \alpha,\beta\in E,

\left(\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\right)_3=\left(\frac{\beta}{\alpha}\right)_3.
