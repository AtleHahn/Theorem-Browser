Statement

For every integer N\geq1 there is a positive integer c_N, depending only on N, with the following property. Let E\subseteq\mathbb R^N be bounded, and let \mathcal F be a family of open Euclidean balls such that every point of E is the center of some ball in \mathcal F. There exist subfamilies \mathcal A_1,\ldots,\mathcal A_{c_N}\subseteq\mathcal F, each consisting of pairwise disjoint balls, such that

E\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^{c_N}\ \bigcup_{B\in\mathcal A_i} B.
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate point-set topology suffices for arbitrary covers, subfamilies and bounded subsets of Euclidean space. The nested family selection and dimension-uniform bound are most naturally understood with that background; measure theory belongs only to applications.
