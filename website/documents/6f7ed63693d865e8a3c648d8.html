Statement

In the hyperbolic plane of curvature -1/R^2, R>0, a right triangle with legs a,b and opposite side c satisfies \sinh^2\frac{c}{2R}=\sinh^2\frac{a}{2R}+\sinh^2\frac{b}{2R}+2\sinh^2\frac{a}{2R}\sinh^2\frac{b}{2R}.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Apply the hyperbolic double-angle identity — outline

The source eliminates the hyperbolic cosines from \cosh(c/R)=\cosh(a/R)\cosh(b/R) using the double-angle identity \cosh(2t)=1+2\sinh^2t. Expanding, subtracting one and dividing by two gives the displayed formula.
