Statement

Let D\subset\mathbb R^2 be the closed bounded region enclosed by a positively oriented piecewise smooth Jordan curve \gamma:[a,b]\to\mathbb R^2. Let \psi be a continuous, piecewise smooth map from a neighborhood of D into \mathbb R^3, and let F:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3 be smooth. Put \Gamma=\psi\circ\gamma. Then

\int_a^b F(\Gamma(t))\cdot\Gamma'(t)\,dt =\iint_D(\nabla\times F)(\psi(u,v))\cdot(\psi_u\times\psi_v)\,du\,dv.

The derivatives and integrals are taken piece by piece across the finite smooth pieces. The right side is the oriented surface integral with the stated parametrization, including multiplicity; the boundary on the left is the parametrized path \Gamma, which need not be simple.
