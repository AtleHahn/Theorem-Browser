Global hyperbolicity entails a global temporal organization of spacetime. This connects causal restrictions with a space-times-time decomposition, without asserting that all physically conceivable spacetimes satisfy those restrictions.

Statement

Let (M,g) be a globally hyperbolic spacetime, that is, a smooth time-oriented Lorentzian manifold without boundary which is causal and for which every causal diamond J^+(p)\cap J^-(q) is compact. Here causal means that there are no closed causal curves, and J^+(p) and J^-(q) consist of the points reachable respectively to the future of p and to the past of q along causal curves, including the endpoints.

There exist a Cauchy hypersurface S and a homeomorphism

\Phi:S\times\mathbb R\longrightarrow M

such that each \Phi(S\times\{a\}), for a\in\mathbb R, is a Cauchy hypersurface, and each curve t\mapsto\Phi(s,t), for s\in S, is a continuous timelike curve.

A Cauchy hypersurface meets every inextendible timelike curve exactly once. The hypersurface S is a topological hypersurface; differentiability of S or of \Phi is not asserted.
