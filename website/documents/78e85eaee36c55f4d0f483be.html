Statement

For every positive integer k, \mathrm{DSPACE}(n^k)\subsetneq\mathrm{PSPACE}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The fixed-exponent and union classes refer to arbitrary machine-decidable languages and their space resources. Their prerequisite burden is not established by the short inclusion argument. Proof 1: Use larger exponents within the polynomial-space union

The space hierarchy theorem gives problems in larger polynomial space classes that are not in \mathrm{DSPACE}(n^k); those classes still lie in their union \mathrm{PSPACE}. Thus no fixed exponent captures the entire union.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification
