Statement

Let f_1,\ldots,f_k\in K[x_1,\ldots,x_n] be homogeneous polynomials of specified degrees d_i\ge0 over a field K, and put J=(f_1,\ldots,f_k) and \mathfrak m=(x_1,\ldots,x_n). For d\ge0, form a matrix whose rows are the degree-d monomials and whose columns are the coefficient vectors of mf_i for monomials m of degree d-d_i (no such columns when d<d_i). Then \mathfrak m^d\subseteq J if and only if this matrix has full row rank.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Polynomial ideals and homogeneous degree are introductory algebra; coefficient vectors and full row rank are linear algebra. The matrix is constructed explicitly, so the equivalence adds no commutative-geometric prerequisites.

Proof 1: Identify the column span with the homogeneous ideal component

The columns span exactly the degree-d homogeneous part of J: in that degree, homogeneous multiples of each f_i are linear combinations of its indicated monomial multiples. Full row rank means this span is the entire vector space of degree-d homogeneous polynomials. That says all degree-d monomials belong to J, equivalently that their generated ideal \mathfrak m^d is contained in J.
