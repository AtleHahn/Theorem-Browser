Statement

Let \Psi(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\Psi_nz^n, where \Psi_n>0 and \Psi_{n+1}/\Psi_n\to0. Then \Psi is entire.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Calculus and real analysis supply power series, finite differences and explicit growth estimates. Complex exponentials and complex differentiability extend familiar definitions, while all generalized coefficients are supplied by formulas. No abstract analytic continuation or Riemann surfaces are needed.

Proof 1: Ratio test

The coefficient-ratio condition gives convergence of the power series for every complex argument by the ratio test. A power series with infinite radius of convergence defines an entire function.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the ratio test for each fixed complex argument and use power-series holomorphy. Standard undergraduate complex analysis suffices.
