Statement

For any integer k\ge1 and rational numbers a_1<\cdots<a_k and b_1<\cdots<b_k, there is an increasing bijection h:\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q with h(a_i)=b_i for every i, obtained by restricting an increasing piecewise-linear bijection of \mathbb R whose breakpoints are among the a_i.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Increasing rational bijections and piecewise-linear interpolation require real ordering and rational affine arithmetic; the prescribed values are finite.

Proof 1: Interpolate the prescribed rational points — outline

Construct a piecewise-linear order automorphism of the rationals with breakpoints at the given k points, taking them in increasing order to the prescribed target points.
