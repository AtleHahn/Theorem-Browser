Statement

Let H^\infty(D) be the unital Banach algebra of bounded holomorphic functions on the open unit disc D, with the supremum norm. Let S be its maximal ideal space with the Gelfand topology. Identify z\in D with the evaluation ideal M_z=\{f\in H^\infty(D):f(z)=0\}, using the standard topological embedding of D in S. Then D is a proper subset of S.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The Gelfand topology on the maximal ideal space of a holomorphic Banach algebra requires substantial functional analysis. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compact spectrum versus noncompact disc

The maximal ideal space S is compact, whereas the embedded open unit disc D is not compact. Hence D cannot be the whole of S.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compare compactness of the maximal ideal space with the embedded disc. Banach-algebra spectra, Gelfand topology and bounded holomorphic function algebras are substantial graduate prerequisites despite the elementary topological contradiction.
