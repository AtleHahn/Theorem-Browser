Statement

For every integer n\ge0, n!=\int_0^\infty x^n e^{-x}\,dx.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Factorials; Real exponential; Improper integrals. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Repeated integration by parts — outline

Repeated integration by parts reduces the integral to the preceding exponent, until the integral of e^{-x} remains. This gives n!.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Repeated integration by parts reduces the nonnegative integer exponent, with exponential decay removing the boundary terms. The endpoint integral is elementary. Ordinary improper-integral calculus suffices.
