Statement

Let M be a closed Riemannian manifold with strictly negative sectional curvature. Every nontrivial abelian subgroup of \pi_1(M) is isomorphic to the additive group \mathbb Z.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Closed Riemannian manifolds; Negative sectional curvature; Abelian subgroups of fundamental groups. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Hyperbolic-group classification reduction (outline)

The stated negative-curvature results give a torsion-free fundamental group and a universal cover that is CAT(-\varepsilon) for some \varepsilon>0. Its geometric action on the cover makes \pi_1(M) a Gromov-hyperbolic group. Let A be a nontrivial abelian subgroup. Torsion-freeness makes A infinite. The stated classification of abelian subgroups in hyperbolic groups makes A virtually infinite cyclic, meaning that it has an infinite cyclic subgroup of finite index. As A is torsion-free and abelian, this implies A\cong\mathbb Z.
