Statement

Let A,B:\mathbb R\to\mathbb C be 2\pi-periodic measurable functions with \int_{-\pi}^{\pi}|A(x)|^2\,dx<\infty and \int_{-\pi}^{\pi}|B(x)|^2\,dx<\infty. Define their Fourier coefficients, for n\in\mathbb Z, by

a_n=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}A(x)e^{-inx}\,dx, \qquad b_n=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}B(x)e^{-inx}\,dx.

Then

\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}a_n\overline{b_n} =\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}A(x)\overline{B(x)}\,dx.

Integrals are Lebesgue integrals and the bar denotes complex conjugation.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The complete arbitrary measurable L2 formulation requires Lebesgue integration and Fourier coefficients in that setting, beyond the conservative baseline of introductory real analysis.
