Statement

Let X be a real or complex topological vector space and M\subseteq X a vector subspace. If K\subseteq X is nonempty, open and convex with K\cap M=\varnothing, then there is a closed codimension-one vector subspace N of X containing M and disjoint from K.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses unrestricted topological vector spaces and closed codimension-one subspaces.

Proof 1: Geometric separation and the subspace condition — outline

The result follows from geometric Hahn–Banach separation and the convexity of M.

The separation result used is the following: if A and B are convex nonempty disjoint subsets of a real topological vector space and A is open, there exists a continuous linear functional f and s\in\mathbb R such that f(a)<s\leq f(b) for all a\in A,b\in B. If the space is complex, the same claim holds for \operatorname{Re}f.

Apply this with A=K and B=M. The source leaves the subspace and kernel deductions implicit.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply geometric Hahn–Banach and use the subspace property to force the separating functional to vanish on M. General topological vector spaces and continuous functional separation require advanced functional analysis.
