Statement

Let f be nonnegative and continuous on the closed ball \overline B(x_0,R)\subset\mathbb R^n, R>0, and harmonic in its interior (its Laplacian vanishes there). For r=|x-x_0|<R,

{\displaystyle {\frac {1-(r/R)}{[1+(r/R)]^{n-1}}}f(x_{0})\leq f(x)\leq {1+(r/R) \over [1-(r/R)]^{n-1}}f(x_{0}).}

For n=2, this reads

{\displaystyle {R-r \over R+r}f(x_{0})\leq f(x)\leq {R+r \over R-r}f(x_{0}).}
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Multivariable derivatives; Harmonic functions; Euclidean balls. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Bounds on the Poisson kernel

By Poisson's formula

{\displaystyle f(x)={\frac {1}{\omega _{n-1}}}\int _{|y-x_{0}|=R}{\frac {R^{2}-r^{2}}{R|x-y|^{n}}}\cdot f(y)\,dy,}

where ωn − 1 is the area of the unit sphere in Rn and r = |x − x0|.

Since

{\displaystyle R-r\leq |x-y|\leq R+r,}

the kernel in the integrand satisfies

{\displaystyle {\frac {R-r}{R(R+r)^{n-1}}}\leq {\frac {R^{2}-r^{2}}{R|x-y|^{n}}}\leq {\frac {R+r}{R(R-r)^{n-1}}}.}

Harnack's inequality follows by substituting this inequality in the above integral and using the fact that the average of a harmonic function over a sphere equals its value at the center of the sphere:

{\displaystyle f(x_{0})={\frac {1}{R^{n-1}\omega _{n-1}}}\int _{|y-x_{0}|=R}f(y)\,dy.}

Level justification

Bound the explicit Poisson kernel using triangle inequalities and replace its boundary integral by the supplied center mean value. Multivariable calculus suffices to apply these stated identities; constructing harmonic solutions is not required.
