Statement

Let R be a semiprime ring satisfying the ascending chain condition on right annihilators. Then its right singular ideal is zero, so R is right nonsingular. Here the right singular ideal consists of elements whose right annihilator is an essential right ideal, and semiprime means having no nonzero nilpotent two-sided ideal.

Proof 1: Apply nilpotence of the right singular ideal

The Mewborn–Winton theorem says that under the maximal condition on right annihilators, the right singular ideal is nilpotent. The maximal condition is the stated ascending chain condition. Since R is semiprime, that nilpotent ideal must be zero. Hence R is right nonsingular.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The final nilpotence implication is direct, but right annihilators, essential right ideals and the singular ideal of a noncommutative ring introduce a linked structural framework. A small-extension undergraduate classification is not confidently justified.
