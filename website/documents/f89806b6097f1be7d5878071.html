Statement

Let U_1,\ldots,U_N be independent uniform variables on [0,1], and let F_N(t)=N^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N1_{\{U_i\le t\}}. For 0<t<1, NF_N(t) has the binomial distribution with parameters N,t, mean Nt and variance Nt(1-t).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Independent uniform samples give finitely many Bernoulli events at the fixed threshold. The count, mean and variance are finite binomial calculations, a small elementary probability extension without empirical-process theory.

Proof 1: Identify independent Bernoulli threshold trials — outline

Given 0<t<1, we have that NF_N(t) is distributed as a binomial distribution with mean Nt and variance Nt(1-t).

The source leaves the identification of the threshold trials and their binomial moments implicit.
