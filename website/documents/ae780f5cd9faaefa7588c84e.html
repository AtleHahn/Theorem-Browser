Statement

Let H,K be subgroups of a group G, and let a,b\in G with HaK=HbK. Then \operatorname{rank}(H\cap aKa^{-1})=\operatorname{rank}(H\cap bKb^{-1}), where rank is the least cardinality of a generating set.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies direct products, cosets, automorphisms and generating sets. The coordinate actions or double-coset comparison are direct combinations of these; rank is explicitly defined as minimal generator cardinality.

Proof 1: Use conjugacy of the two intersections

If H,K\leq G are two subgroups of a group G and if a,b\in G define the same double coset HaK=HbK then the subgroups H\cap aKa^{-1} and H\cap bKb^{-1} are conjugate in G and thus have the same rank.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies conjugation and generating sets. Double cosets and rank as least generating-set cardinality are small related definitions; the supplied conjugacy transports generators bijectively.
