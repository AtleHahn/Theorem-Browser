Statement

Let n\geq1, let m\geq0 be an integer, and let A\subseteq\mathbb R^n be closed. For every multi-index \alpha=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n) of nonnegative integers with |\alpha|=\sum_i\alpha_i\leq m, suppose a real-valued function f_\alpha on A is given. Define

R_\alpha(x,y)=f_\alpha(x)-\sum_{|\beta|\leq m-|\alpha|}\frac{f_{\alpha+\beta}(y)}{\beta!}(x-y)^\beta.

Assume the following compatibility condition at every a\in A: for every \varepsilon>0, there exists \delta>0 such that, whenever x,y\in A, x\ne y, \|x-a\|<\delta and \|y-a\|<\delta,

|R_\alpha(x,y)|\leq\varepsilon\|x-y\|^{m-|\alpha|}\quad\text{for every }|\alpha|\leq m.

Then there exists F\in C^m(\mathbb R^n) with

D^\alpha F(x)=f_\alpha(x)\qquad(x\in A,\ |\alpha|\leq m),

and F can be chosen real analytic at every point of \mathbb R^n\setminus A. In particular F|_A=f_0.

Here \beta!=\prod_i\beta_i!, (x-y)^\beta=\prod_i(x_i-y_i)^{\beta_i}, and D^\alpha is the corresponding iterated partial derivative. Class C^m means continuous partial derivatives through order m; real analytic means locally represented by a convergent real power series.
