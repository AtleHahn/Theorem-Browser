Proof 1: Take the progression one modulo two

Dirichlet’s theorem gives infinitely many primes of the form 1+2n, a coprime arithmetic progression. These are odd primes.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Set a=1 and d=2 in the explicitly supplied prime-progression theorem. School primes, parity and coprimality suffice; Dirichlet theorem is used without its analytic proof.
