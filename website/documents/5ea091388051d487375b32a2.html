Statement

Let T preserve a probability measure \mu on (\Omega,\Sigma) and let g_n\in L^1(\mu), n\ge1, be real functions satisfying g_{n+m}(x)\le g_n(x)+g_m(T^n x) almost everywhere for every n,m\ge1. Define X(m,m+n)=g_n\circ T^m for integers m\ge0,n\ge1. Then the integrable family satisfies X(m+1,n+1)=X(m,n)\circ T and X(0,n)\le X(0,m)+X(m,n) for 0<m<n. Conversely, every integrable family with these properties is obtained this way from g_n=X(0,n). The sequences g_n/n and X(0,n)/n coincide.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The family is defined using measure-preserving transformations and integrable random variables.

Proof 1: Convert stationary increments by the source substitutions

The two formulations are equivalent by setting

  • g_n=X(0,n) with n\ge1;
  • X(m,m+n)=g_n\circ T^m with m\ge0 and n\ge1.
