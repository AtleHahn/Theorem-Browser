Proof 1: Integration by parts for a product of many functions — proof

Integrating the product rule for three multiplied functions, {\displaystyle u(x)}, {\displaystyle v(x)}, {\displaystyle w(x)}, gives:

{\displaystyle \int _{a}^{b}uv\,dw\ =\ {\Big [}uvw{\Big ]}_{a}^{b}-\int _{a}^{b}uw\,dv-\int _{a}^{b}vw\,du.}

In general, for {\displaystyle n} factors

{\displaystyle \left(\prod _{i=1}^{n}u_{i}(x)\right)'\ =\ \sum _{j=1}^{n}u_{j}'(x)\prod _{i\neq j}^{n}u_{i}(x),}

which leads to

{\displaystyle \left[\prod _{i=1}^{n}u_{i}(x)\right]_{a}^{b}\ =\ \sum _{j=1}^{n}\int _{a}^{b}u_{j}'(x)\prod _{i\neq j}^{n}u_{i}(x)\,dx.}
