Statement

If p is prime and 0<j<p, then p\mid\binom pj.

Level
High school only
Level justification

This is elementary divisibility and the factorial formula.

Mathematical Field
High School Mathematics
Required topics
Prime divisibilityFactorialsBinomial coefficients
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Compare numerator and denominator prime factors

The numerator p! in \binom pj=p!/(j!(p-j)!) is divisible by p. Every factor in the denominator is less than p and coprime to it. Since the quotient is an integer, it is divisible by p.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Compare the prime factors in the factorial numerator and denominator and use integrality of the binomial coefficient. School integer divisibility suffices.
