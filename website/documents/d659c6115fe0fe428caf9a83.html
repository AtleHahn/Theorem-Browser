Statement

Let A be a topological space and f_n:A\to\mathbb R or \mathbb C continuous. If |f_n(x)|\le M_n for all x,n, where M_n\ge0 and \sum_nM_n<\infty, the sum \sum_nf_n is continuous.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Real analysis supplies continuous functions, uniform bounds and norm convergence; linear algebra supplies linearity and finite dimension. The continuous-function norm is the supremum norm, and an isometry preserves it. These direct normed-space definitions suffice without dual or spectral theory.

Proof 1: M-test and uniform limit theorem

By the Weierstrass M-test, a series bounded termwise by a summable numerical series converges uniformly. Its finite partial sums are continuous. The uniform limit theorem therefore gives continuity of the sum.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the M-test to get uniform convergence and then the uniform limit theorem to continuous partial sums. Undergraduate real analysis and elementary topology suffice, including the arbitrary topological domain with scalar-valued functions.
