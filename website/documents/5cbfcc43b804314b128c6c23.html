Statement

Let ABCD be a planar quadrilateral with no three consecutive vertices collinear, and let E,F,G,H be the consecutive midpoints of AB,BC,CD,DA. Assume the midpoint parallelogram EFGH is nondegenerate. Then EFGH is a rhombus if and only if |AC|=|BD|.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The stated configuration and equivalence use elementary plane geometry.

Proof 1: Use the midpoint side lengths — outline

Triangles ADC and HDG are similar by the side-angle-side criterion, since DH=DA/2, DG=DC/2, and the angle at D is common. So angles DAC and DHG are equal, making HG parallel to AC. In the same way EF is parallel to AC, so HG and EF are parallel to each other; the same holds for HE and GF.

A side of the Varignon parallelogram is half as long as the diagonal in the original quadrilateral it is parallel to.

The Varignon parallelogram is a rhombus if and only if the two diagonals of the quadrilateral have equal length, that is, if the quadrilateral is an equidiagonal quadrilateral. Under the stated nondegeneracy assumption, the final equal-side check using these lengths is implicit in the source.
