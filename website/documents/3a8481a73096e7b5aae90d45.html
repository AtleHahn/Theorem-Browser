Statement

Let X_n and X be random elements in a metric space S, with X_n converging in distribution to X. If g:S\to S' is continuous and S' is a metric space, then g(X_n) converges in distribution to g(X).

Proof 1: Compose bounded continuous test functions

We will need a particular statement from the portmanteau theorem: that convergence in distribution X_n\xrightarrow{d}X is equivalent to

\mathbb E f(X_n)\to\mathbb E f(X)

for every bounded continuous real-valued functional f on S.

So it suffices to prove that \mathbb E f(g(X_n))\to\mathbb E f(g(X)) for every bounded continuous real-valued functional f on S'. Here g is assumed continuous. Note that F=f\circ g is itself a bounded continuous functional. And so the claim follows from the statement above.
