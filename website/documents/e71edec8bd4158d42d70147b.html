Statement

Let U\subset\mathbb R^n be open and let f_m:U\to\mathbb R be smooth for m\ge0, with all supports contained in one compact set C\subset U. For every open interval I\subset\mathbb R containing 0, there exists F\in C^{\infty}(I\times U) such that \partial_t^mF(0,x)=f_m(x) for all m\ge0 and x\in U.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves smooth functions on Euclidean open sets; higher partial derivatives; compact support; sequences. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Sum successively concentrated cutoffs — outline

Choose a smooth bump function \psi with compact support in a bounded interval about 0, equal identically to 1 near 0.

For each m, let

{\displaystyle F_{m}(t,x)={t^{m} \over m!}\cdot \psi \left({t \over \varepsilon _{m}}\right)\cdot f_{m}(x),}

where εm is chosen sufficiently small that

{\displaystyle \|\partial ^{\alpha }F_{m}\|_{\infty }\leq 2^{-m}}

for |\alpha|<m, where \alpha is a multi-index in (t,x) and the norm is the supremum over \mathbb R\times U. These estimates imply that each sum

{\displaystyle \sum _{m\geq 0}\partial ^{\alpha }F_{m}}

is uniformly convergent and hence that

{\displaystyle F=\sum _{m\geq 0}F_{m}}

is a smooth function with

{\displaystyle \partial ^{\alpha }F=\sum _{m\geq 0}\partial ^{\alpha }F_{m}.}

By construction

{\displaystyle \partial _{t}^{m}F(t,x)|_{t=0}=f_{m}(x).}

Restrict F to I\times U.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Scaled smooth cutoffs multiply the prescribed coefficients, and derivative estimates force uniform convergence of every derivative series. For a derivative of total order less than m, each term contains a positive power of epsilon_m, while derivatives of f_m are bounded on the common compact support. Multivariable differentiation and uniform convergence from undergraduate real analysis suffice, with bump functions a small related extension.

Routine omissions

For each m only finitely many derivative orders below m are involved. Their cutoff scaling has positive powers of epsilon_m; the source leaves this routine bound implicit. For fixed alpha finitely many initial terms precede the geometrically bounded tail.

Uniform convergence of all derivative series permits termwise differentiation
Kind:

Theorem

Treatment:

Implicitly assumed

Statement used:

If a series of smooth functions and each of its derivative series converge uniformly, its sum is smooth and derivatives may be taken termwise.

Application in proof:

Obtain smoothness and evaluate the prescribed derivatives at t=0.

Undergraduate level
