Statement

Let X be an irreducible complex projective variety of dimension n, D an ample Cartier divisor and \mathcal F a coherent sheaf. As positive integers m\to\infty,

h^0(X,\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal O_X(mD))=\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal F)\frac{D^n}{n!}m^n+O(m^{n-1}).

Here D^n is the top self-intersection number.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Projective varieties; Ample Cartier divisors; Coherent sheaf cohomology; Intersection numbers. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Higher cohomology vanishes for sufficiently ample twists — outline

The asymptotic Riemann–Roch formula states that if {\displaystyle {\mathcal {F}}} is any coherent sheaf on X then

{\displaystyle \chi \left(X,{\mathcal {F}}\otimes {\mathcal {O}}_{X}(mD)\right)=\operatorname {rank} ({\mathcal {F}}){\frac {(D^{n})}{n!}}.m^{n}+O(m^{n-1}).}

Moreover, if D is ample, then Serre vanishing implies that

{\displaystyle h^{0}\left(X,{\mathcal {F}}\otimes {\mathcal {O}}_{X}(mD)\right)=\operatorname {rank} ({\mathcal {F}}){\frac {(D^{n})}{n!}}.m^{n}+O(m^{n-1}).}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The deduction removes higher cohomology terms from the Euler characteristic, but understanding that step requires coherent sheaves, their twists and cohomology, ample Cartier divisors and intersection numbers. These interdependent algebraic-geometric prerequisites substantially exceed the undergraduate allowance. The growth formula alone does not capture the burden of its objects and hypotheses.
