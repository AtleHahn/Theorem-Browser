Statement

There exists an integer N_0 such that every odd integer N>N_0 can be written N=p+2a, where p is prime and a is a positive integer with at most two prime factors. A positive integer has at most two prime factors if it is 1, a prime, or a product of two primes, with multiplicity counted.
