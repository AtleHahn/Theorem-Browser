Statement

The rational points on x^2+y^2=1, except (-1,0), are exactly x=\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2},\quad y=\frac{2t}{1+t^2}\quad(t\in\mathbb Q). The inverse parameter is t=y/(1+x).

Proof 1: Source calculation — outline

Except for (-1,0), the unit circle may also be defined by a parametric equation

{\displaystyle x={\frac {1-t^{2}}{1+t^{2}}},\quad y={\frac {2t}{1+t^{2}}}.}

These formulas and the inverse relationship t = y / (1 + x) mean that, except for (−1, 0), a point (x, y) on the circle is rational if and only if the corresponding value of t is a rational number.
