Statement

Let X be a separable real Hilbert space and T>0. A function h:[0,T]\to X is regulated if its norm limits from the left and right exist at every point where the corresponding side is present. Put \|h\|_\infty=\sup_{t\in[0,T]}\|h(t)\| and \operatorname{Var}(h)=\sup_{0=t_0<\cdots<t_m=T}\sum_{j=1}^m\|h(t_j)-h(t_{j-1})\|. Suppose (f_n) are regulated functions and for every \varepsilon>0 there exists L_\varepsilon>0 such that for each n there is a function u_n of finite variation with \|f_n-u_n\|_\infty<\varepsilon and \|u_n(0)\|+\operatorname{Var}(u_n)\le L_\varepsilon. Then there is a subsequence (f_{n_k}) and a regulated f:[0,T]\to X such that \ell(f_{n_k}(t))\to\ell(f(t)) for every t\in[0,T] and every continuous linear functional \ell:X\to\mathbb R.
