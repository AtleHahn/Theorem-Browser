Statement

Let k be a field and M a finitely generated k[x]-module. For any finite generating set, the kernel of its presentation map k[x]^r\to M is free; consequently M has a free resolution of length at most one.

Proof 1: The first relation module is a submodule over a PID — outline

For a finite generating set of M, take the surjection L_0\to M from the finite free module on its generators. Its kernel is the first syzygy module. Since k[x] is a principal ideal domain, the theorem that submodules of free modules over a PID are free applies to this kernel.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Present M by a finite free module and use the PID submodule theorem on its kernel. General modules, free presentations and resolutions together exceed the small-extension allowance for ordinary undergraduate algebra; retain conservatively.
