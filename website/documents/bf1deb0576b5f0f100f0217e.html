Statement

Let X be a connected complete simply connected metric space with non-positive curvature. Then any two points of X are joined by a unique minimizing geodesic, and X is contractible. Here non-positive curvature means that each point has a neighborhood U in which points are joined by geodesics and, for z\in U and a constant-speed geodesic \gamma:[0,1]\to U, d(z,\gamma(1/2))^2\le\tfrac12d(z,\gamma(0))^2+\tfrac12d(z,\gamma(1))^2-\tfrac14d(\gamma(0),\gamma(1))^2.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns completeness and local curvature of general metric spaces, simple connectivity, geodesics and contractibility.

Proof 1: Specialization of the covering theorem

Use the metric Cartan–Hadamard theorem: the universal cover of a connected complete nonpositively curved metric space is a Hadamard space. For simply connected X, this cover is X itself. The Hadamard-space properties used in the source are unique minimizing geodesics between points and contractibility. They give both conclusions.

Specialize a universal-cover theorem in nonpositively curved metric geometry and use the Hadamard-space consequences. Universal covers, metric geodesics and curvature form a cumulative framework not confidently small beyond undergraduate topology; retain beyond undergraduate.
