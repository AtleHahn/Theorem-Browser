Statement

Let \widehat{\mathbb R^2}=\mathbb R^2\cup\{\infty\} be the one-point compactification of the plane, homeomorphic to the sphere; neighborhoods of \infty are complements of compact subsets of \mathbb R^2, together with \infty. Let A,B be closed subsets with connected intersection A\cap B. If p,q\in\widehat{\mathbb R^2}\setminus(A\cup B) can be joined by a continuous path avoiding A and also by a continuous path avoiding B, then they can be joined by a continuous path avoiding A\cup B. A path from p to q is a continuous map \gamma:[0,1]\to\widehat{\mathbb R^2} with \gamma(0)=p and \gamma(1)=q.
