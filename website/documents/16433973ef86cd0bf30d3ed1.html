Statement

Let M be a closed, connected smooth three-dimensional manifold, where closed means compact and without boundary. If its fundamental group \pi_1(M) is finite, then M admits a Riemannian metric of constant positive sectional curvature.

The fundamental group consists of based loops modulo homotopy fixing the base point, with concatenation as its operation. The metric conclusion means that there is a number c>0 such that the sectional curvature is c on every two-dimensional tangent plane at every point of M.
