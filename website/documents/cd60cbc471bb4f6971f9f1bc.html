Statement

Work in ZFC and assume GCH: 2^\kappa=\kappa^+ for every infinite cardinal \kappa, where \kappa^+ is the next larger cardinal. If ordinals \beta+1<\alpha satisfy \aleph_\beta\ge\operatorname{cf}(\aleph_\alpha), then \aleph_\alpha^{\aleph_\beta}=\aleph_{\alpha+1}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The formula combines transfinite aleph indexing, cofinality and infinite cardinal exponentiation, requiring substantial set-theoretic preparation. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Combine the König lower bound with the GCH upper bound

Kőnig’s theorem gives \aleph_\alpha^{\aleph_\beta}\ge\aleph_\alpha^{\operatorname{cf}(\aleph_\alpha)}>\aleph_\alpha. On the other hand \aleph_\alpha^{\aleph_\beta}\le\aleph_\alpha^{\aleph_\alpha}\le(2^{\aleph_\alpha})^{\aleph_\alpha}=2^{\aleph_\alpha\cdot\aleph_\alpha}=2^{\aleph_\alpha}=\aleph_{\alpha+1}. Since the latter cardinal is the successor of \aleph_\alpha, these bounds force equality.
