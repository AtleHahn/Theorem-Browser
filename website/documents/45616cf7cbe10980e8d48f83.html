Statement

For a complex number q with |q|<1, define (a;q)_n=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(1-aq^j), with empty product 1, and (a;q)_\infty=\prod_{j=0}^{\infty}(1-aq^j). Then

\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{q^{n^2}}{(q;q)_n}=\frac1{(q;q^5)_\infty(q^4;q^5)_\infty},\qquad \sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{q^{n^2+n}}{(q;q)_n}=\frac1{(q^2;q^5)_\infty(q^3;q^5)_\infty}.

These are also identities of formal power series in q.

In partition form, for every integer N\ge0:

  1. The number of partitions of N whose adjacent parts differ by at least two equals the number whose parts are all 1 or 4 modulo 5.
  2. The number of partitions of N whose adjacent parts differ by at least two and whose smallest part is at least two equals the number whose parts are all 2 or 3 modulo 5.

A partition is an unordered sum of positive integers, written in decreasing order for the adjacency condition; the empty partition is counted at N=0. Alternatively, the left count in (1) counts partitions with k parts and smallest part at least k, summed over k; in (2) replace k by k+1 in this lower bound.
