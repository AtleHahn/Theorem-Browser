Statement

Every convex body K\subset\mathbb R^3 is contained in a cube each of whose six faces meets K. Here a convex body is a compact convex set with nonempty interior; convex means containing the line segment between any two of its points. The cube may have arbitrary position and orientation.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate compactness plus elementary spatial geometry suffices for the full existence assertion.

Level

Level justification

For the three-dimensional statement, the separation argument takes place in a planar annulus. Polygonal approximation and crossing parity supply its separating curve; continuity, compactness and orthogonal rotations then give an equal-value frame. These use undergraduate analysis, linear algebra and basic topology, with only a short introduction to polygonal crossing parity. Applying this to the continuous width function gives the six supporting faces of a cube.
