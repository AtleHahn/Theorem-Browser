Statement

For every Riemannian metric on the compact two-dimensional torus T, \int_TK\,dA=0. In particular K cannot be everywhere strictly positive or everywhere strictly negative.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns Gaussian curvature and surface integration for arbitrary Riemannian metrics on the torus, beyond the stipulated calculus and linear-algebra background.

Proof 1: A Riemannian torus has zero total Gaussian curvature — outline

The torus has Euler characteristic zero, so Gauss–Bonnet gives total curvature zero. Consequently it cannot carry a metric with Gaussian curvature strictly of one sign everywhere.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Gauss-Bonnet to an arbitrary Riemannian metric on the torus. Gaussian curvature, intrinsic area and Riemannian integration require differential geometry; substituting chi=0 does not remove that framework.
