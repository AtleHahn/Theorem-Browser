Statement

For every integer n\ge1, the integer n(n+1) has strictly more distinct prime divisors than n.

Level

Proof 1: Use coprimality of consecutive integers

Since each natural number greater than 1 has at least one prime factor, and two successive numbers n and (n+1) have no prime factor in common, the product n(n+1) has more different prime factors than the number n itself.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Every prime divisor of n remains in the product, and a prime divisor of n+1 is new because consecutive integers are coprime. This includes n=1 and uses only elementary divisibility.
