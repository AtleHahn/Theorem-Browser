Statement

The group \operatorname{Diff}(S^3) of smooth diffeomorphisms of the standard sphere S^3=\{x\in\mathbb R^4:\|x\|=1\}, with its C^\infty topology, has the homotopy type of O(4)=\{A\in\mathbb R^{4\times4}:A^TA=I\}. A diffeomorphism is a smooth bijection with smooth inverse. The C^\infty topology is convergence of maps and all derivatives in local charts, uniformly on compact sets. Spaces X,Y have the same homotopy type if continuous maps u:X\to Y,v:Y\to X exist whose composites are homotopic to the respective identity maps; a homotopy is a continuous family parameterized by [0,1].
