Statement

The group \operatorname{Diff}(S^3) of smooth diffeomorphisms of the standard sphere S^3=\{x\in\mathbb R^4:\|x\|=1\}, with its C^\infty topology, has the homotopy type of O(4)=\{A\in\mathbb R^{4\times4}:A^TA=I\}. A diffeomorphism is a smooth bijection with smooth inverse. The C^\infty topology is convergence of maps and all derivatives in local charts, uniformly on compact sets. Spaces X,Y have the same homotopy type if continuous maps u:X\to Y,v:Y\to X exist whose composites are homotopic to the respective identity maps; a homotopy is a continuous family parameterized by [0,1].

Proof 1: Ricci-flow deformation of round-metric spaces (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Theorems1.2–1.3; §9 pp.104–108 including Theorem9.1 and final homotopy-equivalence deduction. Supporting construction §§4–8 pp.19–104.).

For S^3, the constant-curvature metric space in Theorem 1.2 is contractible. The metric-orbit fibration used for Theorem 1.3 identifies the inclusion of the full isometry group into the full diffeomorphism group as a homotopy equivalence. For the standard round sphere that isometry group is O(4), including orientation-reversing maps. The deformation in §9 uses families of singular Ricci flows and conformal rounding.
