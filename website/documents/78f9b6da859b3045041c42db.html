Statement

Fix a compact connected Riemann surface X of genus greater than one. Up to biholomorphic isomorphism, there are only finitely many compact connected Riemann surfaces Y of genus greater than one for which a nonconstant holomorphic map X\to Y exists. A Riemann surface is a complex one-dimensional manifold; biholomorphic means a holomorphic bijection with holomorphic inverse, and genus is the number of handles of its underlying surface.

Proof 1: Bounded correspondences: a proof sketch (external)

A link to the proof outline can be found here (§II.8(a,b), printed pp.227–229, especially the de Franchis–Severi discussion on p.229.).

This is a proof sketch using the Humbert–Castelnuovo finite-fiber theorem. Associate f with f* f_* on H1(X); the degree is bounded by Riemann–Hurwitz. These integral correspondences are bounded in the positive Hodge norm, hence form a finite set. Apply the cited Humbert–Castelnuovo finite-fiber result to obtain finitely many maps up to target isomorphism, hence finitely many targets.
