Statement

Let G be a group and N\triangleleft G a normal subgroup. The map A\mapsto A/N is a bijection from subgroups A\le G containing N to subgroups of G/N, with inverse S\mapsto\pi^{-1}(S) for \pi(g)=gN. For subgroups A,B containing N, it preserves inclusion, intersections and generated subgroups: A\subseteq B\iff A/N\subseteq B/N,\quad(A\cap B)/N=(A/N)\cap(B/N),\quad\langle A,B\rangle/N=\langle A/N,B/N\rangle. If A\subseteq B, the indices satisfy [B:A]=[B/N:A/N], including infinite cardinalities. Also A\triangleleft G if and only if A/N\triangleleft G/N. Here [B:A] counts left cosets, and \langle A,B\rangle denotes the subgroup generated by A\cup B.
