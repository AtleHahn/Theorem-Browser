Statement

For characteristic p=0 or prime p, the first-order theory of algebraically closed fields of characteristic p has at least two nonisomorphic countably infinite models, and hence is not \aleph_0-categorical.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement is about first-order field models over arbitrary characteristic.

Proof 1: Countable models of different transcendence degrees

The theory of algebraically closed fields of a given characteristic is not categorical in ω (the countable infinite cardinal); there are models of transcendence degree over the prime field 0, 1, 2, ..., ω.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The deduction compares two models using transcendence degree. Understanding it requires algebraic closures, prime fields, algebraic independence and transcendence bases, plus the model-theoretic meaning of a countable model and categoricity. That accumulated field-theory and logic preparation cannot confidently be reduced to a brief extension of introductory abstract algebra.
