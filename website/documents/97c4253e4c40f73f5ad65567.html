Statement

If G,H are complex Hilbert spaces of dimensions n,m\ge1 and \Phi:B(G)\to B(H) is completely positive, then there are operators V_i:H\to G, 1\le i\le nm, with \Phi(a)=\sum_{i=1}^{nm}V_i^*aV_i.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The finite operator formula is concrete, but complete positivity is an operator-algebra prerequisite not defined by a finite-matrix positivity criterion in this source.

Proof 1: Decompose the dilation into defining matrix representations — outline

Let (\pi,V,K) be a minimal Stinespring representation of \Phi, so \Phi(a)=V^*\pi(a)V. By the tensor-quotient construction, K has dimension at most that of \mathbb C^{n\times n}\otimes\mathbb C^m. Use the representation theorem that a finite-dimensional unital representation of M_n(\mathbb C) is a direct sum of its defining representation. Thus K can be identified with

K=\bigoplus_{i=1}^r\mathbb C_i^n,\qquad r\leq nm.

Each \mathbb C_i^n is a copy of the n-dimensional Hilbert space. From the tensor-quotient action \pi(a)(b\otimes g)=ab\otimes g, with b\in M_n(\mathbb C) and g\in H, the identification can be arranged so that \pi(a) acts as a on each summand. In particular,

P_i\pi(a)P_i^*=a,

where P_i is the coordinate projection from K to \mathbb C_i^n. Let V_i=P_iV. We have

\Phi(a)=\sum_{i=1}^r(V^*P_i^*)(P_i\pi(a)P_i^*)(P_iV) =\sum_{i=1}^rV_i^*aV_i,

and the result is proved, padding with zero operators if r<nm.
