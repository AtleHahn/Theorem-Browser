Statement

Let a be a normal element of a unital C*-algebra A with unit 1, and let \Phi_a:C(\sigma(a))\to A be its continuous functional calculus, writing f(a)=\Phi_a(f). For f\in C(\sigma(a)), f(a) is positive if and only if f(\sigma(a))\subseteq[0,\infty).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves C*-algebras; Normal elements and spectra; Continuous functional calculus; Positive elements and nonnegative functions. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.
