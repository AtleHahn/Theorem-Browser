Statement

For a Euclidean hexagon ABCDEF with six distinct vertices on a nondegenerate conic, suppose AB is parallel to DE and the other opposite pairs meet at finite points Y=BC∩EF and Z=CD∩FA. Then YZ is parallel to AB and DE.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The affine geometric conclusion is elementary, while its proof uses projective geometry.

Mathematical Field
High School Mathematics

Proof 1: Points on the line at infinity

In the projective completion, X=AB∩DE is the point at infinity of that direction. Pascal’s theorem puts X,Y,Z on one line. Its affine part YZ therefore has the same point at infinity and is parallel to AB and DE.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the projective point representing a parallel direction and apply Pascal collinearity. Undergraduate linear algebra suffices with homogeneous coordinates and the line at infinity as a small related extension.
