Statement

Every elliptic curve E over \mathbb Q admits a nonconstant rational map defined over \mathbb Q

X_0(N)\longrightarrow E

for some positive integer N. The least such N equals the conductor of E.

Here an elliptic curve is a smooth projective curve of genus one with a specified rational point. The classical modular curve X_0(N) is the smooth projective modular curve over \mathbb Q whose noncuspidal moduli points represent elliptic curves together with a cyclic subgroup of order N; it includes the cusps in its compactification. The conductor is the arithmetic conductor of the elliptic curve. A map as above is called a modular parametrization.
