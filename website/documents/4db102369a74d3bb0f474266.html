Statement

Let M be a compact complex manifold admitting a Kähler class, with vanishing real first Chern class c_1(M). Every Kähler class contains exactly one Ricci-flat Kähler metric.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves compact complex manifolds; Kähler classes; first Chern class; Ricci curvature. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Ricci-flat representative when the first Chern class vanishes

Use the Calabi–Yau theorem: for a prescribed real closed (1,1)-form representing the Ricci class on a compact Kähler manifold, there is exactly one Kähler metric in each Kähler class with that Ricci form. When c_1(M)=0, prescribe the zero form. The resulting metric is Ricci-flat, and uniqueness is the uniqueness in the prescribed class.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Applying the prescribed-Ricci theorem requires Kähler metrics and classes, the first Chern class, cohomology representatives and Ricci forms. The zero-form specialization does not remove that substantial geometric prerequisite burden.
