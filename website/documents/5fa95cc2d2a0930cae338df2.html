Statement

Let \Lambda(n)=\log p if n=p^k for a prime p and integer k\ge1, and \Lambda(n)=0 otherwise. If \sum_{n=2}^\infty\Lambda(n)e^{-ny}\sim1/y as y\downarrow0, then \sum_{n\le x}\Lambda(n)\sim x as x\to\infty.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Von Mangoldt function; Asymptotic equivalence; Infinite series. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Specialize the Tauberian theorem to von Mangoldt coefficients

Use the Hardy–Littlewood Tauberian theorem: for nonnegative coefficients a_n,

\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_ne^{-ny}\sim\frac1y\quad(y\downarrow0)

implies \sum_{n=0}^Na_n\sim N.

Taking a_n=\Lambda(n) for n\geq2 and the earlier coefficients zero, from

\sum_{n=2}^\infty\Lambda(n)e^{-ny}\sim\frac1y

one concludes

\sum_{n\leq x}\Lambda(n)\sim x,

an equivalent form of the prime number theorem.
