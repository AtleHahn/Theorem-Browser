Statement

For an integer n\ge1, applying the cotangent-residue method to z^{-2n-1} over symmetric half-integer squares yields only 0+\zeta(2n+1)-\zeta(2n+1)=0, rather than determining the odd zeta value. Here \zeta(s)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k^{-s} for real s>1, and the squares have positively oriented boundaries of [-N-\tfrac12,N+\tfrac12]^2, N a positive integer.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level

The assertion concerns the result of a complex contour method, including its vanishing integral, rather than only cancellation of real sums. Proof 1: Pair positive and negative integer residues — outline

The residue at zero vanishes for z^{-2n-1}\pi\cot(\pi z). The residues at positive and negative nonzero integers are their opposite odd reciprocal powers, so the two convergent sums cancel. The contour integral tends to zero, leaving the stated identity.
