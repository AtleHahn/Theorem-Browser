Statement

Let X be a real locally convex Hausdorff topological vector space, and let A,B\subseteq X be nonempty closed convex sets. Define \operatorname{recc}(S)=\{v\in X:x+tv\in S\text{ for all }x\in S,\ t\ge0\}. If at least one of A,B is locally compact in its subspace topology and \operatorname{recc}(A)\cap\operatorname{recc}(B) is a linear subspace, then A-B=\{a-b:a\in A,b\in B\} is closed. Locally convex means that zero has a neighborhood basis of convex sets; local compactness means each point has a neighborhood with compact closure in the indicated subspace.
