Statement

For n\in\{6,10,14\} there are no positive integers a,b,c satisfying a^n+b^n=c^n.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The hypotheses and conclusion use only finite integer arithmetic.

Mathematical Field

Review notes

Proof 1: Square the variables and use the odd-exponent cases

Use the known Fermat theorems for exponents 3,5,7 as external prerequisites. Write n=2h with h=3,5,7. A solution would imply (a^2)^h+(b^2)^h=(c^2)^h, contradicting the theorem for exponent h.

Level justification

Square each positive integer coordinate and invoke the explicitly supplied nonexistence results at exponents three, five and seven. Only power laws are used, not the proofs of those Fermat cases.
