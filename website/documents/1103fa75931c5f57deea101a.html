Statement

Let X be a connected compact Riemann surface of genus g\ge2. Its group \operatorname{Aut}(X) of biholomorphic self-maps is finite and satisfies

|\operatorname{Aut}(X)|\le84(g-1).

Here a Riemann surface is a one-dimensional complex manifold, and the genus is the topological genus of its underlying oriented surface.

A finite group G occurs as \operatorname{Aut}(X) for such a surface attaining equality if and only if G is generated by elements a,b of orders exactly 2,3, respectively, whose product ab has order exactly 7. Equivalently, these are the finite quotients of the triangle group

\langle a,b\mid a^2=b^3=(ab)^7=1\rangle

in which the images of a,b,ab have those exact orders. Such groups and surfaces are called Hurwitz groups and Hurwitz surfaces.
