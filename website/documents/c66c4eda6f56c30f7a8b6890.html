Proof 1: Applying uniformization to the universal cover

Use two stated external results: every connected Riemann surface has a universal covering carrying a Riemann-surface structure for which the covering is holomorphic, and the uniformization theorem says that a simply connected Riemann surface is biholomorphic to the sphere, plane or disk. The universal cover is simply connected, so applying the second result to the cover yields the three possibilities.
