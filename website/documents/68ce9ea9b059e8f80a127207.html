Statement

Let p,q be rational primes congruent to 1 modulo 4, with Legendre symbol (p/q)=1. In the rings of integers of K_q=\mathbb Q(\sqrt q) and K_p=\mathbb Q(\sqrt p), respectively, write

(p)=\mathfrak p\mathfrak p',\qquad(q)=\mathfrak q\mathfrak q'.

Let \varepsilon_p,\varepsilon_q be fundamental units of K_p,K_q. Then

[\varepsilon_p/\mathfrak q]=[\varepsilon_q/\mathfrak p].

The ring of integers consists of elements integral over \mathbb Z. A fundamental unit generates the infinite cyclic unit group modulo its subgroup \{1,-1\}. For a unit u and a prime ideal \mathfrak l of odd residue characteristic, [u/\mathfrak l] is 1 when its image in the finite field \mathcal O_K/\mathfrak l is a square, and -1 otherwise. The Legendre symbol (p/q)=1 means p is a nonzero square modulo q.
