Statement

Let \Omega be a bounded Jordan domain, p\in\Omega, and \Omega_n bounded Jordan domains containing \Omega, with \overline{\Omega_{n+1}}\subset\Omega_n and Carathéodory kernel \Omega at p. Let f_n:\Omega_n\to\Omega be conformal bijections satisfying f_n(p)=p and f_n^{\prime}(p)>0. Then f_n|_\Omega\to\operatorname{id}_\Omega uniformly on compact subsets.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement compares conformal maps of nested complex domains in the kernel topology.

Proof 1: Normalized outer Jordan maps converge to the identity — outline

By the Carathéodory kernel theorem, f_n(z) converges uniformly on compacta in \Omega to z. In fact, Carathéodory's theorem implies that the inverse maps tend uniformly on compacta to z. Given a subsequence of f_n, it has a subsequence convergent on compacta in \Omega, by Montel's theorem for the bounded family of holomorphic maps into \Omega. Since the inverse functions converge to z, it follows that the subsequence converges to z on compacta. Hence f_n converges to z on compacta in \Omega.

Level justification

Identify inverse-map limits by the kernel theorem and combine them with normal-family subsequences for the forward maps. Caratheodory convergence of domains and normalized conformal inverses is a cumulative extension beyond confidently ordinary undergraduate complex analysis.
