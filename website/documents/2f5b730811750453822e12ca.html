Statement

In the hyperbolic plane of curvature -1, let a triangle have side lengths a,b,c opposite angles A,B,C, with B=\pi/2. Then \sin C=\sinh c/\sinh b.

Proof 1: Set the right-angle sine to one

The hyperbolic sine law gives \sin C/\sinh c=\sin B/\sinh b. Since B=\pi/2, its sine is 1; multiplying by \sinh c gives the result.
