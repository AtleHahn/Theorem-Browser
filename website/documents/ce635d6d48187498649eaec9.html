Statement

Let \Phi be a finite reduced root system with simple roots \alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n, and set a_{ij}=2(\alpha_i,\alpha_j)/(\alpha_j,\alpha_j). Let \widetilde{\mathfrak g} be the complex Lie algebra with generators e_i,f_i,h_i and relations

[h_i,h_j]=0,\quad[e_i,f_j]=\delta_{ij}h_i,\quad[h_i,e_j]=a_{ij}e_j,\quad[h_i,f_j]=-a_{ij}f_j.

Let \mathfrak h be the span of the h_i, identify \alpha_j\in\mathfrak h^* by \alpha_j(h_i)=a_{ij}, and put Q_+=\sum_j\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\alpha_j. Write \widetilde{\mathfrak g}_\alpha=\{x:[h,x]=\alpha(h)x\text{ for all }h\in\mathfrak h\}. In the root-space decomposition \widetilde{\mathfrak g}=\bigoplus_\alpha\widetilde{\mathfrak g}_\alpha of the preliminary Serre algebra, let \widetilde{\mathfrak g}_0=\mathfrak h and assume that its ideals are homogeneous. The sum \mathfrak r of all ideals \mathfrak i with \mathfrak i\cap\mathfrak h=0 also satisfies \mathfrak r\cap\mathfrak h=0. Moreover, writing \widetilde{\mathfrak n}_\pm for the sums of the positive and negative root spaces, \mathfrak r=(\mathfrak r\cap\widetilde{\mathfrak n}_-)\oplus(\mathfrak r\cap\widetilde{\mathfrak n}_+).

Proof 1: Take sums componentwise in the root grading

For each ideal {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {i}}} of {\displaystyle {\widetilde {\mathfrak {g}}}}, by the assumed homogeneity, {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {i}}} satisfies {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {i}}=\bigoplus _{\alpha }({\widetilde {\mathfrak {g}}}_{\alpha }\cap {\mathfrak {i}})}. It follows that the sum of ideals intersecting {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {h}}} trivially itself intersects {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {h}}} trivially. Let {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {r}}} be the sum of all ideals intersecting {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {h}}} trivially. Then there is a vector space decomposition: {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {r}}=({\mathfrak {r}}\cap {\widetilde {\mathfrak {n}}}_{-})\oplus ({\mathfrak {r}}\cap {\widetilde {\mathfrak {n}}}_{+})}.
