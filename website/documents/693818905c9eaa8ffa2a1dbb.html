Statement

Let X\subseteq Y\subseteq Z be Banach spaces with respective norms \|\cdot\|_X,\|\cdot\|_Y,\|\cdot\|_Z. Suppose every bounded sequence in X has a subsequence convergent in Y, and there is a constant k with \|y\|_Z\le k\|y\|_Y for all y\in Y. Then for every \varepsilon>0 there is C(\varepsilon)<\infty such that \|x\|_Y\le\varepsilon\|x\|_X+C(\varepsilon)\|x\|_Z\quad(x\in X). Banach means complete for the norm metric; the inclusions are linear.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Comparing multiple infinite-dimensional Banach topologies and compact embeddings gives a borderline cumulative burden; conservatively beyond undergraduate.
