Statement

Let K be a finite extension of \mathbb Q, let \mathcal O_K be its algebraic integers, and let \alpha\in\mathcal O_K generate K over \mathbb Q. Let f\in\mathbb Z[x] be its monic minimal polynomial. If a rational prime p does not divide the additive-group index [\mathcal O_K:\mathbb Z[\alpha]], factor f\bmod p=\prod_{i=1}^g\pi_i^{e_i} into distinct monic irreducible polynomials over \mathbb F_p. Then there are distinct prime ideals \mathfrak p_i of \mathcal O_K such that p\mathcal O_K=\prod_{i=1}^g\mathfrak p_i^{e_i},\qquad |\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p_i|=p^{\deg\pi_i}. An algebraic integer satisfies a monic integer polynomial; the displayed quotient cardinality is the ideal norm.
