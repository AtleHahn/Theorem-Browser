Statement

For each integer n\ge1, no subset of \mathbb R^n is homeomorphic to S^n=\{x\in\mathbb R^{n+1}:\|x\|_2=1\}. Here a homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse.

Proof 1: Injectivity contradicts antipodal equality (outline)

Use the Borsuk–Ulam theorem: every continuous map S^n\to\mathbb R^n identifies some antipodal pair. A homeomorphism of S^n onto a subset of \mathbb R^n, followed by the inclusion, would be a continuous injective map. But x and -x are distinct on the unit sphere, so their forced equality of images contradicts injectivity.
