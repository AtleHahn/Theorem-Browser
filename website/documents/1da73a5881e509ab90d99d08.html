Statement

For a number field K of degree n=[K:\mathbb Q], let r_1 be the number of real embeddings K\to\mathbb R and r_2 the number of conjugate pairs of nonreal embeddings K\to\mathbb C. Then n=r_1+2r_2.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A number field is a finite extension of Q; its degree and embeddings are undergraduate abstract-algebra concepts. Signature is explicitly just the real-embedding count and nonreal conjugate-pair count.

Proof 1: Count conjugate pairs of embeddings

There are n embeddings of K into \mathbb C. They either take values in \mathbb R or occur in pairs related by complex conjugation. Counting these gives n=r_1+2r_2.
