Statement

Let (M,g) be a smooth pseudo-Riemannian manifold with Levi-Civita connection \nabla. Write \Delta for the trace-of-Hessian Laplacian (the corresponding rough Laplacian on vector fields), \operatorname{div} for divergence, \mathcal L_Xg for the Lie derivative of the metric, and \operatorname{Ric} for Ricci curvature. Contractions and the pairing \langle\ ,\ \rangle use g, with vectors and covectors identified by g. For a smooth vector field X, \frac12\Delta\langle X,X\rangle=\langle\nabla X,\nabla X\rangle-\nabla_X\operatorname{div}X+\langle X,\operatorname{div}(\mathcal L_Xg)\rangle-\operatorname{Ric}(X,X).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Pseudo-Riemannian metrics; Levi-Civita connection and tensor contraction; Lie derivative, rough Laplacian and Ricci tensor. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Contract the Ricci-commutation formula — outline

As an application of the Ricci commutation identities, the formula

{\displaystyle \Delta X=-\nabla (\operatorname {div} X)+\operatorname {div} ({\mathcal {L}}_{X}g)-\operatorname {Ric} (X,\cdot )}

holds for any vector field X on a pseudo-Riemannian manifold. As a consequence, there is

{\displaystyle {\frac {1}{2}}\Delta \langle X,X\rangle =\langle \nabla X,\nabla X\rangle -\nabla _{X}\operatorname {div} X+\langle X,\operatorname {div} ({\mathcal {L}}_{X}g)\rangle -\operatorname {Ric} (X,X).}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof contracts a vector-field Ricci identity using metric compatibility and the covariant product rule. Pseudo-Riemannian metrics, connections, rough Laplacians and curvature tensors require a substantial differential-geometric background.
