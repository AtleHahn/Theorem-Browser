Statement

There are infinitely many prime positive integers, and no two distinct primes divide one another. Thus they form an infinite antichain for divisibility, meaning a set in which no distinct pair is related by divisibility. A prime is an integer greater than one whose only positive divisors are one and itself.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All elements are ordinary positive integers; primality and the pairwise divisibility condition are defined explicitly.
