Statement

Let K be a field with the following property: for every integer r>0 there is an integer \psi(r) such that, whenever n\geq\psi(r) and a_1,\ldots,a_n\in K, the equation

a_1x_1^r+\cdots+a_nx_n^r=0

has a solution in K^n other than (0,\ldots,0).

For every integer k\geq1, every choice of positive integers r_1,\ldots,r_k, and every integer l\geq0, there is an integer \omega=\omega_K(r_1,\ldots,r_k,l) such that the following holds. If n\geq\omega and f_1,\ldots,f_k\in K[x_1,\ldots,x_n] are homogeneous polynomials of degrees r_1,\ldots,r_k, respectively, then there is an l-dimensional affine subspace M\subseteq K^n on which all of the polynomials vanish:

f_1(x)=\cdots=f_k(x)=0\qquad(x\in M).

Here an affine subspace of dimension l is a translate of an l-dimensional K-linear subspace; homogeneity of degree r means that every nonzero monomial has total degree r.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory algebra and linear algebra suffice for polynomial coefficients in a field and finite-dimensional subspaces over that field. Homogeneous degree and affine translates are locally defined small extensions; no p-adic fields, field extensions or algebraic-geometric machinery are needed to understand this general conditional statement.
