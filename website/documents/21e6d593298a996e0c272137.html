Statement

Let X be a complex Banach space, let X^* and X^{**} be its continuous dual and bidual, and let J:X\to X^{**} be the canonical embedding J(x)(\varphi)=\varphi(x). Write B_X,B_{X^{**}} for the closed unit balls. If X is not reflexive, meaning J(X)\ne X^{**}, then J(B_X) is not norm dense in B_{X^{**}}.

Proof 1: Use the closed canonical subspace (outline)

The image of X in its bidual X^{**} is always norm closed, being the continuous image of a complete metric space under an isometry. Thus J(B_X) cannot be norm dense in B_{X^{**}} if X is not reflexive.

The source leaves the unit-ball scaling implication implicit.

Level justification

Use the isometric canonical map into the bidual, its closed range and scaling. Continuous duals, biduals and reflexivity require graduate functional analysis even though the metric closedness argument is elementary.
