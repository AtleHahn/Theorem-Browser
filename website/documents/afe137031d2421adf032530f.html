Statement

Let G be a finite simple undirected graph (edges join distinct vertices with at most one edge per pair). A Hamiltonian path visits every vertex once, and a Hamiltonian cycle returns to its start after visiting every vertex once. If every vertex has degree three and G has a Hamiltonian cycle, then it has a different Hamiltonian cycle.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Graph vertices, edges and degrees are locally explained; proofs use finite counting, parity and induction.

Proof 1: A cubic Hamiltonian graph has a second Hamiltonian cycle — outline

The source applies Smith’s theorem: a cubic graph has an even number of Hamiltonian cycles through any fixed edge. Choose an edge of the given cycle. At least one such cycle exists, so evenness forces at least two.
