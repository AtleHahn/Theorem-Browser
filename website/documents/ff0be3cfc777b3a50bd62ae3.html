Every finite-rank smooth vector bundle on real or complex affine space, viewed as a smooth manifold with its usual structure, is smoothly trivial.

Level

Level justification

Proof 1: Smooth vector bundles on affine space are trivial — proof

Affine space is contractible. The triviality theorem for smooth vector bundles over a contractible paracompact smooth base therefore gives smooth triviality.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply bundle triviality over a contractible paracompact base. Smooth bundles, local trivializations and their global classification are not a small extension of undergraduate vector spaces; the elementary contraction of affine space does not lower that burden.
