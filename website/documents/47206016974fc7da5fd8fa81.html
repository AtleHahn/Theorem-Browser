Statement

Assume the Boolean prime ideal theorem: every nontrivial Boolean algebra has a prime ideal. The category \mathrm{DLat}_{01} of bounded distributive lattices and homomorphisms preserving finite meets, finite joins, 0 and 1 is dual to \mathrm{CohSp}, the category of coherent (spectral) spaces and coherent maps.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves bounded distributive lattices; coherent spaces and maps; categorical duality; Boolean prime ideal theorem. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compose the stated categorical correspondences

The category CohSp of coherent spaces (and coherent maps) is equivalent to the category CohLoc of coherent (or spectral) locales (and coherent maps), on the assumption of the Boolean prime ideal theorem. The significance of this result stems from the fact that CohLoc in turn is dual to the category \mathrm{DLat}_{01} of bounded distributive lattices. Hence, \mathrm{DLat}_{01} is dual to CohSp—one obtains Stone's representation theorem for distributive lattices.
