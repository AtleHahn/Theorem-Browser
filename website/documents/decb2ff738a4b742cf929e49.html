Statement

Let \zeta(s) be the meromorphic continuation to \mathbb C of \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}, initially defined for \operatorname{Re}s>1. For T>0, let N(T) count the zeros \rho of \zeta with 0<\operatorname{Im}\rho\leq T, counted with multiplicity. Put

E(T)=N(T)-\left(\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi}-\frac{T}{2\pi}\right),

where \log is the natural logarithm.

Assume the Riemann hypothesis: every zero of \zeta other than its zeros at the negative even integers has real part 1/2. Then there exist constants C>0 and T_0>e such that

|E(T)|\leq C\frac{\log T}{\log\log T}\qquad(T\geq T_0).

Level justification
