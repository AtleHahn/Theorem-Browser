Statement

Let m,n be positive integers, A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n} and b\in\mathbb R^m. Inequalities between vectors are componentwise. If y\in\mathbb R^m satisfies y\ge0, y^TA=0 and y^Tb=-1, then there is no x\in\mathbb R^n with Ax\le b.

Proof 1: A nonnegative combination certifies linear infeasibility — source argument

Note that y^TA is a combination of the left-hand sides, y^Tb a combination of the right-hand sides of the inequalities Ax\leq b. Since the nonnegative combination produces a zero vector on the left and a -1 on the right, the contradiction 0\leq-1 is apparent.
