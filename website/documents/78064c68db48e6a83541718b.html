Statement

Let K\subset\mathbb R^3 be a smooth embedded closed curve, with arc-length parametrization \gamma and curvature \kappa(s)=\|\gamma^{\prime\prime}(s)\|. If \int_K\kappa(s)\,ds\le4\pi, then K is an unknot: it is ambient isotopic to a round circle. Ambient isotopy means a continuous family of homeomorphisms of \mathbb R^3, starting with the identity, whose final member carries K to the circle. The same conclusion holds for an embedded closed polygonal curve if the sum of its exterior turning angles is at most 4\pi; at each vertex the turning angle is the angle in [0,\pi] between the incoming and outgoing directed edges.
