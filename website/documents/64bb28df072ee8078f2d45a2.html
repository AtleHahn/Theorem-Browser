Proof 2: Use the partial derivatives of addition

Use the two-input chain rule, evaluated at x=a.

If the function f is addition, that is, if

{\displaystyle f(u,v)=u+v,}

then {\textstyle D_{1}f={\frac {\partial f}{\partial u}}=1} and {\textstyle D_{2}f={\frac {\partial f}{\partial v}}=1}. Thus, the chain rule gives

{\displaystyle {\frac {d}{dx}}(g(x)+h(x))=\left({\frac {d}{dx}}g(x)\right)D_{1}f+\left({\frac {d}{dx}}h(x)\right)D_{2}f={\frac {d}{dx}}g(x)+{\frac {d}{dx}}h(x).}
