Statement

Fix an admissible numbering \varphi_e of partial computable functions \mathbb N\rightharpoonup\mathbb N. There is a total computable map U:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N such that every \varphi_{U(k)} is total and zero except at finitely many inputs, and every such function occurs in the list.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Admissible numbering requires effective universal indexing and parameterization, not just a set-theoretic list. Those requirements with total index compilation leave the preparation burden uncertain. The cumulative prerequisite preparation cannot confidently fit approximately 30 minutes beyond the stipulated undergraduate baseline. Under the conservative threshold rule, the classification is therefore assessed as Beyond undergraduate level.
