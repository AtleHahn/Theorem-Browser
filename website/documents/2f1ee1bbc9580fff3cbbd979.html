Statement

Let T be a distribution on an open set \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n, and let \rho_\varepsilon be a smooth radial approximate identity supported in the ball of radius \varepsilon. Suppose that on every relatively compact open U\Subset\Omega, the function x\mapsto\langle T,\rho_\varepsilon(x-\cdot)\rangle is independent of all sufficiently small \varepsilon>0. Then T is induced by a smooth function.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement compares local mollifications of a distribution and its smooth representative.

Proof 1: Testing against the fixed mollification

Fix U\Subset\Omega and choose \varepsilon_0>0 small enough that the assumed independence holds and all mollifications are defined on U. Fix \varepsilon_1\in(0,\varepsilon_0).

Now let {\displaystyle \varphi \in {\mathcal {D}}\left(U\right)}. Then, by the usual trick when convolving distributions with test functions,

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\int _{U}\left\langle T,\rho _{\varepsilon }(x-\cdot )\right\rangle \varphi (x)\mathrm {d} x&=\left\langle T,\int _{U}\rho _{\varepsilon }(x-\cdot )\varphi (x)\mathrm {d} x\right\rangle \\&=\left\langle T,\rho _{\varepsilon }*\varphi \right\rangle \end{aligned}}}

and so for {\displaystyle \varepsilon \in \left(0,\varepsilon _{1}\right)} we have

{\displaystyle \left\langle T,\rho _{\varepsilon }*\varphi \right\rangle =\int _{U}\left\langle T,\rho _{\varepsilon _{1}}(x-\cdot )\right\rangle \varphi (x)\mathrm {d} x}.

Hence, as {\displaystyle \rho _{\varepsilon }*\varphi \rightarrow \varphi } in {\displaystyle {\mathcal {D}}(\Omega )} as {\displaystyle \varepsilon \searrow 0}, we get

{\displaystyle \langle T,\varphi \rangle =\int _{U}\left\langle T,\rho _{\varepsilon _{1}}(x-\cdot )\right\rangle \varphi (x)\mathrm {d} x}.

Consequently {\displaystyle \left.T\right|_{U}\in \mathrm {C} ^{\infty }\left(U\right)}, and since {\displaystyle U} was arbitrary, we are done.
