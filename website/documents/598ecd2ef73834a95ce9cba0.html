Statement

Let X be a smooth variety over a field, and let q\geq0 be an integer. Let \operatorname{CH}^q(X) be the Chow group of codimension-q algebraic cycles modulo rational equivalence. Let \mathcal K_q be the Zariski sheaf associated to the presheaf U\mapsto K_q(U), where U ranges over Zariski open subsets of X and K_q denotes algebraic K-theory. There is an isomorphism

\operatorname{CH}^q(X)\cong H^q(X,\mathcal K_q),

where the right side is Zariski sheaf cohomology.
