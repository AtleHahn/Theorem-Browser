Statement

The groups A_5 and \operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_4) are isomorphic, where \mathbb F_4=\mathbb F_2[t]/(t^2+t+1) and \mathbb F_2=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z.

Here A_n is the group of even permutations of \{1,\ldots,n\} under composition. For a field F, \operatorname{SL}_2(F) is the group of 2\times2 matrices of determinant 1, and

\operatorname{PSL}_2(F)=\operatorname{SL}_2(F)/\{aI:a\in F,\ a^2=1\}.

Here I is the 2\times2 identity matrix. An isomorphism is a bijection preserving group multiplication.
