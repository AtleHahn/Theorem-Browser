Statement

A genus-g handlebody is a compact three-manifold homeomorphic to a regular neighborhood of a bouquet of g circles; a Heegaard splitting is a decomposition into two such handlebodies along their common boundary. Let V,W be oriented handlebodies of the same genus and f:\partial V\to\partial W an orientation-reversing homeomorphism. The quotient M=V\cup_f W is a closed oriented three-manifold with Heegaard surface the identified boundaries.

Proof 1: Gluing two handlebodies into an oriented three-manifold — outline

Let V and W be oriented handlebodies of genus g, and let f be an orientation reversing homeomorphism from the boundary of V to the boundary of W. By gluing V to W along f we obtain the compact oriented three-manifold

M=V\cup_f W.

The decomposition of M into two handlebodies is called a Heegaard splitting, and their common boundary H is called the Heegaard surface of the splitting.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Glue oriented handlebodies by reversing boundary orientation and use collar neighborhoods to recognize a closed oriented manifold. Three-manifolds, boundary orientation and Heegaard gluing require graduate geometric topology.
