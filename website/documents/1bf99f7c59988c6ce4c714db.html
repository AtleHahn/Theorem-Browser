Statement

Let k be a field and A a normal integral domain finitely generated as a k-algebra. If k\subseteq L\subseteq\operatorname{Frac}(A) is a field with \operatorname{trdeg}_kL\le2, then L\cap A is finitely generated as a k-algebra. Normal means integrally closed in the fraction field: an element of \operatorname{Frac}(A) satisfying a monic polynomial with coefficients in A belongs to A. Finite generation as a k-algebra means every element is a polynomial over k in some fixed finite list of elements. Transcendence degree is the maximum size of an algebraically independent subset, where independence means no nonzero polynomial over k vanishes on any finite tuple of distinct members.
