Likely alternative prerequisite level: High school only; lowest recorded proof level: Undergraduate level. For a rational unit-circle point other than (-1,0), set t=-y/(1+x). Substitution in x^2+y^2=1 gives x=(1-t^2)/(1+t^2), y=-2t/(1+t^2). Write t=d/c in lowest terms; the exceptional point is c=0,d=1. Conversely the displayed formulas satisfy the circle equation by expansion for every nonzero integer pair. School coordinate algebra replaces Hilbert 90.

Statement

The rational solutions of x^2+y^2=1 are exactly

x=\frac{c^2-d^2}{c^2+d^2},\qquad y=-\frac{2cd}{c^2+d^2},

where c,d\in\mathbb Z are not both zero.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The rational formula is understood using elementary algebra.

Proof 1: Apply the norm-one theorem in Q(i)

Let {\displaystyle L/K} be the quadratic extension {\displaystyle \mathbb {Q} (i)/\mathbb {Q} }. The Galois group is cyclic of order 2, its generator {\displaystyle \sigma } acting via conjugation:

{\displaystyle \sigma :c+di\mapsto c-di.}

An element {\displaystyle a=x+yi} in {\displaystyle \mathbb {Q} (i)} has norm {\displaystyle a\sigma (a)=x^{2}+y^{2}}. An element of norm one thus corresponds to a rational solution of the equation {\displaystyle x^{2}+y^{2}=1} or in other words, a point with rational coordinates on the unit circle. Hilbert's Theorem 90 then states that every such element a of norm one can be written as

{\displaystyle a={\frac {c-di}{c+di}}={\frac {c^{2}-d^{2}}{c^{2}+d^{2}}}-{\frac {2cd}{c^{2}+d^{2}}}i,}

where c,d are rational and not both zero; clearing a common denominator makes them integers. Here the quotient is \sigma(b)/b for b=c+di, equivalently the Hilbert–90 form applied to \sigma(b). Conversely, these quotients have norm one.

Level justification

Use the explicitly specialized norm-one theorem in Q(i), then multiply the displayed conjugate quotient and clear denominators. Introductory field algebra and complex conjugation suffice: the only norm is x squared plus y squared and the only automorphism is conjugation. General cyclic extensions or a proof of Hilbert 90 are not needed.
