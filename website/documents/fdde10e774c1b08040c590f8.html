Statement

Let X be a based finite CW complex, let \Sigma^{2}X denote its 2-fold reduced suspension, and let \widetilde K(X) denote its reduced topological complex K-theory classes. There is a natural bijection of these classes

\widetilde K(\Sigma^{2}X)\longleftrightarrow \widetilde K(X).

Level justification

These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Suspension–loop adjunction (outline)

For the infinite unitary group, the space BU is the classifying space for stable complex vector bundles. Bott periodicity states that

\Omega^{2}BU\simeq\mathbb Z\times BU.

This yields the consequence that complex K-theory is a 2-fold periodic theory.

For the stated reduced classes on based finite CW complexes, the prerequisites for this consequence are their standard representation by E=\mathbb Z\times BU, namely \widetilde K(X)=[X,E]_*, and the suspension–loop adjunction [\Sigma^2X,E]_*\cong[X,\Omega^2E]_*. Here brackets denote based homotopy classes, and based loops in E stay in its zero integer component.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof identifies K-theory classes with homotopy classes into a representing space and applies suspension–loop adjunction and Bott periodicity. Vector bundles, CW complexes, classifying spaces and generalized cohomology are substantial graduate topology.
