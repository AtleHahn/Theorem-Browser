Proof 1: Apply the interval fixed-point theorem to cosine

Brouwer's fixed-point theorem, in one dimension, states that every continuous map from [-1,1] into itself has a fixed point.

The cosine function is continuous in [-1,1] and maps it into [-1,1], and thus must have a fixed point.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Cosine is a continuous self-map of the closed interval [-1,1]. Apply only the explicitly stated one-dimensional fixed-point theorem, whose application uses introductory continuity.
