Statement

The set \mathbb Q of rational real numbers has Lebesgue measure zero.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Real analysis supplies the rational subspace metric, countability and interval covers. Measure zero here means covers by intervals of arbitrarily small total length, a limited null-set definition without general integration.

Proof 1: Countable-null-set specialization

The rational numbers form a countable set. Every countable subset of the real line is Lebesgue measurable with measure zero. Applying this measure-theoretic fact to \mathbb Q proves the claim.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification
