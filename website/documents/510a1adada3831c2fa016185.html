Statement

Let K be a field and L(X) the free Lie algebra on a set X. Give each generator degree one. Its universal enveloping algebra U(L(X)) and symmetric algebra S(L(X)), with the induced total degree, are isomorphic as graded K-vector spaces. This is a vector-space assertion, not an algebra isomorphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Free Lie algebras, universal enveloping algebras and their induced gradings introduce several substantial algebraic constructions. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply the Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem

Apply the Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem to the free Lie algebra. With every element of X assigned degree one, its graded vector-space conclusion gives the asserted comparison.
