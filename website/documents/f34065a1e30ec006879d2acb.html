Statement

For even integers u,v, u^2-v^2 is divisible by 4. For odd integers u,v, u^2-v^2 is divisible by 8.

Level

Level justification

Proof 1: Read the parity from the square-gap product — outline

The difference of two arbitrary perfect squares is calculated as follows:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}(n+k)^{2}-n^{2}&=((n+k)+n)((n+k)-n)\\[5mu]&=k(2n+k).\end{aligned}}}

Therefore, the difference of two even perfect squares is a multiple of 4 and the difference of two odd perfect squares is a multiple of 8.
