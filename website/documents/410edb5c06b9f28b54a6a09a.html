Statement

Let H be a real Hilbert space and V a linear subspace with its own norm. Suppose \|v\|_H\le C\|v\|_V for all v\in V, where C>0. Let B:H\times V\to\mathbb R be a continuous bilinear map satisfying B(v,v)\ge\alpha\|v\|_V^2 for every v\in V, where \alpha>0. Then for every continuous linear functional f on V there exists h\in H with B(h,v)=f(v) for all v\in V. A Hilbert space is a complete inner-product space; continuity of B uses the indicated norms on its two factors.
