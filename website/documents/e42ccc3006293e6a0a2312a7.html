Statement

For integer k\ge1 and complex s\ne1, \sum_{j=1}^k\zeta(s,j/k)=k^s\zeta(s). Here \zeta(s,a) is the Hurwitz zeta function, initially \sum_{r\ge0}(r+a)^{-s} for a>0, \operatorname{Re}s>1, and continued meromorphically in s; \zeta(s)=\zeta(s,1).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Set the Hurwitz parameter to 1/k

Use the Hurwitz multiplication identity \sum_{j=0}^{k-1}\zeta(s,z+j/k)=k^s\zeta(s,kz). Its special case z=1/k has arguments (j+1)/k on the left and 1 on the right. Since \zeta(s,1)=\zeta(s), reindexing gives the claimed Riemann-zeta formula.
