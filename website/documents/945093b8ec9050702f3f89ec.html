Statement

In the real Hamilton quaternion algebra \mathbb H, with standard unit i, the polynomial P(x)=x+ixi+1 has no zero in \mathbb H.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Hamilton quaternion multiplication is a concrete extension of real linear algebra by its four-basis multiplication table. Evaluating the displayed noncommutative polynomial needs no general root theory.

Proof 1: Use the explicit two-leading-term polynomial — outline

Take P(x)=x+ixi+1 as the counterexample with multiple monomials of highest degree. It has no quaternionic zero.
