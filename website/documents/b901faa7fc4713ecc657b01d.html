Independent fluctuations with uniformly bounded variances average out in probability even without identical distributions. This gives a mathematical mechanism for stable aggregate behavior, with independence and variance control identifying the conditions under which randomness supports reliable inference.

Statement

Let X_j be independent real random variables with \sup_j\operatorname{Var}(X_j)\le C<\infty. Then n^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^n(X_j-\mathbb EX_j)\to0 in probability.

Level

Proof 1: Bound the variance sum by nC

The normalized variance sum is bounded by n^{-2}\sum_{j=1}^nC=C/n\to0. The Chebyshev variance criterion therefore applies to the centered averages.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification
