Statement

Let a,b,c\in\mathbb Z and

D=-4a^3c+a^2b^2+18abc-4b^3-27c^2\ne0.

On the nonsingular cubic E:y^2=x^3+ax^2+bx+c, let P=(x,y) be an affine rational point of finite order in the elliptic-curve group. Then x,y\in\mathbb Z, and either y=0 and P has order 2, or y\ne0 and y^2 divides D (hence also y divides D). The group includes the point O at infinity as identity. For affine points its addition is the chord-and-tangent rule: the line through two points, or the tangent if they coincide, meets the cubic in a third point counted with multiplicity, and reflection (x,y)\mapsto(x,-y) of that point gives the sum. Finite order means mP=O for some positive integer m.
