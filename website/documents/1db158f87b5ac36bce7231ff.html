Statement

Let f(z,w) be holomorphic on a neighborhood of (0,0)\in\mathbb C\times\mathbb C^{n-1}, where n\geq1. Suppose f(0,0)=0 and the one-variable function z\mapsto f(z,0) is not identically zero near 0. Let k\geq1 be its order of vanishing at 0.

On a sufficiently small neighborhood of (0,0), there are holomorphic functions h(z,w) and g_0(w),\ldots,g_{k-1}(w) such that

f(z,w)=h(z,w)\left(z^k+g_{k-1}(w)z^{k-1}+\cdots+g_0(w)\right),h(0,0)\ne0,\qquad g_j(0)=0\quad(0\leq j<k).

Thus the polynomial factor is monic in the distinguished variable z, with coefficients holomorphic in the remaining variables. The order k is the smallest exponent with nonzero coefficient in the Taylor expansion of f(z,0).
