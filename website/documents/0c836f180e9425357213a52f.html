Statement

For a connected compact Riemann surface X of genus g, its canonical line bundle K has h^0(X,K)=g, where h^0 is the complex dimension of holomorphic sections.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The canonical bundle and its holomorphic sections are complex-geometric objects.

Proof 1: Apply Riemann–Roch to the trivial bundle

Use line-bundle Riemann–Roch: h^0(X,L)-h^0(X,L^{-1}\otimes K)=\deg L+1-g.

The theorem can be applied to show that there are g linearly independent holomorphic sections of K, or one-forms on X, as follows. Taking L to be the trivial bundle, {\displaystyle h^{0}(X,L)=1} since the only holomorphic functions on X are constants. The degree of L is zero, and {\displaystyle L^{-1}} is the trivial bundle. Thus,

{\displaystyle 1-h^{0}(X,K)=1-g}.

Therefore, {\displaystyle h^{0}(X,K)=g}, proving that there are g holomorphic one-forms.
