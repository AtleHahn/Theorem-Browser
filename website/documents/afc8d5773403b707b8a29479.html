Statement

If E is an elliptic curve over a finite field \mathbb F_q and N=\#E(\mathbb F_q), including the point at infinity, then |N-(q+1)|\le 2\sqrt q.

Proof 1: Triangle inequality for the two point-count roots

The source invokes the point-count formula that the difference between N and q+1 is the sum of two complex numbers, each of absolute value \sqrt q. The triangle inequality bounds the absolute value of their sum by 2\sqrt q.

Proof 2: Specialize the Hasse–Weil bound to genus one

The Hasse–Weil bound is |\#C(\mathbb F_q)-(q+1)|\le 2g\sqrt q for a smooth projective geometrically connected curve of genus g. An elliptic curve has genus g=1. Substituting g=1 gives the stated Hasse bound.

Level justification

Specialize Hasse–Weil to genus one. Smooth projective geometrically connected curves and their genus require algebraic geometry even though the numerical substitution is immediate.
