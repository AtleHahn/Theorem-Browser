Statement

Let M be a closed oriented n-manifold, let T_i be the torsion subgroup of H_i(M;\mathbb Z), and put F_i=H_i(M;\mathbb Z)/T_i. Then the free and torsion duality pairings have adjoint isomorphisms F_i\cong\operatorname{Hom}(F_{n-i},\mathbb Z),\qquad T_i\cong\operatorname{Hom}(T_{n-i-1},\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Integral homology; Torsion and free abelian groups; Perfect pairings. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Combine duality with universal coefficients

This result is an application of Poincaré duality

H_i(M;\mathbb Z)\cong H^{n-i}(M;\mathbb Z),

together with the universal coefficient theorem, which gives an identification

H^{n-i}(M;\mathbb Z)/\operatorname{torsion}H^{n-i}(M;\mathbb Z) \cong\operatorname{Hom}(H_{n-i}(M;\mathbb Z),\mathbb Z) =\operatorname{Hom}(F_{n-i},\mathbb Z)

and

\begin{aligned} \operatorname{torsion}H^{n-i}(M;\mathbb Z) &\cong\operatorname{Ext}(H_{n-i-1}(M;\mathbb Z),\mathbb Z)\\ &\cong\operatorname{Hom}(T_{n-i-1},\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z). \end{aligned}

Here the homology groups of the closed manifold are finitely generated. Composing gives the two stated adjoint isomorphisms.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Combine integral manifold duality with the Hom/Ext description of cohomology and finite generation. Homology, cohomology, Ext and torsion pairings require graduate algebraic topology.
