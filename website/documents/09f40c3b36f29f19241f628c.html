Statement

Let F/\mathbb Q be a finite abelian Galois extension and G=\operatorname{Gal}(F/\mathbb Q). Let m\ge1 be its conductor, the least positive integer for which F\subseteq\mathbb Q(\zeta_m), with \zeta_m a primitive mth root of unity. Put

\theta_F=\frac1m\sum_{\substack{1\le a\le m\\(a,m)=1}}a\,\operatorname{res}_F(\sigma_a^{-1})\in\mathbb Q[G],\qquad\sigma_a(\zeta_m)=\zeta_m^a.

For every \alpha\in\mathbb Z[G] for which \alpha\theta_F=\sum_{\sigma\in G}a_\sigma\sigma has integer coefficients, and every nonzero fractional ideal J of F, the fractional ideal

\prod_{\sigma\in G}\sigma(J)^{a_\sigma}

is principal. Equivalently, \theta_F\mathbb Z[G]\cap\mathbb Z[G] annihilates the ideal class group of F. The group rings consist of finite formal sums of group elements with the indicated coefficients, with multiplication extending composition. A fractional ideal is a nonzero finitely generated module over the ring of integers contained in F; principal means generated by one nonzero field element. The ideal class group is fractional ideals modulo principal fractional ideals, and G acts by applying field automorphisms to ideals. Restriction is denoted by \operatorname{res}_F.
