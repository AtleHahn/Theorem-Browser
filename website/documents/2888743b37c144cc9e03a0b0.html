Statement

For a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group G, write \widehat G for its continuous homomorphisms to \mathbb T=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=1\}, with pointwise multiplication and the compact-open topology. Then G is compact if and only if \widehat G is discrete, and G is discrete if and only if \widehat G is compact.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The stated objects require locally compact abelian groups, compact-open topology, pontryagin duality, beyond the calculus and finite-dimensional linear-algebra baseline.

Proof 1: Apply the forward compact-open facts to the bidual

Use the source-stated compact-open facts: the dual of a compact abelian group is discrete, and the dual of a discrete abelian group is compact. These give the forward implications. If \widehat G is discrete, applying the second fact to it makes \widehat{\widehat G} compact; Pontryagin duality identifies that group topologically with G. If \widehat G is compact, applying the first fact makes the bidual discrete, and duality again transfers this to G.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the two compact-open dual facts to the bidual and transfer the topology through Pontryagin evaluation. Locally compact groups, character spaces and compact-open duality form a substantial graduate framework.
