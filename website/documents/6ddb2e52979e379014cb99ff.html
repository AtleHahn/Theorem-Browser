Statement

Let M be a compact Kähler manifold of complex dimension n and L a positive holomorphic line bundle on M. Then H^q(M,L^{-1})=0 for 0\le q<n.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Kähler manifolds; Positive holomorphic line bundles; Sheaf cohomology. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply Serre duality

Kodaira vanishing gives H^{n-q}(M,K_M\otimes L)=0 when q<n. Serre duality identifies H^q(M,L^{-1}) with the dual of this group, giving the asserted vanishing. Here K_M is the canonical line bundle.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use Kodaira vanishing in the complementary degree and identify the desired group by Serre duality. Kahler manifolds, positive line bundles and sheaf cohomology require advanced complex geometry.
