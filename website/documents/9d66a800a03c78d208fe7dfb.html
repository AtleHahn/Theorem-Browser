Statement

For real s>-1, the plane map F_s(z)=z|z|^s for z\ne0, extended by F_s(0)=0, is an orientation-preserving homeomorphism. At every nonzero point its differential has major/minor stretch ratio \max(1+s,(1+s)^{-1}).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Plane coordinate maps; Real differentiability; Ellipses; Differential stretch ratios. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: The radial power construction — outline

Use the source radial construction F_s(z)=z|z|^s with s>-1. Its stated constant differential dilatation off the origin is \max(1+s,(1+s)^{-1}).

Level justification

The source states the radial stretch construction and its ratio without expanding the derivative calculation. Undergraduate polar coordinates, multivariable derivatives and singular stretch factors suffice; the radial and tangential directions give the relevant two stretches.
