Statement

For an integer n\ge1, real u\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)\cap W^{1,2}(\mathbb R^n) and every \rho>0, \|u\|_2^2\le \rho^{-2}\|Du\|_2^2+\omega_n\rho^n\|u\|_1^2, where \omega_n is the volume of the Euclidean unit ball and Du is the weak gradient.

Proof 1: Add the displayed Fourier tail and ball estimates

The inequality follows from basic properties of the Fourier transform. Use the unitary angular-frequency convention

\widehat u(\xi)=(2\pi)^{-n/2}\int_{\mathbb R^n}e^{-ix\cdot\xi}u(x)\,dx.

Indeed, integrating over the complement of the ball of radius \rho,

\begin{aligned} \int_{|\xi|\ge\rho}|\widehat u(\xi)|^2\,d\xi &\le \int_{|\xi|\ge\rho}\frac{|\xi|^2}{\rho^2}|\widehat u(\xi)|^2\,d\xi\\ &\le \rho^{-2}\int_{\mathbb R^n}|Du|^2\,dx, \end{aligned}

because 1\le|\xi|^2/\rho^2. On the other hand, |\widehat u|\le\|u\|_{L^1}, which, when integrated over the ball of radius \rho, gives

\int_{|\xi|\le\rho}|\widehat u(\xi)|^2\,d\xi \le \rho^n\omega_n\|u\|_{L^1}^2,

where \omega_n is the volume of the unit ball in n dimensions. Adding these two estimates and applying Parseval's theorem, \|\widehat u\|_{L^2}=\|u\|_{L^2}, gives the stated bound.
