Statement

Let G be a connected noncompact real semisimple Lie group with finite center and K a maximal compact subgroup. For K-biinvariant smooth compactly supported f, put \widetilde f(\lambda)=\int_G f(g)\varphi_{-\lambda}(g)\,dg, where \varphi_\lambda are normalized spherical functions. Suppose a spectral measure \nu and constant C satisfy Cf(e)=\int\widetilde f(\lambda)\,d\nu(\lambda) for every such f. Then Cf(x)=\int\widetilde f(\lambda)\varphi_\lambda(x)\,d\nu(\lambda)\qquad(x\in G), with the usual convergence of the given inversion integrals.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Semisimple Lie groups and maximal compact subgroups; Biinvariant functions and convolution; Haar measure; Spherical functions. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Spherical inversion away from the origin by averaging translates — source argument

For fixed x form the averaged translate f_x(g)=\int_K f(xkg)\,dk, using normalized Haar measure on K. It is again biinvariant and f_x(e)=f(x). The spherical matrix-coefficient identity gives \widetilde {f_x}(\lambda)=\widetilde f(\lambda)\varphi_\lambda(x). Applying the assumed origin identity to f_x gives the required formula at x.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Average translates over a compact subgroup and apply the spherical transform identity. Haar integration, semisimple Lie groups and spherical functions require advanced harmonic analysis.
