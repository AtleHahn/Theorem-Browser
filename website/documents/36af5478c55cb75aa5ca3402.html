Statement

Assume that every even integer at least 4 is a sum of two primes. Let C be the smallest positive integer such that every integer greater than 1 is a sum of at most C primes. Then C exists and C=3.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All hypotheses and the least-bound conclusion use only ordinary integer primes and finite sums.
