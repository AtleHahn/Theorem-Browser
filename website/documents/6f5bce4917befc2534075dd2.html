Statement

Define F_1=F_2=1 and F_{j+2}=F_{j+1}+F_j for j\ge1. For each integer N\ge1, Euclidean remainder division starting with a=F_{N+2} and b=F_{N+1} takes exactly N divisions, counting the final zero-producing division.
