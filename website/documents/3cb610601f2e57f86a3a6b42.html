Statement

Let X\in\mathbb R^{n\times K} be deterministic with full column rank, and let y=X\beta+\varepsilon, where \beta\in\mathbb R^K is unknown and deterministic, E\varepsilon=0 and \operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon)=\sigma^2 I_n with 0\le\sigma^2<\infty. Put B=(X^TX)^{-1}X^T and \widehat\beta=By. If C=B+D and DX=0, then \operatorname{Cov}(Cy)-\operatorname{Cov}(By)=\sigma^2DD^T.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Finite-vector covariance is the matrix of centered second moments; together with expectation it is a limited extension of undergraduate integration and matrix algebra.

Proof 1: Cancel the cross terms using unbiasedness

Put \tilde\beta=Cy; for a random vector, \operatorname{Var} below denotes its covariance matrix. Since \operatorname{Cov}(y)=\sigma^2 I_n, we have

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\operatorname {Var} \left({\tilde {\beta }}\right)&=\operatorname {Var} (Cy)\\&=C{\text{ Var}}(y)C^{\operatorname {T} }\\&=\sigma ^{2}CC^{\operatorname {T} }\\&=\sigma ^{2}\left((X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}X^{\operatorname {T} }+D\right)\left(X(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}+D^{\operatorname {T} }\right)\\&=\sigma ^{2}\left((X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}X^{\operatorname {T} }X(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}+(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}X^{\operatorname {T} }D^{\operatorname {T} }+DX(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}+DD^{\operatorname {T} }\right)\\&=\sigma ^{2}(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}+\sigma ^{2}(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}(DX)^{\operatorname {T} }+\sigma ^{2}DX(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}+\sigma ^{2}DD^{\operatorname {T} }\\&=\sigma ^{2}(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}+\sigma ^{2}DD^{\operatorname {T} }&&DX=0\\&=\operatorname {Var} \left({\widehat {\beta }}\right)+\sigma ^{2}DD^{\operatorname {T} }&&\sigma ^{2}(X^{\operatorname {T} }X)^{-1}=\operatorname {Var} \left({\widehat {\beta }}\right)\end{aligned}}}

Level justification

Transform the covariance by deterministic matrices, expand CC transpose and cancel the cross terms using DX=0. Undergraduate linear algebra and elementary random-vector covariance suffice.
