Statement

Let P\in\mathbb R[x] be nonconstant. Form the nonzero Sturm sequence P_0=P, P_1=P' and P_{j+1}=-\operatorname{rem}(P_{j-1},P_j), stopping before the first zero remainder. For real t, let V(t) be the number of sign changes in (P_0(t),P_1(t),\ldots) after zero terms are omitted.

If a<b and neither endpoint is a multiple root of P, then the number of distinct real roots of P in (a,b] is V(a)-V(b). In particular, for square-free P no restriction on finite endpoints is needed. One may also take a=-\infty or b=+\infty: evaluate signs there by each polynomial's leading term, and count roots in the corresponding unbounded interval.
