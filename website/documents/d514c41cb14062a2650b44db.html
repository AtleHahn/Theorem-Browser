Statement

Let a connected closed orientable surface M of genus g have a Riemannian metric of constant Gaussian curvature K. Then K has the sign of 2-2g: it is positive for g=0, zero for g=1, and negative for g>1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses global surface topology and Riemannian curvature.

Proof 1: Gauss–Bonnet and positive area

The Gauss–Bonnet theorem implies that for a closed surface with constant curvature, the sign of that curvature must match the sign of the Euler characteristic. The Euler characteristic is equal to 2-2g, where g is the genus of the 2-manifold, i.e. the number of “holes”.

Explicitly, Gauss–Bonnet gives \int_M K\,dA=2\pi\chi(M), so for constant K,

K\operatorname{Area}(M)=2\pi(2-2g).

The area is finite and strictly positive.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Gauss-Bonnet turns total curvature into 2pi times Euler characteristic, then positive Riemannian area determines the sign. The intrinsic curvature and metric-surface setting remains advanced geometry.
