Statement

For square matrices A,B of the same size over \mathbb R or \mathbb C and an integer n\ge1, A^n-B^n=\sum_{k=1}^n A^{k-1}(A-B)B^{n-k}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Real or complex matrices; Matrix products and powers; Finite sums. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Insert the inductive difference between two outer factors

Proof via induction: For {\displaystyle n=1}, the statement is true. Assuming the equation holds for an {\displaystyle n\geq 1}, then the induction step is

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\mathbf {A} ^{n+1}-\mathbf {B} ^{n+1}&=\mathbf {A} \mathbf {A} ^{n}-\mathbf {A} \mathbf {B} ^{n}+\mathbf {A} \mathbf {B} ^{n}-\mathbf {B} \mathbf {B} ^{n}\\[1ex]&=\mathbf {A} (\mathbf {A} ^{n}-\mathbf {B} ^{n})+(\mathbf {A} -\mathbf {B} )\mathbf {B} ^{n}\\[1ex]&=\mathbf {A} \sum _{k=1}^{n}\mathbf {A} ^{k-1}(\mathbf {A} -\mathbf {B} )\mathbf {B} ^{n-k}+(\mathbf {A} -\mathbf {B} )\mathbf {B} ^{n}\\[1ex]&=\sum _{k=0}^{n}\mathbf {A} ^{k}(\mathbf {A} -\mathbf {B} )\mathbf {B} ^{n-k}\\[1ex]&=\sum _{k=1}^{n+1}\mathbf {A} ^{k-1}(\mathbf {A} -\mathbf {B} )\mathbf {B} ^{(n+1)-k}.\end{aligned}}}

That is, the statement holds true for {\displaystyle n+1} and the result follows by the principle of induction.
