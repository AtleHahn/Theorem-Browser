Statement

Let \tau be a finite relational first-order vocabulary, and use effectively coded sentences and finite nonempty \tau-structures. Assume \tau contains a binary relation symbol. There is no proof calculus with an effective enumeration of its theorems that is both sound and complete for truth in all finite nonempty \tau-structures.

Proof 1: Completeness would enumerate all finite validities

Use the theorem that finite validity for this vocabulary is not recursively enumerable. An effective sound and complete calculus would enumerate exactly those finitely valid sentences, contradicting that theorem.
