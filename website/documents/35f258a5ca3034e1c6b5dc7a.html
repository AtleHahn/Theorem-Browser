Statement

For x,y,z\in\{0,1\}, let juxtaposition mean AND, a\vee b mean OR (one if at least one input is one), and \bar a=1-a mean NOT. Then xy\vee\bar xz\vee yz=xy\vee\bar xz.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All operations on the two truth values are defined locally; only elementary finite logic is needed.

Proof 2: Distribution and absorption

\begin{aligned} xy\vee\bar xz\vee yz &=xy\vee\bar xz\vee(x\vee\bar x)yz\\ &=xy\vee\bar xz\vee xyz\vee\bar xyz\\ &=(xy\vee xyz)\vee(\bar xz\vee\bar xyz)\\ &=xy(1\vee z)\vee\bar xz(1\vee y)\\ &=xy\vee\bar xz. \end{aligned}
Level
High school only
Level justification

Insert x OR NOT x=1, distribute, and absorb the repeated terms. This is finite Boolean arithmetic with the elementary laws.
