Statement

For a completely positive map \Phi:A\to B(H) on a unital C-star algebra A and complex Hilbert space H, a Stinespring representation is a triple (\pi,V,K) with K a complex Hilbert space, \pi:A\to B(K) a unital star homomorphism, V:H\to K bounded, and \Phi(a)=V^*\pi(a)V. Given two representations (\pi_j,V_j,K_j) of the same \Phi, set L_j=\overline{\operatorname{span}\pi_j(A)V_jH} for j=1,2. There is a partial isometry W:K_1\to K_2 with initial space L_1 and final space L_2 satisfying W\pi_1(a)V_1h=\pi_2(a)V_2h for all a\in A,h\in H. In particular, WV_1=V_2 and W\pi_1(a)=\pi_2(a)W on V_1H.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Stinespring representations defined locally; Closed generated Hilbert subspaces; Partial isometries; Intertwining relations. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Map the common generated vectors — outline

Define W on the generating vectors by W\pi_1(a)V_1h=\pi_2(a)V_2h. This gives the partial isometry between the generated subspaces. On V_1H the formula gives the intertwining relation W\pi_1(a)=\pi_2(a)W.
