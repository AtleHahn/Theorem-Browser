Statement

For real \phi put R(\phi)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\phi&-\sin\phi\\\sin\phi&\cos\phi\end{pmatrix}. Then R(\phi)^n=R(n\phi) for every integer n.

Proof 1: Transfer De Moivre through the real matrix representation

Under the multiplicative identification a+ib\mapsto\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\b&a\end{pmatrix}, the complex number \cos\phi+i\sin\phi corresponds to R(\phi). Apply De Moivre’s formula through this identification.
