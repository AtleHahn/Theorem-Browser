Statement

Let G be a finite directed graph with at least one edge, whose underlying undirected graph is connected. Then G has a directed closed trail using every edge exactly once if and only if the indegree equals the outdegree at every vertex. Indegree counts entering edges and outdegree counts departing edges; a closed trail starts and ends at the same vertex and repeats no edge.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The objects are finite vertices and directed edges, with elementary counting and a precisely defined traversal. No advanced graph theory is needed to understand the claim.
