Statement

For integers n\geq1, let f_n(x)=x^n on [0,1]. Each f_n is continuous and f_{n+1}(x)\leq f_n(x). The pointwise limit is f(x)=0 for 0\leq x<1 and f(1)=1, which is discontinuous. Consequently this convergence is not uniform, and continuity of the limit in Dini’s theorem cannot be omitted.

Consider the functions x^n on [0,1]. This sequence shows that continuity of the pointwise limit cannot be inferred from the other hypotheses. A uniform limit of continuous functions must be continuous, so this example also cannot converge uniformly.
