Statement

Let E be a finite set with n\ge0 elements and let \mathcal S\subseteq\mathcal P(E) be an antichain: no distinct members contain one another. Then

|\mathcal S|\le\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}.

Equality holds if and only if \mathcal S is the family of all \lfloor n/2\rfloor-element subsets of E, or the family of all \lceil n/2\rceil-element subsets of E.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The family condition and bound use elementary finite sets and counting.
