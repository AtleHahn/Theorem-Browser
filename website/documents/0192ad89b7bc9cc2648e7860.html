Statement

Let n\ge2, let u\in C_c^1(\mathbb R^n) be nonnegative, and let t>0 be a regular value such that E=\{u>t\} is nonempty. Let B be the ball centered at 0 with |B|=|E|, where bars denote Lebesgue volume. Then \mathcal H^{n-1}(\{u=t\})\ge\mathcal H^{n-1}(\partial B).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Surface-area comparison for a regular superlevel set and its ball — proof

The ball and the superlevel set have the same volume. The classical isoperimetric inequality therefore gives that the boundary area of E is at least that of B. At this regular level the boundary of E is \{u=t\}, giving the comparison.

Level justification

Apply isoperimetry to the regular superlevel domain and identify its boundary with the level set. The full general-n Hausdorff boundary measure and Lebesgue volume setting requires geometric measure and surface theory beyond elementary volume geometry.
