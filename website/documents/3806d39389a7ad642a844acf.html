Statement

Let Y be a closed subvariety of positive codimension k in a smooth variety X, with ideal sheaf \mathcal I_Y and cycle class [Y] in the rational Chow ring of X. Then

c_k(\mathcal I_Y)=(-1)^k(k-1)![Y].
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Ideal sheaves, Chern classes and rational Chow cycle classes require substantial algebraic geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Leading Chern class of an ideal sheaf — outline

The source uses the exact sequence 0\to\mathcal I_Y\to\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal O_Y\to0 and the resulting identity 1=c(\mathcal O_X)=c(\mathcal O_Y)c(\mathcal I_Y). The supplied leading-class formula is c_k(\mathcal O_Y)=(-1)^{k-1}(k-1)![Y]. Since the lower positive Chern classes of a sheaf supported in codimension k vanish, the degree-k part of the product identity gives c_k(\mathcal I_Y)=-c_k(\mathcal O_Y), as claimed.

Level justification

Take degree k in total Chern-class multiplicativity after lower supported-sheaf classes vanish. Ideal sheaves, rational Chow classes and coherent-sheaf Chern classes require graduate algebraic geometry.
