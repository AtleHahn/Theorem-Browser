Statement

Let E be a holomorphic vector bundle of rank r on \mathbb{CP}^1, the complex projective line of one-dimensional linear subspaces of \mathbb C^2. A holomorphic vector bundle is locally a product with \mathbb C^r, with holomorphic invertible linear transition maps on fibers. There exist integers a_1,\ldots,a_r such that E\cong\bigoplus_{j=1}^r\mathcal O(a_j) holomorphically, and their multiset is uniquely determined by E. Here \mathcal O(-1) is the tautological line bundle, whose fiber over a line is that line; \mathcal O(1) is its dual, and \mathcal O(a)=\mathcal O(1)^{\otimes a} for a\ge0, using dual powers for negative a and the trivial bundle for a=0.
