Statement

Let H be any infinite-dimensional real Hilbert space. There is no countably additive Borel probability measure \mu on H, with its norm topology, such that for every finite orthonormal family e_1,\ldots,e_m\in H the coordinate map

x\longmapsto(\langle x,e_1\rangle,\ldots,\langle x,e_m\rangle)

has, under \mu, the standard Gaussian distribution on \mathbb R^m, whose density with respect to Lebesgue measure is

(2\pi)^{-m/2}\exp\!\left(-\tfrac12\sum_{j=1}^m y_j^2\right).

Thus the canonical Gaussian cylindrical probability, specified by these finite orthogonal coordinate distributions, has no Borel probability extension on H. This holds without a separability assumption and rules out any Borel probability extension, not only a Radon extension.

Level justification
