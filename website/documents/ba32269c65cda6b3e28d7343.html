Statement

Let A,B,C,D be the successive vertices of a planar quadrilateral, possibly concave or self-intersecting, with nonzero side lengths. On its directed sides AB,BC,CD,DA, construct four squares, all to the right of their directed sides or all to the left. Let their centers in this order be P,Q,R,S. Then

|R-P|=|S-Q|,\qquad (R-P)\cdot(S-Q)=0.

Thus when the two opposite-center segments are nonzero, they have equal lengths and perpendicular supporting lines. Here |\cdot| and \cdot denote Euclidean length and dot product. For a convex quadrilateral this includes both the external and the internal square constructions.

High school only
