Statement

Let n\ge1, and let G_1\subseteq G_2\subseteq\cdots be domains of holomorphy in \mathbb C^n. Then \bigcup_{k\ge1}G_k is a domain of holomorphy.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Domains of holomorphy and their geometric characterization require several complex variables.

Proof 1: Pseudoconvexity and the Levi problem — outline

This is related to the fact that an increasing union of pseudoconvex domains is pseudoconvex and so it can be proven using that fact and the solution of the Levi problem.

Here the solution of the Levi problem gives the characterization: a domain in \mathbb C^n is a domain of holomorphy if and only if it is pseudoconvex.

Level

Level justification

The argument translates through pseudoconvexity using the solution of the Levi problem. Understanding domains of holomorphy in several complex variables and the meaning of pseudoconvexity requires substantial complex-analysis preparation. The final composition of two implications does not make those objects a short extension of the undergraduate baseline.
