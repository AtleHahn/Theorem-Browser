Statement

Let G be a finite group. Every virtual complex character \chi of G can be written as a finite sum

\chi=\sum_{j=1}^{s}n_j\operatorname{Ind}_{H_j}^{G}\lambda_j,

where n_j\in\mathbb Z, each H_j\leq G is an elementary subgroup, and each \lambda_j:H_j\to\mathbb C^\times is a one-dimensional character. Thus these induced characters generate the character ring of G as an abelian group.

A virtual character is an integer linear combination of the characters of irreducible finite-dimensional complex representations; a character is the trace of a representation. An elementary subgroup is a direct product of a cyclic group and a group of prime-power order, with the prime allowed to depend on the subgroup. For a character \lambda of H\leq G, induction is given by

(\operatorname{Ind}_{H}^{G}\lambda)(g)=\frac{1}{|H|}\sum_{\substack{t\in G\\t^{-1}gt\in H}}\lambda(t^{-1}gt).

Level justification

Although induction has a finite-sum formula, interpreting irreducible representations and the character ring together with subgroup induction requires multiple representation-theoretic concepts beyond the introductory algebra baseline.
