Statement

Let G be a finite group with generating set S. Form its colored directed Cayley graph on vertex set G, with a directed edge g\to gs colored by s for every g\in G and s\in S. Its automorphisms that preserve edge colors and orientations form a group isomorphic to G.

Within undergraduate level

Proof 1: Colored directed Cayley graphs realize finite groups — outline

The main idea of the proof is to observe that the Cayley graph of G, with the addition of colors and orientations on its edges to distinguish the generators of G from each other, has the desired automorphism group.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Left translations preserve every directed generator edge. Conversely an automorphism is determined by its value at the identity, since edge colors propagate its values along generator words. Basic finite groups and Cayley graphs suffice.
