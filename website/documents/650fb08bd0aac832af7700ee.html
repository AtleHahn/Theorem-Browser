Statement

For 0<s<1 and 1\le p<\infty, there is C=C(n,s,p) such that for every ball B_R(a)\subset\mathbb R^n with R>0 and u\in W^{s,p}(B_R(a)), \|u-u_{B_R(a)}\|_{L^p(B_R(a))}\le CR^s[u]_{W^{s,p}(B_R(a))}, where u_B=|B|^{-1}\int_Bu and [u]_{W^{s,p}(B)}^p=\int_B\int_B|u(x)-u(y)|^p/|x-y|^{n+sp}\,dx\,dy.

Proof 1: Dilate to a unit ball — outline

Apply the unit-ball fractional Poincare inequality to the dilated function v(z)=u(a+Rz). Changing variables in the norm and in the fractional difference seminorm gives \|u-u_{B_R(a)}\|_p=R^{n/p}\|v-v_{B_1}\|_p and [u]_{W^{s,p}(B_R(a))}=R^{n/p-s}[v]_{W^{s,p}(B_1)}, leaving the factor R^s in the original-ball inequality. This is the source’s dilation recipe; the change-of-variables details are implicit.
