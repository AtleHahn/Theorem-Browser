Statement

Let G be a group, and let M,N be nilpotent normal subgroups of G, of nilpotency classes m,n, respectively. Then

MN=\{ab:a\in M,\ b\in N\}

is a nilpotent normal subgroup of G, of nilpotency class at most m+n.

For a group H, define H_0=H and H_{j+1}=[H_j,H], where [A,B] is the subgroup generated by the commutators a^{-1}b^{-1}ab with a\in A and b\in B. The group H is nilpotent if H_r=\{1\} for some nonnegative integer r; its nilpotency class is the least such r.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies normal and generated subgroups. Iterating the explicitly defined commutator subgroup is a small extension; abelian groups give an immediate class-one example. No finite-group classification or Lie theory is required.
