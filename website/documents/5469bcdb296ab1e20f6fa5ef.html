Statement

Let A be an open arc of the unit circle and U a neighborhood of A. Suppose f is holomorphic on U\cap\{|z|>1\} and g on U\cap\{|z|<1\}. Assume their radial boundary limits on A exist locally in the same Sobolev space H^s, s\in\mathbb R, and agree. Then f and g extend to a common holomorphic function on a neighborhood of A.

Proof 1: Holomorphic gluing from common Sobolev boundary values — outline

Let {\displaystyle f} and {\displaystyle g} be holomorphic functions defined exterior and interior to some arc on the unit circle such that locally they have radial limits in the same Sobolev space and these limits agree. Then, letting

{\displaystyle D=z{\partial \over \partial z},}

the equations

{\displaystyle D^{k}F=f,\,\,\,D^{k}G=g}

can be solved locally in such a way that the radial limits of G and F tend locally to the same function in a higher Sobolev space. For k large enough, this convergence is uniform by the Sobolev embedding theorem. By continuous-boundary holomorphic gluing (Morera’s theorem), F and G therefore patch to give a holomorphic function near the arc and hence so do f and g.

Level justification

Lift matching boundary values through local primitives to higher Sobolev regularity, embed into continuous functions and glue holomorphically. Sobolev spaces of arbitrary order and boundary regularity require advanced analysis.
