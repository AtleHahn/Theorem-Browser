Statement

Let (X,\mathcal T) be a locally convex Hausdorff topological vector space over \mathbb R or \mathbb C, and let X' be its continuous linear dual. Then \mathcal T is the topology generated by the seminorms

p_A(x)=\sup_{f\in A}|f(x)|,

where A ranges over all nonempty equicontinuous subsets of X'. Here equicontinuity means that for every \epsilon>0 there is a neighborhood U of zero in X such that |f(x)|<\epsilon for all f\in A and x\in U. The generated topology has a zero-neighborhood base given by finite intersections of sets \{x:p_A(x)<\epsilon\}. Locally convex means having a base of convex zero-neighborhoods.
