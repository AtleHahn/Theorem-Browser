Statement

Let \gamma_1,\gamma_2:I\to\mathbb R^3 be C^3 curves on the same interval I, parametrized by arc length, so \|\gamma_i^{\prime}(s)\|=1. Suppose their curvature functions \kappa_i(s)=\|\gamma_i^{\prime\prime}(s)\| are everywhere positive. Define T_i=\gamma_i^{\prime}, N_i=T_i^{\prime}/\|T_i^{\prime}\|, B_i=T_i\times N_i and torsion \tau_i=-B_i^{\prime}\cdot N_i. If \kappa_1(s)=\kappa_2(s) and \tau_1(s)=\tau_2(s) for every s\in I, then the curves are congruent: there is a Euclidean rigid motion R:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3 with \gamma_2(s)=R(\gamma_1(s)) for every s\in I.
