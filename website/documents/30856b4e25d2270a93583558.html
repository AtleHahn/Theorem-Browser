Statement

Let G be a connected compact Hausdorff abelian group with Haar measure m, and let A,B be nonempty measurable subsets. Write m_* for inner Haar measure and A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,b\in B\}. Then m_*(A+B)\ge\min\{m(A)+m(B),m(G)\}.

Level justification

Connected topological groups and Haar inner measure require topology and measure theory beyond the calculus baseline.

Proof 1: Use absence of proper open subgroups

Kneser's theorem states that if A and B are nonempty m-measurable subsets of G satisfying m_*(A+B)<m(A)+m(B), then the stabilizer

H:=\{g\in G:g+(A+B)=A+B\}

is compact and open.

Because connected groups have no proper open subgroups, this immediately implies that if G is connected, then

m_*(A+B)\geq\min\{m(A)+m(B),m(G)\}.

Indeed, in the strict-inequality case H=G, and the nonempty sumset is all of G.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Kneser to Haar inner measure and an open stabilizer, then connectedness forces the stabilizer to be all of G. Haar measure and measurable sumsets in compact groups are advanced harmonic analysis.
