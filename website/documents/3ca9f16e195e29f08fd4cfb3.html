Statement

Let H be a complex inner-product space, with inner product B linear in its first argument and conjugate-linear in its second, and norm \|x\|=\sqrt{B(x,x)}. Its norm completion \widehat H carries the continuous extension of B as an inner product and is a Hilbert space.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Extend the inner product to the norm completion — outline

The sesquilinear form B:H\times H\to\mathbb C is separately uniformly continuous in each of its two arguments and can be extended to a continuous sesquilinear form on the completion of H. Since H is an inner-product space, this completion is a Hilbert space.

The source prescribes this extension and states the conclusion; the bounded-form extension and the positivity and induced-norm checks are left implicit.
