Statement

Let M be a smooth state manifold, let U,x_1,\ldots,x_m,X_1,\ldots,X_m be smooth real functions, and put \omega=dU-\sum_{i=1}^m X_i\,dx_i. Suppose \omega is nowhere zero and the adiabatic surfaces form a smooth regular codimension-one foliation tangent to \ker\omega. Then locally there exist smooth functions T,S with T\ne0, dS\ne0 and

dU=\sum_{i=1}^m X_i\,dx_i+T\,dS.
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves smooth manifolds; differential forms; regular foliations. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Apply the local integrating-factor theorem to the heat form

Now, define the one-form on the state space

{\displaystyle \omega :=dU-\sum _{i}X_{i}dx_{i}}

Now, since the adiabatic surfaces are tangent to {\displaystyle \omega } at every point in state space, {\displaystyle \omega } is integrable, so by Carathéodory's theorem, there exist locally two smooth scalar functions {\displaystyle T,S} on state space, such that {\displaystyle \omega =TdS}. Here Carathéodory's theorem is used in its local regular form: a nowhere-zero one-form tangent to a regular codimension-one foliation is locally f\,dg. Nonvanishing gives T\ne0 and dS\ne0, and hence

dU=\sum_iX_i\,dx_i+T\,dS.
