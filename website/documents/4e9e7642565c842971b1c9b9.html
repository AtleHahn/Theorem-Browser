Statement

Let n,m\geq1 be integers and let \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n be a bounded domain of diameter d, satisfying the strong cone property: there is a finite open cover \{O_j\} of \partial\Omega and corresponding finite-height solid cones C_j of positive height and opening, with vertices at the origin such that x+C_j\subseteq\Omega whenever x\in\Omega\cap O_j.

There is a constant C=C(m,\Omega) such that, for every 1\leq p\leq\infty and every u\in W_p^m(\Omega), there is a polynomial v of total degree at most m-1 satisfying, simultaneously for k=0,\ldots,m,

|u-v|_{W_p^k(\Omega)}\leq C d^{m-k}|u|_{W_p^m(\Omega)}.

The constant is independent of p and u.

Here W_p^m(\Omega) consists of functions whose weak derivatives through order m belong to L^p(\Omega). For a multiindex \alpha=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n) of nonnegative integers, write |\alpha|=\sum_j\alpha_j and D^\alpha for the corresponding weak partial derivative. The seminorms are

|u|_{W_p^k(\Omega)}=\left(\sum_{|\alpha|=k}\|D^\alpha u\|_{L^p(\Omega)}^p\right)^{1/p}\quad(p<\infty),\qquad |u|_{W_\infty^k(\Omega)}=\max_{|\alpha|=k}\|D^\alpha u\|_{L^\infty(\Omega)}.

In particular, the order-zero seminorm is the L^p norm.
