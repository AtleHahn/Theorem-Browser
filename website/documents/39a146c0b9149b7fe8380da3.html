Statement

Color the cells of an n\times n rhombus-shaped Hex board independently black with probability p\in[0,1] and white otherwise. Let A be existence of a black left-to-right crossing and B existence of a black top-to-bottom crossing. Then \Pr(A\cap B)\ge\Pr(A)\Pr(B).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The stated model has finitely many explicit outcomes, independent choices and ordinary finite probabilities; understanding its inequality needs no measure theory. The proof separately imports the advanced correlation theorem.

Proof 1: Apply the appropriate monotonicity form of Harris — outline

Both black crossing events are increasing in the black cells. The Harris inequality for independent colors therefore gives nonnegative correlation.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Adding black cells preserves each black crossing, so the supplied finite-event Harris inequality applies. Finite independent trials, events and monotonicity suffice.
