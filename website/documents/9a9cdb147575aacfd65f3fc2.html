Statement

For equivalent irreducible unitary representations on a complex Hilbert space, any two unitary intertwiners differ by a scalar of absolute value one.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns irreducible unitary representations and intertwiners on a Hilbert space.

Proof 1: Apply Schur’s lemma and unitarity

Schur’s lemma implies that two intertwiners between the irreducible representations differ by a scalar. Since both are unitary, that scalar has absolute value one.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Schur for irreducible unitary Hilbert-space representations and then use unitarity to bound the scalar modulus. Infinite-dimensional unitary representation theory remains an advanced prerequisite despite the elementary final norm argument.
