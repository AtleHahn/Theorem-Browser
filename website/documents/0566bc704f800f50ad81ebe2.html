An isometric embedding in Minkowski space imposes a steep global time function. The result links a proposed ambient representation with intrinsic causal restrictions, rather than treating embedding as a purely pictorial choice.

Statement

Let (M,g) embed isometrically as a Lorentzian submanifold of Minkowski space (\mathbb R^N,-dt^2+\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}dx_i^2), with the induced time orientation. Then \tau=t|_M has past-directed timelike gradient and satisfies |g(\nabla\tau,\nabla\tau)|\ge1. Such a function is called steep temporal.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Lorentzian submanifolds, timelike gradients and time orientation require substantial differential-geometric preparation. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Restrict the ambient Minkowski time coordinate — outline

If the isometric embedding in Lorentz–Minkowski spacetime holds, then M admits a temporal function \tau that additionally satisfies being steep, that is, \tau fulfills |g(\nabla\tau,\nabla\tau)|\geq1. Indeed, \tau is given by the restriction of the natural time coordinate of Minkowski space to the image of the embedding.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Identify the intrinsic gradient with the tangent projection of ambient time and use positivity of the normal complement. Lorentzian submanifolds, induced metrics and metric gradients require advanced differential geometry.
