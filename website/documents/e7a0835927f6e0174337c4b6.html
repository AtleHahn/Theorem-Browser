Statement

Let Ω be an open subset of a complex manifold and φ a plurisubharmonic function on Ω. Let \mathcal J(\varphi) be its multiplier ideal sheaf. The common zero locus of this ideal sheaf is an analytic subset of Ω.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Plurisubharmonic functions and their multiplier ideal sheaves require substantial several-complex-variable and analytic-sheaf theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use coherence to obtain local analytic equations

For a plurisubharmonic function \varphi on \Omega, the multiplier ideal sheaf \mathcal J(\varphi) is coherent on \Omega, by the coherence theorem for multiplier ideals, and therefore its zero variety is an analytic set.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use coherence to obtain local finite holomorphic generators for the multiplier ideal. Plurisubharmonic functions, ideal sheaves and coherence are graduate complex geometry.
