Statement

For compactly supported distributions u,v on \mathbb R^n, n\ge1,

\operatorname{conv}(\operatorname{supp}(u*v))=\operatorname{conv}(\operatorname{supp}u)+\operatorname{conv}(\operatorname{supp}v).

A distribution is a continuous linear functional on smooth compactly supported test functions (convergence on a fixed compact support means uniform convergence of every derivative); its support is the complement of the largest open set where it vanishes on every supported test function. Convolution is defined by (u*v)(\phi)=u_x(v_y(\phi(x+y))), using cutoffs equal to one near the compact supports to interpret the pairings. The convex hull \operatorname{conv}(S) consists of finite convex combinations of points of S, and A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,b\in B\}. The convex hull of the empty set is empty.
