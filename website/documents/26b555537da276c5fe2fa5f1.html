Statement

Let (M,g) be a compact oriented Riemannian four-manifold without boundary. Let W be its Weyl curvature tensor, Z=\operatorname{Ric}-(R/4)g its traceless Ricci tensor, R its scalar curvature, and d\mu its Riemannian volume. With tensor norms induced by g,

\chi(M)=\frac1{8\pi^2}\int_M\left(\frac14|W|^2-\frac12|Z|^2+\frac1{24}R^2\right)d\mu.
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Weyl and Ricci curvature tensors, Riemannian integration and the Euler characteristic require substantial differential geometry. Proof 1: Rewrite using orthogonal Ricci decomposition — outline

Start with the four-dimensional formula

\chi(M)=\frac1{32\pi^2}\int_M\bigl(|\operatorname{Riem}|^2-4|\operatorname{Ric}|^2+R^2\bigr)d\mu.

Apply the standard orthogonal Ricci decomposition of the Riemann curvature tensor. In terms of its Weyl part W and the traceless Ricci tensor Z, the formula becomes

\chi(M)=\frac1{8\pi^2}\int_M\left(\frac14|W|^2-\frac12|Z|^2+\frac1{24}R^2\right)d\mu.
