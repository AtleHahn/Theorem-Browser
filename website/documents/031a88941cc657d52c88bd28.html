Statement

Let \Sigma_\infty=\bigcup_n\Sigma_n under the standard inclusions, let B\Sigma_\infty be its classifying space, and let B\Sigma_\infty^+ denote Quillen’s plus construction killing its perfect commutator subgroup. Write Q_0=\varinjlim_n\operatorname{Map}_0(S^n,S^n), using suspension maps, where the mapping spaces consist of based degree-zero maps with compact-open topology. Then B\Sigma_\infty^+\simeq Q_0, also written B\Sigma_\infty^+\simeq\Omega_0^\infty S^\infty.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Classifying spaces, the plus construction, mapping-space topology and stable homotopy form a chain of graduate algebraic-topology prerequisites that exceeds the half-hour criterion. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use the acyclic-map characterization of plus construction (outline)

Barratt–Priddy acyclic-map theorem (dependency)

There is a natural map \varphi:B\Sigma_\infty\to Q_0 inducing isomorphisms on homology with every local coefficient system. This acyclicity statement is assumed here.

Plus-construction characterization

The Barratt–Priddy theorem implies that the space B\Sigma_\infty^+ resulting from applying Quillen's plus construction to B\Sigma_\infty can be identified with Q_0. (Since \pi_1(Q_0)\cong H_1(\Sigma_\infty)\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z, the map \varphi:B\Sigma_\infty\to Q_0 satisfies the universal property of the plus construction once it is known that \varphi is an acyclic map.)

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof requires classifying spaces, stable mapping spaces, local-coefficient homology, acyclic maps and Quillen’s plus construction. These form several interdependent parts of graduate algebraic topology. The short appeal to a characterization cannot be understood from the undergraduate baseline within the allowance.
