Statement

Let W be a compact smooth cobordism with boundary M\sqcup N. Suppose a smooth function f:W\to[a,b], a<b, has no critical points, with f^{-1}(a)=M and f^{-1}(b)=N. Then W is diffeomorphic to M\times[a,b], relative to M=M\times\{a\}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses smooth cobordisms, critical points and boundary-relative diffeomorphisms, beyond the stipulated calculus and linear-algebra baseline.

Proof 1: A critical-point-free cobordism is a product (outline)

Choose a smooth Riemannian metric on W. With no critical points, integrating the non-zero gradient vector field of f gives the desired diffeomorphism to the trivial cobordism M\times[a,b].

The source gives this gradient-integration recipe only; gradient normalization, boundary-to-boundary existence and the inverse-flow checks are left implicit.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The flow construction requires smooth manifolds with boundary, cobordisms, Riemannian gradients and a global diffeomorphism from a boundary flow. These are multiple differential-topological prerequisites; the omitted normalization and global flow checks do not lower the level.
