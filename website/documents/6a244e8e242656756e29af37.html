Statement

Let n\ge1 and let f_1,\ldots,f_n:[-1,1]^n\to\mathbb R be continuous functions. For each i\in\{1,\ldots,n\} assume f_i(x)\le0 at every point of the face x_i=-1 and f_i(x)\ge0 at every point of the opposite face x_i=1. Then there exists x\in[-1,1]^n such that f_i(x)=0 for every i.
