Statement

Let D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\} carry its Poincaré metric of curvature -1, with length element 2|dz|/(1-|z|^2) and induced path distance d_D. Let S be a Riemann surface with a smooth Hermitian metric whose Gaussian curvature is everywhere at most -1, and let d_S be its induced path distance. Every holomorphic map f:D\to S satisfies

d_S(f(z_1),f(z_2))\le d_D(z_1,z_2)\qquad(z_1,z_2\in D).

A Riemann surface is a complex manifold of complex dimension one. A Hermitian metric is a smoothly varying positive Hermitian inner product on its complex tangent lines; the path distance is the infimum of lengths of piecewise smooth connecting curves. Holomorphicity is complex differentiability in local charts.
