Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate triangle in the Euclidean plane. Choose points A', B', and C' on the lines BC, CA, and AB, respectively, allowing extensions of the sides. Suppose each of AB'C', A'BC', and A'B'C is a nondegenerate triangle, so that its circumcircle, the unique circle through its three vertices, is defined.

The circumcircles of these three triangles have exactly one common point, called the Miquel point.
