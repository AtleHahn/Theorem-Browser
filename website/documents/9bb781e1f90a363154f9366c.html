Proof 1: Scaling a representation of one

if a and b are coprime, then Bézout's identity guarantees the existence of integers x and y such that ax + by = 1. Multiplying both sides by n gives {\displaystyle a(xn)+b(yn)=n}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Multiply a supplied integer representation of one by n. This uses only Bezout identity in its explicit integer form.
