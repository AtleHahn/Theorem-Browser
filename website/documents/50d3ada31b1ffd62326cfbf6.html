Statement

For every integer n\ge1 and every list of 2n-1 integers a_1,\ldots,a_{2n-1}, there are distinct indices i_1,\ldots,i_n such that a_{i_1}+\cdots+a_{i_n} is divisible by n. Equivalently, those n entries have an integer average; repetitions among the integer values are allowed.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Integer arithmetic and index selection suffice.

Level justification

Polynomial expansion, the factor theorem, arithmetic modulo a prime and finite counting suffice. The required Chevalley–Warning case follows by summing monomials; Fermat’s identity follows by permuting the nonzero residues. Grouping disjoint blocks handles composite moduli.
