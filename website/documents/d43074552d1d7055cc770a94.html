Statement

For every sequence (a_n)_{n\geq1} of nonnegative real numbers,

\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(a_1a_2\cdots a_n)^{1/n}\leq e\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n,

where e=\exp(1) and sums are interpreted in [0,\infty]. The constant e is optimal: replacing it by any smaller real constant makes the inequality false for some nonnegative sequence. If 0<\sum_{n\geq1}a_n<\infty, the inequality is strict.
