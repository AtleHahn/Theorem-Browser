Statement

Let A be a prime associative algebra with identity over a field k, satisfying a nonzero polynomial identity over k. Let Z be its center and Q=\operatorname{Frac}(Z). Then

A\otimes_Z Q

is a central simple algebra over Q: it is finite-dimensional over Q, has no nonzero proper two-sided ideals, and has center exactly Q. Prime means A\ne0 and the product of any two nonzero two-sided ideals is nonzero. A polynomial identity is a nonzero polynomial in finitely many noncommuting variables with coefficients in k that evaluates to zero for every substitution from A. The center consists of elements commuting with every element of A; in a prime algebra it is an integral domain. The tensor product here is central localization, adjoining inverses to every nonzero element of Z.
