Statement

Let M be a closed oriented (2k+1)-manifold with k\ge0, and let F_j=H_j(M;\mathbb Z)/\operatorname{torsion}. There is a perfect integral bilinear pairing F_k\otimes F_{k+1}\to\mathbb Z.

Proof 1: Specialize the free pairing to adjacent middle dimensions

The free homology duality pairing couples F_i and F_{n-i} in a closed oriented n-manifold. Substituting n=2k+1 and i=k gives n-i=k+1, so it pairs the lower and upper middle groups as claimed.

Level justification

Substitute the odd dimension into free integral homology duality. Closed oriented manifolds, integral homology and perfect pairings require graduate algebraic topology despite the elementary index calculation.
