Statement

Let X be a topological space and let U\subseteq A\subseteq X be subspaces. Suppose that

\overline{U}^{\,X}\subseteq\operatorname{int}_X(A),

where the closure and interior are taken in X. Then the inclusion of pairs

j:(X\setminus U,A\setminus U)\longrightarrow(X,A)

induces an isomorphism

j_*:H_n(X\setminus U,A\setminus U;\mathbb Z)\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }H_n(X,A;\mathbb Z)

for every integer n\geq0. Here H_n(Y,B;\mathbb Z) denotes the relative singular homology group of the pair (Y,B) with integer coefficients.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Relative singular homology requires singular chain complexes, boundaries and quotient complexes, followed by induced maps of pairs. These interdependent algebraic-topology prerequisites go well beyond a confident 30-minute extension of undergraduate topology and algebra.

Proof availability

The source gives a barycentric-subdivision sketch. Its deletion step refers to simplices in the interior of U rather than those in A, and it does not supply the injectivity argument for the induced map. Thus it does not provide a complete proof of the stated isomorphism.
