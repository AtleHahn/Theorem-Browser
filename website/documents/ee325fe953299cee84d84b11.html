Statement

Let n\ge2 and a_1,\ldots,a_n\ge0 be real numbers. Put e_0=1 and

e_k=\sum_{1\le i_1<\cdots<i_k\le n}a_{i_1}\cdots a_{i_k},\qquad S_k=\frac{e_k}{\binom nk}\quad(0\le k\le n).

Then, for every integer 1\le k\le n-1,

S_{k-1}S_{k+1}\le S_k^2.
