Statement

Let F,G,H\in\mathbb C[X,Y,Z] be homogeneous polynomials, with F,G nonzero and relatively prime and h=\deg H>f=\deg F, h>g=\deg G. At each common projective zero of F,G, choose a nonzero coordinate representative p\in\mathbb C^3. Assume H belongs to the ideal generated by F,G in the ring

R_p=\{N/D:N,D\in\mathbb C[X,Y,Z],\ D(p)\ne0\}.

Then there exist homogeneous polynomials A,B of degrees h-f,h-g respectively such that H=AF+BG (the zero polynomial is allowed). Any two choices A,A' differ by a polynomial multiple of G, and any two choices B,B' differ by a polynomial multiple of F. A projective point is a nonzero triple up to multiplication by a nonzero scalar. Homogeneous means every monomial has the same total degree. The local ideal condition means H=uF+vG for some u,v\in R_p; it is independent of the chosen representative.
