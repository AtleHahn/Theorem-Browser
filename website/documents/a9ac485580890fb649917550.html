Statement

Let K be a number field, that is, a finite extension of \mathbb Q, and let A be an abelian variety over K. Then the abelian group A(K) of K-rational points is finitely generated: there are finitely many points P_1,\ldots,P_m\in A(K) such that every point of A(K) is an integer linear combination of them under the group law of A.

Here an abelian variety is a smooth projective geometrically connected algebraic group, and A(K) denotes its points defined over K.
