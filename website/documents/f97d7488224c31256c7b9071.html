Statement

Let d\geq1 be an integer and let C\subseteq\mathbb R^d. The following conditions are equivalent:

  1. There exist an integer m\geq1 and a matrix A\in\mathbb Z^{m\times d} such that
C=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:Ax\leq0\},

where the inequality is componentwise. 2. There exist an integer r\geq1 and vectors v_1,\ldots,v_r\in\mathbb Z^d such that

C=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^r t_jv_j:t_j\in\mathbb R,\ t_j\geq0\right\}.

Thus finite homogeneous systems of integer linear inequalities define exactly the nonnegative real spans of finite sets of integer vectors. No assumption that C contains no line, or that its span is all of \mathbb R^d, is required.
