Statement

Let a_n\in\mathbb C and suppose there is 0<r<1 with |a_n|<r/4 for every n. For each fixed \zeta\in\mathbb C with |\zeta|<1, the finite continued fractions

\cfrac{a_1\zeta}{1+\cfrac{a_2\zeta}{1+\cdots+\cfrac{a_n\zeta}{1}}}

converge as n\to\infty.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Complex numbers; Finite continued fractions; Limits of sequences. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: A common strict interior disc

Use the forward-compositions theorem: if analytic maps f_n on a simply connected domain S all map S into a common compact subset of S, then F_n=f_1\circ\cdots\circ f_n converges uniformly on compact subsets to a constant.

Consider the continued fraction

{\displaystyle {\cfrac {a_{1}\zeta }{1+{\cfrac {a_{2}\zeta }{1+\cdots }}}}}

with

{\displaystyle f_{n}(z)={\frac {a_{n}\zeta }{1+z}}.}

Stipulate that |ζ| < 1 and |z| < R < 1. Then for 0 < r < 1,

{\displaystyle |a_{n}|<rR(1-R)\Rightarrow \left|f_{n}(z)\right|<rR<R\Rightarrow {\frac {a_{1}\zeta }{1+{\frac {a_{2}\zeta }{1+\cdots }}}}=F(\zeta )}. Set R = 1/2.

Here \zeta is fixed, S=\{z:|z|<R\}, and the common compact subset is \{w:|w|\leq rR\}. Evaluating the forward compositions at z=0 gives the finite continued fractions.
