Statement

Every finite-rank topological vector bundle on real or complex affine space, with its usual topology, is trivial.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Vector bundles and their classification are topology/complex geometry outside the finite-dimensional calculus baseline.

Mathematical Field
Algebraic topology

Proof 1: Apply contractibility and bundle classification

Affine space is contractible. The classification of vector bundles over a contractible paracompact base therefore gives triviality.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use classification of vector bundles over a contractible paracompact base. Contracting affine space is elementary, but bundles, trivializations and their classification require substantial topology.
