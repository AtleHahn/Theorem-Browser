Statement

Let A,B be abelian varieties of the same dimension over a field k. A morphism f:A\to B with dense image and f(0_A)=0_B is an isogeny, meaning a surjective group homomorphism with finite kernel.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves abelian varieties; morphisms of varieties; algebraic groups. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Dense pointed morphisms of equal-dimensional abelian varieties — proof

Every dense morphism between two abelian varieties of the same dimension is automatically surjective with finite fibres, and if it preserves identities then it is a homomorphism of groups.

The kernel is the fibre over the identity, so f is a surjective group homomorphism with finite kernel, as required.

Level justification

The application requires abelian varieties, algebraic morphisms, density and finite fibers, and their group structures. The short identification of the kernel does not reduce this algebraic-geometric burden.
