Every closed connected three-manifold with trivial fundamental group is homeomorphic to the three-sphere S^3.

Proof 1: Apply elliptization and the spherical covering theorem

Use elliptization as an external theorem: a closed connected three-manifold with finite fundamental group admits a Riemannian metric of constant positive sectional curvature. A trivial fundamental group is finite, so this applies.

Use the spherical covering theorem: a closed connected three-manifold with this metric is covered by S^3. If the base manifold has trivial fundamental group, the connected covering is one-sheeted, hence its covering map is a homeomorphism. This proves the claim.
