Proof 1: Sixteen is not a rational or 2-adic eighth power

It is clear that 16 is not a 2-adic eighth power, and hence not a rational eighth power, since the 2-adic valuation of 16 is 4 which is not divisible by 8.

Here the valuation is normalized by v_2(2)=1; for nonzero x\in\mathbb Q_2, v_2(x^8)=8v_2(x) with v_2(x)\in\mathbb Z, and \mathbb Q\subset\mathbb Q_2.

Level

Level justification

The valuation contradiction is immediate from the supplied integer-valued rules, but the full claim concerns Q2 and its embedding of Q. Understanding the p-adic field together with its discrete valuation is not confidently a small extension of ordinary undergraduate preparation; retain beyond undergraduate.
