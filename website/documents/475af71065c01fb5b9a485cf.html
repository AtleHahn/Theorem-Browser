Statement

Let S be nonempty, \Sigma=\{\varnothing,S\} and \mu(S)=\infty. Then f=1_S has \|f\|_\infty=1, but \sup\{|\int fg\,d\mu|:g\in L^1(\mu),\|g\|_1\le1\}=0.

Proof 1: Failure of norm duality on an infinite trivial measure space

Every measurable scalar function on this trivial sigma-field is constant. An integrable constant must be zero since \mu(S)=\infty. Thus every admissible g is zero, giving supremum zero, whereas the essential supremum of f is one.

Level justification

Identify measurable functions on the trivial sigma-field and calculate integrability and essential supremum. The calculations are short, but arbitrary infinite measure, L1 and essential-supremum duality are a cumulative measure-theoretic prerequisite; conservatively retain beyond undergraduate.
