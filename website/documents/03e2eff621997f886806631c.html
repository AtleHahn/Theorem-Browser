Statement

Let M be a smooth Riemannian manifold with its Levi–Civita connection, and p\in M. Let B_\epsilon(0)\subset T_pM be a ball on which the exponential map is defined and smooth, with \exp_p(tv)=\gamma_{p,v}(t) for the geodesic having initial velocity v. Identify the tangent spaces of the vector space T_pM with T_pM itself. For v\in B_\epsilon(0) and \gamma(t)=\exp_p(tv), (d\exp_p)_v(v)=\gamma^{\prime}(1)=\mathcal P_{\gamma,0\to1}v, where \mathcal P denotes parallel transport. In particular \|(d\exp_p)_v(v)\|_{\exp_p(v)}=\|v\|_p.

Proof 1: Differentiate a radial curve and transport its velocity

Let us construct a curve \alpha:I\to T_pM, where I is an interval about zero, such that \alpha(0)=v and \alpha'(0)=v\in T_vT_pM\cong T_pM. Since T_vT_pM\cong T_pM\cong\mathbb R^n, we can put \alpha(t)=v(t+1). Therefore,

\begin{aligned} (d\exp_p)_v(v) &=\left.\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(\exp_p\circ\alpha(t)\bigr)\right|_{t=0}\\ &=\left.\frac{d}{dt}\exp_p(tv)\right|_{t=1} =\gamma'(1)=\mathcal P_{\gamma,0\to1}v, \end{aligned}

where \mathcal P is the parallel transport operator and \gamma(t)=\exp_p(tv). The last equality is true because \gamma is a geodesic, therefore \gamma' is parallel. Metric-compatible parallel transport preserves length, giving

\|(d\exp_p)_v(v)\|_{\exp_p(v)}=\|v\|_p.
