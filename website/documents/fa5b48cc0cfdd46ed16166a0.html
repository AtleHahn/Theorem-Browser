Statement

There exist a discrete valuation ring R, an abelian variety A over its fraction field K, and its Néron model \mathcal A over R, such that the identity component

G=(\mathcal A\times_R k)^0,\qquad k=R/\mathfrak m_R,

of the special fiber is a connected algebraic group over the residue field k which is neither affine nor proper over k. A discrete valuation ring is a local principal ideal domain with a unique nonzero maximal ideal \mathfrak m_R. An abelian variety is a smooth geometrically connected proper group variety. The Néron model is a smooth separated R-group scheme with generic fiber A and the following mapping property: for every smooth separated R-scheme Y, every K-morphism Y\times_RK\to A extends uniquely to an R-morphism Y\to\mathcal A. The special fiber is the base change to k, and its identity component is the connected component containing the identity. Affine means isomorphic to the spectrum of a ring; proper means separated, of finite type and universally closed.
