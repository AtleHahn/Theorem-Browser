Statement

Let A be a finite set of positive integers with cardinality X\geq1, and P a set of primes. For d\geq1 let A_d=\{a\in A:d\mid a\}. Let w be a real multiplicative function: w(1)=1 and w(ab)=w(a)w(b) whenever a,b are coprime positive integers. Use the sieve-density convention 0\leq w(p)/p<1 for primes p, and define R_d by

|A_d|=Xw(d)/d+R_d.

For real z\geq2 set

P(z)=\prod_{p\in P,\ p\leq z}p,\qquad V(z)=\prod_{p\in P,\ p\leq z}(1-w(p)/p),

and let S(A,P,z) be the number of elements of A divisible by no prime in P at most z. Empty products equal 1. All logarithms below are natural.

Fix constants \kappa>0 and C>0. Suppose that for all real 2\leq\eta\leq\xi,

\prod_{\eta\leq p\leq\xi}(1-w(p)/p)^{-1}<\left(\frac{\log\xi}{\log\eta}\right)^\kappa\left(1+\frac C{\log\eta}\right),

where the product ranges over primes. Then, uniformly for z\geq2 and real u\geq1,

S(A,P,z)=XV(z)\{1+O_{\kappa,C}(u^{-u/2})\}+O_{\kappa,C}\left(\sum_{d\leq z^u,\ d\mid P(z)}|R_d|\right).

The notation means that the absolute difference between S(A,P,z) and XV(z) is at most a constant depending only on \kappa,C times the sum of XV(z)u^{-u/2} and the displayed remainder sum. In particular the bound is uniform in A,X,P,z,u and admissible w.
