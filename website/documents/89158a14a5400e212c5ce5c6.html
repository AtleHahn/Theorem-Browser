Statement

For topological spaces X,Y, a principal ideal domain R and k≥0, there is a natural short exact sequence

0\to\bigoplus_{i+j=k}H_i(X;R)\otimes_R H_j(Y;R)\to H_k(X\times Y;R)\to\bigoplus_{i+j=k-1}\operatorname{Tor}^R_1(H_i(X;R),H_j(Y;R))\to0.

Homology in negative degree is zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Singular homology; Principal ideal domains; Tensor products; Tor functors; Short exact sequences. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Read the two-column filtration — outline

Use the source spectral sequence

E^2_{p,q}=\bigoplus_{i+j=q}\operatorname{Tor}^R_p(H_i(X;R),H_j(Y;R))\Longrightarrow H_{p+q}(X\times Y;R).

Over a PID Tor_p vanishes for p≥2, leaving columns p=0,1 only. All higher differentials vanish, so the spectral sequence collapses. Its two-step filtration in total degree k gives the short exact sequence, with column zero the tensor-product term and column one the Tor_1 term in degree k−1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use higher Tor vanishing to leave two columns and read the limiting filtration as an exact sequence. Derived functors, spectral-sequence differentials and homology filtrations require graduate background.
