Statement

Let X be a compact Kähler manifold. Every smooth d-exact complex (1,1)-form \alpha has the form \alpha=i\partial\bar\partial f for a smooth function f:X\to\mathbb C.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The Kähler dd-bar assertion requires complex differential geometry and several interacting differential operators. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Specialize the ddbar potential degree to zero

The compact Kähler \partial\bar\partial-lemma states that a d-exact form of bidegree (p,q), with p,q\ge1, is i\partial\bar\partial\beta for a form \beta of bidegree (p-1,q-1). When the complex differential form has bidegree (1,1), its potential has bidegree (0,0). In this case the lemma states that an exact differential form \alpha\in\Omega^{1,1}(X) has a \partial\bar\partial-potential given by a smooth function f\in C^\infty(X,\mathbb C):

\alpha=i\partial\bar\partial f.
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The degree substitution occurs in the ddbar lemma for compact Kahler manifolds. Complex differential forms, Dolbeault operators and Kahler structure are several substantial prerequisites.
