Statement

Let (M,g) be a connected smooth pseudo-Riemannian manifold of dimension n\geq2. Then (M,g) is locally projectively flat if and only if it has constant sectional curvature.

Here a pseudo-Riemannian metric is a smooth nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form on each tangent space. Local projective flatness means that each point has a coordinate neighborhood in which the nonconstant geodesics, considered as unparametrized curves, are portions of straight lines. Constant sectional curvature means that there is a single real number K equal to the sectional curvature of every nondegenerate tangent two-plane at every point of M.

Proof 1: Pure-trace connections and local space forms (external)

The following editorial argument completes the stated scope. A supporting source is Metric Connections in Projective Differential Geometry (Eastwood–Matveev, equation (1.1), projective curvature decomposition, Proposition 5.5 and Corollary 5.6 (PDF pp.2 and 9); additional signature-independent repair and converse bridge given below.).

Own repair and scope extension of the projective-connection argument: in straight-geodesic coordinates Gamma^k_ij=delta^k_i psi_j+delta^k_j psi_i. Taking the trace gives (n+1)psi_i=partial_i log sqrt(|det g|), so dpsi=0; this is the missing closedness argument. Set A_ij=partial_i psi_j-psi_i psi_j. The curvature has the delta-A form. The metric skew-symmetry in the lowered output/input indices, contracted with g^{-1}, gives n A_ij=(tr_g A)g_ij, so A=lambda g, using only nondegeneracy. Direct differentiation and dpsi=0 give nabla_i A_jk=nabla_j A_ik. Thus (partial_i lambda)g_jk=(partial_j lambda)g_ik; contraction gives (n-1)d lambda=0, including n=2. This yields locally constant sectional curvature (with sign determined by the curvature convention), hence a single constant on connected M. Conversely invoke the standard local classification of pseudo-Riemannian constant-curvature metrics: near a point they are isometric to the flat model or a nondegenerate quadric space form of the same signature and curvature. Geodesics on the quadric are plane sections through the origin; central projection into an affine hyperplane makes them straight locally. Alternatively the local Beltrami model g_ij=b_ij/(1+K b(x,x))-K(bx)_i(bx)_j/(1+K b(x,x))^2 has pure-trace Christoffel symbols. The tensor calculation is an additional argument; local space-form classification is a standard dependency.
