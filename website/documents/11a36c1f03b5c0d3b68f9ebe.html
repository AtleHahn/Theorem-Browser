Statement

Let (M,g) be a connected smooth pseudo-Riemannian manifold of dimension n\geq2. Then (M,g) is locally projectively flat if and only if it has constant sectional curvature.

Here a pseudo-Riemannian metric is a smooth nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form on each tangent space. Local projective flatness means that each point has a coordinate neighborhood in which the nonconstant geodesics, considered as unparametrized curves, are portions of straight lines. Constant sectional curvature means that there is a single real number K equal to the sectional curvature of every nondegenerate tangent two-plane at every point of M.
