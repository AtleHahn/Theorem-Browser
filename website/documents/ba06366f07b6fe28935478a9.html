Proof 1: Even-dimensional real projective space has the fixed-point property — outline

Lift f\circ q:S^{2n}\to\mathbb{RP}^{2n} through the double cover q:S^{2n}\to\mathbb{RP}^{2n} to a map s:S^{2n}\to S^{2n}. The even-sphere corollary gives s(p)=p or s(p)=-p. Both points project to q(p), so f(q(p))=q(s(p))=q(p).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Lifting the sphere map through the projective double cover uses simple connectivity and covering-space lifting in addition to the projective quotient. These are multiple linked algebraic-topology concepts.
