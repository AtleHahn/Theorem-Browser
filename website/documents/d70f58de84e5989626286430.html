Statement

Let M be a smooth finite-dimensional real manifold and let E\subseteq TM be a smooth subbundle of its tangent bundle of constant rank r. Then the following are equivalent:

  1. For every pair of smooth local vector fields X,Y taking values in E, their Lie bracket [X,Y] also takes values in E.
  2. There is a smooth regular foliation of M whose leaves have tangent spaces E_p at every point p.

Here E_p is an r-dimensional subspace of T_pM, varying smoothly with p. The Lie bracket acts on smooth functions f by [X,Y]f=X(Yf)-Y(Xf). A regular foliation of dimension r partitions M into connected immersed submanifolds called leaves; each point has smooth coordinates (x^1,\ldots,x^n) in which the local pieces of leaves are given by fixing x^{r+1},\ldots,x^n. Thus the leaves are the maximal connected integral manifolds tangent to E.
