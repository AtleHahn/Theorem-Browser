Statement

Let M be a smooth finite-dimensional real manifold and let E\subseteq TM be a smooth subbundle of its tangent bundle of constant rank r. Then the following are equivalent:

  1. For every pair of smooth local vector fields X,Y taking values in E, their Lie bracket [X,Y] also takes values in E.
  2. There is a smooth regular foliation of M whose leaves have tangent spaces E_p at every point p.

Here E_p is an r-dimensional subspace of T_pM, varying smoothly with p. The Lie bracket acts on smooth functions f by [X,Y]f=X(Yf)-Y(Xf). A regular foliation of dimension r partitions M into connected immersed submanifolds called leaves; each point has smooth coordinates (x^1,\ldots,x^n) in which the local pieces of leaves are given by fixing x^{r+1},\ldots,x^n. Thus the leaves are the maximal connected integral manifolds tangent to E.

Proof 1: Commuting local frames and gluing integral plaques (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Lemma14.11, Proposition14.1, Theorems14.13 and15.4; PDFpp.100–108).

The source calls bracket closure “integrability.” For the global step in Theorem15.4, equip each union of intersecting plaques with the topology and smooth charts supplied by the plaques. Their intersections are open in each plaque, with smooth transition maps. A countable ambient foliation atlas gives only countably many plaques in each leaf: a plaque meets each other chart in at most countably many connected components, and finite chains preserve countability. This yields a second-countable Hausdorff immersed manifold. Thus the leaf topology supplies the global construction without requiring finite plaque unions to be embedded in the ambient topology.
