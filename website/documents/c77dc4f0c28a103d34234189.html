Statement

The spaces X=S^2\times\mathbb{RP}^3 and Y=\mathbb{RP}^2\times S^3 have isomorphic homotopy groups in every positive degree, but are not homotopy equivalent. Here S^n is the n-sphere and \mathbb{RP}^n real projective n-space.

Proof 1: Common universal cover and different homology — outline

Take X= S2 × RP3 and Y= RP2 × S3. Then X and Y have the same fundamental group, namely the cyclic group Z/2, and the same universal cover, namely S2 × S3; thus, they have isomorphic homotopy groups. On the other hand, their homology groups are different (as can be seen from the Künneth formula); thus, X and Y are not homotopy equivalent.

Level justification

Compare universal covers and higher homotopy groups, then distinguish the spaces by product homology. Covering-space theory, projective-space homology and Kunneth form a substantial algebraic-topology prerequisite chain.
