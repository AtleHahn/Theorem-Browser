Statement

Let K be a field and L a Lie algebra over K: a K-vector space with a bilinear alternating bracket satisfying the Jacobi identity. Let X be a basis of L equipped with a total order. The universal enveloping algebra is

U(L)=T_K(L)/\langle u\otimes v-v\otimes u-[u,v]:u,v\in L\rangle,

where T_K(L)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}L^{\otimes n} is the tensor algebra, L^{\otimes0}=K, and the angle brackets denote the two-sided ideal generated by the displayed elements. Write h:L\to U(L) for the canonical linear map.

The products

h(x_1)\cdots h(x_n),\qquad n\geq0,\quad x_1\leq\cdots\leq x_n,\quad x_j\in X,

indexed by all finite nondecreasing sequences in X, form a K-basis of U(L); the empty sequence gives the unit 1. In particular, different such sequences give different basis elements, and each element of U(L) has a unique finite linear combination in this basis.

The relation checks still reduce to skew-symmetry and Jacobi. Induction is on finite word length, so the basis may be infinite and its total order need not be a well-order. Alternation [x,x]=0 handles repeated letters in every characteristic.
