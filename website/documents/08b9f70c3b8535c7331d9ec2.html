Statement

On the smooth genus-two moduli stack \mathcal M_2, work in the rational Chow ring. Let \pi:\mathcal C_2\to\mathcal M_2 be the universal curve, \omega_\pi its relative dualizing sheaf, \mathbb E=\pi_*\omega_\pi the rank-two Hodge bundle, \lambda_i=c_i(\mathbb E), and \kappa_1=\pi_*(c_1(\omega_\pi)^2). Then

\lambda_1=\kappa_1/12,\qquad \lambda_2=\lambda_1^2/2=\kappa_1^2/288.

Beyond undergraduate level

Proof 1: Genus-two Hodge Chern classes on the smooth moduli locus — outline

The source supplies the identities \operatorname{ch}(\mathbb E)_2=0 and \lambda_1=(\kappa_1+\delta)/12, where \delta is the boundary class. On the smooth locus the boundary contribution vanishes. Analyzing the degree-two Chern character, \operatorname{ch}(\mathbb E)_2=(\lambda_1^2-2\lambda_2)/2, gives \lambda_2=\lambda_1^2/2 and then the stated substitution.
