Statement

Let \Sigma be a finite alphabet and \Sigma^* its finite words, including the empty word; |w| denotes word length and juxtaposition denotes concatenation. A language L\subseteq\Sigma^* is context-free if generated from a start symbol by finitely many grammar productions A\to w, where A is one nonterminal symbol and w is a word of terminals and nonterminals; generated terminal-only words form L. If L is context-free, there is a constant c>0 such that for all integers n\ge m\ge2 and every set R\subseteq L\cap\Sigma^n, where \Sigma^n denotes the words of length n, there are distinct words z_1,\ldots,z_k\in R with k\ge |R|/(cn^2) and decompositions z_i=w_ix_iy_i satisfying: each of |w_i|,|x_i|,|y_i| is independent of i; m/2<|x_i|\le m; and w_ix_jy_i\in L for every 1\le i,j\le k.
