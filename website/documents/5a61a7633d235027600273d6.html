Statement

Let R be a ring with identity. Suppose 0\to K\to P\xrightarrow{\phi}M\to0 and 0\to K^\prime\to P^\prime\xrightarrow{\phi^\prime}M\to0 are short exact sequences of R-modules, with P,P^\prime projective. If K is projective, then K^\prime is projective, and conversely.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The hypotheses and conclusion require module theory and projectivity.

Mathematical Field
Category theory; homological algebra
Required topics
Modules over a unital ringProjective modulesShort exact sequencesDirect sums and isomorphisms
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Apply the Schanuel isomorphism — outline

Schanuel’s lemma gives K\oplus P^\prime\cong K^\prime\oplus P. Consequently projectivity of the kernel in one projective presentation implies projectivity in the other.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Schanuel lemma and closure of projectivity under finite sums and direct summands. Modules, exact sequences, projective presentations and projectivity together require homological algebra beyond the introductory baseline.
