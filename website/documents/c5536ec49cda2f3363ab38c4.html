Statement

Let n\geq1 be an integer, and let K,L\subseteq\mathbb R^n be convex bodies, meaning compact convex sets with nonempty interior. Write V for n-dimensional Lebesgue volume and define the Minkowski sum by

K+\varepsilon L=\{x+\varepsilon y:x\in K,\ y\in L\}.

Define the first mixed volume by

V_1(K,L)=\frac1n\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\frac{V(K+\varepsilon L)-V(K)}{\varepsilon}.

Then

V_1(K,L)\geq V(K)^{(n-1)/n}V(L)^{1/n}.

Equality holds if and only if K and L are homothetic: L=v+cK for some v\in\mathbb R^n and some real c>0.
