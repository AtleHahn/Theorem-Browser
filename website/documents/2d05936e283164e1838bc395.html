Statement

Let M be a compact complex manifold of complex dimension n, and let F be a holomorphic line bundle on M. Write \Omega^p(F) for the sheaf of holomorphic p-forms with values in F, and H^q(M;\Omega^p(F)) for its sheaf cohomology.

Positivity of the first Chern class c_1(F)\in H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(M;\mathbb R) means that it is represented by a smooth closed real (1,1)-form \omega with \omega(v,Jv)>0 for every nonzero real tangent vector v, where J is the complex structure. Negativity means that -c_1(F) is positive in this sense. The class c_1(F) is the de Rham class of i\Theta/(2\pi), where \Theta is the curvature of the Chern connection of any smooth Hermitian metric on F; that connection preserves the metric and has (0,1) part equal to the holomorphic structure operator \bar\partial.

For integers 0\leq p\leq n and q\geq0:

  • If c_1(F) is positive, then H^q(M;\Omega^p(F))=0 whenever p+q>n.
  • If c_1(F) is negative, then H^q(M;\Omega^p(F))=0 whenever p+q<n.

No vanishing assertion at p+q=n is made.
