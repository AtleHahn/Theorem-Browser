Statement

For each integer n\ge1, define the n\times n matrix R_n=(r_{ij}) by r_{ij}=1 if i\mid j or j=1, and r_{ij}=0 otherwise, for 1\le i,j\le n. The Riemann hypothesis holds if and only if, for every \varepsilon>0, \det R_n=O(n^{1/2+\varepsilon}) as the positive integer n\to\infty. Here RH asserts that every nontrivial zero of the meromorphic Riemann zeta function has real part 1/2.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The matrix is elementary but RH refers to nontrivial zeros of a particular meromorphic continuation. Proof 1: Substitute the Mertens determinant identity (outline)

Put M(x)=\sum_{m\le x}\mu(m), where \mu is the Möbius function. The external Mertens criterion says that RH is equivalent to M(x)=O(x^{1/2+\varepsilon}) for every positive \varepsilon. The identity \det R_n=M(n) therefore expresses the same growth condition in terms of these determinants.
