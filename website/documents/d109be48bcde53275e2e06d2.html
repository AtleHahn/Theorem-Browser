Statement

Let E\subset\mathbb R^d, d\ge2, and all its coordinate projections be Lebesgue measurable. If a finite number P\ge0 satisfies 2|\pi_j(E)|\le P for every j, then |E|^{d-1}\le2^{-d}P^d. In the source application, P is a boundary measure for which these projection estimates hold.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement involves general Lebesgue measurable sets and their projected measures, beyond ordinary calculus.

Proof 1: Bound every projection by half the boundary measure

Use |E|^{d-1}\le\prod_{j=1}^d|\pi_j(E)|. The assumed bounds 2|\pi_j(E)|\le P give \prod_j|\pi_j(E)|\le(P/2)^d, yielding the asserted inequality.
