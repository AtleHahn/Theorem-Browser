Statement

Let (A,\mathfrak m) be a commutative Noetherian local ring with identity, complete in its maximal-ideal topology, and put k=A/\mathfrak m. Noetherian means all ideals are finitely generated, local means there is a unique maximal ideal, and complete means A\to\varprojlim_r A/\mathfrak m^r is an isomorphism. A local ring is regular if the minimal number of generators of its maximal ideal equals its Krull dimension, the supremum of lengths of strictly increasing chains of prime ideals. There exists a complete regular Noetherian local ring R and a surjective ring homomorphism R\to A.
