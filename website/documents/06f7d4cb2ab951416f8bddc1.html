Statement

Let K be an algebraically closed field, n\ge1 an integer, and A\in K^{n\times n}. If \lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r are the distinct roots of p(t)=\det(A-tI) and their multiplicities are n_1,\ldots,n_r, then \sum_{i=1}^r n_i=n.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and introductory abstract algebra supply determinants, matrices, field roots, ring operations and divisibility. The displayed assertion uses these directly; no graduate concepts are needed to understand it.

Proof 1: Read the degree of the factored polynomial

The characteristic polynomial has degree n and factors as p(t)=\prod_{i=1}^r(\lambda_i-t)^{n_i}. Thus the algebraic multiplicities sum to n.

Level justification
