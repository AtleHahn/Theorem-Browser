Statement

Let f:X\to Y be a surjection of sets. The family \{f^{-1}(\{y\}):y\in Y\} is a partition of X into nonempty, pairwise disjoint cells. The relation x\sim x^{\prime}\iff f(x)=f(x^{\prime}) is an equivalence relation with these cells as classes.

Proof 1: Surjections determine partitions into fibers — outline

Take the preimages of the singletons in the codomain. These fibers determine a partition of the domain, and hence an equivalence relation on it.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use surjectivity for nonempty fibers, uniqueness of function values for disjointness and the same-cell relation for equivalence. Undergraduate set and function reasoning suffices for arbitrary sets.
