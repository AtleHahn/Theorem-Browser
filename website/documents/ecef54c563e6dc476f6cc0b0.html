Statement

For every set A, let R_A=\{x\in A:x\notin x\}. Then R_A\notin A.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Set comprehension and finite subsets of an abelian group are undergraduate foundations. Sum/difference sets are explicitly defined, so no advanced set or additive-combinatorial theory is needed to understand the claim.

Mathematical Field

Proof 1: Substitute the Russell subset into its defining condition

By restricted comprehension, R_A\in R_A if and only if (R_A\in A\ \wedge\ R_A\notin R_A). Thus the hypothesis R_A\in A yields a contradiction and must be rejected.
