Statement

Let C be a positively oriented piecewise smooth simple closed plane curve enclosing D. Then \operatorname{area}(D)=\oint_Cx\,dy=-\oint_Cy\,dx=\tfrac12\oint_C(-y\,dx+x\,dy).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Real partial derivatives; Line and double integrals; Oriented planar boundaries. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Apply the source three unit-curl choices

Green's theorem can be used to compute area by line integral. The area of the planar region D is given by

A=\iint_D dA.

Choose L and M such that \frac{\partial M}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial L}{\partial y}=1. By Green's theorem the area is given by

A=\oint_C(L\,dx+M\,dy).

The choices (L,M)=(0,x),(-y,0),(-y/2,x/2) therefore give

A=\oint_C x\,dy=-\oint_C y\,dx =\frac12\oint_C(-y\,dx+x\,dy).

Choose three vector fields with unit scalar curl and apply Green to the enclosed planar area. Undergraduate multivariable calculus suffices.
