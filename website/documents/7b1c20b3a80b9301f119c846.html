Statement

Let a,b,c,d_0 be positive integers with u=a/c<v=b/d_0, and set w=v-u, m=(u+v)/2. Put

M(x)=\frac{ax+b}{cx+d_0},\qquad N(z)=M^{-1}(z)=\frac{d_0z-b}{-cz+a}.

Let D_0 be the closed disk centered at m with radius w/2, and let D_\pm be the closed disks centered at m\pm iw/(2\sqrt3) with radius w/\sqrt3. On the finite plane excluding the pole u, N maps \partial D_0 to the imaginary axis and the other two boundary circles to the lines \operatorname{Im}z=\pm\sqrt3\operatorname{Re}z. Points outside D_0 map into the left half-plane; points outside D_+\cup D_- map into \{z:\operatorname{Re}z<0,\ |\operatorname{Im}z|\leq\sqrt3|\operatorname{Re}z|\}.

Proof 1: Circle images from three points outline

Consider the Möbius transformation

M(x)=\frac{ax+b}{cx+d_0},\qquad a,b,c,d_0\in\mathbb Z_{>0},

and assume that a/c<b/d_0. Write N=M^{-1}, as in the statement. Use the fact that Möbius transformations preserve generalized circles.

  • The circle

    \left|x-\tfrac12\left(\tfrac ac+\tfrac b{d_0}\right)\right| =\tfrac12\left(\tfrac b{d_0}-\tfrac ac\right),

    whose diameter lies on the real axis, with endpoints a/c and b/d_0, is mapped by the inverse Möbius transformation

    N(x)=\frac{d_0x-b}{-cx+a}

    onto the imaginary axis, excluding the pole from the finite plane. For example the point

    \tfrac12\left(\tfrac ac+\tfrac b{d_0}\right) +\tfrac i2\left(\tfrac b{d_0}-\tfrac ac\right)

    gets mapped onto the point -i\,d_0/c. The exterior points get mapped into the half-plane with \operatorname{Re}x<0.

  • The two circles with center

    \tfrac12\left(\tfrac ac+\tfrac b{d_0}\right) \pm\tfrac{i}{2\sqrt3}\left(\tfrac b{d_0}-\tfrac ac\right)

    and radius

    \tfrac1{\sqrt3}\left(\tfrac b{d_0}-\tfrac ac\right)

    are mapped by the inverse Möbius transformation N onto the lines \operatorname{Im}x=\pm\sqrt3\operatorname{Re}x, again excluding the pole. For example the point

    \tfrac12\left(\tfrac ac+\tfrac b{d_0}\right) -\tfrac{3i}{2\sqrt3}\left(\tfrac b{d_0}-\tfrac ac\right)

    gets mapped to the point

    -\frac{d_0}{2c}(1-i\sqrt3).

    The exterior points, those outside D_+\cup D_-, get mapped into the sector

    S_{\sqrt3}=\{x=-\alpha+i\beta:|\beta|\leq\sqrt3|\alpha|,\ \alpha>0\}.

The source leaves the circle-preservation and side-selection steps implicit.
