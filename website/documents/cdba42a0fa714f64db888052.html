Statement

Let G be a finite group whose Sylow 2-subgroups are generalized quaternion groups. Assume that G has no nontrivial normal subgroup of odd order. Then its center

Z(G)=\{z\in G:zg=gz\text{ for every }g\in G\}

has order 2.

Here a generalized quaternion 2-group has, for some integer m\geq2, the presentation

\langle x,y\mid x^{2^m}=y^4=1,\ x^{2^{m-1}}=y^2,\ y^{-1}xy=x^{-1}\rangle.

A Sylow 2-subgroup has order equal to the largest power of 2 dividing |G|.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies normality, center and Sylow subgroups. The single explicit two-generator quaternion presentation is a small extension of familiar finite-group examples; no character or block theory is needed for the statement.
