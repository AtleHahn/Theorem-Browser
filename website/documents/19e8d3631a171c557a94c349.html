Statement

Let M be a connected compact oriented smooth manifold of dimension d without boundary. The twisted de Rham Poincaré duality theorem specializes under the chosen orientation to ordinary oriented duality: integration identifies H^d_{\mathrm{dR}}(M;\mathbb R) with \mathbb R, and the product pairing H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(M;\mathbb R)\times H^{d-k}_{\mathrm{dR}}(M;\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R is nondegenerate for every k.

Proof 1: Trivialize the orientation coefficient system

The twisted Poincaré duality theorem for de Rham cohomology uses the flat real orientation line bundle o(M). For a compact connected manifold M without boundary, the top degree cohomology is equipped with the trace morphism

\theta:H^d(M;o(M))\to\mathbb R,

that is to be interpreted as integration on M. The theorem asserts that the trace morphism is a linear isomorphism and that the cup product, or exterior product of differential forms,

H^k(M;\mathbb R)\otimes H^{d-k}(M;o(M))\to H^d(M;o(M))\simeq\mathbb R

is nondegenerate.

The oriented Poincaré duality is contained in this statement, as understood from the fact that the orientation bundle o(M) is trivial if the manifold is oriented, an orientation being a global trivialization, i.e., a nowhere vanishing parallel section.
