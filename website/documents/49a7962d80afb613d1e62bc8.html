Statement

Let G be a connected noncompact real semisimple Lie group with finite center and K a maximal compact subgroup. Let H_0=L^2(K\backslash G/K), the Hilbert space of square-integrable K-biinvariant functions, and let \mathfrak A be the operator-norm closure on H_0 of compactly supported continuous biinvariant convolution operators. If X is its character spectrum, there are a measure \mu on X, unique in measure class, and a unitary U:H_0\to L^2(X,\mu) carrying each a\in\mathfrak A to multiplication by its Gelfand transform \widehat a.

Proof 1: Spectral construction of the spherical transform — source argument

The Hecke algebra C_c(K\backslash G/K), consisting of compactly supported K-biinvariant continuous functions on G, acts by convolution on the Hilbert space H=L^2(G/K). Because G/K is a symmetric space, this *-algebra is commutative. It leaves invariant the subspace H_0 of K-invariant vectors in H, which can be identified with L^2(K\backslash G/K), the space of K-biinvariant square integrable functions on G.

The closure of its image on H_0 in the operator norm is a commutative C^*-algebra \mathfrak A, so by the Gelfand isomorphism it can be identified with the continuous functions vanishing at infinity on its spectrum X. Points in the spectrum are given by nonzero continuous *-homomorphisms of \mathfrak A into \mathbb C, i.e. characters of \mathfrak A.

Moreover, the abelian von Neumann algebra it generates on H_0 is maximal Abelian. By spectral theory, there is a measure \mu, unique in measure class, on the locally compact space X and a unitary transformation U between H_0 and L^2(X,\mu) which carries the operators in \mathfrak A onto the corresponding multiplication operators.

Level justification

The proof moves from a biinvariant convolution representation of a semisimple Lie group to a commutative operator-norm-closed C-star algebra and a maximal abelian von Neumann algebra, then realizes the representation as multiplication on an L2 space. Interpreting each step requires harmonic analysis, operator algebras and measure-class spectral representations. Gelfand representation and multiplicity-one spectral theorem
Kind:

Theorem

Treatment:

Cited without proof

Statement used:

A commutative C-star algebra is represented by its Gelfand transforms in C0 of its character spectrum. In the separable nondegenerate multiplicity-one setting, its Hilbert-space representation is unitarily equivalent to multiplication on L2 of that spectrum for a measure unique up to measure class.

Application in proof:

Identify A with C0(X) and use the maximal abelian representation on H0 to obtain the measure and unitary U in the statement.

Application level:

Beyond undergraduate level

Level justification:

Characters, C-star algebra representations, spectral measure classes and multiplicity one are interconnected advanced notions rather than a single new theorem name.
