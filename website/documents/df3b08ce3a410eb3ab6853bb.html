Statement

Let d>0 be a squarefree integer with d>1. If 3 divides the class number of the real quadratic field \mathbb Q(\sqrt d), then 3 divides the class number of the imaginary quadratic field \mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3d}). The class number is the number of equivalence classes of nonzero fractional ideals of the ring of integers, where ideals I,J are equivalent when I=\alpha J for a nonzero field element \alpha. The ring of integers consists of field elements satisfying monic polynomials over \mathbb Z. Squarefree means not divisible by the square of a prime.

Proof 1: Cubic trace forms and the reflection inequality (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Corollary6.9 p.698; Theorems6.4–6.5 pp.696–697, Proposition6.7 and Theorem6.8 pp.697–698.).

Use the positive fundamental discriminant D of \mathbb Q(\sqrt d), which is d or 4d. Then \mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3D})=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3d}). Corollary 6.9 gives the directed inequality of 3-ranks, including 3\mid D. A finite abelian class group has order divisible by 3 exactly when its 3-rank is positive. In passing from Proposition 6.7 to Theorem 6.8, the possible order-six groups have the same order-two part \langle C_D\rangle; thus their order-three parts still distinguish them.
