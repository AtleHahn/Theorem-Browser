Statement

For real \lambda\ne0, with the integrand continuously extended at t=0, \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\sin(\lambda t)}{\lambda\sinh t}\,dt=\frac{\pi}{\lambda}\tanh\frac{\pi\lambda}{2}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Improper real integrals; Trigonometric and hyperbolic functions. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Integral of sin(lambda t) divided by sinh(t) (outline)
