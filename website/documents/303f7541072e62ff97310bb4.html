Statement

Let k be a field and C a chain of projective lines over k. Thus C is a reduced projective curve with components C_1,\ldots,C_s, each isomorphic to \mathbb P^1_k, obtained by identifying one k-rational point of C_i with one k-rational point of C_{i+1} for 1\le i<s. The two points used on an interior component are distinct; there are no other identifications. The resulting intersections are ordinary nodes, locally two transverse branches (completed local ring k[[x,y]]/(xy)). Every algebraic vector bundle E of finite rank r on C is isomorphic to a direct sum of line bundles L_1\oplus\cdots\oplus L_r. The multiset of isomorphism classes of these line bundles is uniquely determined by E. A vector bundle here is a locally free \mathcal O_C-module of finite rank, and a line bundle has rank one.
