Statement

Let F be a field and 0\to U\xrightarrow{j}V\xrightarrow{T}R\to0 a short exact sequence of F-vector spaces: j is injective, T is surjective, and \operatorname{im}j=\ker T. Then \dim_F U+\dim_F R=\dim_F V. Dimensions may be infinite cardinals, and the addition is cardinal addition.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Exactness is explicitly injectivity, surjectivity and image equals kernel, all linear algebra. Infinite dimensions use basis cardinalities and disjoint-union addition, a short set extension.

Proof 1: Take dimensions of the split sequence

The splitting theorem for vector spaces gives V\cong U\oplus R. Taking dimensions yields \dim_F V=\dim_F U+\dim_F R.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply vector-space splitting and count the disjoint union of bases. Undergraduate linear algebra and elementary cardinal arithmetic suffice with the usual basis-existence convention; no module homological algebra is needed.
