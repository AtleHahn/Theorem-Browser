Statement

Let X be a proper scheme over a perfect field k. The reduced identity component

G=(\operatorname{Pic}^0_{X/k})_{\mathrm{red}}

of its relative Picard scheme is a connected algebraic group over k. There exist such X and k for which G is neither affine nor proper over k. Here \operatorname{Pic}_{X/k} is the group scheme representing the faithfully-flat sheafification of the functor sending a k-scheme T to \operatorname{Pic}(X\times_kT)/\operatorname{Pic}(T), where the denominator denotes pullbacks from T. The Picard group consists of isomorphism classes of line bundles, with tensor product. The superscript 0 denotes the connected component of the identity (the trivial line bundle), and the subscript red means that nilpotent sections of the structure sheaf are removed. Proper means separated, of finite type and universally closed; affine means isomorphic to the spectrum of a ring. An algebraic group is a separated scheme of finite type with multiplication, inverse and identity morphisms satisfying the group axioms. A perfect field is one whose algebraic extensions are all separable.
