Statement

The class of Sahlqvist formulas is a proper subset of the modal formulas having first-order frame correspondents. A first-order frame correspondent defines exactly the Kripke frames on which the modal formula is valid.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Sahlqvist syntactic restrictions and modal frame correspondence require specialized modal-logic preparation beyond a short introduction. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compare a decidable class with an undecidable property

Sahlqvist formulas form a decidable syntactic class and all have first-order frame correspondents. Chagrova’s theorem says that having a first-order frame correspondent is undecidable for arbitrary modal formulas. Consequently the two classes cannot be equal, so some formulas with first-order correspondents are not Sahlqvist.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The final decidability comparison is simple, but understanding the two classes requires modal syntax, Kripke frame validity and first-order correspondence together. This is a substantial specialized logic framework.
