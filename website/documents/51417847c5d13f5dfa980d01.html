Statement

Let n\geq1 be an integer such that either n is even and n\leq12, or n is odd and n\leq23. For any positive real numbers x_1,\ldots,x_n, extend the indices periodically by x_{i+n}=x_i. Then

\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{x_i}{x_{i+1}+x_{i+2}}\geq\frac n2.
Level
High school only
Level justification

Only arithmetic, parity and periodic finite indexing are needed.
