Statement

Let \pi:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak{gl}(V) be a finite-dimensional representation of a finite-dimensional solvable Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Its image is contained in the stabilizer of a complete flag of V, a Borel subalgebra of \mathfrak{gl}(V).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level

Proof 1: A solvable representation lies in a flag stabilizer — source deduction

Lie’s theorem supplies a complete flag in V invariant under \pi(\mathfrak g). To each such flag there corresponds a Borel subalgebra (consisting of linear transformations that stabilize the flag); thus, the theorem says that \pi(\mathfrak g) is contained in some Borel subalgebra of \mathfrak{gl}(V).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Lie triangularization and identify the full-flag stabilizer as Borel. Solvable Lie representations and maximal solvable subalgebras require advanced algebra despite the short deduction.
