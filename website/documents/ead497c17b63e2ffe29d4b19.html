Statement

Let (M,\omega) be a symplectic manifold with a compatible almost-complex structure J. For closed J-holomorphic curves representing a fixed class A\in H_2(M;\mathbb Z), their energy (with the convention E(u)=\int u^*\omega) is the same number \langle[\omega],A\rangle, and in particular is uniformly bounded.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Pseudoholomorphic curves, compatible almost-complex structures and symplectic homology pairings require substantial symplectic geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Evaluate the symplectic cohomology class

The source uses the energy identity E(u)=\int u^*\omega for pseudoholomorphic curves. Integration of the closed symplectic form over the curve evaluates its cohomology class on the curve’s homology class. Hence every curve in class A has energy \langle[\omega],A\rangle.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the symplectic-area energy identity and evaluation of a closed differential form on a homology class. Symplectic and almost-complex structures, pseudoholomorphic maps and the homology-cohomology pairing require graduate geometry.
