Statement

Fix one of the smooth, piecewise-linear or topological categories. Let n\ge5, and let W be a compact (n+1)-dimensional manifold in that category with boundary the disjoint union of closed n-manifolds M and N. Suppose W,M,N are simply connected and both inclusions M\hookrightarrow W and N\hookrightarrow W are homotopy equivalences; such a cobordism is called an h-cobordism.

Then there is an isomorphism \Phi:M\times[0,1]\to W in the chosen category with \Phi(x,0)=x for every x\in M. Here an isomorphism means a diffeomorphism in the smooth category, a piecewise-linear homeomorphism in the piecewise-linear category, or a homeomorphism in the topological category. A homotopy equivalence is a continuous map admitting a continuous inverse up to homotopy.

Proof 1: Handle cancellation in the smooth, PL and TOP categories (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Morgan Theorem 1.1 and Sections 2 onward; Rourke-Sanderson Chapter 6, Lemma 6.16 and Theorem 6.17, pp.86-87; Solopko, Exact Morse functions in the category TOP (in dimension >5), Theorem 1 and Section 2, pp.697-700.).

Supporting sources: Rourke–Sanderson, Chapter 6, Theorem 6.17; Solopko, Theorem 1 and Section 2.

Write d=n+1\ge6. Morgan proves the smooth case using handle cancellation. Rourke–Sanderson Theorem 6.17 proves the PL case by eliminating low handles, applying Lemma 6.16 and dualizing. For TOP, Solopko's Theorem 1 gives an exact Morse function whose critical counts are determined by relative homology and its torsion. Both vanish for the given h-cobordism, so the no-critical-point product lemma applies. Solopko's locally flat disk argument supplies the dimension-six step; it uses TOP transversality and local straightening rather than global smoothability.

In each category, if a product isomorphism restricts to g on M\times\{0\}, precompose it with g^{-1}\times\operatorname{id} to make that restriction the pointwise identity.
