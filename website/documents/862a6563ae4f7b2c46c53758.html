Statement

Fix one of the smooth, piecewise-linear or topological categories. Let n\ge5, and let W be a compact (n+1)-dimensional manifold in that category with boundary the disjoint union of closed n-manifolds M and N. Suppose W,M,N are simply connected and both inclusions M\hookrightarrow W and N\hookrightarrow W are homotopy equivalences; such a cobordism is called an h-cobordism.

Then there is an isomorphism \Phi:M\times[0,1]\to W in the chosen category with \Phi(x,0)=x for every x\in M. Here an isomorphism means a diffeomorphism in the smooth category, a piecewise-linear homeomorphism in the piecewise-linear category, or a homeomorphism in the topological category. A homotopy equivalence is a continuous map admitting a continuous inverse up to homotopy.
