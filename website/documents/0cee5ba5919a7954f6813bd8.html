Statement

Let A be an n\times n matrix over a field. Choose increasing m-element index lists u=(u_1<\cdots<u_m) and v=(v_1<\cdots<v_m) from \{1,\ldots,n\}, with 1\le m\le n. For index sets I,J, let A^I_J be obtained by deleting rows I and columns J, retaining the original order of the remaining rows and columns; the empty determinant is 1.

Define the m\times m matrix B by

B_{ij}=\det A^{u\setminus\{u_i\}}_{v\setminus\{v_j\}}.

Then

\det B=\det A\,\bigl(\det A^u_v\bigr)^{m-1}.

For m=1, the factor with exponent zero is 1, including when its base is zero.
