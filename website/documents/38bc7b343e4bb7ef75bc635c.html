Statement

Let X be a real or complex Hausdorff locally convex topological vector space. For K\subseteq X, write \operatorname{ext}(K) for points of K that do not lie strictly between two distinct points of K, and \overline{\operatorname{co}}(A) for the smallest closed convex set containing A. If K is compact and convex, then K=\overline{\operatorname{co}}(\operatorname{ext}(K)).

Proof 1: Identify the closed convex hull of a compact convex set

Use the source’s general theorem: a compact subset K of a Hausdorff locally convex space satisfies \overline{\operatorname{co}}(\operatorname{ext}(K))=\overline{\operatorname{co}}(K). Here K is closed, since it is compact in a Hausdorff space, and is convex. Thus \overline{\operatorname{co}}(K)=K, giving the desired identity.
