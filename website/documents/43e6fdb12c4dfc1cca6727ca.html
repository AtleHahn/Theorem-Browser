Statement

Let D\subset\mathbb R^2 be the bounded region enclosed by a smooth convex closed oval, where smooth means C^\infty at every boundary point and D has nonempty interior. For (a,b)\ne(0,0) and a line ax+by=c meeting the interior, let

A(a,b,c)=\operatorname{area}\bigl(D\cap\{(x,y):ax+by\le c\}\bigr).

This area is not an algebraic function of the secant parameters a,b,c: there is no nonzero polynomial P\in\mathbb R[a,b,c,T] such that

P(a,b,c,A(a,b,c))=0

for all such secants. The line coefficients are allowed to vary freely, without imposing a normalization such as a^2+b^2=1.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Smooth planar boundaries, areas and polynomial relations are accessible from undergraduate calculus and algebra; the local definition of algebraic dependence makes the complete assertion understandable without algebraic geometry.
