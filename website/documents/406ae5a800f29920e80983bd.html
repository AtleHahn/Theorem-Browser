Proof 1: Interior angles of a Euclidean triangle sum to pi — proof

The geodesic-triangle formula says that the sum of interior angles is \pi plus the total Gaussian curvature enclosed by the triangle.

In the case of the plane (where the Gaussian curvature is 0 and geodesics are straight lines), we recover the familiar formula for the sum of angles in an ordinary triangle.

Level justification

The actual proof specializes a geodesic-triangle curvature integral formula on Riemannian surfaces. Gaussian curvature and its integrated angle relation require differential geometry, even though the Euclidean conclusion has elementary alternative proofs.
