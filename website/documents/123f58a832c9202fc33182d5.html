Statement

Let X be a compact Kähler manifold and let \omega,\omega' be Kähler forms with the same de Rham cohomology class. Then \omega-\omega'=i\partial\bar\partial f for some smooth complex function f on X.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Compact Kähler manifolds; Complex differential forms; Dolbeault operators; De Rham cohomology; Kähler forms; Smooth complex functions. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Subtract the equal de Rham classes

Let \alpha=\omega-\omega' be the difference of two Kähler forms which are in the same de Rham cohomology class [\omega]=[\omega']. In this case [\alpha]=[\omega]-[\omega']=0 in de Rham cohomology so the \partial\bar\partial-lemma applies. For bidegree (1,1), this lemma states that an exact differential form \alpha\in\Omega^{1,1}(X) has a \partial\bar\partial-potential given by a smooth function f\in C^\infty(X,\mathbb C):

\alpha=i\partial\bar\partial f.

Level justification

Subtract the cohomology classes and apply the ddbar lemma in bidegree (1,1). Kahler forms, de Rham classes and Dolbeault operators require substantial graduate geometry.
