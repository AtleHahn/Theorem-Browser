Statement

For a smooth real manifold M, a smooth bundle endomorphism A:TM\to TM and smooth vector fields X,Y, define N_A(X,Y)=-A^2[X,Y]+A([AX,Y]+[X,AY])-[AX,AY]. Its value at a point depends only on the values of X,Y at that point, and it defines a tensor of type (1,2).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Bundle endomorphisms, vector-field brackets and tensoriality on manifolds require substantial differential geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: The Nijenhuis expression is tensorial — source calculation

The individual expressions on the right depend on the choice of the smooth vector fields X and Y, but the left side actually depends only on the pointwise values of X and Y, which is why NA is a tensor. This is also clear from the component formula

{\displaystyle -(N_{A})_{ij}^{k}=A_{i}^{m}\partial _{m}A_{j}^{k}-A_{j}^{m}\partial _{m}A_{i}^{k}-A_{m}^{k}(\partial _{i}A_{j}^{m}-\partial _{j}A_{i}^{m}).}

Here A_i^k are the components of A in local coordinates and repeated indices are summed.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Interpret the component calculation for a tangent-bundle endomorphism and Lie brackets to establish tensoriality. Smooth manifolds, tangent bundles and tensor fields require graduate differential geometry, beyond merely multivariable derivatives.
