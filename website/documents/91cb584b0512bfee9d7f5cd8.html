Statement

For eight distinct points of \mathbb P^2(K) over an algebraically closed field, the vector space of homogeneous cubic forms in three variables vanishing at them has dimension at least two.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A projective point is a nonzero coordinate triple modulo nonzero scaling; homogeneous cubic vanishing is independent of the representative. Along with the algebraically closed field definition, these are short additions to undergraduate field algebra and polynomial vector spaces. The statement is a dimension bound, not an intersection-theory assertion.

Proof 1: Impose eight linear conditions on ten coefficients

Because the vector space of homogeneous polynomials P(x, y, z) of degree three in three variables x, y, z has dimension 10, the system of cubic curves passing through eight (different) points is parametrized by a vector space of dimension ≥ 2 (the vanishing of the polynomial at one point imposes a single linear condition).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Count ten homogeneous cubic monomials and impose eight linear evaluation equations. Projective points need only homogeneous-coordinate representatives; scaling preserves vanishing. This is a small extension of linear algebra over fields, not intersection theory.
