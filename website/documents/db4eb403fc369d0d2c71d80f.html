Statement

For a positive integer n, let \sigma(n) be the sum of its positive divisors, and let \varphi(n) count integers from 1 to n coprime to n. Suppose n=pq for distinct primes p,q, and put u=p+q. The unordered pair \{p,q\} is determined by any of (n,\sigma(n)), (u,\sigma(n)), or (u,\varphi(n)).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The claim concerns recovering two integers from finite arithmetic quantities.

Proof 1: Recover the missing sum or product — outline

Use \sigma(n)=n+1+u and \varphi(n)=n+1-u. Each indicated pair of data recovers the missing one of n,u. The primes are then the roots of x^2-ux+n.
