Statement

Let X be a real separable reflexive Banach space, with continuous dual X^*. Reflexive means that the canonical map X\to X^{**} is onto. Let T:X\to X^* be norm-continuous and map bounded sets to bounded sets. Suppose it is monotone, meaning \langle T(x)-T(y),x-y\rangle\ge0 for all x,y\in X, and coercive in the sense that \langle T(x),x\rangle/\|x\|\to+\infty as \|x\|\to\infty. Here \langle f,x\rangle=f(x) is the dual pairing. Then T is surjective: for every g\in X^* there exists u\in X with T(u)=g. No linearity of T is assumed.
