Statement

For a regular embedding X\hookrightarrow Y with ideal sheaf I, the normal cone \operatorname{Spec}_X(\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^n/I^{n+1}) is the vector bundle associated with the normal sheaf (I/I^2)^\vee.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Regular embeddings, relative Spec, associated graded ideal sheaves and normal bundles require substantial scheme theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Take spectra of the associated-graded isomorphism

The regular-sequence facts say that I/I^2 is locally free and that the natural map \operatorname{Sym}(I/I^2)\to\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^n/I^{n+1} is an isomorphism. Taking relative spectra identifies the normal cone with the vector bundle of the dual of I/I^2.

Level justification

Apply the conormal associated-graded isomorphism and take relative spectra. Ideal sheaves, regular embeddings, symmetric sheaf algebras and vector bundles require graduate algebraic geometry.
