Statement

Let G be a finite group and p a prime. Work over a splitting p-modular system (F,R,k): R is a complete discrete valuation ring with fraction field F of characteristic zero and residue field k of characteristic p, and F and k are splitting fields for G (all irreducible representations over them remain irreducible over algebraic closures). Let e be a primitive central idempotent of RG, defining the block eRG, and let \bar e be its image in kG. Suppose the block has defect zero, equivalently its modular block algebra \bar e kG is a full matrix algebra over k. Then there is exactly one isomorphism class of irreducible FG-modules on which e acts as the identity, and exactly one isomorphism class of irreducible kG-modules on which \bar e acts as the identity. In character terminology, the block contains exactly one ordinary irreducible character and exactly one irreducible Brauer character. A primitive central idempotent is a nonzero central element e satisfying e^2=e which cannot be written as a sum of two nonzero orthogonal central idempotents.
