Statement

For every positive integer k, infinitely many primitive Pythagorean triples have hypotenuse minus odd leg equal to 2k^2.

Proof 1: Source calculation — outline

In Euclid’s formula put n=k and let m>k run through integers coprime to k and of opposite parity. Then (m^2+k^2)-(m^2-k^2)=2k^2. These give the source’s infinite family; the routine infinitude of the admissible parameters is left implicit.
