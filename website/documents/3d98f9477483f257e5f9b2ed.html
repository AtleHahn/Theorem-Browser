Statement

The elementary Euclidean geometry axiomatized by Tarski is complete and decidable.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The assertion concerns completeness and decision procedures for a formal first-order geometric axiom system.

Proof 1: Transfer the real-field decision procedure to coordinates — outline

Tarski’s coordinatization identifies a line with a real closed field R and the plane with R^2. The coordinate interpretation translates the elementary geometric assertions to real-closed-field assertions. Completeness and decidability of that field theory therefore give completeness and a decision procedure for the elementary geometric theory.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The argument transfers completeness and decidability through a first-order coordinate interpretation into real closed fields. These logical, model-theoretic and field-theoretic prerequisites substantially exceed elementary geometric understanding.
