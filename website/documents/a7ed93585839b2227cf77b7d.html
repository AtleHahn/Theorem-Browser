Statement

Let K be a field, n\ge1 an integer, and let S_n act on K^n by permuting the standard basis. Every transposition is a pseudoreflection, meaning a nonidentity map with fixed subspace of codimension one. Consequently this linear group is generated by pseudoreflections.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group/ring theory and linear algebra supply the objects. The extra Sylow, normalizer, invariant-polynomial or pseudoreflection condition is explicitly defined in subgroup, coordinate or polynomial terms; no specialist extension or representation theory is required.

Proof 1: Use the generating transpositions — outline

The symmetric group is generated by transpositions (ij), which act by pseudoreflections on K^n.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A transposition fixes exactly the hyperplane x_i=x_j, and the transpositions generate S_n. Undergraduate linear algebra and permutation groups suffice over any field.
