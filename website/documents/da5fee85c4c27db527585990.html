Statement

The polynomial x^3-x splits into linear factors over \mathbb Q, but its derivative does not. Here the formal derivative sends each term a_kx^k with k\ge1 to ka_kx^{k-1} and sends constants to zero.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Splitting means factoring into linear polynomials with rational coefficients, and formal differentiation is explicitly an algebraic rule.

Proof 1: Compare rational and real linear factorizations

The factorization is x(x-1)(x+1), whereas the derivative factors over \mathbb R as 3(x-1/\sqrt3)(x+1/\sqrt3) and its roots are irrational.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Use the explicitly defined formal derivative and compare the displayed linear factors. Only polynomial arithmetic and irrational square roots are needed.
