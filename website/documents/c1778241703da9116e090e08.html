Statement

For positive real x,y,z and real t<0, x^t(x-y)(x-z)+y^t(y-z)(y-x)+z^t(z-x)(z-y)\ge0.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The claim is a real-power inequality on its positive-base domain.

Mathematical Field
High School Mathematics
Required topics
Positive real numbersNegative real powersInequalities
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Reverse power monotonicity in the second grouping — outline

By symmetry order x\ge y\ge z>0. The expression equals (y-z)[z^t(x-z)-y^t(x-y)]+x^t(x-y)(x-z). Since z^t\ge y^t>0 and x-z\ge x-y\ge0, the bracket is nonnegative, as is the remaining term. Thus the expression is nonnegative.
