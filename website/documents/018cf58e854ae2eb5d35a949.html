Statement

Over the two-element field \mathbb F_2, the subgroup E_2(\mathbb F_2) generated by elementary transvections is strictly larger than the commutator subgroup of \operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_2).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory ring/group theory and linear algebra supply polynomial coefficients, ideals, determinants, bases and matrix products. Elementary transvections are explicitly given elementary row-operation matrices, and division ring means every nonzero element has an inverse. These direct definitions suffice without specialist theory.

Proof 1: The S3 identification — outline

{\displaystyle \operatorname {Alt} (3)\cong [\operatorname {GL} _{2}(\mathbb {Z} /2\mathbb {Z} ),\operatorname {GL} _{2}(\mathbb {Z} /2\mathbb {Z} )]<\operatorname {E} _{2}(\mathbb {Z} /2\mathbb {Z} )=\operatorname {SL} _{2}(\mathbb {Z} /2\mathbb {Z} )=\operatorname {GL} _{2}(\mathbb {Z} /2\mathbb {Z} )\cong \operatorname {Sym} (3),}

Here Alt(3) and Sym(3) are the alternating and symmetric groups on three letters.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the stated identifications with S3 and its commutator subgroup. Finite matrix groups, elementary row operations and commutators are undergraduate algebra.
