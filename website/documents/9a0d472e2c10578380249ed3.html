Statement

For a rectangle ABCD in consecutive order, the diagonal satisfies AC^2=AB^2+AD^2.

Level
High school only

Proof 1: Place the point at a rectangle vertex

Use the British flag theorem as an external premise:

AP^2+CP^2=BP^2+DP^2

for a point P of the rectangle, including a vertex. Place P=A. Then AP=0, CP=AC, BP=AB, and DP=AD, giving AC^2=AB^2+AD^2.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Substitute a vertex for the point in the supplied squared-distance identity. School rectangle geometry and arithmetic suffice.
