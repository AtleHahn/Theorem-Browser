Statement

Let T:X\to Y be a bounded linear operator between Banach spaces, with closed range R(T). Let T':Y'\to X' be its transpose, T'y'=y'\circ T. Then

R(T')=N(T)^\perp=\{x'\in X':x'(x)=0\text{ for all }x\in N(T)\}.

The statement uses closed operator ranges and annihilators in Banach duals, beyond calculus and basic linear algebra.

Proof 1: Factor through the quotient and use open mapping

{\displaystyle T} factors as {\displaystyle X{\overset {p}{\to }}X/\operatorname {ker} T{\overset {T_{0}}{\to }}\operatorname {im} T{\overset {i}{\hookrightarrow }}Y}. Here p is the quotient map, T_0(x+N(T))=Tx, and i is inclusion. Dually, {\displaystyle T'} is

{\displaystyle Y'\to (\operatorname {im} T)'{\overset {T_{0}'}{\to }}(X/\operatorname {ker} T)'\to X'.}

Now, if {\displaystyle \operatorname {im} T} is closed, then it is Banach and so by the open mapping theorem, {\displaystyle T_{0}} is a topological isomorphism. It follows that {\displaystyle T_{0}'} is an isomorphism and then {\displaystyle \operatorname {im} (T')=\operatorname {ker} (T)^{\bot }}.

Here the first dual arrow is the restriction i^\prime, which is onto by Hahn–Banach, and the last is p^\prime, whose range is N(T)^\perp by the dual-of-quotient identification.
