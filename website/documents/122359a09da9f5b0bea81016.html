Statement

Let K/\mathbb Q be a number field of degree n, field discriminant D, and r_2 pairs of complex embeddings. Put M_K=\sqrt{|D|}(4/\pi)^{r_2}n!/n^n. The class group is generated by the classes of prime ideals of norm at most M_K.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The simultaneous use of number-field discriminants, embeddings, ideal norms and class groups requires substantial algebraic-number-theory preparation. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Factor a bounded-norm representative into small primes

Every class in the ideal class group of K contains an integral ideal of norm not exceeding Minkowski's bound M_K. The source states as a further consequence that the ideal class group is generated by the prime ideals of norm at most M_K; the prime-factorization deduction is not expanded.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Factor a Minkowski-bounded representative and use norm multiplicativity to bound its prime factors. Number-field class groups, ideal factorization and discriminants are substantial graduate algebra.

Unique prime ideal factorization and multiplicativity of ideal norms
Kind:

Theorem

Treatment:

Implicitly assumed

Statement used:

Integral ideals factor into prime ideals and the norm is multiplicative, with each nonzero prime ideal norm at least two.

Application in proof:

Bound each prime factor and express the class as their product.
