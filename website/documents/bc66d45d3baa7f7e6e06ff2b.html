Statement

Let K be any field and L a finite-dimensional Lie algebra over K. Thus L is a finite-dimensional K-vector space with a bilinear operation [\ ,\ ]:L\times L\to L satisfying [x,x]=0 and [x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0 for all x,y,z\in L. There exist a positive integer n and an injective K-linear map \rho:L\to M_n(K) such that \rho([x,y])=\rho(x)\rho(y)-\rho(y)\rho(x) for every x,y\in L, where M_n(K) denotes the n\times n matrices over K.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and introductory abstract algebra suffice. The additional concepts are a bilinear bracket satisfying two displayed identities and a linear injection preserving it. A student can use the matrix commutator as a basic example; this is a small extension within roughly 30 minutes, without Lie groups, manifolds or general representation theory. The cumulative burden is limited by spelling out the representation as one matrix identity.

  • The existence of faithful finite-dimensional matrix representations is a central structural theorem; retain the arbitrary-characteristic generalization explicitly noted in History.

Proof 1: Nilrepresentations and a positive-characteristic PBW quotient (external)

The following editorial argument completes the stated scope. A supporting source is Ado’s Theorem (Theorem 3 and proof, pp. 4–5; preparatory results §§1–2.).

Reviewer-supplied companion argument: if char K=p, choose a basis x_i of L. In the finite-dimensional algebra End_K(L), the sequence (ad x_i)^(p^j), j>=1, is linearly dependent. Choose a monic p-polynomial f_i(T) of degree d_i=p^m>=p, with no constant or linear term, annihilating ad x_i. In U(L), z_i=f_i(x_i) is central since ad(x_i^(p^j))=(ad x_i)^(p^j). PBW and leading terms show that the products z_1^a_1...z_r^a_r x_1^b_1...x_r^b_r, 0<=b_i<d_i, form a K-basis: their distinct leading PBW monomials give independence, and induction on degree gives spanning. Thus U(L) is free over K[z_1,...,z_r] on those bounded monomials. The quotient by (z_1,...,z_r) is finite dimensional and the images of all x_i are independent, since d_i>=2. Left multiplication on this quotient is a faithful representation of L (evaluate an operator at 1). The zero algebra embeds in M_1(K).
