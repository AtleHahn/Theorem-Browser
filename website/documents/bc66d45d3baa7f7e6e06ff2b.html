Statement

Let K be any field and L a finite-dimensional Lie algebra over K. Thus L is a finite-dimensional K-vector space with a bilinear operation [\ ,\ ]:L\times L\to L satisfying [x,x]=0 and [x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0 for all x,y,z\in L. There exist a positive integer n and an injective K-linear map \rho:L\to M_n(K) such that \rho([x,y])=\rho(x)\rho(y)-\rho(y)\rho(x) for every x,y\in L, where M_n(K) denotes the n\times n matrices over K.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and introductory abstract algebra suffice. The additional concepts are a bilinear bracket satisfying two displayed identities and a linear injection preserving it. A student can use the matrix commutator as a basic example; this is a small extension within roughly 30 minutes, without Lie groups, manifolds or general representation theory. The cumulative burden is limited by spelling out the representation as one matrix identity.
