Statement

A continuous map f:X\to Y between simply connected CW complexes that induces isomorphisms H_n(X;\mathbb Z)\to H_n(Y;\mathbb Z) for all n\ge0 is a homotopy equivalence.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns homology and homotopy of CW complexes.

Proof 1: Combining Hurewicz and Whitehead — outline

Use Whitehead’s theorem (weak homotopy equivalences between CW complexes are homotopy equivalences) and the Hurewicz theorem. Combining these gives the asserted homological corollary, as stated in the source.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Combine relative Hurewicz with Whitehead to turn homology isomorphisms into a homotopy equivalence. CW complexes, homology and homotopy groups require graduate algebraic topology.
