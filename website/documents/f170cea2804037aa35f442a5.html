Statement

Let f(t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nt^n have rational coefficients and positive radius of convergence. Suppose that the analytic function represented by this series near 0 is algebraic: it satisfies a nonzero polynomial equation P(t,f(t))=0 with P\in\mathbb Q[t,u].

Then there exists a nonzero integer A such that

A^{n+1}a_n\in\mathbb Z\qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq0.
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Power series are undergraduate analysis, and algebraic function is explicitly a polynomial relation. The arithmetic scaling conclusion needs only rational coefficients and integers.
