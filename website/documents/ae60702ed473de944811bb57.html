Statement

Let K be an algebraically closed field, let n\geq1, and let f_1,\ldots,f_n\in K[x_0,\ldots,x_n] be nonzero homogeneous polynomials of positive degrees d_1,\ldots,d_n. Their common zero set in projective space \mathbb P^n(K) is either infinite or has total intersection multiplicity d_1\cdots d_n.

For fixed degrees, a generic tuple of such polynomials has exactly d_1\cdots d_n distinct common projective zeros, each of multiplicity one. Here generic means that the property holds on a nonempty Zariski-open subset of the space of coefficient tuples: the exceptional set is contained in a proper set defined by polynomial equations in the coefficients.

A projective point is a nonzero coordinate vector modulo multiplication by a nonzero scalar. In the finite-intersection case, one may define the multiplicities by the U-resultant

R(U)=\operatorname{Res}(f_1,\ldots,f_n,U_0x_0+\cdots+U_nx_n).

Here \operatorname{Res} is the multivariate homogeneous resultant. The multiplicity of [a_0:\cdots:a_n] is the exponent of the corresponding linear factor a_0U_0+\cdots+a_nU_n in R(U).
