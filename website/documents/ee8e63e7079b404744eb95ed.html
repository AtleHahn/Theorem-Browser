Statement

Let f:G\to H be a surjective group homomorphism. Then G/\ker f\cong H, where \ker f=\{x\in G:f(x)=e_H\}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group/ring theory and linear algebra supply the objects. The extra Sylow, normalizer, invariant-polynomial or pseudoreflection condition is explicitly defined in subgroup, coordinate or polynomial terms; no specialist extension or representation theory is required.

Proof 1: Quotient description of a surjective group homomorphism — proof

The first isomorphism theorem for groups gives G/\ker f\cong\operatorname{im}f. Surjectivity identifies this image with H, giving the conclusion.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the group first isomorphism theorem and replace the image by the surjective codomain. Introductory abstract algebra suffices.
