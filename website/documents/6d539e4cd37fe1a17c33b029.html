Statement

Let X be a real random variable with \mathbb E X=0 and \mathbb E X^2<\infty. For a standard real Brownian motion (W_t)_{t\ge0} starting at 0, there is a stopping time \tau for its natural filtration such that W_\tau has the law of X and

\mathbb E\tau=\mathbb EX^2,\qquad \mathbb E\tau^2\le4\mathbb EX^4.

The last inequality is interpreted in [0,\infty]. Standard Brownian motion has continuous paths and independent increments with W_t-W_s normally distributed with mean 0 and variance t-s for t\ge s. Its natural filtration is \mathcal F_t=\sigma(W_s:0\le s\le t); a stopping time satisfies \{\tau\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t for each t. Equality of laws means equality of probabilities of every Borel set.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Continuous-time stochastic processes, adapted information and stopping times require cumulative graduate probability preparation.
