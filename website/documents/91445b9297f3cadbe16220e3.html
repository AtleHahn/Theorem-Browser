Statement

Let n be a positive integer. For a random vector Z in \mathbb R^n with density f, let h(Z)=-\int f\log f and N(Z)=(2\pi e)^{-1}\exp(2h(Z)/n). Let X,Y be independent random vectors with densities in L^p(\mathbb R^n) for some p>1. Suppose independent nondegenerate Gaussian vectors X',Y' with proportional covariance matrices are chosen so that h(X')=h(X) and h(Y')=h(Y). Then the entropy power inequality N(X+Y)\ge N(X)+N(Y) is equivalently h(X+Y)\ge h(X'+Y'). In particular the latter inequality holds, with a possible +\infty left-hand side.
