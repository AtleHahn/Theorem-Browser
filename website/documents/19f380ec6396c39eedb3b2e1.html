Statement

For every real \theta with \sin\theta\ne0, 1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta, where \cot\theta=\cos\theta/\sin\theta and \csc\theta=1/\sin\theta.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement is an identity between explicitly defined real trigonometric functions, within the high-school criterion.

Proof 1: Divide the unit-circle identity

The unit-circle equation gives \sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1. Dividing by \sin^2\theta\ne0 gives

\frac{\sin^2\theta}{\sin^2\theta} +\frac{\cos^2\theta}{\sin^2\theta} =\frac{1}{\sin^2\theta},

and hence the derived identity

1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta.
