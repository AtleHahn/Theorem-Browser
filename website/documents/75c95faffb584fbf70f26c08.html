Statement

For a Lie algebra L over a commutative ring K, filter U(L) by product length. There is a canonical graded algebra morphism S(L)\to\operatorname{gr}U(L) induced by the canonical map on L.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Universal enveloping algebras, their filtration and the associated graded map from the symmetric algebra require substantial Lie-algebra preparation. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Pass to the associated graded and kill commutators

Consider U(L) as a filtered algebra, equipped with the filtration given by specifying that v_1v_2\cdots v_n lies in filtered degree \le n. The map L\to U(L) of K-modules canonically extends to a map T(L)\to U(L) of algebras, where T(L) is the tensor algebra on L, by the universal property of tensor algebras. This is a filtered map equipping T(L) with the filtration putting L in degree one (actually, T(L) is graded). Then, passing to the associated graded, one gets a canonical morphism T(L)\to\operatorname{gr}U(L), which kills the elements vw-wv for v,w\in L, and hence descends to a canonical morphism S(L)\to\operatorname{gr}U(L).

Level justification

Extend through the tensor algebra, pass to associated graded and factor out commutators. Lie enveloping algebras, module tensor algebras and graded filtrations are substantial linked algebra prerequisites.
