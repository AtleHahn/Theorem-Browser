Statement

For propositions P,Q,R,S with classical truth values, if P\to Q, R\to S, and \neg Q\lor\neg S are all true, then \neg P\lor\neg R is true. Equivalently,

(((P\to Q)\land(R\to S))\land(\neg Q\lor\neg S))\to(\neg P\lor\neg R)

is a tautology.

High school only
