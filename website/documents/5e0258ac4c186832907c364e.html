Statement

Let X be a complex Banach space, let X^* and X^{**} be its continuous dual and bidual, and let J:X\to X^{**} be the canonical embedding J(x)(\varphi)=\varphi(x). The subspace J(X) is norm closed in X^{**}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Infinite-dimensional functional analysis and dual spaces are beyond the calculus/linear-algebra baseline.

Proof 1: Use completeness of the isometric image

The image of X in its bidual X^{**} is always norm closed, being the continuous image of a complete metric space under an isometry. Here the canonical embedding J is the isometry.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The final complete-subspace argument is undergraduate topology, but the full proof concerns the continuous dual, its operator norm, the bidual and the canonical evaluation embedding as an isometry. These linked functional-analytic constructions exceed a confident short extension of the baseline. Merely viewing J as an unnamed isometry would omit the actual objects of the proof.

Complete subspaces of metric spaces are closed
Kind:

Theorem

Treatment:

Statement used:

A complete subspace of a metric space is closed in that space.

Application in proof:

Conclude that the isometric image J(X) is norm closed.

Application level:

Undergraduate level
