Statement

Let R be a ring of subsets of \mathbb R containing all real intervals, and let \mu be a premeasure on R. Then there is a measure on the Borel sigma-algebra of \mathbb R agreeing with \mu on R intersected with that sigma-algebra.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

This consequence concerns measure extension and Borel generation.

Proof 1: An interval premeasure extends to the Borel sets — outline

By the premeasure extension theorem, extend \mu to the sigma-algebra generated by R. Since R contains all intervals, this sigma-algebra contains the Borel sigma-algebra. Restrict the extension to the Borel sets.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Extend the premeasure to the generated sigma algebra and restrict to Borel sets. Premeasures on rings, measure extension and generated sigma algebras form a substantial measure-theory framework not developed locally.
