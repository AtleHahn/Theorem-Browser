Statement

Let \Omega\subset\mathbb R^d be bounded and open, with d\ge1, and let f:\Omega\to\mathbb C be locally integrable. Suppose its distributional Laplacian is represented by a locally integrable function g, meaning

\int_\Omega f\,\Delta\varphi=\int_\Omega g\varphi\quad\text{for every }\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega).

Set s(x)=\overline{f(x)}/|f(x)| when f(x)\ne0 and s(x)=0 otherwise. Then for every nonnegative real \varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega),

\int_\Omega |f|\Delta\varphi\ge\int_\Omega\operatorname{Re}(s g)\varphi.

Here integrals are with respect to Lebesgue measure, local integrability means integrability on compact subsets, C_c^\infty denotes smooth functions with compact support, and \Delta=\sum_{j=1}^d\partial_j^2.
