Statement

For five real points (x_i,y_i), suppose the five row vectors (x_i^2,x_iy_i,y_i^2,x_i,y_i,1) are linearly independent in \mathbb R^6. Then the real coefficient vectors (A,B,C,D,E,F) satisfying the equations Ax_i^2+Bx_iy_i+Cy_i^2+Dx_i+Ey_i+F=0, 1\le i\le5 form a one-dimensional vector space. Consequently all their nonzero members are scalar multiples of one another.

Proof 1: Count the nullspace dimension of five independent equations

For each fixed point, the equation Ax_i^2+Bx_iy_i+Cy_i^2+Dx_i+Ey_i+F=0 is linear in the six coefficients. The five independent constraints cut the dimension from six to one; after scalar multiples are identified, a single coefficient class remains.
