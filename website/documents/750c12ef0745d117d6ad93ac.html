Statement

If PA is consistent, then PA does not prove its standard consistency sentence \operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})=\neg\operatorname{Prov}_{\mathrm{PA}}(\ulcorner\bot\urcorner), where \bot is an arithmetic contradiction.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Formal derivability and arithmetized provability are mathematical logic beyond the two elementary baselines.

Proof 1: Specialize the provability implication

Use Löb’s theorem: if PA proves \operatorname{Prov}_{\mathrm{PA}}(\ulcorner P\urcorner)\to P, then PA proves P. Substitute P=\bot. If PA proved its consistency sentence, it would prove \operatorname{Prov}_{\mathrm{PA}}(\ulcorner\bot\urcorner)\to\bot. Löb would then give a PA proof of \bot, contradicting consistency.
