Statement

Let X be a smooth projective surface over a field, and let K_X be its canonical divisor. Assume the following Reider criterion holds on X: for every nef divisor D with D^2>8, the system |K_X+D| is very ample unless a nonzero effective divisor E satisfies (D\cdot E,E^2)=(0,-1),(0,-2),(1,0),(1,-1),(2,0), or D\cdot E=3, D=3E, E^2=1. Then for every ample line bundle L and integer m\ge4, the system |K_X+mL| is very ample.

Proof 1: Exclude the exceptional intersection numbers

Set D=mL. Ampleness gives L^2\ge1 and L\cdot E\ge1 for each nonzero effective divisor E, so D^2\ge m^2>8 and D\cdot E\ge m>3 for every nonzero effective divisor E. Every exceptional case in the assumed very-ampleness criterion has D\cdot E\in\{0,1,2,3\}. None can occur, so the system is very ample.
