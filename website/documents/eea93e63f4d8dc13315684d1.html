Statement

Fix an integer base b\geq2, and let

S_b=\{p+b^k:p\text{ is a positive prime integer},\ k\geq1\text{ is an integer}\}.

The set S_b has positive lower asymptotic density:

\liminf_{x\to\infty}\frac{\#(S_b\cap[1,x])}{x}>0.

Equivalently, there are constants c_b>0 and x_b>0, depending only on b, such that for every real x\geq x_b, at least c_bx distinct positive integers at most x can be written as p+b^k.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Elementary number theory and undergraduate limits suffice; lower asymptotic density is explicitly translated into a uniform eventual counting bound.
