Statement

For every positive integer N, there are 2N^3 distinct planar points and N^3 distinct lines with exactly N^4 incidences.

Level
High school only
Level justification

This is an existence claim for finite Euclidean configurations with explicit integer counts.

Proof 1: Rectangular integer lattice construction

Take

P=\{(a,b)\in\mathbb Z^2:1\le a\le N,\ 1\le b\le2N^2\},

and take the lines y=mx+b for integers 1\le m\le N, 1\le b\le N^2. There are 2N^3 points and N^3 lines. Each line contains exactly one point of P for every integer abscissa 1,\ldots,N, hence exactly N points. The total is N^4 incidences.
