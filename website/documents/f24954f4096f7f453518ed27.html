Statement

Let X be a topological space, \mathcal F a sheaf of abelian groups on X, and \mathcal U an open cover. Suppose every nonempty finite intersection V=U_1\cap\cdots\cap U_p of members of \mathcal U satisfies H^i(V,\mathcal F|_V)=0 for every i\ge1. Then for every integer q\ge0 there is a canonical isomorphism

\check H^q(\mathcal U,\mathcal F)\cong H^q(X,\mathcal F).

Here a sheaf assigns abelian groups of sections to open sets, with compatible restriction maps and unique gluing of sections that agree on overlaps. Sheaf cohomology H^q(X,\mathcal F) is the cohomology of the global-sections complex of an injective resolution of \mathcal F. The group \check H^q(\mathcal U,\mathcal F) is the cohomology of the Čech complex: its degree-q cochains assign a section on each ordered (q+1)-fold intersection, and its differential is the alternating sum of restrictions after omitting one set.
