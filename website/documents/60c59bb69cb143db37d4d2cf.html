Statement

For even n≥2, choose one edge of the complete graph on n vertices. The perfect matchings containing this edge form a pairwise edge-intersecting family of size (n-3)!!. A perfect matching pairs all vertices, and (-1)!!=1.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement defines the pairing objects and the count is a finite product.

Proof 1: Fix an edge and match the remaining vertices — outline

Fix the edge and choose all ways of matching the remaining n−2 vertices. There are (n-3)!! such matchings, using the formula that a complete graph on an even number m of vertices has (m-1)!! perfect matchings.

Remove the fixed edge endpoints and apply the supplied pairing count to the remaining even-sized set. All selected matchings share that edge. School finite counting suffices.
