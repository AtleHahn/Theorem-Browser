Statement

Let G be a finite group with a generalized quaternion Sylow 2-subgroup and no nontrivial normal subgroup of odd order. Then G is not simple.

Level

Proof 1: The center of order two — outline

By the Brauer–Suzuki theorem, G has a center of order 2. In particular, such a group cannot be simple.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the center theorem and note its order-two center is proper and normal. Sylow theory and generalized quaternion groups are not defined locally and cannot confidently be assumed from introductory abstract algebra; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally while recognizing the final normal-subgroup inference is elementary.
