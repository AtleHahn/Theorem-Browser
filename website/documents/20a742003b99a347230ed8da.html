Statement

In each of Gentzen’s first-order sequent calculi LK (classical logic) and LJ (intuitionistic logic), every sequent that has a finite derivation has a finite derivation with the same endsequent and no applications of the cut rule.

A sequent \Gamma\vdash\Delta consists of finite lists of first-order formulas; \Gamma gives the assumptions and \Delta the conclusions. In LJ, \Delta has at most one formula. The cut rule has the form

\frac{\Gamma\vdash\Delta,A\qquad A,\Pi\vdash\Lambda}{\Gamma,\Pi\vdash\Delta,\Lambda},

with the single-conclusion restriction in LJ. A derivation without this rule is called cut-free.
