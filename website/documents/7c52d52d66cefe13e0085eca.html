Proof 1: Semigroup forgetful functor (outline)

The forgetful functor from semigroups to sets is monadic, but does not preserve arbitrary coequalizers. These two source-stated facts give the required counterexample.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the two supplied categorical properties of the semigroup forgetful functor. Monads, coequalizers and preservation of colimits are advanced category-theoretic prerequisites.
