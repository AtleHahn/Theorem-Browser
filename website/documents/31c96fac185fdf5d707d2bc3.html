Statement

There exist a prime p and an uncountable abelian group A such that:

  • Every element of A has finite order equal to a power of p.
  • Every nonzero a\in A has finite p-height: for some integer h\geq0, a\in p^hA but a\notin p^{h+1}A, where p^hA=\{p^hb:b\in A\}.
  • A is not isomorphic to a direct sum of cyclic groups.

A direct sum here consists of tuples from the cyclic groups with only finitely many nonzero coordinates and coordinatewise addition.

Level justification

Infinite p-groups, divisibility-height filtrations and arbitrary direct sums form a cumulative structural prerequisite burden beyond introductory group theory and the conservative reading allowance.
