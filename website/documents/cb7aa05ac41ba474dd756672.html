Statement

Let g be a smooth pseudo-Riemannian metric on a coordinate domain of dimension n, with Levi-Civita Christoffel symbols \Gamma^k_{ij}=\rho_i\delta^k_j+\rho_j\delta^k_i for smooth real functions \rho_i. Assume \partial_i\rho_j=\partial_j\rho_i for all i,j. Then the symmetric covariant tensor with components A_{jk}=\partial_j\rho_k-\rho_j\rho_k is a Codazzi tensor: \nabla_iA_{jk}=\nabla_jA_{ik}. Here all indices range from 1 to n, \delta is the Kronecker delta, \partial_i is coordinate differentiation and \nabla is the Levi-Civita covariant derivative.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Covariant differentiation, Christoffel symbols and Codazzi tensors on a pseudo-Riemannian manifold require a coordinated tensor-calculus framework beyond a half-hour introduction. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Covariant differentiation using the Christoffel form — outline

It is direct to verify that A_{jk}=\partial_j\rho_k-\rho_j\rho_k is a (locally defined) Codazzi tensor, using the given form of the Christoffel symbols and the assumed identity \partial_i\rho_j=\partial_j\rho_i.
