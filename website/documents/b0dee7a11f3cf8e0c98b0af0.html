Statement

Fix T>0. On classical Wiener space \Omega=C_0([0,T];\mathbb R) with Wiener measure, let B_t(\omega)=\omega(t) be standard Brownian motion and let \mathcal F_t be its completed natural filtration. For every F\in\mathbb D^{1,2},

F=\mathbb E F+\int_0^T\mathbb E[D_tF\mid\mathcal F_t]\,dB_t

almost surely and in L^2. The integrand is taken in its predictable version, and the integral is the Itô integral. The Malliavin Sobolev domain \mathbb D^{1,2} is the closure of smooth cylindrical functionals F=f(B_{t_1},\ldots,B_{t_m}), where f is smooth and bounded with bounded derivatives and 0\le t_j\le T, under

\|F\|_{1,2}^2=\mathbb E|F|^2+\mathbb E\int_0^T|D_tF|^2\,dt.

On such cylindrical functionals D_tF=\sum_{j=1}^m(\partial_jf)(B_{t_1},\ldots,B_{t_m})\mathbf1_{[0,t_j]}(t), and D on \mathbb D^{1,2} is the closed extension in this norm. Thus Malliavin differentiability here includes square-integrability of the closed derivative, not merely pointwise directional differentiability.
