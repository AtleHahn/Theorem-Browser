Statement

Let M be a closed connected Riemannian manifold with nonpositive sectional curvature. Its fundamental group G=\pi_1(M) admits a quadratic isoperimetric inequality: for any finite presentation P of G, there is a constant C with \delta_P(n)\le Cn^2+Cn+C. For a finite presentation P=\langle X\mid R\rangle, define \operatorname{Area}_P(w) for a freely reduced word representing the identity as the least number of conjugates of elements of R^{\pm1} whose product is w in the free group on X. Its Dehn function is \delta_P(n)=\max\{\operatorname{Area}_P(w):w=1,\ |w|\le n\}. An isoperimetric function is a nondecreasing upper bound for these areas in terms of word length. For nondecreasing functions f,g:\mathbb N\to[0,\infty), write f\preceq g if there is an integer C\ge1 such that f(n)\le Cg(Cn+C)+Cn+C for all n\ge0, and f\approx g if both dominations hold.

Proof 1: Apply the CAT(0) group isoperimetric theorem

The universal cover of a closed nonpositively curved Riemannian manifold is a CAT(0) space, and its fundamental group acts by deck isometries properly discontinuously and cocompactly. Apply the theorem that a group with such an action has a quadratic isoperimetric inequality. This gives the stated fundamental-group case.
