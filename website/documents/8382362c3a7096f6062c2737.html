Statement

Let G be a finite simple maximal plane graph with n\ge4 vertices, so every face is triangular. Then G has at least four vertices of degree at most five.

Level

Proof 1: Sum the vertex deficiencies

By Euler's formula for planar graphs, G has 3n-6 edges; equivalently, if one defines the deficiency of a vertex v in G to be 6-\deg(v), the sum of the deficiencies is 12. Since G has at least four vertices and all faces of G are triangles, it follows that every vertex in G has degree at least three. Therefore each vertex in G has deficiency at most three, so there are at least four vertices with positive deficiency, that is, degree at most five.

Level justification

The total deficiency is twelve and each positive deficiency is at most three, forcing at least four positive terms. School finite graph counting suffices.
