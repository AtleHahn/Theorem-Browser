Statement

Let X be a Stein manifold and F a coherent sheaf of \mathcal O_X-modules, where \mathcal O_X is the sheaf of holomorphic functions. For every x\in X, the germs at x of global sections in H^0(X,F) generate the stalk F_x as an \mathcal O_{X,x}-module. A stalk consists of local sections identified when they agree near x. An \mathcal O_X-module sheaf is coherent if it is locally finitely generated and on every open subset the kernel of every morphism from a finite-rank free module sheaf into its restriction is locally finitely generated. A Stein manifold is a complex manifold admitting a proper holomorphic embedding into some \mathbb C^N; proper means that inverse images of compact sets are compact.
