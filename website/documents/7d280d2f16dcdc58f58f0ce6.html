Statement

Let p(n) be the number of partitions of a nonnegative integer n into positive integer summands, with the order of the summands ignored. For every integer k\ge0,

\begin{aligned} p(5k+4)&\equiv0\pmod5,\\ p(7k+5)&\equiv0\pmod7,\\ p(11k+6)&\equiv0\pmod{11}. \end{aligned}
Level
High school only
Level justification

Partitions are defined by elementary counting and the conclusions use only integer divisibility.
