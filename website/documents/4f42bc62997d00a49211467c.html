Statement

If a smooth map between finite-dimensional smooth manifolds has injective (respectively surjective) differential at a point p, then its differential is injective (respectively surjective) throughout some neighborhood of p. In particular its rank is constant there.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns derivatives between tangent spaces of smooth manifolds.

Proof 1: Full derivative rank persists locally — outline

The rank function is lower semicontinuous. Injectivity or surjectivity means the rank already reaches its maximal possible value, so this rank persists locally and cannot increase further.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply lower semicontinuity at maximal rank, but the full statement concerns smooth manifolds and their tangent differentials. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because manifold charts, tangent spaces and differential maps are several linked additions beyond multivariable calculus.
