Statement

Let P be a nonconstant square-free real polynomial. Form its nonzero Sturm chain P_0=P, P_1=P', P_{i+1}=-\operatorname{rem}(P_{i-1},P_i), stopping before the first zero remainder. Let V(x) count sign changes in its values after zeros are omitted. For real a<b, the number of real roots in (a,b] is V(a)-V(b). At +\infty use leading-coefficient signs, and at -\infty reverse them for odd-degree terms; the same count holds for unbounded intervals.

Proof 1: Local sign-variation changes along the negative-remainder chain — outline

When the value of x increases from a to b, it may pass through a zero of some P_i (i>0); when this occurs, the number of sign variations of (P_{i-1},P_i,P_{i+1}) does not change. Here the negative-remainder relation gives opposite signs to the adjacent terms; square-freeness ensures that adjacent terms have no common root and that the final term is a nonzero constant.

When x passes through a root of P_0=P, the number of sign variations of (P_0,P_1) decreases from 1 to 0. These are the only values of x where some sign may change. Omitting the zero at a root of P gives the value on its right, accounting for the half-open interval (a,b]. At infinity the signs are those of the leading terms.
