Statement

Let K be an algebraically closed field, n\ge1 an integer, and A\in K^{n\times n}. Suppose Q is invertible, \Lambda is diagonal, and A=Q\Lambda Q^{-1}. Then A^m=Q\Lambda^mQ^{-1} for every positive integer m.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and introductory abstract algebra supply determinants, matrices, field roots, ring operations and divisibility. The displayed assertion uses these directly; no graduate concepts are needed to understand it.

Proof 1: Repeat cancellation of adjacent inverse factors — outline

For the square, A^2=(Q\Lambda Q^{-1})(Q\Lambda Q^{-1})=Q\Lambda(Q^{-1}Q)\Lambda Q^{-1}=Q\Lambda^2Q^{-1}. Repeating this cancellation gives A^m=Q\Lambda^mQ^{-1}.

Level justification

Associativity and cancellation of adjacent inverse matrices give the power formula by induction. Linear algebra over a field suffices; algebraic closure is unused.
