Statement

Fix a>0. Suppose g is continuous on every upper semicircle C_R=\{Re^{i\theta}:0\le\theta\le\pi\} for all sufficiently large R, and M_R=\max_{C_R}|g|\to0. For f(z)=e^{iaz}g(z), with C_R oriented from R to -R, \lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{C_R}f(z)\,dz=0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex differentiability and Laurent expansions extend calculus and power series; contour integrals on the given semicircles are ordinary parametrized integrals. The displayed identities use no abstract complex geometry or residue theory.

Proof 1: Squeezing by the Jordan estimate

Jordan’s lemma gives \left|\int_{C_R}f(z)\,dz\right|\le(\pi/a)M_R. The right side tends to 0, so the integral tends to 0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the explicit Jordan bound and squeeze the complex integral modulus to zero. Undergraduate complex integration and real limits suffice.
