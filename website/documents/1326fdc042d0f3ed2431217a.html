Statement

Let I be a real interval and f:I\to\mathbb R be concave, meaning f(tx+(1-t)y)\geq tf(x)+(1-t)f(y) for x,y\in I and 0\leq t\leq1. Let n\geq1, and let x_1\geq\cdots\geq x_n and y_1\geq\cdots\geq y_n lie in I, with \sum_{j=1}^i x_j\geq\sum_{j=1}^i y_j for 1\leq i<n. If the total sums of the two lists are equal, then \sum_{i=1}^n f(x_i)\leq\sum_{i=1}^n f(y_i).

Level
High school only
Level justification

Concavity and majorization are explicitly finite real inequalities on sorted lists. Their names add no prerequisite.

Proof 1: Apply the convex inequality to the negative function

The function -f is convex. Apply Karamata’s convex inequality to -f and the given lists: \sum_i(-f(x_i))\geq\sum_i(-f(y_i)). Multiplying by -1 reverses the inequality and gives the claim.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Negate the function to apply the given convex inequality, then reverse signs. Elementary finite real inequalities suffice.
