Statement

Let X be a compact absolute neighborhood retract and f:X\to X homotopic to the identity. Its rational homology is finite-dimensional and zero in sufficiently high degrees; define \Lambda_f by the alternating sum of the traces of f_*. Then \Lambda_f=\chi(X), with Euler characteristic defined by the alternating Betti sum.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Absolute neighborhood retracts and their homology are general topological objects outside calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Finite simplicial homotopy type of compact ANRs (outline)

Use the stated theorem that a compact absolute neighborhood retract is homotopy equivalent to a finite simplicial complex. Homotopy invariance transfers the finiteness and vanishing of homology to X. Since f is homotopic to the identity, its induced maps on homology are identity maps. Their traces are the Betti numbers, whose alternating sum is \chi(X).

Level justification

Transfer finite homology through the finite-complex homotopy type and compute traces of identity maps. ANRs, homotopy equivalence and singular homology are substantial algebraic-topology prerequisites.
