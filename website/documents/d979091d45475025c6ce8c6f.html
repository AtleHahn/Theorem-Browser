Statement

Fix distinct points A,B in the Euclidean plane, an integer n\ge3, and a choice of left or right. For each point C not on the line AB, construct regular n-gons on the directed edges A\to C and C\to B, both lying on the chosen side of their respective directed edges. On the circumcircle of the first polygon let D_1 be the point diametrically opposite C, and on that of the second let D_2 be the point diametrically opposite C. Then the midpoint M of D_1D_2 depends only on A,B,n and the chosen side, and not on C.

For n=4, D_1,D_2 are the square vertices opposite C. If S is the perpendicular projection of M onto the line AB, then AS=BS=MS.
