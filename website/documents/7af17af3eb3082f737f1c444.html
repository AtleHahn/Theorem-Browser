Statement

If W\subseteq[0,1] is closed, 1\in W, and w\in W implies both 1-w\in W and w/2\in W, then W=[0,1].

Proof 1: Generate dense dyadic subdivisions — outline

From the four properties 1\in W, closure under w\mapsto1-w, closedness of W, and closure under w\mapsto w/2, it follows that W=[0,1].

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Halving and complement also give (1+w)/2, so induction generates every dyadic subdivision. Density and closedness then fill the interval. This is elementary real analysis.
