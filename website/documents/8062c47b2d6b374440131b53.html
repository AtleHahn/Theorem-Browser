Statement

Suppose a symmetric block design exists with parameters (v,b,r,k,\lambda): there are v points and b=v blocks, each block is a k-element subset of the point set, each point lies in r blocks, and each pair of distinct points lies in exactly \lambda blocks. The parameters are positive integers and k<v. Then:

  • If v is even, k-\lambda is the square of an integer.
  • If v is odd, there exist integers x,y,z, not all zero, such that
x^2-(k-\lambda)y^2-(-1)^{(v-1)/2}\lambda z^2=0.

The two alternatives give necessary conditions for existence of the design.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All combinatorial objects are locally defined as finite subsets with counting conditions, and the conclusion uses integer arithmetic and parity only. No design theory or quadratic-form theory is required to understand the statement.
