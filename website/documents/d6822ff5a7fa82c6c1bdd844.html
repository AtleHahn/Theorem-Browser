Statement

Let X and Y be CW complexes, and let f:X\to Y be continuous. Suppose that f induces a bijection on path components,

f_*:\pi_0(X)\longrightarrow\pi_0(Y),

and, for every x\in X and every integer n\geq1, an isomorphism

f_*:\pi_n(X,x)\longrightarrow\pi_n(Y,f(x)).

Then f is a homotopy equivalence: there is a continuous map g:Y\to X with g\circ f\simeq\operatorname{id}_X and f\circ g\simeq\operatorname{id}_Y.

Here \pi_0 is the set of path components and \pi_n is the group of based homotopy classes of based maps from the n-sphere. The induced map is composition with f; abstract isomorphisms of the groups alone do not suffice. A CW complex is a space built by attaching cells with the closure-finite and weak-topology conditions. The notation u\simeq v means that u and v are joined by a continuous homotopy.
