Statement

For integers 1\le r\le n and a fixed element x of an n-element set, the family of all r-element subsets containing x is pairwise intersecting and has size \binom{n-1}{r-1}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The construction and count use only finite choices and a common element.

Proof 1: Choose the common element and count the remainder

Choose a fixed element x and all r-element subsets containing it. Every two intersect at x. After choosing x, one chooses the other r-1 elements from the remaining n-1, giving \binom{n-1}{r-1} sets.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Every pair shares the chosen element; count choices of the other r-1 elements. Elementary binomial counting suffices.
