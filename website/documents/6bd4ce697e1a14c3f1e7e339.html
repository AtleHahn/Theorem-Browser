Statement

Let u,v,w be positive integers with u^2+v^2=w^2. Then (u-v)^2,w^2,(u+v)^2 are an arithmetic progression with common difference 2uv, which is four times the triangle’s area uv/2.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Only elementary arithmetic, polynomial equations and Euclidean right-triangle geometry are needed.

Proof 1: A Pythagorean triangle gives three equally spaced squares — outline

The source recipe takes the difference of the legs, the hypotenuse and the sum of the legs, and squares them. It identifies the resulting congruum as four times the area of the Pythagorean triangle.
