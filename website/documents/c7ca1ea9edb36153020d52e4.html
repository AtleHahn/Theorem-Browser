Statement

Let G\le S_n be a primitive permutation group on \{1,\ldots,n\}. This means G acts transitively and preserves no partition into equally sized nonempty blocks other than the partition into singletons and the one-block partition. Suppose G contains a permutation that cyclically permutes exactly p points and fixes all other points, where p is prime and p<n-2. Then G=A_n or G=S_n, where S_n is the group of all permutations and A_n the subgroup of even permutations.
