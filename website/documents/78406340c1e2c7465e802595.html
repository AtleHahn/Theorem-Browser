Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate triangle, and let D,E,F lie on BC,CA,AB, respectively, with D\ne B, E\ne C and F\ne A. Put x=CD/BD, y=AE/CE, z=BF/AF, and let P=AD\cap BE, Q=BE\cap CF, R=CF\cap AD. Then [PQR]/[ABC]=(xyz-1)^2/((xy+y+1)(yz+z+1)(zx+x+1)), where brackets denote unsigned area and concurrent cevians give area zero.

{\displaystyle {\frac {AF}{FB}}\times {\frac {BC}{CD}}\times {\frac {DR}{RA}}=1}.

Then {\displaystyle {\frac {DR}{RA}}={\frac {BF}{FA}}\times {\frac {CD}{CB}}={\frac {zx}{x+1}}}. Thus the area of triangle {\displaystyle ARC} is

{\displaystyle S_{ARC}={\frac {AR}{AD}}S_{ADC}={\frac {AR}{AD}}\times {\frac {DC}{BC}}S_{ABC}={\frac {x}{zx+x+1}}}

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}S_{PQR}&=S_{ABC}-S_{ARC}-S_{BPA}-S_{CQB}\\&=1-{\frac {x}{zx+x+1}}-{\frac {y}{xy+y+1}}-{\frac {z}{yz+z+1}}\\&={\frac {(xyz-1)^{2}}{(xz+x+1)(yx+y+1)(zy+z+1)}}.\end{aligned}}}
