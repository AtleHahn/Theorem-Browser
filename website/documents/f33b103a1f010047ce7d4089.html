Statement

Let (A_i)_{i\in I} be a finite indexed family of sets, allowing repetitions and allowing individual sets to be infinite. There exist pairwise distinct representatives a_i\in A_i for all i\in I if and only if, for every J\subseteq I, the union \bigcup_{i\in J}A_i contains at least |J| distinct elements. Equivalently, there is an injective map f:I\to\bigcup_{i\in I}A_i with f(i)\in A_i exactly when

\left|\bigcup_{i\in J} A_i\right|\ge |J|\qquad(J\subseteq I).
Level
High school only
Level justification

Only elementary set membership and finite counting are required. Even infinite member sets are tested by containing a specified finite number of distinct elements; no infinite-cardinal arithmetic is needed.
