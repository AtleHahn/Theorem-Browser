Statement

Write \mathrm{Hotc} for the homotopy category of pointed connected CW complexes, with pointed homotopy classes of maps as morphisms. Let F,G:\mathrm{Hotc}^{op}\to\mathrm{Set} have chosen natural isomorphisms F\cong\operatorname{Hom}(-,C) and G\cong\operatorname{Hom}(-,D). Every natural transformation \eta:F\to G corresponds to a unique morphism h:C\to D inducing it by postcomposition under these isomorphisms. The correspondence preserves identities and composition.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves homotopy category; contravariant representable functors; natural transformations. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Apply Yoneda to the chosen representing isomorphisms

The representing object C can be shown to depend functorially on F: any natural transformation from F to another represented functor G necessarily induces a map of the representing objects. This is a consequence of Yoneda's lemma, in the form

\operatorname{Nat}(\operatorname{Hom}(-,C),\operatorname{Hom}(-,D))\cong\operatorname{Hom}(C,D),

naturally and compatibly with composition. Apply this correspondence under the chosen representing isomorphisms.
