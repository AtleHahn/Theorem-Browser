Statement

Let X be a real or complex Banach space and let A\subseteq X. Equip X with its weak topology, the coarsest topology making every continuous linear functional \ell:X\to\mathbb R (respectively \mathbb C) continuous. The following are equivalent:

  1. Every sequence in A has a subsequence converging weakly to an element of X.
  2. Every sequence in A has a weak cluster point in X: a point every weak neighborhood of which contains terms with arbitrarily large indices.
  3. The weak closure of A is compact in the weak topology, meaning that each weakly open cover has a finite subcover.

Weak convergence x_j\to x means \ell(x_j)\to\ell(x) for every continuous linear functional \ell. Limits and cluster points in the first two conditions are allowed to lie outside A.
