The generalized continuum hypothesis cannot first fail at a singular cardinal of uncountable cofinality. This shows that independence of cardinal arithmetic still leaves deep structural restrictions on possible universes of sets.

Statement

Let \kappa be a singular cardinal with uncountable cofinality. If 2^\lambda=\lambda^+ for every infinite cardinal \lambda<\kappa, then 2^\kappa=\kappa^+. Here 2^\lambda is the cardinality of the power set of a set of size \lambda, \lambda^+ is the least larger cardinal, and \operatorname{cf}(\kappa) is the least cardinality of an unbounded subset of the initial ordinal \kappa. Singular means \operatorname{cf}(\kappa)<\kappa, and uncountable cofinality means \operatorname{cf}(\kappa)>\aleph_0.
