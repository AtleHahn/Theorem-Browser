Statement

Every finite maximal outerplanar graph is chordal: it has no induced cycle of length at least four.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Finite graph terminology is a short set-based extension: outerplanar admits a crossing-free drawing with all vertices on the outer face, maximal forbids adding an edge, and chordal is explicitly defined by induced cycles. These concrete definitions suffice.

Proof 1: Maximal outerplanar graphs are chordal — outline

For graphs with at least three vertices, use the source-stated inclusion of maximal outerplanar graphs in the class of 2-trees. The source also states that k-trees are chordal. Applying the latter fact with k=2 gives the conclusion in this case; the smaller graphs satisfy the conclusion trivially.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Combine the two class inclusions and handle small graphs separately. Introductory undergraduate graph theory suffices to learn outerplanarity, recursively built 2-trees and induced cycles; no structural proof of the imported results is required.
