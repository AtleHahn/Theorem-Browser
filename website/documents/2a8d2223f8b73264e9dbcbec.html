Statement

Let U,V,W be complete algebraic varieties over an algebraically closed field k, and choose points u\in U,v\in V,w\in W. If a line bundle L on U\times_kV\times_kW restricts to a trivial line bundle on each of

U\times_kV\times\{w\},\quad U\times\{v\}\times_kW,\quad\{u\}\times_kV\times_kW,

then L is trivial on the whole product. A variety means an integral separated scheme of finite type over k; complete means proper over k. A line bundle is equivalently a locally free sheaf of modules of rank one, and trivial means isomorphic to the structure sheaf as a module. Restriction is pullback along the indicated inclusions.
