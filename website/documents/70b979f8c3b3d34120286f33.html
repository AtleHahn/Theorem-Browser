Statement

Projections. Let \mathcal M be a von Neumann algebra. For projections E,F\in\mathcal M, write E\sim F if a partial isometry U\in\mathcal M satisfies U^*U=E and UU^*=F. Write E\precsim F if such a U instead satisfies UU^*\le F, where the order is inclusion of the projection ranges. Then

E\precsim F\text{ and }F\precsim E\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E\sim F.

Representations. Let \rho:A\to\mathcal B(H) and \sigma:A\to\mathcal B(G) be *-representations of a C^*-algebra A on Hilbert spaces. A subrepresentation is the restriction to the range of an orthogonal projection commuting with every represented operator. If each of \rho and \sigma is unitarily equivalent to a subrepresentation of the other, then they are unitarily equivalent: there is a unitary U:G\to H such that

\rho(a)U=U\sigma(a)\qquad(a\in A).
