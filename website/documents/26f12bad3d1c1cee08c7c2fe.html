Statement

For a number field K, there is a unique abelian extension L/K in a fixed algebraic closure, unramified at all places, whose Galois group is isomorphic to the ideal class group of K.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Ramification at all places and the ideal class group require substantial algebraic-number-theory preparation. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Specialize to modulus one

Apply the Takagi existence correspondence with modulus \mathfrak m=1 and subgroup H=P_1. Here I_1 is the group of nonzero fractional ideals and P_1 is its subgroup of principal ideals, so the generalized ideal class group I_1/P_1 is the ordinary ideal class group. The unramified specialization of the correspondence gives the claimed extension, called the Hilbert class field.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the modulus-one case of Takagi correspondence and identify fractional ideals modulo principal ideals. Number fields, ramification, class groups and class field correspondence require graduate number theory.
