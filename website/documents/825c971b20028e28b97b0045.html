Statement

There exist a prime p, a number field K and a noncyclotomic \mathbb Z_p-extension L/K with positive Iwasawa \mu-invariant.

A \mathbb Z_p-extension is an infinite Galois extension whose Galois group, with its profinite topology, is isomorphic to the additive group \mathbb Z_p=\varprojlim_n\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z. For each n\geq0 it has a unique intermediate field K_n of degree p^n over K. The invariant \mu is the nonnegative integer in the eventual formula

v_p(h(K_n))=\mu p^n+\lambda n+\nu,

valid for all sufficiently large n, where \lambda\geq0 and \nu are integers. Here h is the order of the ideal class group (nonzero fractional ideals of the ring of integers modulo principal ideals), and v_p is the exponent of p in that order. Noncyclotomic means that L is not the cyclotomic \mathbb Z_p-extension of K, namely the unique such extension contained in \bigcup_{m\geq1}K(\zeta_{p^m}).
