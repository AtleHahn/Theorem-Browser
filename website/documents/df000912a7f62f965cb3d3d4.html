Statement

For positive real numbers p,q, \sqrt{pq}\le(p+q)/2.

Level

Proof 1: Compare the altitude with the parallel radius (outline)

Use a semicircle of diameter p+q with AD=p and DB=q, with D between A and B, and let the perpendicular at D meet it at C. The geometric mean theorem gives DC=\sqrt{pq}. The radius is (p+q)/2. Draw a radius parallel to DC; the height of the circle point C above its diameter cannot exceed that radius. Thus DC\le(p+q)/2, as required.

Level justification

Identify the perpendicular height as the geometric mean and bound it by the radius. School circle geometry and the stated geometric-mean identity suffice.
