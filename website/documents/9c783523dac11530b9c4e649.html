Statement

Let G be a connected noncompact real semisimple Lie group with finite center and K a maximal compact subgroup. Let G=KAN, M=C_K(A), and realize spherical principal-series representations \pi_\lambda on L^2(K/M). Write W=N_K(A)/C_K(A) and take s\in W. If a defined standard intertwiner A(s,\lambda) satisfies A(s,\lambda)\pi_\lambda(g)=\pi_{s\lambda}(g)A(s,\lambda), then for the constant vector \xi_0=1 one has A(s,\lambda)\xi_0=c_s(\lambda)\xi_0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Iwasawa decompositions, spherical principal series and standard intertwiners require substantial semisimple Lie representation theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: A spherical intertwiner acts by a scalar on the fixed vector — source argument

The constant vector spans the one-dimensional subspace fixed by K. Since the intertwiner commutes with the respective K actions, its image is also K-fixed, and hence is a scalar multiple of \xi_0. The scalar is c_s(\lambda) (equivalently the value of this constant function at the identity).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the one-dimensional K-fixed subspace in the spherical principal-series compact model. The final scalar argument is elementary linear algebra, but understanding these representations, Lie-group decompositions and L2 quotient spaces requires advanced background.
