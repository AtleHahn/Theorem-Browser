On \mathbb R^2 put \|(x,y)\|_1=|x|+|y|. The noncollinear vectors u=(1,0) and v=(0,1) satisfy \|u+v\|_1=\|u\|_1+\|v\|_1.

Proof 1: Evaluate the two coordinate vectors

Here u+v=(1,1), so \|u+v\|_1=|1|+|1|=2=1+1=\|u\|_1+\|v\|_1. The two coordinate vectors are not multiples of one another.
