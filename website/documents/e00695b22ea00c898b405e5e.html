Statement

For a number field of degree n, discriminant D, and r_2 complex embedding pairs, \sqrt{|D|}\ge(\pi/4)^{r_2}\frac{n^n}{n!}\ge(\pi/4)^{n/2}\frac{n^n}{n!}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The bound uses the field discriminant, degree and complex embedding count. These are algebraic-number-theory notions beyond the calculus/linear algebra baseline.

Proof 1: Rearrange the lower bound one on ideal norms

Let K be the number field and use Minkowski's ideal bound: every class in the ideal class group of K contains a nonzero integral ideal of norm not exceeding

M_K=\sqrt{|D|}\left(\frac4\pi\right)^{r_2}\frac{n!}{n^n}.

Since a nonzero integral ideal has norm at least one, we have 1\leq M_K, so that

\sqrt{|D|}\geq\left(\frac\pi4\right)^{r_2}\frac{n^n}{n!} \geq\left(\frac\pi4\right)^{n/2}\frac{n^n}{n!}.

Here 2r_2=n-r_1\leq n, where r_1 is the number of real embeddings.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use positive integer ideal norms in Minkowski bound, then rearrange and bound the number of complex embeddings. Number-field discriminants, embeddings and ideal classes are substantial algebraic number theory prerequisites.
