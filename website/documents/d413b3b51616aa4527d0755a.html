Nondeterministic polynomial-space reasoning has the same decision power as deterministic polynomial space. This shows that the apparent benefit of existential computational branching depends on which resource is measured; the equality does not establish an analogous polynomial-time collapse.

Statement

The classes of languages decided by deterministic and nondeterministic polynomial-work-space Turing machines coincide.

Proof 1: Square the polynomial space bound

For space-constructible f(n)=\Omega(\log n), Savitch’s theorem simulates nondeterministic space f(n) in deterministic space O(f(n)^2). The square of a polynomial is a polynomial, so nondeterministic polynomial space is contained in deterministic polynomial space. The reverse containment follows by regarding a deterministic machine as nondeterministic.
