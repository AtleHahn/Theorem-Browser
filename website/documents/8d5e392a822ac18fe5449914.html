Statement

Let x=(0,42,2,42,4,41,5,39,4,38,2,38,0,40) and use cyclic subscripts modulo 14. All denominators x_{i+1}+x_{i+2} are positive, and \sum_{i=1}^{14}\frac{x_i}{x_{i+1}+x_{i+2}}<7. This tuple has zero coordinates and is not a strictly positive tuple.

Level
High school only

The claim checks one explicit integer tuple and a finite rational inequality.
