Statement

Let A\subseteq B be commutative rings with the same identity, with B flat as an A-module. For prime ideals \mathfrak p_1\subseteq\mathfrak p_2 of A and a prime ideal \mathfrak q_2 of B satisfying \mathfrak q_2\cap A=\mathfrak p_2, there is a prime ideal \mathfrak q_1\subseteq\mathfrak q_2 of B with \mathfrak q_1\cap A=\mathfrak p_1. Consequently a finite prime chain can be extended downward over any prescribed finite downward chain in A.

Proof 1: Lift a localized prime through a faithfully flat map

Let \mathfrak p_1\subseteq\mathfrak p_2 be prime ideals of A and let \mathfrak q_2 be a prime ideal of B such that \mathfrak q_2\cap A=\mathfrak p_2. We wish to prove that there is a prime ideal \mathfrak q_1 of B contained in \mathfrak q_2 such that \mathfrak q_1\cap A=\mathfrak p_1. Since A\subseteq B is a flat extension of rings, it follows that A_{\mathfrak p_2}\to B_{\mathfrak q_2} is a flat homomorphism of rings. In fact, it is faithfully flat since it is a local homomorphism. Therefore, the induced map on spectra

\operatorname{Spec}(B_{\mathfrak q_2})\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}(A_{\mathfrak p_2})

is surjective and there exists a prime ideal of B_{\mathfrak q_2} that contracts to the prime ideal \mathfrak p_1A_{\mathfrak p_2} of A_{\mathfrak p_2}. The contraction of this prime ideal of B_{\mathfrak q_2} to B is a prime ideal \mathfrak q_1 of B contained in \mathfrak q_2 that contracts to \mathfrak p_1. This proves the one-step assertion; repeated application extends the prescribed finite chain downward.
