Statement

Let K be a field of characteristic different from 2, let V be an n-dimensional K-vector space, and let b:V\times V\to K be a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form. Every linear automorphism T of V satisfying

b(Tx,Ty)=b(x,y)\qquad(x,y\in V)

is a composition of at most n reflections. Here, for v\in V with b(v,v)\ne0, the reflection with normal v is

s_v(x)=x-\frac{2b(x,v)}{b(v,v)}v.

The empty composition is the identity. Nondegeneracy means that if b(x,y)=0 for every y\in V, then x=0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate linear algebra and introductory algebra supply vector spaces, forms and field operations. Nondegeneracy and reflections are explicitly defined by familiar linear equations; characteristic not two ensures the same division-by-two framework. No quadratic-form classification or field extension theory is needed.
