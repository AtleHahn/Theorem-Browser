Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate triangle, and let D,E,F lie in the interiors of BC,CA,AB, respectively. Put x=CD/BD, y=AE/CE, z=BF/AF, and let P=AD\cap BE, Q=BE\cap CF, R=CF\cap AD. If x=y=z=2, then [PQR]=[ABC]/7.

Proof 1: Specialize the side ratios — outline

Use Routh’s formula [PQR]/[ABC]=(xyz-1)^2/((xy+y+1)(yz+z+1)(zx+x+1)) for the three cevian ratios. Substitute x=y=z=2 into Routh’s formula: the area ratio is (8-1)^2/(7\cdot7\cdot7)=1/7.
