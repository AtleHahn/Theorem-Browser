Statement

For every integer n\geq1 and every pair of invertible symmetric matrices A,B\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}, there exists an invertible complex matrix P such that

P^{\mathsf T}AP=B.

Equivalently, any two nonsingular quadratic forms x\mapsto x^{\mathsf T}Ax and x\mapsto x^{\mathsf T}Bx of the same dimension over \mathbb C are related by an invertible complex-linear change of variables. The transpose \mathsf T does not take complex conjugates.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex linear algebra and invertible coordinate changes suffice; transpose is explicitly distinguished from conjugate transpose.
