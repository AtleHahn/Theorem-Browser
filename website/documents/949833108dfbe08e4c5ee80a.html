Statement

If the sides of a right triangle are positive integers a,b,c with a^2+b^2=c^2, then every interior angle has rational sine, cosine and half-angle tangent.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement concerns elementary Euclidean right triangles and rational real values.

Proof 1: Use side ratios in the half-angle identity

Starting with a Pythagorean triangle with side lengths a, b, and c that are positive integers and satisfy a2 + b2 = c2, it follows immediately that each interior angle of the triangle has rational values for sine and cosine, because for the acute angles these are just ratios of side lengths, while at the right angle they are 1 and 0. Thus each of these angles has a rational value for its half-angle tangent, using \tan(\varphi/2)=\sin\varphi/(1+\cos\varphi).
