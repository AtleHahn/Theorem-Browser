Statement

Let G be a locally compact Hausdorff group. For every strongly continuous unitary representation \rho:G\to U(H) on a complex Hilbert space and h,k\in H, let c_{h,k}^{\rho}(g)=\langle h,\rho(g)k\rangle, taking the inner product linear in its second argument. Let \mathcal A be the set of all such coefficient functions, allowing all representations. Then \mathcal A\subseteq C(G;\mathbb C) is closed under scalar multiplication, addition, multiplication and complex conjugation.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Unitary representation theory of locally compact groups is beyond undergraduate calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Use direct sums, tensor products and conjugate representations (outline)

A strongly continuous unitary representation \rho:G\to U(H) defines for each pair of vectors h,k\in H a continuous function on G, the matrix coefficient, by

g\mapsto\langle h,\rho(g)k\rangle.

The set of all matrix coefficients for all unitary representations is closed under scalar multiplication (because we can replace {\displaystyle k\to \lambda k}), addition (because of direct sum representations), multiplication (because of tensor representations) and complex conjugation (because of the complex conjugate representations).
