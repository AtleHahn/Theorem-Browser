Statement

Let k be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. If f,g\in k[t] satisfy k[f(t),g(t)]=k[t], then there is a polynomial automorphism \Phi:k^2\to k^2 with

\Phi(t,0)=(f(t),g(t))\quad\text{for all }t\in k.

A polynomial automorphism is a map given by two polynomials with a polynomial inverse. The condition on f,g says that the map of coordinate rings k[X,Y]\to k[t], X\mapsto f, Y\mapsto g, is surjective, which expresses an algebraic closed embedding of the affine line in the affine plane. Equivalently, every algebraic embedding of a line into the plane extends to an automorphism of the plane.
