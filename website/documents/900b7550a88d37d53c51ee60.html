Statement

On each side of a nondegenerate parallelogram in the Euclidean plane, construct a square externally to the parallelogram. Joining the centers of these four squares in the cyclic order of the sides gives a square.

Level

Review notes

  • With counterclockwise vertices 0,u,u+v,v and J the counterclockwise quarter-turn, adjacent center differences are (u+v+J(u-v))/2 and J times that vector. The vector cannot vanish because that would force v=-Ju, contradicting positive orientation.
