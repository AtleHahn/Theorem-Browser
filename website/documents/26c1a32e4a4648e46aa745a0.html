Statement

Let M be a compact smooth two-dimensional manifold without boundary with \chi(M)\ne0. Every continuous tangent vector field on M has a zero.

Level

Proof 1: Tangent fields on closed surfaces with nonzero Euler characteristic

Use Poincaré–Hopf: the sum of the indices of a continuous tangent field with isolated zeros on a compact manifold without boundary is the Euler characteristic. A zero-free field has empty index sum.

By the Poincaré–Hopf theorem, it follows that for any compact smooth 2-dimensional manifold without boundary with non-zero Euler characteristic, any continuous tangent vector field has at least one zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the zero-free case of Poincare-Hopf, but the actual field is a section of the tangent bundle of an arbitrary smooth surface. Tangent fields and their global index theorem require substantial manifold background.
