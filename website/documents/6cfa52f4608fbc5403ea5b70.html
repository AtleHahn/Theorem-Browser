Statement

Let F be a smooth curl-free field on an open U\subseteq\mathbb R^3. Let c:[0,1]\to U be a piecewise smooth loop based at p, and suppose a piecewise smooth H:[0,1]^2\to U satisfies H(t,0)=c(t), H(t,1)=p and H(0,s)=H(1,s)=p. Then \int_cF\cdot dc=0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Open subsets of R³; Curl and line integrals; Piecewise smooth loop contractions. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Comparison with the constant loop

Apply homotopy invariance of circulation to c and the constant loop at p. The latter has zero line integral, so the former does too.
