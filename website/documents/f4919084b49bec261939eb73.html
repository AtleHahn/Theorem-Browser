Statement

Let c\in\mathbb R and let n,k be integers with 1\leq k<n. There is a finite constant C(n,k,[c,\infty)) such that every real n-times differentiable function f on [c,\infty) with bounded f and bounded f^{(n)} satisfies

\|f^{(k)}\|_\infty\leq C(n,k,[c,\infty))\,\|f\|_\infty^{1-k/n}\,\|f^{(n)}\|_\infty^{k/n}.

Here \|g\|_\infty=\sup_{x\geq c}|g(x)|, f^{(j)} is the jth derivative, and derivatives at c are understood from the right. The constant does not depend on f.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Higher derivatives and uniform suprema with constants independent of a function are part of calculus and introductory analysis; no proof-specific extremal-function theory is required.
