Statement

Let X,Y be topological spaces and k a commutative ring with identity. Write C_*(X;k) for singular chains and C^*(X;k)=\operatorname{Hom}_k(C_*(X;k),k). The tensor chain complex has differential \partial(\sigma\otimes\tau)=\partial\sigma\otimes\tau+(-1)^p\sigma\otimes\partial\tau for \sigma\in C_p(X;k). There is a natural isomorphism H_*(X\times Y;k)\cong H_*(C_*(X;k)\otimes_k C_*(Y;k)).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Singular chains and homology; Tensor products over commutative rings; Natural isomorphisms. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Pass chain-homotopy inverses to homology

The Eilenberg–Zilber chain maps are inverse up to chain homotopy. Consequently they induce mutually inverse maps on homology.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Passing the chain equivalence to homology requires singular chains, tensor complexes and chain-homotopy invariance. These are substantial algebraic-topology prerequisites even though the final inference is short.
