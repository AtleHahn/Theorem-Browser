Statement

Let (Y_1,\nu_1) and (Y_2,\nu_2) be standard probability spaces, and form the two-sided Bernoulli systems

(X_i,\mu_i,T_i)=\bigl(Y_i^{\mathbb Z},\nu_i^{\mathbb Z},T_i\bigr),\qquad (T_i x)_j=x_{j+1}.

The product measure makes the coordinates independent with common law \nu_i. If T_1 and T_2 have equal Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy, then they are isomorphic as measure-preserving dynamical systems: there are invariant subsets X_i' of full measure and an invertible measurable measure-preserving map F:X_1'\to X_2' with measurable inverse such that F\circ T_1=T_2\circ F.

For a measure-preserving transformation T, the entropy is

h(T)=\sup_{\mathcal P}\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1nH\left(\bigvee_{j=0}^{n-1}T^{-j}\mathcal P\right),

where \mathcal P ranges over finite measurable partitions, the join is their common refinement, and H(\{P_a\})=-\sum_a\mu(P_a)\log\mu(P_a) with 0\log0=0. Entropy may be infinite.
