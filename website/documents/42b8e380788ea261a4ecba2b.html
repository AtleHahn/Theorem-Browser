Statement

Let H be a real Hilbert space and I:H\to\mathbb R be continuously Fréchet differentiable. Assume that its derivative I':H\to H^* is Lipschitz continuous on every bounded subset of H, where H^* is the continuous dual with its operator norm. Assume the Palais–Smale condition: every sequence (u_j) for which (I(u_j)) is bounded and \|I'(u_j)\|_{H^*}\to0 has a subsequence converging in H.

Suppose I(0)=0, and that there are r,a>0 and v\in H such that

I(u)\geq a\quad\text{whenever }\|u\|=r,\qquad \|v\|>r,\qquad I(v)\leq0.

Let \Gamma be the set of continuous paths g:[0,1]\to H satisfying g(0)=0 and g(1)=v, and put

c=\inf_{g\in\Gamma}\max_{0\leq t\leq1}I(g(t)).

Then c is a critical value of I: there exists u\in H with I'(u)=0 and I(u)=c.
