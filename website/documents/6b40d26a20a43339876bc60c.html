Statement

For a Levi-Civita connection, the commutator identity for covariant derivatives of a tensor remains valid for tensor densities of any fixed real weight w, without an additional density-curvature term. In local coordinates the metric density is \delta=\sqrt{|\det g_{ij}|}\,|dx^1\cdots dx^n|; after writing a tensor density as T\delta^w, the curvature commutator acts only on the tensor slots of T.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Tensor densities; Levi-Civita covariant derivatives; Ricci commutator identity. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use the parallel metric-density factor — outline

The metric density is covariantly constant for the Levi-Civita connection: \nabla_\mu\sqrt{|\det g_{ij}|}=0 in density notation. Consequently the density factor contributes no terms to the covariant-derivative commutator, leaving the ordinary tensor Ricci identity unchanged.
