Statement

Let M=(0,1) and let the one-dimensional real abelian Lie algebra \mathfrak g=\mathbb R act infinitesimally by a(s)=s\,\partial/\partial x. This infinitesimal action is not induced by a global smooth action on M of a Lie group with Lie algebra \mathfrak g.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns integration of a Lie algebra action to a global smooth Lie group action, beyond calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Escape of the translation orbit — outline

Use the standard prerequisite that a vector field induced by a global Lie group action is complete: the orbit of the corresponding one-parameter subgroup supplies an integral curve for every real time. For the displayed vector field \partial/\partial x, an integral curve starting at x_0\in(0,1) satisfies x'(t)=1 and hence x(t)=x_0+t. It cannot remain in (0,1) for all real t (at t=1-x_0 it reaches 1). Thus this vector field is not complete, ruling out a global integrating action.

Level justification

Use completeness of fundamental vector fields of global Lie-group actions, then solve the scalar translation equation. The ODE calculation is elementary, but Lie algebras, infinitesimal actions and one-parameter subgroups are substantial prerequisite machinery.
