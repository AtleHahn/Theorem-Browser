Statement

Let (M_i)_{i\in I} be nonempty structures in the same first-order signature and \mathcal U an ultrafilter on I. Their ultraproduct M is the quotient of \prod_iM_i by agreement on a set in \mathcal U. Functions are interpreted coordinatewise and a relation holds on classes precisely when its coordinate truth set lies in \mathcal U. For every first-order formula \phi(v_1,\ldots,v_n) and tuples a^1,\ldots,a^n\in\prod_i M_i, one has M\models\phi([a^1],\ldots,[a^n]) if and only if \{i:M_i\models\phi(a_i^1,\ldots,a_i^n)\}\in\mathcal U.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

First-order model theory and ultraproduct semantics are outside the undergraduate calculus/linear-algebra baseline.

Proof 1: Induction on formula complexity — outline

Proceed by induction on the complexity of \phi, starting with the coordinate interpretations of the atomic relations and operations. The ultrafilter property, rather than merely the filter property, is needed for the negation clause. The existential quantifier step uses the axiom of choice.
