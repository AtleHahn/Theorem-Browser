Statement

Let (S_i,\Sigma_i,\mu_i), i=1,2, be sigma-finite measure spaces. Let f,g be real or complex measurable functions on their product. For p,q\in(1,\infty) with 1/p+1/q=1,

\int_{S_1}\!\int_{S_2}|fg|\,d\mu_2d\mu_1\le\left(\int_{S_1}\!\int_{S_2}|f|^p\,d\mu_2d\mu_1\right)^{1/p}\left(\int_{S_1}\!\int_{S_2}|g|^q\,d\mu_2d\mu_1\right)^{1/q}.

All integrals are nonnegative extended integrals; 0\cdot\infty=0.

Proof 1: Hölder inequality for iterated integrals

Apply Hölder on the product measure space (S_1\times S_2,\Sigma_1\otimes\Sigma_2,\mu_1\otimes\mu_2). Tonelli’s theorem rewrites each nonnegative product-space integral as the iterated integral in the statement.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Holder on the product measure and use Tonelli on every nonnegative integrand. Sigma-finite product measures and extended Lebesgue integrals require graduate measure theory.
