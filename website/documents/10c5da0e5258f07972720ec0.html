Statement

Let D>0 be an integer such that -D is the discriminant of an imaginary quadratic field K. Equivalently, -D=d when d<0 is squarefree and d\equiv1\pmod4, and -D=4d when d<0 is squarefree and d\equiv2,3\pmod4. Let h be the number of proper equivalence classes of primitive positive definite integral binary quadratic forms ax^2+bxy+cy^2 of discriminant b^2-4ac=-D. Primitive means \gcd(a,b,c)=1; proper equivalence is change of the two integer variables by a matrix of determinant one. This h is the ideal class number of K. Let w be the number of roots of unity in K (w=6 for D=3, w=4 for D=4, and w=2 otherwise). Define \chi(r)=\left(\frac{-D}{r}\right) for positive integers r, multiplicatively in prime factors of r: at an odd prime it is zero if the prime divides D, and otherwise 1 or -1 according as -D is or is not a quadratic residue; at 2 it is zero when D is even, 1 when -D\equiv1,7\pmod8, and -1 when -D\equiv3,5\pmod8. Set \chi(1)=1. For x>0 and \operatorname{Im}z>0, respectively, put

\Gamma(x)=\int_0^\infty t^{x-1}e^{-t}\,dt,\qquad \eta(z)=e^{\pi iz/12}\prod_{n=1}^\infty(1-e^{2\pi inz}).

Choose one form [a,b,c] from each of the h proper classes and set \tau=(-b+i\sqrt D)/(2a) for that form. With the sum over precisely these h representatives,

\frac w4\sum_{r=1}^{D-1}\chi(r)\log\Gamma(r/D)=\frac h2\log(4\pi\sqrt D)+\sum_{\tau}\log\bigl(\sqrt{\operatorname{Im}\tau}\,|\eta(\tau)|^2\bigr).

All logarithms are real logarithms of positive numbers.
