Statement

Let E/F be an algebraic Galois extension, possibly infinite. Thus every element of E is algebraic over F, and every irreducible polynomial over F having a root in E splits in E into distinct linear factors. Let G=\operatorname{Aut}_F(E), the group of field automorphisms of E fixing F pointwise.

Give G its Krull topology: the weakest topology making every restriction map G\to\operatorname{Gal}(L/F) continuous, where F\subseteq L\subseteq E runs through the finite Galois intermediate extensions and each finite group has the discrete topology. Then the maps

K\longmapsto\operatorname{Gal}(E/K)=\{\sigma\in G:\sigma(a)=a\text{ for all }a\in K\},H\longmapsto E^H=\{a\in E:\sigma(a)=a\text{ for all }\sigma\in H\}

are mutually inverse bijections between intermediate fields F\subseteq K\subseteq E and closed subgroups H of G.

For finite E/F, the topology is discrete, so every subgroup occurs. In this case the correspondence reverses inclusion, and for every subgroup H\leq G,

[E:E^H]=|H|,\qquad [E^H:F]=[G:H].

Here a field degree is dimension as a vector space and [G:H] is subgroup index. Furthermore E^H/F is Galois if and only if H is normal in G; when this holds, restriction induces

G/H\cong\operatorname{Gal}(E^H/F).

Level justification
