Bandlimited square-integrable functions extend to entire functions

Statement

Let a>0 and let f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb C be a continuous representative of an L^2(\mathbb R) function whose L^2 Fourier transform F is supported in [-a,a], with inverse convention f(x)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{-a}^a F(\omega)e^{ix\omega}\,d\omega. There exists an entire function on \mathbb C whose restriction to \mathbb R is f.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The hypothesis uses an L² Fourier transform and the conclusion a holomorphic extension to the entire complex plane.

Proof 1: Apply the compact-support Paley–Wiener theorem

The bandlimited continuous functions f in the statement are the restrictions to \mathbb R of entire holomorphic functions, by the Paley–Wiener theorem.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The single theorem application still requires understanding Lebesgue L2 equivalence classes, their Fourier transform in the L2 sense, essential support, continuous representatives and entire holomorphic functions. These linked harmonic- and complex-analysis notions exceed a small extension of the undergraduate baseline. The explicit inverse convention does not remove the general L2 Fourier framework.

1. Paley–Wiener compact-support implication: the continuous representative of an L² function with compactly supported L² Fourier transform is the restriction of an entire function
Kind:

Theorem

Treatment:

Cited without proof

Statement used:

If an L2 Fourier transform is supported in a bounded interval [-a,a], its inverse has an entire extension z->(2pi)^(-1) integral_[-a,a] F(omega) exp(i z omega) d omega; any continuous representative agrees with this inverse on the real line.

Application in proof:

Apply the compact-support implication to obtain an entire function restricting to the stated f.

Application level:

Beyond undergraduate level

Level justification:

The application involves L2 Fourier inversion, almost-everywhere representatives and holomorphic extension.

Review notes:

Compact support makes F integrable by Cauchy–Schwarz. Equality almost everywhere on the real line upgrades to equality everywhere for the continuous representative. The full Paley–Wiener characterization and growth converse are not needed.
