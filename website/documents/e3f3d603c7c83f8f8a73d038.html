Statement

Let N≥1, let J_{jk}≥0 be real for 1≤j,k≤N, and let μ_1,…,μ_N be even positive measures on R such that \int e^{bt^2}\,d\mu_j(t)<\infty for every real b and \int e^{ht}\,d\mu_j(t)\ne0 whenever Re h>0. Define Z(z)=\int_{\mathbb R^N}\exp(\sum_{j,k}J_{jk}S_jS_k+z\sum_jS_j)\,d\mu_1(S_1)\cdots d\mu_N(S_N). Every complex zero of Z has real part zero.

Proof 1: Specialize the fields and use spin-reversal symmetry — outline

Use the multivariate Lee–Yang theorem stated in the source: with the given ferromagnetic couplings and measures, the partition integral is nonzero when all external fields have positive real part. Set every field equal to z to obtain Z(z)≠0 for Re z>0. Since each measure is even, simultaneously replacing every S_j by −S_j leaves the interaction terms unchanged and reverses the common field, so Z(−z)=Z(z). Consequently Z is also nonzero when Re z<0. Its zeros can therefore occur only on the imaginary axis.
