Statement

For each fixed integer k\ge3, let r_k(N) be the largest size of a subset of \{1,\ldots,N\} containing no arithmetic progression a,a+d,\ldots,a+(k-1)d with integer d\ge1. For all sufficiently large integers N (depending on k),

r_k(N)\le\frac{N}{(\log\log N)^{2^{-2^{k+9}}}}.

Logarithms are natural.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Finite-set extremum and explicit real powers/logarithms are elementary; large-N quantifier spelled out.
