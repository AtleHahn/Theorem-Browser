Statement

A single-vertex crease pattern consists of distinct rays from an interior point of a flat sheet. Flat-foldable means that all prescribed creases are folded, each as a mountain or valley, with no other creases, to a flat configuration with compatible layer order and no paper crossing. Every flat-foldable single-vertex pattern has an even number of creases.

High school only

Proof 1: Read parity from Maekawa’s difference formula

To fold flat, the number of creases must be even. This follows, for instance, from Maekawa's theorem, which states that the number of mountain folds at a flat-folded vertex differs from the number of valley folds by exactly two folds. If these numbers are M,V, then M+V=(M-V)+2V is even.
