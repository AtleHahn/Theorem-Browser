Statement

If \alpha,\beta,\gamma are the angles of a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle, then \tan(\alpha/2)+\tan(\beta/2)+\tan(\gamma/2)\ge\sqrt3.

Proof 1: Triangle half-angle tangents sum to at least square root of three — source argument

All half-angles lie in (0,\pi/2), where tangent is convex. Jensen’s inequality and \alpha+\beta+\gamma=\pi give \tan(\alpha/2)+\tan(\beta/2)+\tan(\gamma/2)\ge3\tan((\alpha+\beta+\gamma)/6)=\sqrt3.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply finite Jensen to the three half-angles using convexity of tangent on the acute interval. Elementary undergraduate convex-function inequalities and calculus suffice.
