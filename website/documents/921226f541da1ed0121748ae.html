Statement

Every finite-dimensional simple unital algebra R over a field k is isomorphic to M_n(D) for a finite-dimensional division k-algebra D; n and the isomorphism type of D are unique.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding simple algebras, division algebras and their isomorphism types requires abstract algebra.

Proof 1: Finite dimensionality and Artinian classification

A finite-dimensional algebra over a field is Artinian. Apply the full simple-Artinian case of the Wedderburn–Artin theorem, stated as an external prerequisite: a simple Artinian ring is a matrix ring over a division ring, uniquely in size and division-ring isomorphism type. In the finite-dimensional k-algebra case the factors are finite-dimensional over k.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof invokes the simple-Artinian classification for finite-dimensional algebras, including division-algebra structure and uniqueness. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because simple algebras, Artinian conditions and their classification together exceed a confidently small extension.
