Statement

For formal indeterminates s,t, the following identity holds in \mathbb Z[t,t^{-1}][[s]]:

\prod_{n\ge1}(1-s^n)(1-s^nt)(1-s^{n-1}t^{-1})(1-s^{2n-1}t^2)(1-s^{2n-1}t^{-2}) =\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}s^{(3n^2+n)/2}(t^{3n}-t^{-3n-1}).

This means equality of the coefficient of every nonnegative power of s, each coefficient being a finite Laurent polynomial in t (a finite integer linear combination of positive or negative integer powers of t). Each such coefficient is well-defined by finitely many terms or factors.
