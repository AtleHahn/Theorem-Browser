Statement

Let G be a finite simple group of sectional 2-rank at least five. Sectional 2-rank is the maximum rank of an elementary abelian 2-group occurring as a quotient of a subgroup. Then G is of component type or of characteristic 2 type. Component type means that, for some involution centralizer C, the quotient C/O(C) has a component, where O(C) is the largest normal odd-order subgroup and a component is a quasisimple subnormal subgroup. Characteristic 2 type means that F^*(Y) is a 2-group for every 2-local subgroup Y; here F^* denotes the generalized Fitting subgroup and a 2-local subgroup is the normalizer of a nontrivial 2-subgroup.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement combines sectional rank, involution centralizers, components, local subgroups and the generalized Fitting subgroup; understanding the full classification requires substantial finite-group theory. Proof 1: Compose MacWilliams and the balance theorem

MacWilliams showed that a group of sectional 2-rank at least five has connected Sylow 2-subgroups. The balance theorem implies that a simple group with connected Sylow 2-subgroups is either of component type or of characteristic 2 type. Applying these two results in succession gives the dichotomy.
