Statement

Let G be a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group with Haar measure \mu. If f,g\in L^1(G), then the convolution (f*g)(x)=\int_G f(x-y)g(y)\,d\mu(y) exists almost everywhere and \|f*g\|_1\le\|f\|_1\|g\|_1.

Level justification

Proof 1: Fubini–Tonelli convolution estimate (outline)

Apply the source's Fubini–Tonelli argument and Haar translation invariance:

\int_G\int_G|f(x-y)||g(y)|\,d\mu(y)d\mu(x)=\int_G|g(y)|\left(\int_G|f(x-y)|\,d\mu(x)\right)d\mu(y)=\|f\|_1\|g\|_1.

This is finite, so the convolution is absolutely defined almost everywhere. Taking the absolute value inside its integral gives the asserted norm bound.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Interchange Haar integrals and use translation invariance to obtain absolute almost-everywhere convergence and the norm bound. Locally compact groups and general Haar-L1 integration require graduate harmonic analysis.
