Statement

Let M be a von Neumann algebra with a faithful normal semifinite trace \tau. Let

M_0=\{a\in M:\tau(a^*a)<\infty\},\qquad H=L^2(M,\tau),

where H is the Hilbert-space completion of M_0 for the inner product \langle a,b\rangle=\tau(b^*a). Identify M with its representation on H by left multiplication. The map a\mapsto a^* on M_0 extends to a conjugate-linear isometry J:H\to H with J^2=I, and

JMJ=M',

where M'=\{T\in B(H):Ta=aT\text{ for every }a\in M\} is the commutant and B(H) denotes the bounded linear operators on H.

Here a trace is a nonnegative extended-valued additive, positively homogeneous functional on positive elements of M, invariant under unitary conjugation. Normality means complete additivity on orthogonal families of projections; semifiniteness means that every projection is an orthogonal sum of projections of finite trace; faithfulness means that every nonzero projection has positive trace. The expression \tau(b^*a) uses the linear extension of the trace to its integrable ideal.

Proof availability

The article proves the finite trace-vector case and states that the semifinite case follows by repeated use of a projection-cutoff fact. It does not supply the required cutoff construction or reduction. No full proof of the semifinite formulation is retained, and the missing reduction has not been invented.
