Statement

For a fixed positive nonsquare integer n, the integer matrices M(p,q)=\begin{pmatrix}p&q\\nq&p\end{pmatrix} with p^2-nq^2=1 have determinant one and are closed under multiplication.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves two-by-two matrices; matrix multiplication; determinants. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Matrix multiplication and determinant

The determinant is p^2-nq^2=1. Direct multiplication gives M(p,q)M(r,s)=M(pr+nqs,ps+qr). The product still has determinant one, so its two parameters solve the same Pell equation.
