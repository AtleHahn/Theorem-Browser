Statement

The Bernoulli schemes BS(1/2,1/2) and BS(1/3,1/3,1/3), namely the two-sided independent shifts with these symbol probabilities, are not isomorphic as measure-preserving dynamical systems.

Proof 1: Entropy obstruction — outline

The two Bernoulli schemes \operatorname{BS}(1/2,1/2) and \operatorname{BS}(1/3,1/3,1/3) are not isomorphic: two different schemes cannot be isomorphic if they do not have the same entropy. Specifically, the entropy of a Bernoulli scheme \operatorname{BS}(p_1,p_2,\ldots,p_N) is given by

H=-\sum_{i=1}^{N}p_i\log p_i.

The entropy formula and invariance under isomorphism are used as prerequisites; the source leaves the substitutions for these two schemes implicit.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although substituting the probabilities gives only log 2 and log 3, the claim concerns isomorphism of measure-preserving systems on two-sided infinite product spaces. Understanding Bernoulli shifts, measure-preserving isomorphism and metric entropy involves substantial measure-theoretic dynamics, beyond a small extension of ordinary undergraduate probability.
