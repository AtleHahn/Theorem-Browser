Statement

Let p>1 and let f:(0,\infty)\to[0,\infty] be measurable. Then

{\displaystyle \int _{0}^{\infty }\left({\frac {1}{x}}\int _{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p}\,dx\leq \left({\frac {p}{p-1}}\right)^{p}\int _{0}^{\infty }f(x)^{p}\,dx.}

Proof 1: Integration by parts and Hölder — outline

Hardy’s original proof begins with an integration by parts to get

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\int _{0}^{\infty }\left({\frac {1}{x}}\int _{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p}dx&=\int _{0}^{\infty }\left(\int _{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p}{\frac {1}{x^{p}}}dx\\[0.2em]&={\frac {p}{p-1}}\int _{0}^{\infty }\left(\int _{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p-1}{\frac {f(x)}{x^{p-1}}}dx\\[0.2em]&={\frac {p}{p-1}}\int _{0}^{\infty }\left({\frac {1}{x}}\int _{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p-1}f(x)dx\end{aligned}}}

Then, by Hölder's inequality,

{\displaystyle \int _{0}^{\infty }\left({\frac {1}{x}}\int _{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p}dx\leq {\frac {p}{p-1}}\left(\int _{0}^{\infty }\left({\frac {1}{x}}\int _{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p}dx\right)^{1-{\frac {1}{p}}}\left(\int _{0}^{\infty }f(x)^{p}\,dx\right)^{\frac {1}{p}},}

and the conclusion follows.

Proof 2: Scaling and Minkowski

A change of variables gives

{\displaystyle \left(\int _{0}^{\infty }\left({\frac {1}{x}}\int _{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt\right)^{p}\ dx\right)^{1/p}=\left(\int _{0}^{\infty }\left(\int _{0}^{1}f(sx)\,ds\right)^{p}\,dx\right)^{1/p},}

which is less or equal than {\displaystyle \int _{0}^{1}\left(\int _{0}^{\infty }f(sx)^{p}\,dx\right)^{1/p}\,ds} by Minkowski's integral inequality. Finally, by another change of variables, the last expression equals

{\displaystyle \int _{0}^{1}\left(\int _{0}^{\infty }f(x)^{p}\,dx\right)^{1/p}s^{-1/p}\,ds={\frac {p}{p-1}}\left(\int _{0}^{\infty }f(x)^{p}\,dx\right)^{1/p}.}
