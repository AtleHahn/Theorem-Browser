Statement

For each integer i\geq3 with i\ne6, prescribe a nonnegative integer p_i, with only finitely many p_i nonzero. There exists a nonnegative integer p_6 and a simple convex three-dimensional polyhedron having exactly p_i faces with i sides for every i\geq3 if and only if

\sum_{\substack{i\geq3\\i\ne6}}(6-i)p_i=12.

Here simple means that exactly three edges meet at each vertex. A convex polyhedron in this statement is a bounded three-dimensional convex polytope, with its polygonal faces.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement requires only elementary solid geometry, counting faces by their number of sides, and a finite linear equation. The term simple is explicitly defined.
