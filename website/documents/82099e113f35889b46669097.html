Statement

Assuming first-order Peano arithmetic (PA) is consistent, primitive recursive arithmetic (PRA) does not prove \operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA}), the standard arithmetized consistency sentence.

Proof 1: Primitive recursive arithmetic cannot prove Peano consistency — source argument

The source invokes the consistency-proof corollary of second incompleteness: a suitable consistent theory cannot prove the consistency of another arithmetic theory that proves its consistency. PA proves the consistency of PRA. Therefore PRA cannot also prove the consistency of PA.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Invoke the mutual-consistency obstruction and the formal arithmetic relationship between PA and PRA. Arithmetized consistency and the hypotheses of second incompleteness need substantial mathematical logic beyond a small introductory extension.
