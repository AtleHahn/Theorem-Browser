Statement

Let \mu be a nonnegative measure on \mathbb R with density f relative to Lebesgue measure, d\mu(x)=f(x)\,dx. Suppose every moment m_n=\int_{\mathbb R}x^n\,d\mu(x), n=0,1,2,\ldots, is finite and

\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{-\log f(x)}{1+x^2}\,dx<\infty,

with -\log0=+\infty. Then there is a different nonnegative measure \nu\ne\mu on \mathbb R such that \int x^n\,d\nu(x)=m_n for every integer n\ge0. Thus the full moment sequence does not determine the measure uniquely.
