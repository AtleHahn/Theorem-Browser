Statement

Let G be a group, N\trianglelefteq G, and q:G\to G/N the quotient map. The assignments

K\longmapsto K/N=q(K),\qquad L\longmapsto q^{-1}(L)

are inverse inclusion-preserving bijections between subgroups K\leq G containing N and subgroups L\leq G/N. Under this correspondence, K is normal in G if and only if K/N is normal in G/N.
