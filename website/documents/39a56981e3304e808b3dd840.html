Proof 1: Subtracting Laurent principal parts

If the holomorphic map from the Riemann sphere to itself {\displaystyle f} is nonconstant, then since the set of {\displaystyle z} where {\displaystyle f(z)} is infinity is isolated and the Riemann sphere is compact, there are finitely many {\displaystyle z} with {\displaystyle f(z)} equal to infinity. Consider the Laurent expansion at all such {\displaystyle z} and subtract off the singular part: we are left with a function on the Riemann sphere with values in {\displaystyle \mathbb {C} }, which by Liouville's theorem is constant. Thus {\displaystyle f} is a rational function.
