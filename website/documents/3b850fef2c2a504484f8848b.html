Statement

Let K be a non-archimedean local field, K^s a separable closure and G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(K^s/K). If A is a finite discrete G_K-module whose order is prime to \operatorname{char}K, then H^i(G_K,A)=0 for every integer i>2, where H^i denotes continuous group cohomology.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Local-field absolute Galois groups and their continuous cohomology require substantial Galois cohomology. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply the definition of cohomological dimension

Use the source-stated cohomological-dimension bound for the absolute Galois group of a non-archimedean local field: its cohomological dimension is two. By the meaning of cohomological dimension, cohomology of discrete torsion modules in degrees greater than that bound vanishes. The finite module A is such a module, so H^i(G_K,A)=0 for i>2.
