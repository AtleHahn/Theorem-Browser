Statement

Let X,Y be Polish spaces, \mu a Borel probability measure on Y, \pi:Y\to X Borel and \nu=\pi_*\mu. Let (\mu_x) be a disintegration: it is a Borel probability kernel, is supported on \pi^{-1}(x) for \nu-almost every x, and satisfies \int_Y f\,d\mu=\int_X\int_Y f\,d\mu_x\,d\nu(x) for every nonnegative Borel f. For every Borel E\subseteq Y, \mu(E)=\int_X\mu_x(E)\,d\nu(x).

Beyond undergraduate level

The result concerns probability kernels and measure-theoretic integral identities.

Proof 1: Substitute the event indicator

Take f=\mathbf1_E in the disintegration integral identity. The integrals of the indicator are respectively \mu(E) and \mu_x(E), yielding the asserted formula.
