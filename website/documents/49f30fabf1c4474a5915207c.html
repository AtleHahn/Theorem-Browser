Statement

Let k\subseteq K be fields and let v:K\to\Gamma\cup\{\infty\} be a valuation, where \Gamma is a totally ordered abelian group: v(x)=\infty exactly when x=0, v(xy)=v(x)+v(y), and v(x+y)\ge\min\{v(x),v(y)\}. Equip k with the restricted valuation. For F=k,K, put \Gamma_F=v(F^\times), \mathcal O_F=\{x\in F:v(x)\ge0\}, \mathfrak m_F=\{x\in F:v(x)>0\} and \kappa_F=\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak m_F. The inclusions induce \Gamma_k\subseteq\Gamma_K and \kappa_k\subseteq\kappa_K. Then

\operatorname{trdeg}_k K\ \ge\ \operatorname{trdeg}_{\kappa_k}\kappa_K+\dim_{\mathbb Q}\bigl((\Gamma_K/\Gamma_k)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q\bigr).

Here transcendence degree is the cardinality of a transcendence basis, and the last term is the rational rank of the quotient abelian group. Dimensions and addition are interpreted as cardinals if infinite.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the complete statement requires general valuations, their valuation rings and residue fields, transcendence bases, and tensoring a quotient group with the rationals. These interdependent constructions exceed a confident 30-minute extension of introductory abstract algebra; a short inequality does not remove that cumulative burden.
