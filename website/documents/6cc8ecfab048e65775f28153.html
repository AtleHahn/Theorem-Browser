Statement

Let F_1,F_2 be linearly independent homogeneous polynomials of the same degree d\ge1 over a field K. Their nonzero combinations sF_1+tF_2, modulo nonzero scalar, are parametrized injectively by [s:t]\in\mathbb P^1(K) and form a projective line in the projective space of degree-d equations.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra supplies polynomial coefficient spaces. Projectivization is explicitly the set of nonzero vectors modulo nonzero scalar, with dimension one less. This one quotient-coordinate definition suffices for the parameter-space claim.

Proof 1: Take projective linear combinations — outline

Taking projective linear combinations of the two independent defining equations gives a pencil (one-parameter linear system); this corresponds to two distinct points determining a projective line in the parameter space of equations, which is simply projective space.
