Statement

For integer n\ge2, let T_n(x)=\{nx\} on 0\le x<1 and T_n(1)=1. Then T_n has exactly n fixed points.

Proof 1: Count intersections on the linear branches — outline

The fixed points of a function can be easily found graphically: they are simply the x coordinates of the points where the graph of the function intersects the graph of the line y=x. For example, the fixed points of T_3(x) are 0, 1/2, and 1.

There are three fixed points, corresponding to the intervals [0,1/3), [1/3,2/3), [2/3,1] of x, and the same geometrical argument applies for all n\ge2. Here the graph has the branches y=nx-j on [j/n,(j+1)/n), for j=0,\ldots,n-1, together with the separately defined endpoint (1,1). The source leaves the general branch-intersection checks implicit.
