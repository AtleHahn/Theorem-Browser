Statement

Let n\geq1 be an integer, let X be a set of n elements, and let A_1,\ldots,A_n be pairwise distinct subsets of X. There exists a subset S\subseteq X with |S|=n-1 such that the subsets A_1\cap S,\ldots,A_n\cap S are pairwise distinct. Equivalently, from an n\times n matrix with entries in \{0,1\} and pairwise distinct rows, one can delete a column while keeping all rows pairwise distinct.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The complete statement uses finite sets, counting, intersections and distinguishable binary rows; no college mathematics is necessary.
