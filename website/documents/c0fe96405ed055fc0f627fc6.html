Statement

Let K be a nonempty compact subset of R² and d=max{|p−q|:p,q∈K}. There is a closed disk containing K with radius at most d/√3.

Proof 1: Set the dimension equal to two

Apply Jung’s Euclidean theorem, which gives enclosing radius at most d\sqrt{n/[2(n+1)]} in R^n. For n=2 this is d\sqrt{2/6}=d/\sqrt3.
