Statement

Let X be a bounded real random variable, Z=X-\mathbb EX, and K(t)=\log\mathbb E e^{tZ}. For each real t, define the tilted law by d\mathbb P_t/d\mathbb P=e^{tZ}/\mathbb E e^{tZ}. Then K^{\prime\prime}(t)=\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P_t}(Z).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses exponential tilting and probabilistic variance.

Proof 1: Differentiate the log moment-generating function

Explicit calculation shows

{\displaystyle K''(t)={\frac {E[(X-E[X])^{2}e^{t(X-E[X])}]}{E[e^{t(X-E[X])}]}}-\left({\frac {E[(X-E[X])e^{t(X-E[X])}]}{E[e^{t(X-E[X])}]}}\right)^{2}}

Notice that the quantity {\displaystyle {\frac {E[(X-E[X])e^{t(X-E[X])}]}{E[e^{t(X-E[X])}]}}=E\left[(X-E[X]){\frac {e^{t(X-E[X])}}{E[e^{t(X-E[X])}]}}\right]} is precisely the expectation of a random variable obtained by exponentially tilting {\displaystyle X-E[X]}. Let this variable be {\displaystyle Y_{t}}. Here Y_t has the law of Z=X-E[X] under \mathbb P_t defined in the statement. The first ratio is its second moment, so the displayed difference is \operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P_t}(Z).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The differentiation is elementary, but the statement explicitly changes an arbitrary probability measure by a Radon-Nikodym density and interprets moments under the new law. That measure-theoretic framework exceeds the baseline; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
