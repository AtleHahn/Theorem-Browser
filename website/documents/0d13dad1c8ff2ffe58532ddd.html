Statement

Let a<b be positive integers with a\nmid b. Then a\times b rectangles tile an (a+b)\times ab rectangle, although the target sides are not integer multiples of the corresponding brick sides in either orientation.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The assertion concerns planar rectangles and nondivisibility of positive integer side lengths.

Proof 1: The two-strip construction for nonharmonic sides — outline

A rectangle of dimensions a and ab is packed with a copies of an a\times b brick placed side by side. A rectangle of dimensions ab and b is packed with copies of the same brick in the other orientation. Rotate one strip so that their length-ab sides are parallel, then place the strips side by side. The result is an (a+b)\times ab rectangle tiled by the original bricks.

This target rectangle is a multiple exactly when the shorter brick side divides the longer one. The nonharmonic hypothesis therefore makes it a nonmultiple.
