Statement

Let E be any complex elliptic curve, regarded as a complex Lie group, and let \mathbb C denote the additive complex Lie group. In \mathbb C\times E there is a family of closed complex analytic subgroups, each holomorphically isomorphic as a group to \mathbb C and not an algebraic subgroup, whose union is dense in the ordinary complex topology. In particular, there is no single largest closed complex analytic subgroup isomorphic to a connected affine algebraic group that contains all these subgroups. An elliptic curve here is a smooth connected projective curve of genus one with a chosen identity, with its algebraic group law. A complex analytic subgroup is a subgroup that is a complex submanifold and whose inclusion is holomorphic; closed refers to the ordinary topology. Algebraic subgroup means a Zariski-closed subgroup for the product algebraic variety structure. A connected affine algebraic group is a connected affine algebraic variety with algebraic group operations.
