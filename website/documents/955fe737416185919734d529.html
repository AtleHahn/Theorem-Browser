Statement

Let E be a real or complex normed vector space and K\subset E a nonempty convex set compact in the weak topology. Let S be a semigroup of affine isometries K\to K. Then some x\in K satisfies s(x)=x for every s\in S. The weak topology is the coarsest topology making every continuous linear functional on E continuous. Convexity means tx+(1-t)y\in K for x,y\in K and 0\le t\le1. An affine isometry preserves these convex combinations and distances induced by the norm. A semigroup here is a nonempty collection closed under composition; its maps need not be assumed surjective.
