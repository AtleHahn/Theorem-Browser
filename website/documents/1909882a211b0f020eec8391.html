Statement

A Pythagorean triple means positive integers (a,b,c) with a^2+b^2=c^2; it is primitive if \gcd(a,b,c)=1. Then a\ne b.

Level
High school only

Proof 1: Source argument

Equal legs would make the ratio of the hypotenuse to a leg equal to \sqrt2. That ratio is rational, whereas \sqrt2 is irrational.

Level justification

Equal legs force the rational side ratio to equal the irrational square root of two. School radicals and rational numbers suffice.
