Statement

Let ABCD be a planar quadrilateral with no three consecutive vertices collinear, and let E,F,G,H be the midpoints of AB,BC,CD,DA, respectively. Then EF and HG are parallel to AC and have length |AC|/2, while FG and EH are parallel to BD and have length |BD|/2. Consequently EFGH is a parallelogram and its perimeter is |AC|+|BD|.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement concerns elementary planar midpoint geometry and segment lengths.

Proof 1: Four midpoint similarities

Triangles ADC and HDG are similar by the side-angle-side criterion, since DH=DA/2, DG=DC/2, and the angle at D is common. So angles DAC and DHG are equal, making HG parallel to AC. In the same way EF is parallel to AC, so HG and EF are parallel to each other; the same holds for HE and GF.

A side of the Varignon parallelogram is half as long as the diagonal in the original quadrilateral it is parallel to.

From this proof, one can see that the sum of the diagonals is equal to the perimeter of the parallelogram formed.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply side-angle-side similarity to midpoint triangles and read parallel side directions, lengths and right angles. Elementary Euclidean geometry suffices.
