Statement

Let q be an odd prime power and K=\operatorname{GF}(q). Define \chi(0)=0, \chi(a)=1 for nonzero squares in K, and \chi(a)=-1 for nonsquares. Let Q=(\chi(a-b))_{a,b\in K}, let j be the all-ones column of length q, and let I_m denote the m\times m identity matrix. A Hadamard matrix of order m is a real m\times m matrix with entries \pm1 and HH^T=mI_m.

  1. If q\equiv3\pmod4, put C_-=\begin{bmatrix}0&j^T\\-j&Q\end{bmatrix}. Then H=I_{q+1}+C_- is a Hadamard matrix of order q+1 and is skew Hadamard: H+H^T=2I_{q+1}.
  2. If q\equiv1\pmod4, put C_+=\begin{bmatrix}0&j^T\\j&Q\end{bmatrix}. Replace each zero entry of C_+ by \begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\-1&-1\end{bmatrix} and each entry \varepsilon\in\{1,-1\} by \varepsilon\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}. The resulting matrix is a symmetric Hadamard matrix of order 2(q+1).
