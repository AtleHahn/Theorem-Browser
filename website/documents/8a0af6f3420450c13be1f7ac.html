Statement

Let n\ge1 and let l_j\le u_j be real numbers for 0\le j\le n, with 0\notin[l_n,u_n]. Consider all real polynomials p(s)=\sum_{j=0}^n a_js^j with a_j\in[l_j,u_j] independently. Call a polynomial stable if every complex root has strictly negative real part. Every polynomial in this family is stable if and only if four particular members are stable. Their coefficient choices, repeated with period four in the index j and truncated at j=n, are respectively

(l,l,u,u),\quad(u,u,l,l),\quad(l,u,u,l),\quad(u,l,l,u).

In this notation choosing l at index j means a_j=l_j, and choosing u means a_j=u_j.
