Statement

Every closed, orientable, connected 3-manifold is homeomorphic to a manifold obtained from S^3 by Dehn surgery on a finite link, with every component unknotted and every surgery coefficient equal to +1 or -1. Here closed means compact without boundary, S^3 is the unit sphere in \mathbb R^4, and a link is a disjoint union of embedded circles; unknotted means each circle individually bounds an embedded disk in S^3 (the disks need not avoid other components). Dehn surgery removes open solid-torus neighborhoods of the components and glues solid tori back. Coefficient \pm1 means that the meridian of the inserted torus is attached along the slope \ell\pm m, where m is the original meridian and \ell the preferred longitude, which is null-homologous in that component's knot complement.
