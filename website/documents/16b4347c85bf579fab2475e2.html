Statement

Let M be a smooth manifold. Smooth chains are finite integer linear combinations of smooth singular simplices; \partial denotes their boundary and integration extends linearly. If \omega is a smooth k-form with d\omega=0 and c is a smooth (k+1)-chain, then \int_{\partial c}\omega=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses differential forms, smooth chains and boundaries, exterior derivative.

Proof 1: Apply chain Stokes to a closed form

Stokes' theorem for chains reads

\int_{\partial c}\omega=\int_c d\omega.

On the level of forms, this means that closed forms, i.e., d\omega=0, have zero integral over boundaries.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Applying Stokes here requires differential forms on arbitrary smooth manifolds, exterior differentiation, and integration over smooth singular chains. The final substitution is immediate, but that combined framework is beyond the undergraduate baseline.
