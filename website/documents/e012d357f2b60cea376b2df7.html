Statement

Let S\subseteq\mathbb R^3 be the centers of a packing of unit balls: \|s-t\|\ge2 for distinct s,t\in S. For each s\in S, its Voronoi cell is V_s=\{x:\|x-s\|\le\|x-t\|\text{ for every }t\in S\}. Then \operatorname{vol}(V_s) is at least the volume of a regular dodecahedron circumscribed about a unit ball, meaning that each of its twelve congruent regular pentagonal faces is tangent to that ball. Volume may be infinite for an unbounded cell.

Level justification

The infinite-set quantifier, intersection of halfspaces and possibly infinite volume are understood through undergraduate geometry and analysis; no packing proof methods are needed.
