Statement

Let D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\} and let h:D\to D be holomorphic and injective with h(0)=0. Composition C_hf=f\circ h is contractive on the Hardy spaces H^p(D) and Bergman spaces A^p(D) for 1\le p<\infty, and for the Dirichlet energy seminorm:

\|C_hf\|_{H^p}\le\|f\|_{H^p},\qquad \|C_hf\|_{A^p}\le\|f\|_{A^p},\qquad [C_hf]_{\mathcal D}\le[f]_{\mathcal D}.

These spaces consist of holomorphic functions for which the respective quantities are finite, with

\|f\|_{H^p}^p=\sup_{0<r<1}\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}|f(re^{i\theta})|^p\,d\theta,\qquad \|f\|_{A^p}^p=\frac1\pi\iint_D|f(z)|^p\,dx\,dy,[f]_{\mathcal D}^2=\frac1\pi\iint_D|f'(z)|^2\,dx\,dy.

The energy is a seminorm since it vanishes on constants; it defines a norm modulo constants. Holomorphic means complex differentiable on D.

The corresponding circular-mean inequality holds for every holomorphic f on D, every 0<r<1 and every p>0:

\int_0^{2\pi}|f(h(re^{i\theta}))|^p\,d\theta\le\int_0^{2\pi}|f(re^{i\theta})|^p\,d\theta.
