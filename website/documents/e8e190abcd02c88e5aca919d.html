Statement

For every finite complex sequence u_1,\ldots,u_N, \left|\sum_{\substack{1\le r,s\le N\\r\ne s}}\frac{u_r\overline{u_s}}{r-s}\right|\le\pi\sum_{r=1}^N|u_r|^2. The constant \pi cannot be replaced by a smaller constant uniformly over all finite sequences. The inequality also holds for an infinite complex sequence with \sum_{r=1}^{\infty}|u_r|^2<\infty, interpreting the double sum as the limit of the displayed finite sums as N\to\infty.
