Statement

Let p,q be distinct odd primes with q\nmid p-1. Put \zeta=e^{2\pi i/p}, K=\mathbb Q(\zeta) and F=K\cap\mathbb R, the maximal real subfield. Let G^+=\operatorname{Gal}(F/\mathbb Q), the group of field automorphisms of F fixing \mathbb Q.

Write \mathcal O_F for the ring of algebraic integers in F (elements satisfying a monic polynomial over \mathbb Z), and E=\mathcal O_F^\times for its unit group. Define

D=\left\langle -1,\zeta,\frac{1-\zeta^a}{1-\zeta}\ (1\leq a\leq p-1)\right\rangle\subseteq K^\times,\qquad C=D\cap E,

so C is the group of real cyclotomic units. Angle brackets here mean the multiplicative subgroup generated by the indicated elements, allowing integer powers.

Let \mathrm{Cl}(F) be the ordinary ideal class group: the group of nonzero fractional ideals of \mathcal O_F under ideal multiplication, modulo the principal fractional ideals x\mathcal O_F for x\in F^\times. A fractional ideal is a nonzero finitely generated \mathcal O_F-submodule I\subseteq F with dI\subseteq\mathcal O_F for some nonzero d\in\mathcal O_F. Put E^q=\{u^q:u\in E\} and \mathrm{Cl}(F)^q=\{c^q:c\in\mathrm{Cl}(F)\}.

The Galois group acts on units and ideals by applying its automorphisms. For an element \theta=\sum_{\sigma\in G^+}n_\sigma\sigma of the integral group ring \mathbb Z[G^+], its action in multiplicative notation is

u^\theta=\prod_{\sigma\in G^+}\sigma(u)^{n_\sigma},\qquad [I]^\theta=\prod_{\sigma\in G^+}[\sigma(I)]^{n_\sigma}.

If u^\theta\in CE^q for every u\in E, then [I]^\theta\in\mathrm{Cl}(F)^q for every ideal class [I]\in\mathrm{Cl}(F). Equivalently, every element of \mathbb Z[G^+] annihilating E/CE^q also annihilates \mathrm{Cl}(F)/\mathrm{Cl}(F)^q.
