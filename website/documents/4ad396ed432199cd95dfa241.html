Statement

Let k\ge1 be an integer and let p=4k+1 be prime and S=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb Z_{>0}^3:x^2+4yz=p\}. The map on S given by the three branches below is an involution with exactly one fixed point (1,1,k): \phi(x,y,z)=\begin{cases}(x+2z,z,y-x-z)&x<y-z,\\(2y-x,y,x-y+z)&y-z<x<2y,\\(x-2y,x-y+z,y)&x>2y.\end{cases} An involution is a map whose square is the identity.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The set is defined by a positive-integer polynomial equation and all three branches are explicit; involution means the displayed map applied twice is the identity.
