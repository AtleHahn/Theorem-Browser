Statement

Let \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n be a nonempty bounded Lipschitz domain and 1\le p\le\infty. There is no finite C such that \|u\|_{L^p(\Omega)}\le C\|\nabla u\|_{L^p(\Omega)} for all u\in W^{1,p}(\Omega). Even restricting to nonconstant u does not give such a uniform bound.

Proof 1: Use constants and additive shifts — outline

A nonzero constant has zero gradient and positive norm, already disproving the unrestricted bound. Also, adding constants to any fixed nonconstant Sobolev function leaves its gradient unchanged but makes its norm arbitrarily large on this finite positive-measure domain. Hence excluding just the constant functions does not cure the problem.

Level justification

Test constants and shifts, but the full claim concerns general Sobolev functions, weak gradients and Lebesgue p norms. These objects require graduate analysis despite the elementary counterexample mechanism.
