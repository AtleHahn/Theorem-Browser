Proof 1: Nonexistence of a rational power of two (outline)

Use that 3/2 is not a rational power of 2, an elementary prime-factorization fact. Since 2^{\log_2(3/2)}=3/2, its logarithm cannot be rational. The angular statement is the same assertion after division by 2\pi.
