Statement

Under a local isometric bending between patches of a catenoid and a helicoid, Gaussian curvatures at corresponding points are equal.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses named differential-geometric surfaces and local isometries; their definitions are prerequisites.

Proof 1: Apply curvature invariance (outline)

The catenoid and the helicoid are two very different-looking surfaces. Nevertheless, each of them can be continuously bent into the other: they are locally isometric. It follows from Theorema Egregium that under this bending the Gaussian curvature at any two corresponding points of the catenoid and helicoid is always the same.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The imported theorem is short to state, but its application requires smooth surface patches, local isometries of their induced metrics and Gaussian curvature, together with the catenoid–helicoid geometry. This is a linked differential-geometric framework rather than one small new definition.
