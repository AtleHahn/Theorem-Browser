Statement

There are constants C,c>0 such that, for all sufficiently large integers N, every A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\} containing no arithmetic progression a,a+d,a+2d,a+3d with integer d\ge1 satisfies

|A|\le C\frac{N}{(\log N)^c}.

The logarithm is natural.
