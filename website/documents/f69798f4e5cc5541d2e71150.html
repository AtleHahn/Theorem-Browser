Statement

A Pythagorean triple means positive integers (a,b,c) with a^2+b^2=c^2; it is primitive if \gcd(a,b,c)=1. Every triple, after possibly exchanging its legs, has the form (a,|n-a^2/(4n)|,n+a^2/(4n)) with positive integers a,n, a^2/(4n) integral and a\ne2n. Conversely these conditions give a positive Pythagorean triple.

Proof 1: Source calculation — outline

The source’s displayed formula satisfies the Pythagorean equation by algebra. Conversely orient a given triple so that b+c is even and put n=(b+c)/2. Then a^2/(4n)=(c-b)/2 is integral, and substitution recovers b,c. Such an orientation exists because the two legs cannot both be odd. The nondegenerate triangle condition excludes a=2n.
