Statement

Let a,b,c be positive integers with a^2+b^2=c^2, and let 0<\theta<\pi/2 satisfy \sin\theta=a/c, \cos\theta=b/c. For every integer k put P_k=(\cos(2k\theta),\sin(2k\theta)). Every distance |P_j-P_k| is rational.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All angles, coordinates and integer parameters are explicit elementary trigonometry; no infinitude or density conclusion is asserted.

Proof 1: Use the even-angle circle construction — outline

Take the points on the unit circle at even multiples of the chosen acute angle of the integer-sided right triangle. These have rational pairwise distances.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The given rational sine and cosine remain rational under integer angle addition, and the chord between even-angle points is twice the absolute sine of an integer multiple of theta. Elementary trigonometry suffices to understand the supplied construction.
