Statement

For an open set \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n, W^{1,p}(\Omega) denotes the Sobolev space of L^p functions whose first weak partial derivatives are in L^p. For p<\infty, W_0^{1,p}(\Omega) is the closure of smooth compactly supported functions in the W^{1,p} norm. All norms and integrals use Lebesgue measure.

Zero boundary values. If 1\le p<\infty and \Omega is bounded in at least one direction (contained in a slab between two parallel hyperplanes), there is C=C(\Omega,p) such that

\|u\|_{L^p(\Omega)}\le C\|\nabla u\|_{L^p(\Omega)} \quad\text{for every }u\in W_0^{1,p}(\Omega).

Subtracting the mean (Poincaré–Wirtinger). If 1\le p\le\infty and \Omega is a nonempty bounded connected open set with Lipschitz boundary, there is C=C(\Omega,p) such that

\|u-u_\Omega\|_{L^p(\Omega)}\le C\|\nabla u\|_{L^p(\Omega)} \quad\text{for every }u\in W^{1,p}(\Omega),\qquad u_\Omega=\frac1{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega u(x)\,dx.

Here |\Omega| is its volume and a Lipschitz boundary is locally a graph of a Lipschitz function.
