Statement

Let X be a connected closed Riemannian manifold with its distance d. Let B(X) be the bounded real Borel functions on X with the supremum norm. The map \iota:X\to B(X) defined by \iota(p)(x)=d(p,x) is a topological embedding.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement requires metric topology and a Riemannian distance space; the construction has routine metric verification.

Proof 1: Map each point to its distance function (outline)

Use the imbedding of X into the Banach space of bounded Borel functions on X, equipped with the sup norm. The imbedding is defined by mapping a point p of X, to the real function on X given by the distance from the point p.
