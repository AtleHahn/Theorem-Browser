Statement

Let G=(V,E) be a finite undirected simple graph. A matching is a set of edges with no common endpoints, and \nu(G) denotes the largest number of edges in a matching. For U\subseteq V, let G-U be the induced graph on V\setminus U, and let \operatorname{odd}(G-U) be the number of its connected components with an odd number of vertices. Then

\nu(G)=\frac12\min_{U\subseteq V}\bigl(|V|+|U|-\operatorname{odd}(G-U)\bigr).

Equivalently, the number of vertices left unmatched by a maximum matching is

|V|-2\nu(G)=\max_{U\subseteq V}\bigl(\operatorname{odd}(G-U)-|U|\bigr).
