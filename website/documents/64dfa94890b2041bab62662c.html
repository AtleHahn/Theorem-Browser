Statement

Let K_1,K_2 be number fields (finite extensions of \mathbb Q), and choose algebraic closures \overline K_1,\overline K_2. Write

G_{K_i}=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline K_i/K_i)

for the group of automorphisms of \overline K_i fixing K_i, with its profinite topology: the subgroups fixing finite extensions of K_i form a neighborhood basis of the identity.

For every topological group isomorphism \phi:G_{K_1}\to G_{K_2} there is a unique field isomorphism \sigma:\overline K_1\to\overline K_2 such that \sigma(K_1)=K_2 and

\phi(g)=\sigma\circ g\circ\sigma^{-1}\qquad(g\in G_{K_1}).

A topological group isomorphism here is a group isomorphism that is a homeomorphism.

Proof 1: Arithmetic reconstruction and the centralizer argument (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Tohoku Math. J.31 (1979), pp.359–362; existence pp.360–361, Lemmas 2–3 and uniqueness p.362.).

Algebraic closures are solvably closed Galois extensions. Equivariance identifies the fixed fields with K_1,K_2; Lemma 3 makes the centralizer trivial, giving uniqueness of the inducing closure isomorphism.
