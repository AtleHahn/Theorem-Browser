Statement

Let f:C\to D be a functor between small categories (their objects and morphisms form sets). For an object d of D, the comma category (d\downarrow f) has objects (c,\alpha) with c\in C and \alpha:d\to f(c), and a morphism (c,\alpha)\to(c',\alpha') is a morphism v:c\to c' with f(v)\alpha=\alpha'. A morphism u:d\to d' induces the functor

u^*:(d'\downarrow f)\longrightarrow(d\downarrow f),\qquad(c,\alpha)\longmapsto(c,\alpha u).

For a small category A, let BA be the geometric realization of its nerve: glue standard topological simplices indexed by strings of composable morphisms, with face identifications given by composition or deletion and degeneracies by insertion of identity morphisms. Functors induce continuous maps of these classifying spaces. Assume that B(u^*) is a homotopy equivalence for every morphism u in D, meaning that it has a continuous inverse up to homotopy.

For each object d, regarded as a vertex of BD, form the homotopy fiber

F_d=\{(z,\gamma)\in BC\times (BD)^{[0,1]}:\gamma(0)=d,\ \gamma(1)=Bf(z)\},

with the subspace topology and the compact-open topology on the path space. Let p:(d\downarrow f)\to C forget the arrow \alpha. The arrows \alpha:d\to f(c) define a natural transformation from the constant functor with value d to fp. Its realization gives a homotopy from the constant map at d to Bf\circ Bp, and hence a comparison map

B(d\downarrow f)\longrightarrow F_d.

This comparison is a weak homotopy equivalence: it gives a bijection of path components and isomorphisms of all positive-degree homotopy groups at every basepoint.

In particular, for an object (c,\alpha) of (d\downarrow f) there is the homotopy-fiber long exact sequence

\cdots\longrightarrow\pi_{i+1}(BD,d)\longrightarrow\pi_i(B(d\downarrow f),(c,\alpha))\longrightarrow\pi_i(BC,c)\longrightarrow\pi_i(BD,d)\longrightarrow\cdots.

The path represented by \alpha supplies the basepoint identification between d and f(c). The sequence continues through path components, where exactness is interpreted for pointed sets. Here \pi_i for i\geq1 denotes based homotopy classes of maps from the i-sphere, and \pi_0 denotes path components. The homotopy-fiber assertion also applies when the comma category is empty; the displayed based sequence requires a chosen object.
