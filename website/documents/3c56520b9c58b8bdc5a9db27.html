Statement

Let n\ge1 and 1\le p<\infty. Let u\ge0 belong to W^{1,p}(\mathbb R^n), the space of Lebesgue L^p functions whose first weak partial derivatives also belong to L^p. Let u^* be its symmetric decreasing rearrangement: the radial, radially nonincreasing function whose positive-threshold superlevel sets \{u^*>t\} are centered balls, possibly empty, with the same measures as \{u>t\}, interpreted up to null sets. Then u^*\in W^{1,p}(\mathbb R^n) and

\int_{\mathbb R^n}|\nabla u^*(x)|^p\,dx \le\int_{\mathbb R^n}|\nabla u(x)|^p\,dx.

Proof availability

The coarea argument assumes smoothness and regular level sets without supplying the approximation needed for arbitrary Sobolev functions or proving rearranged Sobolev membership. The other arguments treat p=2 only; the cylindrical expansion also has an incorrect 1/epsilon scaling in its claimed energy limit. No complete proof of the full statement is retained.
