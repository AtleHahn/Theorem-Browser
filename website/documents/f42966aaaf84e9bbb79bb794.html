Statement

Let \varphi:R\to S be a surjective ring homomorphism. Then R/\ker \varphi\cong S, where \ker \varphi=\{x\in R:\varphi(x)=0\}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group/ring theory and linear algebra supply the objects. The extra Sylow, normalizer, invariant-polynomial or pseudoreflection condition is explicitly defined in subgroup, coordinate or polynomial terms; no specialist extension or representation theory is required.

Proof 1: Quotient description of a surjective ring homomorphism — proof

The first isomorphism theorem for rings gives R/\ker \varphi\cong\operatorname{im}\varphi. Surjectivity identifies this image with S, giving the conclusion.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the ring first isomorphism theorem and use surjectivity. Introductory rings, ideals and quotient rings suffice.
