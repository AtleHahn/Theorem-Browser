Statement

Write \mathrm{Hotc} for the homotopy category of pointed connected CW complexes, with pointed homotopy classes of maps as morphisms. If C is an object of \mathrm{Hotc} and F(Z)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Hotc}}(Z,C), then F satisfies the wedge and weak-pullback axioms: it sends arbitrary wedges to products of sets and homotopy pushout squares to weak pullback squares.

Proof 1: Coproduct and weak-pushout properties (outline)

Write \mathrm{Hotc} for the homotopy category of pointed connected CW complexes, with pointed homotopy classes of maps as morphisms.

A contravariant functor F:\mathrm{Hotc}^{op}\to\mathrm{Set} satisfies the wedge axiom when F(\bigvee_\alpha X_\alpha)\cong\prod_\alpha F(X_\alpha) under the canonical restriction map. It satisfies the weak-pullback axiom when it sends a homotopy pushout square to a square for which the map to the set-theoretic pullback is surjective.

For F=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Hotc}}(-,C), both conditions follow from the relevant universal properties: wedges are coproducts, and compatible homotopy classes on a homotopy pushout extend to the pushout. Thus restriction gives the product bijection in the wedge case and a surjection onto compatible pairs in the pushout case.
