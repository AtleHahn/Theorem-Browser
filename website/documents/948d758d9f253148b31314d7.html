Statement

Let (M,g) be a nonempty connected complete Riemannian manifold with finite diameter for its Riemannian distance. Then M is compact.

Level

Proof 1: Compact tangent ball and the exponential map

The diameter of (M,g) is finite. Therefore M must be compact, as a closed (and hence compact) ball of finite radius in any tangent space is carried onto all of M by the exponential map.

Here the radius is chosen to bound the diameter. Surjectivity follows from the Hopf–Rinow theorem: on a connected complete Riemannian manifold, every point is reached by a minimizing geodesic from a fixed point. The exponential map is continuous, so its image of this compact tangent ball is compact.

Level justification
