Statement

There exist positive integers a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4,b such that a_1^5+a_2^5+a_3^5+a_4^5=b^5. Hence Euler’s proposed lower bound of five summands fails for fifth powers.

Proof 1: Lander–Parkin positive witness — outline

Take (a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4,b)=(27,84,110,133,144). The displayed equality is 144^5=27^5+84^5+110^5+133^5, giving four positive summands with 4<5.
