In classical logic an inconsistent arithmetic theory proves even its own consistency, because it proves every sentence. Thus a theory's internal claim to reliability is not by itself evidence of reliability, and consistency assumptions in incompleteness theorems are indispensable.

Proof 1: An inconsistent classical theory proves its consistency sentence — source argument

An inconsistent classical theory proves every sentence by explosion, so in particular it proves its consistency sentence. Thus the mere presence of an internal consistency proof does not establish actual consistency.

Level
