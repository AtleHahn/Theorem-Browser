Statement

If d(n) counts the positive divisors of the positive integer n, then \liminf_{n\to\infty}d(n)=2.

Proof 1: Use the infinitely many two-divisor integers

There are infinitely many primes, and each prime has exactly two positive divisors. Every integer n>1 has at least the divisors 1,n. Thus the limit inferior is 2.
