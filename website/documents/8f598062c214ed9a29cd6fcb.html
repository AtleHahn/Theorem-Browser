Statement

Let K be a number field and let M,N be finite Galois extensions of K inside a fixed algebraic closure. If the sets of nonzero prime ideals of \mathcal O_K that split completely in M and in N are equal, then M=N. Here \mathcal O_K is the ring of algebraic integers in K. A prime \mathfrak p splits completely in an extension E/K if

\mathfrak p\mathcal O_E=\mathfrak P_1\cdots\mathfrak P_{[E:K]}

with distinct prime ideals \mathfrak P_j and residue fields \mathcal O_E/\mathfrak P_j each isomorphic to \mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p over the latter. In particular, ramified primes are not counted as completely split. Equivalently, the splitting set determines the finite Galois extension up to K-isomorphism.
