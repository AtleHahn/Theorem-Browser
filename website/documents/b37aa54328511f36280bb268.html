Statement

Let X_1,\ldots,X_N be independent points uniformly distributed on \mathbb S^{n-1}=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:|x|=1\}, where n,N\ge1. Then

\mathbb P\left(0\notin\operatorname{conv}\{X_1,\ldots,X_N\}\right)=2^{1-N}\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\binom{N-1}{k}.

The convex hull consists of all sums \sum_i t_iX_i with t_i\ge0 and \sum_i t_i=1. Uniform distribution means normalized surface measure (equal probabilities at -1,1 when n=1); binomial coefficients are zero for k>N-1.
