Statement

Let R=K\oplus R_1\oplus R_2\oplus\cdots be a finitely generated commutative graded algebra over a field K. Let A,B\subset R be graded polynomial subrings over which R is finite. Then R is free as a graded A-module if and only if it is free as a graded B-module.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement compares freeness over two abstract graded polynomial subrings.

Proof 1: Compare both normalizations with Cohen–Macaulayness

The graded miracle-flatness criterion says that R is Cohen–Macaulay if and only if it is free as a graded module over such a polynomial subring. The intervening Cohen–Macaulay condition depends only on R, so freeness is independent of the chosen polynomial subring.
