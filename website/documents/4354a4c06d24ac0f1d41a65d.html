Statement

Let (\mathcal F_k) be an increasing filtration on a probability space and \mathcal F_\infty=\sigma(\bigcup_k\mathcal F_k). For every event A\in\mathcal F_\infty, \Pr(A\mid\mathcal F_k)\to1_A almost surely and in L^1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns conditional event probabilities, increasing sigma algebras, and almost-sure and L1 limits.

Proof 1: Apply Levy upwards convergence to the event indicator

Apply the stated Levy upwards theorem to the integrable variable 1_A: its conditional expectations converge almost surely and in L^1 to \mathbb E[1_A\mid\mathcal F_\infty]. Since A\in\mathcal F_\infty, this terminal conditional expectation equals 1_A.

Level justification

Apply Levy upwards convergence to the indicator and use terminal measurability. Filtrations, conditional expectations and almost-sure/L1 martingale convergence require graduate probability.
