Statement

For odd p\ge3, the existence of positive integers a,b,c with a^p+b^p=c^p is equivalent to the existence of nonzero integers x,y,z with x^p+y^p+z^p=0.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The hypotheses and conclusion use only finite integer arithmetic.

Proof 1: Change signs and permute coordinates

A positive solution gives the signed solution (a,b,-c) because p is odd. Conversely, a nonzero signed solution must have both positive and negative coordinates. Change all signs if necessary and permute them so exactly one is negative; moving its odd power to the other side gives a positive solution.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Use the sign change of odd powers, permute coordinates and move the unique negative term to the other side. Elementary integer powers suffice.
