Statement

Assume the continuum hypothesis. Any two real closed fields of cardinality 2^{\aleph_0} with the \eta_1 order property are order-isomorphic. Here \eta_1 means that whenever countable subsets L,U satisfy l<u for all l\in L,u\in U, some element lies strictly above all of L and below all of U.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Real closed fields with countable-cut interpolation and continuum cardinality involve abstract fields and infinite cardinal arithmetic.

Proof 1: Substitute continuum equals aleph-one

Two \eta_\alpha real closed fields both of cardinality \aleph_\alpha are order isomorphic. We use this uniqueness theorem with \alpha=1.

If the continuum hypothesis holds, 2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_1, so all real closed fields with cardinality of the continuum and having the \eta_1 property are order isomorphic.

Level justification

Although the final step substitutes a cardinal equality, the theorem concerns real closed fields with an eta-one saturation-style order property and specified uncountable cardinality. The combined ordered-field and set-theoretic framework exceeds a small related extension of the baseline.
