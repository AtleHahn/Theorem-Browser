Statement

Let R be a commutative ring with identity, n\ge2, f\in R[X_1,\ldots,X_n] and g\in R[X_2,\ldots,X_n]. Then X_1-g divides f if and only if f(g,X_2,\ldots,X_n) is the zero polynomial.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Polynomial substitution and divisibility are introductory algebra. Formal power series use the same coefficient operations without a convergence requirement, a single direct extension sufficient for the displayed identity.

Proof 1: View the remaining variables as coefficients — outline

View the multivariate polynomials as univariate polynomials in X_1 over the commutative coefficient ring R[X_2,\ldots,X_n]. Apply the factor theorem over a commutative ring: a polynomial h(T) is divisible by T-a exactly when h(a)=0. Taking a=g gives the assertion.
