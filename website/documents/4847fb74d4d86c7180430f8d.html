Statement

Let k=\mathbb F_q, let \ell be a prime different from its characteristic, let X be a smooth n-dimensional variety over k, and let \mathcal F be a constructible \mathbb Q_\ell-sheaf on X. Write H_c^i(X_{\bar k},\mathcal F) for its compactly supported étale cohomology and F for geometric Frobenius. For every finite extension E/k, let F_E denote its geometric Frobenius action, including its action on stalks \mathcal F_x for x\in X(E). Then

\sum_{x\in X(E)}\operatorname{tr}(F_E\mid\mathcal F_x)=\sum_{i=0}^{2n}(-1)^i\operatorname{tr}(F_E\mid H_c^i(X_{\bar k},\mathcal F)).
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Smooth varieties over finite fields; Constructible ℓ-adic sheaves; Compactly supported étale cohomology; Geometric Frobenius. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Logarithmically differentiate the stated L-function identity — outline

The following cohomological expression for the L-function of {\displaystyle {\mathcal {F}}} holds:

{\displaystyle L(X,{\mathcal {F}},t)=\prod _{i=0}^{2n}\det(1-t\cdot F\,\,|\,\,H_{c}^{i}(X_{\bar {k}},{\mathcal {F}}))^{(-1)^{i+1}}={\frac {\det(1-t\cdot F\,\,|\,\,H_{c}^{1}(X_{\bar {k}},{\mathcal {F}}))\cdots \det(1-t\cdot F\,\,|\,\,H_{c}^{2n-1}(X_{\bar {k}},{\mathcal {F}}))}{\det(1-t\cdot F\,\,|\,\,H_{c}^{0}(X_{\bar {k}},{\mathcal {F}}))\cdots \det(1-t\cdot F\,\,|\,\,H_{c}^{2n}(X_{\bar {k}},{\mathcal {F}}))}}}

where F is everywhere a geometric Frobenius action on l-adic cohomology with compact supports of the sheaf {\displaystyle {\mathcal {F}}}. Taking logarithmic derivatives of both formal power series produces a statement on sums of traces for each finite field extension E of the base field k:

{\displaystyle \sum _{x\in X(E)}\operatorname {tr} (F_{E}\,\,|\,\,{\mathcal {F}}_{x})=\sum _{i=0}^{2n}(-1)^{i}\operatorname {tr} (F_{E}\,\,|\,\,H_{c}^{i}(X_{\bar {k}},{\mathcal {F}}))}
Level

Level justification

Logarithmically differentiate the sheaf L-function determinant formula and identify Frobenius traces over each extension. The finite determinant manipulation is elementary, but the sheaf L-function and etale cohomology are graduate prerequisites.
