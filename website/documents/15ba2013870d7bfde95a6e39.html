Statement

Let A be an n\times n matrix over a field, n\geq2, and let i<j and k<\ell be row and column indices. Write A^I_J for the matrix obtained by deleting rows I and columns J, with the determinant of the empty matrix equal to 1. Then \det(A)\det(A^{i,j}_{k,\ell})=\det(A^j_\ell)\det(A^i_k)-\det(A^j_k)\det(A^i_\ell).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and introductory abstract algebra supply determinants, matrices, field roots, ring operations and divisibility. The displayed assertion uses these directly; no graduate concepts are needed to understand it.

Proof 1: Set m equal to two in Sylvester’s identity — outline

Use Sylvester's determinant identity: for increasing m-element index subsets u,v, form \widetilde A with entries

\widetilde A_{ab}=\det A^{u\setminus\{u_a\}}_{v\setminus\{v_b\}}.

Then \det(A)(\det A^u_v)^{m-1}=\det\widetilde A.

Take m=2, u=(i,j) and v=(k,\ell). The auxiliary matrix is

\begin{pmatrix}\det A^j_\ell&\det A^j_k\\\det A^i_\ell&\det A^i_k\end{pmatrix}.

Expansion of this determinant gives the asserted identity.
