Statement

A closed loop in the family of smooth complex cubic surfaces induces a permutation of the 27 lines on its initial surface, by continuous continuation of those lines.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The family and continuation refer to algebraic-geometric parameter spaces and topology, beyond finite permutation arithmetic.

Proof 1: Continue the lines around a closed loop — outline

As the coefficients of a smooth complex cubic surface are varied, the 27 lines move continuously. As a result, a closed loop in the family of smooth cubic surfaces determines a permutation of the 27 lines.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although a closed-loop continuation permutes a finite set, the objects are families of smooth complex projective cubic surfaces and the continued algebraic lines. Understanding that deformation setting and continuation theorem is substantial algebraic geometry.
