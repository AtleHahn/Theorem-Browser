Statement

In set theory with the axiom of choice, let B be a Boolean algebra, let I be an ideal of B, and let F be a filter of B, with I\cap F=\varnothing. Then there is a prime ideal P of B such that I\subseteq P and P\cap F=\varnothing.

Here an ideal is a nonempty lower set closed under finite joins, a filter is a nonempty upper set closed under finite meets, and a prime ideal is a proper ideal P such that a\wedge b\in P implies a\in P or b\in P.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate algebra and basic order notions suffice; the locally defined ideal/filter conditions are a small related extension. The statement asserts an extension in the usual choice setting, not an independence or model-theoretic result.
