Statement

If M is a closed oriented 2k-manifold, its free middle homology F_k=H_k(M;\mathbb Z)/\operatorname{torsion} carries a perfect integral bilinear pairing F_k\otimes F_k\to\mathbb Z.

Proof 1: Specialize the paired dimensions

Write F_i=H_i(M;\mathbb Z)/\operatorname{torsion}. Use the free homology duality pairing for a closed oriented n-manifold:

F_i\otimes F_{n-i}\longrightarrow\mathbb Z.

It is a duality pairing, meaning that its adjoint map F_i\to\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(F_{n-i},\mathbb Z) is an isomorphism. While for most dimensions, Poincaré duality induces a bilinear pairing between different homology groups, in the middle dimension it induces a bilinear form on a single homology group. For even dimension n=2k, this is literally the middle dimension k, and there is a form on the free part of the middle homology:

F_k\otimes F_k\longrightarrow\mathbb Z.

Level justification

Substituting n=2k is elementary, but the perfect pairing concerns integral manifold homology modulo torsion and Poincare duality. Those objects require substantial algebraic topology.
