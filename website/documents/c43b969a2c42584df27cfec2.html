Statement

Let K\subset S^3 be a nontrivial tame knot, meaning an embedded circle ambient isotopic to a polygonal knot but not to a round circle. Remove an open tubular neighborhood of K, leaving a compact exterior E whose boundary is a torus. The meridian slope is the unoriented isotopy class of an essential simple closed curve on \partial E bounding a disk in the removed solid torus. Glue a solid torus to E by a boundary homeomorphism sending the meridian of the attached torus to any essential simple closed curve with slope different from this meridian slope. The resulting closed three-manifold is not homeomorphic to S^3. Here essential means not contractible in the boundary torus.
