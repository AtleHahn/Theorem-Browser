Statement

Let n\geq1 be an integer, let 0<\lambda<1, and let f,g,h:\mathbb R^n\to[0,\infty) be Lebesgue-measurable functions. If

h((1-\lambda)x+\lambda y)\geq f(x)^{1-\lambda}g(y)^\lambda\qquad\text{for all }x,y\in\mathbb R^n,

then

\int_{\mathbb R^n}h(x)\,dx\geq\left(\int_{\mathbb R^n}f(x)\,dx\right)^{1-\lambda}\left(\int_{\mathbb R^n}g(x)\,dx\right)^\lambda.

The nonnegative Lebesgue integrals may be infinite; the right side is interpreted as zero if either factor integral is zero.
