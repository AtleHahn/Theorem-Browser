Statement

No universal constant c>1/4 can replace 1/4 in the assertion that the image of every injective holomorphic map f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C contains the open disk centered at f(0) of radius c|f\prime(0)|, where \mathbb D=\{|z|<1\}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex differentiability is the complex difference-quotient extension of calculus; univalent means injective. Power-series coefficients, disks and derivative scale are familiar analysis concepts. These definitions suffice for the image or coefficient bound without complex-geometric theory.

Proof 1: Use the omitted point of the Koebe function

The Koebe function k(z)=z/(1-z)^2 is a normalized univalent function: k(0)=0 and k\prime(0)=1. Its image omits -1/4. Consequently that image cannot contain any disk centered at zero of radius greater than 1/4.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the explicit normalized rational map and its omitted value to rule out every larger universal disk. Undergraduate complex analysis and elementary disk geometry suffice; univalence here is simply injectivity of the supplied map.
