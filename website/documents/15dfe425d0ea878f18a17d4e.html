Statement

Let P\in\mathbb Z[x] be a nonconstant polynomial. The set

\{q:q\text{ is a positive prime and }q\mid P(n)\text{ for some }n\in\mathbb N\text{ with }P(n)\ne0\}

is infinite. Here \mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\} and divisibility is in the integers; including or excluding n=0 does not affect infinitude.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Integer polynomial values, prime divisibility and infinitude are accessible through elementary high-school reasoning.
