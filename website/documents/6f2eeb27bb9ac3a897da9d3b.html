Statement

Let f\in\mathbb Z[x] be monic and irreducible over \mathbb Q, of degree n\ge1. There are only finitely many equivalence classes of matrices A\in M_n(\mathbb Z) having characteristic polynomial \det(xI-A)=f(x), under the equivalence A\sim B if B=P^{-1}AP for some P\in\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb Z). Here \operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb Z) consists of the integer matrices with determinant 1 or -1, whose inverses also have integer entries.
