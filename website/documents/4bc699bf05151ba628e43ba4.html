Statement

Let \mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}. The polynomial Q(x,y)=\tfrac12((x+y)^2+x+3y) is a bijection from \mathbb N_0^2 to \mathbb N_0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A pairing polynomial asserts a bijection between infinite sets, requiring the undergraduate concepts of injectivity, surjectivity and countability. A bright US college student should be able to understand the statement after about 30 minutes of focused background research; this rating concerns understanding the claim, not proving it.

Proof 1: The transposed Cantor polynomial is a pairing function — outline

The Cantor polynomial P(x,y)=\tfrac12((x+y)^2+3x+y) is a pairing function. Swap its two variables to obtain the pairing function Q(x,y)=P(y,x).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Compose the supplied bijection with the coordinate-swap bijection and identify the polynomial. Elementary functions on infinite countable sets suffice.

Routine omissions
