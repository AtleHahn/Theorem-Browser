Statement

Let E\to X be a topological G-bundle with fiber F, and fix continuous G-valued transition maps t_{ij} on a trivializing open cover (U_i) satisfying t_{ik}=t_{ij}t_{jk}. Using G itself as fiber with its continuous left multiplication action gives a principal G-bundle over X with the same transition maps.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

General topology and topological group actions are necessary to understand the full statement and construction.

Proof 1: Use the group as fiber — outline

The associated-bundle construction forms a bundle with fiber F' by replacing F by the left G-space F' in the fiber bundle construction theorem, keeping the transition data fixed. If one takes F' to be G with the action of left multiplication then one obtains the associated principal bundle.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof reuses the cocycle construction with fiber G. Understanding why the resulting bundle is principal requires local trivializations and the right G-action on each fiber; this action descends because the transition maps act by left multiplication, which commutes with right multiplication. The combined bundle and action framework exceeds the undergraduate allowance even though the group-law check is simple.

Proof 1 — Use the group as fiber — outline

Statement used:

A fixed continuous G-valued transition cocycle can be used with any continuous left G-space F prime to glue a bundle with fiber F prime over the same base.

Application in proof:

Take F prime=G with left multiplication; right multiplication gives the free transitive action on fibers.

Beyond undergraduate level
