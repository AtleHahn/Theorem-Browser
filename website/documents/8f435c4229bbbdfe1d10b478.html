Statement

Let K be a nonarchimedean local field with valuation ring \mathcal O_K and residue field k, and let A be an abelian variety over K. For any prime \ell\ne\operatorname{char}k, A has good reduction if and only if its \ell-adic Tate module is unramified. Here a nonarchimedean local field is a complete discretely valued field with finite residue field. An abelian variety is a geometrically connected smooth projective algebraic group. Good reduction means that A is the generic fiber of an abelian scheme over \mathcal O_K, a smooth proper group scheme with geometrically connected fibers. With K^{\mathrm{sep}} a separable closure,

T_\ell(A)=\varprojlim_{m\ge1} A[\ell^m](K^{\mathrm{sep}}),

where A[\ell^m] is the kernel of multiplication by \ell^m and transition maps multiply by \ell. Unramified means that the inertia subgroup of \operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K), the subgroup acting trivially on the algebraic closure of the residue field, acts trivially on this module. Elliptic curves are included as the dimension-one case.
