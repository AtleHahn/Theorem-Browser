Statement

Let o be a Dedekind domain with fraction field K, and let L/K be a finite separable extension. Let O be the integral closure of o in L, and choose \theta\in O with L=K(\theta). Write f\in o[x] for its monic minimal polynomial and

\mathfrak F=\{a\in O:aO\subseteq o[\theta]\}

for the conductor. Let \mathfrak p be a nonzero prime ideal of o such that \mathfrak pO+\mathfrak F=O. Factor the reduction of f as

\overline f=\prod_{i=1}^r\overline f_i^{\,e_i}\quad\text{in }(o/\mathfrak p)[x],

where the \overline f_i are pairwise distinct monic irreducible polynomials and e_i\ge1. Choose lifts f_i\in o[x] of these polynomials. Then

\mathfrak P_i=\mathfrak pO+f_i(\theta)O

are distinct prime ideals of O, and

\mathfrak pO=\prod_{i=1}^r\mathfrak P_i^{\,e_i}.

A Dedekind domain is a Noetherian integrally closed integral domain whose nonzero prime ideals are maximal. Integral closure consists of the elements satisfying a monic polynomial over o; \mathfrak pO denotes the ideal generated in O by \mathfrak p. Reduction and lifting mean reducing or choosing coefficients modulo \mathfrak p.
