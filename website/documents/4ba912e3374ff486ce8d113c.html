Statement

For a pointed space Y, write \operatorname{SP}(Y) for its infinite symmetric product: finite unordered tuples of points of Y, with basepoint entries ignored, with the usual direct-limit topology of finite symmetric products. Fix an integer n\geq1. Suppose \varphi_Y:\pi_n\operatorname{SP}(Y)\to\widetilde H_n(Y;\mathbb Z) is a natural family of homomorphisms for connected pointed CW complexes. Let i_Y:Y\to\operatorname{SP}(Y) send a point to its one-point tuple, and let h_Y:\pi_n(Y)\to\widetilde H_n(Y;\mathbb Z) be the Hurewicz homomorphism. If \varphi_{S^n}(i_{S^n})_*([\mathrm{id}_{S^n}])=h_{S^n}([\mathrm{id}_{S^n}]), then \varphi_Y(i_Y)_*=h_Y for every such Y.

Level justification

Proof 1: Track the sphere generator through naturality

Every element of \pi_n(Y) is represented by a pointed map f:S^n\to Y and is the image of [\mathrm{id}_{S^n}]. Naturality of i, \varphi and the Hurewicz homomorphism gives the commuting sides of the comparison. Tracking this sphere generator through them shows that the equality on S^n gives \varphi_Y(i_Y)_*([f])=h_Y([f]).
