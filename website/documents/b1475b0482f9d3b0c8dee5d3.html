Statement

Let X be a regular topological space and let Z_\varepsilon be X-valued random variables with Borel probability laws \mu_\varepsilon, for \varepsilon>0. Suppose these laws satisfy a large-deviation principle with good rate function I:X\to[0,\infty]: I is lower semicontinuous with compact sets \{I\le a\} for finite a, and

-\inf_G I\le\liminf_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\mu_\varepsilon(G),\qquad \limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\mu_\varepsilon(C)\le-\inf_C I

for open G and closed C. Let \phi:X\to\mathbb R be continuous. Assume either

\lim_{M\to\infty}\limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\mathbb E\left[e^{\phi(Z_\varepsilon)/\varepsilon}\mathbf1_{\{\phi(Z_\varepsilon)\ge M\}}\right]=-\infty,

or for some \gamma>1,

\limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\mathbb E\left[e^{\gamma\phi(Z_\varepsilon)/\varepsilon}\right]<\infty.

Then

\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\mathbb E[e^{\phi(Z_\varepsilon)/\varepsilon}]=\sup_{x\in X}(\phi(x)-I(x)).

Regularity means a point and a disjoint closed set have disjoint open neighborhoods. The symbol \mathbf1 denotes the event indicator; use \log0=-\infty and \inf\varnothing=\infty.
