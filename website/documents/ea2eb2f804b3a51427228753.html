Statement

The real numbers a,a+d,a+2d are the positive side lengths of a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle if and only if a>0 and -a/3<d<a.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The progression and its permissible real parameters are elementary algebra.

Proof 1: Solve the arithmetic-progression inequalities — outline

Local side-existence construction

Let a,b,c>0 satisfy all three non-strict triangle inequalities. Place A=(0,0) and B=(c,0), and attempt to place C=(q,h) with AC=b and BC=a. We need to prove that there exists a real number h consistent with the values a,b,c, in which case this triangle exists.

By the Pythagorean theorem we have b^2=h^2+q^2 and a^2=h^2+(c-q)^2. Subtracting these yields a^2-b^2=c^2-2cq. This equation allows us to express q in terms of the sides of the triangle:

q=\frac{-a^2+b^2+c^2}{2c}.

For the height of the triangle we have h^2=b^2-q^2. By replacing q with the formula given above, we have

h^2=b^2-\left(\frac{-a^2+b^2+c^2}{2c}\right)^2.

For a real number h to satisfy this, h^2 must be non-negative. Since 4c^2 and a+b+c are positive, the following inequalities are equivalent:

\begin{aligned} 0&\le b^2-\left(\frac{-a^2+b^2+c^2}{2c}\right)^2,\\ 0&\le\left(b-\frac{-a^2+b^2+c^2}{2c}\right) \left(b+\frac{-a^2+b^2+c^2}{2c}\right),\\ 0&\le\bigl(a^2-(b-c)^2\bigr)\bigl((b+c)^2-a^2\bigr),\\ 0&\le(a+b-c)(a-b+c)(b+c+a)(b+c-a),\\ 0&\le(a+b-c)(a+c-b)(b+c-a). \end{aligned}

The last inequality holds if the triangle inequality is satisfied for all sides. Therefore, there does exist a real number h\ge0 consistent with the sides a,b,c, and the triangle exists. If each triangle inequality holds strictly, h>0 and the triangle is nondegenerate (has positive area); but if one of the inequalities holds with equality, h=0 and the triangle is degenerate. Conversely, a nondegenerate triangle satisfies all three strict triangle inequalities.

Solve the progression inequalities

Consider a triangle whose sides are in an arithmetic progression and let the sides be a,a+d,a+2d. Then the triangle inequality requires that

\begin{array}{rcccl} 0&<&a&<&2a+3d,\\ 0&<&a+d&<&2a+2d,\\ 0&<&a+2d&<&2a+d. \end{array}

To satisfy all these inequalities requires

a>0\quad\text{and}\quad-\frac a3<d<a.

Conversely, these bounds satisfy all the displayed inequalities, so the side-existence construction applies.

Level justification
