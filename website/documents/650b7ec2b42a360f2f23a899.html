Statement

Let X be the complex projective line with at most two orbifold points, of arbitrary finite cyclic orders. Every holomorphic vector bundle E of finite rank r on this orbifold is isomorphic to a direct sum L_1\oplus\cdots\oplus L_r of holomorphic orbifold line bundles. The multiset of isomorphism classes of the L_i is uniquely determined by E. The underlying complex curve is \mathbb{CP}^1. Near an orbifold point of order m\ge2 its chart is a disk with the action z\mapsto\zeta z of the mth roots of unity, and the coarse coordinate is z^m; away from those points the charts have trivial group. An orbifold vector bundle consists of holomorphic vector bundles on these charts with compatible actions of the chart groups, linear on fibers, and compatible equivariant transition identifications. A line bundle has fiber dimension one.
