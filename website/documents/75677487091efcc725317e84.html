Statement

Let X be a locally Euclidean topological space which is not Hausdorff. Then X is locally metrizable but is not metrizable.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Hausdorffness, product topology, metrizability and local Euclidean neighborhoods belong to basic topology or are direct definitions within it. No smooth-manifold structure is asserted.

Proof 1: Euclidean charts and the Hausdorff obstruction

Each point has a neighbourhood homeomorphic to a Euclidean open set, hence a metrizable neighbourhood. But every metric space is Hausdorff and this is invariant under homeomorphism. Therefore a non-Hausdorff space cannot admit a compatible metric.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Transport Euclidean metrics to chart neighborhoods and use the Hausdorff obstruction globally. Undergraduate metric and point-set topology suffice; no smooth manifold or tangent-bundle theory is used.
