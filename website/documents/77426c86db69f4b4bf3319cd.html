Statement

Let A be a commutative Noetherian integral domain, B a finitely generated commutative A-algebra, and M a finitely generated B-module. Then there is 0\ne f\in A such that M_f=M\otimes_A A[f^{-1}] is free as an A[f^{-1}]-module. Noetherian means every ideal is finitely generated, A[f^{-1}] consists of fractions with denominators powers of f, and free means possessing a basis, which need not be finite. If additionally B=\bigoplus_{d\ge0}B_d is graded with A acting in degree zero and M is a graded B-module, f can be chosen so that every graded component of M_f is free over A[f^{-1}] simultaneously. Grading means multiplication and module action add degrees.
