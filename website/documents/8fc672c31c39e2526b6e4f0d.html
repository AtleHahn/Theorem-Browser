Statement

Let \Omega be a set and let \mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal P(\Omega) be an algebra of sets: it contains \Omega and is closed under complements relative to \Omega and finite unions. A monotone class is a family of subsets closed under unions of increasing countable sequences and intersections of decreasing countable sequences.

Let M(\mathcal G) be the smallest monotone class containing \mathcal G. Then

M(\mathcal G)=\sigma(\mathcal G),

where \sigma(\mathcal G) is the smallest family containing \mathcal G and closed under complements and countable unions.

Proof 1: Auxiliary monotone classes and minimality (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Theorem0.2 and Lemma0.1, pp.1–2).

In Lemma 0.1 read F_k=\bigcup_{i=1}^k E_i. The omitted closure checks use De Morgan’s laws and the identities E\cup\bigcup F_k=\bigcup(E\cup F_k) and E\cup\bigcap F_k=\bigcap(E\cup F_k). These make both auxiliary families monotone classes. If \Omega=\varnothing, its only algebra is \{\varnothing\}, and the conclusion is immediate.
