Statement

Let p be a positive prime and L,M integers satisfying 4p=L^2+27M^2. Every integer divisor of L or M is a cube modulo p.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The claim concerns integer divisors and modular cubes.

Proof 1: Apply the prime-divisor criterion to a divisor — outline

The first branch of Lehmer’s stated criterion says that any prime q dividing LM is a cubic residue modulo p. It follows that every number dividing L or M is a cubic residue modulo p.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply the supplied numerical criterion to prime factors and multiply their cube representatives; negative signs are also cubes. The proof uses integer factorization and congruences only.
