Statement

Let a finite-dimensional representation V of a finite group over a field be expressed in two ways as a finite direct sum of irreducible subrepresentations. The multisets of isomorphism classes of summands in the two decompositions are equal.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement compares isomorphism classes and multiplicities of irreducible group representations, requiring abstract algebra.

Proof 1: Compare the induced composition series

For a decomposition V=V_1\oplus\cdots\oplus V_r, the chain 0\subset V_1\subset V_1\oplus V_2\subset\cdots\subset V has successive quotients isomorphic to the irreducible summands, so it is a composition series. Do the same for the other decomposition. The Jordan–Hölder theorem states that any two composition series have the same multiset of simple factors. Applying it gives equality of the irreducible multiplicities.
