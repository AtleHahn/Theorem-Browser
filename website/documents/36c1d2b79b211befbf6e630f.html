Statement

Let f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb C be 2\pi-periodic, let r be a positive integer, and let 0<\alpha<1. Suppose there exist C>0, a positive integer n_0, and trigonometric polynomials P_n of degree n for every integer n\ge n_0, such that

\sup_{0\le x\le2\pi}|f(x)-P_n(x)|\le Cn^{-(r+\alpha)}.

A trigonometric polynomial of degree n is a finite sum \sum_{j=-n}^n c_j e^{ijx} with at least one of c_n,c_{-n} nonzero. Then \varphi=f-P_{n_0} has a bounded rth derivative which is \alpha-Hölder continuous: there is H<\infty such that |\varphi^{(r)}(x)-\varphi^{(r)}(y)|\le H|x-y|^\alpha for all real x,y.
