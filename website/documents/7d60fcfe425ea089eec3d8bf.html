Statement

Let \Omega\subset\mathbb R^2 be a Lipschitz domain and u\in H_0^1(\Omega), the closure of C_c^\infty(\Omega) in the H^1 Sobolev norm. There is a constant C, depending only on \Omega, such that \|u\|_{L^4(\Omega)}\le C\|u\|_{L^2(\Omega)}^{1/2}\|u\|_{H_0^1(\Omega)}^{1/2}, where the last factor uses the full inherited Sobolev norm.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement involves weak differentiability, Sobolev closure and Lebesgue norms, beyond calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Substitute the stated Gagliardo–Nirenberg parameters

Use the Gagliardo–Nirenberg interpolation inequality displayed in the source,

\|u\|_{L^p}\le C\|u\|_{L^q}^\alpha\|u\|_{H_0^s}^{1-\alpha},

under the conditions p>q\ge1, s>n(1/2-1/p) and 1/p=\alpha/q+(1-\alpha)(1/2-s/n). The source specifies p=4,q=2,s=1 and, for n=2, \alpha=1/2. These parameters give precisely the asserted Sobolev-norm inequality.
