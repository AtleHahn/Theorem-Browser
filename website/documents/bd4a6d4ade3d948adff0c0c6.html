Statement

Let U=\varinjlim U(N), O=\varinjlim O(N) and \mathrm{Sp}=\varinjlim\mathrm{Sp}(N) be the stable classical groups under the standard inclusions. Use identity basepoints, based loop spaces \Omega, and the standard based homotopy types for classifying spaces BU,BO,B\mathrm{Sp}. Then

\Omega^2U\simeq U,\qquad\Omega^8O\simeq O,\qquad\Omega^8\mathrm{Sp}\simeq\mathrm{Sp}.

Proof 1: Iterate the two-cycle and eight-cycle (outline)

Write C=\mathbb Z\times BU, R=\mathbb Z\times BO and Q=\mathbb Z\times B\mathrm{Sp}, based at the zero component. Use the following stated Bott equivalences as external prerequisites:

\Omega U\simeq C,\quad\Omega C\simeq U,\Omega O\simeq O/U,\quad\Omega(O/U)\simeq U/\mathrm{Sp},\quad \Omega(U/\mathrm{Sp})\simeq Q,\quad\Omega Q\simeq\mathrm{Sp},\Omega\mathrm{Sp}\simeq\mathrm{Sp}/U,\quad\Omega(\mathrm{Sp}/U)\simeq U/O,\quad \Omega(U/O)\simeq R,\quad\Omega R\simeq O.

Here the quotients use the stable standard inclusions of classical groups. Looping preserves based homotopy equivalences.

These equivalences immediately yield the Bott periodicity theorems:

\Omega^2U\simeq U,\qquad\Omega^8O\simeq O,\qquad\Omega^8\mathrm{Sp}\simeq\mathrm{Sp}.
