Statement

Let X=[0,1] carry Lebesgue measure \mu and let Y=[0,1] carry counting measure \nu on all subsets. For f(x,y)=\mathbf1_{\{x=y\}}, \int_Y\int_X f\,d\mu\,d\nu=0 while \int_X\int_Y f\,d\nu\,d\mu=1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Comparing Lebesgue and counting measures requires measure theory.

Proof 1: A diagonal gives unequal iterated integrals without sigma-finiteness

Each horizontal section is a singleton of Lebesgue measure zero, so integration first over X gives the zero function. Each vertical section is a singleton of counting measure one, so integration first over Y gives the constant one function. The outer integrals are therefore zero and one.
