Statement

Let J be a directed partially ordered set and (Y_j,p_{ij}) an inverse system of Hausdorff spaces with continuous maps p_{ij}:Y_j\to Y_i for i\le j, with p_{ii}=\mathrm{id} and p_{ik}=p_{ij}p_{jk}. Give X=\{(y_j)\in\prod_jY_j:y_i=p_{ij}(y_j)\text{ for }i\le j\} the subspace topology of the product, and let p_j:X\to Y_j be coordinate projection. Suppose probability measures \mu_\varepsilon on X have pushforward laws (p_j)_*\mu_\varepsilon satisfying a large-deviation principle with good rate I_j for every j. Then \mu_\varepsilon satisfies such a principle on X with good rate I(x)=\sup_{j\in J}I_j(p_j(x)).

A good rate is a lower semicontinuous map to [0,\infty] with compact finite sublevel sets, not identically infinite. The large-deviation principle for laws \rho_\varepsilon and rate R means \limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\rho_\varepsilon(F)\le-\inf_F R for closed F and \liminf_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\rho_\varepsilon(G)\ge-\inf_G R for open G, using \log0=-\infty and \inf\varnothing=\infty. Directed means each pair of indices has a common upper bound.
