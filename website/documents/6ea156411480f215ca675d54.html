Statement

For abelian groups A,X,Y, there is a natural bijection \operatorname{Hom}(X\otimes A,Y)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(X,\operatorname{Hom}(A,Y)). Thus -\otimes A is left adjoint to \operatorname{Hom}(A,-).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full assertion requires abelian groups, tensor-product universal property, natural transformations.

Proof 1: Source construction outline

The correspondence is natural on the basis, firstly, that these are two alternative descriptions of the bilinear mappings from X × A to Y.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The argument appeals to two descriptions of biadditive maps but gives no construction of the tensor product or explanation of naturality. Following it in the stated category of abelian groups requires the group tensor-product universal property, the abelian-group structure on Hom(A,Y), and naturality of currying in both variables. The cumulative prerequisite burden is borderline from the stated baseline, so keep Beyond undergraduate provisionally rather than assuming prior tensor-product or category-theory coursework.

For abelian groups X,A,Y, homomorphisms X tensor A -> Y correspond uniquely to maps X times A -> Y additive in each variable.

Application in proof:

Identify a tensor-product homomorphism with its biadditive map, then curry it to a homomorphism X->Hom(A,Y); evaluation reverses the correspondence.
