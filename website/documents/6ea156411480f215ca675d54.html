Statement

For abelian groups A,X,Y, there is a natural bijection \operatorname{Hom}(X\otimes A,Y)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(X,\operatorname{Hom}(A,Y)). Thus -\otimes A is left adjoint to \operatorname{Hom}(A,-).

Level

Level justification

Proof 1: Source construction outline

The correspondence is natural on the basis, firstly, that these are two alternative descriptions of the bilinear mappings from X × A to Y.

Level

Level justification

The full statement is a natural adjunction involving the tensor product and Hom functors on abelian groups. The tensor product is constructed by imposing additivity relations on finite formal sums, and its universal property identifies maps from it with biadditive maps. Currying and evaluation give inverse correspondences; compatibility with precomposition and postcomposition establishes naturality.

Kind:

Definition

Treatment:

Implicitly assumed

Statement used:

For abelian groups X,A,Y, homomorphisms X tensor A -> Y correspond uniquely to maps X times A -> Y additive in each variable.

Application in proof:

Identify a tensor-product homomorphism with its biadditive map, then curry it to a homomorphism X->Hom(A,Y); evaluation reverses the correspondence.

Application level:

Undergraduate level

Level justification:

The quotient of the free abelian group on pairs by the two additivity relations gives the tensor product and its universal property. Pointwise addition, currying and direct composition identities establish the required Hom correspondence and naturality.
