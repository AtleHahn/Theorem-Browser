Statement

In characteristic zero, a projective variety has a finite family of birational models such that every valuation in its Zariski–Riemann space has a nonsingular center on at least one model.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Birational models, valuations and their centers in the Zariski-Riemann space require substantial birational geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Extract a finite subcover from local uniformizations — outline

Local uniformization provides a model with a nonsingular center for each valuation. The corresponding nonsingular-center loci give an open cover of the Zariski–Riemann space. Its quasi-compactness yields a finite subcover and thus a finite family of models.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Cover valuation space by nonsingular-center loci and extract a finite subcover. The compactness inference is elementary, but birational models and Zariski–Riemann valuation spaces require advanced algebraic geometry.
