Statement

Let G be a connected complex semisimple group, T\subset B a maximal torus in a Borel subgroup, W its Weyl group and P the lattice of integral weights (characters of T). Let \rho be half the sum of the positive roots. Then

w*\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho

defines an action of W on P.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Semisimple complex groups; Weyl groups and integral weights; Positive roots. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Cancellation of the shift (outline)

For any integral weight \lambda and w in the Weyl group W, we set

w*\lambda:=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho,

where \rho denotes the half-sum of positive roots of G. It is straightforward to check that this defines a group action, although this action is not linear, unlike the usual Weyl group action.

The source leaves the action-law check implicit. For well-definedness on P, use the root-system facts that W preserves P and that w\rho-\rho belongs to the root lattice, which is contained in P.
