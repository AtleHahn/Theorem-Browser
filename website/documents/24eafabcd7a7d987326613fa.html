Statement

If u,v,w are positive integers, r\ge1 is an integer, \gcd(u,v)=1 and uv=w^r, then u and v are each rth powers of positive integers.

Proof 1: Separate prime exponents in coprime factors

The fundamental theorem of arithmetic states that any positive integer can be written uniquely as a product of prime numbers, up to rearrangement, with the empty product for 1.

One consequence of this unique factorization property is that if an rth power of a number equals a product such as

w^r=uv

and if u and v are coprime (share no prime factors), then u and v are themselves the rth powers of two other numbers.

The source states this consequence without spelling out the prime-exponent check.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Unique prime factorization assigns each prime entirely to one coprime factor. Its exponent is a multiple of r, so each factor is an rth power.
