Statement

Let f:X\to Y be a proper morphism of smooth quasi-projective schemes over a field and let \mathcal F^\bullet be a bounded complex of coherent sheaves on X. Let f_! be the alternating higher-direct-image map on K_0, f_* the pushforward on rational Chow groups, ch the Chern character, and td the Todd class. Put T_f=[TX]-[f^*TY]\in K_0(X). Then

\operatorname{ch}(f_!\mathcal F^\bullet)=f_*(\operatorname{ch}(\mathcal F^\bullet)\operatorname{td}(T_f)).
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Derived direct images, algebraic K-theory, Chow groups and characteristic classes require substantial algebraic geometry. Proof 1: Relative-tangent form of Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch — outline

The Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch theorem relates the pushforward maps by the formula

\operatorname{ch}(f_!\mathcal F^\bullet)\operatorname{td}(TY) =f_*(\operatorname{ch}(\mathcal F^\bullet)\operatorname{td}(TX)).

In fact, since the Todd class is functorial and multiplicative in exact sequences, we can rewrite the Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch formula as

\operatorname{ch}(f_!\mathcal F^\bullet) =f_*(\operatorname{ch}(\mathcal F^\bullet)\operatorname{td}(T_f)),

where T_f is the relative tangent class of f, defined as the element TX-f^*(TY) in K_0(X).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use Todd multiplicativity, the projection formula and the inverse Todd class to rewrite GRR with the virtual relative tangent class. K-theory, coherent complexes and Chow pushforwards are graduate algebraic geometry.
