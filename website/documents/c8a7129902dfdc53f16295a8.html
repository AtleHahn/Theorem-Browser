Statement

Rings below are associative. The assertions hold both without a multiplicative-identity requirement and in the unital convention with identity-preserving homomorphisms and unital subrings. An ideal is two-sided unless a side is specified.

Let I be an ideal of a ring R and q:R\to R/I its quotient map. Sending A to A/I=q(A) is an inclusion-preserving bijection between subrings of R containing I and subrings of R/I, with inverse B\mapsto q^{-1}(B). The same assignments restrict to bijections between left ideals containing I and left ideals of R/I, between right ideals containing I and right ideals of R/I, and between two-sided ideals containing I and two-sided ideals of R/I.
