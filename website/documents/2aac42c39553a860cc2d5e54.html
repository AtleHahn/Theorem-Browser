Statement

Over a field of characteristic p>6, threefold resolution follows from Albanese’s multiplicity reduction and Abhyankar’s resolution theorem for threefold singularities of multiplicity less than p.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Fields of positive characteristic; Threefold singularities; Resolution. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compare the multiplicity bound with the characteristic

The restriction on the characteristic arises because Abhyankar shows that it is possible to resolve any singularity of a 3-fold of multiplicity less than the characteristic, and then uses Albanese's method to show that singularities can be reduced to those of multiplicity at most (dimension)! = 3! = 6.

Level justification

The geometric prerequisites clearly exceed undergraduate level. The primary source additionally assumes an algebraically closed field and distinguishes local resolution from global patching; the companion does not state these qualifications. Retain Beyond undergraduate provisionally pending clarification of its scope.

Since the preceding reduction gives multiplicity <=6 and p>6, apply the small-multiplicity resolution result.
