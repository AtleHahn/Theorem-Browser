Statement

A left Hilbert algebra is an associative complex algebra \mathfrak A with involution \sharp and inner product such that left multiplication is bounded, (xy,z)=(y,x^\sharp z), the involution is closable, and the span of products is dense. Its Hilbert completion is taken in this inner product.

Let H be its Hilbert completion. For x\in\mathfrak A, left multiplication y\mapsto xy extends to a bounded operator \lambda(x)\in B(H), and \lambda:\mathfrak A\to B(H) is a *-homomorphism, with \lambda(x^\sharp)=\lambda(x)^*.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Left Hilbert algebras, closable involutions and their operator representations require substantial operator-algebra background. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Extend left multiplication and its adjoint law — outline

Extend left multiplication to H using its boundedness from the first axiom. Associativity gives the multiplication law, and the second axiom supplies the adjoint law. Thus the extended operators form the stated *-homomorphism. Extension from the dense subspace to its Hilbert completion is the standard bounded-operator extension theorem.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Extend bounded multiplication to the Hilbert completion and transfer multiplication and adjoint identities by density. Involutive Hilbert algebras, closable involutions and operator adjoints require advanced functional analysis.
