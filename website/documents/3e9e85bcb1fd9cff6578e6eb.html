Statement

Every finite abelian extension K of \mathbb Q is contained in a cyclotomic field: for some positive integer n, K\subseteq\mathbb Q(\zeta_n) inside a fixed algebraic closure of \mathbb Q, where \zeta_n is a primitive nth root of unity. Finite abelian extension means a finite-dimensional Galois field extension whose group of automorphisms fixing \mathbb Q is abelian. Galois means normal and separable; in characteristic zero separability is automatic. Normal means every irreducible rational polynomial having a root in K splits completely there.
