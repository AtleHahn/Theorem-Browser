Statement

Let n\geq1 be an integer, and let g be any Riemannian metric on complex projective space \mathbb{CP}^n, the space of complex lines through the origin in \mathbb C^{n+1}.

Write \operatorname{stsys}_2(g) for the infimum of the g-areas of rational two-dimensional cycles representing the homology class of a complex projective line \mathbb{CP}^1\subset\mathbb{CP}^n. Here rational cycles have rational coefficients and zero boundary; area is counted with absolute coefficients and multiplicities. Write \operatorname{vol}_{2n}(g) for the total Riemannian volume.

Then

\operatorname{stsys}_2(g)^n\leq n!\,\operatorname{vol}_{2n}(g).

The constant n! is optimal: equality is attained by the Fubini–Study metric, the quotient metric on \mathbb{CP}^n=S^{2n+1}/S^1 induced from the round unit sphere in \mathbb C^{n+1}, where S^1 acts by scalar multiplication.
