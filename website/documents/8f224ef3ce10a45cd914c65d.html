Statement

Let (M,g) be a smooth globally hyperbolic spacetime. Every smooth Lorentzian metric conformal to g admits an isometric embedding into a Lorentz–Minkowski space of sufficiently large dimension.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Globally hyperbolic spacetimes; Conformal Lorentzian metrics; Isometric Minkowski embeddings. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Preserve global hyperbolicity and invoke steep temporal functions

Global hyperbolicity is preserved under conformal changes of the metric. The source-stated theorem says every globally hyperbolic spacetime admits a steep temporal function, whose gradient has squared norm at most −1. The isometric embedding criterion says precisely that existence of such a function permits an embedding into some Lorentz–Minkowski space. Apply these results to the conformally changed metric.

Level justification

Preserve global hyperbolicity under the conformal change, obtain a steep temporal function and apply the embedding criterion. Lorentzian causal structure, metric gradients and isometric embeddings require graduate geometry.
