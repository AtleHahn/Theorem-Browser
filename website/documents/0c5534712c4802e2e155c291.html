Statement

Let a convex 2n-gon have its sides paired into opposite parallel pairs, with pair separations h_1,\ldots,h_n. For any interior point P, the sum of the perpendicular distances from P to the supporting lines of all sides equals h_1+\cdots+h_n and is independent of P. In particular this holds for every parallelogram.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses convex polygons, parallel lines and their fixed separations.

Proof 1: Sum the fixed separations of parallel pairs

An interior point lies between the two supporting lines of each opposite parallel pair. The two perpendicular distances therefore add to their fixed separation h_i. Sum these identities over the n pairs, exactly as in the source.

Level
High school only
Level justification

For each parallel pair add the two perpendicular distances to get the fixed separation, then sum over pairs. School plane geometry and finite addition suffice.
