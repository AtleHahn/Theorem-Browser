Proof 1: Digits 3 and 7 encode binary sequences injectively — outline

Replace each binary digit zero by 3 and each binary digit one by 7, and regard the resulting sequence as a decimal fraction. Different such digit strings represent different real numbers, giving an injection into the reals.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The allowed decimal digits avoid the terminating-versus-nine-tail ambiguity, so different binary strings give different real sums. Understanding infinite expansions and their convergent geometric tails is elementary real analysis.
