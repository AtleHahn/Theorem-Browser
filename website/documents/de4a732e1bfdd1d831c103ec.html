Statement

For integers k\ge2 and n\ge0, let p(n,k) be the probability that n independent tosses of a fair coin contain no run of k consecutive heads. Let \alpha_k be the smallest positive real root of x^{k+1}=2^{k+1}(x-1) and set \beta_k=(2-\alpha_k)/(k+1-k\alpha_k). Then \lim_{n\to\infty}p(n,k)\alpha_k^{n+1}=\beta_k.

Review notes

  • The generating-function numerator is corrected as follows: the avoidance generating function is (1-(z/2)^k)/(1-z+z^(k+1)/2^(k+1)). Its dominant coefficient times alpha is (2-alpha)/(k+1-k*alpha). The denominator after canceling 1-z/2 is 1-sum(j=1..k)(z/2)^j; monotonicity and strict triangle inequality give its unique least-modulus root, simple. Other poles contribute polynomial factors times strictly smaller exponentials. Partial fractions follow from polynomial division and coprime-factor Bezout identities.; Proof boundary: Feller, Chapter XIII §7, pp.323–325, equations (7.6)–(7.11).; Statement match: Set p=q=1/2 and r=k.
