Statement

For integers k\ge2 and n\ge0, let p(n,k) be the probability that n independent tosses of a fair coin contain no run of k consecutive heads. Let \alpha_k be the smallest positive real root of x^{k+1}=2^{k+1}(x-1) and set \beta_k=(2-\alpha_k)/(k+1-k\alpha_k). Then \lim_{n\to\infty}p(n,k)\alpha_k^{n+1}=\beta_k.
