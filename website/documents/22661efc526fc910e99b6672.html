Statement

Let M be a connected three-dimensional manifold whose fundamental group is finitely generated. Compactness of M is not assumed. There is a compact connected three-dimensional submanifold C\subseteq M, possibly with boundary, such that inclusion induces an isomorphism

\pi_1(C,x_0)\longrightarrow\pi_1(M,x_0)

for a base point x_0\in C.

Here the fundamental group consists of based loops modulo based homotopy, with concatenation as product, and finitely generated means that finitely many elements and their inverses generate the group. Manifolds use the Hausdorff and second-countable convention. Such a submanifold C is called a compact core.
