Statement

Every finite triangle-free planar simple undirected graph G=(V,E) has a proper coloring with at most three colors: there is a map

c:V\longrightarrow\{1,2,3\}

with c(u)\ne c(v) for every edge \{u,v\}\in E.

A simple graph has no loops or parallel edges. It is triangle-free if no three distinct vertices are pairwise adjacent, and planar if it can be drawn in the plane with edges intersecting only at shared endpoints.
