Statement

Let R_* be a graded MU_*-algebra which is flat as an MU_*-module, where MU_* is the coefficient ring of complex cobordism. Then E_*(X)=MU_*(X)\otimes_{MU_*}R_* defines a generalized homology theory on CW complexes, with the analogous formula on CW pairs.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex cobordism and generalized homology theories require substantial algebraic topology. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Preserve the homology axioms by flat tensoring — outline

Define for a topological space X

E_*(X)=MU_*(X)\otimes_{MU_*}R_*.

It is obviously a homotopy invariant functor, which fulfills excision. The problem is that tensoring in general does not preserve exact sequences. Here R_* is flat over MU_*, so tensoring does preserve exact sequences, giving the stated homology theory.

Level justification

Tensor the complex-cobordism theory and preserve its exact sequences by flatness. Generalized homology, CW pairs, graded coefficient rings and flat modules are substantial algebraic topology and homological algebra.
