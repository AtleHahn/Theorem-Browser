Statement

A trigonometric polynomial of degree at most d is a function T(x)=\sum_{j=-d}^d a_je^{ijx} with complex coefficients. Let k\ge1 be an integer. There is a constant c_k>0 such that for every continuous 2\pi-periodic f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb C and integer n\ge1, there is a trigonometric polynomial T of degree at most n satisfying \sup_x|f(x)-T(x)|\le c_k\omega_k(1/n,f). Here \omega_k(\delta,f)=\sup_{0\le h\le\delta}\sup_{x\in\mathbb R}\left|\sum_{j=0}^k(-1)^{k-j}\binom kj f(x+jh)\right| is the periodic modulus of smoothness of order k.
