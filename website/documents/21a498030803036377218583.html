Statement

A signed subset assigns to each of its elements one sign in \{1,-1\}. For 1\le r\le n, fix one element of an n-element set and its sign in \{1,-1\}. The signed r-subsets including that element with that sign form a family of size 2^{r-1}\binom{n-1}{r-1}. Any two share an element with the same sign.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All signed-object definitions and finite choices are explicit.
