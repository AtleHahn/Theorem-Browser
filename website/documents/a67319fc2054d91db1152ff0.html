Statement

Let g be a smooth pseudo-Riemannian metric on a coordinate domain of dimension n, with metric coefficients g_{ij} and Levi-Civita Christoffel symbols

\Gamma^k_{ij}=\rho_i\delta^k_j+\rho_j\delta^k_i

for smooth real functions \rho_i. Here i,j,k,l range from 1 to n, \delta is the Kronecker delta and \partial_i is coordinate differentiation. Use the curvature convention R_{ijkl}=g(R(\partial_i,\partial_j)\partial_k,\partial_l) with R(X,Y)=\nabla_X\nabla_Y-\nabla_Y\nabla_X-\nabla_{[X,Y]}. Then

{\displaystyle R_{ijkl}=(\partial _{i}\rho _{j}-\partial _{j}\rho _{i})g_{kl}+g_{jl}(\partial _{i}\rho _{k}-\rho _{i}\rho _{k})-g_{il}(\partial _{j}\rho _{k}-\rho _{j}\rho _{k}).}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Pseudo-Riemannian metrics; Levi-Civita connection; Christoffel symbols; Riemann curvature tensor. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.
