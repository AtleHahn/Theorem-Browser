Statement

Let G be a connected noncompact real semisimple Lie group with finite center and K a maximal compact subgroup. Let W be the restricted Weyl group with length \ell. For standard spherical intertwiners A(s,\lambda) and their scalars A(s,\lambda)\xi_0=c_s(\lambda)\xi_0 on the constant fixed vector, at parameters where the operators and scalars are defined and finite, c_{s_1s_2}(\lambda)=c_{s_1}(s_2\lambda)c_{s_2}(\lambda) whenever \ell(s_1s_2)=\ell(s_1)+\ell(s_2).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Weyl group length; Standard spherical intertwiners; Fixed-vector eigenvalues. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Multiplicativity of spherical c-functions — source argument

The key property of the intertwining operators is the multiplicative cocycle property

A(s_1s_2,\lambda)=A(s_1,s_2\lambda)A(s_2,\lambda),

whenever

\ell(s_1s_2)=\ell(s_1)+\ell(s_2).

The c-functions are defined by the equation

A(s,\lambda)\xi_0=c_s(\lambda)\xi_0,

where \xi_0 is the constant fixed vector. The cocycle property of the intertwining operators implies a similar multiplicative property for the c-functions:

c_{s_1s_2}(\lambda)=c_{s_1}(s_2\lambda)c_{s_2}(\lambda)

provided \ell(s_1s_2)=\ell(s_1)+\ell(s_2). This follows by applying the operator identity to the nonzero vector \xi_0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the intertwiner cocycle to the fixed vector. Understanding spherical principal-series intertwiners, restricted Weyl groups and their parameters requires advanced Lie representation theory despite the scalar multiplication step.
