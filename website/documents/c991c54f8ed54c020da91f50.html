Statement

Let H be an infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space with its norm topology. Its unit sphere S(H)=\{v\in H:\|v\|=1\} is contractible.

Proof 1: Transfer convex contractibility through the sphere diffeomorphism

Use the source-stated theorem that S(H) is diffeomorphic to H. The Hilbert space H is convex and hence contractible. Transporting this contraction across the diffeomorphism shows that S(H) is contractible.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Transport a linear contraction across the asserted sphere diffeomorphism. The infinite-dimensional differentiable sphere setting is not a small extension of ordinary calculus, despite the short final argument; retain the advanced level conservatively.
