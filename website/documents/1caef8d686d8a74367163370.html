Statement

Let M_1f(x,y,r)=r^{1/2}f(x,r) lift a smooth function on the hyperbolic upper half-plane to hyperbolic three-space. Let P average functions on three-space over the stabilizer SU(2) of its origin, with normalized Haar measure, and set M_0=PM_1. For the positive hyperbolic Laplacians, \Delta_3M_0=M_0(\Delta_2+3/4).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Hyperbolic Laplace operators; Compact group averaging; Haar measure. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compact averaging preserves Hadamard intertwining — source argument

If f(x,r) is a function on {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {H}}^{2}} and

{\displaystyle M_{1}f(x,y,r)=r^{1/2}\cdot f(x,r)}

then

{\displaystyle \Delta _{3}M_{1}f=M_{1}\left(\Delta _{2}+{\tfrac {3}{4}}\right)f,}

where Δn is the positive hyperbolic Laplacian on {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {H}}^{n}}.

Since the action of SL(2,C) commutes with Δ3, the operator M0 obtained by averaging M1f by the action of SU(2) also satisfies

{\displaystyle \Delta _{3}M_{0}=M_{0}\left(\Delta _{2}+{\tfrac {3}{4}}\right).}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The lift identity is explicit, but its averaged version uses hyperbolic Laplacians, the SL(2,C) isometric action, the SU(2) stabilizer and Haar averaging. These are substantial geometric and harmonic-analysis prerequisites beyond basic calculus and topology. The proof does not need a proof of the unaveraged identity, but must understand those operators and the commutation under averaging.

2. Isometry invariance of the Laplace operator
Kind:

Theorem

Treatment:

Cited without proof

Statement used:

The Laplace operator of a Riemannian metric commutes with pullback by isometries; for smooth functions it commutes with averaging over a compact isometry group.

Application in proof:

Move Delta_3 through the SU(2) Haar average P to obtain the displayed relation for M_0=P M_1.

Application level:

Beyond undergraduate level

Level justification:

The application requires isometric group actions, invariant Laplacians and compact-group integration.

Review notes:

The differential operator passes through the integral because the integrand is smooth and the averaging group is compact.
