Statement

For every integer p\geq0 there is a constant c_p>0 such that every finite simple undirected p-degenerate graph G with n\geq1 vertices satisfies

r(G)\leq c_p n.

Here p-degenerate means that every nonempty subgraph of G has a vertex of degree at most p. The Ramsey number r(G) is the least integer N such that every red-blue coloring of the edges of the complete graph on N vertices contains a monochromatic copy of G (not necessarily an induced copy).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement is finite counting and coloring with graph and degeneracy terminology locally defined. The quantifier that one constant works for every graph at fixed p requires no college mathematics.
