Statement

For integers L\ge1 and odd K\ge1, \sum_{j=1}^{K}(-1)^{j-1}\binom Lj=1+\binom{L-1}{K}. Here \binom ab=a!/(b!(a-b)!) for 0\le b\le a, and is zero for b>a.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All coefficients and finite index ranges are explicitly elementary.

Proof 1: Telescope Pascal’s identity

By Pascal’s rule, \sum_{j=1}^K(-1)^{j-1}\binom Lj=\sum_{j=1}^K(-1)^{j-1}\left(\binom{L-1}{j-1}+\binom{L-1}{j}\right). The sum telescopes to 1+\binom{L-1}{K} because K is odd.

Level justification

Expand each binomial coefficient by Pascal rule and telescope, using the odd endpoint sign. School finite sums and combinatorial algebra suffice.
