Statement

Write R(n_1,\ldots,n_c) for the least positive integer N such that every assignment of c colors to all unordered pairs of an N-element vertex set contains, for some i, a subset of size n_i whose pairs all have color i. A one-vertex subset is monochromatic in every color. R(4,4)\le18.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Finite graph terminology is defined by pairs of vertices; the statements require only finite counting and elementary logic.

Proof 1: Apply the parity bound and then the ordinary recurrence

Using R(4,2)=4 and R(3,3)=6, the even-value improvement gives R(4,3)\le4+6-1=9. Symmetry gives R(3,4)\le9, and the ordinary recurrence then gives R(4,4)\le9+9=18.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Insert the two supplied small values into the improved recurrence, use symmetry and apply the ordinary recurrence. School arithmetic and explicitly defined finite colorings suffice.
