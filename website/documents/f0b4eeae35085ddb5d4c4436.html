Statement

Let M be a Riemannian symmetric space, s_p its geodesic symmetry at p, and G the identity component of its isometry group. Then \sigma(h)=s_p\circ h\circ s_p is an involutive Lie-group automorphism of G.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Riemannian symmetric spaces and the Lie-group structure of their isometry groups require substantial geometric foundations. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Conjugation by an involution

Conjugate by the involutive isometry s_p. Conjugation preserves the identity component G of the isometry group, preserves composition, and squares to the identity because s_p^2=\mathrm{id}. Thus it gives the stated Lie-group involution.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Conjugation by the geodesic involution preserves the identity component and is a smooth group automorphism. Riemannian symmetric spaces, isometry Lie groups and geodesic symmetries require graduate geometry.
