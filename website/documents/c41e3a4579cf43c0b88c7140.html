Statement

Let K be a number field, let A be an abelian variety over K, and let X\subseteq A be a closed subvariety defined over K. Suppose that after extension to an algebraic closure \overline K, X_{\overline K} contains no translate a+B of a positive-dimensional abelian subvariety B\subseteq A_{\overline K} by a point a\in A(\overline K). Then the set X(K) of K-rational points is finite. A number field is a finite extension of \mathbb Q. An abelian variety is a smooth connected projective algebraic group; an abelian subvariety is a connected closed subgroup that is itself an abelian variety. Translation uses the group law of A. A K-rational point means a point whose projective coordinates can be chosen in K, or equivalently a morphism \operatorname{Spec}K\to X over K.
