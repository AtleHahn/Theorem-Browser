Statement

Let (\Omega_1,\Sigma_1,\mu_1) and (\Omega_2,\Sigma_2,\mu_2) be sigma-finite measure spaces. All L^p spaces below are complex spaces. Let 1\le p_0,p_1,q_0,q_1\le\infty, and let

T:L^{p_0}(\mu_1)+L^{p_1}(\mu_1)\longrightarrow L^{q_0}(\mu_2)+L^{q_1}(\mu_2)

be complex-linear, with bounded restrictions T:L^{p_j}(\mu_1)\to L^{q_j}(\mu_2) of norms M_j for j=0,1. The sums are sums of measurable functions modulo equality almost everywhere. For 0<\theta<1 define

\frac1{p_\theta}=\frac{1-\theta}{p_0}+\frac\theta{p_1},\qquad \frac1{q_\theta}=\frac{1-\theta}{q_0}+\frac\theta{q_1},\qquad \frac1\infty=0.

Then T maps all of L^{p_\theta}(\mu_1) boundedly into L^{q_\theta}(\mu_2), and

\|Tf\|_{q_\theta}\le M_0^{1-\theta}M_1^\theta\|f\|_{p_\theta} \qquad(f\in L^{p_\theta}(\mu_1)).
