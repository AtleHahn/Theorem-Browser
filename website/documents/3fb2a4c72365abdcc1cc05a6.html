Statement

For a fixed existential second-order sentence \varphi over a finite relational vocabulary, deciding whether an input finite structure satisfies \varphi is in NP.

Level

Proof 1: Guess interpretations and evaluate — outline

Nondeterministically choose the relations interpreting all existentially quantified second-order variables. Check the remaining fixed first-order formula on the resulting finite structure. The fixed arities make the guessed relation tables polynomial in the universe size, and evaluation of a fixed first-order formula is polynomial time.
