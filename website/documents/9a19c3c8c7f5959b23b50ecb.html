Proof 1: Applying Ursescu to the graph relation

Use Ursescu's theorem in the full-image case: a closed convex relation from a complete metrizable locally convex space to a barrelled space, with image the entire target, sends every neighborhood of x to a neighborhood of each y related to x. Fréchet spaces are barrelled, and for a full image its relative algebraic interior and affine span are the entire target.

Clearly, T is a closed and convex relation whose image is Y: its graph is closed by continuity and the Hausdorff property of Y, and convex by linearity. Let U be a non-empty open subset of X, let y be in T(U), and let x in U be such that y=Tx. From the Ursescu theorem it follows that T(U) is a neighborhood of y. This holds for every y\in T(U), so T(U) is open.
