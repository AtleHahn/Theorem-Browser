Statement

Let n\ge1 and give the torus \mathbb T^{2n}=\mathbb R^{2n}/\mathbb Z^{2n} its standard symplectic form \omega=\sum_{j=1}^n dx_j\wedge dy_j. Among its smooth Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms, the property that all fixed points are nondegenerate is generic in the C^1 topology. A Hamiltonian diffeomorphism is the time-one map of the flow of a smooth time-dependent Hamiltonian vector field X_{H_t}, defined by \iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=-dH_t for a smooth real function H on [0,1]\times\mathbb T^{2n}. A fixed point x of \phi is nondegenerate if D\phi_x-I is invertible. The C^1 topology is uniform convergence of maps and first derivatives in local charts on the compact torus, restricted to this set of smooth Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms. Generic means that the maps with this property contain a countable intersection of open dense subsets of this space.
