Statement

A genus-g handlebody is a compact three-manifold homeomorphic to a regular neighborhood of a bouquet of g circles; a Heegaard splitting is a decomposition into two such handlebodies along their common boundary. For S^3=\{(x,y,z,w)\in\mathbb R^4:x^2+y^2+z^2+w^2=1\}, the equator w=0 is a genus-zero Heegaard surface.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The coordinate sphere is explicit, but the complete handlebody/Heegaard definition includes regular neighborhoods of bouquets in three-manifolds. Proof 1: Equatorial genus-zero splitting of the three-sphere — outline

Intersect S^3 with the xyz hyperplane. This gives the two-sphere separating the two hemispheres, which are three-balls (genus-zero handlebodies).
