Statement

Let \Psi(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}\Psi_nz^n have positive coefficients with \Psi_{n+1}/\Psi_n\to0. Let the entire function f(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}f_nz^n have finite \Psi-type \tau\ge0, meaning that \tau is the infimum of nonnegative growth parameters s for which |f(re^{i\theta})|\le M\Psi(sr) for some M and all sufficiently large r, uniformly in \theta. Then F(w)=\sum_{n\ge0}f_n/(\Psi_nw^{n+1}) converges and is holomorphic for |w|>\tau; any finite singularity of this exterior function lies in |w|\le\tau.
