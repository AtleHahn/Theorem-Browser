Statement

Let D be a noncommutative division ring. Its projective plane is Desarguesian (Desargues’s perspectivity theorem holds) but is not Pappian (Pappus’s hexagon theorem does not hold universally).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns a projective plane coordinatized by an arbitrary noncommutative division ring and two incidence properties.

Proof 1: Apply the coordinate commutativity criterion

The coordinatization characterization gives Desargues’s theorem for a plane over a division ring. Pappus’s theorem holds universally precisely when the coordinate division ring is commutative. Since D is noncommutative, its plane is not Pappian.

Level justification
