Statement

For distinct odd primes p,q, (p/q)(q/p)=(-1)^{(p-1)(q-1)/4}, where (u/v) is 1 or -1 according as u is or is not a square modulo the prime v.

Proof 1: Quadratic reciprocity from Kronecker’s sign product — outline

Use Kronecker’s formula \left(\frac pq\right)=\operatorname{sgn}\prod_{i=1}^{(q-1)/2}\prod_{k=1}^{(p-1)/2}\left(\frac kp-\frac iq\right). Switching p and q reverses every factor, and gives quadratic reciprocity.
