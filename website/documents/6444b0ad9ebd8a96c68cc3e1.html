Statement

The lattice of subgroups of a finite cyclic group is distributive: its meet is intersection and its join is the subgroup generated by the union.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group and ring theory supplies subgroups, quotients, commutators and homomorphisms. Any added terms such as perfect, nilpotent or virtually free describe explicit subgroup conditions; they are small algebraic extensions without representation or geometric-group theory.

Proof 1: Transporting distributivity through the divisor-lattice dual

For a cyclic group of order n, its subgroup lattice is isomorphic to the dual of the divisor lattice of n, with divisor d corresponding to the subgroup of order n/d. Divisibility lattices are distributive. Their duals are distributive too, so the subgroup lattice is distributive.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the supplied divisor correspondence and swap meet and join under order reversal. Lattices as partial orders with pairwise meet and join are a small extension of introductory group theory and divisibility.
