Statement

Let \Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n be bounded open, n\ge1, and k\ge1 an integer. Let H^k(\Omega) consist of L^2 functions whose weak partial derivatives D^\alpha u of orders |\alpha|\le k belong to L^2, where \alpha is a tuple of nonnegative integers and |\alpha| its sum. Suppose the inclusion H^k(\Omega)\hookrightarrow H^{k-1}(\Omega) is compact, meaning every bounded sequence for the full derivative norm has a subsequence convergent in H^{k-1}. Then N(u)=\left(\sum_{|\alpha|\le k}\|D^\alpha u\|_2^2\right)^{1/2},\qquad N_*(u)=\left(\|u\|_2^2+\sum_{|\alpha|=k}\|D^\alpha u\|_2^2\right)^{1/2} are equivalent norms: there are c,C>0 with cN(u)\le N_*(u)\le CN(u) for all u\in H^k(\Omega). Here weak derivatives are defined by integration by parts against smooth compactly supported test functions.
