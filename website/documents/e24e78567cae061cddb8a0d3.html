Statement

The closed immersion X=\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb C[x,y,z]/(xz,yz))\hookrightarrow\mathbb A^3 is not a regular embedding.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Regular embeddings of affine schemes require scheme theory and regular-sequence local algebra. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compare the dimensions of the two components — outline

The scheme is the union of the xy-plane and the z-axis. Away from the origin its closed points on the axis have local dimension one, while its closed points on the plane have local dimension two. A regular embedding into the regular ambient three-space has the locally constant codimension determined by a regular sequence. These different codimensions on the connected union therefore obstruct regularity.
