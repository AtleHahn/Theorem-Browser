Statement

Let integers 2\le p\le q\le r satisfy R=1-1/p-1/q-1/r>0. Then R\ge1/42, with equality exactly for (p,q,r)=(2,3,7).

Level

Proof 1: Three ordered integer cases

For p\ge3, positivity excludes (3,3,3) and gives R\ge1-1/3-1/3-1/4=1/12. If p=2 and q\ge4, positivity excludes (2,4,4) and gives R\ge1-1/2-1/4-1/5=1/20. The case p=q=2 has nonpositive defect. Thus the only remaining case is p=2,q=3, and positivity forces r\ge7. Here R=1/6-1/r\ge1/42, with equality precisely at r=7.
