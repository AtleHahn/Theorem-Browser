Existence and uniqueness of the Levi-Civita connection

Statement

On every smooth pseudo-Riemannian manifold (M,g), including every Riemannian manifold, there is a unique affine connection \nabla satisfying Xg(Y,Z)=g(\nabla_XY,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_XZ) and \nabla_XY-\nabla_YX=[X,Y] for all smooth vector fields X,Y,Z. The bracket is the Lie bracket, and Xg(Y,Z) is differentiation along X.

Proof 1: Construct the connection from the Koszul formula — outline

Torsion-freeness refers to the condition that

{\displaystyle \nabla _{X}Y-\nabla _{Y}X=[X,Y],}

and metric-compatibility refers to the condition that

{\displaystyle X\left(g(Y,Z)\right)=g(\nabla _{X}Y,Z)+g(Y,\nabla _{X}Z),}

where X, Y, and Z are arbitrary vector fields. Using these conditions, we compute

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}X\left(g(Y,Z)\right)&+Y\left(g(X,Z)\right)-Z\left(g(X,Y)\right)\\&={\Big (}g(\nabla _{X}Y,Z)+g(Y,\nabla _{X}Z){\Big )}+{\Big (}g(\nabla _{Y}X,Z)+g(X,\nabla _{Y}Z){\Big )}-{\Big (}g(\nabla _{Z}X,Y)+g(X,\nabla _{Z}Y){\Big )}\\&=g(\nabla _{X}Y+\nabla _{Y}X,Z)+g(\nabla _{X}Z-\nabla _{Z}X,Y)+g(\nabla _{Y}Z-\nabla _{Z}Y,X)\\&=g(2\nabla _{X}Y+[Y,X],Z)+g([X,Z],Y)+g([Y,Z],X).\end{aligned}}}

The equations displayed above can be rearranged to produce the Koszul formula or identity

{\displaystyle 2g(\nabla _{X}Y,Z)=X\left(g(Y,Z)\right)+Y\left(g(X,Z)\right)-Z\left(g(X,Y)\right)+g([X,Y],Z)-g([X,Z],Y)-g([Y,Z],X).}

This proves the uniqueness of a torsion-free and metric-compatible connection, since if g(W, Z) is equal to g(U, Z) for arbitrary Z, then W must equal U. This is a consequence of the non-degeneracy of the metric. Furthermore, by the same reasoning, the Koszul formula can be used to define a vector field ∇XY when given X and Y, and it is routine to check that this defines a connection that is torsion-free and metric-compatible.
