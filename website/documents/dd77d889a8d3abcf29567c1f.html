Statement

Let (X,\Sigma,\mu) be a measure space and let f,f_1,f_2,\ldots be integrable real- or complex-valued measurable functions. If f_n(x)\to f(x) for \mu-almost every x, then

\int_X|f_n-f|\,d\mu\longrightarrow0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\int_X|f_n|\,d\mu\longrightarrow\int_X|f|\,d\mu.

Integrable means that the integral of the absolute value is finite; almost everywhere means outside a measurable set of measure zero.
