Statement

Let \omega be a smooth nowhere-zero real one-form on a smooth manifold M. Suppose \ker\omega is the tangent bundle of a regular codimension-one foliation. Around every point there are smooth real functions f,g with f\ne0, dg\ne0, and \omega=f\,dg.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves smooth manifolds; differential forms; regular foliations. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Label plaques and rescale the transverse differential (outline)

Work in a local regular foliation chart, with the transverse coordinate as scalar label.

Assign each plaque in the foliation a scalar label. Now for each point {\displaystyle p}, define {\displaystyle g(p)} to be the scalar label of the surface containing point {\displaystyle p}.

For each point p, the one-form {\displaystyle \omega (p)} is visualized as a stack of parallel planes. The planes are quilted together, but with "uneven thickness". With a scaling at each point, {\displaystyle \omega } would have "even thickness", and become an exact differential.

Now, {\displaystyle dg} is a one-form that has exactly the same planes as {\displaystyle \omega }. However, it has "even thickness" everywhere, while {\displaystyle \omega } might have "uneven thickness". This can be fixed by a scalar scaling by {\displaystyle f}, giving {\displaystyle \omega =fdg}.

The transverse coordinate has dg\ne0; the proportionality factor f is smooth and nowhere zero because \omega is smooth and nowhere zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Choose a transverse foliation coordinate and smoothly compare the annihilating one-forms. Smooth manifolds, cotangent forms and regular foliations are substantial prerequisites not reducible to the final scalar rescaling.
