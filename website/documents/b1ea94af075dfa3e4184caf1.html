Proof 1: Reflexivity and weak compactness

Hilbert spaces are reflexive, as noted in the source. The closed unit ball of a reflexive Banach space is weakly compact. Thus bounded sets in a Hilbert space are weakly relatively compact, and every bounded net has a weakly convergent subnet.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use reflexivity for weak compactness and extract a subnet. Biduality, weak topology and nets together require graduate functional analysis.
