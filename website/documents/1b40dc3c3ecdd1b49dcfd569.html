Statement

Let R be a ring spectrum and let MU be the complex cobordism spectrum. Every element in the kernel of the homomorphism

\pi_*R\longrightarrow MU_*(R)=\pi_*(MU\wedge R)

is nilpotent: for each such element x there is an integer m\ge1 with x^m=0 in the homotopy ring \pi_*R. A ring spectrum is a spectrum with an associative multiplication and a unit in the stable homotopy category; its multiplication induces the graded ring structure on \pi_*R. The symbol \wedge denotes the smash product of spectra. The displayed map is induced by smashing the unit map from the sphere spectrum to MU with R. The spectrum MU is the Thom spectrum whose even spaces are Thom spaces of the universal complex n-plane bundles over BU(n), with structure maps given by adjoining a trivial complex line. The Thom space is the disk bundle modulo its sphere bundle, and BU(n) is the classifying space for complex rank-n bundles.
