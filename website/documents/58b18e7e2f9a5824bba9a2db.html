Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with orthocenter H, the intersection of its altitude lines, and suppose H is distinct from A,B,C. Let L be a line through H. Suppose the reflections of HA,HB,HC in L meet BC,CA,AB, respectively, in well-defined points A_1,B_1,C_1 distinct from H, so that the reflected lines are HA_1,HB_1,HC_1. Then A_1,B_1,C_1 are collinear.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All objects are ordinary finite Euclidean triangle geometry, with orthocenter and the required intersections specified locally.

Proof 1: Orthocenter specialization of Goormaghtigh’s theorem

Use Goormaghtigh's theorem: for a triangle ABC, a point P distinct from its vertices, and a line L through P, if points A_1\in BC, B_1\in CA, C_1\in AB have PA_1,PB_1,PC_1 equal to the reflections of PA,PB,PC in L, respectively, then A_1,B_1,C_1 are collinear. Apply this theorem with P=H.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Insert the orthocenter into the explicitly supplied reflected-line theorem, whose incidence assumptions are exactly those of the statement. School Euclidean reflection and triangle geometry suffice.
