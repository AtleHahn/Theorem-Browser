Statement

Let X have mean \mu and finite variance \sigma^2>0. For every a>0, Selberg’s interval bound specializes to \Pr(\mu-a\le X\le\mu+a)\ge\max\{0,1-\sigma^2/a^2\}.

Proof 1: Set the two endpoint distances equal — outline

Use the source-stated Selberg bound for [\mu-\alpha,\mu+\beta] and put \alpha=\beta=a. When a^2\ge\sigma^2>0, the middle branch applies and is (4a^2-4\sigma^2)/(2a)^2=1-\sigma^2/a^2. When a^2\le\sigma^2, its zero branch gives the other part. The branches agree at equality.
