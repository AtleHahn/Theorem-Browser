Statement

Among compact complex surfaces X of general type satisfying c_1^2(X)=3c_2(X), the minimum value of c_1^2(X) is 9, attained by Mumford’s fake projective plane. Here c_1^2 and c_2 are the Chern numbers of the complex tangent bundle.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves complex surfaces of general type; Chern numbers; fake projective planes. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Divisibility and the Mumford example outline

Use the standard positivity c_2(X)>0 for surfaces in the stated equality case, implicitly assumed in the minimality argument, as an external prerequisite.

David Mumford (1979) found a fake projective plane with c2 1 = 3c2 = 9, which is the minimum possible value because c2 1 + c2 is always divisible by 12.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The arithmetic forces c2 to be a positive multiple of three and c1 squared a positive multiple of nine. But the claim and its imported existence example concern complex surfaces of general type, tangent-bundle Chern numbers and fake projective planes. Understanding these objects requires substantial complex and algebraic geometry.
