Statement

Let G be a smooth connected algebraic group over F_q, base changed to its algebraic closure, and σ its q-power Frobenius. For every geometric point a, the map f_a:G→G defined by f_a(x)=x^{-1}aσ(x) is étale.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Smooth algebraic groups; Finite-field Frobenius; Étale morphisms. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use the invertible differential criterion

Because dσ=0, the differential of f_a at the identity is the negative identity after translation of tangent spaces. From f_a(bx)=f_{f_a(b)}(x), the differential is invertible at every b. Both source and target are smooth varieties of the same dimension, so the differential criterion for étaleness applies. This is the implication explicitly noted in source Note4.

Level justification

Compute the differential at the identity and transport it, then apply the smooth-variety etale criterion. Etale morphisms and algebraic tangent spaces are graduate prerequisites.
