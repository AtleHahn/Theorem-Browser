Statement

Let R be the algebra of subsets of \mathbb R generated by the half-open bounded intervals [a,b). Define \mu_0(\varnothing)=0 and \mu_0(A)=\infty for every nonempty A\in R. This premeasure has distinct extensions to the Borel sets: counting measure and the measure assigning infinity to every nonempty Borel set.

Proof 1: An infinite interval premeasure has different extensions — outline

Take the algebra generated by all half-open bounded intervals [a,b) on the real line, and give such intervals measure infinity if they are nonempty. The Carathéodory extension gives all nonempty sets measure infinity. Another extension is given by the counting measure.

The source supplies these extensions without the routine algebra-member and singleton checks.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compare two infinite-valued measure extensions on the interval algebra and Borel sigma-algebra. Premeasures, generated set algebras and extension theory form cumulative measure-theoretic background, even though distinguishing the extensions at singletons is simple.
