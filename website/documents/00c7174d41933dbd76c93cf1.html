Statement

The projection p:E\to B of a topological fiber bundle is open: every open subset of E has open image in B. In particular, covering projections are open.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns locally trivial bundles and coverings over arbitrary topological spaces, requiring general topology.

Proof 1: Fiber-bundle projections are open maps — outline

In local trivializations, fiber-bundle projections are natural product projections. Natural projections from products of topological spaces are open, so these local projections are open, and the bundle projection is open. Covering projections are the corresponding local products with a discrete fiber.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Local product projections are elementary topology, but the full claim concerns topological fiber bundles, their local trivializations and covering projections. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally rather than assuming this geometric framework is a small addition.
