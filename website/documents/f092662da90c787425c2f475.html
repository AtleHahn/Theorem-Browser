Statement

Let G be a finite-dimensional real Lie group, that is, a smooth real manifold whose group multiplication and inversion are smooth, and let H be a subgroup of G. If H is closed in G, then H is an embedded smooth submanifold of G and, with this induced smooth structure, is a Lie group. Its topology is the subspace topology inherited from G.

Conversely, every embedded Lie subgroup of G is closed. Thus a subgroup of G is an embedded Lie subgroup if and only if it is closed. Here embedded means that near each point of H there is a smooth coordinate chart of G in which H is a coordinate subspace, with the subspace topology.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding a general smooth Lie group and the induced embedded manifold structure requires smooth manifolds, compatible charts, smooth group operations and the distinction between immersion and embedding. This cumulative preparation is not confidently covered by a 30-minute extension of undergraduate topology and multivariable calculus; conservatively beyond undergraduate.
