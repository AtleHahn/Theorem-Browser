Statement

A finite simple undirected graph G=(V,E) has vertices V and edges consisting of unordered pairs of distinct vertices. A proper vertex coloring assigns different colors to the ends of every edge. Write \chi(G) for its minimum number of colors. For nonadjacent vertices u,v, let G+uv add their edge and let G/uv identify them, suppressing duplicate edges. Then \chi(G)=\min\{\chi(G+uv),\chi(G/uv)\}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Coloring, edge addition and vertex identification are explicitly finite graph operations.

Proof 1: Zykov recurrence for the chromatic number

The source separates colorings according to whether u,v have different colors or the same color. The former are colorings of G+uv and the latter correspond to colorings of G/uv. Taking the least number of colors over those two possibilities gives the stated recurrence.
