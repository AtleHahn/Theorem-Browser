Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer, \mu Lebesgue measure on \mathbb R^n, A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,b\in B\} and tA=\{ta:a\in A\}. For nonempty compact A,B and 0\le t\le1, \mu(tA+(1-t)B)^{1/n}\ge t\mu(A)^{1/n}+(1-t)\mu(B)^{1/n}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

From basic topology and calculus, interpret compact-set Lebesgue volume by approximating boxes or neighborhoods. Weighted Minkowski addition is explicitly defined in the statement, and the conclusion compares three nonnegative volume roots. This is a small concrete extension within about 30 minutes, without arbitrary measurable functions or a theory of convex bodies.

Proof 1: Rescale both sets in Brunn–Minkowski

Apply Brunn–Minkowski to tA and (1-t)B: \mu(tA+(1-t)B)^{1/n}\ge\mu(tA)^{1/n}+\mu((1-t)B)^{1/n}=t\mu(A)^{1/n}+(1-t)\mu(B)^{1/n}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the additive inequality to two dilated compact sets and use the nth-power scaling of volume. At t=0 or 1 the assertion is equality. Only undergraduate Euclidean volume and algebra are needed for the application.
