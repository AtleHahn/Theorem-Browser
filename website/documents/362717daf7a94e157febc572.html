Statement

Let X be a homology n-sphere, for an integer n\geq1: an n-dimensional topological manifold whose singular homology groups with integer coefficients are

H_0(X;\mathbb Z)\cong H_n(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad H_i(X;\mathbb Z)=0\quad(i\ne0,n).

For a topological space Y, let SY denote its unreduced suspension, obtained from Y\times[0,1] by collapsing Y\times\{0\} to one point and Y\times\{1\} to another point. Then

S(SX)\cong S^{n+2}

by a homeomorphism, where S^{n+2} is the usual (n+2)-dimensional sphere.
