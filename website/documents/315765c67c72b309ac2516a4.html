Statement

Let K be a field and k\ge0 an integer. Let L be the free Lie algebra over K on generators x_1,\ldots,x_k. Then its universal enveloping algebra U(L) is isomorphic as a graded associative unital K-algebra to the free associative algebra K\langle x_1,\ldots,x_k\rangle, by the map sending each named generator to the generator with the same name. The free Lie algebra has no relations beyond bilinearity, alternation [x,x]=0 and the Jacobi identity; equivalently every assignment of its generators to a K-Lie algebra extends uniquely to a Lie homomorphism. The free associative algebra has as a K-basis all finite words in the generators, including the empty word as unit, with multiplication given by concatenation. The enveloping algebra is T_K(L)/J, where T_K(L)=\bigoplus_{m\ge0}L^{\otimes_K m} is the tensor algebra and J is generated by x\otimes y-y\otimes x-[x,y] for x,y\in L. The grading in the assertion assigns degree one to each x_i, with brackets and products adding degrees; it is not the tensor-length filtration that assigns every element of L filtration degree one.
