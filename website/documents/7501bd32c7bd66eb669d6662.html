Statement

Let K have signature (r_1,r_2) and unit rank r=r_1+r_2-1. Let u_1,\ldots,u_r generate O_K^\times modulo roots of unity. Choose the r+1 archimedean embeddings \sigma_j, one from each nonreal conjugate pair, and set N_j=1 for a real embedding and N_j=2 otherwise. Define the real r\times(r+1) matrix A_{ij}=N_j\log|\sigma_j(u_i)|. The absolute determinant of the r\times r matrix obtained by deleting a column of A is independent of the column; for r=0 use determinant 1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The logarithm matrix is defined using number-field units and embeddings.

Proof 1: Use the zero row sums — outline

The sum of the entries of each row is zero: it is the logarithm of the absolute norm of the corresponding unit, and that absolute norm is 1. Thus the absolute determinant of a submatrix formed by deleting one column is independent of which column is deleted.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the absolute norm formula for number-field units to establish zero logarithmic row sums, then compare maximal minors. Number-field units, archimedean embeddings and their norm formula remain advanced prerequisites despite the final determinant argument.
