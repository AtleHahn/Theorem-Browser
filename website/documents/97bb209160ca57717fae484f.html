With \|v\|=(\sum_{j=1}^8v_j^2)^{1/2}, one has \|a\times b\|=\|a\|\|b\|.

Proof 1: Interpret the eight-square identity as a product norm

The eight-square identity can be written in the form of a product of two inner products of 8-dimensional vectors, yielding again an inner product of 8-dimensional vectors: (a·a)(b·b) = (a×b)·(a×b).

Taking nonnegative square roots gives \|a\times b\|=\|a\|\|b\|.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The product and norm are given by explicit finite coordinate formulas. Take nonnegative square roots of the supplied eight-square identity. No octonion or abstract normed-algebra theory is used.
