Statement

Fix an integer n\ge1 and start with the infinite sequence 1,2,3,\ldots. For d=n,n-1,\ldots,2 in that order, perform these two operations on the current sequence:

  1. Delete its entries in positions d,2d,3d,\ldots, numbering positions starting at 1.
  2. Replace the remaining sequence by its successive partial sums.

After these n-1 passes, the resulting sequence is 1^n,2^n,3^n,\ldots. For n=1 there are no passes, so the starting sequence is the output.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Algorithm and conclusion require only elementary counting and addition, with the iteration made explicit.
