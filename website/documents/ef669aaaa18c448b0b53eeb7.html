Statement

Let (M,g) be a smooth pseudo-Riemannian manifold of dimension n\ge3, with Levi-Civita connection \nabla, Ricci tensor R_{ij} and scalar curvature R. Put

S_{ij}=\frac2{n-2}R_{ij}-\frac{R}{(n-2)(n-1)}g_{ij}.

If a smooth one-form \omega satisfies

\nabla_i\omega_j=\frac12\omega_i\omega_j-\frac14g^{pq}\omega_p\omega_qg_{ij}-S_{ij},

then \omega is closed and locally equals du for a smooth real function u. Repeated indices are summed and g^{pq} is the inverse metric.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Levi-Civita differentiation, Ricci and scalar curvature, and the Schouten tensor require substantial pseudo-Riemannian geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Symmetry and the Poincaré lemma

The right-hand side of the displayed differential equation is symmetric in i and j. Since the Levi-Civita connection is torsion free, this implies that \omega is a closed one-form. The Poincaré lemma then gives a locally defined real function u with \omega=du.
