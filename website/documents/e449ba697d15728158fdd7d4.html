Statement

Let F,F_1,F_2,\ldots be cumulative distribution functions on \mathbb R: each is nondecreasing, right-continuous, and has limits 0 at -\infty and 1 at +\infty. Suppose F_n(x)\to F(x) at every continuity point x of F. Then for every bounded continuous g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R, \lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{\mathbb R}g(x)\,dF_n(x)=\int_{\mathbb R}g(x)\,dF(x). The integrals are improper Riemann–Stieltjes integrals, defined on finite intervals by limits of sums \sum_i g(\xi_i)(F(t_i)-F(t_{i-1})) with \xi_i\in[t_{i-1},t_i], as the partition mesh tends to zero, and then by limits as the endpoints tend to \pm\infty.
