Statement

Let N,d\ge1 and x_1,\ldots,x_N\in\mathbb R^d. Let C(x_1,\ldots,x_N) be the number of binary labelings (\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_N)\in\{-1,1\}^N for which there exists w\in\mathbb R^d satisfying

\varepsilon_i(w\cdot x_i)>0\quad\text{for every }i.

Then

C(x_1,\ldots,x_N)=2\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\binom{N-1}{j}

if and only if the vectors are in homogeneous general position: every subfamily of at most d vectors is linearly independent. A binomial coefficient with j>N-1 is taken to be zero.

Proof availability

The source gives only a counting recurrence and an instruction to use induction. It does not prove the geometric recurrence or the necessity of general position. Its later shattering discussion mixes affine and homogeneous thresholds and is not retained as a proof.
