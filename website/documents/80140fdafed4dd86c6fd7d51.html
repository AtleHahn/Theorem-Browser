Statement

For twice differentiable real functions f,g on an open interval, (fg)^{\prime\prime}=f^{\prime\prime}g+2f^{\prime}g^{\prime}+fg^{\prime\prime}.

Proof 1: Substitute order two

The general Leibniz rule is

(fg)^{(n)}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk f^{(n-k)}g^{(k)}.

If, for example, n=2, the rule gives an expression for the second derivative of a product of two functions:

(fg)''(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{2}\binom 2k f^{(2-k)}(x)g^{(k)}(x) =f''(x)g(x)+2f'(x)g'(x)+f(x)g''(x).
