Statement

There are no weight-two newforms on \Gamma_0(2).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Weight-two newforms and the congruence subgroup defining their level require substantial modular-form theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use genus zero and the differential correspondence

The modular curve X_0(2) has genus zero. Weight-two cusp forms correspond to holomorphic differentials on this curve, and a genus-zero curve has no nonzero holomorphic differentials. Thus there can be no weight-two newform at level two.
