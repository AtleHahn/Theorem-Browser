Statement

Positive Pythagorean triples with legs a,a+1 correspond to positive integer solutions x>1,y>0 of x^2-2y^2=-1, by a=(x-1)/2 and hypotenuse y.

Proof 1: Source calculation — outline

There are right-angled triangles with integral sides for which the lengths of the non-hypotenuse sides differ by one, such as

3^2+4^2=5^2

and

20^2+21^2=29^2.

They can be completely parameterized as

\left(\frac{x-1}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{x+1}{2}\right)^2=y^2,

where x,y are the solutions to the Pell equation x^2-2y^2=-1, with x>1 and y>0.

Expanding gives exactly the Pell equation. Any integer solution has odd x, so the displayed legs are integers; conversely put x=2a+1.

Level justification

Expand the two squared legs to the negative Pell equation. Modulo two, any solution has odd x, making the proposed legs integral; the inverse substitution is x=2a+1. No theory for solving Pell equations is used.
