Statement

Let k be a field of characteristic zero and H a reductive linear algebraic k-group. Every algebraic-group homomorphism u:G_a→H factors through i:G_a\to SL_2, i(t)=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix}. Any two homomorphisms SL_2→H inducing the same u are conjugate by an element of H(k). Here G_a is the additive algebraic group.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Specialize the pro-reductive adjunction to the additive group — outline

Use the generalized Jacobson–Morozov theorem in the category whose morphisms G→H are taken modulo H(k)-conjugation: inclusion of pro-reductive groups has a left adjoint E, and E(G_a)=SL_2 with the stated inclusion i. The adjunction gives a bijection between conjugacy classes of maps SL_2→H and maps G_a→H. Thus the class of u has a preimage. Its restriction is conjugate to u, so conjugating the representative makes the restriction equal to u. Injectivity of the bijection implies that any two such maps belong to the same H(k)-conjugacy class.
