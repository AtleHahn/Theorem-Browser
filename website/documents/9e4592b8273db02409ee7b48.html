Statement

Let G=\{g_1,\ldots,g_n\} be a finite group, and associate an independent commuting indeterminate x_g to each g\in G. Form the n\times n matrix

X_G=(x_{g_i g_j})_{1\leq i,j\leq n}.

If r is the number of conjugacy classes of G, then there are pairwise nonproportional irreducible polynomials P_1,\ldots,P_r\in\mathbb C[x_g:g\in G] such that

\det X_G=\prod_{j=1}^r P_j^{\deg P_j}.

Thus the number of distinct irreducible factors is r, and each has multiplicity equal to its total degree. Two polynomials are proportional if one is a nonzero constant multiple of the other; a conjugacy class consists of elements hgh^{-1} for a fixed g and varying h\in G.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Finite groups, determinants and irreducibility are undergraduate algebra. Multivariable total degree and multiplicity are small extensions of polynomial factorization; no representation theory is required to understand this formulation.

The source identifies the multiplication-table matrix with a regular-representation matrix without accounting for inverse indexing and its determinant sign.
