Statement

For a finite nonempty family \mathcal S of axis-parallel squares of positive side length in the Euclidean plane, let r(\mathcal S) be the maximum area of the union of a pairwise disjoint subfamily, divided by the area of the union of \mathcal S. Set F=\inf_{\mathcal S}r(\mathcal S), allowing different side lengths. Then F\le1/4-1/384.

Level justification

The proof uses a finite configuration of squares, area counting and an elementary limiting estimate. In an N by N patch, boundary contributions grow linearly while the total area grows quadratically. Understanding the infimum and the limiting area ratio requires undergraduate real analysis.
