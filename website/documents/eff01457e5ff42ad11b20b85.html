Statement

In a projective plane over a field, fix distinct points U,V and three distinct lines of the pencil at U with three distinct prescribed images in the pencil at V. If their projectivity is nonperspective, its Steiner conic is uniquely determined. Here the pencil at a point consists of its incident lines, and the Steiner conic consists of intersections of corresponding lines.

Proof 1: Apply uniqueness of the pencil projectivity

A projectivity between the two pencils is uniquely determined by the images of three distinct lines. Consequently, once those images and the two pencil centers are fixed, all corresponding intersections, and hence the conic, are determined.
