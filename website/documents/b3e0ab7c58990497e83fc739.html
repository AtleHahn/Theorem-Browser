Statement

For every positive integer n, let \zeta_n=e^{2\pi i/n} and let \mathbb Q(\zeta_n) be the smallest subfield of \mathbb C containing \mathbb Q and \zeta_n. This is a finite Galois extension of \mathbb Q, and the map

\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\zeta_n)/\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times,\qquad \sigma\longmapsto a\pmod n\quad\text{where }\sigma(\zeta_n)=\zeta_n^a,

is a group isomorphism. The group on the left consists of field automorphisms fixing every rational number, with composition; the group on the right consists of residue classes relatively prime to n, with multiplication. For n=1 both groups are trivial. Galois means normal and separable: every irreducible rational polynomial with a root in the field splits there into distinct linear factors. In particular the extension is abelian.
