Statement

Work in the projective plane over any field K. A pencil at a point consists of all lines through that point. A perspectivity between distinct pencils is the bijection obtained by intersecting their lines with a fixed axis line and joining to the other center; its corresponding lines meet on that axis. A projectivity is a finite composition of perspectivities.

Let U,V be distinct points and let \pi be a projectivity from the pencil at U to the pencil at V which is not a perspectivity. The points \ell\cap\pi(\ell), as \ell ranges over the pencil at U, form a nondegenerate projective conic (a nonsingular projective curve defined by a homogeneous quadratic equation).

Dually, let u,v be distinct lines and let \pi be a projectivity between their point sets, meaning a finite composition of projections between lines from centers. If it is not a perspectivity (the joins of corresponding points do not all pass through one center), then the lines X\pi(X) form a nondegenerate conic in the dual projective plane, whose points represent lines of the original plane. This dual construction is a statement in the dual plane; it does not assert that the tangents of a point conic form a nondegenerate dual conic in characteristic two.
