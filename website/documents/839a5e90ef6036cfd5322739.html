Statement

Let A be a finite subfield of a field. With A+A=\{a+b:a,b\in A\} and A\cdot A=\{ab:a,b\in A\}, one has A+A=A\cdot A=A, and both sets have cardinality |A|.

Proof 1: Subfields have no sum-product growth — outline

Let \mathbb F be a finite field and take A=\mathbb F. Then since \mathbb F is closed under addition and multiplication, A+A=A\cdot A=\mathbb F, and so |A+A|=|A\cdot A|=|A|. This example extends to taking A to be any finite subfield of the field in question.

Level justification

Use closure for one inclusion and zero and one for the reverse inclusions. Introductory field axioms suffice; no extension degrees or finite-field structure theorem is used.
