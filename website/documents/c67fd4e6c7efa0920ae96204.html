Statement

Fix n\ge3. Let S:\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb C^n be S(a_1,\ldots,a_n)=(a_2,\ldots,a_n,a_1), and let I denote the identity. For \alpha\in\mathbb C with |\alpha|=1 and \alpha\ne1 put T_\alpha=(S-\alpha I)/(1-\alpha). Let \omega=e^{2\pi i/n} and P=T_\omega T_{\omega^2}\cdots T_{\omega^{n-2}}A. Then (S-I)(S-\omega^{n-1}I)P=0 for every A\in\mathbb C^n.

Proof 1: Factor the polynomial S to the n minus identity

The factors (1-\omega^j)^{-1}(S-\omega^jI) commute, since they are all polynomials in the same operator S. The result now follows from the following computations:

\begin{aligned} &(S-I)(S-\omega^{n-1}I)P\\ &=(S-I)(S-\omega^{n-1}I)(1-\omega)^{-1}(S-\omega I) (1-\omega^2)^{-1}(S-\omega^2I)\cdots (1-\omega^{n-2})^{-1}(S-\omega^{n-2}I)A\\ &=(1-\omega)^{-1}(1-\omega^2)^{-1}\cdots(1-\omega^{n-2})^{-1} (S-I)(S-\omega I)(S-\omega^2I)\cdots(S-\omega^{n-1}I)A\\ &=(1-\omega)^{-1}(1-\omega^2)^{-1}\cdots(1-\omega^{n-2})^{-1}(S^n-I)A\\ &=0, \end{aligned}

since S^n=I.
