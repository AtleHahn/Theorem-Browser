Statement

Let (X_n) be independent real random variables. The event that \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}X_n converges in \mathbb R has probability zero or one.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns almost-sure behavior of a random series in general probability.

Proof 1: Apply the tail-event zero–one law

The convergence event is a tail event for the random variables. Therefore Kolmogorov’s zero–one law gives probability zero or one.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Identify convergence as measurable in every tail sigma algebra and apply Kolmogorov. Tail sigma algebras and infinite-sequence measurability require advanced probability; the supplied proof uses that formal framework.
