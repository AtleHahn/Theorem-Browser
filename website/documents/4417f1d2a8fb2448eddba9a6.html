Proof 1: Separate squared-logarithmic from linear deterministic space

Savitch’s theorem gives \mathrm{NL}\subseteq\mathrm{DSPACE}(\log^2n). The deterministic space hierarchy theorem gives \mathrm{DSPACE}(\log^2n)\subsetneq\mathrm{DSPACE}(n). Since \mathrm{DSPACE}(n)\subseteq\mathrm{PSPACE}, the desired inclusion is strict.
