Statement

For a nondegenerate triangle with side lengths a,b,c and corresponding positive altitudes h_a,h_b,h_c, \frac1{h_a}<\frac1{h_b}+\frac1{h_c}. The analogous inequalities hold after permuting labels.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement concerns elementary plane geometry and real arithmetic.

Proof 1: Triangle inequality for reciprocal altitudes — source argument

Since the area of the triangle is {\displaystyle {\tfrac {1}{2}}ah_{a}={\tfrac {1}{2}}bh_{b}={\tfrac {1}{2}}ch_{c}}, the triangle inequality {\displaystyle a<b+c} implies

{\displaystyle {\frac {1}{h_{a}}}<{\frac {1}{h_{b}}}+{\frac {1}{h_{c}}}}.
Level
High school only
Level justification

Divide the side-length triangle inequality by twice the common area. School triangle geometry and fractions suffice.
