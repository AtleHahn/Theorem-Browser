Statement

Let S^3=\{x\in\mathbb R^4:\|x\|=1\} and let f:S^3\to S^3 be a smooth diffeomorphism of finite order: f^m=\operatorname{id} for some integer m\geq1. If its fixed-point set

\operatorname{Fix}(f)=\{x\in S^3:f(x)=x\}

is a knot (the image of a smooth embedding of the circle in S^3), then it is the unknot: it is smoothly ambient isotopic to a standard round circle. An ambient isotopy is a smooth family of diffeomorphisms of S^3, starting at the identity, carrying one embedded circle to the other.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The explicit sphere and isotopy definitions make the claim accessible with undergraduate topology and multivariable calculus plus a short introduction to knots. Proof machinery is not a statement prerequisite.
