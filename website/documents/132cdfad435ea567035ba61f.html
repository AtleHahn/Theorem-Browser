Statement

Let n\geq1 and let \Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n be a nonempty connected open set. Suppose \Omega is a domain of holomorphy: there are no nonempty open sets U\subseteq\Omega\cap V, with V\subseteq\mathbb C^n connected and open and V\not\subseteq\Omega, such that every holomorphic function on \Omega agrees on U with a holomorphic function on V.

Then \Omega is pseudoconvex. Explicitly, there exists a continuous real-valued plurisubharmonic function \rho on \Omega such that, for every real c, the set

\{z\in\Omega:\rho(z)<c\}

has compact closure contained in \Omega. A continuous function is plurisubharmonic when its restriction to every complex affine line is subharmonic on its domain of definition: its value at the center of each closed disk contained there is at most the average of its values on the boundary circle.
