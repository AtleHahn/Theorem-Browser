Statement

Let M be a compact connected oriented n-dimensional manifold without boundary, n\ge1, and let S^n=\{x\in\mathbb R^{n+1}:\|x\|=1\} with its standard orientation. For continuous maps f,g:M\to S^n, \deg(f)=\deg(g)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f\text{ and }g\text{ are homotopic}. The integer degree is defined by f_*[M]=\deg(f)[S^n] in the top-dimensional singular homology group H_n(S^n;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z, where [M] and [S^n] are the fundamental classes determined by orientation. Homotopic means that there is a continuous F:M\times[0,1]\to S^n with F(x,0)=f(x) and F(x,1)=g(x).
