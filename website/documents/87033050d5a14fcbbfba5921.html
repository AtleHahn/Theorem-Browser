Statement

Let B_1,\ldots,B_r\in\mathbb C^{n\times n} satisfy \sum_iB_i^*B_i=I_n, and put \Phi(S)=\sum_iB_iSB_i^*. Suppose \Phi(S) is positive definite for every nonzero positive semidefinite Hermitian matrix S. Then there exist positive definite Hermitian matrices x_0,x_1 for which

\Psi(S)=x_1\Phi(x_0^{-1}Sx_0^{-1})x_1

satisfies \Psi(I_n)=I_n and \Psi^*(I_n)=I_n. Thus \Psi preserves the identity and trace. Here * on matrices is conjugate transpose. A Hermitian matrix S is positive semidefinite when v^*Sv\ge0 for every v, and positive definite when this is strict for every v\ne0. The adjoint of a matrix map is with respect to \langle A,B\rangle=\operatorname{tr}(A^*B); in particular \Phi^*(S)=\sum_iB_i^*SB_i.

Level justification
