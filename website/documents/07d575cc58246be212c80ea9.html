Statement

Let K be a number field, meaning a finite field extension of \mathbb Q, and let \mathcal O_K be its ring of integers, the elements of K satisfying a monic polynomial with integer coefficients. Every nonzero proper ideal I\subsetneq\mathcal O_K has a factorization I=\mathfrak p_1\cdots\mathfrak p_r\qquad(r\ge1) into nonzero prime ideals, unique up to permutation of the factors, with repetitions allowed. A proper ideal \mathfrak p is prime if ab\in\mathfrak p implies a\in\mathfrak p or b\in\mathfrak p; an ideal product consists of finite sums of products of elements from the respective ideals.
