Statement

Let (X_t)_{0\le t\le T} be a real submartingale with almost surely right-continuous paths, where 0<T<\infty. For C>0, \Pr(\sup_{0\le t\le T}X_t\ge C)\le\mathbb E[X_T^+]/C. The supremum is interpreted through its measurable countable-dense version. Here x^+=\max(x,0).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns a stochastic path supremum, right continuity and terminal expectation.

Proof 1: Exhaust a countable dense set containing the terminal time — outline

This is a corollary of the discrete-time Doob probability inequality, obtained by writing, almost surely,

\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}X_t=\sup\{X_t:t\in D\}=\lim_{i\to\infty}\sup\{X_t:t\in D_i\}.

In this expression, D is a countable dense subset of [0,T] containing T, and D_1\subset D_2\subset\dots is a sequence of finite sets containing T whose union is D. The first equality is a consequence of the right-continuity assumption, while the second equality is purely set-theoretic.

The discrete-time inequality applies to say that

\Pr\left[\sup_{t\in D_i}X_t\geq C\right]\leq\frac{\mathbb E[\max(X_T,0)]}{C}.

This relation holds for each i, and this passes to the limit to yield the submartingale inequality, with the routine threshold limit from levels below C needed for the non-strict inequality.
