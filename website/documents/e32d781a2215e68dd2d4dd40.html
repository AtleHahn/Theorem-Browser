Statement

Combining local uniformization in dimension at most three with Zariski’s threefold globalization yields resolution of threefolds in every characteristic.

Proof 1: Combine local uniformization with threefold globalization

Cossart and Piltant establish local uniformization in dimension at most three. The stated extension of Zariski’s argument shows that this implies threefold resolution in positive characteristic as well; together with the characteristic-zero case this gives every characteristic.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Combine local uniformization with globalization for threefolds. Even understanding the application requires valuations, birational geometry and resolution of singularities beyond ordinary undergraduate algebra.
