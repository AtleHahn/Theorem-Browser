Statement

Let H be a complex Hilbert space and let M\subseteq B(H) be a unital von Neumann algebra and \Omega\in H with M^{\prime}\Omega dense in H. If a\in M and a\Omega=0, then a=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding a dense commutant orbit and separation requires infinite-dimensional Hilbert-space operator theory.

Proof 1: Vanishing on the dense commutant orbit

If a\Omega=0 and b\in M^{\prime}, then ab\Omega=ba\Omega=0. Thus a vanishes on the dense set M^{\prime}\Omega, and hence a=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The final continuity argument is elementary, but the statement presupposes a von Neumann algebra, its commutant, and dense Hilbert-space operator orbits. Understanding the actual objects requires operator-algebra background.
