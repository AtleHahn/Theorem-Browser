Statement

Let E,F,G be real with E>0 and EG-F^2>0. Put D=\sqrt{EG-F^2}, \lambda=(E+G+2D)/4, and \mu=(E-G+2iF)/(4\lambda). For z=x+iy, E\,dx^2+2F\,dx\,dy+G\,dy^2=\lambda|dz+\mu\,d\bar z|^2, with \lambda>0 and |\mu|<1. If the coefficients are smooth functions satisfying these inequalities, so are \lambda,\mu.

Proof 1: Expanding the displayed metric factorization

In the complex coordinate z=x+iy, the positive quadratic form

ds^2=E\,dx^2+2F\,dx\,dy+G\,dy^2

takes the form

ds^2=\lambda|dz+\mu\,d\overline z|^2,

where

\lambda=\frac14\left(E+G+2\sqrt{EG-F^2}\right), \qquad \mu=\frac1{4\lambda}(E-G+2iF),

so that \lambda>0 and |\mu|<1. When E,F,G are smooth, \lambda and \mu are smooth.

The source supplies this factorization and these properties without writing out the coefficient expansion or the modulus and smoothness checks.
