Statement

Every morphism f:X\to Y between smooth varieties over a common field is a local complete intersection morphism: locally it factors as a regular closed immersion followed by a smooth morphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Smooth varieties; Regular embeddings; Local complete intersections. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Factor through the graph

Factor f as the graph morphism X\to X\times Y followed by projection X\times Y\to Y. The graph is a closed immersion between smooth varieties and hence a regular embedding. The projection is smooth. This is the required factorization.
