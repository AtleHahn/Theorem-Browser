Statement

Let G\subseteq\mathbb C be open and connected, let H be a complex Hilbert space, and let \mathcal B(H) be the bounded complex-linear operators on H, with the operator norm. Suppose B:G\to\mathcal B(H) is analytic in operator norm and each B(\lambda) is compact (it maps bounded sets to relatively compact sets). Let I be the identity on H. Then either I-B(\lambda) has no bounded inverse for every \lambda\in G, or there is a subset S\subseteq G having no limit point in G such that (I-B(\lambda))^{-1} exists for all \lambda\in G\setminus S, depends analytically on \lambda there, and for every \lambda\in S the solution space \{\psi\in H:B(\lambda)\psi=\psi\} is finite-dimensional.
