Statement

Let X be a compact complex manifold of complex dimension n, and let E be an ample holomorphic vector bundle of rank r\ge1. Denote by \Omega_X^p the sheaf of holomorphic p-forms. For integers 0\le p,q\le n with p+q\ge n+r, H^q(X,\Omega_X^p\otimes E)=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Compact complex manifolds; Ample holomorphic vector bundles; Holomorphic differential forms; Sheaf cohomology. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply the Dolbeault cohomology identification

Le Potier's stated vanishing theorem gives H^{p,q}(X,E)=0 when p+q\ge n+r. The Dolbeault theorem identifies H^{p,q}(X,E) with H^q(X,\Omega_X^p\otimes E). Consequently the latter cohomology group vanishes in the same range.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Transfer Dolbeault vanishing through its sheaf-cohomology identification. Both cohomology constructions and ample holomorphic bundles require graduate geometry.
