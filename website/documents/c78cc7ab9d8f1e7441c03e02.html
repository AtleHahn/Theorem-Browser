Statement

Let G act on X from the left, with ex=x and g(hx)=(gh)x. Assume X is nonempty and the action is transitive. For x\in X set H=G_x=\{g:gx=x\}. The map G/H\to X, gH\mapsto gx, is an equivariant bijection for the left-multiplication action on cosets.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory field/group theory and linear algebra supply all objects. Commutator, graph automorphism and 2-rank are explicit group or finite-coordinate definitions; the displayed identities and subgroup assertions require no specialist structural theory.

Proof 1: A transitive action is a coset action — outline

The source orbit–stabilizer map gG_x\mapsto gx is a bijection onto Gx, and transitivity makes this orbit X. With the stated left action on cosets, multiplication by a sends gG_x to agG_x, whose image is (ag)x=a(gx). Thus this is the asserted isomorphism of G-sets.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the orbit-stabilizer bijection and check equivariance by associativity of the action. Introductory abstract algebra suffices.
