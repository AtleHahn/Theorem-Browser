Statement

Let T be an ergodic measure-preserving transformation of a probability space (\Omega,\mathcal B,\mu) and let P be a finite or countable measurable partition with finite entropy H(P)=-\sum_{A\in P}\mu(A)\log\mu(A). Let P^{(n)}=\bigvee_{k=0}^{n-1}T^{-k}P and let P^{(n)}(x) be its atom containing x. Then the functions -n^{-1}\log\mu(P^{(n)}(x)) converge in L^1(\mu) to the constant h_T(P)=\lim_n n^{-1}H(P^{(n)}).

Proof 1: Triviality of the invariant sigma-algebra

General theorem and entropy identity used

Write I_{P\mid Q} for conditional information of a partition P given Q. Let J=\lim_n I_{P\mid\bigvee_{k=1}^n T^{-k}P} and let \mathcal I be the invariant sigma-algebra. The general Shannon–McMillan–Breiman theorem, as stated in the source, gives L^1 convergence of n^{-1}I_{P^{(n)}} to \mathbb E[J\mid\mathcal I]. The entropy identity used there is \mathbb E J=\lim_n H(P\mid\bigvee_{k=1}^n T^{-k}P)=h_T(P).

Ergodic specialization

When T is ergodic, \mathcal I is trivial, so the conditional expectation simplifies to the constant \mathbb E J=h_T(P).
