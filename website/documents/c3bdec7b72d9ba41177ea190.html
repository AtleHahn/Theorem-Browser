Statement

Let M be a nonempty compact oriented smooth manifold of dimension n\ge1 with nonempty boundary. There is no smooth map r:M\to\partial M restricting to the identity on \partial M.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns smooth manifolds with orientation and boundary.

Proof 1: Stokes contradicts the positive boundary volume

Suppose there is a smooth retraction r:M\to\partial M. If \omega is a positive volume form on the oriented boundary then by Stokes' theorem,

0<\int_{\partial M}\omega =\int_{\partial M}r^*(\omega) =\int_M d r^*(\omega) =\int_M r^*(d\omega) =\int_M r^*(0)=0,

giving a contradiction. Here pullback commutes with exterior differentiation, and d\omega=0 because \omega is a top-degree form on \partial M.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Pull back a positive top-degree boundary form and use Stokes and naturality of exterior differentiation. Differential forms on oriented manifolds with boundary require graduate differential topology.
