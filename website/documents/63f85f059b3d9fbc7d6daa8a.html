Statement

Fix a finite relational vocabulary \tau. A property of finite \tau-structures means a class \mathcal K of such structures invariant under isomorphism. Then membership in \mathcal K is decidable in nondeterministic polynomial time if and only if there is an existential second-order sentence \Phi such that, for every finite \tau-structure A,

A\in\mathcal K\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A\models\Phi.

A finite relational vocabulary specifies finitely many relation symbols of fixed finite arities; a structure interprets them as relations on a finite domain. An existential second-order sentence has the form

\exists R_1\cdots\exists R_m\,\theta,

where the quantified variables R_i range over relations of fixed arities on the domain and \theta is a first-order sentence in the expanded vocabulary. The sentence is fixed for \mathcal K, independently of the input structure. Nondeterministic polynomial time (NP) means acceptance by a nondeterministic Turing machine in time polynomial in the length of a standard explicit finite encoding of the input structure. No ordering of the domain is required as part of \tau.

Level justification

The statement relates semantic definability over arbitrary finite structures to nondeterministic complexity. Finite-model semantics, relation quantification, machine acceptance and encoding-independent polynomial bounds form several interdependent prerequisites beyond a confident 30-minute undergraduate extension.
