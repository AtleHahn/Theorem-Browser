Statement

Let X,Y be Hausdorff locally convex topological vector spaces over the same field \mathbb R or \mathbb C, and let u:X\to Y be linear. Locally convex means that zero has a neighborhood basis of convex sets. Suppose X is a locally convex inductive limit of a family of Banach spaces: there are linear maps from these spaces whose images span X, and the topology of X is the finest locally convex topology making these maps continuous.

If Y is K-analytic and the graph of u is closed in X\times Y, then u is continuous. Here K-analytic means a continuous image of a K_{\sigma\delta} space, namely a subspace of a Hausdorff space expressible as a countable intersection of countable unions of compact subsets.
