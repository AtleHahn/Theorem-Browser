Statement

Let T>0, let K\subset\mathbb R\times(0,T) be compact, and let u_\epsilon be measurable functions on this strip with \|u_\epsilon(\cdot,t)\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb R)}\le M<\infty for almost every t, uniformly in \epsilon. Set w_\epsilon=u_\epsilon\mathbf1_K, extended by zero to \mathbb R^2; |K| denotes Lebesgue measure. Assume also that, for a measurable initial function u_0 and each real h, the spatial contraction estimate \int_{\mathbb R}|u_\epsilon(x-h,t)-u_\epsilon(x,t)|\,dx\le\|u_0(\cdot-h)-u_0\|_{L^1(\mathbb R)} holds for almost every t\in(0,T). Then \|w_\epsilon(\cdot-h,\cdot)-w_\epsilon\|_{L^1(\mathbb R^2)}\le T\|u_0(\cdot-h)-u_0\|_{L^1(\mathbb R)}+M\|\mathbf1_K(\cdot-h,\cdot)-\mathbf1_K\|_{L^1(\mathbb R^2)}.

Proof 1: Spatial translation bound for compactly cut off solutions

We consider

\begin{aligned} &\|w_\epsilon(\cdot-h,\cdot)-w_\epsilon\|_{L^1(\mathbb R^2)}\\ &\quad\leq \|(u_\epsilon(\cdot-h,\cdot)-u_\epsilon)\mathbf1_K(\cdot-h,\cdot)\|_{L^1(\mathbb R^2)} +\|u_\epsilon(\mathbf1_K(\cdot-h,\cdot)-\mathbf1_K)\|_{L^1(\mathbb R^2)}. \end{aligned}

The first term on the right-hand side satisfies

\|(u_\epsilon(\cdot-h,\cdot)-u_\epsilon)\mathbf1_K(\cdot-h,\cdot)\|_{L^1(\mathbb R^2)} \leq T\|u_0(\cdot-h)-u_0\|_{L^1(\mathbb R)}

by the spatial L^1-contraction. The second term satisfies

\|u_\epsilon(\mathbf1_K(\cdot-h,\cdot)-\mathbf1_K)\|_{L^1(\mathbb R^2)} \leq M\|\mathbf1_K(\cdot-h,\cdot)-\mathbf1_K\|_{L^1(\mathbb R^2)}

by the L^\infty-bound. Adding these estimates gives the stated inequality.
