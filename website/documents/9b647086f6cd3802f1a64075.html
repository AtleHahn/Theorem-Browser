Statement

For independent uniform symbols Z_1,Z_2,\ldots in an alphabet of q\ge2 symbols, and any fixed word w of length m\ge1, infinitely many of the consecutive nonoverlapping blocks of length m equal w, almost surely. In particular w occurs as a substring infinitely often.

Proof 1: Apply Borel–Cantelli to disjoint blocks

Let E_k be the event that block k equals w. Disjoint character blocks give independent events and \mathbb P(E_k)=q^{-m}=p>0. Therefore \sum_{k\ge1}\mathbb P(E_k)=\sum_{k\ge1}p=\infty. The second Borel–Cantelli lemma gives infinitely many such events almost surely.
