Let R be a unital ring and U a nonzero simple unital right R-module. For every nonzero u∈U and every v∈U, some r∈R satisfies ur=v.

Proof 1: Use the nonzero cyclic submodule

The cyclic submodule uR is nonzero since u≠0 and U is unital. Simplicity gives uR=U, so v belongs to uR.
