Statement

For each n\ge3, take n tangents at distinct points of an open semicircle. Their simple line arrangement has exactly n-2 bounded triangular faces.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The construction and its face count use ordinary plane tangency and finite integers.

Proof 1: Count consecutive triples of semicircle tangents — outline

For lines tangent at distinct points of an open semicircle, the only triangles are the ones formed by three lines with consecutive points of tangency. The other faces of this arrangement are either bounded quadrilaterals, or unbounded. As the {\displaystyle n} lines have {\displaystyle n-2} consecutive triples, they also have {\displaystyle n-2} triangles.

Level justification
