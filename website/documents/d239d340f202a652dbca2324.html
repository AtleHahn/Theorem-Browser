Statement

In a projective plane over a field, let \pi be a projective but nonperspective bijection from the points of a line u to those of a distinct line v. Then the lines joining X\in u to \pi(X)\in v form a nondegenerate conic in the dual projective plane.

Proof 1: Exchange points and lines — outline

Dualize the point-conic Steiner construction: exchange points with lines and intersections with joins. The dual of a projective plane over a field is again such a plane. The two pencils become the point sets of u,v, and the intersections of corresponding pencil lines become the joins of corresponding points. The Steiner theorem in the dual plane gives the claimed dual conic.

Level justification

Apply the point-conic construction in the dual plane. Projective duality, nonperspective correspondences and general-field conics cumulatively exceed a confidently small undergraduate extension.
