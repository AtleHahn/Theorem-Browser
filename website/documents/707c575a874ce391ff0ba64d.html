Statement

Let R be a simple unital ring satisfying a polynomial identity, and let Z(R)=\{z\in R:zr=rz\text{ for all }r\in R\} be its center. A polynomial identity here is a nonzero polynomial in finitely many noncommuting variables over the central field Z(R) that vanishes under every substitution from R. Let A be a simple Z(R)-subalgebra of R containing its identity. With

C_R(S)=\{r\in R:rs=sr\text{ for every }s\in S\},

one has

C_R(C_R(A))=A.

A simple ring or algebra is nonzero and has no nonzero proper two-sided ideals.
