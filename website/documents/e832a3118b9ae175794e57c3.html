Statement

Let \Omega\subset\mathbb R^2 be open, connected and convex, with smooth boundary, and let D=\{x\in\mathbb R^2:|x|<1\}. Given any homeomorphism \mu:\partial D\to\partial\Omega, there is a unique harmonic map u:D\to\Omega that extends continuously to \overline D with boundary values \mu. This map is a diffeomorphism from D onto \Omega. Harmonic means that the two real coordinate functions of u have zero Laplacian, \partial_1^2u_j+\partial_2^2u_j=0. A homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse; a diffeomorphism is a smooth bijection with smooth inverse.
