Statement

Let P,Q be relatively prime nonzero integers such that D=P^2-4Q>0. Define the Lucas sequence

U_0=0,\qquad U_1=1,\qquad U_n=P U_{n-1}-Q U_{n-2}\quad(n\geq2).

For every positive integer n\notin\{1,2,6\}, except when n=12 and (P,Q)=(1,-1) or (-1,-1), there is a prime q such that

q\mid U_n\quad\text{and}\quad q\nmid U_m\quad\text{for every integer }1\leq m<n.

Thus q is a primitive prime divisor among the positive-index terms of the sequence.

High school only
Level justification

All parameters, recurrence and divisibility conditions are explicitly elementary integer arithmetic; understanding the theorem requires neither algebraic number theory nor knowledge of Lucas sequences in advance.
