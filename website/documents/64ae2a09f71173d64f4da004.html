Proof 1: Add the squared-norm expansions

The basic relation between the norm and the inner product is given by the equation

\|v\|^2=\langle v,v\rangle.

Then

\begin{aligned} \|u+v\|^2&=\langle u+v,u+v\rangle\\ &=\langle u,u\rangle+\langle u,v\rangle+\langle v,u\rangle+\langle v,v\rangle\\ &=\|u\|^2+\|v\|^2+2\langle u,v\rangle, \end{aligned}

and similarly

\|u-v\|^2=\|u\|^2+\|v\|^2-2\langle u,v\rangle.

Adding these two equations together gives the parallelogram law.
