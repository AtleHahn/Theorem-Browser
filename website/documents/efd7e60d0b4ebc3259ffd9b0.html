Statement

Let f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R be f(x)=x^2, and let A_1=[-4,2], A_2=[-2,4]. Then f(A_1\cap A_2)=[0,4] is a proper subset of f(A_1)\cap f(A_2)=[0,16].

Level
High school only
Level justification

Squaring intervals, their intersection and proper inclusion are elementary real function and set operations.

Proof 1: Evaluate the intervals specified in the caption

For the map f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R defined by x\mapsto x^2, the source gives

f(A_1\cap A_2)\subsetneq f(A_1)\cap f(A_2),

with A_1=[-4,2], A_2=[-2,4] and their intersection A_3=[-2,2].

The caption supplies this counterexample; the interval-image evaluations in the statement are implicit, rather than calculated in the source.
