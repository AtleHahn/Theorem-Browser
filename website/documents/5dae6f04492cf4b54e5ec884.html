Proof 1: Apply the characteristic-sphere congruence to a small sphere

Here a characteristic sphere is an embedded two-sphere whose mod-two homology class is dual to w_2(M), and a spin structure implies w_2(M)=0. Use the Kervaire–Milnor characteristic-sphere theorem: for a characteristic embedded sphere \Sigma in a smooth closed oriented four-manifold, \sigma(M)\equiv\Sigma\cdot\Sigma\pmod{16}. Since w_2(M)=0, a small null-homologous sphere is characteristic and has self-intersection zero. The congruence gives \sigma(M)\equiv0\pmod{16}.
