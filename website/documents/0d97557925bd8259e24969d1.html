Statement

Let X be a Polish space, meaning a separable topological space with a compatible complete metric, and let (\Omega,\mathcal F) be any measurable space. Suppose \psi assigns to each \omega\in\Omega a nonempty closed subset \psi(\omega)\subseteq X, and

\{\omega\in\Omega:\psi(\omega)\cap U\ne\varnothing\}\in\mathcal F

for every open U\subseteq X. Then there is a function s:\Omega\to X with s(\omega)\in\psi(\omega) for every \omega, such that s^{-1}(B)\in\mathcal F for every Borel set B\subseteq X. The Borel sigma-algebra is generated by the open sets.
