Statement

Let f be holomorphic on A=\{z:r<|z-c|<R\}, where 0\le r<R\le\infty. For an integer n, the value \frac1{2\pi i}\oint_\gamma\frac{f(z)}{(z-c)^{n+1}}\,dz is unchanged under a deformation in A between positively oriented piecewise smooth simple closed curves surrounding c.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex differentiability and Laurent series extend the derivative and power-series definitions. The contour integrals are parametrized ordinary integrals on an annulus, and coefficient uniqueness is an equality of convergent series. These limited additions suffice without Riemann surfaces.

Proof 1: Apply Cauchy’s contour-deformation theorem

The integrand f(z)/(z-c)^{n+1} is holomorphic on the annulus. Cauchy’s integral theorem in its contour-deformation form therefore leaves its integral unchanged under the stated deformation.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Check the quotient integrand is holomorphic on the annulus and apply the supplied deformation theorem. Undergraduate complex contour integration suffices; the singular center lies outside the deformation domain.
