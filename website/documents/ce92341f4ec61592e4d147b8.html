Proof 1: A coordinate projection need not be closed — outline

Projections need not be closed. Consider the projection p:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R on the first component; then the set A=\{(x,1/x):x\ne0\} is closed in \mathbb R^2, but p(A)=\mathbb R\setminus\{0\} is not closed in \mathbb R.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The hyperbola is the inverse image of the closed singleton one under the continuous product map. Its coordinate image omits zero and is not closed. Basic topology of the plane suffices.
