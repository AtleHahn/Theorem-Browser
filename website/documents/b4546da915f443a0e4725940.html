Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle and let D lie strictly between B and C on the side BC. Then AD bisects the interior angle BAC if and only if

\frac{|BD|}{|CD|}=\frac{|AB|}{|AC|}.

Here |PQ| denotes the length of the segment joining P and Q, and bisecting means that \angle BAD=\angle DAC.

High school only
