Statement

Let \varphi,\psi be Lebesgue integrable real or complex functions on [0,\infty) and \kappa>0. If

\int_0^x\varphi(t)\psi(x-t)\,dt=0

for almost every x\in(0,\kappa), then there are \lambda,\mu\ge0 with \lambda+\mu\ge\kappa such that \varphi=0 almost everywhere on (0,\lambda) and \psi=0 almost everywhere on (0,\mu). Consequently, if the convolution vanishes for every positive x where defined, one of the two functions vanishes almost everywhere on [0,\infty). Here almost everywhere refers to Lebesgue measure.
