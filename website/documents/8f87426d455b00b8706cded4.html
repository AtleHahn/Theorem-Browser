Statement

Let n\ge1 and let (a_i,b_i)_{i=1}^n be pairs of nonnegative integers with a_1\ge\cdots\ge a_n. There is a directed graph on vertices 1,\ldots,n, with no loops and at most one arrow for each ordered pair of distinct vertices, in which vertex i has indegree a_i and outdegree b_i, if and only if \sum_{i=1}^na_i=\sum_{i=1}^nb_i and \sum_{i=1}^ka_i\le\sum_{i=1}^k\min(b_i,k-1)+\sum_{i=k+1}^n\min(b_i,k) for every 1\le k\le n. It suffices to check these inequalities only at k=n and at the indices 1\le k<n where a_k>a_{k+1}. Indegree counts incoming arrows and outdegree outgoing arrows; opposite arrows between a pair of vertices are allowed.
