Statement

Let A\subseteq\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\} have positive upper density, meaning \limsup_{n\to\infty}|A\cap\{1,\ldots,n\}|/n>0. Then there exist infinite sets B,C\subseteq\mathbb N such that B+C=\{b+c:b\in B,c\in C\}\subseteq A.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Integer sets are elementary; the sole extra limiting-density concept is standard undergraduate real analysis.
