Statement

Let (F,P) be an ordered field and E/F a Galois extension. There is a maximal pair (M,Q) with F\subseteq M\subseteq E and a field ordering Q on M extending P.

Proof 1: Apply Zorn to ordered intermediate fields — outline

Apply Zorn’s lemma to the ordered intermediate fields in E extending the given ordering of F, ordered by field inclusion and extension of the ordering. A chain has an upper bound obtained by taking the union of its fields and compatible orderings. Hence a maximal ordered subextension exists.
