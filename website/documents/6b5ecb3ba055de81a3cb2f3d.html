Statement

If bounded normal operators M,N on a complex Hilbert space satisfy MN=NM, their product MN is normal.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Hilbert-space operators and adjoints require functional analysis beyond calculus and linear algebra.

Mathematical Field
Functional analysis

Review notes
none

Proof 1: Commute the cross-adjoint factors and use normality

The argument invokes only Fuglede's theorem: an operator commuting with a bounded normal operator also commutes with its adjoint. One can directly compute

(MN)(MN)^*=MN(NM)^*=MNM^*N^*.

By Fuglede, the above becomes

=MM^*NN^*.

But M and N are normal, so

=M^*MN^*N.

By Fuglede (again), the above becomes

=M^*N^*MN=(NM)^*MN

and the hypothesis MN=NM shows that it is

=(MN)^*MN.
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The algebraic rearrangement uses Fuglede for arbitrary bounded normal Hilbert-space operators. Infinite-dimensional adjoints and normal-operator theory are beyond ordinary matrix linear algebra; retain the advanced level.
