Statement

Let L be the Laplacian of a finite simple undirected graph on n\ge2 vertices. All cofactors (-1)^{i+j}\det L_{\widehat i,\widehat j} are equal. Here the Laplacian is L=D-A, where D is the diagonal vertex-degree matrix and A is the adjacency matrix. The matrix L_{\widehat i,\widehat j} is obtained by deleting row i and column j.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves finite graphs; Laplacian matrices; determinants. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Equality of graph Laplacian cofactors — outline

The entries of each row and each column of L sum to zero. Thus, by adding rows and columns, interchanging them, and multiplying a row or column by -1, one can transform any deleted-row-and-column minor into any other. Accounting for these signs with the cofactor factor (-1)^{i+j} gives the same value for every cofactor.
