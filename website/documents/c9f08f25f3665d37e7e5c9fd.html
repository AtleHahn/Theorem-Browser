Statement

Let X=U\cup V be a topological space covered by open path-connected subsets U,V, with U\cap V nonempty and path-connected. Fix x_0\in U\cap V. Put

A=\pi_1(U,x_0),\quad B=\pi_1(V,x_0),\quad C=\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0),

and let i:C\to A, j:C\to B be the homomorphisms induced by inclusion. The fundamental group \pi_1(Z,x_0) consists of loops in Z based at x_0, modulo homotopies fixing the base point, with concatenation as product.

The inclusion maps induce an isomorphism

\frac{A*B}{\langle\!\langle i(c)j(c)^{-1}:c\in C\rangle\!\rangle}\ \cong\ \pi_1(X,x_0).

Here A*B is the free product of groups, and the denominator is the smallest normal subgroup containing the displayed elements, with i(c) and j(c) viewed in the respective free-product factors. Equivalently, the fundamental group of X is the pushout of A\leftarrow C\to B. Neither i nor j is required to be injective.
