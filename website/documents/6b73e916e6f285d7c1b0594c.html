Proof 1: An everywhere-local eighth power that is not global — outline

Use the field criterion

X^8-16=(X^2-2)(X^2+2)(X^2-2X+2)(X^2+2X+2):

16 is an eighth power in a field if and only if 2, -2 or -1 is a square. Also use the result that 16 is an eighth power in \mathbb Q_p for every odd prime p.

16 is not an eighth power in \mathbb Q(\sqrt7) although it is an eighth power locally everywhere. This follows from the criterion and odd-prime result above and the equality

\mathbb Q_2(\sqrt7)=\mathbb Q_2(\sqrt{-1}).

The local-field equality is an external prerequisite. The source leaves the global square check and passage to the other completions implicit; the real completions have a square root of 2.
