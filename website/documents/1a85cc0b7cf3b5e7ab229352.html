Statement

Let A,B\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}, n\ge1, be Hermitian. Their finite spectra satisfy d_H(\Lambda(A),\Lambda(B))\le\|A-B\|_2, where d_H(S,T)=\max\{\max_{s\in S}\min_{t\in T}|s-t|,\max_{t\in T}\min_{s\in S}|t-s|\} and \|\cdot\|_2 is the induced Euclidean matrix norm.

Level justification

Proof 1: Hermitian spectra are non-expansive in Hausdorff distance proof

For a normal matrix A and any perturbation E, each eigenvalue \mu of A+E lies within \|E\|_2 of some eigenvalue of A (the normal-matrix Bauer–Fike bound).

This remains true when A is Hermitian. In the Hermitian case the Bauer–Fike theorem can be restated by saying that the map taking a matrix to its spectrum is non-expansive for the Hausdorff distance on compact subsets of \mathbb C.
