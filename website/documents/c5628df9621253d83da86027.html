Statement

Let V be a finite-dimensional complex vector space, v_1,\ldots,v_n\in V with n\ge1, and let \lambda=(\lambda_1\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_r>0) be a partition of n. Its Young diagram has \lambda_i boxes in row i; write c_1,\ldots,c_{\lambda_1} for its column lengths. Let \chi^\lambda be the character of the complex Specht representation of S_n of shape \lambda, so \chi^\lambda(\sigma) is its trace at \sigma. Then \frac{\chi^\lambda(e)}{n!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\chi^\lambda(\sigma)\,v_{\sigma(1)}\otimes\cdots\otimes v_{\sigma(n)}\ne0 if and only if the index set \{1,\ldots,n\} can be partitioned into sets I_1,\ldots,I_{\lambda_1} with |I_j|=c_j such that (v_i)_{i\in I_j} is linearly independent for every j. Here e is the identity permutation and the tensor lies in V^{\otimes n}. To specify the representation indexing: a tableau labels the diagram bijectively by 1,\ldots,n; a tabloid identifies tableaux differing only by permutations within rows. In the complex vector space with tabloids as basis, the Specht representation is the span of \sum_{\sigma\in Q_T}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\{\sigma T\} over tableaux T, where Q_T preserves each column as a set and braces denote a tabloid. The group acts by permuting labels.
