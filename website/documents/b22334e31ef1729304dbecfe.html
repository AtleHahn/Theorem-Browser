Statement

Let f(z,z_2,\ldots,z_n) be holomorphic near 0\in\mathbb C^n, with f(0)=0 and f(z,0,\ldots,0) not identically zero. By the Weierstrass preparation theorem write f=Wh locally, where h(0)\ne0 and W is monic in z with holomorphic coefficients vanishing at the remaining origin. On a sufficiently small neighborhood, f=0 if and only if W=0.

Proof 1: Nonvanishing of the unit factor

Use the factorization f=Wh supplied by the Weierstrass preparation theorem, with h analytic and h(0,\ldots,0) not zero. Shrink the neighborhood so that h remains nonzero.

This has the immediate consequence that the set of zeros of f, near (0, ..., 0), can be found by fixing any small values of z2, ..., zn and then solving the equation W(z)=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply preparation for a several-variable holomorphic germ and remove its nonvanishing unit factor. The last continuity step is elementary, but the preparation setting and holomorphic coefficient germs are not confidently a small addition to one-variable complex analysis.
