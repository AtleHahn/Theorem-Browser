Statement

Let X be an algebraic curve over a field k, let x be a smooth k-rational point, and choose a local parameter t identifying its infinitesimal neighborhood with D=\operatorname{Spec}k[[t]] and the punctured neighborhood with D^0=\operatorname{Spec}k((t)). Put U=X\setminus\{x\} and let r\ge1. Isomorphism classes of triples (E,\tau,\sigma), with E a rank-r vector bundle on X and trivializations \tau:E|_U\cong\mathcal O_U^r and \sigma:E|_D\cong\mathcal O_D^r, are in bijection with \operatorname{GL}_r(k((t))). Isomorphisms of triples must respect both trivializations.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The assertion glues algebraic vector bundles over a curve and formal punctured neighborhoods using trivializations. Schemes, local parameters and formal completion require more preparation than a half-hour extension of undergraduate algebra. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Transition functions and the gluing equivalence (outline)

Use the global Beauville–Laszlo gluing theorem as a prerequisite: restriction gives an equivalence between rank-r vector bundles on X and pairs of such bundles on U and D equipped with an identification on D^0.

The corollary follows from the gluing theorem in that the triple is associated with the unique matrix which, viewed as a "transition function" over D^0 between the trivial bundles over U and over D, allows gluing them to form E, with the natural trivializations of the glued bundle then being identified with σ and τ.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof applies a gluing equivalence for algebraic vector bundles on a curve, its formal local disk and punctured disk, then interprets the overlap isomorphism as a matrix. Schemes, completed local rings, bundle trivializations and categorical gluing are several interdependent graduate algebraic-geometric notions. The final matrix parametrization does not remove that prerequisite burden.

Automorphisms of a trivial rank-r bundle on an affine scheme are invertible r-by-r matrices over its ring
Kind:

Theorem

Treatment:

Implicitly assumed

Statement used:

Automorphisms of the trivial rank-r bundle on Spec R correspond to GL_r(R).

Application in proof:

Identify the overlap automorphism with a unique matrix in GL_r(k((t))).

Application level:

Beyond undergraduate level

Level justification:

Although matrix invertibility is elementary, this application interprets bundle morphisms over an affine scheme and their trivializations.
