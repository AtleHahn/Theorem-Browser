Statement

For an F-linear map between two vector spaces of the same finite dimension over a field F, injectivity, surjectivity and bijectivity are equivalent.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Matrices, determinants, kernels, linear independence and bases are standard undergraduate linear algebra. Over an algebraically closed field, the extra condition simply guarantees polynomial roots; infinite dimensions mean cardinalities of bases. The specific matrix or dimension claim adds no specialist construction.

Proof 1: Rank–nullity dimension comparison — outline

The rank–nullity identity makes zero nullity equivalent to rank equal to the common dimension. Thus injectivity or surjectivity implies bijectivity.
