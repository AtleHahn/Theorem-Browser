Statement

For a smooth cubic surface X\subset\mathbf P^3_k over an algebraically closed field, \mathcal O_{\mathbf P^3}(1)|_X\cong\omega_X^{-1}, where \omega_X is its canonical line bundle.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves smooth projective surfaces; line bundles; canonical bundle. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Specialize the hypersurface adjunction formula — outline

The adjunction formula for the degree-three hypersurface in projective 3-space gives that the restricted hyperplane bundle is the anticanonical bundle.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Applying adjunction requires smooth projective varieties, canonical line bundles, divisor bundles and restriction/tensor operations. These are substantial algebraic-geometric prerequisites despite the simple degree arithmetic.
