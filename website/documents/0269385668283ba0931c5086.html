Statement

For invertible A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}, w\in\mathbb C^n, and 1\le i\le n, let e_i be the ith coordinate column. Adding w^T to row i gives A+e_iw^T, whose determinant is \det(A)(1+w^TA^{-1}e_i).

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Insert coordinate vectors in the rank-one formula

For invertible A and columns u,v, use \det(A+uv^T)=\det(A)(1+v^TA^{-1}u).

Set u=e_i and v=w. The product e_iw^T has row i equal to w^T and every other row zero. The determinant formula gives the result.
