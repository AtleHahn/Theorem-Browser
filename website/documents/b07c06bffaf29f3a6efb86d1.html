Statement

For each c\in[0,1], define y_c(t)=0 for 0\leq t\leq c and y_c(t)=(t-c)^2/4 for c\leq t\leq1. Then y_c solves y^{\prime}=\sqrt{|y|} with y(0)=0 on [0,1]. The identically zero solution y_1 differs from y_0(t)=t^2/4, so this IVP is not unique.

Proof 1: Differentiate the waiting-time branches — outline

The ordinary differential equation y'=|y|^{1/2} on [0,1] has solutions starting at y(0)=0: either y(t)=0 or y(t)=t^2/4. The transition between y=0 and y=(t-c)^2/4 can happen at any c\in[0,1], giving the waiting-time solutions in the statement.
