Statement

There exist a complex Banach space X and a nonempty bounded closed convex subset B\subseteq X such that the complex-linear continuous functionals g for which some b\in B satisfies |g(b)|=\sup_{x\in B}|g(x)| are not norm-dense in X^*. Here X^* has its operator norm \|g\|=\sup_{\|x\|\le1}|g(x)|, and convex combinations use real coefficients between zero and one.
