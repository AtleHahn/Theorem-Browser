Statement

Let U\subset\mathbb R^3 be a bounded domain with piecewise smooth boundary and outward unit normal n. Functions are defined on a neighborhood of \overline U. Write \Delta=\nabla\cdot\nabla and \partial_n f=\nabla f\cdot n. Let \psi\in C^2 be harmonic, \Delta\psi=0. Fix \eta\in U. Let G(y,\eta) be a Laplace Green function with \Delta_yG=\delta_\eta, smooth away from its pole and with the boundary derivatives used below. Let A=|\partial U|. If \partial_nG=1/A on \partial U, then \psi(\eta)=A^{-1}\int_{\partial U}\psi\,dS-\int_{\partial U}G\partial_n\psi\,dS.

Proof 1: Substitute the constant normal derivative

Green’s third identity states \psi(\eta)-\int_U G\Delta\psi\,dV=\int_{\partial U}(\psi\partial_nG-G\partial_n\psi)\,dS. Harmonicity removes the volume term. Substituting \partial_nG=1/A makes the first boundary term the surface average.
