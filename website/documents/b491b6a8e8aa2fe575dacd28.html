Statement

Let \mathbb F_q be a finite field with q elements. Let K=\{P_1,\ldots,P_m\}\subset PG(N,q), with m\ge N+1 and N\ge1. Here a projective point is a one-dimensional subspace of \mathbb F_q^{N+1}. Choose a nonzero vector representing each point and put these vectors in the columns of G. Then K is an m-arc, meaning every N+1 representatives are linearly independent, if and only if G generates an [m,N+1,m-N] MDS code. The parameters [m,k,d] mean length, vector-space dimension and minimum Hamming distance; MDS means d=m-k+1.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra over a finite field supplies one-dimensional subspaces, matrices and independence. A code is the row space, and Hamming distance counts nonzero coordinates; every parameter and arc condition is explicit. These finite-coordinate definitions avoid general projective geometry.

Proof 1: Homogeneous-coordinate columns — outline

Use the MDS characterization supplied by the source: a dimension-k code is MDS exactly when every k columns of a generator matrix are independent. The homogeneous-coordinate construction identifies the arc condition with independence of every N+1 columns of G. In that case G has rank N+1 and generates an MDS code of length m and dimension N+1, whose distance is m-(N+1)+1=m-N. Conversely the MDS characterization gives the same independent-column condition and therefore the arc.
