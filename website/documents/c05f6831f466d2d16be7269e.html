Statement

Let \mu be a finite positive Borel measure on \mathbb C, and put n=\mu(\mathbb C). For every 0<H<1 there is a finite or countable family of disks D_i with radii r_i such that \sum_i r_i<5H and, for every z\notin\bigcup_iD_i,

u(z)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb C}\log|z-\zeta|\,d\mu(\zeta)\ \ge\ \frac n{2\pi}\log(H/e).

The integral is interpreted in the extended Lebesgue sense: at the points covered by the conclusion its negative part is integrable, and its positive part may have infinite integral, giving u(z)=+\infty. No value or lower bound is asserted inside the exceptional union. Thus no finite logarithmic moment at infinity is required. Here e is the base of the natural logarithm, and the empty family of disks is allowed.
