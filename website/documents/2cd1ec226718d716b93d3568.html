Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate plane triangle. Erect equilateral triangles inwardly on its three sides and join their centroids. This inner centroid triangle has zero area if and only if ABC is equilateral.

Proof 1: Apply the equality condition in Weitzenböck’s inequality

Let \Delta be the area of the original triangle and a,b,c its side lengths. The area of the inner Napoleon triangle is

A_{in}=-\frac{\Delta}{2}+\frac{\sqrt3}{24}(a^2+b^2+c^2)\geq0,

with equality only in the case in which the original triangle is equilateral, by Weitzenböck's inequality: a^2+b^2+c^2\geq4\sqrt3\Delta, with equality exactly for an equilateral triangle.
