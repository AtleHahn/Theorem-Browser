Statement

Let S=\{(x,y)\in(0,\infty)^2:xy\ge9\}, ordered coordinatewise. Every pair (x,9/x) with x>0 is minimal in S: no distinct (u,v)\in S has u\le x and v\le9/x. Thus S has infinitely many minimal elements.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Coordinate order and minimality are explicitly inequalities for positive real pairs on or above a hyperbola.

Proof 1: Minimal points on the source hyperbola — outline

Consider the points (x,9/x) for positive x. A different point in S cannot be less than or equal to such a point in both coordinates. Thus these points are minimal, one for each positive x.

Level
High school only
Level justification

On the boundary xy=9 decreasing either positive coordinate without increasing the other violates the product constraint. Varying x gives infinitely many minimal pairs. School inequalities and the stated coordinate order suffice.
