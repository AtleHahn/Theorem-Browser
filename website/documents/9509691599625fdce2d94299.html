Statement

Fix an admissible numbering e\mapsto\varphi_e of the partial recursive functions on \mathbb N. Equality \simeq means equality of partial functions, including their domains. For every partial recursive two-variable function Q, there is an index p such that \varphi_p(y)\simeq Q(p,y) for all y.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Computability-theoretic indices and partial recursive functions are outside undergraduate calculus and linear algebra.

Mathematical Field
Mathematical logic

Proof 1: Compile the parameter and apply Rogers

By s-m-n, choose a total computable function F such that \varphi_{F(p)}(y)\simeq Q(p,y). Rogers’s fixed-point theorem supplies p with \varphi_p\simeq\varphi_{F(p)}. Hence \varphi_p(y)\simeq Q(p,y), as required.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compile a parameter into a total index map and invoke Rogers for partial recursive functions. Admissible numberings, partial-function equality and effective program self-reference are cumulative computability prerequisites; retain beyond undergraduate conservatively.
