Statement

Every nonempty simply connected open subset G of \mathbb C is homeomorphic to D=\{z:|z|<1\}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Homeomorphism is baseline topology; simple connectivity means contracting every loop. These planar notions suffice to understand the statement.

Proof 1: Simply connected plane domains are homeomorphic to the disk (outline)

If G\ne\mathbb C, the Riemann mapping theorem supplies a biholomorphism onto D, hence a homeomorphism. For G=\mathbb C, use \phi(z)=z/(1+|z|).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the supplied Riemann map on proper domains and the explicit radial homeomorphism for the whole plane. Undergraduate complex analysis and point-set topology suffice.
