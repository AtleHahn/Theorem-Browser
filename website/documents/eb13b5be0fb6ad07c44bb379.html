Statement

Let V be a finite-dimensional complex vector space and \Lambda\subset V a lattice, so that T=V/\Lambda is a complex torus. Let H be a Hermitian form on V, with the convention that it is complex linear in its first variable, and put E=\operatorname{Im}H. Suppose E(u,v)\in\mathbb Z for all u,v\in\Lambda. Let \alpha:\Lambda\to U(1), where U(1)=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=1\}, satisfy

\alpha(u+v)=e^{i\pi E(u,v)}\alpha(u)\alpha(v)\qquad(u,v\in\Lambda).

The functions

a_u(z)=\alpha(u)\exp\!\left(\pi H(z,u)+\frac{\pi}{2}H(u,u)\right)\qquad(u\in\Lambda,\ z\in V)

form a holomorphic factor of automorphy (a 1-cocycle) defining a holomorphic line bundle on T. Every holomorphic line bundle on T, up to isomorphism, arises in this way from exactly one pair (H,\alpha) satisfying these conditions.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra supplies Hermitian forms but not holomorphic line bundles, descent, and complex tori as complex manifolds. Their cumulative conceptual burden exceeds the conservative undergraduate allowance.
