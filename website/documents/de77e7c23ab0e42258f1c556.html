Statement

If n>0 and integers x,y solve x^2-ny^2=-1, then 4\nmid n and no prime p\equiv3\pmod4 divides n.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The integer constructions use finite algebra and explicitly stated recurrences.

Mathematical Field

Required topics

Review notes

Proof 1: Reduction modulo divisors outline

This is because the negative Pell equation implies that -1 is a quadratic residue modulo n:

x^2\equiv-1\pmod n.

Here one uses the standard obstruction that -1 is not a square modulo 4 or modulo a prime of the form 4k+3.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Reduce the equation modulo divisors of n and use the explicit obstruction to -1 being a square. Elementary integer remainders suffice; quadratic residue here means simply a square remainder.
