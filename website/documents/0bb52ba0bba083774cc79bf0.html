For nonnegative real bases and exponents where real exponentiation is defined, (x,y)\mapsto x^y has no limit as (x,y)\to(0,0).

Proof 1: Compare the two coordinate-axis limits

Along (x,0) with x>0, the value is x^0=1. Along (0,y) with y>0, the value is 0^y=0. Both paths approach the origin but have different limiting values.
