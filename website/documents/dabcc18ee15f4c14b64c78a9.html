Statement

All immersions S^0\to\mathbb R belong to one regular homotopy class.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding immersions of a zero-dimensional manifold and their regular-homotopy equivalence uses the stated differential-topology definitions.

Proof 1: Use connectedness of SO(1)

In the stated codimension-one sphere classification the relevant space is SO(1). It is path connected, so its component set has one element and the zero-sphere has a single regular homotopy class.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the sphere-immersion classification through components of SO(1). Although the component space is trivial, the invoked regular-homotopy classification requires differential-topological concepts not confidently a small extension of the baseline.
