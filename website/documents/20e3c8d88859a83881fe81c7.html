Statement

In the affine plane over \mathbb C, the parabola y=x^2 and the line y=0 have intersection multiplicity two at the origin.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Intersection multiplicity of algebraic curves is an algebraic-geometric notion beyond ordinary plane geometry.

Proof 1: Tensor the two quotient rings — outline

The intersection ring is

\frac{\mathbb C[x,y]}{(y-x^2)}\otimes_{\mathbb C[x,y]}\frac{\mathbb C[x,y]}{(y)}\cong\frac{\mathbb C[x]}{(x^2)}.

It is Cohen–Macaulay of length two. For this proper intersection, the intersection multiplicity is the length of its Cohen–Macaulay local intersection ring; hence it is two.
