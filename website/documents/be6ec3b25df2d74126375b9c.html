Statement

Let X_1,X_2,\ldots be independent identically distributed real random variables with \mathbb EX_1=0, \mathbb EX_1^2=\sigma^2>0, and \rho=\mathbb E|X_1|^3<\infty. Let F_n be the distribution function of (X_1+\cdots+X_n)/(\sigma\sqrt n) and \Phi the standard normal distribution function. For every n\ge1, \sup_x|F_n(x)-\Phi(x)|\le0.4748\rho/(\sigma^3\sqrt n).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The claim concerns arbitrary real random variables, independence, expectation and moments, a normalized sum, its cumulative distribution and the normal distribution. Neither the general probability setting nor this collection of probabilistic constructions is part of the stated baseline. The cumulative additional preparation cannot confidently fit approximately 30 minutes beyond the stipulated undergraduate baseline. Under the conservative threshold rule, this is assessed as Beyond undergraduate level; uncertainty about the exact preparation time does not leave the classification provisional.

Proof 1: Moment comparison in the refined bound

Here C denotes an admissible constant in \sup_x|F_n(x)-\Phi(x)|\le C\rho/(\sigma^3\sqrt n).

The estimate C < 0.4748 follows from the inequality

{\displaystyle \sup _{x\in \mathbb {R} }\left|F_{n}(x)-\Phi (x)\right|\leq {0.33554(\rho +0.415\sigma ^{3}) \over \sigma ^{3}{\sqrt {n}}},}

since σ3 ≤ ρ and 0.33554 · 1.415 < 0.4748.
