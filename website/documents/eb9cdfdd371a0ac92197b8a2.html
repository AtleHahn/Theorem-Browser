Statement

Let K be a number field. Choose a primitive element \alpha with K=\mathbb Q(\alpha), and let f be its minimal polynomial over \mathbb Q. If r_1 counts real embeddings and r_2 conjugate pairs of nonreal embeddings of K, then r_1 is the number of real roots of f and 2r_2 is the number of nonreal roots.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Primitive elements, minimal polynomials and embeddings extend introductory field algebra by related finite-extension definitions. The signature is explicitly a root/embedding count, not arithmetic number theory.

Proof 1: Use a primitive element and count its conjugates — outline

Use the primitive element theorem to write K=\mathbb Q(\alpha). Count the real conjugates of \alpha to obtain r_1, and the nonreal conjugates, paired by complex conjugation, to obtain 2r_2. These are the real and nonreal roots of the minimal polynomial.

Level justification
