Statement

Let G be a connected noncompact real semisimple Lie group with finite center and K a maximal compact subgroup. Write G=KAN for an Iwasawa decomposition, A^*=\operatorname{Hom}(A,\mathbb T) for its unitary characters, and W=N_K(A)/C_K(A) for its Weyl group. The character spectrum X of the reduced radial convolution algebra is in bijection with A^*/W.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Semisimple Lie groups and maximal compact subgroups; Biinvariant functions and convolution; Haar measure; Spherical functions. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Spherical spectrum is parametrized by Weyl orbits — source argument

Let \mathfrak A be the operator-norm closure of the convolution operators from the Hecke algebra C_c(K\backslash G/K) on L^2(G/K), and let X be its character spectrum.

The characters χλ of \mathfrak A (i.e. the points of X) can be described by positive definite spherical functions φλ on G, via the formula

{\displaystyle \chi _{\lambda }(\pi (f))=\int _{G}f(g)\cdot \varphi _{\lambda }(g)\,dg.}

for f in C_c(K\backslash G/K), where π(f) denotes the convolution operator in {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {A}}} and the integral is with respect to Haar measure on G.

The spherical functions φλ on G are given by Harish-Chandra's formula:

{\displaystyle \varphi _{\lambda }(g)=\int _{K}\lambda ^{\prime }(gk)^{-1}\,dk.}

In this formula:

  • the integral is with respect to normalized Haar measure on K;

  • λ is an element of A* =Hom(A,T) where A is the Abelian vector subgroup in the Iwasawa decomposition G =KAN of G;

  • λ' is defined on G by first extending λ to a character of the solvable subgroup AN, using the group homomorphism onto A, and then setting

    {\displaystyle \lambda '(kx)=\Delta _{AN}(x)^{1/2}\lambda (x)}

    for k in K and x in AN, where ΔAN is the modular function of AN.

  • Two different characters λ1 and λ2 give the same spherical function if and only if λ1 = λ2·s, where s is in the Weyl group of A

    {\displaystyle W=N_{K}(A)/C_{K}(A),}

    the quotient of the normaliser of A in K by its centraliser, a finite reflection group.

It follows that

  • X can be identified with the quotient space A*/W.

The spherical parametrization and the equivalence criterion are used here as structural results; this passage does not prove them.
