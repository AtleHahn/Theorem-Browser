Statement

Let X be an affine scheme, meaning X\cong\operatorname{Spec}R for a commutative ring R, and let F be a coherent \mathcal O_X-module sheaf. Then, for every x\in X, the stalk F_x is generated over \mathcal O_{X,x} by the germs of global sections of F. An \mathcal O_X-module sheaf is coherent if it is locally finitely generated and on every open subset the kernel of every morphism from a finite-rank free module sheaf into its restriction is locally finitely generated. Stalks are germs of local sections, and global sections are sections defined on all of X.
