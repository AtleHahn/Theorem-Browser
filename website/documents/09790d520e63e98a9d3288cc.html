Statement

For nonnegative real x,y,z and t>0, x^t(x-y)(x-z)+y^t(y-z)(y-x)+z^t(z-x)(z-y)\ge0. Equality holds exactly when all three variables are equal or two are equal and the third is zero.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The inequality and equality cases use only real arithmetic and powers.

Proof 1: Order the variables and group nonnegative terms — outline

By symmetry assume x\ge y\ge z\ge0. The left side equals (x-y)[x^t(x-z)-y^t(y-z)]+z^t(x-z)(y-z). Both terms are nonnegative since x^t\ge y^t and x-z\ge y-z. If x>y, the first term is strictly positive. If x=y, the expression is z^t(x-z)^2, which vanishes exactly when z=0 or x=z. This gives the equality cases under the ordering and hence by symmetry.

Level justification

Order the variables and group nonnegative factors, then inspect when each vanishes. School algebra and monotonicity of positive powers suffice.
