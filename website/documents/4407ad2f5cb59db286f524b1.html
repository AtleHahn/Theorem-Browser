Statement

In a smooth time-oriented Lorentzian manifold, any two C^k Cauchy hypersurfaces are C^k-diffeomorphic, for k\ge1. Here each Cauchy hypersurface meets every inextendible timelike curve exactly once.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Lorentzian manifolds; Cauchy hypersurfaces; Smooth flows; Causal curves. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Match the two intersections along each timelike orbit — outline

By using the flow of a vector field chosen to be complete, smooth, and timelike, it is elementary to prove that any two C^k-smooth Cauchy surfaces are C^k-diffeomorphic.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Matching intersections along complete timelike orbits requires smooth Lorentzian geometry, Cauchy hypersurfaces and transverse-section flow theory. The brevity of the outline does not reduce those prerequisites.
