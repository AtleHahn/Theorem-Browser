Statement

On D=\{z:|z|<1\} define d(z,w)=\operatorname{arctanh}|(z-w)/(1-\overline zw)|. Every holomorphic map f:D\to D satisfies d(f(z),f(w))\le d(z,w).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Real analysis and calculus supply limits, derivatives, series and integration. Complex differentiability, primitives and logarithms are direct extensions, and circle integrals are parametrized ordinary integrals. The full claims use no Riemann surfaces, residues or abstract differential forms.

Proof 1: Metric interpretation of the two-point bound

The Schwarz–Pick inequality gives

\left|\frac{f(z)-f(w)}{1-\overline{f(z)}f(w)}\right|\le\left|\frac{z-w}{1-\overline zw}\right|.

Apply the increasing function \operatorname{arctanh} on [0,1) to obtain the asserted distance contraction.
