Statement

Let \mathcal F be a smooth regular foliation of a manifold M, and let E_p be the tangent space to the leaf through p. If smooth local vector fields X,Y take values in E, then their Lie bracket [X,Y] also takes values in E.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves smooth manifolds; differential forms; regular foliations. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Check bracket tangency in foliation coordinates (outline)

The source identifies E_p with the tangent space of the leaf through p. In a foliation chart, the leaves have fixed transverse coordinates, and tangent vector fields have no transverse component. Their Lie bracket has no transverse component either, so it is tangent to the same plaques and lies in E.
