Ergodicity criterion for a unimodular torus automorphism

Statement

Let A\in\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb Z), n\ge1, and let T be the automorphism induced by A on \mathbb R^n/\mathbb Z^n with normalized Haar measure. Then T is ergodic if and only if no complex eigenvalue of A is a root of unity.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement involves Haar-measure ergodicity on a compact quotient group.

Proof 1: Specialize the character-periodicity criterion — outline

For a compact abelian group, an automorphism is ergodic exactly when its adjoint on continuous characters has no nontrivial periodic character. Specialize this criterion to the torus automorphism represented by A: it says precisely that no eigenvalue of A is a root of unity.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Specialize the compact-group character criterion to the torus and its integer character lattice. Haar measure, ergodicity and dual characters require substantial harmonic analysis even though the last step concerns eigenvalues.
