Statement

Let K be a Hilbertian field, and place the following algebraic extensions in a common separable closure of K. Let L/K be separable, and suppose there are Galois extensions N/K and M/K such that

L\subseteq NM,\qquad L\not\subseteq N,\qquad L\not\subseteq M.

Then L is Hilbertian. The extensions need not be finite. The compositum NM is the smallest subfield of the common overfield containing both N and M.

Here a field F is Hilbertian if, for every polynomial f(T,X)\in F[T,X] irreducible in F(T)[X] and separable of positive degree in X, there exist infinitely many a\in F for which f(a,X) has the same degree in X and is irreducible in F[X]. Separability means absence of repeated roots over an algebraic closure. An algebraic extension is separable when all minimal polynomials of its elements are separable, and Galois when it is separable and normal (each such minimal polynomial splits in the extension).
