Statement

For -1<t<1, 2\operatorname{artanh}t=\ln\frac{1+t}{1-t}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The companion identifies the inverse parameter through the explicit exponential ratio; the conclusion is an elementary logarithm and inverse-function relation on (-1,1).

Proof 1: Take logarithms of the exponential ratio

Set t=\tanh(\psi/2) for real \psi. The hyperbolic half-angle identity gives e^\psi=(1+t)/(1-t). Taking real logarithms yields \psi=\ln((1+t)/(1-t)). Since \psi=2\operatorname{artanh}t, the formula follows.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Use the given hyperbolic exponential ratio and take real logarithms. This application requires only exponential algebra and inverse functions; no calculus is used.
