Statement

Let f:X\to S be a proper morphism of noetherian schemes, \mathcal F a coherent sheaf on X, and \mathcal I a coherent ideal defining a closed subscheme S_0. Put \mathcal J=\mathcal I\mathcal O_X, X_0=f^{-1}(S_0), and let i:\widehat S\to S, i':\widehat X\to X be the corresponding formal-completion maps, with induced \widehat f:\widehat X\to\widehat S. Write \mathcal F_n=\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_S}(\mathcal O_S/\mathcal I^{n+1}). For each fixed cohomological degree q\geq0, the canonical completion comparison map factors as

(R^qf_*\mathcal F)^\wedge\longrightarrow R^q\widehat f_*\widehat{\mathcal F}\longrightarrow\varprojlim_n R^qf_*\mathcal F_n.

Here the first arrow is induced by base change to the formal completion and the second by reduction to the infinitesimal neighborhoods. No assertion that these maps are isomorphisms is included.

Proof 1: Base change followed by infinitesimal reduction (outline)

Let i':\widehat X\to X, i:\widehat S\to S be the canonical maps. Then we have the base-change map of \mathcal O_{\widehat S}-modules

i^*R^qf_*\mathcal F\longrightarrow R^q\widehat f_*(i'^*\mathcal F),

where \widehat f:\widehat X\to\widehat S is induced by f:X\to S. Since \mathcal F is coherent, we can identify i'^*\mathcal F with \widehat{\mathcal F}. Since R^qf_*\mathcal F is also coherent (as f is proper), doing the same identification, the above reads:

(R^qf_*\mathcal F)^\wedge\longrightarrow R^q\widehat f_*\widehat{\mathcal F}.

Using the induced morphisms f_n:X_n\to S_n, where

X_n=(X_0,\mathcal O_X/\mathcal J^{n+1}),\qquad S_n=(S_0,\mathcal O_S/\mathcal I^{n+1}),

one also obtains (after passing to the limit):

R^q\widehat f_*\widehat{\mathcal F}\longrightarrow \varprojlim_n R^qf_*\mathcal F_n.

One can verify that the composition of the two maps is the canonical completion comparison map in the statement. This compatibility verification is left implicit in the source.
