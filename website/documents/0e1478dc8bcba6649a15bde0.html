Statement

For nondegenerate triangles with side lengths a,b,c and A,B,C and respective areas f,F, A^2(b^2+c^2-a^2)+B^2(a^2+c^2-b^2)+C^2(a^2+b^2-c^2)\geq16Ff. Equality holds precisely when the triangles are similar with corresponding pairs (A,a),(B,b),(C,c).

Level
High school only
Level justification

Elementary triangle area and finite real inequalities suffice.

Proof 1: Heron area identities and Cauchy–Schwarz

By Heron's formula, the area of the two triangles can be expressed as:

{\displaystyle 16f^{2}=(a+b+c)(a+b-c)(a-b+c)(b+c-a)=(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2})^{2}-2(a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4})}{\displaystyle 16F^{2}=(A+B+C)(A+B-C)(A-B+C)(B+C-A)=(A^{2}+B^{2}+C^{2})^{2}-2(A^{4}+B^{4}+C^{4})}

and then, using Cauchy-Schwarz inequality we have,

{\displaystyle 16Ff+2a^{2}A^{2}+2b^{2}B^{2}+2c^{2}C^{2}}{\displaystyle \leq {\sqrt {16f^{2}+2a^{4}+2b^{4}+2c^{4}}}{\sqrt {16F^{2}+2A^{4}+2B^{4}+2C^{4}}}}{\displaystyle =(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2})(A^{2}+B^{2}+C^{2})}

So,

{\displaystyle 16Ff\leq A^{2}(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2})-2a^{2}A^{2}+B^{2}(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2})-2b^{2}B^{2}+C^{2}(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2})-2c^{2}C^{2}}{\displaystyle =A^{2}(b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2})+B^{2}(a^{2}+c^{2}-b^{2})+C^{2}(a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2})}

and the proposition is proven.

Equality holds if and only if {\displaystyle {\tfrac {a}{A}}={\tfrac {b}{B}}={\tfrac {c}{C}}={\sqrt {\tfrac {f}{F}}}}, that is, the two triangles are similar.

Level justification

Rewrite the two Heron identities, apply the finite four-term Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and read its proportionality equality condition as triangle similarity. School algebra and geometry suffice.
