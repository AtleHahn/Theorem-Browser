Statement

An algebraic number field is a finite extension of \mathbb Q. A central simple K-algebra is a finite-dimensional associative unital K-algebra whose center is K and whose only two-sided ideals are 0 and the whole algebra. For each place v of K, including the archimedean places, write K_v for the completion of K at v.

Let A be a central simple algebra over a number field K, and let d\ge1 be an integer. If A\otimes_K K_v\cong M_d(K_v) as K_v-algebras for every place v, then A\cong M_d(K) as a K-algebra. Here M_d(F) is the algebra of d\times d matrices over F.
