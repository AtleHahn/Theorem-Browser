Statement

Let A\subseteq\mathbb R^n be Borel, n\ge1, and s>0. Define the unnormalized Hausdorff measure by H^s(A)=\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}\inf\left\{\sum_i(\operatorname{diam}U_i)^s:A\subseteq\bigcup_iU_i,\ \operatorname{diam}U_i\le\delta\right\}, where covers are countable. Then H^s(A)>0 if and only if there is a nonnegative Borel measure \mu on \mathbb R^n such that \mu(A)>0 and \mu(B(x,r))\le r^s\quad(x\in\mathbb R^n,\ r>0), where B(x,r) is the open Euclidean ball.
