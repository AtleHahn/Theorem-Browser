Statement

If nonnegative integers m,n each are sums of four integer squares, then so is mn.

Level

Level justification

Proof 1: Products of integer four-square sums are four-square sums — outline

Write m=\sum a_i^2 and n=\sum b_i^2 with integer coordinates. The four-square identity supplies mn=\sum c_i^2 with integer components \begin{aligned}c_1&=a_1b_1-a_2b_2-a_3b_3-a_4b_4,\\c_2&=a_1b_2+a_2b_1+a_3b_4-a_4b_3,\\c_3&=a_1b_3-a_2b_4+a_3b_1+a_4b_2,\\c_4&=a_1b_4+a_2b_3-a_3b_2+a_4b_1.\end{aligned}

Level justification

Substitute integer coordinates into the displayed four-square identity; all four resulting coordinates remain integers. High-school polynomial arithmetic suffices.
