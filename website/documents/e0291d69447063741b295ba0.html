Statement

Let X be a complex manifold and \alpha\in\Omega^{p,q}(X) with integers p,q\ge1. If each point has a neighborhood U and a smooth form \beta\in\Omega^{p-1,q-1}(U) such that \alpha|_U=i\partial\bar\partial\beta, then d\alpha=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Complex manifolds; Differential forms; Dolbeault operators; Local potentials. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Square-zero and anticommuting Dolbeault operators

On each such neighborhood, d\alpha=i(\partial+\bar\partial)(\partial\bar\partial\beta)=0, because \partial^2=\bar\partial^2=0 and \partial\bar\partial=-\bar\partial\partial. Thus d\alpha vanishes locally at every point and hence everywhere.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The cancellation takes place in complex differential forms with Dolbeault operators on a complex manifold. Those structures exceed the baseline even though the square-zero manipulation is immediate.
