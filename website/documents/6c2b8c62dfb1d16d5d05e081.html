Statement

For a sequence of nondegenerate convex unit-sphere triangles with side lengths a_j,b_j,c_j\in(0,\pi) tending to zero and opposite angles A_j,B_j,C_j, \frac{\sin A_j/a_j}{\sin B_j/b_j}\longrightarrow1,\qquad \frac{\sin B_j/b_j}{\sin C_j/c_j}\longrightarrow1.

Proof 1: Replace small arc sines by arc lengths outline

The spherical sine law gives \frac{\sin A_j/a_j}{\sin B_j/b_j}=\frac{\sin a_j/a_j}{\sin b_j/b_j}. Both numerator and denominator on the right tend to 1 by \lim_{t\to0}\sin t/t=1. This proves the first limit; the cyclic comparison proves the second.
