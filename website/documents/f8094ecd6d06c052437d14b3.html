Statement

For every fixed real A>0 there are constants C_A>0 and x_A>1 such that whenever x\ge x_A and x^{1/2}(\log x)^{-A}\le Q\le x^{1/2},

\sum_{1\le q\le Q}\ \sup_{0\le y\le x}\ \max_{\substack{1\le a\le q\\(a,q)=1}}\left|\psi(y;q,a)-\frac y{\varphi(q)}\right|\le C_A x^{1/2}Q(\log x)^5.

Here \varphi(q) is the number of residue classes modulo q coprime to q, \psi(y;q,a)=\sum_{1\le m\le y,\ m\equiv a\pmod q}\Lambda(m), and \Lambda(m)=\log p if m=p^j for a prime p and integer j\ge1, and zero otherwise. The logarithm is natural.
