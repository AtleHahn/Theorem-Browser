Statement

For a,b,x,y\in\mathbb R^3, (a\cdot x)(b\cdot y)-(a\cdot y)(b\cdot x)=(a\times b)\cdot(x\times y).

Proof 1: Identify the three cross-product coordinate terms

Use the Cauchy–Binet formula

\det(AB)=\sum_{|S|=m}\det(A_{[m],S})\det(B_{S,[m]})

for an m\times n matrix A and an n\times m matrix B, with selected indices in increasing order. Put the row vectors a,b in A and the column vectors x,y in B. For m=2,n=3, this gives

\begin{aligned} \begin{vmatrix}a\cdot x&a\cdot y\\b\cdot x&b\cdot y\end{vmatrix} &=\begin{vmatrix}a_2&a_3\\b_2&b_3\end{vmatrix} \begin{vmatrix}x_2&y_2\\x_3&y_3\end{vmatrix} +\begin{vmatrix}a_3&a_1\\b_3&b_1\end{vmatrix} \begin{vmatrix}x_3&y_3\\x_1&y_1\end{vmatrix}\\ &\qquad+\begin{vmatrix}a_1&a_2\\b_1&b_2\end{vmatrix} \begin{vmatrix}x_1&y_1\\x_2&y_2\end{vmatrix}\\ &=(a\times b)\cdot(x\times y). \end{aligned}

The determinant on the left is (a\cdot x)(b\cdot y)-(a\cdot y)(b\cdot x).
