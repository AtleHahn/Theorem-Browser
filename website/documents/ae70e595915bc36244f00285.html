Statement

Let F be a finite-dimensional Hausdorff topological vector space over \mathbb K=\mathbb R or \mathbb C. Every linear functional \ell:F\to\mathbb K is continuous.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Finite-dimensional vector spaces and Hausdorff topology are baseline concepts. A topological vector space requires continuity of addition and scalar multiplication; this single compatibility definition suffices for the finite-dimensional continuity or compact-convex-hull assertion.

Proof 1: Take the scalar field as target — outline

Use the stated theorem that every linear map from a finite-dimensional Hausdorff topological vector space to a Hausdorff topological vector space is continuous. Take the target to be the scalar field \mathbb K; this gives continuity of each linear functional.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Specialize the supplied continuity theorem to the scalar target. Linear functionals are ordinary linear maps to the field, and the finite-dimensional Hausdorff vector topology adds only continuity of the algebraic operations to basic topology; no dual topology is involved.
