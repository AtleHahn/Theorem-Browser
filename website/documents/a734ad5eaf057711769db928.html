Statement

For every real N there is C_N>0 such that, as x\to\infty, uniformly for integers q\ge1 and a with \gcd(a,q)=1 and q\le(\log x)^N,

\psi(x;q,a)=\frac{x}{\varphi(q)}+O_N\!\left(xe^{-C_N\sqrt{\log x}}\right).

Here \psi(x;q,a)=\sum_{1\le n\le x,\ n\equiv a\pmod q}\Lambda(n), where \Lambda(n)=\log p if n=p^k for a prime p and integer k\ge1, and \Lambda(n)=0 otherwise. The totient \varphi(q) counts the integers 1\le r\le q coprime to q. Logarithms are natural. The error bound means that its absolute value is bounded by A_Nxe^{-C_N\sqrt{\log x}} for some A_N and all sufficiently large x, independently of the admissible a,q.
