Statement

A Pythagorean triple means positive integers (a,b,c) with a^2+b^2=c^2; it is primitive if \gcd(a,b,c)=1. If a<b<c, then c-b=1 exactly for (a,b,c)=(2n+1,2n^2+2n,2n^2+2n+1), n\ge1. All these triples are primitive.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement and supplied calculations use integer arithmetic, elementary algebra or Euclidean geometry.

Proof 1: Source argument

When it is the longer non-hypotenuse side and hypotenuse that differ by one, such as in

5^2+12^2=13^2,\qquad 7^2+24^2=25^2,

the source gives the complete solution for the primitive Pythagorean triple as

a=2n+1,\qquad b=2n^2+2n,\qquad c=2n^2+2n+1,

and

(2n+1)^2+(2n^2+2n)^2=(2n^2+2n+1)^2,

where the integer n>0 is the generating parameter. Any triple with c-b=1 is primitive, since any common divisor divides 1.

The source supplies the family and the displayed identity but does not prove that every triple with this difference has that form.

Level justification

Use the displayed polynomial family and the fact that a common divisor of consecutive sides divides one. School algebra and integer divisibility suffice; the source does not establish exhaustiveness of its family.
