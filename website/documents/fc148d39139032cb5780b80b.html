Statement

For a finite CW complex X and a continuous self-map f, define \Lambda_f=\sum_{k\ge0}(-1)^k\operatorname{tr}(f_*:H_k(X;\mathbb Q)\to H_k(X;\mathbb Q)). If f,g:X\to X are homotopic, then \Lambda_f=\Lambda_g.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

CW complexes, homotopy and singular homology are outside calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Homology homotopy invariance and equality of traces

Homotopic maps induce the same maps on singular homology. Thus f_*=g_* in every degree; their traces and hence the alternating sums of traces agree.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply homotopy invariance of singular homology and take traces degree by degree. The trace arithmetic is undergraduate, but the CW and homology framework is substantial algebraic topology.
