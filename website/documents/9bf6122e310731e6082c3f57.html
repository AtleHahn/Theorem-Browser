Statement

Let (X,\mathcal B,\mu) be a probability space, let T:X\to X be measurable and measure preserving, and let f:X\to\mathbb R be integrable. Define

f^*(x)=\sup_{N\ge1}\frac1N\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}f(T^ix).

For every \lambda\in\mathbb R,

\int_{\{x:f^*(x)>\lambda\}} f\,d\mu\ \ge\ \lambda\,\mu(\{x:f^*(x)>\lambda\}).

Measure preserving means \mu(T^{-1}A)=\mu(A) for every A\in\mathcal B, and integrable means measurable with \int_X|f|\,d\mu<\infty. The map T need not be invertible; T^0 is the identity and T^i its i-fold iterate.
