Statement

All rings are unital. A right Goldie ring has finite uniform dimension as a right module over itself and satisfies the ascending chain condition on right annihilators. Every commutative integral domain R is a right Goldie ring.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statements and arguments use advanced noncommutative ring and module theory.

Proof 1: Specialize the right Ore domain fact

Every right Ore domain is a right Goldie domain, and hence so is every commutative integral domain.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The deduction invokes Ore domains and Goldie conditions involving uniform module dimension and annihilator chain conditions. Together these noncommutative ring-theory concepts exceed a small extension of introductory ring theory.
