Statement

All rings are unital. A right Goldie ring has finite uniform dimension as a right module over itself and satisfies the ascending chain condition on right annihilators. Every commutative integral domain R is a right Goldie ring.

Proof 1: Specialize the right Ore domain fact

Every right Ore domain is a right Goldie domain, and hence so is every commutative integral domain.
