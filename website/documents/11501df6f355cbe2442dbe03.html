Statement

Let A and B be commutative Noetherian rings with identity and let A\to B be a unital ring homomorphism. Then A\to B is regular if and only if B, as an A-algebra, is a filtered direct limit of smooth A-algebras.

Here a homomorphism of Noetherian rings is regular if it is flat and has geometrically regular fibers: for every prime ideal \mathfrak p\subset A and every finite field extension k'/\kappa(\mathfrak p), the ring B\otimes_A k' is regular. The residue field is \kappa(\mathfrak p)=\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak p). A Noetherian ring is regular if its local ring at every prime is a regular local ring; a Noetherian local ring (R,\mathfrak m) is regular when \dim R=\dim_{R/\mathfrak m}\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2.

A smooth A-algebra C is finitely presented and formally smooth: for every A-algebra D, every ideal I\subset D with I^2=0, and every A-algebra map C\to D/I, there is a lift C\to D. A filtered direct limit is the colimit of an A-algebra diagram indexed by a filtered category (every finite diagram admits a compatible cocone).
