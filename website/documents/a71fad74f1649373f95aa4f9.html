Statement

The entire function f(z)=\exp(\exp z) has |f(x\pm i\pi/2)|=1 for real x, but is unbounded in the strip |\operatorname{Im}z|<\pi/2.

Proof 1: Compare boundary rays and the real axis

As a concrete example, let us examine the behavior of the holomorphic function {\displaystyle f(z)=\exp(\exp(z))} in the unbounded strip

{\displaystyle S=\left\{z:\Im (z)\in \left(-{\frac {\pi }{2}},{\frac {\pi }{2}}\right)\right\}}.

Although {\displaystyle |f(x\pm \pi i/2)|=1}, so that {\displaystyle |f|} is bounded on boundary {\displaystyle \partial S}, {\displaystyle |f|} grows rapidly without bound when {\displaystyle |z|\to \infty } along the positive real axis.
