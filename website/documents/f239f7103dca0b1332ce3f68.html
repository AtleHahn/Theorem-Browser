Statement

Let V be a finite-dimensional complex vector space and G\subseteq\operatorname{GL}(V) a finite group. Its invariant polynomial ring A=\mathbb C[V]^G is a finite-rank free module over some polynomial subring of A.

Proof 1: Apply the graded Cohen–Macaulay freeness criterion

In general, the ring of invariants of a finite group acting linearly on a complex vector space is Cohen–Macaulay, so it is a finite-rank free module over a polynomial subring. The implication uses homogeneous Noether normalization and the graded Cohen–Macaulay freeness criterion for finitely generated graded algebras.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification
