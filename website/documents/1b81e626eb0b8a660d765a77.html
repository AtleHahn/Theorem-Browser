Statement

Let \Omega\subset\mathbb C^n, n\ge1, be a domain of holomorphy, and put d(z)=\operatorname{dist}(z,\partial\Omega). Then u(z)=-\log d(z) is plurisubharmonic on \Omega. For \Omega=\mathbb C^n, use d=+\infty and u=-\infty. A domain is a connected open set. It is a domain of holomorphy if there are no nonempty open sets U\subset\Omega\cap V, with V\subset\mathbb C^n connected and open and V\not\subset\Omega, such that every holomorphic function on \Omega agrees on U with a holomorphic function on V. A function is plurisubharmonic if it is upper semicontinuous and its restriction to each complex line is subharmonic (allowing the constant -\infty). Subharmonicity means the value at the center of any closed disk in the domain is at most the average on its boundary circle.
