Statement

Any homeomorphism between two simple closed polygonal curves in the plane extends to a homeomorphism between the closures of their bounded interiors.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic topology supplies homeomorphisms and compact sets; the curves are finite polygonal chains in the plane. Piecewise linear means affine on finitely many polygonal pieces, a concrete geometric definition without manifold theory.

Proof 1: Conjugate to a triangle and extend radially — outline

By the piecewise linear Jordan–Schoenflies theorem, for each polygon there is a homeomorphism of a given triangle onto the closure of their interior. These two homeomorphisms and the given boundary homeomorphism yield a single homeomorphism of the boundary of the triangle. By the Alexander trick this homeomorphism can be extended to a homeomorphism of closure of interior of the triangle. Reversing this process this homeomorphism yields a homeomorphism between the closures of the interiors of the polygonal curves.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Conjugate the boundary map to a triangle, apply the explicitly stated radial extension and conjugate back. Undergraduate point-set topology and planar coordinates suffice; no higher-dimensional manifold topology is used.
