Statement

Let R be a commutative unital ring, M a finitely generated R-module, and N a submodule. If M=N+J(R)M, where J(R) is the Jacobson radical, then M=N.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The radical-product and submodule equality are abstract module statements.

Proof 1: Apply radical Nakayama to the quotient

Apply the radical form of Nakayama's lemma to M/N: a finitely generated module Q satisfying J(R)Q=Q is zero. Here Q=M/N is finitely generated and satisfies that equality, so M/N=0 and M=N.
