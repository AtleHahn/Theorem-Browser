Statement

For coprime positive integers r,m, there are infinitely many triples of positive integers (x,y,z) satisfying x^r+y^r=z^m.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The infinitude assertion quantifies over positive integer solutions for two coprime exponents; no general infinite-set machinery is required.

Proof 1: Use the supplied parameter identity — outline

Use the displayed construction

((k^r+1)^s)^r+(k(k^r+1)^s)^r=(k^r+1)^{rs+1}.

For coprime exponents r,m, choose a positive integer s with m\mid rs+1. For each positive integer k, take x=(k^r+1)^s, y=k(k^r+1)^s and z=(k^r+1)^{(rs+1)/m}. The formula gives the asserted solutions as k varies.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Choose a positive solution of the coprime congruence and substitute the displayed integer powers. Increasing k gives distinct positive triples. All steps are integer arithmetic and exponent rules.
