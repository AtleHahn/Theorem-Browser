Statement

Let H\subset\mathbb R^3 be the Alexander horned sphere, and let V be the unbounded component of \mathbb R^3\setminus H. Then V is not homeomorphic to E=\{x\in\mathbb R^3:\|x\|>1\}, the exterior of the closed unit ball.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the stated sphere and its complementary region requires topological embedding background.

Proof 1: Distinguish exteriors by simple connectedness

Use the source-stated facts that the unbounded component of the Alexander horned sphere complement is not simply connected, whereas the exterior E of the unit ball is simply connected. Simple connectedness is preserved by homeomorphisms. Hence these two exterior regions cannot be homeomorphic.
