Statement

Let G be a finite undirected simple graph with n vertices and e edges, where e>7n. Its crossing number \operatorname{cr}(G) is the minimum number of edge crossings among drawings of G in the plane. Then

\operatorname{cr}(G)\geq \frac{e^3}{29n^2}.

High school only
