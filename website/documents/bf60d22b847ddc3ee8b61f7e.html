Statement

For integers 1\le t\le r\le n, the family of all r-subsets of an n-element set containing a fixed t-subset has \binom{n-t}{r-t} members, and every two members intersect in at least t elements.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The fixed-core recipe and its count require only finite choices.

Proof 1: Choose the fixed core and all remaining elements — outline

Designate t elements as common to every member, and take all r-subsets including those designated elements.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Choose the remaining r minus t elements from n minus t available elements. Every pair shares the prescribed core. This is elementary combinations and intersection counting.
