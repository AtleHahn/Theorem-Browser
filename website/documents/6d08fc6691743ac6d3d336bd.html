Statement

Let R=\bigoplus_{d\ge0}R_d be a nonnegatively graded ring, let R_+=\bigoplus_{d>0}R_d, and let M=\bigoplus_{i\in\mathbb Z}M_i be a graded left R-module with M_i=0 for all sufficiently negative i. If R_+M=M, then M=0.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Inspect the least nonzero degree

If M is nonzero, choose the smallest i with M_i nonzero. Every homogeneous contribution to degree i in R_+M has the form R_d M_{i-d} with d>0. Its module factor is zero by the minimality of i. Thus the degree-i part of R_+M is zero, whereas M_i is not. This contradicts R_+M=M, proving M=0.
