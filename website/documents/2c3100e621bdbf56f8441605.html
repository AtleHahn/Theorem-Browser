Statement

Start with the one-letter word a and repeatedly replace every letter simultaneously according to

a\mapsto a1,\qquad1\mapsto10,\qquad0\mapsto00.

The successive words are nested prefixes of an infinite word. Replace its initial a by 0, leaving 0 and 1 unchanged, and index the resulting sequence from 0. Its jth entry is 1 exactly when j=2^m for an integer m\ge0.

Level
High school only

All rewriting rules and the numerical conclusion are explicit; the statement requires only elementary sequences and integer powers.

Proof 1: Iterate a length-two substitution and recode its initial letter (outline)

Use the length-two substitution a\mapsto a1, 1\mapsto10, 0\mapsto00. Its infinite word begins

a11010001\cdots.

Apply the letter-to-letter coding a\mapsto0, 0\mapsto0, 1\mapsto1. This gives

011010001\cdots,

the characteristic sequence of the powers of two.
