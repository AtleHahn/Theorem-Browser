Statement

Let G_0 be a normal real form of a complex semisimple group G, K_0 a maximal compact subgroup, K its complexification, and U a compact real form of G with U\cap G_0=K_0. Let A_+ be the exponential of a closed positive chamber in the noncompact Cartan factor. The double coset spaces K\backslash G/U and K_0\backslash G_0/K_0 have a canonical identification through their common representatives A_+.

Proof 1: Double cosets of a normal real form share a chamber parameter — source argument

Let G_0=K_0P_0 be the Cartan decomposition and let A be a maximal abelian subgroup of P_0. Flensted–Jensen's decomposition gives

G=KA_+U,

where A_+ is the image of the closure of a Weyl chamber in the Lie algebra \mathfrak a of A under the exponential map. Moreover,

K\backslash G/U=A_+.

Since the real Cartan decomposition gives

K_0\backslash G_0/K_0=A_+,

it follows that there is a canonical identification between K\backslash G/U, K_0\backslash G_0/K_0 and A_+. Thus K_0-biinvariant functions on G_0 can be identified with functions on A_+, as can functions on G that are left invariant under K and right invariant under U.

Use two chamber-representative decompositions to identify double cosets. Semisimple Lie groups, normal real forms, Cartan decompositions and Weyl chambers require graduate Lie theory.
