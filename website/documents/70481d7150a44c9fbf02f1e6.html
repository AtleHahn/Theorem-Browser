Statement

Let (M,g) and (N,h) be connected smooth Riemannian manifolds. If a surjective map f:M\to N preserves their Riemannian distances, then f is a smooth diffeomorphism and

h_{f(x)}(df_xv,df_xw)=g_x(v,w)\qquad(x\in M,\ v,w\in T_xM).

A Riemannian metric is a smoothly varying positive-definite inner product on the tangent spaces. Its distance is the infimum of the lengths of piecewise smooth curves joining the two points. A diffeomorphism is a smooth bijection with smooth inverse.
