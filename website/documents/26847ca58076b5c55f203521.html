Statement

Let n\geq1, let E\subset\mathbb R^n be open and let C\subset\mathbb R^n be a nonempty open cone. Suppose a hyperfunction u on E is the common boundary value of holomorphic functions on the wedges E+iC and E-iC near E. Then u is real analytic on E.

Proof 1: Common boundary hyperfunctions of opposite wedges are analytic — outline

The analytic wave front set of a boundary hyperfunction of a holomorphic function on a wedge lies in the dual cone. Thus the wave front set of u lies in both duals of C and -C. In the nonzero cotangent directions these duals have empty intersection. Hence the analytic wave front set of u is empty, which implies that u is analytic.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Constrain the analytic wave-front set to opposite dual cones and invoke the empty-wave-front regularity criterion. Hyperfunctions, microlocal cotangent singularities and holomorphic wedge boundary values require graduate microlocal analysis.
