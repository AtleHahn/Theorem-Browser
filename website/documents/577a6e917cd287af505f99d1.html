Statement

There exist positive integers a,b,c,d such that a^4+b^4+c^4=d^4. Consequently Euler’s proposed lower bound of four summands fails for fourth powers.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement asks whether three positive fourth powers can equal one fourth power.

Proof 1: Frye positive fourth-power witness — outline

The tuple (a,b,c,d)=(95800,217519,414560,422481) supplies 95800^4+217519^4+414560^4=422481^4. Its three positive summands contradict the proposed lower bound of four.

valid when u^2=22030+28849v-56158v^2+36941v^3-31790v^4. Substitute the supplied rational point with v=-31/467, clear denominators and remove common factors. The resulting positive fourth-power equality is

Thus three positive fourth powers sum to a fourth power.
