Statement

The affine halfspaces of \mathbb R^d have VC dimension d+1. Here a finite point set is shattered if each assignment of its points to two classes can be realized by membership and nonmembership in an affine halfspace, and VC dimension is the largest cardinality of a shattered set.

Proof 1: Combine a simplex witness with the Radon obstruction — outline

The d+1 vertices of a regular simplex can be separated by an affine hyperplane for every division into two nonempty classes, so they are shattered (the empty or full class is immediate). For any d+2 points, Radon’s theorem gives two disjoint parts with intersecting convex hulls. Such parts cannot be separated by a hyperplane, so that labelling cannot be realized by a halfspace. The maximum is therefore d+1.
