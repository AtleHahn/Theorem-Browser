Statement

Let m,n\ge0 be integers. If nonempty open subsets U\subseteq\mathbb R^n and V\subseteq\mathbb R^m are homeomorphic, then n=m.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic point-set topology supplies continuity, products, subspaces, closure and homeomorphisms. The full graph, separation or fixed-point assertion uses these familiar definitions on explicitly described spaces, without additional structures.

Proof 1: Compare punctured-pair homology — outline

The dimension comparison follows by applying excision and the long exact homology sequence to the punctured pair (\mathbb R^n,\mathbb R^n\setminus\{x\}), using the deformation retraction of punctured Euclidean space onto a sphere. The corresponding local groups are preserved by the homeomorphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compute local relative homology by excision and the punctured-space sphere retraction, then use invariance under homeomorphisms. Relative homology and its exact sequence require algebraic topology.
