Proof 1: Expand the source angle sums

Using the sum of two angles identity, the product 4\sin\theta\sin(60^\circ+\theta)\sin(120^\circ+\theta) can be shown to be equal to

-4\sin^3\theta+3\sin\theta.

The equation

\sin(3\theta)=-4\sin^3\theta+3\sin\theta

can be verified by applying the sum of two angles identity to the left side twice and eliminating the cosine.

The source prescribes these expansions but does not display their intermediate calculations.
