Statement

Let X_1,X_2,\ldots be independent identically distributed real random variables with \mathbb EX_1=0 and \mathbb EX_1^2<\infty, and set S_n=\sum_{j=1}^nX_j. For a standard real Brownian motion W starting at 0, there are stopping times 0=\tau_0\le\tau_1\le\cdots for its natural filtration such that (W_{\tau_n})_{n\ge1} has the same finite-dimensional joint laws as (S_n)_{n\ge1}, and the increments \tau_n-\tau_{n-1} are independent and identically distributed with

\mathbb E(\tau_n-\tau_{n-1})=\mathbb EX_1^2,\qquad\mathbb E(\tau_n-\tau_{n-1})^2\le4\mathbb EX_1^4.

The fourth-moment bound permits +\infty. Brownian motion has continuous paths and independent centered normal increments of variance equal to elapsed time. Its natural filtration is \mathcal F_t=\sigma(W_s:s\le t), and a stopping time \tau satisfies \{\tau\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t. Finite-dimensional joint laws agree when every finite vector of coordinates has the same distribution.
