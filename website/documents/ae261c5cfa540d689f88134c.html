Proof 1: Apply Wedderburn to the coordinate division ring

A Desarguesian plane can be coordinatized over a division ring. For a finite plane the coordinate division ring is finite. Wedderburn’s little theorem makes every finite division ring commutative, hence a field. The characterization of Pappus by commutativity of coordinates now makes the plane Pappian.

Level

Level justification

The proof links projective-plane incidence theorems with division-ring coordinatization and its commutativity criterion. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally: understanding this geometric/algebraic coordinatization is not confidently a small extension, despite the short finite-ring implication.
