Statement

Let \mathcal A be an abelian category with arbitrary set-indexed coproducts, exact filtered colimits, and a generator G. Exact filtered colimits means that taking a filtered colimit of short exact sequences preserves exactness; generator means that \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal A}(G,-) is faithful. Put R=\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal A}(G), with multiplication given by composition, and let \operatorname{Mod}\!\text{-}R denote the category of right R-modules. Define S:\mathcal A\to\operatorname{Mod}\!\text{-}R by S(X)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal A}(G,X), with right action h\cdot r=h\circ r and action on morphisms by postcomposition. Then S is full and faithful: for every X,Y, the induced map \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal A}(X,Y)\to\operatorname{Hom}_R(SX,SY) is bijective. Moreover, there is an exact functor L:\operatorname{Mod}\!\text{-}R\to\mathcal A left adjoint to S, meaning natural bijections \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal A}(LM,X)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,SX); exact means preservation of short exact sequences.
