Statement

Let \theta\in U\subseteq\mathbb R^d with U open, and let T be a real random-vector estimator with mean \psi(\theta) and finite covariance \Sigma(\theta). Let I(\theta) be a positive-definite Fisher information matrix. Assume the multivariate Cramér–Rao inequality \Sigma\ge\Phi I^{-1}\Phi^T applies, where \Phi=D\psi. If E_\theta T=\theta, then \Sigma(\theta)\ge I(\theta)^{-1}.

Beyond undergraduate level

Proof 1: Substitute the identity mean map

Unbiasedness gives \psi(\theta)=\theta, whose Jacobian is the identity matrix. Substituting into the general bound \Sigma\ge\Phi I^{-1}\Phi^T gives \Sigma\ge I^{-1}.
