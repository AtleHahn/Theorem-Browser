Statement

Let X be a nonsingular complex projective surface. Suppose \omega_1,\omega_2 are linearly independent over \mathbb C global holomorphic one-forms on X and \omega_1\wedge\omega_2=0. Then there exist a nonsingular complex algebraic curve C, a morphism \varphi:X\to C, and holomorphic one-forms \eta_1,\eta_2 on C such that \varphi^*\eta_i=\omega_i for i=1,2. Here the wedge is the alternating exterior product of differential forms, and pullback is defined by (\varphi^*\eta)_x(v)=\eta_{\varphi(x)}(d\varphi_x(v)).
