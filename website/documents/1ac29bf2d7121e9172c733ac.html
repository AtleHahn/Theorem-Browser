Statement

Let F be a field, let A be a finite-dimensional central simple F-algebra, and let B\subseteq A be a simple F-subalgebra with the same identity. Here simple means nonzero with no nonzero proper two-sided ideals, and central means that the center of A is F\,1.

For a subset S of a ring R, write C_R(S)=\{r\in R:rs=sr\text{ for every }s\in S\}.

Then

C_A(C_A(B))=B,\qquad \dim_F B\,\dim_F C_A(B)=\dim_F A.
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although dimension is undergraduate, understanding noncommutative simple and central algebras and their subalgebras is not confidently a small extension of introductory abstract algebra.
