Statement

Every coherent sheaf \mathcal F of \mathcal O_{\mathbb{CP}^1}-modules is isomorphic to a finite direct sum

\mathcal F\cong\bigoplus_{i=1}^{r}\mathcal O(a_i)\ \oplus\ \bigoplus_{j=1}^{s}\mathcal O_{n_jx_j},

where a_i\in\mathbb Z, n_j are positive integers and x_j\in\mathbb{CP}^1. Repeated points and repeated summands are allowed; either sum may be empty. The multisets of integers a_i and pairs (x_j,n_j) are uniquely determined by the isomorphism class of \mathcal F, and conversely determine that class. Here \mathcal O is the sheaf of holomorphic functions, coherence means local finite presentation as an \mathcal O-module, \mathcal O(1) is the dual of the tautological line bundle on \mathbb{CP}^1, and \mathcal O(a) denotes its ath tensor power (using the dual for negative powers). If \mathcal I_x is the ideal sheaf of functions vanishing at x, then \mathcal O_{nx}=\mathcal O/\mathcal I_x^n is the structure sheaf of the length-n fat point supported at x.
