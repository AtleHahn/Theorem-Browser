Statement

Let H be a complex Hilbert space and let T:H\to H be a bounded linear operator. If an integer n>2 satisfies \|T\|<1-2/n, then there exist n unitary operators U_1,\ldots,U_n on H such that

T=\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^nU_j.

Here \|T\|=\sup_{\|x\|\le1}\|Tx\|. A Hilbert space is a complete inner-product space, and a unitary operator is a surjective linear map preserving the inner product.
