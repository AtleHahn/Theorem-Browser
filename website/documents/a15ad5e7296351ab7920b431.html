Statement

In ZF, assume the generalized continuum hypothesis in its full cardinal form: for every infinite cardinal \lambda there is no cardinal \kappa with \lambda<\kappa<2^\lambda. Then the axiom of determinacy AD is false.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses set-theoretic axioms including determinacy for infinite games.

Proof 1: Combine choice from GCH with incompatibility of determinacy

Sierpiński’s theorem gives the axiom of choice from GCH. Since choice is incompatible with AD, GCH entails not-AD.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The inference is elementary, but its premises concern full-cardinal GCH in ZF, choice and determinacy of infinite games. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because understanding these linked foundational assertions exceeds a small extension.
