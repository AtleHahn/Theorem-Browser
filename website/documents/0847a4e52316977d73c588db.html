Statement

Let G,H be Hilbert spaces and T:D(T)\subseteq G\to H a densely defined closed linear operator, possibly unbounded. Then T^*T with domain

D(T^*T)=\{x\in D(T):Tx\in D(T^*)\}

is densely defined in G and self-adjoint: (T^*T)^*=T^*T, including equality of domains. Closed means the graph of T is closed in G\times H. The adjoint domain consists of those y\in H for which there is z\in G with \langle Tx,y\rangle_H=\langle x,z\rangle_G for every x\in D(T), and then T^*y=z. A Hilbert space is a complete inner-product space.
