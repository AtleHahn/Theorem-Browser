Weak operator relative compactness of bounded operator sets

Statement

For a real or complex Hilbert space H, every operator-norm bounded subset of B(H) is relatively compact in the weak operator topology. Here B(H) is the space of bounded linear operators on H, and the weak operator topology is pointwise convergence of the matrix coefficients \langle Tx,y\rangle.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns compact closure in the matrix-coefficient topology on an operator space.

Proof 1: Pass from the ultraweak topology to the weak operator topology

The source identifies the ultraweak topology on B(H) with its weak-star topology as the dual of the trace-class operators. By Banach–Alaoglu, its closed norm balls are ultraweakly compact. The weak operator topology is weaker than the ultraweak topology, so bounded sets are weak-operator relatively compact.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof uses trace-class operators as a predual, the ultraweak topology and comparison with the weak operator topology. These require substantial Hilbert-space operator theory and functional analysis.
