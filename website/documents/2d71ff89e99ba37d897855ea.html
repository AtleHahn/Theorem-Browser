Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle. Choose two distinct points C_A,C_B in the interior of side AB, two distinct points A_B,A_C in the interior of BC, and two distinct points B_C,B_A in the interior of CA. The six points lie on one conic if and only if

\frac{|AC_A|}{|BC_A|}\frac{|AC_B|}{|BC_B|}\frac{|BA_B|}{|CA_B|}\frac{|BA_C|}{|CA_C|}\frac{|CB_C|}{|AB_C|}\frac{|CB_A|}{|AB_A|}=1.

Here |PQ| is the ordinary Euclidean distance. A conic is the locus of a nonzero polynomial of total degree two in Cartesian plane coordinates.

High school only

The finite product and plane quadratic loci are high-school algebra and geometry; no projective proof prerequisites are required.
