Statement

Let R be a ring and \mathcal K a category. Let F,V be covariant functors from \mathcal K to chain complexes of R-modules, zero in negative degrees. Write their differentials as d:F_n\to F_{n-1} and d:V_n\to V_{n-1}, with d^2=0, and put H_n=\ker d_n/\operatorname{im}d_{n+1}. For each n\ge0, suppose F_n has a basis on a collection of models \mathcal M_n: there are elements b_j\in F_n(M_j) with M_j\in\mathcal M_n such that, for each object X, the elements F_n(u)(b_j) indexed by pairs (j,u:M_j\to X) form an R-module basis of F_n(X). Suppose also that

H_k(V(M))=0\qquad(k>0,\ M\in\mathcal M_k\cup\mathcal M_{k+1}).

Every natural transformation \varphi:H_0(F)\to H_0(V) is induced by a natural chain map f:F\to V. If natural chain maps f,g induce transformations \varphi,\psi whose components agree on all models in \mathcal M_0, then f and g are naturally chain homotopic. Thus a lift of \varphi is unique up to natural chain homotopy. Here a natural chain map is a family of natural module homomorphisms f_n:F_n\to V_n commuting with d. A natural chain homotopy is a family of natural homomorphisms h_n:F_n\to V_{n+1} satisfying f-g=dh+hd.
