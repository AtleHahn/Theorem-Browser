Statement

Let u:(M,g)\to(N,h) be a smooth harmonic map of Riemannian manifolds. Write du for its differential and \nabla du for its covariant derivative using the Levi-Civita connections on M and the pullback of TN; harmonic means \operatorname{tr}_g(\nabla du)=0. Use \Delta=\operatorname{div}\operatorname{grad} and curvature convention R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z. Put m=\dim M. At each point, in any g-orthonormal frame e_1,\ldots,e_m,

\frac12\Delta|du|^2=|\nabla du|^2+\sum_{i,j}\operatorname{Ric}_M(e_i,e_j)h(du(e_i),du(e_j))-\sum_{i,j}h(R_N(du(e_i),du(e_j))du(e_j),du(e_i)).

Here |du|^2=\sum_i h(du(e_i),du(e_i)), the squared tensor norm of \nabla du is the sum of squared norms over i,j, and \operatorname{Ric}_M(X,Y)=\sum_i g(R_M(e_i,X)Y,e_i).
