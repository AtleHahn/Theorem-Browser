Statement

Let R be a nonzero ring with identity, not necessarily commutative, such that for every x\in R either x or 1-x is a unit. Every projective left R-module is free. Here projective means isomorphic to a direct summand of a free module, and free means a direct sum of copies of the left module R.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement requires abstract module and ring theory, including arbitrary rather than finite-dimensional free modules.

Proof 1: Reduce to countably generated summands and apply local decomposition

Let N be projective and write it as a direct summand of a free module F. Use the source’s first decomposition lemma: a direct summand of a sum of countably generated modules is again a sum of countably generated modules. Thus N is a direct sum of countably generated summands, each itself a direct summand of F. It suffices to show each is free. For such a countably generated summand, the source’s second-lemma argument applies to the rank-one free summands of F: it decomposes the summand into a countable direct sum of finite sums of those rank-one modules. Their endomorphism rings are the opposite ring of R, which is local whenever R is local. Each finite block is free, and the direct sum of these blocks is free. Taking the sum over the initial countably generated summands proves the theorem.
