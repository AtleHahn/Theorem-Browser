Statement

For four distinct points A,B,C,D on a nondegenerate conic, the four projective intersections AB\cap CD, BC\cap DA, t_A\cap t_C, and t_B\cap t_D are collinear, where t_A is the tangent at A, and similarly for the others.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Projective conics and tangents are quadratic-form and derivative constructions in homogeneous coordinates, a limited extension of undergraduate analytic geometry.

Proof 1: Two coalescing-vertex placements — outline

Use the limiting form of Pascal in which two connected vertices coalesce and their side becomes the tangent. Apply it in the two placements described in the source, represented by the repeated-vertex lists A,A,B,C,C,D and A,B,B,C,D,D. The first gives the two side intersections and t_A\cap t_C on one line; the second gives those same side intersections and t_B\cap t_D on one line. Thus all four lie on the same line.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the explicitly specified tangent version of Pascal in two repeated-vertex orders. Undergraduate analytic geometry and limits suffice with homogeneous projective coordinates as a small extension; no algebraic intersection theory is used.
