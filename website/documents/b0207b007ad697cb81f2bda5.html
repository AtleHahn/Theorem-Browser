Statement

All rings are unital. A ring is semiprime if it has no nonzero nilpotent two-sided ideal. A right Goldie ring has finite uniform dimension as a right module over itself and satisfies the ascending chain condition on right annihilators. Let R be a right order in a semisimple Artinian ring Q: R embeds in Q and Q is its classical right ring of quotients, obtained by inverting regular elements on the right. Then R is semiprime and right Goldie.

Beyond undergraduate level

Proof 1: Combine the two right-order inheritance facts

Use two structural facts: any right order in a right Noetherian ring is right Goldie, and any right order in a right Noetherian semiprime ring is semiprime. The semisimple Artinian ring Q is right Noetherian and semiprime. The first fact makes R right Goldie, and the second makes it semiprime.
