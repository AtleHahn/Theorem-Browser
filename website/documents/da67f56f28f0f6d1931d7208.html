Statement

Let n\geq 0 be an integer, let B=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:\|x\|\leq 1\} be the closed unit ball, and let h:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n be a homeomorphism such that h(B)\subseteq\operatorname{int}B. Then B\setminus h(\operatorname{int}B) is homeomorphic to S^{n-1}\times[0,1], where S^{n-1}=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:\|x\|=1\} (thus S^{-1}=\varnothing when n=0).

Level justification

All objects and the conclusion use standard undergraduate point-set topology in Euclidean spaces. No smooth structures, torus trick, or high-dimensional manifold theory is needed to understand this statement.
