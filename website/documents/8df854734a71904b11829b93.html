Statement

Let F=\{f_1,\ldots,f_m\} and G=\{g_1,\ldots,g_r\} be finite sets of real polynomials in n variables, and put W=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:f_j(x)\ge0\text{ for all }j,\ g_\ell(x)=0\text{ for all }\ell\}. Define

Q=\left\{\sigma_0+\sum_{j=1}^m\sigma_j f_j+\sum_{\ell=1}^r h_\ell g_\ell\right\},

where every \sigma_j is a finite sum of squares of real polynomials and every h_\ell is an arbitrary real polynomial. Suppose there is a real L>0 such that L-\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2\in Q. Then every real polynomial p strictly positive on W belongs to Q.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The full hypothesis and certificate are explicitly algebraic; undergraduate polynomial and inequality reasoning suffices without learning quadratic-module theory.
