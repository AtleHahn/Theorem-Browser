Statement

Let E be a separable Fréchet space over \mathbb R or \mathbb C in which every closed bounded subset is compact (the Fréchet–Montel condition). Then its strong dual E'_\beta is Souslin. The strong dual topology on the continuous dual E' is generated by p_B(\ell)=\sup_{x\in B}|\ell(x)|, where B ranges over bounded subsets of E. Bounded means that for every neighborhood V of zero there is c>0 with B\subset tV for all real t\ge c.

A Fréchet space is a Hausdorff locally convex topological vector space whose topology is induced by a complete translation-invariant metric. Locally convex means that zero has a basis of convex neighborhoods. Write E' for its space of continuous linear functionals. A space is Souslin if it is a continuous image of a Polish space, and Polish means separable and completely metrizable.
