Statement

For a pointed space Y, write \operatorname{SP}(Y) for its infinite symmetric product: finite unordered tuples of points of Y, with basepoint entries ignored, with the usual direct-limit topology of finite symmetric products. For the pointed circle S^1, \pi_1\operatorname{SP}(S^1)\cong\mathbb Z and \pi_n\operatorname{SP}(S^1)=0 for every integer n\geq2.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Use the circle homotopy equivalence

Use the homotopy equivalence \operatorname{SP}(S^1)\simeq S^1. Its homotopy groups are consequently those of the circle.
