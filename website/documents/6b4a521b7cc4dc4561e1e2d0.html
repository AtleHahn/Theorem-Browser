Proof 1: A bounded function on a closed interval need not attain its supremum without continuity — outline

Set f(0)=0 and f(x)=1-x for 0<x\le1. This extends the source nonattainment example to [0,1], where it is discontinuous at zero and still never attains its supremum 1.
