Statement

Let G be a group. A subnormal series of G is a finite chain

\{1\}=G_0\trianglelefteq G_1\trianglelefteq\cdots\trianglelefteq G_r=G,

where each subgroup is normal in the next. Its factor groups are G_i/G_{i-1} for 1\leq i\leq r. A refinement is a subnormal series obtained by inserting subgroups into the chain.

Any two subnormal series of G have equivalent refinements: the refined series can be chosen to have the same number of factors, with a bijection between their factor occurrences such that corresponding quotient groups are isomorphic. The order of the corresponding factors need not agree.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Standard undergraduate group theory suffices; subnormal series, refinements and factor equivalence are all defined.
