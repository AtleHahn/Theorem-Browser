Statement

For positive integers n,m, the natural GL_n(\mathbb C)\times GL_m(\mathbb C) representation on S(\mathbb C^n\otimes\mathbb C^m) is multiplicity-free: each irreducible representation occurs at most once.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns infinite polynomial representations and irreducible multiplicities, beyond finite-dimensional linear algebra.

Proof 1: Read multiplicities from the distinct dual-pair summands

Use the dual-pair decomposition into a direct sum of tensor products of irreducible representations of GL_n(\mathbb C) and GL_m(\mathbb C):

S(\mathbb C^n\otimes\mathbb C^m) =\bigoplus_D\rho_n^D\otimes\rho_m^{D'}.

The summands are indexed by the Young diagrams D, and representations \rho^D are mutually non-isomorphic. And diagram D determines D' and vice versa.

In particular the representation of the big group GL_n(\mathbb C)\times GL_m(\mathbb C) is multiplicity free, that is each irreducible representation occurs only one time. The dual-pair decomposition is used here without proof.
