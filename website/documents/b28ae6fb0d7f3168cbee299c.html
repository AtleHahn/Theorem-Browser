Statement

Let K_1,K_2 be number fields and \overline K_i algebraic closures. If \operatorname{Gal}(\overline K_1/K_1) and \operatorname{Gal}(\overline K_2/K_2) are isomorphic as topological groups, then K_1\cong K_2 as fields.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement compares number fields through their topological absolute Galois groups.

Proof 1: Restrict the supplied algebraic-closure isomorphism

The Neukirch–Uchida theorem supplies a field isomorphism \sigma:\overline K_1\to\overline K_2 inducing the given topological group isomorphism and satisfying \sigma(K_1)=K_2. Its restriction is therefore an isomorphism K_1\to K_2.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Restrict the algebraic-closure isomorphism supplied by Neukirch-Uchida. Absolute Galois groups and their profinite topology are graduate arithmetic prerequisites, despite the elementary final restriction.
