Statement

Let U,V be independent real random variables. If U+V has a normal distribution, then U and V each have a normal distribution. Normal distributions here include constants: a normal variable has the law of m+sZ for some m\in\mathbb R, s\ge0, where Z has density (2\pi)^{-1/2}e^{-x^2/2}.
