Statement

Let X be a complex projective manifold of complex dimension n, and let L be a big nef holomorphic line bundle on X. Then

H^i(X,L\otimes K_X)=0\qquad\text{for every integer }i>0.

Here K_X=\bigwedge^n T_X^* is the canonical line bundle, H^i denotes sheaf cohomology of holomorphic sections, and \otimes is the tensor product of line bundles. Nef means that the restriction of L to every irreducible curve has nonnegative degree. Big means that the section ring \bigoplus_{m\ge0}H^0(X,L^{\otimes m}) has Krull dimension n+1, equivalently that the Iitaka dimension is n. The degree on a curve is the degree of the divisor of a nonzero meromorphic section (on its normalization).
