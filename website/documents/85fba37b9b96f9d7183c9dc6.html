Statement

Fix d\ge1. Every sequence of nonempty compact subsets K_n of a fixed bounded subset of \mathbb R^d has a subsequence converging in Hausdorff distance to a nonempty compact subset K of \mathbb R^d. If all K_n are convex, then K is convex. For nonempty compact sets A,B,

d_H(A,B)=\max\left\{\sup_{a\in A}\inf_{b\in B}\|a-b\|,\ \sup_{b\in B}\inf_{a\in A}\|a-b\|\right\}.

Convex means containing every line segment between two of its points; convergence means d_H(K_{n_j},K)\to0 for a strictly increasing sequence of indices n_j.
