Statement

Let C be a counterclockwise piecewise smooth simple closed curve in \mathbb R^2, bounding the compact region D. Let L,M be real C^1 functions on a neighborhood of D. If M_x-L_y=1 on D, then \operatorname{area}(D)=\oint_C(L\,dx+M\,dy).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Real partial derivatives; Line and double integrals; Oriented planar boundaries. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Substitute a unit scalar curl

Green’s theorem makes the boundary integral equal to \iint_D(M_x-L_y)\,dA=\iint_D1\,dA=\operatorname{area}(D).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Green’s theorem and replace the scalar curl by one, whose area integral is the region’s area. Undergraduate vector calculus suffices.
