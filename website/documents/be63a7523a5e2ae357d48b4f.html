Statement

Let R be a unital semisimple ring, meaning that its right regular module R_R is a direct sum of simple right modules. Then

R\cong\prod_{i=1}^{m}M_{n_i}(D_i),

where m is finite, each n_i is a positive integer, and each D_i is a division ring. Here M_n(D) is the ring of n\times n matrices over D. The factor sizes n_i and division-ring isomorphism classes of D_i are unique up to permutation of the factors. The zero ring is represented by the empty product.

If R is a finite-dimensional semisimple algebra over a field k, the decomposition is by k-algebras, with each D_i a finite-dimensional division k-algebra; uniqueness is then up to k-algebra isomorphism and permutation.
