Statement

For every finite group G, there are infinitely many pairwise nonisomorphic finite simple connected undirected graphs H such that \operatorname{Aut}(H)\cong G. Here a simple graph has no loops or multiple edges, connected means that every two vertices are joined by a path, and \operatorname{Aut}(H) is the group, under composition, of all permutations of its vertices that preserve adjacency in both directions. Two graphs are isomorphic if an adjacency-preserving bijection exists between their vertex sets.

Level justification

Introductory abstract algebra supplies finite groups, permutation groups and group isomorphisms. The graph definitions and adjacency-preserving permutations are given locally; recognizing their composition group and the quantifier over infinitely many nonisomorphic finite graphs is a small extension of that background that fits the approximately 30-minute allowance. No graph constructions or structural graph theory are needed to understand the statement.
