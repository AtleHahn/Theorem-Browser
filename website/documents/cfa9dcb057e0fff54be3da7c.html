Statement

For the Fourier transform convention \widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-ix\xi}\,dx, the Dirac distribution \delta_0 has constant Fourier transform 1. Thus Fourier transforms of arbitrary tempered distributions need not vanish at infinity.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns the Fourier transform of a distribution, not an integrable function.

Proof 1: Dirac delta counterexample (outline)

Use the Dirac delta distribution at zero. Its Fourier transform is a constant and hence does not vanish at infinity.

Level justification

The example is about the Fourier transform on tempered distributions, not an ordinary integral of a function. Schwartz test functions, distributional duality and the extended transform are linked prerequisites beyond introductory analysis.
