Statement

Let n\geq3 be odd and D_{2n} the group of symmetries of a regular n-gon, of order 2n. Its Sylow two-subgroups are the n reflection-generated subgroups of order two; they are conjugate under rotations.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group/ring theory and linear algebra supply the objects. The extra Sylow, normalizer, invariant-polynomial or pseudoreflection condition is explicitly defined in subgroup, coordinate or polynomial terms; no specialist extension or representation theory is required.

Proof 1: Rotation of reflection axes — outline

For n odd, 2=2^1 is the highest power of 2 dividing the order 2n, and thus subgroups of order 2 are Sylow subgroups. These are the groups generated by a reflection, of which there are n, and they are all conjugate under rotations; geometrically the axes of symmetry pass through a vertex and the midpoint of the opposite side.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Identify order-two reflection subgroups and rotate their axes to obtain conjugacy. Introductory groups and geometric polygon symmetries suffice.
