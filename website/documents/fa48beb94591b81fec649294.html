For real x, \cos(3x)=4\cos^3x-3\cos x.

Proof 1: Substitute n=3 in the cosine sum

We use the binomial multiple-angle formula, for real x and nonnegative integers n,

\cos nx=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk(\cos x)^k(\sin x)^{n-k} \cos\frac{(n-k)\pi}{2}.

The final trigonometric function equals one or minus one or zero, thus removing half the entries in the sum. Here is the concrete instance for n=3, using \sin^2x=1-\cos^2x:

\cos 3x=(\cos x)^3+3\cos x((\cos x)^2-1)=4(\cos x)^3-3\cos x.
