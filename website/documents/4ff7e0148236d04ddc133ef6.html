Statement

Let f be a nonzero real polynomial, v(f) its coefficient sign-variation count with zeros ignored, and N_+(f) its number of positive real roots counted with multiplicity. If v(f)\le1, then N_+(f)=v(f).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement is finite polynomial algebra and counting.

Proof 1: Descartes’ zero-root and one-root tests outline

Descartes’ rule states that v(f)-N_+(f) is a nonnegative even integer. In particular, if v(f)\le1, then N_+(f)=v(f).

Level
High school only
Level justification

A nonnegative even deficit from zero or one must be zero when the root count is nonnegative. Apply the supplied sign rule.
