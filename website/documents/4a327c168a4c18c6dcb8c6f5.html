Statement

Let G be a connected complex semisimple group, T\subseteq B a maximal torus in a Borel subgroup, and \lambda an integral weight of T. Write U for the unipotent radical of B. Extend characters of T\cong B/U to B, and let L_\lambda be the homogeneous holomorphic line bundle on G/B associated to the character -\lambda. Use the positive-root and line-bundle convention in which a dominant weight \mu gives H^0(G/B,L_\mu)\cong V_\mu^*, where V_\mu is the irreducible highest-weight representation of G of highest weight \mu.

Let W be the Weyl group, \rho half the sum of positive roots, and \ell(w) the length of w with respect to the simple reflections. Put w*\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho. A weight \mu is dominant when \mu(\alpha^\vee)\geq0 for every simple coroot \alpha^\vee. Exactly one of the following holds:

  • No w\in W makes w*\lambda dominant. Then H^i(G/B,L_\lambda)=0 for every i\geq0.
  • There is a unique w\in W making w*\lambda dominant. Then, as G-modules,H^i(G/B,L_\lambda)=0\quad(i\geq0,\ i\ne\ell(w)),\qquad H^{\ell(w)}(G/B,L_\lambda)\cong V_{w*\lambda}^*.

Here H^i denotes sheaf cohomology of the sheaf of holomorphic sections.
