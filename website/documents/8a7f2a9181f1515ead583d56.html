Statement

Let M\subseteq B(H) be a unital von Neumann algebra on a complex Hilbert space H, and let \Omega be a unit vector such that M\Omega and M^{\prime}\Omega are dense in H and (ab\Omega,\Omega)=(ba\Omega,\Omega) for all a,b\in M. Here M^{\prime} is the commutant and * denotes operator adjoint. The rule J(a\Omega)=a^*\Omega extends uniquely to a conjugate-linear isometry J:H\to H satisfying J^2=I.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The hypotheses are trace-vector conditions on a von Neumann algebra, and the conclusion describes an antiunitary involution.

Proof 1: Trace norm identity and dense isometric extension — outline

The vector \Omega is separating because if a\Omega=0 for a in M, then aM^\prime\Omega=(0), and hence a=0.

It follows from the trace identity that the map

Ja\Omega=a^*\Omega

for a in M defines a conjugate-linear isometry on M\Omega, which extends uniquely to H with square the identity, J^2=I.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The separating-vector argument, trace norm identity and dense extension take place in a von Neumann algebra acting on Hilbert space. Understanding the full setting requires operator algebra and Hilbert-space adjoints beyond the baseline.
