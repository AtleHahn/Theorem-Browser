Statement

Let \mathcal A be an abelian category and

0\longrightarrow A_\bullet\xrightarrow{\alpha}B_\bullet\xrightarrow{\beta}C_\bullet\longrightarrow0

a degreewise short exact sequence of chain complexes in \mathcal A. Their differentials lower degree by one and square to zero; \alpha and \beta commute with those differentials. Write H_n(X_\bullet)=\ker(d_n)/\operatorname{im}(d_{n+1}) for homology. There are connecting morphisms \delta_n:H_n(C_\bullet)\to H_{n-1}(A_\bullet) such that

\cdots\longrightarrow H_n(A_\bullet)\xrightarrow{\alpha_*}H_n(B_\bullet) \xrightarrow{\beta_*}H_n(C_\bullet)\xrightarrow{\delta_n}H_{n-1}(A_\bullet) \xrightarrow{\alpha_*}H_{n-1}(B_\bullet)\longrightarrow\cdots

is exact. The morphisms \alpha_* and \beta_* are induced by the given chain maps.

Proof 1: Categorical snake lemma on cycles and boundary quotients (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Tag0111, explicit diagram, together with the proof at tag010H).

Use the categorical snake-lemma proof with the diagram in Lemma12.13.6. Its upper row consists of Q_n(X)=X_n/\operatorname{im}d_{n+1} and is right exact; its lower row consists of Z_{n-1}(X)=\ker d_{n-1} and is left exact. The differential induces Q_n(X)\to Z_{n-1}(X), whose kernel is H_n(X) and cokernel is H_{n-1}(X). Thus the six-term sequence gives the required connecting map. Repeating this in every degree and identifying the overlapping induced homology maps gives the entire long exact sequence. The construction uses universal properties in the abelian category, so no module embedding or element interpretation is required.
