Statement

Let A be a C*-algebra, and for every pure state f let \pi_f:A\to B(H_f) be its GNS representation. On H=\bigoplus_f H_f, the componentwise map \pi(x)=\bigoplus_f\pi_f(x) is a bounded linear operator with \|\pi(x)\|\le\|x\| for every x\in A.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Pure states and GNS representations of general C*-algebras require substantial operator-algebra foundations. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use the uniform bound on the summand operators (outline)

Each GNS operator \pi_f(x) has norm at most \|x\|. The source takes their Hilbert direct sum; a family with this common norm bound acts boundedly on the direct sum with the same bound.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use contractivity of every pure-state GNS representation and the uniform direct-sum bound. C-star algebras, pure states and GNS representations require substantial graduate operator theory even though the final norm estimate is short.
