Statement

Let q be a prime power and let \mathbb F_q be the field with q elements. A polynomial f\in\mathbb F_q[x] is exceptional if the map x\mapsto f(x) is a bijection of \mathbb F_{q^m} for infinitely many positive integers m.

If f is exceptional and has degree d\ge1, then \gcd(d,q-1)=1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The complete exceptional condition ranges over infinitely many finite-field extensions, a cumulative prerequisite burden beyond introductory algebra.
