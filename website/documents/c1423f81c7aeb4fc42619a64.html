Statement

Let A,B be Hermitian d\times d complex matrices, d\ge1, and let e^A denote the matrix exponential. The number \operatorname{tr}(e^Ae^B) is real.

Proof 1: Move half an exponential around the trace

By cyclicity, \operatorname{tr}(e^Ae^B)=\operatorname{tr}(e^{A/2}e^Be^{A/2}). The sandwiched matrix is Hermitian, so its trace is real.
