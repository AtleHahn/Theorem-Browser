Statement

Let A,B be Hermitian d\times d complex matrices, d\ge1, and let e^A denote the matrix exponential. The number \operatorname{tr}(e^Ae^B) is real.

Level

Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Hermitian matrices; Matrix exponentials; Trace. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Move half an exponential around the trace

By cyclicity, \operatorname{tr}(e^Ae^B)=\operatorname{tr}(e^{A/2}e^Be^{A/2}). The sandwiched matrix is Hermitian, so its trace is real.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use cyclic trace invariance to form a Hermitian sandwich. Undergraduate matrix algebra and the spectral interpretation of Hermitian exponentials suffice.
