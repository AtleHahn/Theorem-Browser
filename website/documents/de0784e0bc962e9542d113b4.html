Statement

Let T be a finite nonabelian simple group, k\ge2, N=T^k, and H=\{(t,\ldots,t):t\in T\}. On the set \Omega of right cosets Hx of H in N, coordinate permutations induce an action of S_k, and simultaneous coordinate automorphisms induce an action of \operatorname{Aut}(T).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies direct products, cosets, automorphisms and generating sets. The coordinate actions or double-coset comparison are direct combinations of these; rank is explicitly defined as minimal generator cardinality.

Proof 1: Preserving the diagonal subgroup

We use the fact that an automorphism of a group preserving a subgroup H induces a permutation Hx\mapsto H\alpha(x) of its right cosets. We can take \{(t_1,\ldots,t_{k-1},1):t_i\in T\} to be a set of coset representatives for H in N.

The group Sk induces automorphisms of N by permuting the entries and fixes the subgroup H and so acts on the set Ω. In fact any automorphism σ of T acts on Ω by taking the coset with representative (t1,...,tk−1, 1) to the coset with representative (t1σ,...,tk−1σ, 1).

Level justification

Coordinate permutations and simultaneous automorphisms preserve the diagonal subgroup, so they induce coset permutations. Direct products, cosets and actions are introductory group theory; simplicity is not used in this deduction.
