Statement

Let M be a connected smooth manifold with universal covering map \pi:N\to M. If N is compact, then \pi_1(M) is finite.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns a universal covering of a connected manifold and its fundamental group.

Mathematical Field
Algebraic topology
Required topics
covering spacesfundamental groupcompactness
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Finite covering argument (outline)

Since N is compact, the covering map is finite. This implies that the fundamental group of M is finite: the fiber of a universal covering is in bijection with that group.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compactness makes the closed discrete fiber finite, and the universal-cover correspondence identifies it with the fundamental group. Covering spaces, universal covers and fundamental groups together exceed basic point-set topology.
