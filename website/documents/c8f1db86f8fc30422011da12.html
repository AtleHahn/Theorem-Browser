Statement

Let (E,\mu) be a measure space and F,G\in L^p(E), where \|h\|_p=(\int_E|h|^p\,d\mu)^{1/p}. For 1\le p\le2, 2^p(\|F\|_p^p+\|G\|_p^p)\ge(\|F+G\|_p+\|F-G\|_p)^p+|\|F+G\|_p-\|F-G\|_p|^p. For 2\le p<\infty the inequality reverses.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Arbitrary measure spaces and Lp functions require measure-theoretic analysis beyond the specified baseline.

Proof 1: Sum-and-difference substitution — outline

Use the first Hanner inequality \|f+g\|_p^p+\|f-g\|_p^p\ge(\|f\|_p+\|g\|_p)^p+|\|f\|_p-\|g\|_p|^p for 1\le p\le2, and its reverse for p\ge2. Substitute F=f+g, G=f-g, so f=(F+G)/2 and g=(F-G)/2. Absolute homogeneity of the norm and multiplication by 2^p give the claimed second inequality in each range.
