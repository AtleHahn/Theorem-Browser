Statement

Let \mathcal O be the ring of integers of a finite extension of \mathbb Q_\ell, with maximal ideal \mathfrak m and residue field k=\mathcal O/\mathfrak m. A continuous representation \rho_f:G_{\mathbb Q}\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathcal O) gives a representation \overline\rho_f:G_{\mathbb Q}\to\mathrm{GL}_2(k) by reduction modulo \mathfrak m.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The full hypothesis involves p-adic local fields, their integer rings and continuous representations of the absolute Galois group of the rationals. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Reduce matrix entries — outline

Reduce the entries of the matrices of \rho_f modulo the maximal ideal of \mathcal O. This defines the mod-\ell representation \overline\rho_f. Compatibility of reduction with matrix products, inverses and continuity gives the representation; these routine checks are implicit.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Reduce matrix entries and compose continuous homomorphisms. The objects remain absolute Galois groups, local number fields and their integral residue rings, requiring advanced arithmetic.
