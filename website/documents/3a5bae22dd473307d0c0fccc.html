Statement

Let X be a topological vector space over \mathbb K\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}, let X^{\#} be its algebraic dual and X' its continuous dual, and let U be a zero neighborhood. Let P=U^{\circ}=U^{\#}=\{f\in X^{\#}:\sup_{u\in U}|f(u)|\le1\} be its polar. Write B_r=\{c\in\mathbb K:|c|\le r\}, T_P=\{R\in[0,\infty)^X:P\subseteq\prod_xB_{R_x}\}, and m_x=\inf\{R_x:R\in T_P\}. Then m\in T_P, \prod_xB_{m_x} is the unique least product of closed centered scalar balls containing P, and P=X^{\#}\cap\prod_xB_{m_x}=X'\cap\prod_xB_{m_x}.

Because U absorbs X, choose r_x>0 with x\in r_xU, taking r_u=1 for u\in U. For f\in P, put u_x=x/r_x\in U. Because f is a positive homogeneous function that satisfies \sup_{u\in U}|f(u)|\leq1,

\frac1{r_x}|f(x)| =\left|\frac1{r_x}f(x)\right| =\left|f\left(\frac1{r_x}x\right)\right| =|f(u_x)| \leq\sup_{u\in U}|f(u)|\leq1.
