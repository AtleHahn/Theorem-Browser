Statement

Let Y\subset\mathbb R^2 be compact with area 1, and let \Lambda be a planar lattice of fundamental area 1. Then Y-Y=\{u-v:u,v\in Y\} contains a nonzero point of \Lambda.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns arbitrary compact plane sets with their measure-theoretic area and lattice differences.

Proof 1: Two points from the compact-set strengthening

By the compact-set strengthening of Blichfeldt's theorem, Y has area 1, so can be translated to cover two points of any lattice whose fundamental region has area 1. Therefore, the set Y-Y of differences of pairs of points in Y contains a nonzero lattice point.

Level justification

Subtract the two lattice points provided by the compact Blichfeldt strengthening. The remaining concern is area for an arbitrary compact set, rather than just polygonal geometry, so retain beyond undergraduate provisionally as in the other measurable-set Blichfeldt applications.
