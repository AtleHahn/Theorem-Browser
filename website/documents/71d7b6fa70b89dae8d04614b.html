Statement

In the projective plane over a commutative field, let A,B,C be distinct points on one line and a,b,c distinct points on another, all six distinct and off the intersection of the two base lines. Set X=Ab\cap aB, Y=Ac\cap aC, Z=Bc\cap bC. Then X,Y,Z are collinear.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Projective planes over a field use homogeneous-coordinate incidence, a limited extension of undergraduate field and linear algebra. This includes nonreal fields and points at infinity.

Proof 1: Affine coordinate proof and projective completion

Use the standard affine/projective Pappus equivalence invoked by the source: an affine plane satisfying the parallel-pair form of Pappus has a projective completion satisfying projective Pappus. The affine form says that for collinear A,B,C and collinear a,b,c, the conditions Ab\parallel aB and Bc\parallel bC imply Ac\parallel aC.

Write g=ABC and h=abc for the distinct affine base lines; take three distinct points on each, off their intersection when present.

Because of parallelism in an affine plane one has to distinguish two cases: g\not\parallel h and g\parallel h. The key for a simple proof is the possibility of introducing a suitable coordinate system.

Case 1: The lines g,h intersect at the point S=g\cap h. In this case coordinates are introduced such that S=(0,0), A=(0,1), c=(1,0). The points B,C have the coordinates B=(0,\gamma), C=(0,\delta), \gamma,\delta\notin\{0,1\}.

From the parallelism of the lines Bc,Cb one gets b=(\delta/\gamma,0), and the parallelism of the lines Ab,Ba yields a=(\delta,0). Hence line Ca has slope -1 and is parallel to line Ac.

Case 2: g\parallel h (little theorem). In this case the coordinates are chosen such that c=(0,0), b=(1,0), A=(0,1), B=(\gamma,1), \gamma\ne0. From the parallelism of cB\parallel bC and Ab\parallel Ba one gets C=(\gamma+1,1) and a=(\gamma+1,0), respectively, and finally the parallelism Ac\parallel Ca.

Thus the affine plane over the field satisfies affine Pappus; applying the stated completion result gives the projective collinearity assertion.
