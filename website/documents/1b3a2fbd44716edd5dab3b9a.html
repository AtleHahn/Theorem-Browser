Statement

Let X be an affine scheme and F a quasi-coherent \mathcal O_X-module sheaf. Then H^p(X,F)=0 for every integer p>0. Conversely, if X is a noetherian scheme and H^1(X,F)=0 for every quasi-coherent \mathcal O_X-module sheaf F, then X is affine.

Affine means isomorphic to \operatorname{Spec}R for a commutative ring R. A sheaf is quasi-coherent if on affine open sets it is associated to modules over their coordinate rings. A noetherian scheme is quasi-compact and locally the spectrum of a noetherian ring. The groups H^p are sheaf cohomology in the Zariski topology.
