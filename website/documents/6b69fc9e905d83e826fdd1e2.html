Statement

For every function f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R, there exists a dense subset D\subseteq\mathbb R such that the restriction f|_D:D\to\mathbb R is continuous in the subspace topology. Explicitly, every nonempty open interval meets D, and for every x\in D and \varepsilon>0 there is \delta>0 such that y\in D and |y-x|<\delta imply |f(y)-f(x)|<\varepsilon.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate real analysis supplies density and continuity relative to a subset; the local epsilon-delta statement removes any confusion with continuity of f at points in the whole real line.
