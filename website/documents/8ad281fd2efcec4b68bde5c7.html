Statement

All rings are unital. A ring is semiprime if it has no nonzero nilpotent two-sided ideal. A right Goldie ring has finite uniform dimension as a right module over itself and satisfies the ascending chain condition on right annihilators. If R is semiprime and right Noetherian, its classical right ring of quotients exists and is semisimple Artinian.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Classical noncommutative quotient rings and semisimple Artinian structure require substantial ring-theoretic preparation. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply Goldie’s theorem to a right Noetherian ring

Use the fact that the ascending chain condition on all right ideals makes every right Noetherian ring right Goldie. Apply the stated Goldie theorem: a semiprime right Goldie ring has a semisimple Artinian classical right ring of quotients. Both premises apply to R, giving the claim.
