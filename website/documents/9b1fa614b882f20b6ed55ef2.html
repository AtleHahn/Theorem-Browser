Statement

Let X be a choice set, T\subseteq\mathbb R^K, and V(t)=\sup_{x\in X}f(x,t). At an interior point t where V and f(x,\cdot) are differentiable, for every x attaining the supremum, \nabla V(t)=\nabla_t f(x,t).

Level justification

Multivariable calculus supplies gradients and suprema, and linear algebra supplies linearity in each argument. The displayed conditions state all additional assumptions without requiring optimization or tensor theory.

Proof 1: Apply the scalar formula coordinatewise

For a multidimensional parameter space T\subseteq\mathbb R^K, the differentiability-point envelope theorem can be applied to partial and directional derivatives of the value function. That theorem says that the derivative of a differentiable value function equals the partial derivative of the objective at each maximizing choice, provided that partial derivative exists. If both the objective function f(x,\cdot) and the value function V are totally differentiable at the interior point t, it implies the envelope formula for their gradients:

\nabla V(t)=\nabla_t f(x,t)

for each x attaining the supremum at t.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the supplied scalar envelope assertion in each coordinate direction and assemble the gradient. Undergraduate multivariable differentiation suffices; total differentiability is assumed.
