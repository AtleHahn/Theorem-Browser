Statement

Fix an integer m\geq1. For a nonzero real homogeneous polynomial F(X,Y,Z) of degree m, let

C_F=\{[X:Y:Z]\in\mathbb{RP}^2:F(X,Y,Z)=0\},

and let c(F) be the number of connected components of C_F. Then

\frac{1-(-1)^m}{2}\leq c(F)\leq\frac{(m-1)(m-2)}2+1.

Conversely, every integer in this interval is c(F) for some such polynomial F.

The real projective plane is the set of nonzero vectors in \mathbb R^3 modulo multiplication by a nonzero real scalar, equipped with the quotient topology. Homogeneous of degree m means that every monomial with nonzero coefficient has total degree m, so the equation defines a subset of projective space. Components are maximal connected subsets in this topology; the empty real zero set has zero components. The assertion concerns the real zero set and does not require nonsingularity or irreducibility.

Level justification

Basic point-set topology and multivariable polynomials suffice. Projective space is explicitly the quotient by nonzero scaling, a small extension of familiar equivalence relations and quotient topology; no genus or algebraic-geometric scheme theory is needed.
