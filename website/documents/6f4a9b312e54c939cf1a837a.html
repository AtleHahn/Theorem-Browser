Proof 1: Apply the prescribed quotient method — outline

All derivatives of circular trigonometric functions can be found from those of \sin x and \cos x by means of the quotient rule. For cosecant use \csc x=1/\sin x, the sine derivative (\sin x)'=\cos x, and the zero derivative of a constant.

The source prescribes this quotient method and lists the formula (\csc x)'=-\csc x\cot x, but does not write out this individual substitution and simplification.
