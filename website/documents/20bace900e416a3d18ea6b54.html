Statement

Let M be a connected compact smooth manifold of dimension d, without boundary; orientability is not assumed. Its orientation bundle o(M) is the flat real line bundle whose transition functions in smooth coordinate charts are the signs of the Jacobian determinants of chart changes.

Let H^k(M;\mathbb R) denote ordinary de Rham cohomology and H^k(M;o(M)) the cohomology of differential forms with values in this flat line bundle, with exterior derivative defined using its locally constant transition functions. Integration of orientation-valued top-degree forms induces an isomorphism

\theta:H^d(M;o(M))\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }\mathbb R.

For every 0\leq k\leq d, wedge product followed by this trace gives a nondegenerate pairing

H^k(M;\mathbb R)\times H^{d-k}(M;o(M))\longrightarrow\mathbb R,\qquad ([\alpha],[\beta])\longmapsto\theta([\alpha\wedge\beta]).

Nondegenerate means that every nonzero class in either factor has a class in the other factor with nonzero pairing.

Proof 1: Hodge star on the orientation cover (external)

A supporting source can be found here (Section 2.3, Corollary 2.3.7 and Theorem 2.3.8, pp.23–25). The deduction below supplies the argument for the stored statement.

The following orientation-cover reduction is supplied here: let pi:Mtilde->M be the orientation double cover with its canonical orientation and deck involution tau. Pull back a Riemannian metric. Ordinary forms identify with tau-invariant forms, orientation-line-valued forms with tau-anti-invariant forms; averaging (1±tau*)/2 proves the same identification on cohomology. Hodge representatives exist uniquely by the standard compact oriented Hodge theorem (componentwise if the cover is disconnected), and preserve these eigenspaces. Since tau reverses orientation, tau* star=-star tau*. Thus star takes a nonzero invariant harmonic k-form to an anti-invariant harmonic (d-k)-form and conversely. The integral downstairs is half the integral upstairs; pairing alpha with star alpha gives half its positive squared L2 norm. Stokes makes the pairing independent of representatives. For k=0, invariant harmonic functions are constants since M is connected. Star therefore makes the twisted top cohomology one-dimensional, with positive volume form giving nonzero integral; theta is an isomorphism. Dimension0 is the one-point case directly.
