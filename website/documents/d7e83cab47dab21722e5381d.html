Statement

Let k>1 be an integer and let M be a k-tape Turing machine solving a problem in time at most f(n) on inputs of length n. For every real constant c>0, there is a k-tape Turing machine N solving the same problem in time at most

\frac{f(n)}{c}+2n+3.

The machine N may have a larger finite tape alphabet and a larger finite state set. The assertion holds for deterministic machines and for nondeterministic machines, preserving the corresponding computation model. For a nondeterministic time bound, every computation branch is bounded and acceptance has its usual existential-branch meaning.
