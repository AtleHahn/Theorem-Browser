Statement

Let n,m\ge1 be integers and x_{ia} (1\le i\le n, 1\le a\le m) commuting complex polynomial variables. On \mathbb C[x_{ia}] put E_{ij}=\sum_{a=1}^m x_{ia}\partial/\partial x_{ja}. Write [P,Q]=PQ-QP for the operator commutator and \delta_{ij} for the Kronecker delta. Put F_{ab}=\sum_{i=1}^n x_{ia}\partial/\partial x_{ib} for 1\le a,b\le m. Then [E_{ij},F_{ab}]=0 for all indices.

Proof 1: Use the two factors of the tensor-product action

The Lie group GL_n\times GL_m acts on the vector space \mathbb C^n\otimes\mathbb C^m in a natural way. Extend this action to its symmetric algebra, identified with \mathbb C[x_{ia}]. One can show that the corresponding action of the Lie algebra \mathfrak{gl}_n\times\mathfrak{gl}_m is given by the differential operators E_{ij} and

E_{ab}^{\mathrm{dual}}=F_{ab}=\sum_{i=1}^n x_{ia}\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{ib}},

respectively. This explains the commutativity of these operators: the two factors of the direct-product Lie algebra commute.
