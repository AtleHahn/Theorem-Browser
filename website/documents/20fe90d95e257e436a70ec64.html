Statement

For a closed oriented smooth n-manifold M, the diagonal normal bundle and Thom isomorphism give a bilinear product H_i(M;\mathbb Z)\otimes H_j(M;\mathbb Z)\to H_{i+j-n}(M;\mathbb Z).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Smooth oriented manifolds; Integral homology; Graded products. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compose cross product, excision and Thom maps

Let V be an open tubular neighbourhood of the diagonal in M\times M, and let \nu M denote the corresponding normal disc bundle, with interior identified with V. All homology groups below have integer coefficients. Consider the maps:

  • H_*M\otimes H_*M\to H_*(M\times M), the homology cross product.
  • H_*(M\times M)\to H_*(M\times M,(M\times M)\setminus V), induced by inclusion of pairs.
  • H_*(M\times M,(M\times M)\setminus V)\to H_*(\nu M,\partial\nu M), the excision identification.
  • H_*(\nu M,\partial\nu M)\to H_{*-n}M, the Thom isomorphism. This map is well-defined as there is a standard identification of the normal vector bundle of the diagonal with TM, which is an oriented bundle, so the Thom isomorphism applies.

Combined, this gives a map

H_iM\otimes H_jM\longrightarrow H_{i+j-n}M,

which is the intersection product.

Level justification
