Statement

Let f be differentiable on an open real interval I, with f'(x)\ne0 throughout I. Then J=f(I) is an open interval and f:I\to J has a differentiable inverse g, with g'(f(x))=1/f'(x).

Proof 1: Global inverse on an interval with nonvanishing derivative — outline

The nonvanishing of the derivative implies that it must be entirely of one sign (positive or negative) according to Darboux's theorem, and therefore the function must be strictly monotone, and thus one-to-one. Continuity and strict monotonicity on the open interval make its image an open interval. The derivative theorem for a continuous strictly monotone inverse gives a differentiable inverse g and

g'(f(x))=\frac1{f'(x)}.
