Statement

Let D\subset\mathbb R^2 be a bounded region with smooth boundary, positively oriented, and let P,Q be smooth near \overline D. Then \int_{\partial D}Pdx+Qdy=\iint_D(\partial_xQ-\partial_yP)\,dxdy.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Partial derivatives; Line and double integrals; Oriented planar regions. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Take the planar component of vector Stokes

Use the stated vector Stokes formula on the surface D in the plane z=0, with upward normal and vector field (P,Q,0). Only the third curl component contributes, so its integrand is \partial_xQ-\partial_yP and its boundary integrand is Pdx+Qdy.
