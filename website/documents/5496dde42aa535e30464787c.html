Statement

Let n\ge1 and A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n} have strictly positive entries. There are diagonal matrices D_1,D_2 with strictly positive diagonal entries such that D_1AD_2 is doubly stochastic: all its row sums and column sums equal 1 and all its entries are nonnegative. The scaling pair is unique up to reciprocal scalar multiplication: if E_1,E_2 is another such pair, then E_1=cD_1 and E_2=c^{-1}D_2 for some real c>0.
