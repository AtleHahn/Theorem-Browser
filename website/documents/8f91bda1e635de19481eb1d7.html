Statement

Let K be a number field other than \mathbb Q or an imaginary quadratic field. Its ring of integers \mathcal O_K has a unit of infinite multiplicative order.

Proof 1: Positive unit rank from Dirichlet’s theorem (outline)

Use Dirichlet’s unit theorem: if r_1 is the number of real embeddings of K and r_2 the number of conjugate pairs of nonreal complex embeddings, then

\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu_K\times\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1},

where \mu_K is the finite group of roots of unity in K. Apart from \mathbb Q and the imaginary quadratic fields, this gives units of infinite order.
