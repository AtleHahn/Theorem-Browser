Statement

Let M be a Riemannian manifold of dimension n\ge2, p\in M, and 0<r<\operatorname{inj}_M(p). For k\in\mathbb R, let N(k) be the complete simply connected n-dimensional space form of constant sectional curvature k. The theorem has two parts:

  • If every sectional curvature of M is at most k, then\lambda_1(B_{N(k)}(r))\le\lambda_1(B_M(p,r)).
  • If \operatorname{Ric}_M(v,v)\ge(n-1)k\,g_M(v,v) for every tangent vector v, then\lambda_1(B_M(p,r))\le\lambda_1(B_{N(k)}(r)).

Here g_M is the Riemannian metric, sectional curvature is the curvature of a tangent two-plane, and \operatorname{Ric}_M is the Ricci tensor (the trace of the curvature tensor). Write B_M(p,r)=\{x:d_M(p,x)<r\} for the geodesic ball.

For an open set D in a Riemannian manifold, use the variational Dirichlet bottom

\lambda_1(D)=\inf_{0\ne f\in C_c^\infty(D)}\frac{\int_D|\nabla f|^2\,dV}{\int_D|f|^2\,dV}.

Here dV is Riemannian volume and C_c^\infty(D) consists of smooth real functions with compact support in D. On the regular bounded balls at issue this is the first Dirichlet eigenvalue of -\Delta, with \Delta the Laplace–Beltrami operator. This variational convention also applies to the whole model sphere, where the bottom is zero. The injectivity radius at p is the supremum of radii for which the exponential map at p is defined and gives normal coordinates on the corresponding geodesic ball. The model N(k) is the complete simply connected n-dimensional space form of constant sectional curvature k; B_{N(k)}(r) is an open geodesic ball of radius r about any model point.
