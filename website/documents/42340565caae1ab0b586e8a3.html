Statement

Let X be a nonzero finite-dimensional real or complex normed space and Y\subsetneq X a closed linear subspace. Write d(u,Y)=\inf_{y\in Y}\|u-y\|. Some unit vector u satisfies d(u,Y)=1.

Proof 1: Attain the limiting distance by compactness — outline

The closed unit ball B is compact, and the continuous function d(\cdot,Y) has compact image on B. Riesz’s lemma supplies distances arbitrarily close to one; compactness therefore gives attainment of one.

Level justification

Apply the supplied concrete distance lemma to approach one and compactness of the finite-dimensional unit ball to attain the limit. Distance is continuous, and a point of distance one in the ball must have norm one. Standard metric analysis and linear algebra suffice.
