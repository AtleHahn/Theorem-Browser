Statement

Let X be a Hausdorff locally convex real or complex topological vector space with continuous linear dual X^*, and let \mathcal A be a sigma-algebra on a set S. Let \mu:\mathcal A\to X be finitely additive. If for every f\in X^* and every pairwise disjoint sequence (E_n) in \mathcal A,

f\!\left(\mu\!\left(\bigcup_{n\ge1}E_n\right)\right)=\sum_{n\ge1}f(\mu(E_n)),

then

\sum_{n=1}^N\mu(E_n)\longrightarrow\mu\!\left(\bigcup_{n\ge1}E_n\right)

in the original topology of X. A sigma-algebra is closed under complements and countable unions. Finite additivity means \mu(\varnothing)=0 and \mu(E\cup F)=\mu(E)+\mu(F) for disjoint sets. Local convexity means that zero has a base of convex neighborhoods; X^* consists of continuous linear scalar-valued maps.
