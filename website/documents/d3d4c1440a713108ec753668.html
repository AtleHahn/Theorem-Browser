Proof 1: Derivative of the natural logarithm

y=e^x (for real x) has inverse x=\ln y (for positive y). By the inverse derivative rule,

\frac{dy}{dx}=e^x;\qquad \frac{dx}{dy}=\frac1y=e^{-x},\frac{dy}{dx}\,\frac{dx}{dy}=e^xe^{-x}=1.
