Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer, \mu Lebesgue measure on \mathbb R^n, A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,b\in B\} and tA=\{ta:a\in A\}. For nonempty compact A,B and 0<\lambda<1, \mu(\lambda A+(1-\lambda)B)\ge\mu(A)^\lambda\mu(B)^{1-\lambda}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Topology supplies nonempty compact sets, and the statement defines scaling and Minkowski addition. Lebesgue volume on these sets can be understood through box approximation; taking positive real powers adds no new structure. These are small extensions of multivariable volume and real analysis within about 30 minutes, without learning general measure integration.

Proof 1: Apply additive Brunn–Minkowski and weighted AM–GM outline

Use the additive Brunn–Minkowski inequality for compact sets. Apply the Brunn–Minkowski inequality to sets {\textstyle \lambda A} and {\textstyle (1-\lambda )B}, which gives

{\textstyle \mu (\lambda A+(1-\lambda )B)\geq (\mu (\lambda A)^{1/n}+\mu ((1-\lambda )B)^{1/n})^{n}=(\lambda \mu (A)^{1/n}+(1-\lambda )\mu (B)^{1/n})^{n},}

where we used the fact that {\textstyle \mu (\cdot )^{1/n}} is homogeneous. Using now the inequality {\textstyle \lambda x+(1-\lambda )y\geq x^{\lambda }y^{1-\lambda }}, which holds for all {\textstyle x,y\geq 0,\lambda \in [0,1]} (coming from concavity of the logarithm {\textstyle \log(\lambda x+(1-\lambda )y)\geq \lambda \log(x)+(1-\lambda )\log(y)}), we find that {\textstyle (\lambda \mu (A)^{1/n}+(1-\lambda )\mu (B)^{1/n})^{n}\geq \mu (A)^{\lambda }\mu (B)^{1-\lambda }}, concluding the proof.

Proof 2: Apply Prékopa–Leindler to indicator functions outline

Use the Prékopa–Leindler inequality: for 0<\lambda<1 and nonnegative measurable functions f,g,h on \mathbb R^n, if h(\lambda x+(1-\lambda)y)\ge f(x)^\lambda g(y)^{1-\lambda} for every x,y, then \int h\ge(\int f)^\lambda(\int g)^{1-\lambda}. Apply this to f=1_A, g=1_B, h=1_{\lambda A+(1-\lambda)B}, where 1_E is the indicator function of E. This gives \mu(\lambda A+(1-\lambda)B)\ge\mu(A)^\lambda\mu(B)^{1-\lambda}.
