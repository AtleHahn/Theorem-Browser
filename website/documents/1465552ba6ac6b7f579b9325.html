Statement

Let k be a positive integer, and let \mathbb N_{>0}=N_1\cup\cdots\cup N_k be a partition of the positive integers into pairwise disjoint sets. For every positive integer m, there exist a set S\subset\mathbb N_{>0} with exactly m distinct elements and an index i\in\{1,\ldots,k\} such that

\sum_{s\in T}s\in N_i\qquad\text{for every nonempty subset }T\subseteq S.

Thus all nonempty subset sums of S, including its individual elements, belong to the same part of the partition. The set S and the index i may depend on m and on the partition.
