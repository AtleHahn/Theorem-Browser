Statement

Let M be a compact two-dimensional Riemannian manifold with smooth boundary, possibly empty. Let K be its Gaussian curvature, dA its Riemannian area measure and ds arc length on the boundary. Then

\int_M K\,dA+\int_{\partial M}k_g\,ds=2\pi\chi(M).

Here \chi(M) is the Euler characteristic, and k_g is the signed geodesic curvature measured toward the interior of M: on a smooth boundary arc with unit tangent T and inward unit conormal \nu, it is k_g=\langle\nabla_TT,\nu\rangle. The vector \nu is tangent to M and perpendicular to its boundary; \nabla is the Levi-Civita covariant derivative. This convention does not require orientability of M.

For a piecewise smooth boundary with finitely many corners of interior angles \theta_j, the formula is

\int_M K\,dA+\sum_{\text{smooth boundary arcs}}\int k_g\,ds+\sum_j(\pi-\theta_j)=2\pi\chi(M).

For empty boundary the boundary and corner contributions are zero.
