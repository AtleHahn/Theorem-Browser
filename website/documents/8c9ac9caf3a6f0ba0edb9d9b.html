Model completeness from Gabrielov’s existential normal form

Statement

Let \mathbb R_{\mathrm{an}} denote the real ordered field with restricted analytic functions, in its language L_{\mathrm{an}}. Assume Gabrielov’s normal-form theorem for this structure: every first-order formula is equivalent modulo \operatorname{Th}(\mathbb R_{\mathrm{an}}) to an existential formula. Then this theory is model complete: every embedding between its models is elementary.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns existential formulas and elementary embeddings for the restricted analytic expansion of the real field.

Proof 1: Existential-form criterion

Gabrielov’s theorem gives an existential equivalent for every formula in L_{\mathrm{an}}. By the model-completeness criterion that such existential equivalents for all formulas imply model completeness, the theory of the real ordered field with restricted analytic functions is model complete.

Level justification

Apply the existential-form model-completeness criterion in the theory of the restricted analytic field. Models, elementary embeddings and formula equivalence modulo a theory require substantial model theory.
