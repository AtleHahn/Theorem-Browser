Statement

Let U and V be n×k and k×n matrices over a unital ring. If I+UV and I+VU are invertible, then (I+UV)^{-1}U=U(I+VU)^{-1}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Matrix multiplication, inverses, determinants and polynomial evaluation are familiar linear algebra. Working over a unital ring retains these operations; the statements explicitly impose all needed invertibility or commutativity conditions.

Proof 1: Multiply the intertwining equality by inverses

The identity follows from

{\displaystyle U(I+VU)=(I+UV)U}

after multiplying by {\displaystyle (I+VU)^{-1}} on the right and by {\displaystyle (I+UV)^{-1}} on the left.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Expand the intertwining equality and multiply by the two inverses on the specified sides. Undergraduate matrix algebra over a ring suffices.
