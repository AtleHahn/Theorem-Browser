Statement

Let N,d\ge1 be integers and let x_1,\ldots,x_N\in\mathbb R^d be in homogeneous general position: every subset of at most d of these vectors is linearly independent. A binary labeling \varepsilon_i\in\{-1,1\} is homogeneously linearly separable if some w\in\mathbb R^d has \varepsilon_i(w\cdot x_i)>0 for every i. The number of such labelings is

C(N,d)=2\sum_{k=0}^{d-1}\binom{N-1}{k},

where a binomial coefficient is zero when k>N-1.
