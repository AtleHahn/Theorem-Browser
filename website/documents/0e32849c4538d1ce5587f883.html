Statement

Let f:X\to S be a proper morphism of noetherian schemes, let \mathcal F be a coherent \mathcal O_X-module, and let \mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_S be the ideal sheaf of a closed subscheme S_0. Put \mathcal J=\mathcal I\mathcal O_X and

\mathcal F_k=\mathcal F/\mathcal J^{k+1}\mathcal F\qquad(k\geq0).

Let \widehat S denote the formal completion of S along S_0, with structure sheaf \varprojlim_k\mathcal O_S/\mathcal I^{k+1} on the underlying space S_0. For each integer p\geq0, the canonical map

\widehat{R^p f_*\mathcal F}=\varprojlim_{k\geq0}\frac{R^p f_*\mathcal F}{\mathcal I^{k+1}R^p f_*\mathcal F}\ \longrightarrow\ \varprojlim_{k\geq0}R^p f_*\mathcal F_k

is an isomorphism of topological \mathcal O_{\widehat S}-modules. The topology on each inverse limit is the inverse-limit topology of its discrete quotient sheaves. The canonical map is induced by the quotient maps \mathcal F\to\mathcal F_k and passage to the limit.

Here R^p f_* is the pth right-derived functor of the direct-image functor on sheaves of modules. A proper morphism is separated, of finite type and universally closed; coherence means local finite presentation over the noetherian structure sheaf.
