Statement

Let G=(V,E) be a finite simple undirected graph on n vertices, with no three distinct vertices pairwise adjacent. Let d(x) count the neighbors of vertex x. Then \sum_{x\in V}d(x)^2\le n|E|.

Proof 1: Sum disjoint-neighborhood bounds over edges

Consider any two adjacent vertices {\displaystyle x} and {\displaystyle y} in the graph with degrees {\displaystyle d(x)} and {\displaystyle d(y)} respectively. Since the graph is triangle-free, the neighbourhoods of {\displaystyle x} and {\displaystyle y} are disjoint, which gives {\displaystyle d(x)+d(y)\leq n}. Summing this inequality over all the edges gives the following.

{\displaystyle \sum _{xy\in E(G)}(d(x)+d(y))\leq \sum _{xy\in E(G)}n}

We then sum over vertices instead of edges.

{\displaystyle \sum _{x\in V(G)}d(x)^{2}\leq n|E|}
