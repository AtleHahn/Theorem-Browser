Statement

If a group of prime order p acts on a set X, each orbit has size 1 or p.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies cyclic groups, actions, products, normal subgroups and commutators. Perfect means generated by commutators, and the counted fixed points/tuples are explicit; no character theory is required.

Proof 1: Orbit–stabilizer and prime divisors

For an action of a group H of prime order p, orbit–stabilizer makes every orbit size a divisor of p, hence either 1 or p.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use orbit-stabilizer to make the orbit size a divisor of the prime group order. Undergraduate group actions suffice.
