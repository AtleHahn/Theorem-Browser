Statement

Let \mathfrak g be a complex simple Lie algebra of rank r. Then Z(U(\mathfrak g)) is isomorphic to a polynomial algebra in r variables.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Universal enveloping algebras and the rank and center of a simple Lie algebra require substantial Lie theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Polynomial center of a simple Lie algebra enveloping algebra

Let \mathfrak h be a Cartan subalgebra and W its Weyl group; S(\mathfrak h) denotes its symmetric algebra. The Weyl-invariant symmetric algebra S(\mathfrak h)^W is a polynomial algebra in r variables by the cited reflection-group invariant theorem. Harish-Chandra identifies Z(U(\mathfrak g)) with this algebra, giving the assertion.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Identify the enveloping-algebra center with Weyl-invariant symmetric polynomials via Harish-Chandra. Cartan subalgebras, Weyl groups, enveloping algebras and invariant theory require graduate Lie theory.
