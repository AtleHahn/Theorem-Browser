Statement

Let (M,\omega) be a smooth symplectic manifold and let k\geq1 be odd. Suppose \alpha is a smooth differential k-form on M such that

\mathcal L_{X_H}\alpha=0\qquad\text{for every smooth function }H:M\to\mathbb R.

Then \alpha=0.

Here a differential k-form is a smooth field of alternating k-linear forms on tangent spaces. A symplectic form is a differential 2-form \omega with exterior derivative d\omega=0 and with nondegenerate pairing at each point: \omega_p(v,w)=0 for all w\in T_pM implies v=0. The Hamiltonian vector field X_H is determined by \omega(X_H,\cdot)=dH. The Lie derivative \mathcal L_{X_H}\alpha is the derivative at time zero of the pullback of \alpha by the local flow of X_H. Thus the hypothesis is invariance under every Hamiltonian local flow. Connectedness of M is not assumed.
