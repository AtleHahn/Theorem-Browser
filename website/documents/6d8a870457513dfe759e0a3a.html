Statement

The power series

f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}z^{n!}

converges for |z|<1 and has a nonisolated singularity at z=1. Here a boundary point is singular if the holomorphic function on the unit disk has no holomorphic extension to a neighborhood of that point agreeing on the overlap. Nonisolated means that other singular boundary points occur arbitrarily close to 1.
