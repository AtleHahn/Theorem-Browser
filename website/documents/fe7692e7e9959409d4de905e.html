Statement

All smooth immersions S^2\to\mathbb R^3 are regularly homotopic. In particular, the embedded two-sphere admits eversion.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns equivalence through paths of smooth immersions and reversal of an embedded sphere.

Proof 1: Apply Smale’s classification and the covering calculation

Smale’s immersion classification identifies these regular homotopy classes with \pi_2(V_2(\mathbb R^3)). Since V_2(\mathbb R^3)\cong SO(3) and \pi_2(SO(3))\cong\pi_2(\mathbb RP^3)\cong\pi_2(S^3)=0, there is only one class. The original and reversed parametrizations of the embedded sphere therefore belong to the same class.
