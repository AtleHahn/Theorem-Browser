Statement

Let n,m\ge1 be integers, and let a_1\ge\cdots\ge a_n\ge0 and b_1,\ldots,b_m\ge0 be integers. There exists a simple bipartite graph with labeled vertex parts \{u_1,\ldots,u_n\} and \{v_1,\ldots,v_m\} in which u_i has degree a_i and v_j has degree b_j if and only if

\sum_{i=1}^n a_i=\sum_{j=1}^m b_j, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^k a_i\le\sum_{j=1}^m\min(b_j,k)\quad(1\le k\le n).

A simple bipartite graph permits at most one edge between each pair of vertices in opposite parts and no edges within a part; the degree counts incident edges. The sequence (b_j) need not be sorted.
