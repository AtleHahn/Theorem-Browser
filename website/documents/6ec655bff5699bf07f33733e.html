Statement

Fix integers k\ge1 and n\ge0. In n independent tosses of a fair coin, let p(n,k) be the probability that no k consecutive tosses are heads. Define the normalized k-step Fibonacci sequence by

F_j^{(k)}=0\ (j\le0),\qquad F_1^{(k)}=1,\qquad F_j^{(k)}=\sum_{r=1}^kF_{j-r}^{(k)}\ (j\ge2).

Then

p(n,k)=\frac{F_{n+2}^{(k)}}{2^n}.

For example this normalization gives 0,1,1,2,4,7,\ldots for k=3; the initial values are part of the definition and specify the indexing in the formula. The same probability applies to avoiding k consecutive tails.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Finite coin outcomes, powers of two and an explicit sum recurrence require only elementary reasoning.
