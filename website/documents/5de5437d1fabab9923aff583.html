Every irreducible complex representation of a finite group is finite-dimensional.

Proof 1: Embed in the regular representation

The finiteness of the dimension follows from the fact that any irreducible representation of a finite group G is contained in the regular representation. The regular representation has dimension |G|.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof embeds an arbitrary irreducible representation in the regular representation. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because irreducibility, regular action and representation embedding together require representation-theoretic background beyond the confidently small allowance.
