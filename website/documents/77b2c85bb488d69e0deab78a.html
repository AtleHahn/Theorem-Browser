Statement

Let f be holomorphic on D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\} and suppose f(z)\notin\{0,1\} for every z\in D. Then for all z\in D,

\log|f(z)|\le\frac{1+|z|}{1-|z|}\bigl(7+\max\{0,\log|f(0)|\}\bigr).

Here \log is the natural logarithm, and holomorphic means complex differentiable at every point of the open disk.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex differentiability is a single local extension of undergraduate derivative limits; the explicit bound otherwise uses elementary complex modulus and real logarithms.
