Statement

Independently draw with replacement from n\geq1 coupon types, each with probability 1/n, and let T be the number of draws until every type has appeared. Here n is an integer. Write H_m=\sum_{j=1}^m1/j, H_m^{(r)}=\sum_{j=1}^m j^{-r} and H_0=0. For c>0, \Pr(|T-nH_n|\geq cn)\leq\pi^2/(6c^2).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement compares the collection time with an explicitly given finite harmonic threshold.

Proof 1: Insert the expectation and variance into Chebyshev

Chebyshev’s inequality gives \Pr(|T-\mathbb ET|\geq cn)\leq\operatorname{Var}(T)/(c^2n^2). Substituting \mathbb ET=nH_n and \operatorname{Var}(T)<\pi^2n^2/6 yields the asserted bound.
