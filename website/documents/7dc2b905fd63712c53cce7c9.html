Statement

On a nonzero finite-dimensional vector space V over a field K, the scalar algebra K I_V is nilpotent as a Lie algebra but is not simultaneously strictly upper triangularizable.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Scalar endomorphisms over a field and strict triangularization are linear algebra; the commutator and lower central series are a small additional definition needed to interpret Lie nilpotence here.

Level

Level justification

For scalar matrices the commutator vanishes, so the lower central series terminates immediately, whereas the identity cannot be strictly triangular in any basis. Undergraduate matrix algebra suffices after learning the commutator and terminating lower-central-series definitions; no Lie structure theory is used in this brief counterexample.
