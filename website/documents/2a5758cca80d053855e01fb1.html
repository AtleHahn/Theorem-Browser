Statement

For an M/M/1 queue in stationary steady state with a Poisson arrival process of rate \lambda>0, the departure process is a Poisson process of rate \lambda.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses queueing theory and continuous-time stochastic processes.

Proof 1: Identify departures with reversed arrivals outline

Consider the reversed stationary process. The M/M/1 queue is a reversible stochastic process: by Kolmogorov’s reversibility criterion, the stationary birth–death chain is reversible. The arrival instants in the forward chain are the departure instants in the reversed chain. Thus the departure process is a Poisson process of rate \lambda.

Level justification

The proof uses a stationary continuous-time birth–death chain, reversibility and identification of arrival/departure point processes under time reversal. These are substantial stochastic-process prerequisites beyond elementary finite probability.
