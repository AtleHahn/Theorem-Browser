Statement

Let G be a connected semisimple real Lie group with finite center and \pi a discrete series representation. Its distribution character is represented by a locally integrable function on G with respect to Haar measure.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Semisimple Lie groups; Discrete series; Distribution characters; Haar measure; Local integrability. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply Harish-Chandra regularity

Harish-Chandra’s regularity theorem states that invariant eigendistributions on a connected semisimple group are represented by locally integrable functions. The distribution character of a discrete series representation is such an invariant eigendistribution, so the theorem gives the conclusion.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The objects are semisimple Lie groups, discrete-series representations, distribution characters and invariant eigendistributions. Their identification and the regularity theorem require substantial representation theory and distribution theory.
