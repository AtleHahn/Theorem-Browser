Statement

Let \mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}, let H^2(\mathbb D) be the Hardy Hilbert space with Szegő kernel k_z(\zeta)=(1-\zeta\overline z)^{-1}. Let z_1,\ldots,z_n,w_1,\ldots,w_n\in\mathbb D, n\ge1, and S=\operatorname{span}\{k_{z_1},\ldots,k_{z_n}\}. Suppose T:S\to S is a linear operator satisfying Tk_{z_j}=\overline{w_j}k_{z_j}. If the Pick matrix [(1-w_i\overline{w_j})/(1-z_i\overline{z_j})]_{i,j=1}^n is positive semidefinite, then \|T\|\le1.

Proof 1: Check the positive defect using the Pick matrix — outline

Using the Szegő kernel and the prescribed values of T on its spanning kernel vectors, positive semidefiniteness of the Pick matrix gives positivity of I-T^*T. Hence \|T\|\le1.

Expand the operator defect using reproducing-kernel inner products, then interpret its positivity as contraction. The full Hardy-space and Szego-kernel framework is not defined locally beyond the kernel formula and exceeds a small undergraduate extension; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
