Statement

If G is a connected Lie group, K a closed subgroup, and G/K is simply connected, then K is connected.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement connects quotient simple connectedness with subgroup connectedness.

Mathematical Field
Topological groups, Lie groupsAlgebraic topology
Required topics

Review notes
none

Proof 1: Homotopy exact-sequence deduction

Apply the long exact homotopy sequence of the fibration K\to G\to G/K. The vanishing of the fundamental group of G/K and the connectedness of G force the component set of K to be trivial.
