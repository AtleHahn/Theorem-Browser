Statement

Let P be a convex polygon with n\ge3 vertices v_1,\ldots,v_n and a finite triangulation meeting face to face. Label v_i by i and all triangulation vertices by labels 1,\ldots,n. On each boundary edge v_i v_{i+1} use only its two endpoint labels, with v_{n+1}=v_1. Then at least n-2 small triangles have three distinct labels.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses only elementary planar geometry and finite label counting.

Proof 1: Substitute the dimension in the polytopal lower bound

Apply the polytopal Sperner theorem, whose lower bound for a convex d-polytope with N original vertices is N-d fully labeled d-simplices under the face-label condition. For the polygon, d=2 and N=n. Fully labeled simplices are triangles with three distinct labels, so the bound becomes n-2.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply only the explicitly stated two-dimensional case of the polytopal labeling bound and subtract two from the vertex count. School finite polygon triangulation and labeling suffice; higher-dimensional topology is not used.
