Statement

Let m\geq2, let \lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m be distinct real numbers, and let u_1,\ldots,u_m\in\mathbb C. Set D(x)=\min_{k\in\mathbb Z}|x-k|. For each r, suppose the finite set

E_r=\{D(\lambda_r-\lambda_s):1\leq s\leq m,\ D(\lambda_r-\lambda_s)>0\}

is nonempty, and choose 0<\tau_r\leq\min E_r. Then

\left|\sum_{\substack{1\leq r,s\leq m\\r\ne s}}\frac{u_r\overline{u_s}}{\lambda_r-\lambda_s}\right|\leq\frac{3\pi}{2}\sum_{r=1}^m\frac{|u_r|^2}{\tau_r}.

Here the bar denotes complex conjugation, |u| complex modulus, and D distance to the nearest integer. The sum is over ordered pairs. Integral node differences are omitted only when forming the positive-distance sets, not from the displayed sum.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Finite weighted sums, rounding distances and complex arithmetic are explicit elementary notions. No operator-theoretic proof prerequisites enter.
