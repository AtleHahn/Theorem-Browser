Jacobi symbol one need not mean a quadratic residue

Statement

The Jacobi symbol (2/15) is 1, but 2 is not a square modulo 15. Here the Jacobi symbol (a/n) for positive odd n=\prod p_j is \prod(a/p_j), with multiplicities, using Legendre symbols at the odd prime factors.

Proof 1: Compare the Jacobi supplement with reduction modulo five

The Jacobi supplement gives (2/15)=(-1)^{(15^2-1)/8}=1. But 2 is not a quadratic residue modulo 5, so it cannot be a square modulo 15.

Level justification

Evaluate the supplied Jacobi supplement and compare the squares modulo five. Undergraduate elementary number theory suffices with Jacobi and Legendre symbols as a small extension of modular arithmetic.
