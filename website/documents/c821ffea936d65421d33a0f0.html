Statement

For a positive integer n, let \Lambda(n)=\log p if n=p^j for a prime p and j\geq1, and 0 otherwise. Let \mu(1)=1, let \mu(n)=0 when a prime square divides n, and otherwise let \mu(n)=(-1)^k when n is a product of k distinct primes. Fix real cutoffs U,V\geq2. Define

a_1(n)=\begin{cases}\Lambda(n)&n\leq U,\\0&n>U,\end{cases}\qquad a_2(n)=-\sum_{mdr=n,\ m\leq U,\ d\leq V}\Lambda(m)\mu(d),a_3(n)=\sum_{hd=n,\ d\leq V}\mu(d)\log h,\qquad a_4(n)=-\sum_{mk=n,\ m>U,\ k>1}\Lambda(m)\sum_{d\mid k,\ d\leq V}\mu(d).

All summation variables are positive integers. Then \Lambda(n)=a_1(n)+a_2(n)+a_3(n)+a_4(n) for every positive integer n.

Proof 1: Four Dirichlet-series terms and coefficient comparison

For \operatorname{Re}s>1, write \zeta(s)=\sum_{r\geq1}r^{-s}. Use the standard absolutely convergent Dirichlet series

-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}=\sum_{n\geq1}\Lambda(n)n^{-s}, \qquad -\zeta'(s)=\sum_{h\geq1}(\log h)h^{-s},

and multiplication and uniqueness of Dirichlet series in this half-plane.

We construct four distinct sums based on the expansion of the logarithmic derivative of the Riemann zeta function in terms of functions which are partial Dirichlet series respectively truncated at the upper bounds of U and V, respectively. More precisely, we define

F(s)=\sum_{m\leq U}\Lambda(m)m^{-s}, \qquad G(s)=\sum_{d\leq V}\mu(d)d^{-s},

which leads us to the exact identity that

-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)} =F(s)-\zeta(s)F(s)G(s)-\zeta'(s)G(s) +\left(-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}-F(s)\right)(1-\zeta(s)G(s)).

This last expansion implies that we can write

\Lambda(n)=a_1(n)+a_2(n)+a_3(n)+a_4(n),

where the component functions are those defined in the statement. The source leaves the coefficient extraction implicit.
