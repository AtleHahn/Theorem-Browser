Statement

If the fundamental group of a connected three-manifold M is finitely generated, then it is finitely presentable.

Proof 1: Finite presentability transferred through a compact core

Use Scott’s core theorem: there is a compact three-dimensional submanifold C whose inclusion induces \pi_1(C)\cong\pi_1(M). A compact three-manifold has finitely presentable fundamental group. Transferring this property through the isomorphism gives the result.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof applies compact-core topology and transfers a finite group presentation through a fundamental-group isomorphism. Manifolds, fundamental groups and compact cores are substantial algebraic topology beyond the baseline.
