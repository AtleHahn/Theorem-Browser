Statement

For a relation R on a set X and S\subseteq X, put R|_S=R\cap(S\times S). Write R^+ for the transitive closure, the smallest transitive relation containing R. In general (R|_S)^+ need not equal R^+|_S.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Restriction is a set intersection and transitive closure is explicitly the smallest relation adding all required transitive pairs; these have elementary pair-and-path meanings.

Proof 1: The excluded intermediate parent

Restricting the relation "x is parent of y" to females yields the relation "x is mother of the woman y"; its transitive closure does not relate a woman with her paternal grandmother. On the other hand, the transitive closure of "is parent of" is "is ancestor of"; its restriction to females does relate a woman with her paternal grandmother.

For this example, take just three distinct people: the grandmother g, father p, and daughter d, with R=\{(g,p),(p,d)\} and S=\{g,d\}. This specifies the family links used in the source example.

Level

Level justification

In the explicit three-person relation, restriction removes both edges while taking closure first adds the grandmother-daughter pair. Elementary finite pairs and two-step paths suffice.
