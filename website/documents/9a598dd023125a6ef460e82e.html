Statement

Let G be a finite group and p a prime. Define its p-residual by

O^p(G)=\bigcap\{N\trianglelefteq G:G/N\text{ has order a power of }p\}.

Then O^p(G) is generated by all Sylow q-subgroups of G, as q ranges over the prime divisors of |G| different from p.

Here N\trianglelefteq G means that N is normal. A Sylow q-subgroup has order equal to the largest power of q dividing |G|. The subgroup generated by a family is the smallest subgroup containing every member of the family; an empty family generates the trivial subgroup. The power p^0=1 is allowed in the defining intersection.
