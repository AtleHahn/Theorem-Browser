Statement

Let \widehat{\mathbb C}=\mathbb C\cup\{\infty\} be the Riemann sphere and let K\subseteq\widehat{\mathbb C} be closed. Let f be a complex-valued holomorphic function on an open neighborhood of K. Let A\subseteq\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus K meet every connected component of the complement. Then there are rational functions r_j, all of whose poles on the sphere lie in A, such that

\sup_{z\in K}|r_j(z)-f(z)|\longrightarrow0.

If \infty\in K, holomorphicity and values at infinity are understood in the local coordinate w=1/z.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate complex analysis and topology suffice; the sphere is explained by adjoining infinity with its local coordinate, a small extension of plane holomorphicity.
