Statement

Let H_1,H_2 be real or complex Hilbert spaces, let U\subseteq H_1, and let L\ge0. If f:U\to H_2 satisfies \|f(u)-f(v)\|\le L\|u-v\| for every u,v\in U, then there is F:H_1\to H_2 with F|_U=f and \|F(x)-F(y)\|\le L\|x-y\| for every x,y\in H_1. A Hilbert space is an inner-product space complete in its induced norm. No closedness of U or finite-dimensional restriction is required.
