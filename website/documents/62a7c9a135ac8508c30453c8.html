Statement

Let p\ge1 be an integer, V a positive-definite symmetric real p\times p matrix, and n>p-1. Let X have the Wishart density on positive-definite symmetric matrices, with density \exp(\operatorname{tr}(\eta_1X)+\eta_2\log|X|-A) relative to Lebesgue measure on independent symmetric-matrix coordinates, whose natural statistic is (X,\log|X|) and parameters are \eta_1=-V^{-1}/2, \eta_2=(n-p-1)/2. Here |X| denotes determinant, matrix products in the exponential pairing are traces, and A(\eta_1,n)=-(n/2)\log|-\eta_1|+\log\Gamma_p(n/2), with \Gamma_p(a)=\pi^{p(p-1)/4}\prod_{j=1}^p\Gamma(a+(1-j)/2). With \psi=(\log\Gamma)\prime, \mathbb E\log|X|=p\log2+\log|V|+\sum_{j=1}^p\psi((n+1-j)/2).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The matrix density, multivariate gamma product and digamma derivative are explicitly defined. Finite-dimensional densities and the scalar Gamma integral add related calculus/probability definitions to matrices, with no Wishart theory assumed.

Proof 1: Differentiate the gamma product in the log-partition function

Use the exponential-family mean identity \operatorname E[T_j]=\partial A/\partial\eta_j. Here \mathbf X=X and \mathbf V=V.

Now, for η2, we first need to expand the part of the log-partition function that involves the multivariate gamma function:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\log \Gamma _{p}(a)&=\log \left(\pi ^{\frac {p(p-1)}{4}}\prod _{j=1}^{p}\Gamma {\left(a+{\frac {1-j}{2}}\right)}\right)\\&={\frac {p(p-1)}{4}}\log \pi +\sum _{j=1}^{p}\log \Gamma {\left(a+{\frac {1-j}{2}}\right)}\end{aligned}}}

We also need the digamma function:

{\displaystyle \psi (x)={\frac {d}{dx}}\log \Gamma (x).}

Then:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\operatorname {E} [\log |\mathbf {X} |]&={\frac {\partial }{\partial \eta _{2}}}A\left(\ldots ,\eta _{2}\right)\\[1ex]&={\frac {\partial }{\partial \eta _{2}}}\left[\left(\eta _{2}+{\frac {p+1}{2}}\right)\log \left(2^{p}\left|\mathbf {V} \right|\right)+\log \Gamma _{p}{\left(\eta _{2}+{\frac {p+1}{2}}\right)}\right]\\[1ex]&={\frac {\partial }{\partial \eta _{2}}}\left[\left(\eta _{2}+{\frac {p+1}{2}}\right)\log \left(2^{p}\left|\mathbf {V} \right|\right)\right]+{\frac {\partial }{\partial \eta _{2}}}\left[{\frac {p(p-1)}{4}}\log \pi \right]\\&{\hphantom {=}}+{\frac {\partial }{\partial \eta _{2}}}\sum _{j=1}^{p}\log \Gamma {\left(\eta _{2}+{\frac {p+1}{2}}+{\frac {1-j}{2}}\right)}\\[1ex]&=p\log 2+\log |\mathbf {V} |+\sum _{j=1}^{p}\psi {\left(\eta _{2}+{\frac {p+1}{2}}+{\frac {1-j}{2}}\right)}\\[1ex]&=p\log 2+\log |\mathbf {V} |+\sum _{j=1}^{p}\psi {\left({\frac {n-p-1}{2}}+{\frac {p+1}{2}}+{\frac {1-j}{2}}\right)}\\[1ex]&=p\log 2+\log |\mathbf {V} |+\sum _{j=1}^{p}\psi {\left({\frac {n+1-j}{2}}\right)}\end{aligned}}}
