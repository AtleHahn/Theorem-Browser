Statement

For a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle of side lengths a,b,c, perimeter p=a+b+c and area T, p^2\ge12\sqrt3\,T. Equality holds for an equilateral triangle.

Level

Proof 1: AM–GM applied to the side-product area bound

The inequality, with equality for the equilateral triangle, is implied via the AM–GM inequality by the stronger inequality

T\le\frac{\sqrt3}{4}(abc)^{2/3}.

The side-product bound is an external prerequisite. The source leaves the AM–GM substitution and equilateral equality check implicit.
