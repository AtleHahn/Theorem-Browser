Statement

Let G be a connected Lie group and \sigma an involutive automorphism. Let K be a compact open subgroup of G^\sigma=\{g:\sigma(g)=g\}. Then G/K admits a G-invariant Riemannian metric making it a Riemannian symmetric space.

Level

Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Lie groups; Involutive automorphisms; Homogeneous quotients; Riemannian symmetric metrics. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Average a metric and construct coset symmetries (outline)

To obtain a Riemannian symmetric space structure we need to fix a K-invariant inner product on the tangent space to G/K at the identity coset eK: such an inner product always exists by averaging, since K is compact, and by acting with G, we obtain a G-invariant Riemannian metric g on G/K.

To show that M=G/K is Riemannian symmetric, consider any point p=hK (a coset of K, where h\in G) and define

s_p:M\to M,\qquad h'K\mapsto h\sigma(h^{-1}h')K,

where \sigma is the involution of G fixing K. Then one can check that s_p is an isometry with (clearly) s_p(p)=p and (by differentiating) ds_p equal to minus the identity on T_pM. Thus s_p is a geodesic symmetry and, since p was arbitrary, M is a Riemannian symmetric space.
