Statement

Let R be a right Artinian ring, meaning that descending chains of right ideals stabilize, and let U be a simple right R-module: U\ne0 and it has no nonzero proper R-submodules. Put E=\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Z}(U), the ring of additive endomorphisms, and

R_U=\{\rho_r:r\in R\}\subseteq E,\qquad \rho_r(u)=ur.

For a subset S of a ring R, write C_R(S)=\{r\in R:rs=sr\text{ for every }s\in S\}. Then

C_E(C_E(R_U))=R_U.

Equivalently, since C_E(R_U)=\operatorname{End}_R(U), every additive endomorphism of U commuting with all R-module endomorphisms is right multiplication by some element of R.
