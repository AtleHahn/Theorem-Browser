Statement

In an isosceles triangle ABC with |AB|=|AC|, the internal bisector of angle A meets side BC at its midpoint.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All terms are elementary Euclidean triangle geometry, angle bisectors and segment lengths or ratios.

Review notes

Proof 1: Equal-side specialization outline

Let D be the intersection of the internal angle bisector with BC. The internal angle-bisector theorem gives |BD|/|CD|=|AB|/|AC|.

An immediate consequence of the theorem is that the angle bisector of the vertex angle of an isosceles triangle will also bisect the opposite side.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Substitute equal adjacent side lengths into the angle-bisector ratio. Elementary Euclidean geometry suffices.
