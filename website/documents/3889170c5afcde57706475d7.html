Statement

Let G be a connected solvable linear algebraic group over an algebraically closed field k, and let \rho:G\to GL(V) be a rational representation on a nonzero finite-dimensional k-vector space. If the representation is irreducible, then \dim_kV=1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves linear algebraic groups; connected solvable groups; rational representations; irreducibility. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use an invariant line and irreducibility

By the Lie–Kolchin invariant-line theorem, V has a one-dimensional invariant subspace L. Irreducibility forces the nonzero subspace L to equal V. Thus \dim_kV=1.
