Statement

Work in ZFC and assume GCH: 2^\kappa=\kappa^+ for every infinite cardinal \kappa, where \kappa^+ is the next larger cardinal. If infinite cardinals \kappa<\lambda, then 2^\kappa<2^\lambda.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate cardinal comparison extends to the explicitly defined successor cardinal, and cardinal exponentiation counts subsets. The stated GCH assumption then makes the monotonicity claim understandable without ordinal recursion or independence theory.

Proof 1: Compare successive cardinals under GCH — outline

GCH identifies each power-set cardinal with the successor cardinal. Thus its successor-cardinal values preserve the strict order of the two infinite cardinals.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the supplied identification of a power-set size with the next cardinal. From kappa<lambda, the least greater cardinal kappa+ is at most lambda, which is less than lambda+. From elementary set cardinality, the successor-cardinal definition is a small extension; no cofinality or transfinite arithmetic is used.
