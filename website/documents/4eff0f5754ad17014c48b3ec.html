Statement

Give the unit circle its shorter-arc distance and \mathbb R^2 its Euclidean distance. The inclusion of the circle in the plane is not distance preserving.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Distances and circle lengths use elementary geometry.

Proof 1: Compare opposite points

Indeed, the Euclidean distance between a pair of opposite points of the circle is only {\displaystyle 2}, whereas in the Riemannian circle it is {\displaystyle \pi }. Here the Riemannian circle distance is the shorter-arc distance.

Level justification

Opposite points have chord length two and shorter-arc length pi. Comparing these elementary lengths proves the claim.
