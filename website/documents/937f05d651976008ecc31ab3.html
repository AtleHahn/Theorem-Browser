Statement

Let E\to X be a topological G-bundle with fiber F, and fix continuous G-valued transition maps t_{ij} on a trivializing open cover (U_i) satisfying t_{ik}=t_{ij}t_{jk}. For any topological space F' with a continuous left G-action, there is a bundle E'\to X with fiber F' and those same transition maps acting on F'.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

General topology and topological group actions are necessary to understand the full statement and construction.

Proof 1: Replace the fiber in the cocycle construction

One can form an associated bundle E'\to X with a fiber F' and structure group G by taking the fixed local trivialization of E and replacing F by F' in the fiber bundle construction theorem. That theorem constructs a bundle from continuous G-valued transition functions satisfying the cocycle condition; the cover and transition functions are unchanged.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although the cover and transition functions are reused, understanding the construction requires fiber bundles, local trivializations, continuous group actions and the cocycle gluing theorem. These interdependent bundle-theoretic concepts exceed the small-extension allowance from basic topology. Simply replacing the displayed fiber symbol does not remove that burden.

Statement used:

Given an open cover U_i of X, continuous maps t_ij:U_i intersect U_j to a topological group G satisfying t_ik=t_ij t_jk, and a continuous left G-action on a space F prime, the local products U_i times F prime glue by (x,v)_j identified with (x,t_ij(x)v)_i to a fiber bundle over X.

Application in proof:

Apply the construction with the existing transition cocycle and the new fiber F prime.
