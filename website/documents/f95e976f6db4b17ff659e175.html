Statement

For a finite simple graph G, a proper coloring of its vertices with three colors is equivalent to a graph homomorphism G\to K_3, where a homomorphism is a vertex map sending every edge to an edge and K_3 is the three-vertex complete graph.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Graph homomorphism and the three-vertex target are explicitly finite adjacency conditions.

Proof 1: Identify colors with the triangle vertices

Use the three vertices of K_3 as the three color labels. An edge of G maps to an edge of K_3 exactly when its endpoints get different labels, which is precisely proper coloring. The same assignment therefore has both descriptions.
