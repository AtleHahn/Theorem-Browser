Statement

Every positive-degree element of the stable homotopy ring of spheres is nilpotent. Explicitly, let k>0 and

x\in\pi_k^S=\varinjlim_n\pi_{n+k}(S^n),

where the transition maps are suspension. Then x^m=0\in\pi_{mk}^S for some integer m\ge1. Here \pi_j(S^n) consists of based homotopy classes of maps S^j\to S^n. The product in \pi_*^S is induced by smash products of representative sphere maps, identifying S^a\wedge S^b with S^{a+b}.

Proof 1: Power operations and primary reduction (proof sketch) (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Section 1, Theorems 1.1–1.5, Remarks 1.3–1.4 and Corollary 1.6.1, pp. 1–2; Sections 2–4.). The linked exposition is a proof sketch with the indicated dependencies.

Hahn gives the power-operation proof at 2 and the following primary reduction in Remark 1.4: Kahn–Priddy lowers the exponent of primary torsion after taking a power; iterate, and use the analogous odd-prime argument explicitly indicated by the author. Positive stable stems are finite (Serre finiteness), so only finitely many primes occur, and nilpotence of every primary localization implies nilpotence of the original element by taking a common sufficiently large power.
