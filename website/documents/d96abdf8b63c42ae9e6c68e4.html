Statement

For n\ge1, let A:I\to\mathbb R^{n\times n} and let \phi(t) be an invertible differentiable fundamental matrix for x'=A(t)x on a real interval I. For t_0\in I, the matrix \Phi(t)=\phi(t)\phi(t_0)^{-1} is a fundamental matrix with \Phi(t_0)=I_n. For 0\in I, x(t)=\phi(t)\phi(0)^{-1}x_0 solves the system with x(0)=x_0.
