Statement

A compact connected smooth manifold M without boundary is almost flat if and only if it is diffeomorphic to a compact infranilmanifold. Almost flat means that for every \varepsilon>0 there is a Riemannian metric g on M with \operatorname{diam}_g M\le1 and |K_g(\sigma)|<\varepsilon for every tangent two-plane \sigma, where K_g is sectional curvature and diameter is the supremum of Riemannian distances between points. An infranilmanifold here is a quotient \Gamma\backslash N, where N is a connected simply connected nilpotent Lie group and \Gamma is a discrete subgroup of its affine group N\rtimes\operatorname{Aut}(N) acting freely, properly discontinuously and cocompactly on N, with finite image in \operatorname{Aut}(N). An affine element (a,\alpha) acts by x\mapsto a\alpha(x). Nilpotent means that the lower central series, obtained by repeatedly taking commutators with the whole group, terminates at the identity. Cocompact means that the quotient is compact. In particular, M has a finite covering by a compact nilmanifold, a quotient of N by a discrete cocompact subgroup of translations.
