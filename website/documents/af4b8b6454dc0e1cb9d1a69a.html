Statement

Let two concentric disks with common center c have radii 0<r<R, and let p lie in the interior of the smaller disk. Take n\ge8 divisible by four and n/2 lines through p making n successive angles 2\pi/n. The portions of the annulus r\le|x-c|\le R in the alternating sectors have equal total area.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses disk sectors, equal angles and ordinary planar area.

Proof 1: Subtract two equal disk partitions

Use the pizza theorem as an external prerequisite: if p lies inside a disk and n\ge8 is divisible by four, the n equal-angle sectors made by n/2 lines through p have equal total areas in the two alternating classes.

The two disks are each partitioned evenly between the alternating sectors. Subtract the smaller-disk areas from the corresponding larger-disk areas. Their differences are the annular portions, which therefore have equal total areas.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply the explicitly stated pizza theorem to both disks and subtract their equal alternating-area totals. School geometric area and subtraction suffice; no proof of the pizza theorem is needed.
