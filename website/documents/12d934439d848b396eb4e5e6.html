Statement

\pi_2(SO(3))=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement is a homotopy-group assertion about the topological rotation group.

Mathematical Field
Topological groups, Lie groupsAlgebraic topology
Required topics
Second homotopy groupSpecial orthogonal group
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Pass to the three-sphere covering

Use SO(3)\cong\mathbb RP^3 and the covering S^3\to\mathbb RP^3 to obtain \pi_2(SO(3))\cong\pi_2(\mathbb RP^3)\cong\pi_2(S^3)=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Identify the rotation group with projective space and transfer higher homotopy through its sphere cover. Higher homotopy groups and covering-space calculations require a substantial algebraic-topology background.
