Statement

Let (M,\omega) be a smooth symplectic manifold of dimension 2n, let p\in M, and let r be an integer with 0\leq r\leq n. Suppose f_1,\ldots,f_r are smooth real functions on an open neighborhood V of p, with linearly independent differentials at p, and with

\{f_i,f_j\}=0\quad\text{on }V\qquad(1\leq i,j\leq r),

where \{-,-\} is the Poisson bracket associated to \omega. Then there is an open neighborhood U\subseteq V of p and smooth functions f_{r+1},\ldots,f_n,g_1,\ldots,g_n on U such that

(f_1,\ldots,f_n,g_1,\ldots,g_n):U\longrightarrow\mathbb R^{2n}

is a coordinate chart and

\omega|_U=\sum_{i=1}^n df_i\wedge dg_i.

The originally given functions are restricted to U.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Symplectic forms, differential forms and their Poisson bracket require substantial differential geometry beyond undergraduate multivariable calculus; the coordinate conclusion does not eliminate those prerequisites.
