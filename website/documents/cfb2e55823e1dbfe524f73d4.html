Statement

Let X be a separable real Banach space and \mu a Borel probability measure on X such that for each bounded linear functional \ell:X\to\mathbb R, the distribution \ell_*\mu, defined by (\ell_*\mu)(A)=\mu(\ell^{-1}(A)), is a real normal distribution with mean zero (allowing variance zero). Then there exists \alpha>0 such that \int_X e^{\alpha\|x\|^2}\,d\mu(x)<\infty. In particular \int_X\|x\|^k\,d\mu(x)<\infty for every real k\ge0, with the zeroth moment interpreted as 1.
