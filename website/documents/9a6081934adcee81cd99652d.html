Statement

Let G be a finite abelian group with identity e. Let A_1,\ldots,A_m be subsets, m\ge1, such that each contains e, each has prime cardinality, and every g\in G has exactly one expression g=a_1\cdots a_m with a_i\in A_i. Then at least one A_i is a subgroup of G.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate group theory and explicit finite-set factorization suffice.

Level justification

Introductory finite-group theory and polynomial arithmetic modulo a prime suffice. Factor replacements use cyclic permutations and congruences; the direction bound uses polynomial interpolation and power sums over a finite field. The remaining argument is induction through subgroups and quotients.
