Statement

Let (M,g) be a complete connected Riemannian manifold of nonnegative sectional curvature and let S be a soul: a closed totally convex, totally geodesic embedded submanifold whose normal bundle is diffeomorphic to M. Then the induced metric on S has nonnegative sectional curvature.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Riemannian or geodesic curvature and its global geometric interpretation require substantial differential geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply the Gauss equation to a totally geodesic submanifold

By the Gauss equation and total geodesicity, the induced Riemannian metric on the soul automatically has nonnegative sectional curvature. Here total geodesicity makes the second fundamental form terms in the Gauss equation vanish.
