Statement

For a completely positive map \Phi:A\to B(H) on a unital C-star algebra A and complex Hilbert space H, a Stinespring representation is a triple (\pi,V,K) with K a complex Hilbert space, \pi:A\to B(K) a unital star homomorphism, V:H\to K bounded, and \Phi(a)=V^*\pi(a)V. It is minimal if K=\overline{\operatorname{span}\pi(A)VH}. Put K_1=\overline{\operatorname{span}\pi(A)VH} and view V as a map into K_1. Then \pi_1(a)=\pi(a)|_{K_1} gives a minimal Stinespring representation (\pi_1,V,K_1).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves C-star algebras; Stinespring representations defined locally; Closed spans in Hilbert spaces; Restricted star homomorphisms. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Restrict to the generated closed subspace

Let K1 be the closed linear span of π(A) VH. By property of *-representations in general, K1 is an invariant subspace of π(a) for all a. Also, K1 contains VH. Define

{\displaystyle \pi _{1}(a)=\pi (a){\Big |}_{K_{1}}.}

We can compute directly

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\pi _{1}(a)\pi _{1}(b)&=\pi (a){\Big |}_{K_{1}}\pi (b){\Big |}_{K_{1}}\\&=\pi (a)\pi (b){\Big |}_{K_{1}}\\&=\pi (ab){\Big |}_{K_{1}}\\&=\pi _{1}(ab)\end{aligned}}}

and if k and ℓ lie in K1

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\langle \pi _{1}(a^{*})k,\ell \rangle &=\langle \pi (a^{*})k,\ell \rangle \\&=\langle \pi (a)^{*}k,\ell \rangle \\&=\langle k,\pi (a)\ell \rangle \\&=\langle k,\pi _{1}(a)\ell \rangle \\&=\langle \pi _{1}(a)^{*}k,\ell \rangle .\end{aligned}}}

So (π1, V, K1) is also a Stinespring representation of Φ and has the additional property that K1 is the closed linear span of π(A) V H. Such a representation is called a minimal Stinespring representation.
