Statement

A nondegenerate square in the Euclidean plane cannot be dissected into an odd finite number of triangles of equal area. A dissection is a finite collection of nondegenerate closed triangles with pairwise disjoint interiors whose union is the square; the triangles need not meet edge-to-edge, and their vertices may have arbitrary real coordinates.
