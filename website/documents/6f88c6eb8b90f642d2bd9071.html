Statement

If f,g are twice differentiable real functions on an open interval where g\ne0, and h=f/g, then h''=(f''-g'' h-2g' h')/g.

Proof 1: Differentiate the product identity twice

Differentiate f=gh twice using the product rule to obtain f''=g'' h+2g' h'+gh''. Solving for h'' gives the formula.

Level

Level justification
