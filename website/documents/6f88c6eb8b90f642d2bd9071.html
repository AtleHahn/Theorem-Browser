Statement

If f,g are twice differentiable real functions on an open interval where g\ne0, and h=f/g, then h''=(f''-g'' h-2g' h')/g.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Real derivatives; Limits; Products and quotients of real functions. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Differentiate the product identity twice

Differentiate f=gh twice using the product rule to obtain f''=g'' h+2g' h'+gh''. Solving for h'' gives the formula.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Differentiate f=gh twice and solve for the second derivative of h. First undergraduate calculus suffices.
