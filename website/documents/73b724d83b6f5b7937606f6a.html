Statement

Let X,F be topological spaces, G a topological group acting continuously on F on the left, and (U_i)_{i\in I} an open cover of X. Let continuous maps t_{ij}:U_i\cap U_j\to G satisfy t_{ik}(x)=t_{ij}(x)t_{jk}(x) on every triple overlap, including repeated indices. Assume the action of G on F is faithful. If two G-bundles E,E'\to X have these same trivializing neighborhoods and transition functions, then they are isomorphic as G-bundles over X.

Beyond undergraduate level

Proof 1: Identity gauge transformation

Use the source isomorphism criterion: if continuous maps t_i:U_i\to G obey t'_{ij}=t_i^{-1}t_{ij}t_j, then the two bundles are isomorphic. For equal transition functions take t_i(x)=1_G for every i,x. The required identity becomes t_{ij}=1_G^{-1}t_{ij}1_G, proving the claim.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Choosing the identity gauge is algebraically trivial, but the isomorphism criterion uses G-bundles, transition cocycles and gauge transformations. Those constructions remain substantial prerequisites.
