Statement

Let X\ge0 have finite expectation, and let 0<p<1. Define the lower quantile Q_X(1-p)=\inf\{t\in\mathbb R:\mathbb P(X\le t)\ge1-p\}. Then Q_X(1-p)\le\mathbb E[X]/p.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Expectation, distribution functions and the explicitly defined quantile add linked scalar probability definitions to undergraduate integration and infima.

Proof 1: Combine the quantile tail property with Markov

Put q=Q_X(1-p). The defining quantile property gives \mathbb P(X\ge q)\ge p. If q=0, the result follows from nonnegativity of the expectation. If q>0, Markov’s inequality gives

p\le\mathbb P(X\ge q)\le\frac{\mathbb E[X]}q.

Multiplying by q/p proves the bound.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the lower-quantile tail property, separate the zero case and apply the explicit Markov inequality at q. Undergraduate probability, expectations and real infima suffice; no measure-theoretic integration is developed.
