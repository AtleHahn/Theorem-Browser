Statement

Let G be a finite simple undirected graph with n vertices and chromatic polynomial

P_G(t)=\sum_{j=0}^n b_jt^j.

Set a_j=|b_j|. Then (a_0,\ldots,a_n) is log-concave:

a_j^2\ge a_{j-1}a_{j+1}\qquad(1\le j\le n-1),

and is unimodal: for some m\in\{0,\ldots,n\},

a_0\le\cdots\le a_m\ge\cdots\ge a_n.

A simple graph has at most one edge between distinct vertices and no loops. Its chromatic polynomial is the polynomial whose value at each nonnegative integer q counts assignments of q labeled colors to vertices in which adjacent vertices receive different colors.
