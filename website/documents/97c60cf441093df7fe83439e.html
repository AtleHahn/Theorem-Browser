Statement

Let \Sigma be a finite alphabet and \Sigma^* its finite words, including the empty word; |w| denotes word length and juxtaposition denotes concatenation. A language L\subseteq\Sigma^* is context-free if generated from a start symbol by finitely many grammar productions A\to w, where A is one nonterminal symbol and w is a word of terminals and nonterminals; generated terminal-only words form L. If |\Sigma|\ge3, then \{xyyz:x,y,z\in\Sigma^*,\ |y|>0\} is not context-free.
