Statement

Let \mathcal G be a groupoid internal to the category of groups. Send each group G to the one-object groupoid BG whose arrows are the elements of G with composition given by the group product, and send group homomorphisms to the induced functors. Applying this construction to \mathcal G gives a double groupoid.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Internal groupoids and double groupoids require several layers of categorical definitions beyond a brief introduction. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: An internal groupoid in groups gives a double groupoid — outline

Groups embed in groupoids by sending G to the one-object groupoid BG with morphisms given by G. Thus the internal groupoid structure in groups gives an internal groupoid in groupoids, which is a double groupoid.
