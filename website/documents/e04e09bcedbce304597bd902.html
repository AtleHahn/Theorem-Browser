Statement

Let G be a connected complex semisimple group, B a Borel subgroup and \chi:B\to\mathbb C^\times a holomorphic character. Let \mathcal H_\chi be the vector space of holomorphic maps f:G\to\mathbb C satisfying f(hb)=\chi(b^{-1})f(h) for h\in G,b\in B. Then

(L_gf)(h)=f(g^{-1}h)

defines a linear action of G on \mathcal H_\chi.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Complex Lie groups; Borel subgroups and holomorphic characters; Holomorphic equivariant functions; Linear group actions. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Verify the supplied translation formula (outline)

Left translation preserves holomorphicity, and

(L_gf)(hb)=f(g^{-1}hb)=\chi(b^{-1})f(g^{-1}h)=\chi(b^{-1})(L_gf)(h).

Thus the formula preserves \mathcal H_\chi and is linear in f. Moreover

(L_{g_1}L_{g_2}f)(h)=f(g_2^{-1}g_1^{-1}h)=f((g_1g_2)^{-1}h)=L_{g_1g_2}f(h),

and L_ef=f.
