Statement

Define the rational Bernoulli numbers B_n by the formal power series identity

\frac{t}{e^t-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B_n\frac{t^n}{n!}.

Let p be a prime, let a\geq0 be an integer, and let h,k be positive even integers neither divisible by p-1. If

h\equiv k\pmod{\varphi(p^{a+1})},

then

(1-p^{h-1})\frac{B_h}{h}\equiv(1-p^{k-1})\frac{B_k}{k}\pmod{p^{a+1}}.

Here \varphi(m) is the number of integers from 1 to m relatively prime to m. For rational numbers x,y, the congruence x\equiv y\pmod{p^b} means that x-y=p^b u/v for some integers u,v with p\nmid v.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Elementary modular arithmetic and undergraduate series algebra suffice; Bernoulli numbers are defined by coefficient extraction, and rational congruence is explicitly an integer divisibility condition. No p-adic analysis is needed for this statement.
