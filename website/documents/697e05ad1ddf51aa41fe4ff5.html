Statement

For truth values a,b,c, let \land,\lor,\neg mean and, or, not, and let a\Rightarrow b mean \neg a\lor b. Logical equivalence means equality of truth values. Then (a\lor b)\Rightarrow c\equiv(a\Rightarrow c)\land(b\Rightarrow c),\qquad (a\land b)\Rightarrow c\equiv(a\Rightarrow c)\lor(b\Rightarrow c).

Level
High school only
Level justification

All connectives are locally defined and the argument uses basic propositional logic.

Proof 1: Antidistributive laws for implication — outline

These two tautologies are a direct consequence of the duality in De Morgan's laws, using the convention u\Rightarrow v\equiv\neg u\lor v and the usual distributive and associative laws of the truth-functional connectives.
