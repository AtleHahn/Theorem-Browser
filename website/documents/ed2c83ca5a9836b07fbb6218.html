Statement

For positive integers x,y,

\begin{aligned}&((1+x)^y+(1+x+x^2)^y)^x((1+x^3)^x+(1+x^2+x^4)^x)^y\\ &=((1+x)^x+(1+x+x^2)^x)^y((1+x^3)^y+(1+x^2+x^4)^y)^x. \end{aligned}
Level

Proof 1: Factor out the common signed-polynomial power — outline

This identity is true for all positive integers x and y, as can be seen by factoring (1-x+x^2)^{xy} out of the second factor on each side. The source leaves the polynomial factorizations and the resulting common product implicit.
