Statement

For n≥1 variables x_1,…,x_n let p_i=Σ_j x_j^i and let e_k be the sum of products of k distinct variables, with e_0=1 and e_k=0 for k>n. For every integer k≥0, as a rational-coefficient polynomial identity, e_k=(-1)^k\sum_{\sum_{i=1}^k i m_i=k}\prod_{i=1}^k\frac{(-p_i)^{m_i}}{i^{m_i}m_i!}, where all m_i are nonnegative integers and the k=0 sum equals 1.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Power sums, elementary symmetric sums, integer multiplicities and the k=0 convention are explicitly defined by finite polynomial arithmetic. The nested indexing is complicated notation but requires only sums, products, factorials and integer equations.

Proof 1: Induct on the signed factorial coefficients — outline

Use Newton's identities

ke_k=\sum_{i=1}^k(-1)^{i-1}e_{k-i}p_i,

with e_0=1. For nonnegative integers m_1,\ldots,m_l with \sum_i i m_i=m, write

f(m;m_1,\ldots,m_l)=\prod_{i=1}^l\frac{1}{i^{m_i}m_i!}.

Terms with a negative multiplicity are taken as zero. The coefficients for the elementary symmetric functions have these absolute values and sign (-1)^{m_2+m_4+\cdots}, equivalently (-1)^{m+\sum_i m_i}.

It can be proved by considering the following inductive step:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}mf(m;m_{1},\ldots ,m_{l})&=f(m-1;m_{1}-1,\ldots ,m_{l})+\cdots +f(m-l;m_{1},\ldots ,m_{l}-1)\\m_{1}\prod _{i=1}^{l}{\frac {1}{i^{m_{i}}m_{i}!}}+\cdots +lm_{l}\prod _{i=1}^{l}{\frac {1}{i^{m_{i}}m_{i}!}}&=m\prod _{i=1}^{l}{\frac {1}{i^{m_{i}}m_{i}!}}\end{aligned}}}

In the signed Newton recurrence, decreasing m_i by one and multiplying by (-1)^{i-1} gives the required sign. The displayed coefficient identity therefore gives the induction for e_m.
