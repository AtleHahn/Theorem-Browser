Statement

Let U be a nonempty subset of a Hilbert space H, and let f:U\to\mathbb R have finite Lipschitz constant L\ge0. Then

F(x)=\inf_{u\in U}\bigl(f(u)+L\|x-u\|\bigr),\qquad x\in H,

is real-valued, satisfies F(u)=f(u) on U, and is L-Lipschitz on H. Thus |F(x)-F(y)|\le L\|x-y\| for all x,y. A Hilbert space is a complete inner-product space, and L-Lipschitz means the corresponding distance inequality holds for every pair of domain points.
