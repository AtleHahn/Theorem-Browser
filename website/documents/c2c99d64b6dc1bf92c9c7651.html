Statement

Let R(z,w)\in\mathbb C(z,w) be a rational function of two complex variables. If the differential equation w'(z)=R(z,w(z)) has a transcendental meromorphic solution on the complex plane, then

R(z,w)=a_0(z)+a_1(z)w+a_2(z)w^2

with rational functions a_0,a_1,a_2\in\mathbb C(z). Meromorphic on the plane means holomorphic except at isolated poles; transcendental here means not a rational function of z. The equation is understood as an identity wherever its expressions are defined, and hence meromorphically. Thus the equation is of Riccati type, including its linear and constant-in-w cases.
