Proof 1: Weitzenböck triangle-area bound (outline)

Use the Hadwiger–Finsler inequality

a^2+b^2+c^2\geq(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2+4\sqrt3\,\Delta.

Weitzenböck's inequality is a straightforward corollary:

a^2+b^2+c^2\geq4\sqrt3\,\Delta.
