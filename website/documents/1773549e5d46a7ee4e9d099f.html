Statement

A finite hypergraph H=(X\sqcup Y,E) is bipartite here if every edge contains exactly one vertex of Y. A matching consists of pairwise disjoint edges and is Y-perfect if it covers Y. For S\subseteq Y, put N(S)=\{e\setminus Y:e\in E,\ e\cap S\ne\varnothing\}. Let Y=\{1,\ldots,m\}, m\ge2, with no nonempty neighbor shared by two tags. Suppose a triangulation T of the simplex on Y has nonempty neighbor-set labels: on a face spanned by S every vertex label belongs to N(S), and labels on adjacent vertices are identical or disjoint. Then H has a Y-perfect matching.

Proof 1: Apply Sperner to uniquely tagged labels

Color each vertex v of T with the unique color i such that the neighbor assigned to v belongs to N(\{i\}). The face condition guarantees that this coloring satisfies Sperner’s boundary condition. Therefore, by Sperner’s lemma, a fully-labeled simplex exists. In this simplex there are m neighbor sets, each of which is a neighbor of a different element of Y, and so they must be disjoint by the adjacent-label condition. Adjoining their distinct elements of Y gives the desired Y-perfect matching.
