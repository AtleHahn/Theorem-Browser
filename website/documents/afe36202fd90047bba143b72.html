Statement

Let q be a prime power and let B\mathbb G_m(\mathbb F_q) denote the groupoid of principal multiplicative-group bundles over \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_q. For a groupoid define its weighted count by \#C=\sum_{[p]}1/|\operatorname{Aut}(p)|. Then \#B\mathbb G_m(\mathbb F_q)=1/(q-1).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves finite fields; group schemes; classifying stacks; torsors. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: One torsor class and its automorphisms

Consider {\displaystyle B\mathbb {G} _{m}=[\operatorname {Spec} \mathbb {F} _{q}/\mathbb {G} _{m}]}, the classifying stack of the multiplicative group scheme (that is, {\displaystyle \mathbb {G} _{m}(R)=R^{\times }}). By definition, {\displaystyle B\mathbb {G} _{m}(\mathbb {F} _{q})} is the category of principal {\displaystyle \mathbb {G} _{m}}-bundles over {\displaystyle \operatorname {Spec} \mathbb {F} _{q}}, which has only one isomorphism class (since all such bundles are trivial by Lang's theorem). Its group of automorphisms is {\displaystyle \mathbb {G} _{m}}, which means that the number of {\displaystyle \mathbb {F} _{q}}-isomorphisms is {\displaystyle \#\mathbb {G} _{m}(\mathbb {F} _{q})=\#\mathbb {F} _{q}^{\times }=q-1}.

Thus the weighted count, which sums 1/|\operatorname{Aut}(p)| over isomorphism classes, is 1/(q-1).
