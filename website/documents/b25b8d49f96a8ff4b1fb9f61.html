Statement

Let M be a countable nonstandard model of first-order Peano arithmetic: M satisfies PA but is not isomorphic to the usual natural-number structure. For every bijection b:\mathbb N\to M, neither of the operations

m\oplus n=b^{-1}(b(m)+_M b(n)),\qquad m\otimes n=b^{-1}(b(m)\cdot_M b(n))

is a recursive (computable) function \mathbb N^2\to\mathbb N. Consequently, no countable nonstandard model of PA has a recursive presentation of its arithmetic structure.
