Statement

There exist real constants c>0 and \varepsilon>0 such that every nonempty finite set A\subset\mathbb Z satisfies

\max\{|A+A|,|A\cdot A|\}\geq c\,|A|^{1+\varepsilon}.

Here |S| is the number of distinct elements of a finite set S,

A+A=\{a+b:a,b\in A\},\qquad A\cdot A=\{ab:a,b\in A\}.

The constants c and \varepsilon are independent of A.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Finite sets, arithmetic, real exponents and fixed constants suffice; the sumset and product set are defined locally. Neither asymptotic notation nor field theory is needed.
