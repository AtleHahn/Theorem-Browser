Statement

Assume every nonempty bounded-above subset of R has a supremum. For any nested sequence of nonempty closed bounded intervals I_n=[a_n,b_n], the intersection is nonempty.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Closed real intervals; Least-upper-bound property; Nested sequences. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Take the supremum of the left endpoints

The set A={a_1,a_2,…} is nonempty and bounded above; each b_n is an upper bound because the intervals are nested. Let s=sup A. Then a_n≤s≤b_n for every n, so s belongs to every I_n.
