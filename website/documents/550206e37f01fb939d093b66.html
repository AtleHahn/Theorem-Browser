Statement

Let (E_n)_{n\ge1} be mutually independent events in a probability space with \sum_n\mathbb P(E_n)=\infty. Then \mathbb P(\limsup_nE_n)=1. Here \limsup_nE_n=\bigcap_{N\ge1}\bigcup_{n\ge N}E_n, the event of infinitely many occurrences.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Only events, independence and countable probability continuity extend undergraduate sets and real series. The limsup event is explicitly defined; no random-variable integration or stochastic convergence mode is needed.

Proof 1: Independent tail complements and a vanishing product

Suppose that {\textstyle \sum _{n=1}^{\infty }\Pr(E_{n})=\infty } and the events {\displaystyle (E_{n})_{n=1}^{\infty }} are independent. It is sufficient to show the event that only finitely many of the En's occur has probability 0. This is just to say that it is sufficient to show that

{\displaystyle 1-\Pr(\limsup _{n\to \infty }E_{n})=0.}

Writing “i.o.” for “infinitely often” and using continuity of probability on increasing events, note that:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}1-\Pr(\limsup _{n\to \infty }E_{n})&=1-\Pr \left(\{E_{n}{\text{ i.o.}}\}\right)=\Pr \left(\{E_{n}{\text{ i.o.}}\}^{c}\right)\\&=\Pr \left(\left(\bigcap _{N=1}^{\infty }\bigcup _{n=N}^{\infty }E_{n}\right)^{c}\right)=\Pr \left(\bigcup _{N=1}^{\infty }\bigcap _{n=N}^{\infty }E_{n}^{c}\right)\\&=\Pr \left(\liminf _{n\to \infty }E_{n}^{c}\right)=\lim _{N\to \infty }\Pr \left(\bigcap _{n=N}^{\infty }E_{n}^{c}\right),\end{aligned}}}

it is enough to show: {\textstyle \Pr \left(\bigcap _{n=N}^{\infty }E_{n}^{c}\right)=0}. Since the {\displaystyle (E_{n})_{n=1}^{\infty }} are independent, continuity of probability on decreasing events gives:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\Pr \left(\bigcap _{n=N}^{\infty }E_{n}^{c}\right)&=\prod _{n=N}^{\infty }\Pr(E_{n}^{c})\\&=\prod _{n=N}^{\infty }(1-\Pr(E_{n})).\end{aligned}}}

The convergence test for infinite products guarantees that the product above is 0, if {\textstyle \sum _{n=N}^{\infty }\Pr(E_{n})} diverges. This completes the proof.
