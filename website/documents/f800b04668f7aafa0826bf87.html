Statement

For a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle ABC, the perpendicular bisectors of any two sides meet at its circumcenter O. The circle centered at O through A also passes through B,C, and the third perpendicular bisector passes through O.

Level
High school only

Proof 1: Intersect bisectors and compare distances — outline

Construct the perpendicular bisectors of two sides and let O be their intersection. Any point on a perpendicular bisector is equidistant from that side’s endpoints. Thus O is equidistant from all three vertices. The circle centered there through one vertex passes through all three, and O also lies on the third perpendicular bisector.
