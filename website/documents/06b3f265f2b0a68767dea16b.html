Statement

Let \operatorname{Im}\tau>0, q=e^{2\pi i\tau}, and interpret q^a=e^{2\pi ia\tau}. Define (a;q)_\infty=\prod_{j=0}^\infty(1-aq^j),

G_M=q^{-1/60}/[(q;q^5)_\infty(q^4;q^5)_\infty],\quad H_M=q^{11/60}/[(q^2;q^5)_\infty(q^3;q^5)_\infty].

With \eta_W(q)=q^{1/24}(q;q)_\infty and \eta_W(q^5)=q^{5/24}(q^5;q^5)_\infty, one has G_MH_M=\eta_W(q^5)/\eta_W(q).

Proof 1: Group product factors by residues modulo five — outline

The product leads to a simplified combination of Pochhammer operators:

{\displaystyle H_{M}(q)\,G_{M}(q)=q^{1/6}{\frac {1}{(q;q^{5})_{\infty }(q^{4};q^{5})_{\infty }(q^{2};q^{5})_{\infty }(q^{3};q^{5})_{\infty }}}=}{\displaystyle =q^{1/6}{\frac {(q^{5};q^{5})_{\infty }}{(q;q)_{\infty }}}={\frac {\eta _{W}(q^{5})}{\eta _{W}(q)}}}

All products converge for |q|<1; the four denominator products contain exactly the positive powers of q not divisible by five.
