Statement

A topological space is separable and metrizable if and only if it is regular, Hausdorff and second-countable. Separable means having a countable dense subset; second-countable means having a countable open base.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Separable, metrizable, Hausdorff, regular and countable-base conditions are standard basic point-set topology. No additional smooth or algebraic topology is needed.

Proof 1: Combine Urysohn with countability properties

Use Urysohn’s theorem that a regular Hausdorff second-countable space is metrizable, together with the fact that every second-countable space is separable. These give one implication. Conversely, metric spaces are regular and Hausdorff, and a separable metric space is second-countable, giving the other implication.
