Statement

Let X be a connected polyhedral cell complex whose intrinsic metric is complete and nonpositively curved in the local metric sense. Then X is aspherical: its universal covering space is contractible. Here non-positive curvature means that each point has a neighborhood U in which points are joined by geodesics and, for z\in U and a constant-speed geodesic \gamma:[0,1]\to U, d(z,\gamma(1/2))^2\le\tfrac12d(z,\gamma(0))^2+\tfrac12d(z,\gamma(1))^2-\tfrac14d(\gamma(0),\gamma(1))^2.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The application concerns polyhedral topology, curvature of metric spaces and contractible universal covers.

Proof 1: Specialization of the covering theorem

The source applies the metric Cartan–Hadamard theorem to the nonpositively curved complex. That theorem makes its universal cover a Hadamard space, hence contractible. A cell complex with contractible universal cover is aspherical, giving the claimed application.
