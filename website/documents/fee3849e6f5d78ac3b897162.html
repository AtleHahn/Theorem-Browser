Statement

Every simple ring satisfying a polynomial identity is Artinian.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The assertion concerns rings and modules, with the stated abstract algebraic structures beyond calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Apply the matrix-ring structure theorem

Such rings are full matrix rings over polynomial-identity division rings. Full finite matrix rings over division rings are Artinian, so simple polynomial identity rings are Artinian.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the structure theorem for simple polynomial-identity rings and the Artinian property of division-ring matrix algebras. Polynomial identities in noncommutative rings and structural chain conditions introduce a cumulative background not confidently a small undergraduate extension.
