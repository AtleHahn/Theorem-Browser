For every finite extension E/\mathbb Q there is an \alpha\in E with E=\mathbb Q(\alpha).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A finite extension of the rationals and a field generated by one element are standard introductory field-algebra concepts.

Proof 1: Apply separability in characteristic zero

The rational field has characteristic zero, so every finite extension of it is separable. Apply the primitive element theorem: a finite separable extension has a single generator.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use characteristic-zero separability and the primitive-element theorem. Undergraduate field theory suffices with separability as the absence of repeated minimal-polynomial roots; neither theorem proof is needed.
