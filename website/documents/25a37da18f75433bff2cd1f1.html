Statement

Let A,B be Hermitian d\times d complex matrices, d\ge1, and let e^A denote the matrix exponential. With \|X\|_{op}=\sup_{\|v\|_2=1}\|Xv\|_2, one has \|e^{A+B}\|_{op}\le\|e^{A/2}e^Be^{A/2}\|_{op}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Hermitian matrices; Matrix exponentials; Finite-dimensional operator norm. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Take the limit of the dyadic Schatten norms

Use the dyadic Schatten inequality \|e^{A+B}\|_{2^N}\le\|e^{A/2}e^Be^{A/2}\|_{2^N}. For each fixed finite matrix the Schatten p norm tends to its largest singular value, the operator norm, as p\to\infty. Take N\to\infty.
