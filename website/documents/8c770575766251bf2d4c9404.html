Statement

Let P,Q be probability measures on R with finite first absolute moments and P≪Q. Put D(P\|Q)=\int\log(dP/dQ)\,dP\in[0,\infty], m=\int x\,P(dx), M_Q(\theta)=\int e^{\theta x}\,Q(dx) and \Psi_Q=\log M_Q, allowing infinity. Then D(P\|Q)\geq\Psi_Q^*(m), where \Psi_Q^*(m)=\sup_{\theta:M_Q(\theta)<\infty}\{\theta m-\log M_Q(\theta)\}.

Proof 1: Tilt the reference measure and apply Gibbs’ inequality

If D(P\|Q)=\infty, the assertion is immediate. Otherwise the added logarithmic-density integral below is finite by the first absolute moment hypothesis.

Let P and Q be probability distributions (measures) on the real line, whose first moments exist, and such that P << Q. For real \theta with M_Q(\theta)<\infty, consider the natural exponential family of Q given by

Q_\theta(A)=\frac{\int_A e^{\theta x}\,Q(dx)}{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{\theta x}\,Q(dx)} =\frac{1}{M_Q(\theta)}\int_A e^{\theta x}\,Q(dx)

for every measurable set A, where {\displaystyle M_{Q}} is the moment-generating function of Q. (Note that Q0 = Q.) The density is strictly positive, so P\ll Q_\theta. By the Radon–Nikodym chain rule,

{\displaystyle D(P\parallel Q)=D(P\parallel Q_{\theta })+\int _{\operatorname {supp} P}\left(\log {\frac {\mathrm {d} Q_{\theta }}{\mathrm {d} Q}}\right)\mathrm {d} P.}

By Gibbs' inequality we have {\displaystyle D(P\parallel Q_{\theta })\geq 0} so that

{\displaystyle D(P\parallel Q)\geq \int _{\operatorname {supp} P}\left(\log {\frac {\mathrm {d} Q_{\theta }}{\mathrm {d} Q}}\right)\mathrm {d} P=\int _{\operatorname {supp} P}\left(\log {\frac {e^{\theta x}}{M_{Q}(\theta )}}\right)P(dx)}

Simplifying the right side, we have, for every real θ where {\displaystyle M_{Q}(\theta )<\infty :}

{\displaystyle D(P\parallel Q)\geq m\theta -\Psi _{Q}(\theta ),}

where m is the first moment, or mean, of P, and {\displaystyle \Psi _{Q}=\log M_{Q}} is called the cumulant-generating function. Taking the supremum completes the process of convex conjugation and yields the rate function:

{\displaystyle D(P\parallel Q)\geq \sup _{\theta:M_Q(\theta)<\infty}\left\{m\theta -\Psi _{Q}(\theta )\right\}=\Psi _{Q}^{*}(m).}
