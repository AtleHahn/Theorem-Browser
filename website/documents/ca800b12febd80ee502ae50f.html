Statement

Let S be a nonsingular projective algebraic surface over \mathbb C. Suppose there is a morphism \varphi:S\to\mathbb P^1_{\mathbb C} whose general fiber is isomorphic to \mathbb P^1_{\mathbb C}. Then S is rational: it is birationally equivalent to \mathbb P^2_{\mathbb C}. An algebraic surface is an integral variety of complex dimension two; nonsingular means smooth. A general fiber being a projective line means that the fibers over all points of some nonempty Zariski-open subset of the base are projective lines. Birational equivalence means that dense Zariski-open subsets of the two varieties are isomorphic.
