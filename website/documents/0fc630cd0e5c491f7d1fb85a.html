Statement

On a measure space (S,\Sigma,\mu) let measurable f_n:S\to\mathbb R converge pointwise to f, and suppose |f_n|\le g for a nonnegative integrable g. Then f is integrable and \int_S f_n\,d\mu\to\int_Sf\,d\mu.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Abstract measure integration and measurable limit functions exceed the calculus/linear-algebra baseline.

Proof 1: Specialize the Fatou–Lebesgue chain to a pointwise limit

Use the Fatou–Lebesgue inequalities \int\liminf f_n\le\liminf\int f_n\le\limsup\int f_n\le\int\limsup f_n valid under the stated domination, together with integrability of the two pointwise limits. Pointwise convergence makes both outer integrals equal to \int f, so all inequalities become equalities.

Level justification

Apply the Fatou–Lebesgue chain and identify its two pointwise limits. General measure-space integration and domination are substantive prerequisites even though squeezing the integrals is short.
