Statement

The functions e_n(x)=e^{2\pi inx} for n\in\mathbb Z form an orthonormal basis of complex L^2([0,1]) with Lebesgue measure.

Proof 1: Pass from uniform density to an L2 basis — outline

By the complex Stone–Weierstrass theorem, the set of linear combinations of functions e_n(x)=e^{2\pi inx}, n\in\mathbb Z, is dense in C([0,1]/\{0,1\}), where we identify the endpoints of the interval [0,1] to obtain a circle. An important consequence of this is that the e_n are an orthonormal basis of the space L^2([0,1]) of square-integrable functions on [0,1].

Here the passage to L^2 uses the density of continuous periodic functions in that space and the orthonormality of the exponentials. The source states this consequence without further proof.
