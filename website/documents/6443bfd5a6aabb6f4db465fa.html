Statement

Let (M,g) be a connected smooth finite-dimensional Riemannian manifold without boundary; g assigns a positive-definite inner product smoothly to each tangent space. Give M its Riemannian distance, the infimum of lengths L(\gamma)=\int\sqrt{g(\dot\gamma,\dot\gamma)}\,dt of piecewise smooth curves joining the endpoints. The following are equivalent: every closed bounded subset of M is compact; every Cauchy sequence for this distance converges in M; every geodesic extends to all real values of its affine parameter. Here a geodesic satisfies \nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma=0 for the Levi-Civita connection of g (the unique torsion-free connection preserving g). Under these equivalent conditions, any two points of M are joined by a geodesic whose length equals their distance.
