Statement

Let G be a finite group and let BG be a CW model of its classifying space. Write BG^{(k)} for its k-skeleton and BG^{(k)}_+ for that skeleton with a disjoint basepoint. Let \pi_S^0(Y) denote degree-zero stable cohomotopy of a based space Y, that is, maps from its suspension spectrum to the sphere spectrum in the stable homotopy category, with its usual cohomotopy ring structure.

Let A(G) be the Burnside ring: the Grothendieck group of finite G-sets under disjoint union, with multiplication induced by Cartesian product with diagonal G-action. Its augmentation \epsilon:A(G)\to\mathbb Z sends a finite G-set to its cardinality. Put I=\ker\epsilon and

\widehat{A(G)}_I=\varprojlim_{r\ge1}A(G)/I^r.

There is an isomorphism of rings

\varprojlim_{k\ge0}\pi_S^0(BG^{(k)}_+)\ \cong\ \widehat{A(G)}_I,

where the maps in the left inverse system are restrictions along skeleton inclusions. This is the skeletal, weak formulation of Segal’s conjecture explicitly stated in the source.
