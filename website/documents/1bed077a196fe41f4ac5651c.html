Statement

Let E be an elliptic curve over Q. If the abelian group E(Q) is finitely generated, then E(Q)/2E(Q) is finite.

Proof 1: Reduce generator coefficients modulo two

Choose a finite list of generators P₁,…,P_r. Modulo 2E(Q), an integer coefficient on each generator can be reduced modulo 2. Thus every quotient class has a representative ∑_{i=1}^r ε_iP_i with ε_i∈{0,1}. There are only finitely many such representatives. This unpacks the source observation that finiteness of the quotient is necessary for finite generation; it does not prove the converse or Mordell’s theorem.
