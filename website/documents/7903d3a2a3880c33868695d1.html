Statement

Let f:I\to\mathbb R be continuous on a real interval. Then f is convex if and only if, for every pair of integers n\ge3, 2\le k\le n-1, and every x_1,\ldots,x_n\in I,

\frac1k\binom{n-2}{k-2}\left(\frac{n-k}{k-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f(x_i)+n f\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n x_i\right)\right)\ge\sum_{1\le i_1<\cdots<i_k\le n} f\left(\frac1k\sum_{j=1}^k x_{i_j}\right).

The right side sums over all k-element subsets of \{1,\ldots,n\}. Convexity means f((1-t)a+tb)\le(1-t)f(a)+tf(b) for a,b\in I and t\in[0,1].
