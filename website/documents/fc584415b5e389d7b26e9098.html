Statement

Let (X_i) be an open cover of a topological space A, and let B be a topological space. If f:A\to B restricts continuously to each X_i, then f is continuous.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic topology and real analysis supply covers, continuity, compactness and uniform convergence. Local finiteness and the uniform norm are explicitly described; relative compactness means compact closure in that familiar metric function space.

Proof 1: Preimages and unions

For an open U\subseteq B, the relative preimage f^{-1}(U)\cap X_i is open in X_i and hence open in A. The union of these preimages is f^{-1}(U), which is therefore open.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Take preimages in each open subspace and unite them; relative openness in an open subset is ambient openness. Undergraduate point-set topology suffices.
