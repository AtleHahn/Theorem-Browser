Statement

Let P(z)=\sum_{j=0}^n a_jz^j, n\ge1, with a_0a_n\ne0, and put c=(a_n/a_0,a_{n-1}/a_0,\ldots,a_1/a_0). For a finite complex vector v, write \|v\|_p=(\sum_j|v_j|^p)^{1/p} for 1\le p<\infty and \|v\|_\infty=\max_j|v_j|. For conjugate exponents 1\le p,q\le\infty, with 1/p+1/q=1, put R_p(v)=\|(1,\|v\|_p)\|_q. Thus R_1(v)=\max(1,\|v\|_1) and R_\infty(v)=1+\|v\|_\infty. Every root \zeta satisfies |\zeta|\ge1/R_p(c) for every 1\le p\le\infty.

Proof 1: Apply the root bound to the reversed polynomial

No root is zero, since a_0\ne0. If P(\zeta)=0, then 1/\zeta is a root of Q(z)=z^nP(1/z)=a_0z^n+a_1z^{n-1}+\cdots+a_n. The coefficient norm upper bound, after dividing Q by a_0, gives |1/\zeta|\le R_p(c).
