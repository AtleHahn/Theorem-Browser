Statement

Let u be a compactly supported distribution on \mathbb R. In the sense of distributions, Hu=D\bigl(u*(\pi^{-1}\log|\cdot|)\bigr), where Hu=u*\operatorname{p.v.}(1/(\pi t)).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Distributions; Convolution; Distributional derivatives. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Logarithmic-kernel representation of the Hilbert transform — proof

The distributional derivative of \pi^{-1}\log|t| is \operatorname{p.v.}(1/(\pi t)). Moving the derivative outside the convolution gives the displayed identity.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Differentiate the logarithmic kernel as a distribution and commute differentiation with convolution. Distributional derivatives, principal values and distribution convolution are graduate analysis.
