Statement

For nonnegative real a,b,c,d, \frac{a+b+c+d}{4}\ge\sqrt{\frac{ab+ac+ad+bc+bd+cd}{6}}\ge\sqrt[3]{\frac{abc+abd+acd+bcd}{4}}\ge\sqrt[4]{abcd}. Equality throughout holds exactly when a=b=c=d.

Proof 1: Specialize the symmetric averages — outline

Use the stated Maclaurin theorem: for nonnegative a_1,\ldots,a_n, put S_k=\binom nk^{-1}\sum_{i_1<\cdots<i_k}a_{i_1}\cdots a_{i_k}. Then S_1\ge S_2^{1/2}\ge\cdots\ge S_n^{1/n}, with equality throughout exactly when all a_i are equal.

Take n=4 in the stated theorem. The numbers of singleton, pair, triple and quadruple products are 4,6,4,1. Thus the four averages are (a+b+c+d)/4, (ab+ac+ad+bc+bd+cd)/6, (abc+abd+acd+bcd)/4 and abcd. Substitution gives the displayed chain and its equality condition.
