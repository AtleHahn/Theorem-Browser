Statement

Let X be proper over a noetherian scheme S, with irreducible components X_1,\ldots,X_r. For each i put V_i=X\setminus\bigcup_{j\ne i}X_j. There is a closed subscheme Y_i\subseteq X, proper over S, with underlying space X_i and restriction Y_i|_{V_i}=V_i. In particular Y_i is irreducible and agrees with X on the open part belonging only to X_i.

Proof 1: Proper irreducible models of the components of a proper scheme — outline

Let X_1,\ldots,X_r be the irreducible components of X, which are finite because X is noetherian. Put V_i=X\setminus\bigcup_{j\ne i}X_j and let Y_i be the scheme-theoretic image of V_i\hookrightarrow X. This immersion is quasi-compact since X is noetherian. Its scheme-theoretic image can be computed affine-locally: its underlying closed set is X_i, and its restriction to V_i is V_i itself. As a closed subscheme of the proper S-scheme X, each Y_i is proper over S.
