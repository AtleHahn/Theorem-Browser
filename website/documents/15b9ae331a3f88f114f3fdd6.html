Statement

Let D be a nonempty bounded connected open subset of a complex Banach space X. For z\in D,v\in X, set \alpha(z,v)=\sup_g|g^{\prime}(z)v|, where g:D\to\{\zeta\in\mathbb C:|\zeta|<1\} is holomorphic. Define \ell(\gamma)=\int_0^1\alpha(\gamma(t),\dot\gamma(t))\,dt for piecewise differentiable curves in D, and d(x,y)=\inf_{\gamma(0)=x,\gamma(1)=y}\ell(\gamma). Let f:D\to D be holomorphic with \operatorname{dist}(f(D),X\setminus D)>0. Then some \delta>0 satisfies d(f(x),f(y))\le(1+\delta)^{-1}d(x,y) for all x,y\in D.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The assertion involves a holomorphic self-map and an intrinsic length metric on a Banach domain.

Proof 1: Perturb the map outward from a chosen image point — outline

The chain rule

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {(g\circ f)^{\prime }(z)=g^{\prime }(f(z))f^{\prime }(z)}}

implies that

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\alpha (f(z),f^{\prime }(z)v)\leq \alpha (z,v)}}

and hence f satisfies the following generalization of the Schwarz-Pick inequality:

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {d(f(x),f(y))\leq d(x,y).}}

For δ sufficiently small and y fixed in D, the same inequality can be applied to the holomorphic mapping

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {f_{\delta ,y}(z)=f(z)+\delta (f(z)-f(y))}}

and yields the improved estimate:

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {d(f(x),f(y))\leq (1+\delta )^{-1}d(x,y).}}

Level justification

The proof manipulates Frechet-holomorphic maps on complex Banach spaces and the infinitesimal and path forms of an intrinsic complex metric. This is substantial infinite-dimensional complex analysis.

Routine omissions

Boundedness of f(D) and its positive boundary margin give one small delta valid for every y. At the point z=y, f_delta,y(y)=f(y) and the derivative is (1+delta)fprime(y); homogeneity of alpha then yields the infinitesimal strict bound. Integration over curves is routine. The source supplies the perturbation and conclusion but suppresses these checks.
