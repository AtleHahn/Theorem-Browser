Statement

Let \mathcal F be a sheaf of abelian groups on a topological space X, and let \mathcal F^\bullet be an acyclic resolution of \mathcal F (each term has zero higher sheaf cohomology on X). For every integer q\ge0, H^q(X,\mathcal F)\cong H^q(\mathcal F^\bullet(X)), where the right-hand side is the cohomology of the complex of global sections.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Sheaves of abelian groups; Sheaf cohomology; Acyclic resolutions; Cohomology of a complex. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Specialize the acyclic-resolution theorem to global sections

Apply the general theorem that derived functors may be computed using acyclic resolutions, rather than only injective resolutions, to the global-sections functor. Its right-derived functors are sheaf cohomology, so the cohomology of the acyclic resolution's global-sections complex gives the displayed groups.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof specializes the acyclic-resolution theorem for derived functors to global sections. Sheaves, cohomology, resolutions and derived functors are substantial homological-algebra prerequisites.
