Statement

For every finite group H, there are only finitely many isomorphism classes of finite simple groups G for which some element t\in G of order 2 has centralizer C_G(t)=\{g\in G:gt=tg\} isomorphic to H. A simple group is a nontrivial group with no proper nontrivial normal subgroup.
