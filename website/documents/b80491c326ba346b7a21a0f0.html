Statement

Let X_i be iid real random variables with probability density f, and let C=(a-h,a+h] for fixed a\in\mathbb R and h>0. If N_n(C)=\#\{1\le i\le n:X_i\in C\}, then N_n(C)/n\longrightarrow\int_{a-h}^{a+h}f(x)\,dx almost surely.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The count and density integral are explicit; the full assertion also requires iid infinite sampling and almost-sure convergence on the joint sample space. Without assuming a probability elective, their combined preparation is not confidently within the allowance. Retain Beyond undergraduate conservatively rather than assessing only the integral. Proof 1: Apply the event-frequency law to an interval

Apply Borel’s event-frequency law to the events X_i\in C. Their common probability is P(X_1\in C)=\int_{a-h}^{a+h}f(x)\,dx, so the relative frequency converges to this integral.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification
