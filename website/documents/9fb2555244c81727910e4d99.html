Statement

For each prime p, let \mathbb C_p denote the completion of an algebraic closure of the p-adic field \mathbb Q_p. As abstract fields, \mathbb C_p and \mathbb C are isomorphic. No compatibility with their topologies is asserted.

Proof 1: Applying categoricity at the continuum — outline

We use the uncountable categoricity of the theory of algebraically closed fields of a given characteristic. The fields \mathbb C_p and \mathbb C are algebraically closed of characteristic 0 and have the same uncountable cardinality.

Categoricity does not say that all algebraically closed fields of characteristic 0 as large as the complex numbers \mathbb C are the same as \mathbb C; it only asserts that they are isomorphic as fields to \mathbb C. It follows that the completed p-adic closures \mathbb C_p are all isomorphic as fields to \mathbb C. The structural and cardinality facts about \mathbb C_p are used here without proof.
