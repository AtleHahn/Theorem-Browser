Proof 1: Apply the ratio-to-root theorem to n!/n^n

Apply the ratio-to-root limit theorem to the positive sequence y_n=n!/n^n:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\lim _{n\to \infty }{\frac {\sqrt[{n}]{n!}}{n}}&=\lim _{n\to \infty }{\frac {(n+1)!(n^{n})}{n!(n+1)^{n+1}}}\\&=\lim _{n\to \infty }{\frac {n^{n}}{(n+1)^{n}}}=\lim _{n\to \infty }{\frac {1}{(1+{\frac {1}{n}})^{n}}}={\frac {1}{e}}\end{aligned}}}

where we used the representation of {\displaystyle e} as the limit of a sequence.
