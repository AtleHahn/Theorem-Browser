Statement

For every pair of positive integers r,k, there is a positive integer N such that every coloring c:\{1,\ldots,N\}\to\{1,\ldots,r\} has a monochromatic arithmetic progression of length k. Explicitly, there are positive integers a,d with a+(k-1)d\le N and

c(a)=c(a+d)=\cdots=c(a+(k-1)d).
