Proof 1: Apply the prescribed quotient method — outline

All derivatives of circular trigonometric functions can be found from those of \sin(x) and \cos(x) by means of the quotient rule. Here apply it to \cot x=\cos x/\sin x, using

(\sin x)'=\cos x,\qquad(\cos x)'=-\sin x.

This gives (\cot x)'=-\csc^2x, where \csc x=1/\sin x. The source leaves this individual substitution and simplification unwritten.
