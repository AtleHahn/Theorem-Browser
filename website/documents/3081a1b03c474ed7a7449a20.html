Statement

Let (\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P) carry a filtration (\mathcal F_n)_{n\in I}, where I=\{0,\ldots,N\} or I=\mathbb N_0. Let X=M+A be the Doob decomposition of an adapted integrable real process, with A_0=0. Then X is a submartingale exactly when A is almost surely nondecreasing; it is a supermartingale exactly when A is almost surely nonincreasing. Here a Doob decomposition means a martingale M plus an integrable predictable process A starting at zero; predictability means A_n is measurable with respect to the preceding sigma algebra.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the equivalence requires martingale and sub/supermartingale definitions on a filtered probability space.

Proof 1: Read the sign of each conditional drift increment

In the Doob construction,

A_n=\sum_{k=1}^n\bigl(\mathbb E[X_k\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}]-X_{k-1}\bigr).

If X is a submartingale, then

\mathbb E[X_k\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}]\geq X_{k-1}\quad\text{a.s.}

for all k\in I\setminus\{0\}, which is equivalent to saying that every term in the displayed definition of A is almost surely nonnegative, hence A is almost surely nondecreasing. The equivalence for supermartingales is proved similarly.

Level justification
