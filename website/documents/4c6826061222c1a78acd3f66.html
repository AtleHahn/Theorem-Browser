Statement

The Prüfer surface cannot be endowed with a Riemann surface structure compatible with its topology.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses a named nonmetrizable surface and complex surface structures without elementary local definitions.

Proof 1: Apply the second-countability obstruction

The Prüfer surface has no countable base for its topology. Radó’s theorem requires every connected Riemann surface to have such a base. Thus a compatible Riemann surface structure would contradict this property of the Prüfer surface.
