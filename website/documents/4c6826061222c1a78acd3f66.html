Proof 1: Apply the second-countability obstruction

The Prüfer surface has no countable base for its topology. Radó’s theorem requires every connected Riemann surface to have such a base. Thus a compatible Riemann surface structure would contradict this property of the Prüfer surface.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Rado second-countability to the specialized non-second-countable surface. Understanding the Prufer construction and compatible complex manifold charts is not supplied and exceeds a confidently small extension of point-set topology; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
