Statement

Fix T>0. Let C_0 be the Banach space of continuous real paths on [0,T] starting at 0, with the supremum norm. Equip it with Wiener measure \gamma, the law of standard Brownian motion, and write B_t(\sigma)=\sigma(t) and \mathcal F_t=\sigma(B_s:0\le s\le t). Let H consist of absolutely continuous paths h with h(0)=0 and \int_0^T|\dot h(t)|^2dt<\infty, with inner product \langle h,k\rangle_H=\int_0^T\dot h\dot k\,dt. Let F:C_0\to\mathbb R be bounded and Fréchet differentiable with bounded derivative DF:C_0\to C_0^*. Let V:C_0\to H be a measurable vector field such that \dot V_t is adapted and Itô integrable, with \mathbb E_\gamma\int_0^T|\dot V_t|^2dt<\infty. Then \mathbb E_\gamma[DF(V)]=\mathbb E_\gamma\left[F\int_0^T\dot V_t\,dB_t\right]. Equivalently, defining \operatorname{div}V=-\int_0^T\dot V_t\,dB_t and \nabla_HF by \langle\nabla_HF,h\rangle_H=DF[h], one has \mathbb E_\gamma\langle\nabla_HF,V\rangle_H=-\mathbb E_\gamma[F\operatorname{div}V].
