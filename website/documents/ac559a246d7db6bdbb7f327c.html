Statement

Let K be a number field, L its Hilbert class field, and I any ideal of its ring of integers \mathcal O_K. Then the extended ideal I\mathcal O_L is principal: there is \alpha\in\mathcal O_L with

I\mathcal O_L=\alpha\mathcal O_L.

A number field is a finite extension of \mathbb Q, and its ring of integers consists of elements satisfying monic polynomials over \mathbb Z. The Hilbert class field is the maximal abelian extension unramified at all finite and infinite places: finite prime ideals have ramification index one, and real places remain real. An abelian extension is Galois with abelian Galois group. The extended ideal consists of finite sums of products of elements of I and \mathcal O_L. The zero ideal is included, with \alpha=0.
