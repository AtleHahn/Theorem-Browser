Statement

Let S^3=\{x\in\mathbb R^4:\|x\|=1\} with its induced round metric. Every smooth embedded minimal torus in S^3 is the image under an isometry of S^3 of the Clifford torus \left\{\frac1{\sqrt2}(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,\cos\varphi,\sin\varphi):\theta,\varphi\in\mathbb R\right\}. A torus means a surface diffeomorphic to S^1\times S^1; minimal means its mean curvature vector in S^3 vanishes everywhere, that is, the trace of its second fundamental form is zero. The second fundamental form is the normal component within S^3 of the ambient covariant derivative of tangent vector fields.
