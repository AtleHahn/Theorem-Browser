Statement

For a based path-connected space X, write \pi_j(X) for based homotopy classes of maps S^j\to X, and H_j(X;R) for singular homology with coefficients R. The Hurewicz map sends [f] to f_*[S^j], where [S^j] is the fundamental homology class of the oriented sphere. The degree-one Hurewicz map induces an isomorphism \pi_1(X)/[\pi_1(X),\pi_1(X)]\cong H_1(X;\mathbb Z), where [G,G] is the subgroup generated by all commutators aba^{-1}b^{-1}.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although abelianization is elementary algebra, interpreting singular homology and its relation to loop classes requires substantial topology preparation.
