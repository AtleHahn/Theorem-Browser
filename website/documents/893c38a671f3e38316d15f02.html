Statement

Let m\ge1 be an integer, 1\le p\le\infty, and P_{m-1} the real polynomials of total degree at most m-1. For a bounded domain \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n, use the Sobolev seminorm |u|_{W_p^k}=(\sum_{|\alpha|=k}\|D^\alpha u\|_p^p)^{1/p} for finite p, and \max_{|\alpha|=k}\|D^\alpha u\|_\infty for p=\infty; \alpha is a multiindex and |\alpha| its sum. Suppose \Omega is a bounded Lipschitz domain. For every continuous linear functional \ell on W_p^m(\Omega) vanishing on P_{m-1} and every u\in W_p^m(\Omega), |\ell(u)|\le C(m,\Omega)\|\ell\|_{(W_p^m)^\prime}|u|_{W_p^m}, where the dual norm uses the usual Sobolev norm.

Proof 1: Apply the polynomial approximation bound in the dual norm outline

Let m\ge1 be an integer, 1\le p\le\infty, and P_{m-1} the real polynomials of total degree at most m-1. For a bounded domain \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n, use the Sobolev seminorm |u|_{W_p^k}=(\sum_{|\alpha|=k}\|D^\alpha u\|_p^p)^{1/p} for finite p, and \max_{|\alpha|=k}\|D^\alpha u\|_\infty for p=\infty; \alpha is a multiindex and |\alpha| its sum.

Use the Bramble–Hilbert approximation theorem: for a bounded Lipschitz domain \Omega of diameter d, and u\in W_p^m(\Omega), a polynomial v\in P_{m-1} satisfies |u-v|_{W_p^k}\le C(m,\Omega)d^{m-k}|u|_{W_p^m} for 0\le k\le m.

The linear-functional bound follows immediately from the Bramble–Hilbert approximation theorem.

Suppose that {\displaystyle \textstyle \ell } is a continuous linear functional on {\displaystyle \textstyle W_{p}^{m}(\Omega )} and {\displaystyle \textstyle \left\Vert \ell \right\Vert _{W_{p}^{m}(\Omega )^{^{\prime }}}} its dual norm. Suppose that {\displaystyle \textstyle \ell \left(v\right)=0} for all {\displaystyle \textstyle v\in P_{m-1}}. Then there exists a constant {\displaystyle \textstyle C=C\left(m,\Omega \right)} such that

{\displaystyle \left\vert \ell \left(u\right)\right\vert \leq C\left\Vert \ell \right\Vert _{W_{p}^{m}(\Omega )^{^{\prime }}}\left\vert u\right\vert _{W_{p}^{m}(\Omega )}.}
