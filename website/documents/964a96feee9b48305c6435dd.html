Statement

For D_N(t)=\sum_{k=-N}^Ne^{ikt}, \frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}|D_N(t)|\,dt\longrightarrow\infty.

Level

Level justification

Proof 1: Sine-integral lower bound — outline

Use the Dirichlet kernel identity D_N(t)=\sin((N+1/2)t)/\sin(t/2), with removable values at multiples of 2\pi. The source gives the estimate

\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}|D_N(t)|\,dt \geq\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{|\sin((N+1/2)t)|}{t/2}\,dt\longrightarrow\infty.
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the explicit finite trigonometric-sum identity, compare sine(t/2) with t/2, and lower-bound the absolute-sine integral over successive periods by a harmonic sum. Ordinary calculus and series suffice despite the L1 terminology.
