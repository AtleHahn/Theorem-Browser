Statement

Let S be a semiring of subsets of a set X: \varnothing\in S, intersections of two members lie in S, and each difference of two members is a finite disjoint union of members. A content on S is a function \mu:S\to[0,\infty] with \mu(\varnothing)=0 which is finitely additive whenever a disjoint finite union is in S. Let R(S) be the ring generated by S (closed under finite unions and differences). Then \mu has a unique content extension \bar\mu to R(S), given on any finite disjoint decomposition A=\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i with A_i\in S by \bar\mu(A)=\sum_{i=1}^n\mu(A_i).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Only finite set operations and finite additivity are used. The semiring of sets, generated ring and content are explicitly defined, requiring no sigma-additive integration theory.

Proof 1: Sum the content over finite disjoint pieces (outline)
