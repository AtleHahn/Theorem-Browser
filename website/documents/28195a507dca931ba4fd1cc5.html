Statement

For the rotational icosahedral symmetry group I of the compound of five tetrahedra, let T stabilize one chosen tetrahedron. The cosets I/T correspond to its images under rotations, and there are five such cosets.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies rotational symmetries, stabilizers, cosets and orbits. The compound consists of the five stated tetrahedra, so the claim is an orbit-coset count on a concrete finite configuration.

Proof 1: Coset count for the five-tetrahedra compound

In the compound of five tetrahedra, the symmetry group is the (rotational) icosahedral group I of order 60, while the stabilizer of a single chosen tetrahedron is the (rotational) tetrahedral group T of order 12. By the orbit–stabilizer theorem, the coset space I/T (of order 60/12=5) is naturally identified with the 5 tetrahedra—the coset gT corresponds to the tetrahedron to which g sends the chosen tetrahedron.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the supplied group and stabilizer orders and identify images with stabilizer cosets. Cosets and orbit-stabilizer are introductory group theory; the numerical quotient alone is elementary.

2. Finite orbit–stabilizer formula
Kind:

Theorem

Treatment:

The orbit of an object under a finite group is in bijection with stabilizer cosets, and its size is the group order divided by stabilizer order.
