Statement

Let G be a connected compact semisimple Lie group, T a maximal torus and W its Weyl group. For an irreducible representation with dominant highest weight \lambda, let \rho be half the sum of the positive roots and set v=\lambda+\rho. On regular elements of T, its character is

\chi_\lambda=\frac{\sum_{w\in W}\det(w)e^{w(v)}}{\prod_{(v,\alpha)>0}(e^{\alpha/2}-e^{-\alpha/2})},

where the product is over roots and exponentials have the usual torus-weight meaning (interpreted on its Lie algebra if individual half-weights do not descend).

Beyond undergraduate level

Proof 1: Set the maximal compact subgroup equal to the group — outline

Let K denote a maximal compact subgroup and W_K its Weyl group relative to T. Use the Harish-Chandra discrete-series character formula on regular elements of T:

(-1)^{(\dim G-\dim K)/2}\frac{\sum_{w\in W_K}\det(w)e^{w(v)}}{\prod_{(v,\alpha)>0}(e^{\alpha/2}-e^{-\alpha/2})}.

When G is compact this reduces to the stated formula with v=\lambda+\rho.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Specializing K=G simplifies the formula, but understanding highest weights, roots, Weyl groups, torus characters and discrete-series parameters requires advanced Lie representation theory.
