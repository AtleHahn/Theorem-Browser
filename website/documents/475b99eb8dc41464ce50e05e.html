Statement

Let m\ge2, u_1,\ldots,u_m\in\mathbb C, and let x_1,\ldots,x_m\in\mathbb R be distinct modulo integers. Define \|t\|_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}=\min_{k\in\mathbb Z}|t-k| and \delta=\min_{r\ne s}\|x_r-x_s\|_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}>0. Then \left|\sum_{r\ne s}u_r\overline{u_s}\csc(\pi(x_r-x_s))\right|\le\delta^{-1}\sum_r|u_r|^2. More generally, for any 0<\delta_r\le\min_{s\ne r}\|x_r-x_s\|_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}, \left|\sum_{r\ne s}u_r\overline{u_s}\csc(\pi(x_r-x_s))\right|\le\frac32\sum_r|u_r|^2\delta_r^{-1}. Here \csc t=1/\sin t and sums range over 1\le r,s\le m.
