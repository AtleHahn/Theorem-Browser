Statement

Let P lie at distance h>0 from a baseline, and let a sequence of distinct rays from P meet it in increasing order. Write \gamma_n\in(-\pi/2,\pi/2) for their signed angles to the perpendicular toward the line and define \theta_n by \tan\gamma_n=\sinh\theta_n. The triangles between consecutive rays have equal inradii if and only if \theta_n=a+nb for constants a\in\mathbb R and b>0. Their common radius satisfies r/(h-r)=\tanh(b/2).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves plane ray configurations; incircles; hyperbolic functions. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Similarity and hyperbolic addition formulas — outline

Let PR=h be the perpendicular distance to the baseline. Lines PS and PT are adjacent rays making angles {\displaystyle \gamma _{n}} and {\displaystyle \gamma _{n+1}} with line PR, which is perpendicular to the baseline, RST.

The line through O, meeting PR, PS and PT at Q, X and Y respectively, is parallel to the baseline and passes through O, the center of the incircle of {\displaystyle \triangle } PST, which is tangent to the rays at W and Z. Also, line PQ has length {\displaystyle h-r}, and line QR has length {\displaystyle r}, the radius of the incircle.

For nondegenerate auxiliary triangles, {\displaystyle \triangle } OWX is similar to {\displaystyle \triangle } PQX and {\displaystyle \triangle } OZY is similar to {\displaystyle \triangle } PQY, and from XY = XO + OY we get

{\displaystyle (h-r)(\tan \gamma _{n+1}-\tan \gamma _{n})=r(\sec \gamma _{n}+\sec \gamma _{n+1}).}

This relation on a set of angles, {\displaystyle \{\gamma _{m}\}}, expresses the condition of equal incircles.

To obtain the parameterization, we set {\displaystyle \tan \gamma _{n}=\sinh(a+nb)}, which gives {\displaystyle \sec \gamma _{n}=\cosh(a+nb)}.

Using {\displaystyle a+(n+1)b=(a+nb)+b}, we apply the addition rules for {\displaystyle \sinh } and {\displaystyle \cosh }, and verify that the equal incircles relation is satisfied by setting

{\displaystyle {\frac {r}{h-r}}=\tanh {\frac {b}{2}}.}

This gives an expression for the parameter {\displaystyle b} in terms of the geometric measures, {\displaystyle h} and {\displaystyle r}.
