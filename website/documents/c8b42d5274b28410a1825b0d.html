Statement

Let X\ge0 be a random variable with 0<m=\mathbb E[X]<\infty. For t>0, \mathbb P(X\ge tm)\le1/t.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The only additional notions are a scalar probability law and its integral mean. Positivity and finiteness are explicit and no convergence theory is involved.

Mathematical Field

Proof 1: Substitute a positive multiple of the mean

When 0<\operatorname E(X)<\infty, we can take a=t\cdot\operatorname E(X) for t>0 to rewrite Markov’s inequality as

\operatorname P(X\geq t\cdot\operatorname E(X))\leq\frac1t.
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Insert the positive mean-scaled threshold and cancel the mean. Undergraduate elementary probability suffices for the expectation and tail assertion.
