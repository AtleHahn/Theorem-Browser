Statement

Every peripheral cycle of a finite chordal graph has length three; equivalently, every chordal graph is strangulated.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The companion does not define chordal or peripheral cycles: a peripheral cycle must be induced and nonseparating, and chordal excludes induced cycles of length greater than three. Learning these finite graph definitions and a small example fits the undergraduate preparation allowance. Proof 1: Peripheral cycles of chordal graphs are triangles

Every chordal graph is a strangulated graph, a graph in which every peripheral cycle is a triangle, because peripheral cycles are a special case of induced cycles.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the supplied fact that every peripheral cycle is induced, then the definition of chordal makes its length three. Understanding the complete statement also requires the omitted definitions of peripheral cycle and chordal graph. Learning these finite graph notions is a short undergraduate preparation; proving a topological graph theorem or an algorithmic result is unnecessary.
