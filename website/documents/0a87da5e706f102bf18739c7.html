Statement

Let V be a vector space over a field and let G\subset V be a finite spanning set. Every linearly independent subset I\subset V is finite and satisfies |I|\le |G|.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and real analysis supply kernels, quotients, projections, sup norms and completeness. The index or operator bound is explicitly defined, and sequence-space examples use specified coordinate formulas. No dual or weak-topology theory is required.

Proof 1: Bound every finite independent subset

For a finite spanning set G, the Steinitz exchange lemma implies that every finite subset of an independent set I has size at most |G|. Hence I itself is finite and |I|\le |G|.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply finite Steinitz exchange to each finite independent subset; a set larger than the finite bound would contain a violating finite subset. This is standard undergraduate linear algebra.
