Statement

For a field K, the ring K[x]/(x^2) is Cohen–Macaulay but is not regular.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Polynomial quotient rings over a field; Cohen–Macaulay and regular rings. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Dimension zero and a nonzero nilpotent — outline

The ring K[x]/(x^2) has dimension zero and hence is Cohen–Macaulay. It is not reduced and therefore is not regular.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although the ring is concrete, the proof uses Krull dimension, Noetherian Cohen-Macaulay structure and regularity versus reducedness. These linked commutative-algebra concepts exceed introductory ring theory.
