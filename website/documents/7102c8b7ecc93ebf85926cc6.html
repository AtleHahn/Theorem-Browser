Statement

Let X,F be topological spaces and G a topological group acting continuously on F on the left. Let (U_i)_{i\in I} be an open cover of X. Suppose continuous maps t_{ij}:U_i\cap U_j\to G satisfy

t_{ik}(x)=t_{ij}(x)t_{jk}(x)

on every triple overlap, including repeated indices. There is a fiber bundle \pi:E\to X with fiber F and structure group G, with local trivializations over U_i and these transition functions. A local trivialization identifies \pi^{-1}(U_i) with U_i\times F over U_i; on overlaps the fiber coordinates change by the action of t_{ij}(x).

Suppose the action is faithful (only the identity acts as the identity on all of F). If E' has the same base, fiber, structure group and trivializing cover, and transition functions t'_{ij}, then E and E' are isomorphic as G-bundles over X if and only if there are continuous gauge maps t_i:U_i\to G with

t'_{ij}(x)=t_i(x)^{-1}t_{ij}(x)t_j(x).

Here a G-bundle isomorphism respects the base and the local G-structure. In particular the same transition data determine a bundle uniquely up to such isomorphism.

The corresponding construction and isomorphism statements hold in the smooth category, with X,F smooth manifolds, G a Lie group, and the action, transition functions, trivializations and gauge maps smooth.

Proof 1: Quotient gluing and local gauge transformations (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Section3.2, Theorem3.2.1, pp.20–22; Section1.3, pp.9–11).

In the continuity diagram, the map on the jth summand is (x,v)\mapsto(x,t_{ij}(x)v), not an inclusion. For an isomorphism with local G-valued maps h_i, compatibility is h_it_{ij}=t\prime_{ij}h_j; setting t_i=h_i^{-1} gives the displayed gauge criterion. Conversely these identities glue the local maps and their inverses. Smooth charts glue in the same way; a countable trivializing subcover gives second countability.
