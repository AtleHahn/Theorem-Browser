Statement

For rings with identity and two-sided ideals, the following global assertions are equivalent: (i) every nonzero ring has a maximal proper ideal; (ii) every proper ideal of every ring is contained in a maximal proper ideal.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory ring theory supplies ideals, prime/maximal ideals, products and inclusion. Noetherian means every ideal is finitely generated. The further conditions are short ideal-based definitions: height counts chains of primes, local means a unique maximal ideal, and primary uses powers in the defining product condition. The relevant definitions are a small extension of familiar ideals, without localization or scheme theory.

Proof 1: Zero ideal and quotient ring

Assuming (ii), take I=(0) in a nonzero ring to obtain (i). Conversely, given a proper ideal I of R, the quotient R/I is a nonzero ring. Apply (i) to R/I and use the ideal correspondence for a quotient to obtain a maximal proper ideal of R containing I.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply one assertion to the zero ideal and the other to a quotient, then transfer maximality through the ideal correspondence. Undergraduate abstract algebra supplies all required concepts.
