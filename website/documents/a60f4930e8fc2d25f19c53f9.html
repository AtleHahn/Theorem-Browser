Statement

No first-order theory with an infinite model has a unique model up to isomorphism, without restriction on cardinality.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

First-order satisfaction and cardinalities of models require model theory and set theory beyond the elementary baselines.

Proof 1: Apply the model-cardinality theorem

Use Löwenheim–Skolem: an infinite structure in signature \sigma has an elementarily equivalent structure of every infinite cardinality \kappa\ge|\sigma|, smaller ones as elementary substructures and larger ones as elementary extensions.

Given an infinite model M, choose an infinite cardinal \kappa larger than both |M| and the signature cardinality. Löwenheim–Skolem supplies an elementary extension N of size \kappa. It is another model of the same theory, but is not isomorphic to M because its cardinality differs.

Level justification

Use upward Lowenheim–Skolem to obtain an elementary extension of larger cardinality. The proof presupposes arbitrary signatures, first-order structures and elementary extensions together with infinite cardinal comparison; retain the advanced classification conservatively.
