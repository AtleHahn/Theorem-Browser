Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle, P an interior point, a=BC, b=CA, c=AB, r=PC, and let x,y,z be the perpendicular distances from P to the supporting lines BC,CA,AB, respectively. Then cr\ge ax+by.

Proof 1: Compare the area with base times the distance sum

The inequality cr\ge ax+by is equivalent to

\frac{c(r+z)}2\ge\frac{ax+by+cz}2.

The right side is the area of ABC. On the left, r+z is at least the height from C, so the inequality holds.
