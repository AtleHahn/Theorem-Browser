Statement

For any smooth Riemannian metric g on S^2, the Gaussian curvature K_g is not identically zero.

Proof 1: Integrate curvature using Gauss–Bonnet

Gauss–Bonnet gives \int_{S^2}K_g\,dA_g=4\pi. If K_g vanished identically, the integral would be zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Gauss-Bonnet to an arbitrary smooth metric on the sphere. Riemannian metrics, intrinsic Gaussian curvature and metric area integration form substantial differential geometry beyond the baseline.
