Statement

Let S=[0,1]^2 and let \mu be two-dimensional Lebesgue measure restricted to S, on its completed Lebesgue sigma-algebra. For x\in[0,1], put L_x=\{x\}\times[0,1]. Every subset E\subseteq L_x is measurable and has \mu(E)=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Completeness of a measure space, including arbitrary subsets of null sets, is a measure-theoretic prerequisite.

Proof 1: Use completeness of planar Lebesgue measure

Consider the one-dimensional subset of S given by the line segment L_x=\{x\}\times[0,1]. L_x has \mu-measure zero; every subset of L_x is a \mu-null set, since the Lebesgue measure space is a complete measure space:

E\subseteq L_x\implies\mu(E)=0.
