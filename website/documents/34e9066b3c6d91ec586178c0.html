Statement

Let k be a field and let Q be any orientation of one of the diagrams A_n,D_n,E_6,E_7,E_8. Sending a finite-dimensional indecomposable representation (V_i) to its dimension vector (\dim_k V_i)_i gives a bijection from the set of isomorphism classes of these representations to the set of positive roots of the diagram. Equivalently, each positive root occurs as the dimension vector of exactly one isomorphism class of indecomposable representation, and there are no others.

A quiver is a directed graph, allowing multiple arrows and loops. A finite-dimensional representation over a field k assigns a finite-dimensional k-vector space V_i to each vertex and a k-linear map V_i\to V_j to each arrow i\to j. An isomorphism is a family of vector-space isomorphisms at the vertices commuting with all arrow maps. Direct sums are taken at every vertex and arrow. A representation is indecomposable if it is nonzero and is not isomorphic to a direct sum of two nonzero representations.

The diagrams A_n (n\geq1) are paths with n vertices. The diagram D_n (n\geq4) is a tree with one vertex of degree three and three arms of lengths 1,1,n-3 edges. The trees E_6,E_7,E_8 have one vertex of degree three and arm lengths (1,2,2), (1,2,3), (1,2,4), respectively.

Here is an explicit definition of the roots used in the assertion. In the real vector space with basis (\alpha_i) indexed by the vertices, give the basis the symmetric bilinear form (\alpha_i,\alpha_i)=2, (\alpha_i,\alpha_j)=-1 when i,j are adjacent, and 0 otherwise. Let s_i(v)=v-(v,\alpha_i)\alpha_i. The roots are all images of the basis vectors under finite compositions of the maps s_i (including the empty composition). A root \sum_i d_i\alpha_i is positive when every coefficient d_i is nonnegative; its coefficient vector is identified with (d_i)_i in the dimension-vector correspondence.
