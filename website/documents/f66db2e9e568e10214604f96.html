Statement

Let k be a perfect field and G a smooth connected algebraic group over k. Then there is a unique smooth connected normal affine algebraic subgroup H\subseteq G for which G/H is an abelian variety. An algebraic group is an algebraic variety with regular multiplication and inverse maps; an abelian variety is a complete connected algebraic group. Affine means isomorphic to a closed subvariety of an affine space. A field is perfect if all its algebraic extensions are separable, equivalently it has characteristic zero or its Frobenius map x\mapsto x^p is surjective in characteristic p>0.
