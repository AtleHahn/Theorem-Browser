Statement

Every separable real normed vector space X admits a linear isometric embedding j:X\to C([0,1],\mathbb R), where C([0,1],\mathbb R) is the space of continuous real-valued functions on [0,1] with norm \|f\|_\infty=\max_{t\in[0,1]}|f(t)|. Thus \|j(x)\|_\infty=\|x\| for every x\in X. If X is a Banach space, its image is a closed linear subspace. Here separable means having a countable dense subset, and a Banach space is a normed vector space complete for its norm metric.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate linear algebra, real analysis and metric topology suffice for norms, countable density, completeness and a norm-preserving linear map. The statement requires no dual-space or weak-star theory.
