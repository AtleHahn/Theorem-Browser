Statement

Let K be any field and let I be an infinite set. Give the set K^I of all functions I\to K its vector-space structure by pointwise addition and scalar multiplication. Then

\dim_K(K^I)=|K^I|.

Here \dim_K means the cardinality of a vector-space basis (a basis in which each vector is a finite linear combination), and |X| denotes the cardinality of a set X.

Equivalently, if E is an infinite-dimensional vector space over K with basis indexed by I, then its algebraic dual E^*=\operatorname{Hom}_K(E,K) satisfies

\dim_K E^*=|K^I|.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate linear algebra and elementary set theory suffice. The algebraic basis and cardinal interpretation are stated locally; no topological duality is involved.
