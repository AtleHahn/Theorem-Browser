Statement

For a prime p, form the formal power series E_p(x)=\exp\!\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{p^j}}{p^j}\right)\in\mathbb Q[[x]],\qquad \exp(z)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{z^m}{m!}. Every coefficient of E_p(x), when written as a reduced rational number, has denominator not divisible by p. All series and compositions are interpreted formally, coefficient by coefficient.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate algebra and power series suffice; formal coefficientwise interpretation is a small extension without p-adic analysis.

Review notes

  • The sum of mu(d) over d dividing N with p not dividing d equals one exactly when N is a p-power, by expanding the product of (1-1) over other prime divisors. For rational c with denominator prime to p, choose an integer a congruent to c modulo p^r with r greater than v_p(i!); the falling-factorial difference is divisible by p^r, so binomial(c,i) is p-integral.
