Statement

Every nonzero real polynomial is a real constant times a product of real linear and real quadratic factors, with the empty product allowed for a constant polynomial.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement only concerns real polynomial factors of degree at most two.

Proof 1: Pair the conjugate factors of a complex factorization — outline

By the fundamental theorem of algebra, choose a complex root and divide by its linear factor; repeat on the polynomial of degree one less. This gives a complex linear factorization. For real coefficients, nonreal roots occur with their conjugates and the same multiplicities. Pair their factors: (x-r)(x-\bar r)=x^2-2\operatorname{Re}(r)x+|r|^2 has real coefficients. Real roots already give real linear factors. The constant polynomial case is the empty product.
