Statement

For spaces X,Y, a field F and k≥0, the homology cross product gives a natural isomorphism

\bigoplus_{i+j=k}H_i(X;F)\otimes_F H_j(Y;F)\cong H_k(X\times Y;F).

Proof 1: Collapse to the tensor-product column — outline

Use the homological Künneth spectral sequence

E^2_{p,q}=\bigoplus_{i+j=q}\operatorname{Tor}^F_p(H_i(X;F),H_j(Y;F))\Longrightarrow H_{p+q}(X\times Y;F).

Over a field higher Tor vanishes and Tor_0 is tensor product. Thus only column p=0 remains, so the spectral sequence collapses to the displayed isomorphism. Its natural edge map is the homology cross product identified in the source.
