Statement

Let \Sigma be a finite set of size at least two and \mu=(\mu_x)_{x\in\Sigma} a probability vector. Let X_1,X_2,\ldots be independent with \mathbb P(X_i=x)=\mu_x, and let L_n(x)=n^{-1}\#\{i\le n:X_i=x\}. Write \Delta=\{(p_x):p_x\ge0,\sum_xp_x=1\}, with its relative Euclidean topology. For every Borel set S\subset\Delta,

-\inf_{\nu\in\operatorname{int}_\Delta S}D(\nu\|\mu)\le\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\ln\mathbb P(L_n\in S)\le\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\ln\mathbb P(L_n\in S)\le-\inf_{\nu\in\overline S^{\Delta}}D(\nu\|\mu).

Here D(\nu\|\mu)=\sum_x\nu_x\ln(\nu_x/\mu_x), with 0\ln(0/a)=0 including a=0, and a term with \nu_x>0=\mu_x equal to +\infty. Set \inf\varnothing=+\infty and \ln0=-\infty.
