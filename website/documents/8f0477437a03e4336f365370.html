Statement

Let \mathfrak g be a complex semisimple Lie algebra with Cartan subalgebra \mathfrak h and fixed positive roots. On a highest weight module V_\lambda, each z\in Z(U(\mathfrak g)) acts by a scalar \chi_\lambda(z), and \chi_\lambda:Z(U(\mathfrak g))\to\mathbb C is an algebra homomorphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Semisimple Lie algebras; Highest weight modules; Universal enveloping algebras. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Central scalar action on a highest weight module — outline

This follows from the fact that the module is generated by a highest weight vector; the center acts by scalar multiplication on the module. The resulting scalar action is the central character.
