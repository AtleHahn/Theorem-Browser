Statement

Let A be an abelian variety over F_q and σ its q-power Frobenius. Then the morphism A→A, x↦σ(x)−x, is surjective.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Abelian varieties; Finite fields; Frobenius morphisms. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply Lang to the additive group law of an abelian variety

An abelian variety is a smooth connected algebraic group. Lang’s theorem therefore applies, as the source explicitly observes; its map x^{-1}σ(x) in additive notation is σ(x)−x. Its surjectivity gives the conclusion.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Recognize the abelian variety as a connected smooth algebraic group and translate Lang into additive notation. Algebraic varieties, Frobenius morphisms and smooth algebraic groups require graduate algebraic geometry.
