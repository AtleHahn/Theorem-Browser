Statement

Let G be a compact Hausdorff topological group with Haar probability measure \mu. A unitary representation of G on a complex Hilbert space H is a homomorphism \pi:G\to U(H) for which g\mapsto\pi(g)v is continuous for each v\in H. It is irreducible if H\neq0 and its only closed invariant subspaces are 0 and H.

The following assertions hold.

  1. The linear span of matrix coefficients of finite-dimensional irreducible unitary representations is dense in C(G) for the uniform norm, and also dense in L^2(G,\mu). A matrix coefficient is a function g\mapsto\langle\pi(g)v,w\rangle.
  2. Every unitary representation on a complex Hilbert space decomposes as an orthogonal Hilbert-space direct sum of finite-dimensional irreducible unitary representations.
  3. Let \Sigma contain one representative of every isomorphism class of irreducible unitary representations. For each \pi\in\Sigma, choose an orthonormal basis e_1,\ldots,e_{d_\pi} of its space E_\pi. Then
\left\{\sqrt{d_\pi}\,\langle\pi(\,\cdot\,)e_j,e_i\rangle:\pi\in\Sigma,\ 1\leq i,j\leq d_\pi\right\}

is an orthonormal basis of L^2(G,\mu). Under the left regular action (\rho(h)f)(g)=f(h^{-1}g), each irreducible representation E_\pi occurs with multiplicity d_\pi:

L^2(G,\mu)\cong\widehat{\bigoplus}_{\pi\in\Sigma}E_\pi^{\oplus d_\pi}.

The hat denotes completion of the orthogonal algebraic direct sum. Haar probability measure is the translation-invariant measure normalized by \mu(G)=1.
