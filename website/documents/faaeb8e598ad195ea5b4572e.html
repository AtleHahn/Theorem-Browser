Statement

If (M,g) is a complete connected noncompact n-dimensional Riemannian manifold with positive sectional curvature, then M is diffeomorphic to \mathbb R^n.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Completeness and sectional curvature of general Riemannian manifolds require substantial differential geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Identify the normal bundle of a point

Use the Gromoll–Meyer point-soul theorem: such a manifold has a soul consisting of a single point. By the soul conclusion, M is diffeomorphic to the normal bundle of that point. This bundle is its n-dimensional normal vector space, hence is diffeomorphic to \mathbb R^n.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the point-soul theorem and identify its normal bundle with a Euclidean vector space. Complete Riemannian manifolds, sectional curvature and normal bundles require graduate geometry.
