Statement

In the ring R=\{a+b\sqrt{-5}:a,b\in\mathbb Z\}, the element 2 is not prime: there exist u,v\in R such that 2\mid uv but 2\nmid u and 2\nmid v.

Proof 1: 2 is not a prime element of Z[√−5] — outline

Take u=1+\sqrt{-5} and v=1-\sqrt{-5}. Their product is 6, divisible by 2, but 2 divides neither 1+\sqrt{-5} nor 1-\sqrt{-5} in R.
