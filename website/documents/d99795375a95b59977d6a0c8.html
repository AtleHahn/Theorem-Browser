Statement

A Pythagorean triple means positive integers (a,b,c) with a^2+b^2=c^2; it is primitive if \gcd(a,b,c)=1. Every primitive triple is pairwise coprime, has exactly one even leg, and has odd hypotenuse.

Proof 1: Source argument

All primitive triples can be written as (a, b, c) where a2 + b2 = c2 and a, b, c are coprime. Thus a, b, c are pairwise coprime (if a prime number divided two of them, it would be forced also to divide the third one). As a and b are coprime, at least one of them is odd. Interchanging the legs if necessary, suppose that a is odd; then b is even and c is odd (if both a and b were odd, c would be even, and c2 would be a multiple of 4, while a2 + b2 would be congruent to 2 modulo 4, as an odd square is congruent to 1 modulo 4).

Level
High school only
Level justification

A prime dividing two entries divides the third; square residues modulo four then exclude two odd legs. School divisibility and parity suffice.
