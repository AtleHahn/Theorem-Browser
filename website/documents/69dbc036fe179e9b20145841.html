Statement

For every integer k\ge1 there is a constant c_k>0 such that the following holds. Let I\subseteq\mathbb R be an interval, finite or infinite, and let \phi:I\to\mathbb R be smooth with |\phi^{(k)}(x)|\ge1 throughout I. For every \varepsilon\ge0,

\bigl|\{x\in I:|\phi(x)|\le\varepsilon\}\bigr|\le c_k\varepsilon^{1/k}.

The outer |\cdot| denotes Lebesgue measure (length); \phi^{(k)} is the kth derivative. At included endpoints smoothness is interpreted by restriction from a neighborhood. The constant depends only on k.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Calculus and basic analysis plus measure-as-length of these closed sublevel sets is a small extension; no integration theory or oscillatory proof methods required.
