Statement

For X\in\mathbb R^{n\times K} of full column rank and y\in\mathbb R^n, the function F(b)=\|y-Xb\|^2 has unique global minimum at b=(X^TX)^{-1}X^Ty.

Proof 1: Solve the normal equation and test the quadratic Hessian

Differentiation gives \nabla F(b)=-2X^T(y-Xb) and Hessian 2X^TX. The normal equation X^TXb=X^Ty has the displayed solution. For v\ne0, full rank implies v^TX^TXv=\|Xv\|^2>0, so the Hessian is positive definite. The quadratic is strictly convex; its stationary point is the unique global minimizer.
