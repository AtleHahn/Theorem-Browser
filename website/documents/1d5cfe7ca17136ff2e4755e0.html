Proof 1: Negative even integers are zeros of the zeta function

Use the functional equation \zeta(s)=2^s\pi^{s-1}\sin(\pi s/2)\Gamma(1-s)\zeta(1-s). At a negative even integer s, the sine factor vanishes, while the other factors are finite. Thus \zeta(s)=0.
