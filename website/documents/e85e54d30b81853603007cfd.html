Statement

Let \Psi(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}\Psi_nz^n with \Psi_n>0 and \Psi_{n+1}/\Psi_n\to0. This is an entire comparison function. For an entire function f(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}f_nz^n, define its \Psi-type by

\tau_\Psi(f)=\inf\{t\ge0:\text{there are }M>0,R\ge0\text{ such that }|f(re^{i\theta})|\le M\Psi(tr)\text{ for all }r\ge R,\theta\in\mathbb R\},

with infimum +\infty if no such t exists. Then

\tau_\Psi(f)=\limsup_{n\to\infty}\left|\frac{f_n}{\Psi_n}\right|^{1/n}.

In particular, for each finite \tau\ge0, f has \Psi-type \tau if and only if the displayed upper limit equals \tau. Entire means holomorphic on the whole complex plane; the power series are centered at zero.

Level justification

The growth inequality and coefficient upper limit are explicitly defined using elementary sequence limits and introductory complex power series. Integral-transform theory is not a prerequisite to this statement.
