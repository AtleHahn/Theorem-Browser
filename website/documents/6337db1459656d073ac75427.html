Statement

Let H be a subgroup of G containing no nontrivial normal subgroup of G. Then left multiplication on G/H gives an injective homomorphism G\to\operatorname{Sym}(G/H).

Proof 1: Embedding from a core-free subgroup — outline

In every group G with subgroup H, left multiplication is an action of G on the set of cosets G/H:

g\cdot aH=gaH

for all g,a in G. In particular if H contains no nontrivial normal subgroups of G this induces an isomorphism from G to a subgroup of the permutation group of degree [G:H].

The prerequisite is that the kernel of an action homomorphism is the intersection of its point stabilizers. Here it is normal in G and is contained in the stabilizer of the coset H, which is H itself, so it is trivial.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The coset action has normal kernel contained in the stabilizer H. The core-free assumption makes the kernel trivial. Cosets, actions and kernels are standard introductory group theory.
