Statement

For each fixed positive integer k, \mathsf{PP}\not\subseteq\mathsf{SIZE}(n^k). Here PP is probabilistic polynomial time with acceptance probability strictly greater than one half for yes instances, and \mathsf{SIZE}(n^k) denotes languages with Boolean circuit families of size O(n^k).

Proof 1: Transfer the hierarchy lower bound through counting-class collapse

Here \mathsf P^{\#P} denotes polynomial time with a counting-function oracle, \mathsf{PH} is the polynomial hierarchy, \Sigma_2^P its second existential level, and \mathsf{MA} is Merlin–Arthur complexity. Use the following source-stated containments and conditional collapse as external results.

  • If \mathsf{PP}\not\subseteq\mathsf{P/poly} then \mathsf{PP}\not\subseteq\mathsf{SIZE}(n^k).
  • Otherwise, \mathsf P^{\#P}\subseteq\mathsf{P/poly}. Since\mathsf P^{\#P}\supseteq\mathsf{PP}\supseteq\mathsf{MA}(by the property of \mathsf{MA}),\mathsf P^{\#P}\supseteq\mathsf{PH}\supseteq\Sigma_2^P\supseteq\mathsf{MA}(by Toda's theorem and the property of \mathsf{MA}), and\mathsf P^{\#P}=\mathsf{MA}(which follows from the assumption using the interactive protocol for the permanent), the containments are equalities and we get\mathsf{PP}=\Sigma_2^P\not\subseteq\mathsf{SIZE}(n^k)by Kannan's theorem: for every fixed positive integer k, some language in \Sigma_2^P has no circuit family of size O(n^k).
