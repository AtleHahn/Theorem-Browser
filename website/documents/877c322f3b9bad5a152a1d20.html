Statement

Let A,B be Hermitian n\times n matrices. For any Hermitian C, write \lambda_1(C)\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_n(C) for its eigenvalues, and let 1\le i_1<\cdots<i_k\le n. Then

\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_{i_j}(A)+\sum_{j=n-k+1}^n\lambda_j(B) \le\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_{i_j}(A+B) \le\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_{i_j}(A)+\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_j(B).

Proof 1: Wielandt formula and the uniform partial-trace bound

For a k-dimensional subspace W, define \operatorname{tr}_W(C) as the sum of w_j^*Cw_j over an orthonormal basis of W. For a flag V_1\subset\cdots\subset V_k with \dim V_j=i_j, put

X(V_1,\ldots,V_k)=\{W:\dim W=k,\ \dim(W\cap V_j)\ge j\text{ for every }j\}.

Use the external Wielandt formula

\sum_j\lambda_{i_j}(C)=\sup_{(V_j)}\inf_{W\in X(V_1,\ldots,V_k)}\operatorname{tr}_W(C)

and the external extremal-trace bound \operatorname{tr}_W(B)\le\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_j(B). The upper inequality is by Wielandt minimax and the uniform partial-trace bound:

\begin{aligned} &\lambda_{i_1}(A+B)+\cdots+\lambda_{i_k}(A+B)\\ &=\sup_{V_1,\ldots,V_k}\inf_{W\in X(V_1,\ldots,V_k)} \bigl(\operatorname{tr}_W(A)+\operatorname{tr}_W(B)\bigr)\\ &\le \sup_{V_1,\ldots,V_k}\left( \inf_{W\in X(V_1,\ldots,V_k)}\operatorname{tr}_W(A) +\lambda_1(B)+\cdots+\lambda_k(B)\right)\\ &=\lambda_{i_1}(A)+\cdots+\lambda_{i_k}(A) +\lambda_1(B)+\cdots+\lambda_k(B). \end{aligned}

The first equality uses linearity of trace. The lower inequality is obtained by negation.

Apply the upper inequality to -A,-B and the increasing indices n+1-i_k,\ldots,n+1-i_1. Since \lambda_j(-C)=-\lambda_{n+1-j}(C), negation gives the lower inequality.
