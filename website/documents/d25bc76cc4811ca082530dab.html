Statement

Let U be a linear isometry of a Hilbert space H. Then N^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}U^n(I-U)=N^{-1}(I-U^N) converges to zero in operator norm.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A Hilbert space adds norm completeness to an inner-product space. Isometry, finite sums of operator powers and the operator norm as unit-ball supremum are direct extensions of linear algebra and limits; no adjoints or spectral theory are required.

Proof 1: Telescope the operator differences — outline

The sum telescopes: N^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}U^n(I-U)=N^{-1}(I-U^N)\to0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Telescope the finite operator sum and bound its norm by 2/N. Undergraduate linear algebra and norm estimates suffice with operator norm as a supremum; no Hilbert spectral theory or completeness is used.
