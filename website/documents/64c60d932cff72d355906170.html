Statement

Let V be a Stein manifold of complex dimension n. Then V admits a Morse function all of whose critical points have Morse index at most n, and V is homotopy equivalent to a CW complex of real dimension at most n. In particular, this applies when V is a smooth complex affine variety of complex dimension n.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding Stein manifolds, Morse index, and CW homotopy type requires complex geometry and algebraic/differential topology together; these are not a small extension of undergraduate point-set topology.
