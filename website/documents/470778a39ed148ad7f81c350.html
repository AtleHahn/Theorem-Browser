Statement

On a filtered probability space (\Omega,\mathcal F,(\mathcal F_t)_{t\ge0},\mathbb P), let X be nonnegative, adapted and right-continuous, and let G be nonnegative, predictable, right-continuous and nondecreasing. Suppose for every bounded stopping time \tau,

\mathbb E[X_\tau\mid\mathcal F_0]\le\mathbb E[G_\tau\mid\mathcal F_0]<\infty\quad\text{almost surely}.

Put X^*=\sup_{t\ge0}X_t and G^*=\sup_{t\ge0}G_t. Adapted means X_t is \mathcal F_t-measurable; predictable means measurable for the sigma-algebra generated by adapted left-continuous processes. A stopping time satisfies \{\tau\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t for every t, and bounded means bounded by a finite deterministic constant. For every real 0<p<1, almost surely,

\mathbb E[(X^*)^p\mid\mathcal F_0]\le\frac{p^{-p}}{1-p}\,\mathbb E[(G^*)^p\mid\mathcal F_0].

Conditional moments may take the value +\infty.

Proof 1: Predictable stopping and conditional localization (external)

The following editorial argument completes the stated scope. A supporting source is A Stochastic Gronwall Lemma and Well-Posedness of Path-Dependent SDEs Driven by Martingale Noise (Preprint Lemma 2.1, pp.3–5, parts (i) and (ii) and proofs (published Lemma 2.2); augmentation bridge supplied here.).

The following additional localization argument handles the stated filtration assumptions. Fix deterministic T and B_m={E[G_T|F0]<=m}. For any A in F0 contained in B_m with P(A)>0 work under Q=P(.|A) with the processes stopped at T. Then E_Q G_T is finite, and the assumed conditional domination gives unconditional domination at every original bounded stopping time. Pass to the usual augmentation under Q. Before completion, a stopping time tau for F_{t+} is approximated from above by original stopping times tau_n=2^{-n}(floor(2^n tau)+1): {tau_n<=k 2^{-n}}={tau<k 2^{-n}} belongs to F_{k 2^{-n}}. Completion only changes these times on null sets. Right continuity of X and G, Fatou for X, and dominated convergence using G_T for the stopped G preserve unconditional domination at augmented stopping times. Original predictability persists in the larger filtration, as do adaptedness and right continuity. Apply the unconditional version of Mehri–Scheutzow Lemma 2.1 (equivalently average its conditional conclusion) under Q. Testing all such A recovers the desired conditional inequality on B_m with respect to the ORIGINAL F0, so no assumption on F0=F0+ is made. Let m tend to infinity, then let integer T tend to infinity using conditional monotone convergence. For the tail term the increasing finite-horizon events {G_T>=d} are contained in {G*>=d}, which suffices. This proves both the tail bound and the fractional moment bound, with exactly p^{-p}/(1-p), without strengthening the stated filtration assumptions.
