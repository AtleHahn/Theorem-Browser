Statement

Let seven distinct points of a projective plane over an algebraically closed field lie on a degenerate conic, which is a union of at most two lines. Then at least four of the points lie on one line.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The full ambient projective plane over an algebraically closed field requires homogeneous coordinates and field algebra, a limited undergraduate extension. The degenerate conic decomposition is supplied.

Proof 1: Count points on the at most two component lines — outline

A degenerate conic is the union of at most two lines. Seven distinct points on it therefore include at least four on one of these lines.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the stated decomposition into at most two projective lines and count seven points. Undergraduate linear algebra and fields suffice after learning projective points and lines as homogeneous-coordinate subspaces; no conic classification proof or algebraic geometry machinery is invoked.
