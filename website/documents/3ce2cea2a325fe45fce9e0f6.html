Statement

Fix an integer k\ge1. As n\to\infty through positive integers, \log\frac{(kn)!}{(n!)^k}=kn\log k+\frac12[\log k-(k-1)\log(2\pi n)]-\frac{k^2-1}{12kn}+\frac{k^4-1}{360k^3n^3}-\frac{k^6-1}{1260k^5n^5}+O(n^{-6}).

Proof 1: Logarithmic Stirling substitution (outline)

By Stirling's approximation, or equivalently the log-gamma function's asymptotic expansion,

\log\binom{kn}{n,n,\ldots,n} =kn\log k+\frac12\bigl(\log k-(k-1)\log(2\pi n)\bigr) -\frac{k^2-1}{12kn}+\frac{k^4-1}{360k^3n^3} -\frac{k^6-1}{1260k^5n^5}+O(n^{-6}).

Here \binom{kn}{n,n,\ldots,n}=(kn)!/(n!)^k, and k is fixed.

Level justification

Subtract k copies of the logarithmic factorial expansion at n from the expansion at kn and collect inverse powers with k fixed. Undergraduate asymptotic analysis suffices; no gamma-function theory is required beyond the supplied formula.
