Statement

Let X,Y be Riemann surfaces, with X connected, and let f,g:X\to Y be holomorphic maps. A Riemann surface is a Hausdorff second countable space locally homeomorphic to open subsets of \mathbb C, with holomorphic transition maps; a map between such surfaces is holomorphic when its expressions in local complex coordinates are holomorphic. Suppose there is A\subseteq X with f(a)=g(a) for every a\in A, and A has an accumulation point p\in X, meaning every neighborhood of p meets A\setminus\{p\}. Then f=g on all of X.

Level justification
