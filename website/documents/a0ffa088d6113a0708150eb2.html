Statement

Let M,N be connected complete Riemannian manifolds with curvature tensors R^M,R^N invariant under parallel transport, and suppose M is simply connected. Let x\in M, y\in N, and let I:T_xM\to T_yN be a linear isometry onto satisfying I(R^M(X,Y,Z))=R^N(IX,IY,IZ) for all X,Y,Z\in T_xM. Then there exists a locally isometric covering F:M\to N with F(x)=y and D_xF=I.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Parallel curvature tensors, Riemannian completeness and curvature-preserving tangent maps require substantial differential geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Parallel curvature and the covering theorem — outline

Use Cartan–Ambrose–Hicks in its simply connected-domain form as an external prerequisite: for connected complete Riemannian M,N with M simply connected, a base-point tangent isometry whose parallel-transported maps intertwine the curvature tensors along all broken geodesics extends to a locally isometric covering F:M\to N with the prescribed value and differential.

A Riemannian manifold is called locally symmetric if its Riemann curvature tensor is invariant under parallel transport:

\nabla R=0.

From the Cartan–Ambrose–Hicks theorem, we have the asserted locally isometric covering map.

The source states this consequence without spelling out the transported-curvature verification.
