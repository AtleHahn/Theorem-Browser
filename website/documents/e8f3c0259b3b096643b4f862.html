Statement

Let M be a connected smooth manifold and P a finitely generated projective module over C^\infty(M), the ring of smooth real-valued functions. Then there is a finite-rank smooth real vector bundle E\to M whose module of smooth sections is isomorphic to P.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Projective modules over smooth function rings and their realization as section modules of bundles require substantial differential geometry and module theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Idempotent image-fiber construction — outline

Represent P by a smooth function f:M\to M_n(\mathbb R) with f(x)^2=f(x), so that P is the image of this idempotent on C^\infty(M)^n. Define the fiber E_x to be the range of f(x). This is the corresponding smooth vector bundle, with the desired section module.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Represent the projective module by an idempotent matrix of smooth functions and use its image fibers as a bundle. Projective modules, smooth bundles and their section modules are precisely the cumulative prerequisites excluded by the conservative undergraduate criterion.
