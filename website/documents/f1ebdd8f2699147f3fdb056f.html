Statement

Let \mathfrak g\subseteq\operatorname{End}_K(V) be a Lie algebra over a field K, with V finite-dimensional, and \mathfrak h an ideal. Then W=\{v\in V:Xv=0\ \forall X\in\mathfrak h\} is stable under \mathfrak g.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra supplies endomorphisms, common kernels, eigenspaces and bilinear operations. A Lie bracket and its ideal/automorphism conditions are short algebraic definitions; for operators the bracket is the commutator. No representation classification or Lie groups are needed for these elementary invariance claims.

Proof 1: Commute an ideal element past an operator

For v\in W, X\in\mathfrak h and Y\in\mathfrak g, X(Yv)=Y(Xv)+[X,Y]v=0, since [X,Y]\in\mathfrak h. Hence Yv\in W.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Commute X past Y and use the ideal condition to kill both terms on the common kernel. Undergraduate linear algebra and algebra suffice with the operator Lie bracket XY-YX and its ideal closure as a small explicit extension.
