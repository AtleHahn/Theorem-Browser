Statement

Every finite simple group G of sectional 2-rank at least 5 is of component type or of characteristic 2 type, with the definitions below.

Sectional 2-rank is the largest r such that a quotient H/N, with H\leq G and N\trianglelefteq H, is isomorphic to (\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^r. Simple means nontrivial with no proper nontrivial normal subgroup.

  • Component type means that for some involution t (an element of order 2), the quotient C_G(t)/O(C_G(t)) has a component. For a finite group H, O(H) is its largest normal subgroup of odd order. A component is a subnormal quasisimple subgroup L: there is a chain of normal subgroup inclusions from L to H, L=[L,L], and L/Z(L) is nonabelian simple. The center is Z(L) and the commutator subgroup [L,L] is generated by x^{-1}y^{-1}xy.
  • Characteristic 2 type means that for every maximal 2-local subgroup M of G, C_M(O_2(M))\leq O_2(M). Here a 2-local subgroup is N_G(Q) for a nontrivial 2-subgroup Q; maximal means maximal by inclusion among these subgroups. A 2-subgroup has order a power of 2, and O_2(M) is the largest normal 2-subgroup of M.

The normalizer is N_G(Q)=\{g:g^{-1}Qg=Q\} and C_H(B)=\{h\in H:hb=bh\text{ for every }b\in B\} is a centralizer (with C_G(t)=C_G(\{t\})).
