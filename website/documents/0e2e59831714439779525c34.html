Statement

Let a<b, let f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R be nonnegative and Lebesgue integrable, and let \varphi:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R be convex. Then \varphi\!\left(\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\right)\le\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b\varphi(f(x))\,dx.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The domain explicitly includes arbitrary Lebesgue-integrable functions, requiring measure-theoretic integration.

Proof 1: Rescale the interval — outline

Rescale [a,b] to an interval of length one by substitution. Applying the probability-measure version of Jensen’s inequality on that interval and undoing the substitution gives the displayed factors 1/(b-a).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Rescale an arbitrary Lebesgue-integrable function to a probability interval and apply measure-theoretic Jensen. The actual setting is general Lebesgue integration, not continuous or Riemann functions.
