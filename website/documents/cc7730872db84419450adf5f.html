Pokhozhaev identity for scalar weak solutions

Statement

Let n\ge1, let g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R be continuous with g(0)=0, and put G(s)=\int_0^s g(t)\,dt. Suppose a real-valued function u on \mathbb R^n is locally essentially bounded, has weak gradient \nabla u\in L^2(\mathbb R^n), satisfies G(u)\in L^1(\mathbb R^n), and solves -\Delta u=g(u) in distributions. Then

\frac{n-2}{2}\int_{\mathbb R^n}|\nabla u|^2\,dx=n\int_{\mathbb R^n}G(u)\,dx.

All integrals use Lebesgue measure. Weak derivatives are distributional derivatives; the equation means \int\nabla u\cdot\nabla\psi=\int g(u)\psi for every smooth compactly supported test function \psi. The notation L^p means finite integral of the absolute value to power p, and locally essentially bounded means bounded outside a null set on each compact subset.
