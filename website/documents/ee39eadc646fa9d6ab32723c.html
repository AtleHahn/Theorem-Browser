Statement

Let P(Q) be a circle specified by its center and a point on it, and let O(r) be a drawn nondegenerate circle. Assume O\notin PQ. Choose R on O(r) with OR\parallel PQ such that PO and QR meet in a finite point X. For M\notin PO, straightedge constructions using available parallels determine the homothety center X taking P(Q) to O(r) and the image of M.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Points, lines, circles, parallels and perpendiculars are elementary geometry.

Proof 1: Find the homothety center and project one point — outline

We use the parallel construction available with the fixed circle and its center. Under the nondegeneracy assumptions of the statement:

  1. Draw a line PQ through the points defining the circle.
  2. Construct a parallel of line PQ through the center O of the provided circle.
    • The parallel intersects the provided circle at two points, choose the point R stipulated in the statement.
  3. Draw a line PO, through the centers of the two circles.
  4. Draw a line QR, connecting the two points on the circumferences of the two circles.
  5. Intersect the lines PO and QR at point X.
    • Point X is the center of homothety sending circle P(Q) to the circle O(r).
  6. For the given point M, which is not on line PO, draw a line PM.
  7. Draw a line MX.
  8. Construct a parallel of line PM through the center O of the given circle.
    • The parallel intersects the line MX at a point N.

The point N is the image of M under the homothety.

Level justification

Intersect lines through corresponding centers and circumference points, then use a parallel to transfer M. School similarity and homothety geometry suffice.
