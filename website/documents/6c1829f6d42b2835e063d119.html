Statement

Let n\ge1 and let u\ge0 belong to L^2(\mathbb R^n). Let u^* be its symmetric decreasing rearrangement, whose positive-threshold superlevel sets agree up to null sets with centered balls (possibly empty) of the same volumes as those of u. For t>0, put K_t(z)=(4\pi t)^{-n/2}\exp(-|z|^2/(4t)) and I_t(v)=\iint_{\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^n}K_t(x-y)v(x)v(y)\,dx\,dy. Then \frac{\|u^*\|_2^2-I_t(u^*)}{t}\le\frac{\|u\|_2^2-I_t(u)}{t}.

Proof 1: Rearrangement decreases the heat-kernel energy deficit — proof

Since for every {\displaystyle t\in (0,+\infty )} the function {\displaystyle K_{t}} is radial and radially decreasing, we have by the Riesz rearrangement inequality

{\displaystyle \int _{\mathbb {R} ^{n}}\int _{\mathbb {R} ^{n}}K_{t}(x-y)\,u(x)\,u(y)\,dx\,dy\leq \int _{\mathbb {R} ^{n}}\int _{\mathbb {R} ^{n}}K_{t}(x-y)\,u^{*}(x)\,u^{*}(y)\,dx\,dy}

Equimeasurability gives \int_{\mathbb R^n}|u|^2=\int_{\mathbb R^n}|u^*|^2. Hence, subtracting the displayed inequality from this common value and dividing by t>0,

\frac1t\left(\int_{\mathbb R^n}|u^*|^2-\iint K_t(x-y)u^*(x)u^*(y)\,dx\,dy\right) \leq \frac1t\left(\int_{\mathbb R^n}|u|^2-\iint K_t(x-y)u(x)u(y)\,dx\,dy\right).
