Statement

Let A=(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)\times[0,1] be the closed annulus, with area measure d\theta\,dr. Let f:A\to A be an orientation-preserving homeomorphism preserving area and each boundary circle. Suppose a lift F:\mathbb R\times[0,1]\to\mathbb R\times[0,1] of f satisfies

F(s+1,r)=F(s,r)+(1,0),\qquad F_1(s,0)<s<F_1(s,1)\quad\text{for all }s\in\mathbb R,

or the same strict inequalities with the two boundary roles interchanged. Then f has at least two distinct fixed points. A lift means that reducing the first coordinate modulo \mathbb Z after applying F gives f(s\bmod\mathbb Z,r); F_1 denotes the first coordinate. Area preservation means preserving this measure on measurable subsets, and a fixed point is z\in A with f(z)=z. The boundary inequalities express rotation in opposite directions using one common lift.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate calculus and point-set topology suffice; the lift is explicitly a periodic-coordinate map and needs no general covering-space theory.
