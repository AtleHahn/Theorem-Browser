Statement

Let \varphi be a differentiable real scalar field on an open subset of \mathbb R^n. Any two oriented, finitely piecewise differentiable curves in its domain from p to q, for which the defining ordinary gradient line integrals exist, have the same integral \varphi(q)-\varphi(p).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Multivariable differentiation; Parametrized line integrals. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Compare the two endpoint formulas

The gradient theorem evaluates each integral as the scalar field at the ending point minus its value at the starting point. Both curves have the same two endpoints, so their integrals agree.
