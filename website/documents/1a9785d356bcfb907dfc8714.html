Statement

For every real p with 2\le p<\infty, the Hilbert transform \mathcal Hf(x)=\pi^{-1}\operatorname{p.v.}\int_{\mathbb R}f(x-t)/t\,dt, initially defined on Schwartz functions, extends boundedly to L^p(\mathbb R). Thus \|\mathcal Hf\|_p\le A_p\|f\|_p for some finite constant A_p.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns a singular integral and its extensions on measure-theoretic function spaces.

Proof 1: Interpolate the dyadic-exponent bounds — outline

Use the L^2 bound and the bounds on L^{2^n} for all integers n\ge2. Interpolation establishes \|\mathcal Hf\|_p\le A_p\|f\|_p for every 2\le p<\infty.
