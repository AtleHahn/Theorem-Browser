Statement

Given a nondegenerate segment AB and positive integers m,n, draw a ray from A not along AB, and mark consecutive equally spaced points P_1,\ldots,P_{m+n} on it. The line through P_m parallel to P_{m+n}B meets AB at a point Q with |AQ|:|QB|=m:n.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The entire construction uses elementary rays, parallels and equal subdivisions.

Proof 1: Equally spaced auxiliary points and an intercept — outline

Draw the auxiliary ray and mark the m+n equally spaced points. Join its last marked point to B, then draw the parallel through the mth point. By the intercept theorem this parallel divides AB in the prescribed m:n ratio.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply similarity to the auxiliary ray and its parallel intercept, then subtract the two segment lengths to obtain m:n. School Euclidean geometry suffices.
