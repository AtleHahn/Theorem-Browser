Statement

A semigroup is a set with an associative operation xy. For a set X, let S=\mathcal P(X) with multiplication AB=A\cap B. Its universal group G(S), generated by [A] with relations [A][B]=[A\cap B], is the trivial group.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Power sets and semigroups are supplied, while group generators and relations are a brief extension of introductory group theory; no general completion theory is needed.

Proof 1: Idempotent generators are identities in a group

Every subset satisfies A\cap A=A. Thus every generator of G(S) satisfies [A]^2=[A]. In a group an idempotent is the identity, so all generators are identities and G(S) is trivial.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use idempotence of each generating subset and group cancellation; all generators then equal the identity. Undergraduate group presentations suffice with the given universal-group presentation as a small extension.
