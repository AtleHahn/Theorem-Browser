Statement

Let M be a closed topological manifold of dimension n\geq0, where closed means compact and without boundary. Let R be a coefficient ring with identity, and choose an R-orientation of M, with fundamental class [M]\in H_n(M;R). For every integer k, cap product with that class is an isomorphism

D_M:H^k(M;R)\longrightarrow H_{n-k}(M;R),\qquad \alpha\longmapsto[M]\frown\alpha.

Here H_* and H^* are singular homology and cohomology with constant coefficients in R; groups in negative degree are zero. An R-orientation chooses compatible generators of the local homology H_n(M,M\setminus\{x\};R), and the fundamental class has those local images. Cap product is the standard operation evaluating a cochain on a face of a chain and retaining the complementary-dimensional face. For a noncommutative ring, use the usual consistent left/right coefficient conventions; the underlying additive groups are isomorphic. In particular, with R=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z every manifold has the required orientation, so orientability over \mathbb Z is unnecessary.

Proof availability

Source proof sketch and its scope

The source uses a triangulation and the dual cell decomposition. A simplex of dimension i corresponds to a dual cell of dimension n-i. With compatible orientations, the incidence relations give the correspondence between cochains on one decomposition and complementary-dimensional chains on the other, and hence the duality isomorphism.

This is a useful proof sketch where the required triangulation and dual-cell construction are available. The article does not justify that construction for every topological manifold, identify all chain-level sign conventions, or supply the general topological proof for the full coefficient scope above. The statement has therefore not been narrowed to the case treated by the sketch.
