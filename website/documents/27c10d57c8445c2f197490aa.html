Statement

For every unitary matrix U\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}, n\ge1, there exist diagonal unitary matrices L,R such that every row and every column of LUR sums to 1. Unitary means U^*U=UU^*=I_n, where * is conjugate transpose; a diagonal matrix is unitary exactly when all its diagonal entries have modulus 1.
