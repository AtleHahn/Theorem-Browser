Statement

A Platonic solid is a convex polyhedron in Euclidean three-space whose faces are congruent regular p-gons and at each vertex of which q faces meet. Let \theta be the interior dihedral angle between adjacent faces. The solid angle \Omega at each vertex, measured as the unit-sphere area of the directions into the solid from that vertex, equals q\theta-(q-2)\pi.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Apply spherical excess to the vertex figure

The spherical vertex figure is a regular q-gon whose interior angles are the dihedral angles \theta. The spherical excess formula for a unit-sphere polygon gives its area as the sum of its angles minus (q-2)\pi. Hence \Omega=q\theta-(q-2)\pi.
