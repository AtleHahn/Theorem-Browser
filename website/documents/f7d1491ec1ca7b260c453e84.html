Statement

Let K be a number field of degree d=[K:\mathbb Q]\ge1 and E/K an elliptic curve. Put M=129(5^d-1)(3d)^6. Then |E(K)_{\mathrm{tors}}|\le (M^M)^2.

Proof 1: Combine prime-power bounds with two cyclic factors — outline

Use Parent’s bound: every torsion point of prime-power order p^r has p^r\le B(d,p)=\begin{cases}129(3^d-1)(3d)^6,&p=2,\\65(5^d-1)(2d)^6,&p=3,\\65(3^d-1)(2d)^6,&p>3.\end{cases} Each bound is at most M. Use also the structure E(K)_{\mathrm{tors}}\cong\mathbb Z/n_1\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/n_2\mathbb Z. Each prime-power divisor in either cyclic order is realized as a point order and is at most M. There are at most M distinct such primes, so n_i\le M^M for i=1,2. Multiplying the two orders gives the stated bound.
