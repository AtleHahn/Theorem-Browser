Statement

Every finite Moufang projective plane is isomorphic as an incidence structure to \operatorname{PG}(2,\mathbb F_q) for some finite field \mathbb F_q. Here a Moufang plane means a projective plane coordinatizable by an alternative division ring. In \operatorname{PG}(2,\mathbb F_q), points are one-dimensional subspaces of \mathbb F_q^3, lines are two-dimensional subspaces, and incidence is containment. An incidence isomorphism is a bijection on points and on lines preserving which points lie on which lines.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Coordinatization of an abstract projective plane by a nonassociative division ring is substantially more than the elementary subspace model of PG(2,q). That extra conceptual construction cannot confidently fit the undergraduate reading allowance.
