Statement

Let \mathfrak g be a finite-dimensional semisimple complex Lie algebra, choose a Cartan subalgebra and a set \Phi^+ of positive roots, and let \lambda,\mu,\nu be dominant integral weights. Write V_\eta for the finite-dimensional irreducible representation of highest weight \eta. The multiplicity of V_\nu in V_\lambda\otimes V_\mu is

\sum_{w,w'\in W}\det(ww')\,P\bigl(w(\lambda+\rho)+w'(\mu+\rho)-(\nu+2\rho)\bigr).

Here W is the Weyl group acting on the real span of the roots, \rho=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}\alpha, and P(\gamma) is the number of tuples of nonnegative integers (n_\alpha)_{\alpha\in\Phi^+} with \gamma=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}n_\alpha\alpha, or zero if no such tuple exists. The Weyl group is generated by root reflections. A dominant integral weight has nonnegative integer pairing with every simple coroot; a highest weight is the weight of a vector killed by all positive-root operators. Multiplicity means the number of copies of the indicated irreducible summand in the tensor-product decomposition.
