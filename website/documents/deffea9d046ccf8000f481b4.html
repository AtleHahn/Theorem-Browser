Statement

There exist a field K of characteristic 2 and a separable extension L/K of degree 6 such that no generator \lambda with L=K(\lambda) has minimal polynomial of the form

t^6+c_4t^4+c_2t^2+c_1t+c_0,\qquad c_4,c_2,c_1,c_0\in K.

Characteristic 2 means 1+1=0. Degree 6 means that L has dimension 6 as a vector space over K. Separable means that each element's minimal polynomial over K has distinct roots in an algebraic closure. The minimal polynomial is the monic polynomial of least positive degree over K vanishing at the element. A generator means that the smallest subfield of L containing K and \lambda is all of L. Thus the obstruction concerns simultaneous removal of the degree-five and degree-three coefficients, not absence of a primitive element.
