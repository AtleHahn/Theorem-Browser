Statement

Let n\ge3. Every quasiregular map f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n that is a local homeomorphism is a homeomorphism onto \mathbb R^n. Here quasiregular means continuous, nonconstant, in W^{1,n}_{\mathrm{loc}}, and satisfying \|Df(x)\|^n\le K\det Df(x) almost everywhere for some finite K\ge1. Membership in W^{1,n}_{\mathrm{loc}} means the components and their first weak derivatives are locally nth-power integrable; Df is the weak derivative matrix and its norm is the Euclidean operator norm. Local homeomorphism means each point has an open neighborhood mapped homeomorphically onto an open set; a homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse.
