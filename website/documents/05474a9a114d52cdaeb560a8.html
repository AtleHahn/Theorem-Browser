Statement

For positive integers a,b with 1<a/b<3/2, \left|\sqrt2-\frac ab\right|\ge\frac1{3b^2}.

Proof 1: Rationalize and bound an integer numerator

Let a and b be positive integers such that 1<⁠a/b⁠< 3/2 (and 1<\sqrt2<3/2, since 1<2<9/4). Now 2b2 and a2 cannot be equal, since the first has an odd number of factors 2 whereas the second has an even number of factors 2. Thus |2b2 − a2| ≥ 1. Multiplying the absolute difference |√2 − ⁠a/b⁠| by b2(√2 + ⁠a/b⁠) in the numerator and denominator, we get

{\displaystyle \left|{\sqrt {2}}-{\frac {a}{b}}\right|={\frac {|2b^{2}-a^{2}|}{b^{2}\!\left({\sqrt {2}}+{\frac {a}{b}}\right)}}\geq {\frac {1}{b^{2}\!\left({\sqrt {2}}+{\frac {a}{b}}\right)}}\geq {\frac {1}{3b^{2}}},}

the latter inequality being true because it is assumed that 1<⁠a/b⁠< 3/2, giving ⁠a/b⁠ + √2 ≤ 3 .
