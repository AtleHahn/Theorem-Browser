Statement

Let \mathfrak g be a finite-dimensional Lie algebra over any field k, and let M be a simple module over its universal enveloping algebra U(\mathfrak g). Every U(\mathfrak g)-linear endomorphism T:M\to M is algebraic over k: there is a nonzero polynomial p(t)\in k[t] with p(T)=0. A Lie algebra is a vector space with a bilinear alternating bracket satisfying the Jacobi identity. Its universal enveloping algebra is the tensor algebra on \mathfrak g modulo the relations x\otimes y-y\otimes x-[x,y]. A simple module is nonzero and has no nonzero proper submodules. Algebra-linearity means T(am)=aT(m) for a\in U(\mathfrak g) and m\in M. No uncountability assumption on k or finite-dimensionality assumption on M is made.
