Statement

Among all open circular disks in \mathbb R^2, the family \{B(0,r):r>0\} is a maximal chain under inclusion.

Level
High school only
Level justification

A chain is a nested family of plane disks and maximal means no disk can be added while keeping nesting. These are elementary set and distance notions.

Proof 1: Concentric disks form a maximal inclusion-chain — outline

Suppose that A is the collection of all circular regions (interiors of circles) in the plane. One maximal totally ordered sub-collection of A consists of all circular regions with centers at the origin.

The source states this example without the inclusion and maximality checks.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Concentric disks are ordered by radius. For a disk with nonzero center, choose an origin-centered radius strictly between its nearest and farthest radial extents; neither disk contains the other. Elementary distances therefore give maximality.
