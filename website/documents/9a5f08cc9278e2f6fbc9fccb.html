Statement

For a Euclidean rectangle ABCD in cyclic order, AB^2+BC^2=AC^2.

Level

Proof 1: Use equality of the two diagonals

The parallelogram law gives 2AB^2+2BC^2=AC^2+BD^2.

If the parallelogram is a rectangle, the two diagonals are of equal lengths AC = BD, so

{\displaystyle 2AB^{2}+2BC^{2}=2AC^{2}}

and the statement reduces to the Pythagorean theorem.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Use equal diagonal lengths in the parallelogram law and divide by two. School Euclidean geometry suffices.
