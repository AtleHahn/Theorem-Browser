Statement

For each integer k\ge5 there are constants C,c>0, depending on k, such that for all sufficiently large integers N, every A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\} containing no k-term arithmetic progression with positive integer common difference satisfies

|A|\le CN\exp\bigl(- (\log\log N)^c\bigr).

An arithmetic progression of length k has the form a,a+d,\ldots,a+(k-1)d with d\ge1. Logarithms are natural.
