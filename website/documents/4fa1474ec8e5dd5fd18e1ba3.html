Statement

On a compact Kähler manifold X, let \mathcal H_d^k=\ker\Delta_d on smooth complex k-forms and \mathcal H_\partial^{p,q},\mathcal H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q} the corresponding Laplacian kernels on (p,q)-forms. Then for each k\ge0, \mathcal H_d^k=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}\mathcal H_\partial^{p,q}=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}\mathcal H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}, orthogonally, with p,q\ge0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Compact Kähler manifolds; Complex differential forms; Dolbeault operators; Laplacians; Harmonic forms; Orthogonal direct sums. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Equal Laplacians on orthogonal bidegrees — outline

The Kähler identity is \Delta_d=2\Delta_\partial=2\Delta_{\bar\partial}. Applied to the orthogonal bidegree decomposition of complex forms, it gives \mathcal H_d^k=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}\mathcal H_\partial^{p,q}=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}\mathcal H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use equality of the three Laplacians and orthogonal preservation of bidegrees. Kahler identities and harmonic differential forms require advanced complex geometry.
