Statement

Let n,m,t be positive integers and let S_1,\ldots,S_m be subsets of [n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}. Suppose each element of [n] belongs to at most t of these sets. Then there is a function x:[n]\to\{-1,+1\} such that

\left|\sum_{i\in S_j}x(i)\right|\le 2t-1\qquad(1\le j\le m).

Thus every set has an imbalance of at most 2t-1 between its numbers of elements assigned the two signs.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All objects are finite sets, membership counts, and choices of two signs. The local imbalance explanation reduces the complete assertion to integer counting and inequalities accessible with high-school mathematics; no discrepancy-theory background is needed.
