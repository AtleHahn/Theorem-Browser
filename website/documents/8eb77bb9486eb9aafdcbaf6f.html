Statement

There exists a closed connected oriented three-manifold with the same integral homology as S^3 that is not homeomorphic to S^3.

Proof 1: Use the Poincaré homology sphere as a witness

Use the standard Poincare homology sphere, whose homology agrees with that of S^3 but whose fundamental group has 120 elements. The three-sphere has trivial fundamental group. As fundamental groups are invariant under homeomorphisms, this supplied example cannot be homeomorphic to S^3.
