Statement

Every matrix of SU(2) has a unique form \begin{pmatrix}\alpha&-\overline\beta\\\beta&\overline\alpha\end{pmatrix} with |\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1. Thus its elements are parametrized by S^3\subset\mathbb C^2=\mathbb R^4. Here SU(2) means the unitary complex two-by-two matrices of determinant one.

Proof 1: SU(2) is parametrized by the unit three-sphere — source argument

The standard matrix description is

\operatorname{SU}(2)= \left\{\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&-\overline{\beta}\\ \beta&\overline{\alpha}\end{pmatrix}: \alpha,\beta\in\mathbb C,\ |\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1\right\}.

Thus, \operatorname{SU}(2) is represented as the 3-sphere S^3 sitting inside \mathbb C^2=\mathbb R^4.
