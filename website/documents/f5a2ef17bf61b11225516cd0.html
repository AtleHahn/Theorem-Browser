Statement

If A,B\subseteq\mathbb R^n are compact, then their Minkowski sum A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,b\in B\} is compact.

Proof 1: Map the compact product by addition

The product A\times B is compact, and A+B is its image under the continuous addition map + :\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n. Hence A+B is compact.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Take the continuous addition image of the compact product. This is a direct elementary compactness argument in Euclidean space.
