Statement

For square matrices A,B over a unital ring such that A,B,A+B and A+AB^{-1}A are invertible, (A+B)^{-1}=A^{-1}-(A+AB^{-1}A)^{-1}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Ring multiplication, units and matrix inverses are introductory algebra. Division rings drop commutativity but retain nonzero inverses; the expressions specify multiplication order and invertibility. No new structural theory is needed.

Proof 1: Specialize Woodbury and factor the correction

Local Woodbury identity

Let A,U,C,V be matrices over a unital ring, of sizes n×n,n×k,k×k,k×n respectively. Assume A,C,A+UCV and C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U are invertible. Then (A+UCV)^{-1}=A^{-1}-A^{-1}U(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U)^{-1}VA^{-1}.

The formula can be proven by checking that {\displaystyle (A+UCV)} times its alleged inverse on the right side of the Woodbury identity gives the identity matrix:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}&\left(A+UCV\right)\left[A^{-1}-A^{-1}U\left(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U\right)^{-1}VA^{-1}\right]\\={}&\left\{I-U\left(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U\right)^{-1}VA^{-1}\right\}+\left\{UCVA^{-1}-UCVA^{-1}U\left(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U\right)^{-1}VA^{-1}\right\}\\={}&\left\{I+UCVA^{-1}\right\}-\left\{U\left(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U\right)^{-1}VA^{-1}+UCVA^{-1}U\left(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U\right)^{-1}VA^{-1}\right\}\\={}&I+UCVA^{-1}-\left(U+UCVA^{-1}U\right)\left(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U\right)^{-1}VA^{-1}\\={}&I+UCVA^{-1}-UC\left(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U\right)\left(C^{-1}+VA^{-1}U\right)^{-1}VA^{-1}\\={}&I+UCVA^{-1}-UCVA^{-1}\\={}&I.\end{aligned}}}

Identity-block specialization

If n = k and U = V = In is the identity matrix, then

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\left(A+B\right)^{-1}&=A^{-1}-A^{-1}\left(B^{-1}+A^{-1}\right)^{-1}A^{-1}\\[1ex]&=A^{-1}-A^{-1}\left(AB^{-1}+{I}\right)^{-1}.\end{aligned}}}

Continuing with the merging of the terms of the far right-hand side of the above equation results in Hua's identity

{\displaystyle \left({A}+{B}\right)^{-1}={A}^{-1}-\left({A}+{A}{B}^{-1}{A}\right)^{-1}.}
