Statement

For n\ge1, put \varphi(n)=\#\{1\le r\le n:\gcd(r,n)=1\}. If n=pq for distinct primes p,q, then p and q are exactly the roots of X^2-(n-\varphi(n)+1)X+n=0. Thus knowledge of n and \varphi(n) determines these factors.
