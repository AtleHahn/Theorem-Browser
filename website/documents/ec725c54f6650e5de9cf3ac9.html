Statement

For subgroups K≤H≤G, with [A:B] denoting the cardinality of the set of left B-cosets in A, one has [G:K]=[G:H]·[H:K], including infinite cardinalities.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group/ring theory and linear algebra supply the objects. The extra Sylow, normalizer, invariant-polynomial or pseudoreflection condition is explicitly defined in subgroup, coordinate or polynomial terms; no specialist extension or representation theory is required.

Proof 1: Refine each H-coset into K-cosets

Let S be a set of coset representatives for K in H, so

H=\bigsqcup_{s\in S}sK

(disjoint union), and |S|=[H:K]. For any a\in G, left-multiplication-by-a is a bijection G\to G, so

aH=\bigsqcup_{s\in S}asK.

Thus each left coset of H decomposes into [H:K] left cosets of K. Since G decomposes into [G:H] left cosets of H, each of which decomposes into [H:K] left cosets of K, the total number [G:K] of left cosets of K in G is [G:H][H:K].

Level justification

Choose coset representatives and refine every H-coset into translated K-cosets. Introductory group theory plus cardinal multiplication as Cartesian-product size suffices for finite and infinite indices.
