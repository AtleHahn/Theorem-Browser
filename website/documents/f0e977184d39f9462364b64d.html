Statement

Let X be a real-valued random variable and let F(u)=\Pr(X\le u). For each x\in\mathbb R, \int_{-\infty}^{x}F(u)\,du=\mathbb E[\max(0,x-X)]=x-\mathbb E[\min(x,X)]. The integral and the expectation of the nonnegative quantity may be +\infty; since \min(x,X)\le x, its expectation is well-defined in [-\infty,\infty).
