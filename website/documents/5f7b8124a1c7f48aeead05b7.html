Statement

Let G be a finite group, p an odd prime and O_p(G)\ne1. Write O_{p'}(G) for the largest normal subgroup of order coprime to p, O_p(G) for the largest normal subgroup of p-power order, and O_{p',p}(G) for the inverse image of O_p(G/O_{p'}(G)) in G. Assume:

  • Whenever P is a Sylow p-subgroup of O_{p',p}(G), C_G(P)\subseteq O_{p',p}(G).
  • Whenever P\le G is a p-subgroup with PO_{p'}(G) normal in G, and x\in N_G(P) satisfies [[u,x],x]=1 for every u\in P, the image of x in N_G(P)/C_G(P) lies in its largest normal p-subgroup. Then for every Sylow p-subgroup S of G, the subgroup O_{p'}(G)Z(J(S)) is normal in G. Here J(S) is generated by all abelian subgroups of S of maximum order, Z(H) is the center, C_G(P) consists of elements commuting with every element of P, N_G(P)=\{g:gPg^{-1}=P\}, and [u,x]=u^{-1}x^{-1}ux. A Sylow p-subgroup has order equal to the largest power of p dividing the ambient group’s order. The two hypotheses express the source’s p-constrained and p-stable conditions.
