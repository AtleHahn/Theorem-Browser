Statement

Let S=\{p+2^k:p\text{ prime},\ k\ge1\text{ integer}\}. The odd positive integers outside S have positive lower asymptotic density, meaning \liminf_{x\to\infty}|\{n\le x:n\text{ odd},n\notin S\}|/x>0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Natural density via liminf/limsup; Prime numbers and powers. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Compare with the density of odd integers — outline

Use the published bound \limsup_{x\to\infty}|S\cap[1,x]|/x<1/2. Since odd positive integers have density 1/2, the odd integers not represented in S have positive lower density.
