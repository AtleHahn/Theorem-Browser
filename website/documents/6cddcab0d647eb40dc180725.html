Statement

Consider a finite resolution-proof directed acyclic graph, whose edges run from premises toward the conclusion. A unit node has a clause containing one literal. Collect selected unit nodes in reverse topological order, starting at the conclusion, and reinsert them in queue order. If a selected node \eta' with unit literal \ell occurs in the derivation of another selected node \eta, then \eta' is reinserted after \eta. Thus a complementary literal \bar\ell propagated into the repaired clause of \eta by removal of \eta' can be cancelled by that later unit resolution.

Level
High school only
Level justification

A finite acyclic dependency graph, reverse ordering, signed literals and unit cancellation have elementary finite meanings; no completeness or resolution-search theory is needed.

Proof 1: Order dependent units from the conclusion upward — outline

Care must be taken with cases when a unit node {\displaystyle \eta ^{\prime }} occurs above in the subproof that derives another unit node {\displaystyle \eta }. In such cases, {\displaystyle \eta } depends on {\displaystyle \eta ^{\prime }}. Let {\displaystyle \ell } be the single literal of the unit clause of {\displaystyle \eta ^{\prime }}. Then any occurrence of {\displaystyle {\overline {\ell }}} in the subproof above {\displaystyle \eta } will not be cancelled by resolution inferences with {\displaystyle \eta ^{\prime }} anymore. Consequently, {\displaystyle {\overline {\ell }}} will be propagated downwards when the proof is fixed and may appear in the clause of {\displaystyle \eta }. Difficulties with such dependencies can be easily avoided if we reinsert the upper unit node {\displaystyle \eta ^{\prime }} after reinserting the unit node {\displaystyle \eta } (i.e. after reinsertion, {\displaystyle \eta ^{\prime }} must appear below {\displaystyle \eta }, to cancel the extra literal {\displaystyle {\overline {\ell }}} from {\displaystyle \eta }’s clause). This can be ensured by collecting the unit nodes in a queue during a bottom-up traversal of the proof and reinserting them in the order they were queued.

Here “bottom-up” means reverse topological order, starting at the conclusion and proceeding toward the premises. If \eta' occurs in the derivation of \eta, then \eta is queued first. The cancellation is the unit-resolution step from C\cup\{\bar\ell\} and \{\ell\} to C.
