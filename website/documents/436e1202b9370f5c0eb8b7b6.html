Statement

An 8\times8 Numbrix completion labels the squares bijectively by 1,\ldots,64, with consecutive labels in squares sharing an edge. If label 1 is fixed in one corner and 64 in the diagonally opposite corner, there is no completion.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All board squares, permitted moves or labels, and endpoint conditions are explicitly finite and elementary.

Proof 1: Reduction to the alternating-color tour obstruction

A completion would produce a tour through every square by one-square horizontal or vertical moves, starting at the corner labeled 1 and ending at the opposite corner labeled 64. Each move changes color, so after 63 moves the endpoint has the opposite color from the start. Opposite corners have the same color, a contradiction.

Level justification
