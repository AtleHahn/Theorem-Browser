Statement

On hyperboloid models of hyperbolic two- and three-space, let E extend a function independently of the third coordinate, and for a radial two-space function written in hyperbolic distance t, put (Sf)(t)=f(2t). Set M_0=ES. Let P average functions on three-space over the stabilizer SU(2) of its origin using normalized Haar measure, and put M=PM_0. Then the positive hyperbolic Laplacians satisfy \Delta_3Mf=4M\Delta_2f for smooth radial f.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Hyperbolic Laplacians; Compact group averaging; Radial descent operators. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Commute compact averaging with the Laplacian

Use the fundamental descent relation of Flensted–Jensen for M_0=ES:

\Delta_3M_0f=4M_0\Delta_2f.

The same relation holds with M_0 replaced by M, where Mf is obtained by averaging M_0f over SU(2). Here the action of SL(2,\mathbb C) on hyperbolic three-space commutes with \Delta_3, so its compact subgroup SU(2) does also.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The formal commutation step is short, but the proof requires hyperbolic-space models and radial differential operators, the SU(2) stabilizer action and integration against normalized Haar measure. These combined geometric and harmonic-analysis prerequisites exceed the undergraduate allowance. It also uses differentiation under compact-group averaging, not just algebraic replacement of operators.

Theorem

Treatment:

Statement used:

The hyperbolic Laplacian commutes with pullback by isometries; for smooth functions, averaging over a compact group of isometries with Haar measure also commutes with the Laplacian.

Application in proof:

Move Delta_3 past P and obtain Delta_3 P M_0 f=4 P M_0 Delta_2 f.
