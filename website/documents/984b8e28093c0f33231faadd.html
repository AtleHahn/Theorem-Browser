Statement

In distributions on the real variable \omega, for any fixed real \nu and either sign, with the oscillatory integral interpreted by Abel damping, \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\int_0^\infty e^{-i(\omega\pm\nu)t-\varepsilon t}\,dt=\pi\delta(\omega\pm\nu)-i\operatorname{PV}(1/(\omega\pm\nu)). The limit is tested against smooth compactly supported functions.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The formula is a distributional Fourier identity, not an ordinary convergent integral.

Proof 1: Damp the exponential and apply the distributional boundary formula

Put u=\omega\pm\nu. For positive \varepsilon, integration in t gives -i/(u-i\varepsilon). Apply the distributional Sokhotski–Plemelj formula \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}(u-i\varepsilon)^{-1}=\operatorname{PV}(1/u)+i\pi\delta(u). Multiplying by -i gives the asserted identity, with the translated variable u=\omega\pm\nu.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Evaluate the damped exponential integral and use the distributional boundary formula. Test-function convergence, Dirac and principal-value distributions require graduate analysis.
