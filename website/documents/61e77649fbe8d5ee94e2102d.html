Statement

Let \omega_\alpha be the \alphath infinite initial ordinal. The ordinals \theta satisfying \theta=\omega_\theta form an unbounded class closed under suprema of its nonempty subsets that are sets.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Transfinite indexing of initial ordinals and unbounded proper classes require substantial ordinal and cardinal theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply the normal-function fixed-point lemma

The source states that the function f(\alpha)=\omega_\alpha is normal. The fixed-point lemma for normal functions therefore supplies a closed unbounded class of fixed points, which are exactly the ordinals satisfying \theta=\omega_\theta.
