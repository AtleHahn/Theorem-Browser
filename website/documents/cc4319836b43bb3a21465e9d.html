Statement

Let n\geq1 and let A^{(0)}=(a_{ij}^{(0)}) be a real n\times n matrix with every entry strictly positive. For each integer k\geq0, normalize rows and then columns by

a_{ij}^{(2k+1)}=\frac{a_{ij}^{(2k)}}{\sum_{\ell=1}^n a_{i\ell}^{(2k)}},\qquad a_{ij}^{(2k+2)}=\frac{a_{ij}^{(2k+1)}}{\sum_{\ell=1}^n a_{\ell j}^{(2k+1)}}.

Then the full sequence A^{(m)} converges entrywise as m\to\infty to the unique doubly stochastic matrix of the form D_1A^{(0)}D_2, where D_1,D_2 are diagonal matrices with strictly positive diagonal entries. Doubly stochastic means that entries are nonnegative and every row sum and column sum is 1.
