Statement

Let R be a unital ring and assume a,b,ab-1 are units. Then a-\bigl(a^{-1}+(b^{-1}-a)^{-1}\bigr)^{-1}=aba.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Ring multiplication, units and matrix inverses are introductory algebra. Division rings drop commutativity but retain nonzero inverses; the expressions specify multiplication order and invertibility. No new structural theory is needed.

Proof 1: Hua’s identity for units in a ring — outline

The elements b^{-1}-a=(1-ab)b^{-1} and a-aba=(1-ab)a are units under the stated hypotheses.

One has

{\displaystyle (a-aba)\left(a^{-1}+\left(b^{-1}-a\right)^{-1}\right)=1-ab+ab\left(b^{-1}-a\right)\left(b^{-1}-a\right)^{-1}=1.}

Since a-aba is a unit, the displayed product identifies a^{-1}+(b^{-1}-a)^{-1} with its two-sided inverse, giving the identity.
