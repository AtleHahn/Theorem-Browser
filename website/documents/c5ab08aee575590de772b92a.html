Statement

If p is an odd prime and a is an integer with p\nmid a, then a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement is a power congruence for integers at a prime modulus.

Proof 1: Square the quadratic-character congruence

For an odd prime p and an integer a not divisible by p, write \chi_p(a)=1 if x^2\equiv a\pmod p has an integer solution, and \chi_p(a)=-1 otherwise. Congruence modulo p means divisibility of the difference by p. Euler’s criterion gives a^{(p-1)/2}\equiv\chi_p(a). Squaring both sides gives a^{p-1}\equiv\chi_p(a)^2=1.
