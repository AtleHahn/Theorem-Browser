The curve cone of a smooth cubic surface is rational polyhedral

Statement

For a smooth cubic surface X over an algebraically closed field, the cone generated by curve classes in the real vector space N_1(X) of 1-cycles modulo numerical equivalence is a rational polyhedral cone in N_1(X)\cong\mathbb R^7. Rational polyhedral here means generated by finitely many rational classes.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves numerical equivalence of curves; cones of curves; rational polyhedral cones. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use the finite set of integral line classes

The cone is spanned by the 27 lines on the surface. Their classes are integral, hence rational. Thus this finite spanning set makes the cone rational polyhedral in the stated seven-dimensional space.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The finite-generation inference is short, but understanding curve classes modulo numerical equivalence, N1 and the cubic surface cone requires intersection theory. This is not just a finite-dimensional cone calculation divorced from its objects.
