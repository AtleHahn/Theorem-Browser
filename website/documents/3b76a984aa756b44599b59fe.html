Statement

For a positive integer n, write \sigma(n)=\sum_{d\mid n,d>0}d, and let \varphi(n) count the integers in \{1,\ldots,n\} relatively prime to n. Let n=pq for primes p<q. Set A=(\sigma(n)-\varphi(n))/4 and B=(\sigma(n)+\varphi(n))/2-1. Then p=A-\sqrt{A^2-B} and q=A+\sqrt{A^2-B}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The formulas and totient count are finite numerical objects with local definitions.

Proof 1: Recover sum and product, then solve the quadratic

As an example, for two primes {\displaystyle p,q:p<q}, let

{\displaystyle n=p\,q}.

Then

{\displaystyle \sigma (n)=(p+1)(q+1)=n+1+(p+q),}{\displaystyle \varphi (n)=(p-1)(q-1)=n+1-(p+q),}

and

{\displaystyle n+1=(\sigma (n)+\varphi (n))/2,}{\displaystyle p+q=(\sigma (n)-\varphi (n))/2,}

where {\displaystyle \varphi (n)} is Euler's totient function.

Then, the roots of

{\displaystyle (x-p)(x-q)=x^{2}-(p+q)x+n=x^{2}-[(\sigma (n)-\varphi (n))/2]x+[(\sigma (n)+\varphi (n))/2-1]=0}

express p and q in terms of σ(n) and φ(n) only, requiring no knowledge of n or {\displaystyle p+q}, as

{\displaystyle p=(\sigma (n)-\varphi (n))/4-{\sqrt {[(\sigma (n)-\varphi (n))/4]^{2}-[(\sigma (n)+\varphi (n))/2-1]}},}{\displaystyle q=(\sigma (n)-\varphi (n))/4+{\sqrt {[(\sigma (n)-\varphi (n))/4]^{2}-[(\sigma (n)+\varphi (n))/2-1]}}.}

With A=(\sigma(n)-\varphi(n))/4 and B=(\sigma(n)+\varphi(n))/2-1, these are the formulas in the statement.
