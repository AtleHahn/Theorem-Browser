Statement

For any measure space (E,\mu) and f,g\in L^2(E), \|f+g\|_2^2+\|f-g\|_2^2=2\|f\|_2^2+2\|g\|_2^2, where \|h\|_2=(\int_E|h|^2\,d\mu)^{1/2}.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Arbitrary measure spaces and Lp functions require measure-theoretic analysis beyond the specified baseline.

Proof 1: Specialize the overlapping ranges to p=2 — outline

At p=2 both directions of the first Hanner inequality apply, so \|f+g\|_2^2+\|f-g\|_2^2=(\|f\|_2+\|g\|_2)^2+|\|f\|_2-\|g\|_2|^2. Expanding the two squares gives the parallelogram equality.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply both directions of Hanner at the common endpoint and expand squares. The algebra is elementary, but the full application is in general measure-space L2; conservatively retain the prerequisite of Lebesgue norms.
