Statement

For a Dirichlet character \chi, set A_\chi(x)=\sum_{n\leq x}\chi(n). For \Re s>1, define L(s,\chi)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\chi(n)n^{-s}. Then L(s,\chi)=s\int_1^\infty A_\chi(x)x^{-s-1}\,dx.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A Dirichlet character is a periodic multiplicative function induced by a character of the finite unit group, extended by zero. This connects directly to introductory group homomorphisms; the series and integral are explicit calculus expressions with complex powers, so no L-function theory is required for comprehension.

Proof 1: Abel summation specialization

In Abel summation g(s)=s\int_1^\infty A(x)x^{-s-1}\,dx, take a(n)=\chi(n). Its Dirichlet series is L(s,\chi) and its summatory function is A_\chi. This gives the formula in the common absolute-convergence half-plane.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The argument substitutes a bounded periodic character sequence into the supplied Abel summation formula. Introductory real analysis with complex-valued sequences and the definition of a finite residue character suffices; analytic continuation and complex contour methods are absent.
