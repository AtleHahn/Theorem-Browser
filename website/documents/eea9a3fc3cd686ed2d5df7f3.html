Statement

Let G(k) be the least s such that every sufficiently large positive integer is a sum of at most s positive integer kth powers. Then G(3)\ge4.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement defines the asymptotic summand count and compares it with four.

Proof 1: Cubic residues modulo nine (outline)

G(3) is at least 4, since cubes are congruent to 0, 1 or -1 modulo 9. The source leaves the residue obstruction and its arbitrarily large instances implicit.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Use the three possible cube residues modulo nine to find unbounded excluded residue classes for sums of three cubes. Integer remainders suffice.
