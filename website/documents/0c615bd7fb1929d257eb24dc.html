Sections of linearly equivalent divisors are isomorphic

Statement

Let X be a connected compact Riemann surface. A divisor is a finite integer sum of points. For a divisor D, let L(D) consist of zero and all meromorphic functions h such that (h)+D\ge0, where (h) records zero orders positively and pole orders negatively. If E-D is the principal divisor of a nonzero meromorphic function, then L(D) and L(E) are naturally isomorphic by multiplication by that function or its inverse.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification
