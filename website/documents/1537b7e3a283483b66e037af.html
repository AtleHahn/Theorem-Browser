Statement

Define \chi(X)=\sum_{j\ge0}(-1)^j b_j(X), where b_j(X)=\dim_{\mathbb Q}H_j(X;\mathbb Q); these dimensions are finite and vanish for all sufficiently large j. Every closed odd-dimensional manifold M has χ(M)=0.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Manifold topology and homology are beyond the baseline.

Proof 1: Use Poincaré duality and the orientable double cover — outline

The Euler characteristic of any closed odd-dimensional manifold is also 0. The case for orientable examples is a corollary of Poincaré duality. It also applies to closed odd-dimensional non-orientable manifolds, via the two-to-one orientable double cover.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Pair Betti numbers using manifold duality, then handle nonorientability with an orientation cover and Euler-characteristic multiplicativity. Homology, orientability, and manifold covering theory are substantial prerequisites despite the short alternating-sum calculation.
