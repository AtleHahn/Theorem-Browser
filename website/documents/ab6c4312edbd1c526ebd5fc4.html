Statement

A straightedge together with a compass of any fixed positive radius can construct every point constructible by an ordinary compass and straightedge.

Level

Proof 1: Draw one fixed-radius circle and invoke Poncelet–Steiner

Draw one circle with the rusty compass, retaining its chosen center. Invoke the Poncelet–Steiner theorem: a straightedge alone, supplied with a nondegenerate circle and its center, can perform all compass-and-straightedge point constructions. Therefore no further change of compass radius is needed.

Level justification
