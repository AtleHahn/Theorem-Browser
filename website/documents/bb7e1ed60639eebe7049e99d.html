Statement

Let A\subseteq B be commutative rings with the same identity and suppose B is flat as an A-module. If \mathfrak q is a prime ideal of B and \mathfrak p=\mathfrak q\cap A, then the induced homomorphism of localizations A_{\mathfrak p}\to B_{\mathfrak q} is faithfully flat.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement and argument require commutative algebra beyond calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Apply faithful flatness of a flat local homomorphism

Localizing the flat extension gives a flat homomorphism A_{\mathfrak p}\to B_{\mathfrak q}. It is local because \mathfrak q\cap A=\mathfrak p. By the theorem that a flat local homomorphism is faithfully flat, it is faithfully flat.

Level justification

Localize the flat extension, check the maximal ideals contract correctly and apply faithful flatness of a local map. Flatness, localization and faithful tensor functors require graduate commutative algebra.
