Statement

Let M be an oriented smooth manifold of dimension n\geq1, with boundary \partial M given the induced boundary orientation. If \omega is a smooth differential (n-1)-form with compact support on M, then

\int_M d\omega=\int_{\partial M}\omega.

Here d is the exterior derivative; on the right, \omega denotes its pullback to the boundary. Compact support means that the closure of the set where \omega is nonzero is compact in M. The induced orientation is the outward-normal-first convention: an outward transverse vector followed by a positively oriented boundary basis is a positively oriented basis of M.

Equivalently, in the smooth-chain formulation, if c is a smooth singular k-chain in a smooth manifold and \eta is a smooth (k-1)-form, for k\geq1, then

\int_c d\eta=\int_{\partial c}\eta.

A smooth singular k-chain is a finite integer linear combination of smooth maps from the standard k-simplex into the manifold. Its boundary is the alternating sum of the restrictions to simplex faces, extended linearly, and integration over a chain uses pullback to each simplex and linearity.
