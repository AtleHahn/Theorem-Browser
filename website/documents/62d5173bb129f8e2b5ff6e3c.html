Assume every even integer greater than 4 is a sum of two odd primes. Then every odd integer greater than 5 is a sum of three primes, with repetitions allowed.

Proof 1: Add the prime three and check seven

For an odd integer n>7, the even integer n-3>4 is a sum p+q of two odd primes by the hypothesis. Thus n=p+q+3. The remaining case is 7=2+2+3.
