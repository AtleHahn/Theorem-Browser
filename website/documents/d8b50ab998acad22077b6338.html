Statement

Let p_i,q_i be continuous real functions on [a,b] and let nonvanishing real C^1 functions x_i, with p_ix_i' continuously differentiable, satisfy (p_ix_i')'+q_ix_i=0, x_i(a)=1, for i=1,2. All integrals are with respect to t, and [F]_a^b=F(b)-F(a). Put r_i=x_i'/x_i and W(x_2,x_1)=x_2' x_1-x_2x_1'. The two-equation specialization of Mingarelli’s identity is [x_1^2(p_2r_2-p_1r_1)]_a^b=\int_a^b(x_1')^2(p_2-p_1)-\int_a^bx_1^2(q_2-q_1)-\int_a^bp_2W(x_2,x_1)^2/x_2^2.

Proof 1: Set n equal to two and cancel the self-Wronskian

Import the general Mingarelli identity for n\ge2 classical nonvanishing real solutions x_i of (p_ix_i')'+q_ix_i=0 on [a,b], x_i(a)=1, with the regularity in the statement. For every index set r_i=x_i'/x_i and W(x_i,x_j)=x_i'x_j-x_ix_j'. Its forward differences are defined by \Delta u_i=u_{i+1}-u_i: [x_{n-1}^2\Delta^{n-1}(p_1r_1)]_a^b=\int_a^b(x_{n-1}')^2\Delta^{n-1}p_1-\int_a^bx_{n-1}^2\Delta^{n-1}q_1-\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\binom{n-1}{k}(-1)^{n-k-1}\int_a^bp_{k+1}W(x_{k+1},x_{n-1})^2/x_{k+1}^2. When n=2 this equality reduces to the displayed two-equation identity.
