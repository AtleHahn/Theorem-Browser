Statement

Let X,Y be topological spaces and F a field. Write b_k(Z)=dim_F H_k(Z;F) as a cardinal and p_Z(t)=∑{k≥0}b_k(Z)t^k. Interpret multiplication coefficientwise by cardinal sums and products if dimensions are infinite. Then p{X×Y}(t)=p_X(t)p_Y(t). In particular, for F=Q these are the Betti-number series; when finitely many nonzero dimensions occur and are finite, the identity is one of Poincaré polynomials.

Proof 1: Take dimensions in the field Künneth isomorphism

The field Künneth formula gives

H_k(X\times Y;F)\cong\bigoplus_{i+j=k}H_i(X;F)\otimes_F H_j(Y;F).

Taking dimensions gives b_k(X\times Y)=\sum_{i+j=k}b_i(X)b_j(Y). These are exactly the coefficients in the product of the two dimension series. The same computation applies to cardinal dimensions, with cardinal arithmetic, and gives the asserted polynomial specialization when all but finitely many dimensions are zero and the remainder are finite.
