Statement

Let T:V\to W be a linear map of vector spaces over a field F. Then \dim_F V=\dim_F\ker T+\dim_F\operatorname{im}T, where dimensions may be cardinal numbers.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Kernels, images and bases are undergraduate linear algebra. In the infinite-dimensional version, dimension is basis cardinality and cardinal addition is disjoint-union size, a direct set-theoretic extension without ordinal arithmetic.

Proof 1: Quotient-space dimension argument (outline)

The first isomorphism theorem gives V/\ker T\cong\operatorname{im}T. Applying the dimension formula for a subspace and its quotient gives \dim V=\dim\ker T+\dim(V/\ker T)=\dim\ker T+\dim\operatorname{im}T.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the vector-space quotient isomorphism and the stated dimension formula. Undergraduate linear algebra suffices with the small extension of counting a basis by a cardinal and interpreting addition as disjoint union.
