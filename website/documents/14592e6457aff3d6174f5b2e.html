Statement

Let k be a field and G a linearly reductive affine algebraic group over k. Let R be a commutative regular Noetherian k-algebra with a rational G-action by k-algebra automorphisms. Then the invariant subring R^G is Cohen–Macaulay.

In the graded representation formulation, if V is a finite-dimensional rational representation of G and k[V] is its ring of polynomial functions, there are algebraically independent homogeneous invariants f_1,\ldots,f_d such that k[V]^G is a finite free graded module over the polynomial subring k[f_1,\ldots,f_d].

Linearly reductive means that every finite-dimensional rational representation is a direct sum of irreducible representations. A rational action makes R a sum of finite-dimensional rational G-subrepresentations. The invariant subring consists of elements fixed by the action. Regular means that each localization at a prime is a regular local ring: the minimal number of generators of its maximal ideal equals its Krull dimension. Cohen–Macaulay means that at each maximal ideal the localization has depth equal to its Krull dimension; depth is the maximal length of a regular sequence in that maximal ideal. A regular sequence consists successively of non-zero-divisors after quotienting by its predecessors, with proper generated ideal. Krull dimension is the supremum of lengths of chains of prime ideals.
