Statement

Let R be a left Artinian unital ring and M a finitely generated left R-module. Then M has a finite composition series: a finite strict chain 0=M_0\subset\cdots\subset M_\ell=M with each quotient M_i/M_{i-1} a simple R-module. The zero module has the length-zero series.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The hypotheses and finite simple-quotient filtration are notions of abstract ring and module theory.

Proof 1: Combine the chain conditions with the finite-length criterion

A module has a finite composition series if and only if it is both an Artinian module and a Noetherian module. If R is a left Artinian ring, then every finitely generated left R-module is Artinian and Noetherian, and thus has a finite composition series.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Combine the two supplied finite-length criteria, but understanding their hypotheses requires general ring modules, Artinian and Noetherian module conditions and simple quotient composition series. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally for the cumulative algebraic background.
