Statement

The ideal class group of every number field K is finite.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The assertion concerns the ideal class group of an algebraic number field. These are algebraic-number-theory notions beyond the calculus/linear algebra baseline.

Proof 1: Count the bounded-norm ideal representatives

Let D be the discriminant of the field, n the degree of K over \mathbb Q, and 2r_2 the number of complex embeddings. Every class in the ideal class group of K contains an integral ideal of norm not exceeding Minkowski's bound

M_K=\sqrt{|D|}\left(\frac4\pi\right)^{r_2}\frac{n!}{n^n}.

Since the number of integral ideals of given norm is finite, the finiteness of the class number is an immediate consequence.
