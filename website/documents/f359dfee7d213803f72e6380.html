Statement

Let R be a right noetherian ring of finite right global dimension. The inclusion of finitely generated projective right R-modules into finitely generated right R-modules induces isomorphisms

K_i(R)\cong G_i(R)\qquad(i\geq0).

Finite right global dimension means that some integer d\geq0 bounds the length of a projective resolution of every right R-module: each has an exact resolution by projective modules of length at most d. A projective module is a direct summand of a free module.

Here R is associative with identity, all modules are right modules, and noetherian means that every right ideal is finitely generated.

To define G_i(S) for a right noetherian ring S, let \mathcal M(S) be the category of finitely generated right S-modules and S-linear maps, with all short exact sequences. Form Quillen's category Q\mathcal M(S): it has the same objects, and a morphism M\to N is an isomorphism class of diagrams M\leftarrow U\rightarrow N whose left arrow is surjective and whose right arrow is injective. Composition uses pullback of the middle diagrams. Let BQ\mathcal M(S) be the geometric realization of its nerve, whose simplices are finite chains of composable morphisms. Using the zero module as basepoint, define

G_i(S)=\pi_{i+1}(BQ\mathcal M(S),0)\qquad(i\geq0).

The symbol \pi_j denotes the homotopy group of based continuous maps from the j-sphere, modulo based homotopy. A small skeleton of the module category can be used in forming the nerve.

For K_i(R) use the category \mathcal P(R) of finitely generated projective right modules with its split short exact sequences in place of \mathcal M(R). Its Q-construction uses diagrams with a split surjection on the left and a split injection with projective cokernel on the right. Then K_i(R)=\pi_{i+1}(BQ\mathcal P(R),0). The inclusion gives the asserted comparison map.
