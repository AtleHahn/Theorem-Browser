Statement

Let u,v,w be positive integers satisfying u^2+v^2=w^2. Put a=u|4v^2-w^2|, b=v|4u^2-w^2|, and c=4uvw. These are positive integer edges of an Euler brick, with face diagonals d=w^3, e=u(4v^2+w^2) and f=v(4u^2+w^2); that is, a^2+b^2=d^2, a^2+c^2=e^2, b^2+c^2=f^2.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Every formula is given in elementary integer arithmetic and the geometric conclusion is spelled out by its three equations.

Proof 1: Use the supplied Pythagorean parameter formulas — outline

Let (u, v, w) be a Pythagorean triple (that is, u2 + v2 = w2.) Then the edges

{\displaystyle a=u|4v^{2}-w^{2}|,\quad b=v|4u^{2}-w^{2}|,\quad c=4uvw}

give face diagonals

{\displaystyle d=w^{3},\quad e=u(4v^{2}+w^{2}),\quad f=v(4u^{2}+w^{2}).}

The source supplies these formulas without expanding the three identities or separately verifying positivity of the edges.
