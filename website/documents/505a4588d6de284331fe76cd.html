Statement

With \mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\} and 2\mathbb N=\{2n:n\in\mathbb N\}, the map f:\mathbb N\to2\mathbb N given by f(n)=2n is a bijection.

Level

Level justification

The sets and doubling map are explicit integer formulas; bijection means each target has exactly one preimage.
