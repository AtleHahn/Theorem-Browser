Statement

For four Euclidean points put x=AB\cdot CD, y=BC\cdot DA, z=AC\cdot BD. Then x\le y+z, y\le x+z, and z\le x+y.

Level
High school only

Proof 1: Relabel the four points

Use Ptolemy’s inequality AB\cdot CD+BC\cdot DA\ge AC\cdot BD for any four Euclidean points. It gives z\le x+y. Relabel the points so that either other opposite-pair product is the diagonal product, giving the other two inequalities.
