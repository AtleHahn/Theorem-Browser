Statement

Let K be a number field with signature (r_1,r_2) and let p be a rational prime. For each prime P of K above p, let U_{1,P} be the principal unit subgroup in the completion K_P. Let E_1 be the subgroup of global units O_K^\times whose images under the diagonal embedding lie in \prod_{P\mid p}U_{1,P}. Then E_1 has rank r_1+r_2-1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Local completions of number fields, principal local units and the diagonal global-unit subgroup require substantial algebraic number theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Preserve rank under finite index

The subgroup E_1 has finite index in the global unit group. Consequently it is an abelian group of the same rank, namely r_1+r_2-1 by Dirichlet’s unit theorem.
