Statement

Every finite simple graph embedded without crossings on the torus admits a proper coloring with at most seven colors.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The torus is the familiar product of two circles or square with opposite edges identified. Finite map/graph coloring and crossing-free drawing are elementary, so the color bound requires no fundamental groups or surface classification.

Proof 1: The genus-one Heawood specialization

The torus has genus g=1 and Euler characteristic zero. The Heawood formula gives \lfloor(7+\sqrt{1+48})/2\rfloor=7, so seven colors suffice.

Level justification
