Proof 1: Almost all zeta zeros lie arbitrarily near the critical line

Most zeros lie close to the critical line. More precisely, Bohr & Landau (1914) showed that for any positive ε, the number of zeros with real part at least 1/2+ε and imaginary part at between −T and T is {\displaystyle O(T)}. Combined with the facts that zeros on the critical strip are symmetric about the critical line and that the total number of zeros in the critical strip is {\displaystyle \Theta (T\log T)}, almost all non-trivial zeros are within a distance ε of the critical line.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Dividing O(T) by Theta(T log T) is baseline analysis, but the full count concerns nontrivial meromorphic zeta zeros with multiplicity and reflection symmetry.
