Statement

Let a_n,\zeta_n>0 for every positive integer n, and suppose \zeta_na_n/a_{n+1}-\zeta_{n+1}=0 for every n. Then \sum_{n=1}^\infty1/\zeta_n diverges if and only if \sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n diverges.

Proof 1: Constant-product recurrence — outline

Under the zero-parameter condition, the requirement that \sum1/\zeta_n diverge reduces to the divergence of \sum a_n itself.
