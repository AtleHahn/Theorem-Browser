Statement

Let X,Y be Banach spaces and T:D(T)\subseteq X\to Y a closed densely defined linear operator. Its transpose has domain

D(T')=\{y'\in Y':x\mapsto y'(Tx)\text{ extends continuously to }X\},

and T'y' is this unique continuous extension. Write N and R for kernel and range. For S\subseteq Y', put S^\perp=\{y\in Y:y'(y)=0\text{ for all }y'\in S\}, and for S\subseteq X put S^\perp=\{x'\in X':x'(x)=0\text{ for all }x\in S\}. Then

R(T)\subseteq N(T')^\perp,\qquad R(T')\subseteq N(T)^\perp.
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns continuous duals and densely defined operators on arbitrary Banach spaces, beyond finite-dimensional linear algebra.

Proof 1: Evaluate the defining transpose pairing

If y=Tx and y'\in N(T'), then

y'(y)=y'(Tx)=(T'y')(x)=0.

This proves the first inclusion. Likewise, for x\in N(T) and y'\in D(T'), the same transpose identity gives (T'y')(x)=y'(Tx)=0, proving the second.
