Statement

Let D,N\ge1 be integers. For a nonempty bounded S\subset\mathbb R^D, define V(S)=\sup_{x\in\operatorname{conv}S}\inf\{\operatorname{Var}(X):X\text{ is finitely supported in }S,\ E X=x\}, where \operatorname{Var}(X)=E\|X-E X\|^2. For nonempty bounded Q_1,\ldots,Q_N\subset\mathbb R^D, put Q=\sum_nQ_n. Then V(Q)\le\sum_{\max D}V(Q_n), where the right side sums the largest \min(D,N) values.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Convex hulls and vector norms are linear-algebra/calculus concepts. Every random vector here has finite support, so expectation and variance are finite weighted sums; the nested infimum/supremum is explicitly defined. No general measure theory is required.

Proof 1: Independent finite random vectors — outline

For every {\displaystyle x\in \operatorname {conv} Q}, by Shapley–Folkman lemma, there exists a representation {\displaystyle x=\sum _{n\in I}x_{n}+\sum _{n\in J}q_{n}}, such that {\displaystyle I,J} partitions {\displaystyle \{1,2,...,N\}}, {\displaystyle |I|\leq D}, {\displaystyle x_{n}\in \operatorname {conv} Q_{n}}, and {\displaystyle q_{n}\in Q_{n}}.

Now, for each {\displaystyle n\in I}, construct random vectors {\displaystyle X_{n}} such that {\displaystyle X_{n}} is finitely supported on {\displaystyle Q_{n}}, with {\displaystyle \mathbb {E} [X_{n}]=x_{n}} and {\displaystyle \operatorname {Var} [X_{n}]<V(Q_{n})+\epsilon }, where {\displaystyle \epsilon >0} is an arbitrary small number.

Let all such {\displaystyle X_{n}} be independent. Then let {\displaystyle X=\sum _{n\in I}X_{n}+\sum _{n\in J}q_{n}}. Since each {\displaystyle q_{n}} is a deterministic vector, we have

{\displaystyle \operatorname {Var} [X]=\operatorname {Var} \left[\sum _{n\in I}X_{n}\right]=\sum _{n\in I}\operatorname {Var} \left[X_{n}\right]\leq \sum _{n\in I}\left(V(Q_{n})+\epsilon \right)\leq \sum _{\max D}V(Q_{n})+D\epsilon .}

The vector X is finitely supported in Q and has mean x. Take the infimum over such vectors X, then let \epsilon\downarrow0 and take the supremum over x\in\operatorname{conv}Q. This gives V(Q)\leq\sum_{\max D}V(Q_n).
