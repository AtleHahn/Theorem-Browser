Statement

For any set X with counting measure, the real space \ell^2(X) is an RKHS with K_x=\mathbf1_{\{x\}} and K(x,y)=1 when x=y, and zero otherwise.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Square-summable families extend undergraduate series, with an arbitrary nonnegative sum defined as the supremum of finite subsums. Completeness and the explicitly defined RKHS evaluation property add linked metric/inner-product definitions.

Proof 1: Use singleton indicators — outline

The space \ell^2(X)=L^2(X,\mu), where \mu is counting measure, has inner product

\langle f,g\rangle=\sum_{y\in X}f(y)g(y).

A reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS) is a Hilbert space of functions in which point evaluations are continuous; its kernel vectors satisfy f(x)=\langle f,K_x\rangle and K(x,y)=K_y(x).

For x\in X, the reproducing kernel K_x is the indicator function of the one point set \{x\}\subset X. The source supplies this construction without writing out the coordinate check.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The RKHS condition is explicitly defined. The singleton indicator has norm one, its inner product with f is f(x), and |f(x)|<=||f|| from the sum of squares. Concrete square-summable coordinates and completeness are a small extension of series and inner products; no representation or duality theorem is used.
