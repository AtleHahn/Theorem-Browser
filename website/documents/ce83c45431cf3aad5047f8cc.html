Statement

Let two circles of positive radii bound disjoint closed disks in the Euclidean plane, with centers P,Q. Choose a point F on the first circle where a tangent from Q touches it, and a point J on the second where a tangent from P touches it. Suppose the line FJ meets the first circle again at F^{\prime}\ne F and the second again at J^{\prime}\ne J. Then |FF^{\prime}|=|JJ^{\prime}|.
