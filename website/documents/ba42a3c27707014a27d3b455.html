Statement

Let (X,e) be a connected pointed CW complex. Its infinite symmetric product is

\operatorname{SP}(X)=\varinjlim_{m\geq0}X^m/S_m,

where S_m permutes the factors, X^0/S_0 is a point, and the maps to the next symmetric product append the basepoint e. Each finite symmetric product has the quotient topology, and the union has the direct-limit topology: a subset is open exactly when its intersection with every finite symmetric product is open. The basepoint is the empty tuple, identified with any tuple consisting only of e.

For every integer n\geq1, there is a natural isomorphism

\varphi_X:\pi_n(\operatorname{SP}(X))\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }\widetilde H_n(X;\mathbb Z),

where \pi_n is the based homotopy group and \widetilde H_n(-;\mathbb Z) is reduced singular homology with integer coefficients. These isomorphisms commute with the maps induced by based continuous maps of connected CW complexes. They can be chosen compatibly with the Hurewicz homomorphism:

\varphi_X\circ i_*=h_X,

where i:X\to\operatorname{SP}(X) sends x to its one-point tuple and h_X:\pi_n(X)\to\widetilde H_n(X;\mathbb Z) is the Hurewicz homomorphism.
