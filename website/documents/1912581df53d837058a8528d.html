Statement

Consider ergodic continuous automorphisms of compact metrizable abelian groups, each equipped with normalized Haar measure. Within this class, two automorphisms are measurably isomorphic if and only if their measure-theoretic entropies are equal. Consequently the set of measurable-isomorphism classes is in bijection with the set of entropy values realized by this class, and these two sets have the same countability status.

Proof 1: Bernoulli models and Ornstein classification

Use the two results recalled in the source: every ergodic compact group automorphism is measurably isomorphic to a Bernoulli shift, and Bernoulli shifts are classified up to measurable isomorphism by their entropy (Ornstein’s theorem). Equal entropies therefore give isomorphic Bernoulli models and hence isomorphic automorphisms. Conversely, measurable isomorphism preserves entropy. Thus each realized entropy corresponds to exactly one measurable-isomorphism class, giving the asserted bijection and the countability consequence.
