Statement

The groups A_6 and \operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_9) are isomorphic, where \mathbb F_9=\mathbb F_3[t]/(t^2+1) and \mathbb F_3=\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z.

Here A_n is the group of even permutations of \{1,\ldots,n\} under composition. For a field F, \operatorname{SL}_2(F) is the group of 2\times2 matrices of determinant 1, and

\operatorname{PSL}_2(F)=\operatorname{SL}_2(F)/\{aI:a\in F,\ a^2=1\}.

Here I is the 2\times2 identity matrix. An isomorphism is a bijection preserving group multiplication.

Proof 1: The ten partitions and the projective line (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Heading The isomorphism PSL2(9) ≅ A6, printed p. 6 (PDF page 6).).

The translations by 1 and i generate all translations of \mathbb F_9. Together with z\mapsto-1/z they generate the determinant-one projective action by elementary row operations. In Wilson’s labeling these transformations agree with even permutations on the ten partitions; the projective action is faithful and has order 360, hence its image equals that of A_6.
