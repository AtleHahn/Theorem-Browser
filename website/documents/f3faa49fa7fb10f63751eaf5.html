Statement

Let C\subseteq\mathbb R^n be closed under multiplication by positive scalars, and let f:C\to\mathbb R satisfy f(sx)=s^kf(x) for every x\in C, s>0, where k\in\mathbb R. If f is continuously differentiable on an open subset U\subseteq C, then \sum_{i=1}^n x_i\partial_i f(x)=kf(x) for x\in U.

Proof 1: Euler identity for a differentiable homogeneous function

For having simpler formulas, we set {\displaystyle \mathbf {x} =(x_{1},\ldots ,x_{n}).} The result follows by using the chain rule for differentiating both sides of the equation {\displaystyle f(s\mathbf {x} )=s^{k}f(\mathbf {x} )} with respect to {\displaystyle s,} and taking the limit of the result when s tends to 1.
