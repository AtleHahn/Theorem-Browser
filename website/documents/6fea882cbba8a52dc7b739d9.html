Statement

Let H be a Hilbert space, let T:H\to H be a bounded linear operator with \|T\|\leq1, and let U on a Hilbert space K\supseteq H be a minimal unitary dilation of T. Thus H is a closed subspace of K, U is unitary,

T^n=P_HU^n|_H\qquad(n=0,1,2,\ldots),

and K is the smallest closed subspace containing H that is invariant under both U and its adjoint U^*. Here P_H:K\to H is orthogonal projection.

For every bounded linear operator R:H\to H satisfying RT=TR, there is a bounded linear operator S:K\to K such that

SU=US,\qquad RT^n=P_HSU^n|_H\quad(n=0,1,2,\ldots),\qquad \|S\|=\|R\|.

The norms are operator norms.
