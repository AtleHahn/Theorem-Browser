Statement

Let X be a smooth complex projective variety, let D be an effective \mathbb Q-divisor on X, and let L be a line bundle such that the rational divisor class L-D is big and nef. Then

H^i\bigl(X,\omega_X\otimes L\otimes\mathcal J(D)\bigr)=0\qquad\text{for every integer }i>0.

Here \omega_X=\mathcal O_X(K_X) is the canonical line bundle and H^i denotes sheaf cohomology. An effective \mathbb Q-divisor is a finite sum of prime divisors with nonnegative rational coefficients. A rational divisor class is nef if its intersection with every irreducible curve is nonnegative, and big if a positive integral multiple has maximal Iitaka dimension \dim X.

The multiplier ideal is

\mathcal J(D)=\mu_*\mathcal O_Y\bigl(K_{Y/X}-\lfloor\mu^*D\rfloor\bigr),

where \mu:Y\to X is a log resolution of (X,D), K_{Y/X}=K_Y-\mu^*K_X, and the floor rounds down divisor coefficients. A log resolution is a proper birational morphism from a smooth variety for which the union of the exceptional divisors and the support of the total transform of D has simple normal crossings.
