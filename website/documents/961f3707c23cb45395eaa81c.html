Statement

The unit rank of a number field is zero precisely for \mathbb Q and imaginary quadratic fields; it is positive for every other number field.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the rank of the unit group and the distinction between rational, imaginary quadratic and other number fields requires algebraic number theory.

Proof 1: Read the zero-rank signatures from the formula — outline

Let r_1 count the real embeddings and r_2 the conjugate pairs of nonreal embeddings of K. Dirichlet’s theorem gives the rank r_1+r_2-1, where [K:\mathbb Q]=r_1+2r_2. The zero-rank cases are \mathbb Q and imaginary quadratic fields; all others have positive rank.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the Dirichlet rank formula and the signature-degree relation to identify zero-rank fields. Rings of integers, unit-group rank and number-field embeddings together require substantial algebraic number theory.
