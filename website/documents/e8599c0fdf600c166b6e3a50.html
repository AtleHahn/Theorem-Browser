Statement

Let P_n be the probability that a uniformly chosen integer from 1,\ldots,10^n-1 is prime. Then P_{2n}/P_n\to1/2 as n\to\infty.

Proof 1: Prime number theorem at decimal cutoffs

By the prime number theorem, P_n=\pi(10^n-1)/(10^n-1)\sim1/\log(10^n-1)\sim1/(n\log10). Substitution of 2n gives the ratio 1/2.
