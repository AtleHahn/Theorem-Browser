Statement

There exist a first-order language L, an integer r\geq0, and an L-formula \varphi(x_1,\ldots,x_r) with no other free variables, having both of the following properties.

  1. For any finite nonempty L-structures A\subseteq B and any tuple \bar a\in A^r,
B\models\varphi(\bar a)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A\models\varphi(\bar a).
  1. There is no universal L-formula \psi(\bar x) equivalent to \varphi(\bar x) on all finite nonempty L-structures. Explicitly, for every such \psi, there exist a finite nonempty L-structure M and \bar a\in M^r for which M\models\varphi(\bar a) and M\models\psi(\bar a) have different truth values.

Here A\subseteq B means that the universe of A is a subset of that of B, contains the interpretations of all constants, is closed under the interpreted functions, and has the functions and relations obtained by restricting those of B. A universal formula is one of the form \forall y_1\cdots\forall y_s\,\theta(\bar x,\bar y), with s\geq0 and quantifier-free \theta. Satisfaction M\models\varphi(\bar a) means that \varphi is true in M when its free variables are assigned \bar a. If r=0, the tuple is empty and the formulas are sentences.
