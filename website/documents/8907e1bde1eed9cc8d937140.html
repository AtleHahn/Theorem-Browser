Statement

All rings are commutative with identity, and subrings have the same identity. A ring A is called Nagata if it is Noetherian and, for every prime ideal \mathfrak p\subset A and every finite field extension L of the fraction field of A/\mathfrak p, the integral closure of A/\mathfrak p in L is a finitely generated A/\mathfrak p-module. Here Noetherian means every ideal is finitely generated; prime ideals are proper ideals with integral-domain quotient. The integral closure consists of elements of L satisfying a monic polynomial over A/\mathfrak p, and a finite field extension has finite vector-space dimension.

Let R be a Nagata ring and let B be a ring containing R that is of finite type over R: there exist b_1,\ldots,b_m\in B such that B=R[b_1,\ldots,b_m]. Then B is a Nagata ring.
