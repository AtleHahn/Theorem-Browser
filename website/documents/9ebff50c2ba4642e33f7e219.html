Statement

Let X be a real martingale or a nonnegative real submartingale on a finite discrete index set ending at T, or on [0,T]. In continuous time assume almost surely right-continuous paths. For p\ge1 with all pth moments finite and C>0, \Pr(\sup_{t\le T}|X_t|\ge C)\le\mathbb E|X_T|^p/C^p, with the supremum taken over the given index set.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement bounds a maximal path probability by an absolute moment on a filtered probability space.

Proof 1: Apply the submartingale inequality to absolute powers

Conditional Jensen makes |X_t|^p a nonnegative submartingale under the stated assumptions. Apply Doob’s probability inequality to that process at threshold C^p. Since p\ge1, its maximal threshold event is precisely \{\sup_{t\le T}|X_t|\ge C\}, giving the bound.

Level justification

Apply conditional Jensen to a martingale or nonnegative submartingale, then use a maximal inequality. Understanding conditional expectation and filtered processes is a substantial prerequisite beyond elementary moment inequalities.
