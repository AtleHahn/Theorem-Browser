Statement

For a prime p and integers x,k with k\ge1, write (x/p)_k=1 if x is a kth power modulo p, and (x/p)_k=-1 otherwise. Let p,q be distinct primes with p\equiv q\equiv1\pmod8 and

(p/q)_4=(q/p)_4=1.

Choose integers a,b,c,d,A,B,C,D satisfying

p=a^2+b^2=c^2+2d^2,\qquad q=A^2+B^2=C^2+2D^2,

with aA odd. Then

(p/q)_8(q/p)_8=((aB-bA)/q)_4\,((cD-dC)/q)_2.
