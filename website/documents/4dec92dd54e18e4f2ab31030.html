Statement

If A is a nonempty finite set of primes, then \sum_{p\in A}1/p is not an integer.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Only finite sets of integers and elementary prime divisibility are needed.

Proof 1: Test the numerator modulo each denominator prime

Rewrite the expression for the sum of the reciprocals of the finite set of primes in terms of the least common denominator, which is the product of all these primes. Then each of these primes divides all but one of the numerator terms and hence does not divide the numerator itself; but each prime does divide the denominator. Thus the expression is irreducible and is non-integer.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The common-denominator numerator is divisible by none of its denominator primes, so the reduced denominator is greater than one. This is elementary integer divisibility.
