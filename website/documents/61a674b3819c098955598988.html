Statement

For \mathbb F=\mathbb R or \mathbb C, let K_G^{\mathbb F}(d) be the least K such that every finite scalar matrix M satisfying |\sum M_{ij}s_it_j|\le1 for all scalars |s_i|,|t_j|\le1 also satisfies |\sum M_{ij}\langle S_i,T_j\rangle|\le K for all vectors in the unit ball of \mathbb F^d. For either field the sequence K_G^{\mathbb F}(d) converges to a finite limit equal to its supremum.

Proof 1: Finite-order Grothendieck constants have finite limits — outline

The sequences K_G^{\mathbb R}(d) and K_G^{\mathbb C}(d) are easily seen to be increasing, and Grothendieck's result states that they are bounded, so they have limits. For each sequence the limit equals its supremum, by monotone convergence of bounded real sequences.
