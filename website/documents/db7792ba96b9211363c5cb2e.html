Statement

If a real sequence x_n has limit \ell\in[-\infty,+\infty], then (x_1+\cdots+x_n)/n\to\ell.

Proof 1: Use partial sums and denominator n

Let (x_n) be a sequence of real numbers which converges to l (finite or infinite), and define

a_n:=\sum_{m=1}^n x_m=x_1+\dots+x_n,\qquad b_n:=n.

Then (b_n) is strictly increasing and diverges to +\infty. We compute

\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_{n+1}-a_n}{b_{n+1}-b_n} =\lim_{n\to\infty}x_{n+1} =\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n=l.

Therefore, by the Stolz–Cesàro theorem (including its infinite-limit cases),

\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{x_1+\dots+x_n}{n} =\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n.
