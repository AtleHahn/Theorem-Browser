Statement

Let M be a compact connected orientable irreducible three-manifold with boundary a torus T. Irreducible means every embedded two-sphere bounds a three-ball. Suppose M is not Seifert-fibered. For a slope r, meaning an unoriented isotopy class of essential simple closed curves on T, let M(r) be obtained by attaching a solid torus so that its meridian represents r. For any two slopes r,s, if \pi_1(M(r)) and \pi_1(M(s)) are cyclic, then \Delta(r,s)\le1, where \Delta is the minimal number of intersections of representatives. In particular, at most three slopes give cyclic fundamental group.

A Seifert fibration is a decomposition into circle fibers locally modeled by fibered solid tori (with boundary half-neighborhoods at boundary fibers). A standard fibered solid torus is the mapping torus of a rational rotation of a disk, with fibers given by its suspension circles.
