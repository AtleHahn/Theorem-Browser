Statement

Let an oriented planar region be tiled by finitely many oriented pieces whose interior edges each occur twice with opposite orientations and whose remaining oriented edges form the outer boundary. For a one-form \omega integrable along these edges, the sum of the integrals along the piece boundaries equals the integral along the outer boundary.

Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Finite sums; Oriented line integrals. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Pair oppositely traversed interior edges

In an oriented tiling, the interior paths are traversed in opposite directions; their contributions to the path integral thus cancel each other pairwise. As a consequence, only the contribution from the boundary remains.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Pair the two oppositely oriented copies of each interior edge and cancel their line integrals. Undergraduate multivariable calculus suffices; a planar one-form is just the ordinary integrand P dx+Q dy here.
