Statement

As the positive integer n\to\infty, \binom{2n}{n}\sim4^n/\sqrt{\pi n}, where a_n\sim b_n means a_n/b_n\to1.

Proof 1: Stirling factorial-ratio simplification

Using Stirling's approximation,

{\displaystyle {\binom {2n}{n}}={\frac {(2n)!}{(n!)^{2}}}\sim {\frac {2^{2n}}{\sqrt {\pi n}}}.}
