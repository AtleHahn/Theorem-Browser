Statement

Let C be a nonempty finite concept class on a finite domain X: each concept assigns a binary label to every point of X. A witness set for a concept is a set of points whose labels distinguish it from every other concept in C. The teaching dimension is the maximum, over concepts, of the minimum size of a witness set. Then the teaching dimension is at most |C|-1.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Concepts are finite binary label lists, witness sets distinguish the lists, and teaching dimension is an explicitly defined maximum of finite minimum sizes. These local definitions require only finite counting and comparisons.

Use the source-stated form of Bondy’s theorem: there is a subset S\subseteq X of size at most |C|-1 which is a witness set for every concept in C. This implies that the teaching dimension is at most |C|-1.
