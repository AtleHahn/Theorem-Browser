Let \Phi_t:S^2\to S^2 be the maps of a smooth flow on S^2, with \Phi_0=\mathrm{id}. Every time map in its time interval has a fixed point.

Proof 1: Sphere flow maps have fixed points — outline

Use the Lefschetz fixed-point theorem. Since the Betti numbers of a 2-sphere are 1, 0, 1, 0, 0, ... the Lefschetz number (total trace on homology) of the identity mapping is 2. The given smooth flow is a one-parameter family of diffeomorphisms on the sphere; and all of the mappings in it are homotopic to the identity. Therefore, they all have Lefschetz number 2, also. Hence they have fixed points (since the Lefschetz number is nonzero).
