Statement

Let R be a ring with identity and let I be a proper two-sided ideal. Assuming Zorn’s lemma, there exists a maximal proper two-sided ideal M of R with I\subseteq M.

Level

Level justification

Introductory ring theory supplies proper two-sided ideals and maximality. Zorn is an explicit assumption, and no localization, module or geometric construction is needed.

Proof 1: Chain unions and Zorn’s lemma

Consider the set S of all proper ideals of R containing I, ordered by inclusion. The set S is nonempty since I\in S. Furthermore, for any nonempty chain T of S, the union of the ideals in T is an ideal J, and a union of ideals not containing 1 does not contain 1, so J\in S. The empty chain has the upper bound I. By Zorn's lemma, S has a maximal element M. This M is a maximal ideal containing I: any proper ideal properly containing M would also contain I, contradicting maximality in S.
