Capping the boundary circles of a compact surface

Statement

Let M be a compact connected Hausdorff second-countable topological surface with boundary. Its boundary is a finite disjoint union of circles, and attaching a disk along each boundary circle gives a closed connected surface.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement describes a topological gluing construction on a compact surface with boundary.

Proof 1: Fill the finitely many boundary circles with disks — outline

The boundary of a compact surface is a closed 1-manifold, and is therefore the disjoint union of a finite number of circles; filling these circles with disks (formally, taking the cone on each circle) yields a closed surface. Since the original surface is connected, the capped surface is connected.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof invokes classification of compact one-manifolds and gluing of manifold boundary charts to disks. This topology of manifolds is outside the baseline and only outlined; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
