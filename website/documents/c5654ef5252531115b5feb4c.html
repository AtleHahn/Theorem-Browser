Statement

Let G be a finite group and O(G) its largest normal subgroup of odd order. Let T be a Sylow 2-subgroup, meaning its order is the largest power of 2 dividing |G|. If t\in T has order 2 and no other element of T is conjugate to t in G, then tO(G) belongs to the center of G/O(G). Elements are conjugate if one is gtg^{-1} for some g\in G; the center consists of elements commuting with every group element.
