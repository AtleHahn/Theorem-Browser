Euclidean spaces of different dimensions are not homeomorphic. The result makes precise a structural distinction between kinds of space that cardinality alone cannot detect.

Statement

For nonnegative integers m\ne n, the spaces \mathbb R^m and \mathbb R^n are not homeomorphic.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic topology supplies continuous injections, connected components, path connectedness and homeomorphisms. The sphere or Jordan curve is explicitly a familiar Euclidean subset or continuous circle image; the full assertion needs no homology or manifold machinery used by its proof.

Proof 1: Use each whole space as its own open set — outline

Apply invariance of dimension for nonempty Euclidean open sets to the whole spaces \mathbb R^m and \mathbb R^n.

Level

Level justification

Apply the supplied dimension-invariance conclusion with each whole Euclidean space as its own open set. Basic topology suffices; the deeper proof of dimension invariance is not used.
