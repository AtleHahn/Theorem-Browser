Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with circumcenter O, circumradius R and inradius r. For each side line, take the perpendicular distance from O with positive sign if O lies in the open half-plane containing the opposite vertex, negative sign if it lies in the other open half-plane, and zero if it lies on the line. Denote these signed distances to BC,CA,AB by d_a,d_b,d_c. Then d_a+d_b+d_c=R+r. The circumcircle passes through the three vertices; the incircle lies inside and is tangent to all three sides.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All geometry is elementary and the signed-distance convention is fully specified, including right triangles.
