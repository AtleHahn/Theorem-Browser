Statement

For a map \varphi:X\to F into a real Hilbert space, K(x,y)=\langle\varphi(x),\varphi(y)\rangle is symmetric and positive semidefinite. Positive semidefinite means that every finite sum \sum_{i,j}c_ic_jK(x_i,x_j) with real coefficients is nonnegative.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic topology and real analysis supply compactness, completeness, continuity and sequence convergence; linear algebra supplies inner products and orthogonality. Hilbert completeness and the explicitly specified bounds or maps are direct extensions, with no spectral or weak-topology theory needed.

Proof 1: Use the Gram form of feature vectors — outline

Symmetry is inherited from the inner product. The finite Gram sum is the squared norm of the corresponding finite linear combination of feature vectors, and is therefore nonnegative.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Expand the finite Gram sum as the squared norm of a finite linear combination. Undergraduate inner-product linear algebra suffices: every calculation lies in the finite span of the selected feature vectors and neither completeness nor infinite-dimensional duality is used.
