Statement

Let X be infinite and suppose there is no injection \mathbb N\to X. Then the least ordinal not injectible into X is \omega.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement is about arbitrary infinite sets without countable subsets, in choice-free set theory.

Review notes

Proof 1: Every finite ordinal embeds but omega does not outline

An infinite set X with no injection \mathbb N\to X embeds only finite ordinals. Its Hartogs number, the least ordinal not embeddable in it, is therefore \omega.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compare injections from finite ordinals and omega into a Dedekind-finite infinite set. Although short, the proof uses choice-free infinitude and ordinal initial segments rather than the ordinary countable-subsequence notion of infinite sets. Retain beyond undergraduate conservatively.
