Statement

Let D be the arrow category of abelian groups: objects are homomorphisms and arrows are commutative squares. The functor G:D\to\mathbf{Ab} taking a homomorphism to its kernel is right adjoint to F(A)=(A\to0).

Here F\dashv G means a bijection \operatorname{Hom}(FY,X)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(Y,GX) natural in X and Y.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full assertion requires abelian groups, arrow categories, kernels, adjunctions.

Proof 1: Source construction outline

Consider the category D of homomorphisms of abelian groups. If f1 : A1 → B1 and f2 : A2 → B2 are two objects of D, then a morphism from f1 to f2 is a pair (gA, gB) of morphisms such that gBf1 = f2gA. Let G : D → Ab be the functor which assigns to each homomorphism its kernel and let F : Ab → D be the functor which maps the group A to the homomorphism A → 0. Then G is right adjoint to F, which expresses the universal property of kernels. The counit of this adjunction is the defining embedding of a homomorphism's kernel into the homomorphism's domain, and the unit is the morphism identifying a group A with the kernel of the homomorphism A → 0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The ordinary kernel factorization is undergraduate algebra, and the arrow category is described explicitly. However, the complete claim is a natural adjunction, with a functor on commutative squares and specified unit and counit. That combination of arrow categories, naturality and adjunction data is not confidently a 30-minute extension for the stated baseline. Retain Beyond undergraduate provisionally, while distinguishing the elementary kernel dependency from the categorical argument.
