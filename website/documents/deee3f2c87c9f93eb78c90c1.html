Statement

For a based path-connected space X, write \pi_j(X) for based homotopy classes of maps S^j\to X, and H_j(X;R) for singular homology with coefficients R. The Hurewicz map sends [f] to f_*[S^j], where [S^j] is the fundamental homology class of the oriented sphere. Suppose X is simply connected (path-connected with \pi_1(X)=0), r\ge1 is an integer, and \pi_i(X)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q=0 for 1\le i\le r. Then the rationalized Hurewicz map \pi_i(X)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q\longrightarrow H_i(X;\mathbb Q) is an isomorphism for 1\le i\le2r and surjective for i=2r+1. Tensoring an abelian group with \mathbb Q means extending scalars from integers to rationals; \pi_1(X) here is trivial.
