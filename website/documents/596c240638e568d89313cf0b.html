Statement

Give each edge color of a finite simple graph a new vertex disjoint from the original vertex set, and replace an edge \{u,v\} of color c by \{u,v,c\}. Hypergraph matchings then correspond exactly to graph matchings with distinct edge colors.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The hyperedges are explicitly triples of endpoints and colors. Matchings mean disjoint finite sets.

Proof 1: Adjoin the edge color as a new vertex

The source treats colors as distinguished vertices. Two triples are disjoint exactly when the original edges have disjoint endpoints and distinct colors. Thus a selection is a matching in the encoded hypergraph precisely when it is a rainbow matching.
