Statement

Let P∈R[x₁,…,x_n] with n≥1. The number of distinct polynomials obtained by permuting its n variables divides n!.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Real polynomial expressions, their finitely many permutations and integer divisibility are elementary.

Proof 1: Identify the polynomial orbit with a subgroup index (outline)

Let H≤S_n be the subgroup of permutations preserving P. As identified in the source, the number of distinct variable-permuted polynomials is the coset index [S_n:H]. Lagrange’s theorem gives n!=[S_n:H]|H|, so this number divides n!.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Identify the orbit with stabilizer cosets and apply Lagrange in the symmetric group. Undergraduate abstract algebra suffices.
