Statement

Let S be a Hausdorff topological space and (C_k)_{k\ge0} a decreasing sequence of nonempty compact subsets of S. Then \bigcap_{k\ge0}C_k\ne\varnothing.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic point-set topology and real analysis supply compactness, closed sets, continuity, metrics and completeness. The Cantor-set construction or cofinite topology, where present, is a direct set-based example learnable briefly. The complete assertion needs no algebraic or differential topology.

Proof 1: Closedness of compact subsets in a Hausdorff space

In a Hausdorff space compact subsets are closed. Thus the C_k are nonempty compact closed subsets. Apply the topological Cantor intersection theorem, which says that any decreasing sequence of such subsets has nonempty intersection.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof combines two standard introductory topology statements: compact subsets of Hausdorff spaces are closed, and nested nonempty compact closed sets intersect. No additional prerequisites arise.
