Statement

A Hausdorff topological manifold is metrizable if and only if it is paracompact. A manifold is locally Euclidean, and paracompact means that every open cover admits a locally finite open refinement.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate point-set topology supplies Hausdorffness, metrics and covers. Locally Euclidean means neighborhoods homeomorphic to Euclidean open sets; locally finite refinement means a refining cover meeting each sufficiently small neighborhood in finitely many members. These closely related local/cover definitions are a small extension, and paracompactness is stated explicitly.

Proof 1: Apply Smirnov to Euclidean charts

Euclidean neighbourhood charts make the manifold locally metrizable. Apply Smirnov’s theorem: a locally metrizable space is metrizable exactly when it is Hausdorff and paracompact. The Hausdorff assumption is already present, leaving precisely paracompactness.
