Statement

Assume every partial order extends to a total order. Then every family of nonempty finite sets has a choice function.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Sets, orders, bijections and choice functions suffice. The statement defines the relevant order/transitivity properties and distinguishes the universal order-extension assumption from its finite-set choice consequence.

Proof 1: Least elements in a common total order

Give the union of the family the order with no comparisons between distinct elements, and extend it to a total order. Choose from each finite nonempty member its least element in this single total order.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Order the union using the assumed extension principle and take the unique least member of each finite set. Undergraduate set theory and partial orders suffice; no independence or relative-consistency argument is used.
