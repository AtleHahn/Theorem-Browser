Proof 1: Specialize the normal-boundary theorem to the unit disc

The normal-boundary-dilation theorem gives any operator with a simply connected spectral set X a normal dilation with spectrum on the boundary \partial X (a dilation N with T^n=P_HN^n|_H for n\ge0, where P_H is orthogonal projection onto the original Hilbert space H).

To see that this generalises Sz.-Nagy’s theorem, note that contraction operators have the closed unit disc \mathbb D as a spectral set, and that normal operators with spectrum in the unit circle \partial\mathbb D are unitary.
