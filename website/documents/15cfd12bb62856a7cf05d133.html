Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate triangle, with a=|BC|, altitude h from A to the line BC, and area K. Then 2K=ah.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All geometric objects and the base-height relation use elementary Euclidean plane geometry.

Proof 1: Use the sideline as the incircle tangent

Harcourt’s theorem states that for any incircle tangent line, aa'+bb'+cc'=2K, where a,b,c are the side lengths opposite A,B,C, and the signed vertex distances a',b',c' are positive on the incenter side. Choose the line BC, which is tangent to the incircle. The distances of B,C are zero and the distance of A is h. The formula therefore reduces to ah=2K.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Choose the side itself as the incircle tangent in the explicit signed-distance formula; two distances vanish and the third is the altitude. School geometry and substitution suffice.
