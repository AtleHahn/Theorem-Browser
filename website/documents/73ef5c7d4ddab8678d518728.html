Statement

Let X be a real or complex normed vector space and suppose x_j\in X converges weakly to x\in X: f(x_j)\to f(x) for every continuous linear functional f on X. For each integer k\ge1 one can choose finitely many real coefficients \lambda_j^{(k)}\ge0, indexed by j\ge k, with \sum_{j\ge k}\lambda_j^{(k)}=1, so that

y_k=\sum_{j\ge k}\lambda_j^{(k)}x_j\quad\text{satisfies}\quad\|y_k-x\|\to0\quad(k\to\infty).

The sums are finite because all but finitely many coefficients are zero. No completeness of X is required.

Level justification

Weak sequence convergence is explicitly defined by testing bounded linear functionals, a short extension of undergraduate normed linear algebra and analysis.
