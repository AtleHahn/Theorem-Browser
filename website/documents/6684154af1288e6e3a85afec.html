Statement

Let X/\mathbb F_q be a smooth projective good reduction of a smooth projective variety Y over a number field embedded in \mathbb C. For \ell\ne\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q, set P_i(T)=\det(1-TF\mid H^i_{\mathrm{et}}(X_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q},\mathbb Q_\ell)), where F is Frobenius. Then \deg P_i=\dim_{\mathbb Q}H^i(Y(\mathbb C),\mathbb Q).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves smooth projective good reduction; etale and singular cohomology; Frobenius determinants; Betti numbers. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compare determinant degrees with complex cohomology outline

Each polynomial P_i is the determinant of I-TF on the \ell-adic cohomology group H^i. The relation with complex Betti numbers of a lift follows from a comparison theorem between \ell-adic and ordinary cohomology for complex varieties.

Here the external prerequisites include smooth proper base change for the good reduction and invertibility of Frobenius. The source leaves the degree/dimension identification implicit.

Level justification

Compare good-reduction etale cohomology with complex singular cohomology and use Frobenius invertibility to identify determinant degree. Etale cohomology and arithmetic geometry are essential.
