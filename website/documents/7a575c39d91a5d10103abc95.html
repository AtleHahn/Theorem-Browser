Statement

Let (X,\Sigma) be a measurable space, let M be the real vector lattice of finite signed countably additive measures on it, and let \mu\in M be nonnegative. Order M by \nu\geq0 when \nu(A)\geq0 for every A\in\Sigma, and give it the total variation norm \|\nu\|=|\nu|(X). Let B_\mu be the smallest band in M containing \mu. Then

L^1(X,\Sigma,\mu)\longrightarrow B_\mu,\qquad f\longmapsto\left(A\longmapsto\int_A f\,d\mu\right)

is an isometric lattice isomorphism.

Here L^1 consists of real integrable measurable functions identified when equal \mu-almost everywhere, with norm \int|f|\,d\mu and the almost-everywhere order. Total variation is |\nu|(A)=\sup\sum_j|\nu(A_j)|, where the supremum ranges over finite measurable partitions of A. A band is a vector subspace B which is solid (\eta\in B, |\nu|\leq|\eta| imply \nu\in B) and closed under suprema of nonempty subsets of its nonnegative elements whenever these suprema exist in M. A lattice isomorphism is a linear bijection preserving suprema and infima of pairs in both directions; isometric means preserving norms.
