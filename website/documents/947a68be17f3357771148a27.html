Statement

For a scheme X of finite type over \mathbb C, let X^{\mathrm{an}} be its analytification: the complex analytic space whose points are the closed points of X, with their complex topology and holomorphic structure sheaf \mathcal O_{X^{\mathrm{an}}}. Write \lambda_X:X^{\mathrm{an}}\to X for the natural morphism of ringed spaces. Locally on an affine presentation, analytification uses the same defining equations in complex coordinate space with holomorphic rather than algebraic functions. Morphisms f:X\to Y induce analytic morphisms f^{\mathrm{an}} satisfying \lambda_Y\circ f^{\mathrm{an}}=f\circ\lambda_X; open immersions analytify to open immersions.

For a sheaf of \mathcal O_X-modules \mathcal F, set

\mathcal F^{\mathrm{an}}= \lambda_X^{-1}\mathcal F\otimes_{\lambda_X^{-1}\mathcal O_X}\mathcal O_{X^{\mathrm{an}}}.

This is an exact functor; coherent algebraic sheaves give coherent analytic sheaves. Coherent here means locally finitely presented over the respective structure sheaf.

The comparison and algebraization assertions are as follows.

  1. For a morphism f:X\to Y of finite-type complex schemes and a coherent \mathcal O_X-module \mathcal F, the natural comparison map(f_*\mathcal F)^{\mathrm{an}}\longrightarrow f^{\mathrm{an}}_*\mathcal F^{\mathrm{an}}is injective. If f is proper, then for every integer i\ge0 the natural maps are isomorphisms:(R^if_*\mathcal F)^{\mathrm{an}}\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }R^if^{\mathrm{an}}_*\mathcal F^{\mathrm{an}}.Here f_* is direct image and R^if_* is its ith right derived functor; proper means separated, of finite type and universally closed.
  2. If X^{\mathrm{an}} is compact and Hausdorff, analytification gives an equivalence from the category of coherent algebraic sheaves on X to the category of coherent analytic sheaves on X^{\mathrm{an}}. Explicitly, for coherent \mathcal F,\mathcal G the natural map\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_X}(\mathcal F,\mathcal G) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_{X^{\mathrm{an}}}}(\mathcal F^{\mathrm{an}},\mathcal G^{\mathrm{an}})is bijective, and every coherent analytic sheaf is isomorphic to \mathcal F^{\mathrm{an}} for some coherent algebraic sheaf \mathcal F.

In particular, for a projective complex algebraic variety X these assertions give both that equivalence and the natural cohomology isomorphisms

H^q(X,\mathcal F)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H^q(X^{\mathrm{an}},\mathcal F^{\mathrm{an}})\qquad(q\ge0)

for every coherent algebraic sheaf \mathcal F, where H^q denotes sheaf cohomology.
