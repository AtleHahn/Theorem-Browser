Statement

Let D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\} and let \mathcal S consist of the injective holomorphic functions f:D\to\mathbb C normalized by f(0)=0 and f'(0)=1. For f\in\mathcal S, define its logarithmic coefficients \gamma_k(f) by

\log\frac{f(z)}z=2\sum_{k=1}^\infty\gamma_k(f)z^k.

The quotient at z=0 is defined by its removable value 1, and the analytic logarithm is chosen to have value 0 there.

Suppose the Milin assertion holds: for every f\in\mathcal S and every integer n\geq1,

\sum_{k=1}^n(n-k+1)\left(k|\gamma_k(f)|^2-\frac1k\right)\leq0.

Then the Robertson assertion holds: for every odd function \phi\in\mathcal S, with Taylor expansion

\phi(z)=z+\sum_{k=1}^\infty b_{2k+1}z^{2k+1},

and every integer n\geq1,

\sum_{k=1}^n|b_{2k+1}|^2\leq n.

Odd means \phi(-z)=-\phi(z). The statement is the implication between the two universal coefficient assertions.
