Statement

A Stone space is a compact, totally disconnected Hausdorff space. For Stone spaces X,Y and a continuous map f:X\to Y, the map \operatorname{Clop}(Y)\to\operatorname{Clop}(X) given by C\mapsto f^{-1}(C) is a Boolean algebra homomorphism. Here \operatorname{Clop}(Z) is the Boolean algebra of clopen subsets of Z, with union, intersection and complement.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic topology supplies compactness, disconnectedness and clopen sets. The Boolean operations are explicitly union, intersection and complement, and the map is inverse image. No abstract Stone duality is needed.

Proof 1: Inverse-image construction — outline

Send a clopen set to its inverse image under f, which is clopen. This is the inverse-image morphism on clopen algebras: inverse images preserve the Boolean set operations.

Level

Level justification

Use continuity to preserve both open and closed sets and the elementary inverse-image identities to preserve Boolean operations. Undergraduate point-set topology suffices; the Boolean algebra is explicitly the set algebra of clopen sets.
