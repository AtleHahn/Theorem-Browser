Statement

A Banach space X is a complete normed vector space over \mathbb R or \mathbb C. It is uniformly convex if for every 0<\epsilon\le2 there exists \delta>0 such that \|x\|,\|y\|\le1 and \|x-y\|\ge\epsilon imply \|(x+y)/2\|\le1-\delta. Its continuous dual X^* consists of bounded linear functionals with the operator norm, and X^{**}=(X^*)^*. Reflexivity means surjectivity of the canonical map J:X\to X^{**}, J(x)(f)=f(x). Every uniformly convex Banach space is reflexive.
