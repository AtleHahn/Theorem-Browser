Statement

Let K be a field. Write \mathcal P(K)=\bigcup_{n\ge1}K((T^{1/n})), where K((T^{1/n})) consists of formal series \sum_{k\ge k_0}c_kT^{k/n} with k_0\in\mathbb Z and c_k\in K. Let P(Y)=\sum_{a_i\ne0}a_iY^i\in\mathcal P(K)[Y] be nonzero. For a nonzero series f, let v(f) be its smallest exponent. The Newton polygon is the lower convex hull of (i,v(a_i)). If y\in\mathcal P(K) is nonzero and P(y)=0, then v(y) is the negative slope of an edge of this polygon.

Proof 1: Cancellation at a supporting line

Given a nonzero Puiseux series y of valuation v_0, the valuation of P(y) is at least the minimum of the numbers iv_0+v(a_i), and is equal to this minimum if this minimum is reached for only one i. So, for y to be a root of P, the minimum must be reached at least twice. That is, there must be two distinct values i_1 and i_2 of i such that

i_1v_0+v(a_{i_1})=i_2v_0+v(a_{i_2}),

and

iv_0+v(a_i)\geq i_1v_0+v(a_{i_1})

for every i.

That is, (i_1,v(a_{i_1})) and (i_2,v(a_{i_2})) must belong to an edge of the Newton polygon, and

v_0=-\frac{v(a_{i_1})-v(a_{i_2})}{i_1-i_2}

must be the opposite of the slope of this edge.
