Statement

Let a Poisson point process of intensity one be given on [0,\infty)^2. Let M_k be the maximum number of points in [0,k)^2 with both coordinates strictly increasing. Let T translate by (-1,-1) and discard points outside the quadrant. For integers k,m\ge1, M_{k+m}\ge M_k+M_m\circ T^k almost surely.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses a Poisson point process, beyond the baseline even though the concatenation is finite combinatorics.

Proof 1: Concatenate increasing subsequences in diagonal squares

Take a longest increasing subsequence in [0,k)^2 and one in [k,k+m)^2. Concatenating them gives an increasing subsequence in [0,k+m)^2, because every coordinate of a point in the first square is smaller than every coordinate of a point in the second. Its length is M_k+M_m\circ T^k, so the maximum M_{k+m} is at least this large.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Concatenation itself is elementary, but the full assertion is an almost-sure inequality of functionals of a planar Poisson point process and its configuration shift. That random-measure framework is not developed locally; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
