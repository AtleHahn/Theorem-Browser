Statement

Let V be a finite-dimensional complex vector space, V^* its linear dual, and p,q\ge0 integers. The group \operatorname{GL}(V) acts on (V^*)^p\oplus V^q by g\cdot(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_p,v_1,\ldots,v_q)=(\alpha_1\circ g^{-1},\ldots,\alpha_p\circ g^{-1},gv_1,\ldots,gv_q). Every complex polynomial function invariant under this action is a polynomial in the pq functions (\alpha_1,\ldots,v_q)\mapsto\alpha_i(v_j). Here a polynomial function is polynomial in linear coordinates, and invariant means unchanged by every g\in\operatorname{GL}(V).
