Statement

Let \mathfrak g be a complex semisimple Lie algebra with Cartan subalgebra \mathfrak h and fixed positive roots. Fix a highest weight \lambda. Among Verma modules (or generalized Verma modules) V_\mu, only finitely many weights \mu admit a nonzero homomorphism V_\lambda\to V_\mu.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Highest weight modules; Verma and generalized Verma modules; Module homomorphisms. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Finitely many target highest weights for nonzero homomorphisms

A nonzero homomorphism of these modules preserves the central character. By the Harish-Chandra character theorem, equality of central characters is equivalent to \lambda+\delta and \mu+\delta lying in the same Weyl group orbit, where \delta is half the sum of the positive roots. This gives only finitely many possible \mu.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Preserve the central character under a nonzero homomorphism and apply its Weyl-orbit classification. Verma modules, roots and central characters require graduate Lie representation theory.
