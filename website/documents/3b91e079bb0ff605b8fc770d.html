First-order validity coincides with provability from no nonlogical assumptions. This connects truth in every interpretation with formal derivability, without implying that arbitrary validity questions admit a terminating decision procedure.

Main-run extraction and separate source/standalone review; not formal verification

Statement

For a sentence s of a well-orderable first-order language, if s is true in every structure, then s is provable in the chosen usual classical first-order deductive system without nonlogical axioms.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The result concerns formal syntax and semantic structures in mathematical logic, beyond undergraduate calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Specialize completeness to the empty theory

Take T=\varnothing in the general completeness theorem. Every structure is a model of the empty theory, so validity is exactly \varnothing\models s; completeness gives \varnothing\vdash s.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Specialize completeness to the empty theory. The substitution is immediate but full first-order structures, validity and formal derivability remain prerequisites; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
