Statement

Put D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}, S^1=\partial D and D^*=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|>1\}\cup\{\infty\} in the Riemann sphere \widehat{\mathbb C}=\mathbb C\cup\{\infty\}. Let h:S^1\to S^1 be an orientation-preserving quasisymmetric homeomorphism. Then there are injective holomorphic maps F_1:D\to\widehat{\mathbb C} and F_2:D^*\to\widehat{\mathbb C} onto the two complementary domains of a Jordan curve \Gamma, extending continuously to boundary homeomorphisms f_1,f_2:S^1\to\Gamma, such that

h=f_1^{-1}\circ f_2.

A Jordan curve is the image of a circle under a homeomorphism onto its image. Orientation preservation means that h(e^{it})=e^{ig(t)} for a continuous strictly increasing lift with g(t+2\pi)=g(t)+2\pi. Quasisymmetry means that an increasing control function \eta:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty) exists such that, for distinct x,y,z\in S^1 with Euclidean distance,

\frac{|h(x)-h(y)|}{|h(x)-h(z)|}\leq\eta\!\left(\frac{|x-y|}{|x-z|}\right).

Holomorphic at infinity is interpreted using the local coordinate 1/z on the sphere.

Proof 1: Quasiconformal extension and cancellation of dilatation (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Theorem2.4, existence proof, PDF pp.7–8).

The source uses the opposite welding convention. Equivalently, perform its construction with a disk extension H:D\to D of h: extend the Beltrami coefficient of H by zero on D^*, and let w be a global quasiconformal solution. Then F_1=w\circ H^{-1} on D and F_2=w on D^* are conformal, their images are the complementary domains of \Gamma=w(S^1), and their boundary maps satisfy f_1^{-1}f_2=h. For the metric definition in the statement, equal small chords give comparable adjacent increments of the angular lift. Periodicity extends this to all real intervals. The [Ahlfors–Beurling integral extension](https://www.acadsci.fi/mathematica/Vol09/vol09pp133-139.pdf, formulas1–2) respects the lift's period and therefore descends, via the exponential covering, to the required disk extension; its central puncture is removable.

The complete statement concerns quasisymmetry and conformal maps of complementary Jordan domains. The construction uses quasiconformal extension, measurable Beltrami coefficients and the measurable Riemann mapping theorem, together with cancellation of dilatation under composition. These require advanced geometric function theory.
