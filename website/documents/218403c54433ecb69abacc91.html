Statement

Put D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}, S^1=\partial D and D^*=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|>1\}\cup\{\infty\} in the Riemann sphere \widehat{\mathbb C}=\mathbb C\cup\{\infty\}. Let h:S^1\to S^1 be an orientation-preserving quasisymmetric homeomorphism. Then there are injective holomorphic maps F_1:D\to\widehat{\mathbb C} and F_2:D^*\to\widehat{\mathbb C} onto the two complementary domains of a Jordan curve \Gamma, extending continuously to boundary homeomorphisms f_1,f_2:S^1\to\Gamma, such that

h=f_1^{-1}\circ f_2.

A Jordan curve is the image of a circle under a homeomorphism onto its image. Orientation preservation means that h(e^{it})=e^{ig(t)} for a continuous strictly increasing lift with g(t+2\pi)=g(t)+2\pi. Quasisymmetry means that an increasing control function \eta:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty) exists such that, for distinct x,y,z\in S^1 with Euclidean distance,

\frac{|h(x)-h(y)|}{|h(x)-h(z)|}\leq\eta\!\left(\frac{|x-y|}{|x-z|}\right).

Holomorphic at infinity is interpreted using the local coordinate 1/z on the sphere.
