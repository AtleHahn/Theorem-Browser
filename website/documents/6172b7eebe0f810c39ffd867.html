Statement

Every closed orientable 3-manifold is homeomorphic to the boundary of a compact simply connected 4-manifold. Closed means compact without boundary. Simply connected means path connected with every loop contractible to a point.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding manifold dimension, boundary and homotopy cumulatively exceeds the undergraduate topology baseline.
