Statement

Let (X,\mu) be a probability space and let A=L^\infty(X,\mu) act on H=L^2(X,\mu) by multiplication. Then its commutant is A^{\prime}=A; hence it is a maximal abelian subalgebra of B(H).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Applying trace commutation to multiplication operators — outline

The constant function 1 is a cyclic separating trace vector for A. Its modular conjugation is complex conjugation, so JAJ=A. The finite-trace commutation theorem therefore gives A^{\prime}=JAJ=A.
