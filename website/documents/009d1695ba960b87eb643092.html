Statement

Let A be a Borel subset of \mathbb R^n, n\ge1. For s\ge0, define

C_s(A)=\left(\inf_{\mu\in\mathcal P(A)} I_s(\mu)\right)^{-1},\qquad I_s(\mu)=\int_{A\times A}|x-y|^{-s}\,d\mu(x)\,d\mu(y),

where \mathcal P(A) is the collection of nonnegative Borel probability measures carried by A. For s>0, the kernel is +\infty on the diagonal; for s=0, use the constant kernel 1, including on the diagonal. Take \inf\varnothing=+\infty, 1/(+\infty)=0, and 1/0=+\infty. Then

\dim_H(A)=\sup\{s\ge0:C_s(A)>0\}.

The supremum is taken in [0,+\infty], so the supremum of the empty set is zero; in particular the formula assigns dimension zero to the empty set. For nonempty A, C_0(A)=1. Here Hausdorff dimension is \inf\{s\ge0:\mathcal H^s(A)=0\}, with the dimension of the empty set set to zero. Hausdorff measure is obtained by taking, as \delta\downarrow0, the infimum of \sum_j(\operatorname{diam}U_j)^s over countable covers of A by sets of diameter at most \delta; at s=0 use counting measure. Multiplicative normalizations of Hausdorff measure do not affect the dimension.
