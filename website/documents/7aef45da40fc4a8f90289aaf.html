Statement

Let f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty) have a continuous derivative on [0,\infty) (using the right derivative at zero) and be infinitely differentiable on (0,\infty). Suppose its derivative is completely monotone with continuity at zero:

(-1)^n f^{(n+1)}(t)\ge0\qquad(n\ge0,\ t>0).

There exist numbers a,b\ge0 and a positive Borel measure \mu on (0,\infty) such that

\int_{(0,\infty)}\min\{1,x\}\,\mu(dx)<\infty

and

f(t)=a+bt+\int_{(0,\infty)}(1-e^{-tx})\,\mu(dx)\qquad(t\ge0).

A positive Borel measure is a countably additive nonnegative measure on the sigma-algebra generated by open sets; the displayed integrals are Lebesgue integrals. Complete monotonicity here includes continuity at zero as well as the derivative signs on the open half-line.
