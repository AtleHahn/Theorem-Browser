Statement

For a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with angles A,B,C and circumradius R, let P,Q,S be the intersections of adjacent internal angle trisectors at the three sides. Their first Morley triangle has area 16√3 R² sin²(A/3)sin²(B/3)sin²(C/3).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses ordinary Euclidean triangle constructions, area and trigonometric values.

Proof 1: Substitute the quoted side length

Use the side-length formula for the first Morley triangle:

a'=b'=c'=8R\,\sin\tfrac13A\,\sin\tfrac13B\,\sin\tfrac13C,

where R is the circumradius of the original triangle and A,B,C are its angles. Since the area of an equilateral triangle is \tfrac{\sqrt3}{4}a'^2, the area of Morley's triangle can be expressed as

\operatorname{Area} =16\sqrt3R^2\,\sin^2\tfrac13A\,\sin^2\tfrac13B\,\sin^2\tfrac13C.
