Statement

A finitely generated group is isomorphic to a subgroup of a finitely presented group if and only if it is recursively presented.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Recursive presentations and abstract groups exceed calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Combine the two embedding directions — outline

By Higman’s embedding theorem, every finitely generated recursively presented group embeds in a finitely presented group. Conversely, every finitely generated subgroup of a finitely presented group is recursively presented. Together these give exactly the asserted class, up to isomorphism.
