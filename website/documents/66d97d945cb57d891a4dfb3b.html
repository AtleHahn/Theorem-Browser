Statement

Let L/K be a separable field extension of degree 6 with \operatorname{char}K\ne2. Then there is \lambda\in L such that L=K(\lambda) and its minimal polynomial over K has the form t^6+c_4t^4+c_2t^2+c_1t+c_0,\qquad c_4,c_2,c_1,c_0\in K. Degree means vector-space dimension over K; the minimal polynomial is the monic polynomial of least positive degree over K vanishing at \lambda. Separable means minimal polynomials of elements have no repeated roots in an algebraic closure, and characteristic is the additive order of 1, or zero if that order is infinite.
