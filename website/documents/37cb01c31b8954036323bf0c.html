Statement

Let K be a field, let R be a finite-dimensional unital K-algebra, and let M be a left R-module finite-dimensional over K. Then M has a finite composition series, and any two such series have the same length and the same simple quotient factors up to permutation and isomorphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Arbitrary-field algebras, modules and their simple factors require abstract algebra.

Proof 1: Specialize the Artinian criterion and apply Jordan–Hölder — outline

Apply the Artinian-ring module criterion to the finite-dimensional algebra and its finite-dimensional module. It gives a finite composition series. The module Jordan–Hölder theorem makes its composition factors and length independent of the chosen series, up to permutation and isomorphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The invoked route uses Artinian rings, modules over algebras, composition factors and module Jordan-Holder. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because these linked module-theoretic notions exceed a confidently small extension of introductory abstract algebra.
