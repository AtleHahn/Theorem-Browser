Statement

Let (K_i)_{i\geq1} be a sequence of number fields, each Galois over \mathbb Q. Let D_i be the discriminant of K_i, h_i its ideal class number, and R_i its regulator (with regulator 1 when the unit rank is zero). If

\frac{[K_i:\mathbb Q]}{\log|D_i|}\longrightarrow0\qquad(i\to\infty),

then

\frac{\log(h_iR_i)}{\log\sqrt{|D_i|}}\longrightarrow1\qquad(i\to\infty).

The hypothesis is understood for the tail where |D_i|>1; it implies |D_i|\to\infty.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Class groups, discriminants and unit regulators require substantial algebraic number theory cumulatively; none are mere new notation for undergraduate linear algebra.
