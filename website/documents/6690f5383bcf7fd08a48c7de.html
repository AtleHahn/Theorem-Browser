Statement

A positive integer N is a congruum if N=y^2-x^2=z^2-y^2 for integers x,y,z. A positive congruent number is the area of a right triangle with rational side lengths. Every positive congruum is a congruent number.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Congruum and congruent number are defined using integer squares and rational right-triangle areas.

Proof 1: The area interpretation of the parameterization — outline

A congruent number is defined as the area of a right triangle with rational sides. Because every congruum can be obtained (using the parameterized solution) as the area of a Pythagorean triangle, it follows that every congruum is congruent.

The parameterized solution used here says that every positive congruum is four times the area of an integer-sided right triangle; multiplying its side lengths by two gives the area formulation above.

High school only
Level justification

Apply the supplied statement that N is four times the area of an integer right triangle and double its sides. The result is a rational-sided right triangle of area N. Only elementary triangle area and scaling are needed.
