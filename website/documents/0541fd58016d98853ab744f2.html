Statement

Every algebraic group over a field k is quasi-projective over k: its underlying variety is isomorphic to a locally closed subvariety of some projective space \mathbb P^N_k. Here an algebraic group is a variety over k equipped with group multiplication and inverse maps that are morphisms, and locally closed means open in a closed subvariety.
