A continuous bijection between the stated Euclidean domains requires matching dimensions. The theorem distinguishes topological representability from mere equality of cardinality.

Statement

Let M and N be nonempty smooth manifolds of dimensions m and n, respectively. If there is a continuous bijection f:M\to N, then m=n.

A smooth manifold has the usual Hausdorff and second-countable topology and local coordinates in Euclidean space with smooth coordinate changes; its dimension is the number of local coordinates. No smoothness of f or continuity of its inverse is assumed.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The assertion uses local-coordinate dimension and elementary continuity/bijection notions. Smooth compatible charts are a modest extension of multivariable calculus and introductory topology; no tangent-bundle machinery is needed.
