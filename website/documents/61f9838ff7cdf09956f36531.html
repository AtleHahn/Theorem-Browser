Statement

Let \Sigma_\infty=\bigcup_n\Sigma_n under the standard inclusions, let B\Sigma_\infty be its classifying space, and let B\Sigma_\infty^+ denote Quillen’s plus construction killing its perfect commutator subgroup. Write Q_0=\varinjlim_n\operatorname{Map}_0(S^n,S^n), using suspension maps, where the mapping spaces consist of based degree-zero maps with compact-open topology. For each integer i\ge1, \pi_i(B\Sigma_\infty^+)\cong\varinjlim_n\pi_{n+i}(S^n), the ith stable homotopy group of spheres.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Classifying spaces, the plus construction and stable homotopy groups require substantial algebraic topology. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Take homotopy groups of the Barratt–Priddy equivalence

Use the Barratt–Priddy equivalence B\Sigma_\infty^+\simeq Q_0=\Omega_0^\infty S^\infty. Its homotopy groups are consequently

\pi_i(B\Sigma_\infty^+)\cong\pi_i(\Omega^\infty S^\infty)\cong\varinjlim_n\pi_{n+i}(S^n),\qquad i\ge1.

The last group is the stable homotopy group of spheres by definition.
