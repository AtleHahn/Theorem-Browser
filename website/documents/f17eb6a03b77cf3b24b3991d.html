Statement

Let \alpha be a smooth regular closed curve in \mathbb R^3, parametrized by arc length s with period L>0. Its curvature is \kappa(s)=\|\alpha''(s)\|. Then its total absolute curvature satisfies

\int_0^L\kappa(s)\,ds\geq2\pi.

Equality holds if and only if the curve is a convex plane curve, that is, it traverses once the boundary of a convex planar region. Regularity means \alpha'(s)\ne0; in the arc-length parametrization \|\alpha'(s)\|=1.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Derivatives, Euclidean norms, definite integrals and elementary convexity suffice once arc-length curvature is defined; these are standard undergraduate calculus concepts.
