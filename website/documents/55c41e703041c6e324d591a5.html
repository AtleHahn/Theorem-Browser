Statement

For an integrable real random variable X, e^{\mathbb EX}\le\mathbb E[e^X], with +\infty allowed on the right.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The only probability background is integrability and expectation as an average/integral of a scalar function. These are a small extension of undergraduate analysis; the displayed exponential moment may be infinite.

Proof 1: Exponential substitution

The exponential function is convex. Substitution of \varphi(x)=e^x into Jensen’s inequality gives the conclusion.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Insert the convex exponential into the expectation form of Jensen. Undergraduate probability and calculus suffice; the infinite upper value is harmless for this direct application.
