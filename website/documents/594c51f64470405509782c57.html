Statement

For a variety X/\mathbb F_q, write N_m=|X(\mathbb F_{q^m})| and Z(X,T)=\exp(\sum_{m\geq1}N_m T^m/m) as a formal power series. If X/\mathbb F_q is smooth projective, then Z(X,T) is a rational function of T.

Level justification

These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: A finite ratio of Frobenius determinant polynomials

The cohomological Lefschetz fixed-point formula, applied to the Frobenius automorphism F, gives the formula for the zeta function

Z(X,T)=\prod_{i=0}^{2n}P_i(T)^{(-1)^{i+1}},

where each polynomial P_i is the determinant of I-TF on the \ell-adic cohomology group H^i=H^i_{\mathrm{et}}(X_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q},\mathbb Q_\ell), with n=\dim X and \ell\ne\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q. These cohomology groups are finite-dimensional.

The rationality of the zeta function follows immediately.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Read rationality from the finite alternating determinant product. The applied formula is a statement about smooth projective varieties and finite-dimensional etale cohomology, which remains substantial graduate background.
