Statement

Let X,Y be Polish spaces (spaces admitting a complete separable metric compatible with their topology), let \mu be a Borel probability measure on Y, and let \pi:Y\to X be Borel measurable. Let \nu=\pi_*\mu, meaning \nu(A)=\mu(\pi^{-1}(A)) for Borel A\subseteq X.

There exists a family (\mu_x)_{x\in X} of Borel probability measures on Y with the following properties:

  1. For every Borel set B\subseteq Y, the function x\mapsto\mu_x(B) is Borel measurable.
  2. For \nu-almost every x, \mu_x(Y\setminus\pi^{-1}(x))=0.
  3. For every nonnegative Borel function f:Y\to[0,\infty],\int_Y f\,d\mu=\int_X\left(\int_Y f\,d\mu_x\right)d\nu(x).

The family is unique up to a \nu-null set: if another family has these properties, its measure equals \mu_x for all x outside one \nu-null set. In particular, for every Borel B\subseteq Y,

\mu(B)=\int_X\mu_x(B)\,d\nu(x).
