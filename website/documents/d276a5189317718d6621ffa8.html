Statement

For every positive integer r and real numbers \delta,\Delta>0, there is \varepsilon=\varepsilon(\delta,\Delta,r)>0 with the following property.

Let R be a finite simple graph with vertices v_1,\ldots,v_r. Replace each v_i by a disjoint set V_i of any positive integer size n_i. Let \mathbf R be the graph on \bigcup_iV_i with all possible edges between V_i and V_j when v_iv_j is an edge of R, and no other edges. Obtain a graph G by replacing each such complete bipartite graph by a bipartite graph satisfying both conditions below:

  • Every a\in V_i has more than \delta|V_j| neighbors in V_j, and every b\in V_j has more than \delta|V_i| neighbors in V_i.
  • Whenever X\subseteq V_i, Y\subseteq V_j, |X|>\varepsilon|V_i| and |Y|>\varepsilon|V_j|, the number e_G(X,Y) of edges between X and Y satisfies e_G(X,Y)>\delta|X||Y|.

Then every finite simple graph H of maximum degree at most \Delta that embeds in \mathbf R also embeds in G. An embedding is an injective vertex map that sends every edge to an edge; it need not preserve nonedges.
