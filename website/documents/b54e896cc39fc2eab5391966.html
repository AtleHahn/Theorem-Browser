Statement

Let \zeta(s) be the meromorphic continuation to the complex plane of \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}, initially defined for \operatorname{Re}s>1. For T>0, let N(T) count its zeros \rho with 0<\operatorname{Im}\rho\le T, with multiplicity. Then, as T\to\infty,

N(T)=\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi}-\frac{T}{2\pi}+O(\log T).

Here \log is the natural logarithm and O(\log T) means that the absolute value of the error is at most C\log T for some constant C and all sufficiently large T.
