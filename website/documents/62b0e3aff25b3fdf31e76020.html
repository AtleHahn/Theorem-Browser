Statement

Let K be a connected compact Lie group, T a maximal torus, \mathfrak k,\mathfrak t their Lie algebras, and W=N_K(T)/T the Weyl group acting on \mathfrak t. Let P:\mathfrak k\to\mathfrak t be orthogonal projection for an \operatorname{Ad}(K)-invariant inner product. For every X\in\mathfrak t, P(\operatorname{Ad}(K)X)=\operatorname{conv}\{wX:w\in W\}.

Proof 1: Identify the orbit projection as a torus moment map

For compact groups, Atiyah (1982) and Guillemin & Sternberg (1982) showed that if M is a compact connected symplectic manifold with a Hamiltonian action of a torus T with Lie algebra {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {t}}}, then the image of the moment map

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {M\rightarrow {\mathfrak {t}}^{*}}}

is a convex polytope with vertices in the image of the fixed point set of T (the image is a finite set). Taking for M a coadjoint orbit of the connected compact group K in {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {k}}^{*}}, the moment map for T is the composition

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {M\rightarrow {\mathfrak {k}}^{*}\rightarrow {\mathfrak {t}}^{*}.}}

Using the Ad-invariant inner product to identify {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {k}}^{*}} and {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {k}}}, the map becomes

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\mathrm {Ad} (K)\cdot X\rightarrow {\mathfrak {t}},}}

the restriction of the orthogonal projection. Taking X in {\displaystyle {\mathfrak {t}}}, the fixed points of T in the orbit Ad(K)⋅X are just the orbit under the Weyl group, W(X). So the convexity properties of the moment map imply that the image is the convex polytope with these vertices.

Proof 2: Specialize the product symmetric space (outline)

Use the symmetric-space form of Kostant’s theorem: in a compact symmetric pair (G,H), projection of an \operatorname{Ad}(H)-orbit in the minus-one eigenspace onto a maximal abelian subspace has image the convex hull of its restricted Weyl orbit.

As specified in the source, take G=K\times K, embed H=K diagonally, and let the involution interchange the two factors. The minus-one eigenspace is \{(Y,-Y):Y\in\mathfrak k\}, with maximal abelian subspace \{(Z,-Z):Z\in\mathfrak t\}. Under Y\leftrightarrow(Y,-Y), the diagonal adjoint action is the original adjoint action, projection is P, and the restricted Weyl action is that of N_K(T)/T. The symmetric-space theorem gives exactly the stated compact-group formula.
