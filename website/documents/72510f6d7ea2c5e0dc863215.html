Statement

For a finite simple undirected graph G on n\ge3 vertices, its closure is obtained by repeatedly adding an edge between nonadjacent vertices whose current degrees sum to at least n, until no such pair remains. If the closure is complete, G has a Hamiltonian cycle.

Level
High school only

Proof 1: Apply Bondy–Chvátal to a complete closure

The Bondy–Chvátal theorem states that a graph is Hamiltonian if and only if its closure is Hamiltonian. As complete graphs on at least three vertices are Hamiltonian, all graphs in the stated scope whose closure is complete are Hamiltonian.
