Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate triangle in the Euclidean plane. Choose points P_a on the line BC, P_b on the line CA, and P_c on the line AB, allowing points on the extensions of the sides. Let \ell_a,\ell_b,\ell_c be the lines through these respective points perpendicular to BC,CA,AB. The three lines have a common point if and only if

|AP_c|^2+|BP_a|^2+|CP_b|^2=|BP_c|^2+|CP_a|^2+|AP_b|^2,

where |XY| denotes Euclidean distance between X and Y.
