Statement

There is a simple convex polyhedron (three edges meeting at each vertex) with exactly three triangular faces, three pentagonal faces and one hexagonal face, and no other faces.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement is a concrete existence claim for a convex solid with prescribed elementary polygon faces; simple means three edges at each vertex.

Proof 1: Cube bisection realizes three triangles, three pentagons and a hexagon — outline

Bisect a cube by a regular hexagonal cross-section passing through its six faces. The two pieces are copies of a simple convex polyhedron, each having three triangular faces, three pentagonal faces and the new hexagonal face.

Level
High school only
