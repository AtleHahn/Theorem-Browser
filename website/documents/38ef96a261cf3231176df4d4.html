Statement

Let R be a unital ring, U a right R-module and V a proper maximal submodule. Then UJ(R)\subseteq V, and in particular UJ(R) is a proper submodule of U.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The assertion concerns right modules, maximal submodules and the Jacobson radical of arbitrary unital rings.

Mathematical Field
Associative algebra

Review notes

Proof 1: Use the simple quotient

The quotient U/V is a simple right R-module. By the characterization of J(R) as the elements annihilating all simple right modules, (U/V)J(R)=0. Hence UJ(R) is contained in V. Since V is proper, UJ(R) is proper.
