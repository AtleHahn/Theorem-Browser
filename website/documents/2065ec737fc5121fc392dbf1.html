Statement

For real or complex sequences (x_k),(y_k) and p,q\in(1,\infty) with 1/p+1/q=1, \sum_{k\ge1}|x_ky_k|\le(\sum|x_k|^p)^{1/p}(\sum|y_k|^q)^{1/q}. Nonnegative sums may be infinite, with 0\cdot\infty=0.

Apply Hölder on \mathbb N with counting measure. Integration of each nonnegative function is its displayed series.
