Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate triangle in the Euclidean plane, with orthocenter H, the common intersection of its three altitude lines. Let L_1,L_2 be perpendicular lines through H, and suppose their intersections with each of the three side lines are well-defined single points. Write

A_i=L_i\cap BC,\qquad B_i=L_i\cap CA,\qquad C_i=L_i\cap AB\qquad(i=1,2),

where AB,BC,CA denote the full lines through the corresponding vertices. Then the midpoints of A_1A_2, B_1B_2 and C_1C_2 are collinear.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement consists entirely of elementary Euclidean geometry; the orthocenter and side-line convention are explicitly explained.
