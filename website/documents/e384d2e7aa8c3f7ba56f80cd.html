Statement

Let U=\varinjlim U(N), O=\varinjlim O(N) and \mathrm{Sp}=\varinjlim\mathrm{Sp}(N) be the stable classical groups under the standard inclusions. Use identity basepoints, based loop spaces \Omega, and the standard based homotopy types for classifying spaces BU,BO,B\mathrm{Sp}. For each integer k\ge1,

\pi_k(O)\cong\pi_{k+4}(\mathrm{Sp}),\qquad\pi_k(\mathrm{Sp})\cong\pi_{k+4}(O).

For k=0 the corresponding assertions hold as bijections of path-component sets.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Take homotopy groups of the four-step chains (outline)

Write R=\mathbb Z\times BO and Q=\mathbb Z\times B\mathrm{Sp}, based at the zero component. Use the following stated Bott equivalences as external prerequisites:

\Omega O\simeq O/U,\quad\Omega(O/U)\simeq U/\mathrm{Sp},\quad \Omega(U/\mathrm{Sp})\simeq Q,\quad\Omega Q\simeq\mathrm{Sp},\Omega\mathrm{Sp}\simeq\mathrm{Sp}/U,\quad\Omega(\mathrm{Sp}/U)\simeq U/O,\quad \Omega(U/O)\simeq R,\quad\Omega R\simeq O.

Here the quotients use the stable standard inclusions of classical groups. Looping preserves based homotopy equivalences.

The real/quaternionic four-step chains give \Omega^4O\simeq\mathrm{Sp} and \Omega^4\mathrm{Sp}\simeq O. Use the standard loop-homotopy identification \pi_k(\Omega^4Y)\cong\pi_{k+4}(Y) for k\ge1, and its path-component version for k=0. Applying it to these two equivalences proves both stated shifts.
