Statement

On a filtered probability space (\Omega,\mathcal F,(\mathcal F_t)_{t\ge0},\mathbb P), let X be nonnegative, adapted and right-continuous, and let G be nonnegative, predictable, right-continuous and nondecreasing. Suppose for every bounded stopping time \tau,

\mathbb E[X_\tau\mid\mathcal F_0]\le\mathbb E[G_\tau\mid\mathcal F_0]<\infty\quad\text{almost surely}.

Put X^*=\sup_{t\ge0}X_t and G^*=\sup_{t\ge0}G_t. Adapted means X_t is \mathcal F_t-measurable; predictable means measurable for the sigma-algebra generated by adapted left-continuous processes. A stopping time satisfies \{\tau\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t for every t, and bounded means bounded by a finite deterministic constant. For every c,d>0, almost surely,

\mathbb P(X^*>c\mid\mathcal F_0)\le\frac1c\mathbb E[\min(G^*,d)\mid\mathcal F_0]+\mathbb P(G^*\ge d\mid\mathcal F_0).

For every real 0<p<1, almost surely,

\mathbb E[(X^*)^p\mid\mathcal F_0]\le\frac{p^{-p}}{1-p}\mathbb E[(G^*)^p\mid\mathcal F_0].

Conditional moments may equal +\infty.
