Statement

Every closed Riemannian three-manifold contains infinitely many distinct smooth closed immersed minimal surfaces. Closed means compact without boundary. An immersed surface is the image of a smooth immersion of a compact surface without boundary; an immersion has injective differential. Minimal means that its mean-curvature vector vanishes everywhere. Distinct surfaces here are counted geometrically, not merely by different parametrizations of the same image.
