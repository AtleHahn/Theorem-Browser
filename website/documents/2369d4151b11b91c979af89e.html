Statement

Let n\ge1 and let L=B\mathbb Z^n\subset\mathbb R^n be a full-rank lattice, where B is an invertible real matrix. Its covolume is d(L)=|\det B|. Let S\subseteq\mathbb R^n be convex and symmetric about the origin, meaning S=-S. If its n-dimensional volume satisfies

\operatorname{vol}_n(S)>2^n d(L),

then S contains a nonzero point of L. Boundedness of S is not required; infinite volume is allowed.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Convexity, determinants and volume belong to undergraduate linear algebra and analysis. A full-rank lattice is explicitly the image of integer vectors under an invertible matrix; covolume is locally defined. This is a small extension of that background.
