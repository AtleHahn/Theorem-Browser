Statement

Let G be a locally compact Hausdorff abelian topological group. Write \mathbb T=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=1\} with multiplication, and let \widehat G be the group of continuous homomorphisms \chi:G\to\mathbb T, with pointwise multiplication and the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets of G. The group \widehat G is again locally compact Hausdorff abelian, so its dual \widehat{\widehat G} is defined in the same way.

The evaluation map

e_G:G\longrightarrow\widehat{\widehat G},\qquad e_G(x)(\chi)=\chi(x),

is an isomorphism of topological groups (a group isomorphism that is a homeomorphism). These isomorphisms are natural: for every continuous homomorphism f:G\to H between locally compact Hausdorff abelian groups, define \widehat f:\widehat H\to\widehat G by \widehat f(\chi)=\chi\circ f. Then

\widehat{\widehat f}\circ e_G=e_H\circ f.
