Statement

For every integer d\geq3, there is a prime q such that q\equiv1\pmod d and

q\leq2^d-1.

Here q\equiv1\pmod d means that d divides q-1. Equivalently, the least prime of the form 1+nd, with an integer n\geq1, is at most 2^d-1.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All objects and the bound are expressed using elementary integer arithmetic.
