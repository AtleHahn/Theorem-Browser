Statement

Let f be a real twice differentiable function whose value and second derivative are bounded on its domain. Write \|g\|_\infty=\sup|g|. On the full real line,

\|f'\|_\infty\le\sqrt2\,\|f\|_\infty^{1/2}\|f''\|_\infty^{1/2}.

On a half-line [c,\infty), the corresponding inequality is

\|f'\|_\infty\le2\,\|f\|_\infty^{1/2}\|f''\|_\infty^{1/2},

with derivatives at c understood from the right. Both constants are optimal for their respective domains.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Single-variable calculus and supremum bounds suffice; no operator theory is required.
