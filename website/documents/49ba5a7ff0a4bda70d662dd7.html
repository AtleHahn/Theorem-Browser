Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer and X a set. Then |X|\ge\aleph_n if and only if every map \Phi:[X]^n\to[X]^{<\omega} admits a subset U\subseteq X of size n+1 such that

\Phi(V)\cap(U\setminus V)=\varnothing\quad\text{for every }V\in[U]^n.

Here [X]^n is the set of n-element subsets, [X]^{<\omega} the set of all finite subsets, \aleph_0 the cardinality of the natural numbers, and \aleph_{j+1} the least infinite cardinal greater than \aleph_j. Cardinal inequality means existence of an injection from a set of the smaller cardinality into one of the larger cardinality.
