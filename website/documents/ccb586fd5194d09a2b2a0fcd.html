Statement

If a local diffeomorphism s fixes a point p of a Riemannian manifold and satisfies s(\gamma(t))=\gamma(-t) for every geodesic through p with \gamma(0)=p, then ds_p=-\mathrm{id}_{T_pM}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns the tangent differential of a local geodesic reversal.

Proof 1: Differentiate geodesic reversal

Differentiate the geodesic-reversal identity at t=0. It sends the initial tangent vector of every geodesic to its negative. Since every tangent vector is the initial vector of a geodesic, the differential is minus the identity.
