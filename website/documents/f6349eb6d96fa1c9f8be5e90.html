Statement

Let R be a commutative unital ring containing \mathbb Q, let n\ge3 be an integer, and let A\in M_n(R). Put s_j=\operatorname{tr}(A^j) and write \det(tI_n-A)=\sum_{i=0}^n c_it^i, c_n=1. Then c_{n-3}=-(s_1^3-3s_1s_2+2s_3)/6.

Proof 1: The third characteristic coefficient from traces

Use the characteristic-coefficient identity c_{n-k}=(-1)^k B_k(s_1,-1!s_2,2!s_3,\ldots,(-1)^{k-1}(k-1)!s_k)/k!, where B_k is the complete exponential Bell polynomial and B_0=1.

Here B_3(x_1,x_2,x_3)=x_1^3+3x_1x_2+x_3.

For a 3\times3 matrix, the determinant is

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\det(A)&={\frac {1}{3!}}B_{3}(s_{1},-1!s_{2},2!s_{3})={\frac {1}{6}}(s_{1}^{3}+3s_{1}(-s_{2})+2s_{3})\\[5pt]&={\frac {1}{6}}\left[(\operatorname {tr} A)^{3}-3\operatorname {tr} (A^{2})(\operatorname {tr} A)+2\operatorname {tr} (A^{3})\right].\end{aligned}}}

This expression gives the negative of coefficient cn−3 of tn−3 in the general case, by the characteristic-coefficient identity.
