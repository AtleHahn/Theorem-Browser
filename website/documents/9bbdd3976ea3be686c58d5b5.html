Statement

For real x and integer n, \cos(nx)=2\cos x\cos((n-1)x)-\cos((n-2)x).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The stated recurrence uses only real trigonometry and integer arithmetic.

Proof 1: Take real parts after replacing one exponential factor

Represent cosines in terms of the real part of a complex expression and perform the manipulations on the complex expression. Using Euler's formula e^{ix}=\cos x+i\sin x, with i^2=-1, gives

\begin{aligned} \cos nx&=\operatorname{Re}(e^{inx})\\ &=\operatorname{Re}(e^{i(n-1)x}\cdot e^{ix})\\ &=\operatorname{Re}\left(e^{i(n-1)x}\cdot \left(\underbrace{e^{ix}+e^{-ix}}_{2\cos x}-e^{-ix}\right)\right)\\ &=\operatorname{Re}(e^{i(n-1)x}\cdot2\cos x-e^{i(n-2)x})\\ &=\cos[(n-1)x]\cdot[2\cos x]-\cos[(n-2)x]. \end{aligned}
