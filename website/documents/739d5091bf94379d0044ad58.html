Statement

There is a constant c>0 such that for all sufficiently large integers N, every subset A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\} with no three-term arithmetic progression a,a+d,a+2d, d\ge1, satisfies

|A|\le N\exp\bigl(-c(\log N)^{1/9}\bigr).

The logarithm is natural.
