Statement

An algebraic number field is a finite extension of \mathbb Q. A central simple K-algebra is a finite-dimensional associative unital K-algebra whose center is K and whose only two-sided ideals are 0 and the whole algebra. For each place v of K, including the archimedean places, write K_v for the completion of K at v.

Let A and B be central simple algebras over a number field K. Then A\cong B as K-algebras if and only if A\otimes_K K_v\cong B\otimes_K K_v as K_v-algebras for every place v of K.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Number-field places and completions, noncommutative central simple algebras, and scalar extension via tensor products form several interdependent concepts beyond introductory algebra. Their cumulative burden exceeds the conservative undergraduate reading allowance.
