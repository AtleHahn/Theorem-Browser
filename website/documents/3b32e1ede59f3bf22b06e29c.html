Statement

Work in ZFC and assume GCH: 2^\kappa=\kappa^+ for every infinite cardinal \kappa, where \kappa^+ is the next larger cardinal. If ordinals \alpha,\beta satisfy \alpha\le\beta+1, then \aleph_\alpha^{\aleph_\beta}=\aleph_{\beta+1}.

Proof 1: Bound the exponent between matching successor cardinals

The upper bound is \aleph_\alpha^{\aleph_\beta}\le\aleph_{\beta+1}^{\aleph_\beta}=(2^{\aleph_\beta})^{\aleph_\beta}=2^{\aleph_\beta\cdot\aleph_\beta}=2^{\aleph_\beta}=\aleph_{\beta+1}. Conversely \aleph_{\beta+1}=2^{\aleph_\beta}\le\aleph_\alpha^{\aleph_\beta}. The two bounds prove the equality.
