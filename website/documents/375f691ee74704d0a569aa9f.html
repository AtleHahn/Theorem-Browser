Statement

Let nondegenerate Euclidean triangles ABC_1 and ABC_2 have the same base AB, with their apices on the same side of its line. They have equal area if and only if C_1,C_2 lie on one line parallel to AB.

Level
High school only

All objects and operations are elementary Euclidean geometry.

Proof 1: Double the triangles and compare nested triangles

Use Euclid I.35: parallelograms with the same base whose top sides are collinear have equal area.

Elements I.37 holds that triangles with the same base and an apex on the same line parallel to the base have equal area. Proof: Let triangles {\displaystyle \triangle ABC_{1}} and {\displaystyle \triangle ABC_{2}} each have its apex on the same line {\displaystyle l} parallel to the base {\displaystyle AB.} Construct new segments {\displaystyle C_{1}D_{1}} and {\displaystyle C_{2}D_{2}} congruent to {\displaystyle AB} with vertices {\displaystyle D_{1}} and {\displaystyle D_{2}} on {\displaystyle l.} The two quadrilaterals {\displaystyle \square ABC_{1}D_{1}} and {\displaystyle \square ABC_{2}D_{2}} are parallelograms, each formed by pasting together the respective triangle and a congruent copy. By I.35, the two parallelograms have the same area, so the original triangles must also have the same area.

Elements I.39 is the converse: two triangles of equal area on the same side of the same base have their apexes on a line parallel to the base. Proof: If two triangles have the same base and same area and the apex of the second is assumed to not lie on the line parallel to the base (the "Lexell line") through the first, then the line through one side of the second triangle can be intersected with the Lexell line to form a new triangle which has a different area from the second triangle but the same area as the first triangle, a contradiction.

Level justification

Double each triangle to a parallelogram and apply the supplied equal-area result. For the converse compare the nested triangle made by the parallel line. Only Euclidean congruence, parallels and area comparisons are used.
