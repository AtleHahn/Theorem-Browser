Statement

A genus-g handlebody is a compact three-manifold homeomorphic to a regular neighborhood of a bouquet of g circles; a Heegaard splitting is a decomposition into two such handlebodies along their common boundary. Let M be a closed oriented reducible three-manifold (it contains an embedded two-sphere not bounding a ball). Every Heegaard splitting M=V\cup_H W has an essential simple closed curve on H bounding a disk in both V and W.

Proof 1: Every splitting of a reducible three-manifold is reducible — outline

Apply Haken’s lemma to an essential two-sphere and the splitting. It gives an essential two-sphere meeting H in a single curve; the two disk sides supply the reducing curve.
