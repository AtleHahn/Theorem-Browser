Statement

Let K be the bounded interior of a simple closed curve C, and let f,g be holomorphic on an open neighborhood of \overline K. If |g|<|f| on C, then f and f+g have the same number of zeros in K, counted with multiplicity.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex differentiability extends the calculus derivative; limits, compact sets, uniform approximation and zeros of functions are familiar analysis. Zero multiplicity is the order of the first nonzero Taylor coefficient, and circle integrals are parametrized ordinary integrals. These limited definitions suffice for the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply the symmetric boundary comparison

Put h=f+g. On C, |h-f|=|g|<|f|\le |h|+|f|. Apply the symmetric theorem: holomorphic functions u,v on a neighborhood of a closed Jordan domain with |u-v|<|u|+|v| on the boundary have equal interior zero counts, with multiplicity.
