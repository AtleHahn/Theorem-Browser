Statement

Let (\Omega,\mathcal A,\mu) be a measure space and 1\le p<\infty. Let measurable f_n:\Omega\to\mathbb C\cup\{\infty\} converge almost everywhere to a measurable f and satisfy |f_n|\le g almost everywhere for a nonnegative g\in L^p. Then f_n,f\in L^p and \|f_n-f\|_p\to0, where null-set values are immaterial.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The result concerns norms of functions on arbitrary measure spaces, beyond finite-dimensional linear algebra.

Proof 1: Dominate the pth powers of the errors — outline

Idea of the proof: Apply the dominated convergence theorem to the function sequence h_n=|f_n-f|^p with the dominating function (2g)^p.

Values on the exceptional null set may be replaced by zero. The source leaves the integrability and norm-limit checks implicit.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Dominate the pth power of the error by (2g)^p and apply Lebesgue dominated convergence. Arbitrary measurable functions, null sets and Lp norms are essential.
