Statement

Suppose a finite Galois extension of discretely valued fields with uniquely extended normalized valuation has Galois group G=Q_8, and its lower ramification groups satisfy G_0=G_1=Q_8, G_2=G_3=C_2 and G_4=1, where C_2 is the order-two subgroup. Here v_L is the normalized valuation on the extension field L and \mathcal O_L its valuation ring. Extend the lower filtration by its valuation definition G_s=\{\sigma:v_L(\sigma a-a)\ge s+1\text{ for all }a\in\mathcal O_L\}. Define \eta(s)=\int_0^s[G_0:G_x]^{-1}\,dx for s\ge0 and G^t=G_{\eta^{-1}(t)}. Then G^t=Q_8 for 0\le t\le1, G^t=C_2 for 1<t\le3/2, and G^t=1 for t>3/2. In particular 3/2 is a noninteger upper jump.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The valuation and Galois-theoretic ramification filtration requires substantial local-field theory, despite the explicit index calculation. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.
