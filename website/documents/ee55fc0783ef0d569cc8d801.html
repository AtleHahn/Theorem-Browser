Statement

For nonnegative integers b,y, 2^b-1=y(y+1)/2 if and only if 2^{b+3}-7=(2y+1)^2. Thus the substitution n=b+3, x=2y+1 identifies these triangular Mersenne representations with positive odd solutions of 2^n-7=x^2 with n\ge3.

Proof 1: Complete the square

The problem of finding all numbers of the form 2^b-1 (Mersenne numbers) which are triangular is equivalent:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}&\ 2^{b}-1={\frac {y(y+1)}{2}}\\[2pt]\Longleftrightarrow &\ 8(2^{b}-1)=4y(y+1)\\\Longleftrightarrow &\ 2^{b+3}-8=4y^{2}+4y\\\Longleftrightarrow &\ 2^{b+3}-7=4y^{2}+4y+1\\\Longleftrightarrow &\ 2^{b+3}-7=(2y+1)^{2}\end{aligned}}}

The substitutions are n=b+3, x=2y+1, with inverse b=n-3, y=(x-1)/2.
