Statement

Let X,Y be Banach spaces and T:X\to Y linear. Let \tau be a topology on X\times Y coarser than the product norm topology. If \Gamma_T is \tau-closed, then T is continuous and bounded.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra supplies inner products, linear or conjugate-linear maps and bilinear forms; analysis supplies norm bounds and completeness. Hilbert and Banach spaces add completeness for the specified norm. The displayed forms, evaluations and inequalities use these direct extensions without weak topologies, biduals or spectral theory.

Proof 1: Pass from coarser closedness to the norm topology

Note, to check the closedness of a graph, it's not even necessary to use the norm topology: if the graph of T is closed in some topology coarser than the norm topology, then it is closed in the norm topology. By the Banach closed graph theorem, T is continuous under the original topology. For a linear map between normed spaces, continuity is equivalent to boundedness.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Closedness in a coarser topology implies norm-product closedness. Apply the explicitly identified Banach closed graph theorem, then continuity-boundedness equivalence. Complete normed spaces are a small extension of metric completeness and linear algebra; duality and weak topologies are not used.
