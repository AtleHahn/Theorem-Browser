Statement

Let S be an unbounded complex domain and f holomorphic there. Suppose for each \varepsilon>0 there are a holomorphic factor h_\varepsilon and a bounded subdomain D_\varepsilon\subset S such that fh_\varepsilon extends continuously to \overline{D_\varepsilon}, has modulus at most M on \partial D_\varepsilon, and also has modulus at most M on S\setminus\overline{D_\varepsilon}. If h_\varepsilon(z)\to1 at every z\in S as \varepsilon\downarrow0, then |f(z)|\le M throughout S.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex differentiability extends the calculus derivative. Power series, circle parametrization, bounds and convergence are familiar analysis; the hypotheses and formulas are explicit, with no abstract complex geometry or distribution theory.

Proof 1: Combine interior and exterior bounds, then remove damping

Apply the bounded-domain maximum modulus principle to fh_\varepsilon on D_\varepsilon. Combined with the assumed exterior bound, this bounds |fh_\varepsilon| by M everywhere in S. Let \varepsilon\downarrow0 at a fixed point to obtain |f|\le M.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Bound each damped holomorphic function by the bounded-domain maximum principle and take its pointwise limit. Undergraduate complex analysis suffices.
