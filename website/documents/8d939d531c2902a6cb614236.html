Statement

Let M be a closed essential Riemannian n-manifold, n\ge1. Essential means its fundamental class maps nontrivially to the homology of K(\pi_1M,1), with integer coefficients in the orientable case and modulo-two coefficients otherwise. Write \operatorname{sys}_{\pi_1}(M) for the least length of a noncontractible loop. There is a constant C_n depending only on n such that \operatorname{sys}_{\pi_1}(M)^n\le C_n\operatorname{vol}_n(M).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Essential manifolds are defined using fundamental classes and homology of classifying spaces, requiring substantial algebraic topology in addition to Riemannian geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Combine the two filling-radius bounds (outline)

Use the source’s two filling-radius inequalities, with \operatorname{FillRad}(M) denoting the filling radius:

\operatorname{sys}_{\pi_1}(M)\le6\operatorname{FillRad}(M),\qquad \operatorname{FillRad}(M)\le A_n\operatorname{vol}_n(M)^{1/n}.

The first holds for essential manifolds and the second for all closed manifolds. Combining them and raising to the nth power gives the assertion with C_n=(6A_n)^n.
