Statement

Let S\subset\mathbb R^3 be a compact smooth oriented surface with smooth boundary, induced boundary orientation and unit normal n. Let E,B be vector fields smooth in space and differentiable in time near S. At each time where \nabla\times E=-\partial_t B, \int_{\partial S}E\cdot dl=-\int_S\partial_tB\cdot n\,dS.

Proof 1: Substitute the curl equation into Stokes

The differential and integral forms are related by the three-dimensional (vector calculus) case of Stokes' theorem. At the fixed time, using \nabla\times E=-\partial_tB, we have

\begin{aligned} \oint_{\partial S}E\cdot dl &=\iint_S(\nabla\times E)\cdot n\,dS\\ &=-\iint_S\frac{\partial B}{\partial t}\cdot n\,dS. \end{aligned}
