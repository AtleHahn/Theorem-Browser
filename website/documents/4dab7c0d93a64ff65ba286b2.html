Statement

Let p be a prime. Let \mathbb Q_p be the completion of \mathbb Q for the p-adic absolute value normalized by |p|_p=p^{-1}, and let \mathbb C_p be the completion of an algebraic closure of \mathbb Q_p for the extended absolute value. On the open disk

D_p=\{z\in\mathbb C_p:|z|_p<p^{-1/(p-1)}\},

define

\exp_p(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{z^n}{n!}.

Then, for every z\in D_p,

\exp_p(z)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad z=0.
