Statement

Let A be an abelian group and n\geq0 an integer. Let C be the nonnegative chain complex with C_n=A, all other groups zero and all differentials zero. Under the Dold–Kan equivalence, its corresponding simplicial abelian group has homotopy group A in degree n and zero in every other degree; it is an Eilenberg–MacLane object K(A,n).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves abelian groups; nonnegative chain complexes and homology; simplicial abelian groups and homotopy groups; Eilenberg–MacLane objects. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Concentrated complex and homology–homotopy correspondence — outline

Use the chain complex with A concentrated in degree n and zero elsewhere. Under the Dold–Kan correspondence, its homology groups are the homotopy groups of the corresponding simplicial abelian group. This gives K(A,n).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The application relates chain-complex homology to homotopy groups of simplicial abelian groups through a categorical equivalence. These linked homological-algebra and algebraic-topology prerequisites are substantial.
