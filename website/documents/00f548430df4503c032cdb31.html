Statement

Let n,m\ge1 and \Phi:M_n(\mathbb C)\to M_m(\mathbb C) be complex-linear. Then \Phi maps Hermitian matrices to Hermitian matrices if and only if \Phi(A)=\sum_{i=1}^{nm}\lambda_i V_iAV_i^* for some real \lambda_i and V_i\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}. One may take \lambda_i to be the eigenvalues of C_\Phi=[\Phi(E_{kl})], and V_i to be its corresponding eigenvectors reshaped by V_i e_k=P_kw_i. Write E_{kl} for the matrix units in M_n(\mathbb C) and * for conjugate transpose; in the Hermitian-preserving case let w_i be an orthonormal eigenbasis of C_\Phi. Here e_k is the kth standard basis vector of \mathbb C^n, and P_k:\mathbb C^{nm}\to\mathbb C^m selects the kth block of m coordinates.

Proof 1: Reshape a signed Hermitian spectral decomposition — outline

Write E_{kl} for the matrix units in M_n(\mathbb C) and * for conjugate transpose; in the Hermitian-preserving direction let w_i be an orthonormal eigenbasis of C_\Phi. Here e_k is the kth standard basis vector of \mathbb C^n, and P_k:\mathbb C^{nm}\to\mathbb C^m selects the kth block of m coordinates.

For a Hermitian-preserving map the Choi matrix is Hermitian. Use its spectral decomposition C_\Phi=\sum_i\lambda_i w_iw_i^*, with real eigenvalues, and reshape w_i to V_i by V_ie_k=P_kw_i. Choi's block computation gives

\Phi(E_{kl})=P_kC_\Phi P_l^*=\sum_i\lambda_iV_iE_{kl}V_i^*,

and linearity gives the displayed signed representation. Conversely each real-weighted conjugation term preserves Hermitian matrices.
