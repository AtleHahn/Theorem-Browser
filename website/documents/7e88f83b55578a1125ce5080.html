Statement

On t\ge0, the initial-value problem y'=y^{1/3}, y(0)=0, with the real cube root, has at least three distinct solutions.

Proof 1: Zero and two signed power trajectories — outline

The right-hand side of the equation dy/dt=y^{1/3} with initial condition y(0)=0 is continuous but not Lipschitz continuous at zero. Indeed, rather than being unique, this equation has at least three solutions on t\ge0:

y(t)=0,\qquad y(t)=\pm\left(\frac23t\right)^{3/2}.

The source gives these witnesses without writing out their derivative and initial-value checks.
