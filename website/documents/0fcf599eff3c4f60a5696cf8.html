Statement

Let K be any field, let X be an indeterminate, and let M be a field with K\subseteq M\subseteq K(X). Then there is f(X)\in K(X) such that M=K(f(X)). Here K(X) consists of fractions of polynomials in X over K with nonzero denominator, and K(f(X)) is the smallest subfield of K(X) containing K and f(X). The case M=K is included, by taking f(X) constant.
