Statement

Let \mathbf A be a graph of groups on a connected graph A with vertex set V and oriented edge set E. Edge reversal e\mapsto\bar e is a fixed-point-free involution, o(e) and t(e)=o(\bar e) are the endpoints, and E^+ chooses one orientation of each geometric edge. Each vertex has a group A_v, each edge has a group A_e=A_{\bar e}, and there are injective boundary maps \alpha_e:A_e\to A_{o(e)}, \omega_e=\alpha_{\bar e}:A_e\to A_{t(e)}. Choose a spanning tree T. Write F(S) for the free group on a set S and \langle\!\langle R\rangle\!\rangle for the normal subgroup generated by relators R. Define G=\pi_1(\mathbf A,T) as the quotient of (\ast_{v\in V}A_v)\ast F(E) by \bar e\alpha_e(c)e=\omega_e(c), e\bar e=1 for all e\in E,c\in A_e, and e=1 for edges of T. Put E^+T=E^+\cap E(T) and S=E^+\setminus E(T). Then

B=(\ast_{v\in V}A_v)/\langle\!\langle\alpha_e(c)\omega_e(c)^{-1}:e\in E^+T,c\in A_e\rangle\!\rangle

is the tree amalgamation of the vertex groups, and

G\cong\langle B,S\mid e^{-1}\alpha_e(c)e=\omega_e(c),\ e\in S,c\in A_e\rangle.

Thus the fundamental group is obtained by tree amalgamation followed by the indicated multiple HNN extension.

Proof 1: Source presentation calculation

Write \operatorname{ncl} for normal closure in the displayed ambient group and E^+(A-T)=S for the chosen oriented edges outside the spanning tree.

The group G = π1(A, T) defined in the statement admits an algebraic description in terms of iterated amalgamated free products and HNN extensions. First, form a group B as a quotient of the free product

{\displaystyle (\ast _{v\in V}A_{v})*F(E^{+}T)}

subject to the relations

  • e−1αe(g)e = ωe(g) for every e in E+T and every {\displaystyle g\in A_{e}}.
  • e = 1 for every e in E+T.

This presentation can be rewritten as

{\displaystyle B=\ast _{v\in V}A_{v}/{\rm {ncl}}\{\alpha _{e}(g)=\omega _{e}(g),{\text{ where }}e\in E^{+}T,g\in A_{e}\}}

which shows that B is an iterated amalgamated free product of the vertex groups Av.

Then the group G = π1(A, T) has the presentation

{\displaystyle \langle B,E^{+}(A-T)|e^{-1}\alpha _{e}(g)e=\omega _{e}(g){\text{ where }}e\in E^{+}(A-T),g\in A_{e}\rangle ,}

which shows that G = π1(A, T) is a multiple HNN extension of B with stable letters {\displaystyle \{e|e\in E^{+}(A-T)\}}.
