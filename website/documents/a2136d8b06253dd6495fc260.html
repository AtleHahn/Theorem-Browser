Statement

In ZFC, for every infinite cardinal \kappa,

\operatorname{cf}(2^\kappa)>\kappa.

Here 2^\kappa is the cardinality of the set of all subsets of a set of size \kappa, regarded as an initial ordinal, and \operatorname{cf}(2^\kappa) is the least cardinality of a subset unbounded in that ordinal. Equivalently, no set of at most \kappa ordinals below 2^\kappa is cofinal in 2^\kappa.
