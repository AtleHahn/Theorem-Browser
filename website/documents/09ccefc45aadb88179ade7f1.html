Proof 1: Exponential of the reciprocal — outline

The source witness is f(z)=e^{1/z}: it has an essential singularity at zero and, as an exponential, never takes the value zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The counterexample relies on the classification of isolated holomorphic singularities and the essential singularity of exp(1/z). Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because understanding that analytic classification is more than simply the formula for the exponential.
