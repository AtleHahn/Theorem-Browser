Statement

Let f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n be smooth and let u_*\in\mathbb R^n satisfy f(u_*)=0. Let A=Df(u_*) be the Jacobian matrix, and suppose no complex eigenvalue of A has real part zero. There are neighborhoods N of u_* and V of 0 and a homeomorphism h:N\to V with h(u_*)=0 which conjugates the local flow of \dot u=f(u) to that of \dot U=AU: writing \phi_t(u) for the solution with initial value u, h(\phi_t(u))=e^{tA}h(u) whenever the intervening local trajectory stays in N. A homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse, and e^{tA}=\sum_{j=0}^\infty t^jA^j/j!.

Level justification

Undergraduate ODE, linear algebra and topology suffice; conjugacy is an explicit solution identity.
