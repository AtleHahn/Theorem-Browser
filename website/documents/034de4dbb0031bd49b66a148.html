Statement

Let \Gamma\subseteq\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z) be a congruence subgroup and let X(\Gamma) be the compact modular curve obtained from \Gamma\backslash\mathbb H, where \mathbb H=\{z\in\mathbb C:\operatorname{Im}z>0\}, by adjoining its cusps. For any two cusps P,Q, the degree-zero divisor class [P-Q] has finite order in the Jacobian of X(\Gamma). Equivalently, some integer m\ge1 makes m(P-Q) the divisor of a nonzero meromorphic function on X(\Gamma). Here a congruence subgroup contains \Gamma(N)=\{A\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z):A\equiv I\pmod N\} for some N\ge1. A matrix \begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix} acts by z\mapsto(az+b)/(cz+d); cusps are the orbits of \mathbb Q\cup\{\infty\}. The Jacobian's complex points identify with degree-zero divisors modulo principal divisors, and the divisor of a meromorphic function counts its zeros positively and poles negatively, with multiplicities.
