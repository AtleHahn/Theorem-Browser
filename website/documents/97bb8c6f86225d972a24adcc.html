Statement

Let f:X\to Y be a morphism of ringed spaces, let \mathcal F be a sheaf of \mathcal O_X-modules, and let \mathcal E be a locally free sheaf of \mathcal O_Y-modules of finite rank. For every integer i\ge0, the natural morphism

R^if_*\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_Y}\mathcal E\longrightarrow R^if_*\bigl(\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}f^*\mathcal E\bigr)

is an isomorphism of \mathcal O_Y-module sheaves. A ringed space is a topological space with a sheaf of commutative rings. A morphism includes a continuous map and a sheaf map f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y\to\mathcal O_X. The direct image is (f_*\mathcal F)(U)=\mathcal F(f^{-1}U), and R^if_* is its ith right derived functor. The pullback is f^*\mathcal E=\mathcal O_X\otimes_{f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y}f^{-1}\mathcal E. Locally free of finite rank means locally isomorphic to a finite direct sum of copies of the structure sheaf. Tensor products in the formula are sheaf tensor products.

Level justification

Sheaf modules, pullback and derived functors are interdependent concepts well beyond the undergraduate allowance.
