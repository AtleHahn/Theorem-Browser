Statement

Let x be a normal point of an algebraic variety X over a field. Write A=\mathcal O_{X,x} for its local ring and \mathfrak m for the maximal ideal of A. Then the \mathfrak m-adic completion

\widehat A=\varprojlim_{n\ge1}A/\mathfrak m^n

is a normal integral domain. Thus x is analytically normal.

A variety is an integral separated scheme of finite type over a field. A normal point means that A is an integrally closed domain. A domain is integrally closed when every element of its fraction field satisfying a monic polynomial with coefficients in the domain lies in it. The inverse-limit maps are the natural quotient maps A/\mathfrak m^{n+1}\to A/\mathfrak m^n.
