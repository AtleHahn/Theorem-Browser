Statement

Let H be an infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space and let GL(H) be the group of invertible bounded linear operators with the operator-norm topology. Then GL(H) is path connected and \pi_n(GL(H),I)=0 for every n≥1, where I is the identity operator.

Proof 1: Apply finite-complex null-homotopy to spheres (outline)

Invoke the source form of Kuiper’s theorem: every continuous map from a finite CW complex into GL(H) is null-homotopic. Spheres are finite CW complexes, so their maps are null-homotopic. Taking the two-point sphere gives path connectedness, and the positive-dimensional spheres give the vanishing homotopy groups. For based maps into this topological group, a null-homotopy can be kept based by multiplying it by the inverse of its value at the basepoint.
