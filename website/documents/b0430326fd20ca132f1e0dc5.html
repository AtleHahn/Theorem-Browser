Statement

For real vectors x,y of length eight, define the following bilinear forms:

\begin{aligned}B_{1}(x,y)&=x_{1}y_{1}-x_{2}y_{2}-x_{3}y_{3}-x_{4}y_{4}-x_{5}y_{5}-x_{6}y_{6}-x_{7}y_{7}-x_{8}y_{8}\\B_{2}(x,y)&=x_{2}y_{1}+x_{1}y_{2}+x_{4}y_{3}-x_{3}y_{4}+x_{6}y_{5}-x_{5}y_{6}-x_{8}y_{7}+x_{7}y_{8}\\B_{3}(x,y)&=x_{3}y_{1}-x_{4}y_{2}+x_{1}y_{3}+x_{2}y_{4}+x_{7}y_{5}+x_{8}y_{6}-x_{5}y_{7}-x_{6}y_{8}\\B_{4}(x,y)&=x_{4}y_{1}+x_{3}y_{2}-x_{2}y_{3}+x_{1}y_{4}+x_{8}y_{5}-x_{7}y_{6}+x_{6}y_{7}-x_{5}y_{8}\\B_{5}(x,y)&=x_{5}y_{1}-x_{6}y_{2}-x_{7}y_{3}-x_{8}y_{4}+x_{1}y_{5}+x_{2}y_{6}+x_{3}y_{7}+x_{4}y_{8}\\B_{6}(x,y)&=x_{6}y_{1}+x_{5}y_{2}-x_{8}y_{3}+x_{7}y_{4}-x_{2}y_{5}+x_{1}y_{6}-x_{4}y_{7}+x_{3}y_{8}\\B_{7}(x,y)&=x_{7}y_{1}+x_{8}y_{2}+x_{5}y_{3}-x_{6}y_{4}-x_{3}y_{5}+x_{4}y_{6}+x_{1}y_{7}-x_{2}y_{8}\\B_{8}(x,y)&=x_{8}y_{1}-x_{7}y_{2}+x_{6}y_{3}+x_{5}y_{4}-x_{4}y_{5}-x_{3}y_{6}+x_{2}y_{7}+x_{1}y_{8}\end{aligned} If \sum_{i=1}^8x_i^2>0, then (\sum_{i=1}^8x_i^2)(\sum_{i=1}^8y_i^2)=\sum_{i=1}^8 B_i(x,y)^2.

Substitution

If all {\displaystyle x_{i}} and {\displaystyle y_{i}} with {\displaystyle i>8} are set equal to zero, then it reduces to Degen's eight-square identity (the terms in blue, denoted by B_i(x,y) in the statement). The assumption \sum_{i=1}^8x_i^2>0 ensures that the displayed denominators are nonzero.
