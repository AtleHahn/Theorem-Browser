Statement

Let a compact Lie group G act smoothly on a smooth manifold M. For x\in M, let G_x be its stabilizer and let N_x=T_xM/T_x(G\cdot x) be the normal space of the orbit. There is a G-equivariant diffeomorphism from a G-invariant neighborhood of the zero section in the associated bundle G\times_{G_x}N_x onto a neighborhood of the orbit in M, extending [g,0]\mapsto g\cdot x.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves smooth compact Lie group actions; isotropy groups; normal bundles; equivariant tubular neighborhoods. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Invariant metric and tubular construction — outline

Since G is compact, there exists an invariant Riemannian metric; i.e., G acts as isometries. One then adapts the usual proof of the existence of a tubular neighborhood using this metric.
