Statement

Let H be a self-adjoint realization on L^2(\mathbb R) of the harmonic-oscillator expression (Hf)(x)=-\tfrac12 f^{\prime\prime}(x)+\tfrac12x^2f(x), with eigenvalues 1/2,3/2,5/2,\ldots. Then H is unbounded and its domain is a proper subset of L^2(\mathbb R).

Proof 1: Unbounded eigenvalues and the domain obstruction

The harmonic oscillator has eigenvalues 1/2,3/2,5/2,\ldots, so it is unbounded. By the Hellinger–Toeplitz theorem, an everywhere-defined self-adjoint operator is bounded. Consequently this operator cannot be defined on the whole Hilbert space.
