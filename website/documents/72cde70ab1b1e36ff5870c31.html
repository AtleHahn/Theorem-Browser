Statement

A generalized continued fraction is interpreted through its finite convergents (the rational functions obtained by truncation); convergence here means that these convergents tend to a finite complex value. Let c_i\in\mathbb C for i\ge2, and suppose |c_i|\le M for a positive real number M. The convergents of

f(z)=\frac1{1+\dfrac{c_2z}{1+\dfrac{c_3z}{1+\cdots}}}

converge uniformly on |z|<1/(4M), and their limit is analytic there.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Real analysis supplies derivatives, maxima, uniform convergence and completeness; complex arithmetic supplies the plane. Holomorphic means complex differentiable, and entire means so throughout the plane. With that short extension, the displayed bound or convergence assertion uses familiar analytic concepts without contour integrals or specialist function-space theory.

Proof 1: Insert bounded linear partial numerators into Worpitzky’s theorem — outline

Worpitzky's theorem states that the continued fraction

x=\cfrac1{1+\cfrac{a_2}{1+\cfrac{a_3}{1+\cfrac{a_4}{1+\ddots}}}}

converges to a finite value if |a_i|\leq1/4 for i=2,3,4,\ldots, and converges uniformly if the partial numerators a_i are complex variables.

Because the proof of Worpitzky's theorem employs Euler's continued fraction formula to construct an infinite series that is equivalent to the continued fraction x, and the series so constructed is absolutely convergent, the Weierstrass M-test can be applied to a modified version of x. If

f(z)=\cfrac1{1+\cfrac{c_2z}{1+\cfrac{c_3z}{1+\cfrac{c_4z}{1+\ddots}}}}

and a positive real number M exists such that |c_i|\leq M (i=2,3,4,\ldots), then the sequence of convergents \{f_i(z)\} converges uniformly when

|z|<\frac1{4M}

and f(z) is analytic on that open disk.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The substitution |c_i z|<1/4 is elementary, but the supplied argument relies on a specialized uniform continued-fraction theorem and holomorphic-limit theory. Its additional reference to an Euler series does not specify that series or its uniform majorant, so that dependency is not fully identified. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally rather than certify the omitted convergence argument.
