Statement

Let H be an infinite-dimensional separable complex Hilbert space. Its norm unit sphere S(H) is path connected and has \pi_n(S(H),v)=0 for every n≥1 and v∈S(H).

Proof 1: Use the unitary stabilizer fibration (outline)

The unit sphere is a homogeneous space for the unitary group U(H). The stabiliser of a single vector v of the unit sphere is the unitary group of the orthogonal complement of v. Thus use the homogeneous-space fibration

U(v^\perp)\longrightarrow U(H)\longrightarrow S(H).

The general linear group and unitary group have the same homotopy type in the separable Hilbert-space setting. Kuiper's theorem makes both U(H) and U(v^\perp) weakly contractible; therefore the homotopy long exact sequence gives that all the homotopy groups of the unit sphere are trivial, and its path-component part gives a single component. This yields a weak homotopy equivalence from a point, which by itself does not supply a contraction.
