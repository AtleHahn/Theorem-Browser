Statement

Let X be a piecewise-linear homology sphere that is not a topological sphere, with a fixed triangulation. The induced double-suspension triangulation of \Sigma^2X triangulates a topological sphere but is not a PL-manifold triangulation.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves PL homology spheres; Simplicial suspension and vertex links; PL and topological spheres. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Double suspension and the link obstruction — outline

Apply the double suspension operation to the triangulation of X. By the double-suspension theorem, the underlying space \Sigma^2X is a topological sphere. Unlike a PL-manifold triangulation, this triangulation has a suspension-point link that is not a sphere, so the triangulation is not piecewise-linear.
