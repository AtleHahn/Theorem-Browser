Statement

Let Y be a complete Hausdorff topological vector space. Suppose that, for every locally convex space X, every continuous linear map from a closed vector subspace of X into Y extends continuously and linearly to X. Then the same extension property holds for arbitrary vector subspaces of locally convex spaces.

Proof 1: Extend first to the closure

If f:M\to Y is a continuous linear map from a vector subspace M of X into a complete Hausdorff space Y, then there always exists a unique continuous linear extension of f from M to the closure of M in X. Using this closure-extension theorem, it suffices to only consider maps from closed vector subspaces into complete Hausdorff spaces: the assumed property then extends the map on \overline M to X.
