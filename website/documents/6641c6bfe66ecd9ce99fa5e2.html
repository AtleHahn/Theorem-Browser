Statement

Let M be a compact complex manifold without boundary that admits a Kähler metric. For every Kähler class \alpha\in H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(M;\mathbb R) and every smooth closed real (1,1)-form R representing the real first Chern class c_1(M), there is exactly one Kähler metric g whose Kähler form \omega_g has class [\omega_g]=\alpha and whose normalized Ricci form is R.

Here a Kähler metric is a Hermitian metric whose associated real (1,1)-form \omega_g(u,v)=g(Ju,v) is closed, where J is the complex structure. A Kähler class is the de Rham cohomology class of such a form. A real form of type (1,1) is real-valued and has one holomorphic and one antiholomorphic differential in complex coordinates. The normalization of the Ricci form used here is

\rho_g=-\frac{i}{2\pi}\,\partial\bar\partial\log\det(g_{j\bar k}),

so [\rho_g]=c_1(M) in real de Rham cohomology. The first Chern class is that of the holomorphic tangent bundle; \partial and \bar\partial are the holomorphic and antiholomorphic parts of exterior differentiation. The prescribed-form assertion is \rho_g=R, without any positivity requirement on R.
