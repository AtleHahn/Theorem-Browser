Statement

Let K be a field of characteristic different from 2 and 3. Let f(x)=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d\in K[x] be irreducible, with a\ne0, and let \Delta=18abcd-4b^3d+b^2c^2-4ac^3-27a^2d^2. The Galois group of its splitting field over K, acting on its three roots, is A_3 if \Delta is a square in K, and S_3 otherwise.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The theorem concerns field automorphisms and their permutation action over an arbitrary field.

Proof 1: Track the sign of the Vandermonde product

Given a cubic irreducible polynomial over a field K of characteristic different from 2 and 3, the Galois group over K is the group of the field automorphisms that fix K of the smallest extension of K (splitting field). As these automorphisms must permute the roots of the polynomials, this group is either the group S3 of all six permutations of the three roots, or the group A3 of the three circular permutations.

The discriminant Δ of the cubic is the square of

{\displaystyle {\sqrt {\Delta }}=a^{2}(r_{1}-r_{2})(r_{1}-r_{3})(r_{2}-r_{3}),}

where a is the leading coefficient of the cubic, and r1, r2 and r3 are the three roots of the cubic. As {\displaystyle {\sqrt {\Delta }}} changes sign if two roots are exchanged, {\displaystyle {\sqrt {\Delta }}} is fixed by the Galois group only if the Galois group is A3. In other words, the Galois group is A3 if and only if the discriminant is the square of an element of K. Here the action is transitive because the polynomial is irreducible and separable, and the transitive subgroups of S_3 are A_3 and S_3. The fixed-field criterion identifies elements fixed by every automorphism with K.

Level justification

Use separability and transitivity of the splitting-field Galois action, then identify the fixed Vandermonde product with a base-field element. Splitting fields, full-group fixed fields and Galois actions are a cumulative prerequisite burden beyond introductory algebra.
