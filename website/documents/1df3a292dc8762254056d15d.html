Statement

Let R be a commutative Noetherian ring and n\ge1. The natural morphism \mathbb P_R^{n-1}=\operatorname{Proj}(R[x_1,\ldots,x_n])\to\operatorname{Spec}R is a closed map for the Zariski topology.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proj, Spec and their structural morphism over a Noetherian ring require substantial scheme-theoretic foundations. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Interpret homogeneous elimination through projective fibers

Use the homogeneous elimination theorem: for every homogeneous ideal I\subseteq R[x_1,\ldots,x_n], there is an ideal \mathfrak r\subseteq R such that its specialized homogeneous equations have a common nonzero zero over an algebraic closure of a field K exactly when a coefficient homomorphism \varphi:R\to K kills \mathfrak r. For a prime \mathfrak p\subseteq R, apply this to the residue field \kappa(\mathfrak p). The projective fiber of the closed subset V_+(I) is nonempty exactly when \mathfrak r\subseteq\mathfrak p. Thus its image is V(\mathfrak r), a closed subset of \operatorname{Spec}R. All closed subsets of the projective space have this form, proving closedness.
