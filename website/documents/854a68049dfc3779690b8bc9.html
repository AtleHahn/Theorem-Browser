Statement

Let G be a finite group, and let H=[G,G] be its derived subgroup, generated by the commutators aba^{-1}b^{-1} with a,b\in G. Let H'=[H,H]. Choose representatives x_1,\ldots,x_r for the left cosets of H in G.

For g\in G, multiplication by g permutes those cosets. Define the permutation \sigma_g and elements h_i(g)\in H by

gx_i=x_{\sigma_g(i)}h_i(g).

The transfer to H is the homomorphism

V:G\longrightarrow H/H',\qquad V(g)=\left(\prod_{i=1}^r h_i(g)\right)H'.

Its value is independent of the chosen coset representatives and of the order of factors in the abelian quotient H/H'.

This transfer is trivial: V(g)=H' for every g\in G. Equivalently, \prod_{i=1}^r h_i(g)\in H' for every g\in G.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies cosets, normal subgroups and quotients. The two successive commutator subgroups and the explicit coset-product transfer definition are a small extension that can be understood within the short-reading allowance; no class field theory is needed to understand this statement.
