Statement

All function spaces below are complex-valued and use Lebesgue measure on \mathbb R. For p=1,2, L^p consists of measurable functions modulo equality almost everywhere with \|f\|_p=(\int_{\mathbb R}|f(x)|^p\,dx)^{1/p}<\infty. A span means finite complex linear combinations.

For f\in L^2(\mathbb R) let \widehat f be its L^2 Fourier transform, defined by extending f\mapsto\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-ix\xi}\,dx continuously from L^1\cap L^2 to L^2. Put f_a(x)=f(x+a). Then

\overline{\operatorname{span}\{f_a:a\in\mathbb R\}}^{\|\cdot\|_2}=L^2(\mathbb R) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \widehat f(\xi)\ne0\text{ for almost every real }\xi.

Equivalently, the zero set of any measurable representative of \widehat f has Lebesgue measure zero.
