Statement

Over a real closed field, the coordinate projection of a semialgebraic set is semialgebraic. From a quantifier-free defining formula one can effectively obtain such a formula for the projection.

Proof 1: Express projection by existential quantification

If P(x,y) defines the original set, where x denotes the eliminated coordinates and y those retained, then the projection is defined by (\exists x)P(x,y). Apply effective quantifier elimination to obtain an equivalent quantifier-free formula in y.
