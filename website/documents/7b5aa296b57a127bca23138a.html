Statement

Two compact connected Riemann surfaces are homeomorphic if and only if their genera agree. This classifies their underlying topological surfaces.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Riemann surfaces, genus and underlying topological equivalence are complex-manifold and surface-topology objects.

Proof 1: Apply oriented-surface classification to the underlying surface

A complex one-manifold is a smooth oriented surface, also called a Riemann surface. By the classification of closed orientable surfaces, compact connected Riemann surfaces are characterized topologically by their genus: 0,1,2,\ldots.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The surface-classification application is simple once the underlying oriented surface is identified, but the full Riemann-surface and canonical-orientation setting is outside the baseline. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally rather than assuming complex manifold concepts are quickly learned.
