Statement

For A\subseteq\mathbb N_{>0} put A(n)=|A\cap\{1,\ldots,n\}| and \sigma(A)=\inf_{n\geq1}A(n)/n. For subsets of positive integers, write A\oplus B for the positive elements of (A\cup\{0\})+(B\cup\{0\}); repeated sums likewise allow zero summands. For l\geq1 and A_1,\ldots,A_l\subseteq\mathbb N_{>0}, \sigma\!\left(\bigoplus_{i=1}^l A_i\right)\geq1-\prod_{i=1}^l(1-\sigma(A_i)).

Level justification

Proof 1: Iterate the complementary-density product — outline

The two-set theorem gives \sigma(A\oplus B)\geq\sigma(A)+\sigma(B)-\sigma(A)\sigma(B)=1-(1-\sigma(A))(1-\sigma(B)). Iterating this identity by induction gives the displayed finite-product bound.
