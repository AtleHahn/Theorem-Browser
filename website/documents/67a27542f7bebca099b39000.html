Simpler proof assessment

Likely alternative prerequisite level: Undergraduate level; lowest recorded proof level: Beyond undergraduate level. Prove the minor-arc spherical triangle formula by counting the areas of lunes, whose area is proportional to their angle. Triangulate the convex polygon into N-2 triangles and add the triangle formulas; the triangle angles sum to the polygon's interior angles. This gives exactly the area excess for every N>=3. Finite spherical geometry and area additivity replace Gram-Euler; the statement's existing undergraduate category is respected.

Statement

For a convex geodesic polygon on the unit sphere with N\ge3 sides, interior angles \alpha_v and area \Omega, \Omega=\sum_v\alpha_v-\pi(N-2).

Proof 1: Convert normalized spherical angles and area — outline

The source specializes the spherical Gram–Euler relation to a polygon. The vertex, edge and interior terms are its angle sum, N half-circle angles and one full-circle angle.
