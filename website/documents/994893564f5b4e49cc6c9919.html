Statement

Let N be a complex n\times n matrix with row vectors r_1,\ldots,r_n. Then |\det N|\le\prod_i\|r_i\|. If all rows are nonzero, equality holds exactly when the rows are pairwise orthogonal.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Complex matrices and determinants; Row norms and orthogonality; Matrix transpose. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Hadamard determinant inequality for rows — source argument

Apply the column form of Hadamard’s inequality to N^{\mathsf T}. Its columns are the rows of N with the same Euclidean norms, and \det(N^{\mathsf T})=\det N. The column equality criterion becomes the stated row criterion.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the column inequality to the transpose and transfer its norms, determinant and orthogonality condition. Undergraduate complex linear algebra suffices.
