Statement

Let G be finite and f:G\to\mathbb C a class function. For each irreducible character \chi_\rho of dimension d_\rho, define \widehat f(\rho)=(|G|d_\rho)^{-1}\sum_{g\in G}f(g)\overline{\chi_\rho(g)}. Then \frac1{|G|}\sum_g|f(g)|^2=\sum_\rho d_\rho^2|\widehat f(\rho)|^2.

Proof 1: Parseval identity for class functions on a finite group (outline)

Use the standard character-theoretic fact that irreducible characters form an orthonormal basis of the class functions for \langle f,h\rangle=|G|^{-1}\sum_g f(g)\overline{h(g)}. The coefficient of \chi_\rho is d_\rho\widehat f(\rho). Parseval’s identity in that finite-dimensional space therefore gives the asserted sum of squared coefficients.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Parseval itself is undergraduate, but the actual basis consists of irreducible finite-group characters with representation dimensions. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because representations, irreducibility and character completeness together are not confidently a small extension.
