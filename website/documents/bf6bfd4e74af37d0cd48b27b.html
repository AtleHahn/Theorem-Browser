Statement

Let G be a topological group with identity e, and let \pi be a continuous unitary representation of G on a Hilbert space H. Fix x\in G and a net (y_\alpha) in G such that y_\alpha x y_\alpha^{-1}\to e. If a vector v\in H satisfies \pi(y_\alpha)v=v for every \alpha, then \pi(x)v=v. A unitary representation is a group homomorphism into the surjective linear isometries of H preserving the inner product; continuity means that g\mapsto\pi(g)w is norm-continuous for every w\in H. A net is a family indexed by a directed set, and convergence means eventual membership in every neighborhood of the limit.
