Proof 1: Interpret quantified variables as projected coordinates — outline

Existential elimination is precisely projection: (\exists x)P(x,y) describes the projection of the set defined by P. Apply this to eliminate existential quantifiers; universal quantifiers are treated by negating an existential statement, and quantifiers are removed successively. Boolean combinations preserve semialgebraicity.

Level justification

Successively translate quantifiers into projections and complements over real closed fields. The arbitrary-field formal-language and effective-elimination framework adds multiple prerequisites beyond ordinary undergraduate algebra; retain beyond undergraduate conservatively.
