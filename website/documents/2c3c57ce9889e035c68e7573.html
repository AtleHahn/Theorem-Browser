Statement

Let three pairwise externally tangent circles have positive radii r_1,r_2,r_3 and centers P_1,P_2,P_3. The area of their center triangle is \sqrt{r_1r_2r_3(r_1+r_2+r_3)}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Only radii, external tangency and an explicit real area expression occur.

Proof 1: Heron’s formula for the center triangle

If three circles are externally tangent, with radii {\displaystyle r_{1},r_{2},r_{3},} then their centers {\displaystyle P_{1},P_{2},P_{3}} form the vertices of a triangle with side lengths {\displaystyle r_{1}+r_{2},} {\displaystyle r_{1}+r_{3},} and {\displaystyle r_{2}+r_{3},} and semiperimeter {\displaystyle r_{1}+r_{2}+r_{3}.} By Heron's formula, this triangle {\displaystyle \triangle P_{1}P_{2}P_{3}} has area

{\displaystyle {\sqrt {r_{1}r_{2}r_{3}(r_{1}+r_{2}+r_{3})}}.}
Level
High school only
Level justification

Use sums of radii for the three side lengths and substitute into Heron. School circle geometry and algebra suffice.
