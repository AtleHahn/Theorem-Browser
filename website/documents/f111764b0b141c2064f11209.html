Statement

Let \Omega be a bounded Jordan domain, p\in\Omega, and \Omega_n bounded Jordan domains containing \Omega, with \overline{\Omega_{n+1}}\subset\Omega_n and Carathéodory kernel \Omega at p. Let f_n:\Omega_n\to\Omega be conformal bijections satisfying f_n(p)=p and f_n^{\prime}(p)>0. For an open set U\subset\mathbb C, write A^2(U) for its holomorphic functions with finite norm \|g\|_U^2=\int_U|g(z)|^2\,dA(z), where dA is planar area measure. Given g\in A^2(\Omega), put g_n=(g\circ f_n)f_n^{\prime} on \Omega_n. Then g_n\in A^2(\Omega_n) and \|g_n\|_{\Omega_n}=\|g\|_\Omega for every n.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The locally defined holomorphic area-integrable function space and kernel-domain setup are complex/measure-theoretic analysis.

Proof 1: Bergman norm of the conformal pullback — outline

Let g be a square integrable holomorphic function on Ω, i.e. an element of the Bergman space A2(Ω). Define gn on Ωn by gn(z) = g(fn(z))fn'(z). By change of variable

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\|g_{n}\|_{\Omega _{n}}^{2}=\|g\|_{\Omega }^{2}.}}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The actual equality is a conformal change-of-variables calculation, but the full supplied setting includes Bergman spaces, normalized conformal bijections on nested Jordan domains and a Carathéodory kernel condition. Understanding those complex-analytic objects together is beyond a confident short undergraduate extension. The kernel and normalization assumptions are not needed for this particular norm identity; that observation is recorded without replacing the stated framework by a simpler one.

For a conformal bijection f:U->V and a nonnegative integrable function h on V, integral_U h(f(z)) |f prime(z)|^2 dA(z)=integral_V h(w)dA(w).

Application in proof:

Take h=|g|^2 to prove the norm identity and integrability of g_n.

Application level:

Undergraduate level

Level justification:

The application is the two-dimensional change-of-variables formula with the complex derivative’s real Jacobian |f prime|^2. For this continuous nonnegative integrand it can be understood through ordinary calculus and exhaustion by compact regions.

Review notes:

Composition and multiplication preserve holomorphicity, giving the other part of membership in A2. Neither the kernel-convergence assumption nor the chosen normalization of f_n enters the integral equality.
