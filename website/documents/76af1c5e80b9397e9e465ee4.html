Statement

Let M be a finitely generated module over a commutative ring A. Suppose M/IM is Noetherian for every nonzero ideal I\subseteq A, and M/N is nonfaithful for every nonzero submodule N\subseteq M. Then M is Noetherian.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The criterion concerns arbitrary module quotients, annihilator actions and Noetherianness.

Proof 1: Trap each submodule above a finitely generated scalar image

Let a submodule 0\ne N\subseteq M be given. Since M/N is not faithful, there is a nonzero element a\in A such that aM\subseteq N. By assumption, M/aM is Noetherian and so N/aM is finitely generated. Since aM is also finitely generated, it follows that N is finitely generated; i.e., M is Noetherian.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use a quotient annihilator to place each submodule above a scalar image, then combine finite generators through a quotient. Faithfulness, general module quotients and Noetherianity together form substantial commutative-algebra prerequisites.
