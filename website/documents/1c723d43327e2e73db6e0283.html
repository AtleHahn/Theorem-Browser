Statement

For every real p>1 there exists C_p>0 such that for every integer n\ge1 and every real-valued f\in L^p(\mathbb R^n),

\|Mf\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}\le C_p\|f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}.

Here, with \lambda^n denoting Lebesgue measure and B_r(x)=\{y:|y-x|<r\} the Euclidean ball,

Mf(x)=\sup_{r>0}\frac{1}{\lambda^n(B_r(x))}\int_{B_r(x)}|f(y)|\,d\lambda^n(y),\qquad\|f\|_p=\left(\int_{\mathbb R^n}|f|^p\,d\lambda^n\right)^{1/p}.

The constant is independent of both dimension n and function f.
