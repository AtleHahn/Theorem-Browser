Statement

Let G be a finite group, p a prime and P a Sylow p-subgroup of G. For g\in G write P^g=g^{-1}Pg. Let \mathcal C consist of the subgroups Q=P\cap P^g for which both N_P(Q) and N_{P^g}(Q) are Sylow p-subgroups of N_G(Q). Then

P\cap [G,G]=\left\langle [Q,N_G(Q)]:Q\in\mathcal C\right\rangle.

Here N_H(Q)=\{h\in H:h^{-1}Qh=Q\} is the normalizer in H, [A,B] is the subgroup generated by all commutators a^{-1}b^{-1}ab with a\in A, b\in B, and the outer angle brackets denote the subgroup generated by the displayed subgroups. A Sylow p-subgroup of a finite group has order equal to the largest power of p dividing the group order. The subgroup P\cap[G,G] is the focal subgroup in this setting.
