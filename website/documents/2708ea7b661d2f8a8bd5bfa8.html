Statement

Let n\geq1 be an integer, let U\subseteq\mathbb R^n be open, and let (f_k)_{k\geq0} be any sequence of smooth real-valued functions on U. For every open interval I\subseteq\mathbb R containing 0, there is a smooth real-valued function F on I\times U such that

\frac{\partial^k F}{\partial t^k}(0,x)=f_k(x)\qquad(k=0,1,2,\ldots,\ x\in U).

Here smooth means that partial derivatives of every order exist and are continuous; the derivative of order zero is F itself.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Multivariable calculus and introductory analysis suffice to understand the prescribed derivatives and quantifiers; no knowledge of jets, distributions or PDE theory is required.

Proof availability

The source supplies a cutoff-series construction for data with common compact support. Its reduction to that case invokes a fixed-grid covering of all of Euclidean space without supplying a localization compatible with an arbitrary open U. Consequently it does not supply a complete proof in the stated generality. The compact-support construction remains in the existing entry; no replacement covering or gluing argument has been invented.
