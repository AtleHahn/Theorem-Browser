Statement

Let B=(B_t)_{0\le t\le1} be standard d-dimensional Brownian motion with B_0=0, for a positive integer d, and let (\mathcal F_t^B) be its natural filtration completed and made right-continuous. For every bounded Borel measurable function f:C([0,1],\mathbb R^d)\to\mathbb R, where the path space has its uniform-norm topology,

-\log\mathbb E[e^{-f(B)}]=\inf_V\mathbb E\left[\frac12\int_0^1\|V_t\|^2\,dt+f\left(B+\int_0^{\cdot}V_t\,dt\right)\right].

The infimum is over \mathbb R^d-valued processes V progressively measurable for (\mathcal F_t^B) with \mathbb E\int_0^1\|V_t\|^2dt<\infty. Progressive measurability means the restriction to [0,t]\times\Omega is measurable for \mathcal B([0,t])\otimes\mathcal F_t^B for every t. The norm is Euclidean and all expectations use the probability law underlying B.
