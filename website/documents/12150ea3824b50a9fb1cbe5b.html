Statement

Let X be a compact complex manifold of complex dimension n, and let E be an ample holomorphic vector bundle of rank r\ge1. Denote by \Omega_X^p the sheaf of holomorphic p-forms. Let E^* denote the dual bundle. For integers 0\le i,j\le n with i+j\le n-r, H^i(X,\Omega_X^j\otimes E^*)=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Compact complex manifolds; Ample and dual holomorphic bundles; Sheaf cohomology of holomorphic forms. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use Serre duality and complementary indices

Serre duality and the perfect wedge pairing of holomorphic forms give

H^i(X,\Omega_X^j\otimes E^*)^*\simeq H^{n-i}(X,\Omega_X^{n-j}\otimes E).

Indeed (\Omega_X^j)^*\otimes K_X\simeq\Omega_X^{n-j}, where K_X=\Omega_X^n. The index condition i+j\le n-r is equivalent to (n-i)+(n-j)\ge n+r. Le Potier's sheaf-cohomology vanishing therefore makes the right-hand group zero, so the left-hand group also vanishes.
