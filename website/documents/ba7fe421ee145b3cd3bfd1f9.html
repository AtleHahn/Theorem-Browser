Statement

Let triangle ABC have its right angle at C, and let D be the foot of the perpendicular from C to hypotenuse AB. Then CD=\frac{AC\,BC}{AB}.

Proof 1: Hypotenuse altitude as the product of the legs divided by the hypotenuse — source argument

Computing the same triangle area using the two perpendicular legs and using the hypotenuse as base gives

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}{\tfrac {1}{2}}AC\cdot BC&={\tfrac {1}{2}}AB\cdot CD\\[4pt]CD&={\frac {AC\cdot BC}{AB}}\\[4pt]\end{aligned}}}
