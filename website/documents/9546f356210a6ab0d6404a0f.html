Statement

For a smooth bounded domain \Omega, let \lambda_1>0 be the first eigenvalue of the negative Dirichlet Laplacian. Then \|u\|_2^2\le\lambda_1^{-1}\|\nabla u\|_2^2 for all u\in W_0^{1,2}(\Omega), and the coefficient \lambda_1^{-1} is optimal.

Proof 1: Rearrange the minimal Rayleigh quotient

For a smooth, bounded domain Ω, since the Rayleigh quotient for the Laplace operator in the space {\displaystyle W_{0}^{1,2}(\Omega )} is minimized by the eigenfunction corresponding to the minimal eigenvalue \lambda_1 of the (negative) Laplacian, it is a simple consequence that, for any {\displaystyle u\in W_{0}^{1,2}(\Omega )},

{\displaystyle \|u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}\leq \lambda _{1}^{-1}\left\|\nabla u\right\|_{L^{2}}^{2}}

and furthermore, that the coefficient \lambda_1^{-1} is optimal.

Equality holds for a first eigenfunction.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The Rayleigh quotient is taken over a Sobolev space for the Dirichlet Laplacian, and equality uses a PDE eigenfunction. These functional-analytic and weak-boundary-value objects remain advanced despite the scalar rearrangement.
