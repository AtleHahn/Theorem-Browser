Statement

For every positive integer n, \log(n+1)<\sum_{i=1}^n1/i, where \log is the natural logarithm.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Understanding this finite inequality uses finite sums and ordinary real logarithmic functions; no limit or integral is present in the statement. Its proof is assessed separately.
