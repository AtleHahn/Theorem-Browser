Statement

Let K be a field and let A=K\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle be the free associative algebra on n\geq1 noncommuting variables: its elements are finite K-linear combinations of words in these variables, with multiplication by concatenation. Grade A by word length.

Let J be the two-sided ideal generated by homogeneous elements f_1,f_2,\ldots, whose degrees satisfy 2\leq d_1\leq d_2\leq\cdots and d_j\to\infty. Homogeneous means that every word occurring in f_j has length d_j. Set

B=A/J,\qquad b_j=\dim_K B_j,\qquad r_i=\#\{\ell:d_\ell=i\},

where B_j is the degree-j component of the quotient grading; in particular b_0=1.

Then, for every integer j\geq1,

b_j\geq n b_{j-1}-\sum_{i=2}^j b_{j-i}r_i,

with an empty sum interpreted as zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The cumulative passage from noncommutative free algebras to homogeneous ideals and graded quotients is not confidently a short extension of introductory abstract algebra; conservatively beyond undergraduate.
