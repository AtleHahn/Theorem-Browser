Statement

Let r,s\geq1 be integers and let x_1,\ldots,x_N be distinct real numbers with N\geq(r-1)(s-1)+1. Among the points (1,x_1),\ldots,(N,x_N) there is a polygonal path, with vertices in increasing first-coordinate order, having either r-1 positive-slope edges or s-1 negative-slope edges.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All points and path slopes are explicitly elementary plane geometry.

Proof 1: Encode indices and values as coordinates

Interpret the positions in the sequence as first coordinates and its values as second coordinates. Under this translation, an increasing subsequence gives a path of positive-slope edges and a decreasing subsequence gives a path of negative-slope edges. The Erdős–Szekeres theorem therefore gives the stated path.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Translate subsequence inequalities into signs of coordinate slopes and apply the stated length threshold. School sequences, coordinates and finite counting suffice.
