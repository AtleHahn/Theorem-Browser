Statement

Let A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n} and u,v\in\mathbb C^n, with n\ge1. Then

\det(A+uv^{\mathsf T})=\det A+v^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{adj}(A)u,

where \operatorname{adj}(A) is the transpose of the cofactor matrix and v^{\mathsf T} denotes transpose, without complex conjugation. The formula holds even when A is singular. When A is invertible, it takes the form

\det(A+uv^{\mathsf T})=(1+v^{\mathsf T}A^{-1}u)\det A.

All objects and both identities belong to standard undergraduate linear algebra.
