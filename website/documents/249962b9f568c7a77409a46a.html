Statement

Let (X,x) and (Y,y) be path-connected pointed CW complexes, with basepoints chosen as vertices. The wedge (X\vee Y,p) is the quotient of X\coprod Y identifying x with y. Then

\pi_1(X\vee Y,p)\cong\pi_1(X,x)*\pi_1(Y,y).
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses CW topology, wedge quotients and free products of fundamental groups.

Proof 1: CW neighborhood cover — outline

Use the Seifert–Van Kampen theorem: for an open path-connected cover with nonempty path-connected intersection, the fundamental group is the pushout of the fundamental groups of the two sets over that of their intersection. For the vertex basepoints in the statement, use the standard CW neighborhoods that contract to their basepoints, so that X\vee V and U\vee Y retract to X and Y, while U\vee V is contractible.

If {\displaystyle x} admits a contractible open neighborhood {\displaystyle U\subset X} and {\displaystyle y} admits a contractible open neighborhood {\displaystyle V\subset Y} (which is the case if, for instance, {\displaystyle X} and {\displaystyle Y} are CW complexes), then we can apply the Van Kampen theorem to {\displaystyle X\vee Y} by taking {\displaystyle X\vee V} and {\displaystyle U\vee Y} as the two open sets and we conclude that the fundamental group of the wedge is the free product of the fundamental groups of the two spaces we started with:

{\displaystyle \pi _{1}(X\vee Y,p)\cong \pi _{1}(X,x)*\pi _{1}(Y,y)}.
