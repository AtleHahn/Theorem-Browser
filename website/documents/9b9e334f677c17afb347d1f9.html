Statement

Let G be a connected algebraic group acting transitively on a nonempty variety X over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Then X is smooth.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Algebraic-group actions and smoothness of arbitrary varieties require substantial algebraic geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Translate a point of the smooth locus

The smooth locus of X is nonempty in characteristic zero. Transitivity carries a smooth point to any point of X, and the action of each group element is an isomorphism, preserving smoothness. Thus X is smooth.
