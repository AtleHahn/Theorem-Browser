Statement

If a Riemannian manifold M has a nonconstant geodesic segment with both endpoints equal to x, then \{x\} cannot be a soul of M. Total convexity means that every geodesic segment whose endpoints lie in the subset lies entirely in the subset.

Proof 1: Violate total convexity

The given geodesic segment has endpoints in \{x\} but is not contained in \{x\}, so that singleton is not totally convex. A soul must be totally convex, hence \{x\} is not a soul.
