Statement

For every real N there is C_N>0 such that, uniformly for integers q\ge1,a with \gcd(a,q)=1 and q\le(\log x)^N as x\to\infty,

\pi(x;q,a)=\frac{\operatorname{Li}(x)}{\varphi(q)}+O_N\!\left(xe^{-(C_N/2)\sqrt{\log x}}\right).

Here \pi(x;q,a) counts primes p\le x with p\equiv a\pmod q, \varphi(q)=\#\{1\le r\le q:\gcd(r,q)=1\}, and \operatorname{Li}(x)=\int_2^xdt/\log t. All logarithms are natural. The implied bound and starting value of x depend only on N, not on a or q.
