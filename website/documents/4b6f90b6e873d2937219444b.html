Statement

Let X be a Polish space, meaning a topological space whose topology is induced by a complete separable metric. Let A,B\subseteq X be disjoint analytic sets. Then there exists a Borel set C\subseteq X such that

A\subseteq C,\qquad B\cap C=\varnothing.

Here a subset is analytic if it is empty or is the image of a Polish space under a continuous map into X. Borel sets are the members of the smallest sigma-algebra containing the open sets, where a sigma-algebra is closed under complements and countable unions.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although the individual definitions use topology and countable operations, understanding arbitrary continuous images and their distinction from Borel sets introduces descriptive-set-theoretic complexity; the cumulative burden is not confidently within the short undergraduate allowance.
