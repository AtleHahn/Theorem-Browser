Statement

Let D=\{(x,y):c\le y\le d,\ h_1(y)\le x\le h_2(y)\} have positively oriented piecewise smooth simple boundary C, with continuous piecewise smooth h_1,h_2 and h_1<h_2 in the interior. For real C^1 M on a neighborhood of D, \oint_CM\,dy=\iint_D M_x\,dA.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Real partial derivatives; Line and double integrals; Oriented planar boundaries. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Use the source symmetric horizontal integration (outline)

Integrating M_x horizontally gives \iint_D M_x\,dA=\int_c^d[M(h_2(y),y)-M(h_1(y),y)]\,dy. On the right boundary y increases and on the left boundary it decreases; horizontal connecting sides contribute zero to M\,dy. Their sum is the same expression.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Integrate M_x horizontally and follow the increasing and decreasing y orientations on the two graph boundaries. Undergraduate multivariable calculus suffices.
