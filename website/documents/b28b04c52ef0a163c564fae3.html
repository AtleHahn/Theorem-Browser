Statement

Let A,B\in\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb C), the group of complex 2\times2 matrices of determinant one. Suppose the subgroup G they generate is discrete in the matrix-entry topology and is non-elementary. Here non-elementary means its limit set is infinite: let the image of G in \operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb C) (the quotient by \{I,-I\}) act by orientation-preserving isometries of hyperbolic three-space, identified with the open unit ball with metric ds^2=4|dx|^2/(1-|x|^2)^2; the limit set is the set of accumulation points on the boundary sphere of the orbit of an interior point. Then

|\operatorname{Tr}(A)^2-4|+|\operatorname{Tr}(ABA^{-1}B^{-1})-2|\ge1,

where \operatorname{Tr} is the sum of diagonal entries.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The trace formula is elementary, but the full non-elementary hypothesis requires hyperbolic actions and boundary limit sets beyond the conservative reading allowance.
