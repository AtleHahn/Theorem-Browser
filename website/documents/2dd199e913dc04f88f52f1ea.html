Statement

For a complex Hermitian matrix M=M^*, let \operatorname{In}(M)=(\pi(M),\nu(M),\delta(M)) count its positive, negative and zero eigenvalues with multiplicity; * denotes conjugate transpose. Let H=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\B^*&D\end{pmatrix} be Hermitian, where A is an invertible square block. Put S=D-B^*A^{-1}B. Then \operatorname{In}(H)=\operatorname{In}(A)+\operatorname{In}(S), where the triples are added coordinatewise.
