Statement

Let G be a compact Hausdorff topological group: multiplication G\times G\to G and inversion G\to G are continuous. There exists a countably additive probability measure \mu on the Borel sets of G, the sigma-algebra generated by its open sets, such that

\mu(gE)=\mu(E)\qquad(g\in G,\ E\subseteq G\text{ Borel}),

where gE=\{gx:x\in E\}.

The measure can be chosen regular: for every Borel set E,

\mu(E)=\inf_{E\subseteq U,\ U\text{ open}}\mu(U) =\sup_{K\subseteq E,\ K\text{ compact}}\mu(K).

It is positive on every nonempty open subset of G. Such a normalized left-invariant measure is a Haar probability measure.

Level justification
