Statement

Let V be a uniformly convex Banach space: for each \varepsilon\in(0,2] there is \delta>0 such that \|u\|,\|v\|\le1 and \|u-v\|\ge\varepsilon imply \|(u+v)/2\|\le1-\delta. Let K\subseteq V be nonempty, closed, bounded and convex. If f:K\to K satisfies \|f(x)-f(y)\|\le\|x-y\| for all x,y\in K, then f has a fixed point.

Normed-space completeness and convexity are undergraduate concepts; the explicit uniform midpoint condition is a small quantifier-based extension that can be understood within the reading allowance.
