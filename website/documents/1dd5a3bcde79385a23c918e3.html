Statement

On an open set in \mathbb R^3, for C^2 scalars \psi,\varphi and C^1 scalar \varepsilon, \nabla\cdot(\psi\varepsilon\nabla\varphi-\varphi\varepsilon\nabla\psi)=\psi\nabla\cdot(\varepsilon\nabla\varphi)-\varphi\nabla\cdot(\varepsilon\nabla\psi).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Partial derivatives; Gradient and divergence. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Expand and cancel the gradient products

Put \mathbf F=\psi\varepsilon\nabla\varphi-\varphi\varepsilon\nabla\psi. Then

{\displaystyle {\begin{array}{lcl}\nabla \cdot \mathbf {F} &=&\nabla \cdot \left(\psi \varepsilon \nabla \varphi -\varphi \varepsilon \nabla \psi \right)\\&=&\nabla \left(\psi \varepsilon \right)\cdot \nabla \varphi +\psi \varepsilon \nabla ^{2}\varphi -\nabla \left(\varphi \varepsilon \right)\cdot \nabla \psi -\varphi \varepsilon \nabla ^{2}\psi \\&=&\left(\varepsilon \nabla \psi +\psi \nabla \varepsilon \right)\cdot \nabla \varphi +\psi \varepsilon \nabla ^{2}\varphi -\left(\varepsilon \nabla \varphi +\varphi \nabla \varepsilon \right)\cdot \nabla \psi -\varphi \varepsilon \nabla ^{2}\psi \\&=&\psi \nabla \varepsilon \cdot \nabla \varphi +\psi \varepsilon \nabla ^{2}\varphi -\varphi \nabla \varepsilon \cdot \nabla \psi -\varphi \varepsilon \nabla ^{2}\psi \\&=&\psi \nabla \cdot \left(\varepsilon \nabla \varphi \right)-\varphi \nabla \cdot \left(\varepsilon \nabla \psi \right)\end{array}}}

is obtained with help from vector calculus identities.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Expand the divergence product rules and cancel the symmetric gradient products. Standard multivariable calculus suffices.
