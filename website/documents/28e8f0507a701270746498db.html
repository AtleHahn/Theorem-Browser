Statement

Let f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R be periodic with period P>0, and put \omega_f(\delta)=\sup\{|f(x)-f(y)|:x,y\in\mathbb R,\ |x-y|\le\delta\}. If \omega_f(\delta)\log\delta\to0 as \delta\downarrow0, then the Fourier series of f converges uniformly to f on \mathbb R. Explicitly, with \widehat f(n)=P^{-1}\int_0^P f(x)e^{-2\pi i nx/P}\,dx, its partial sums S_N(x)=\sum_{n=-N}^N\widehat f(n)e^{2\pi i nx/P} satisfy \sup_{x\in\mathbb R}|S_N(x)-f(x)|\to0.
