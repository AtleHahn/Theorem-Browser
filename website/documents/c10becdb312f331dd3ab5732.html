Statement

Every C^3 globally hyperbolic spacetime admits an isometric embedding into Minkowski space \mathbb L^N for some finite N. Here globally hyperbolic means causal with compact causal diamonds J^+(p)\cap J^-(q) for all p,q, where J^\pm denote causal future and past.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Global hyperbolicity; Lorentzian isometric embeddings; Minkowski space. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compose steep-function existence with the embedding theorem

Use the two source-stated results: every globally hyperbolic spacetime admits a steep temporal function, and a C^3 spacetime admitting a steep temporal function is isometrically embeddable in some \mathbb L^N. Applying the latter to the former gives the claimed embedding.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compose steep-temporal-function existence with the isometric embedding theorem. Their application still requires Lorentzian manifolds, causal diamonds and steep metric gradients; these are graduate geometric concepts.
