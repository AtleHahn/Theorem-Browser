Statement

Let G be a finite group and t\in G an element of prime order p. If the commutator [t,g]=t^{-1}g^{-1}tg has order relatively prime to p for every g\in G, then the image of t is central in G/O_{p'}(G), where O_{p'}(G) is the largest normal subgroup whose order is relatively prime to p. Central means commuting with every element of the quotient group.
