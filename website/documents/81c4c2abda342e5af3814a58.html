There exist quadratic nonresidues modulo a composite integer whose product is also a nonresidue.

Proof 1: Multiply two explicit nonsquares modulo fifteen — outline

Modulo 15, take 7 and 8. Both are quadratic nonresidues, and their product is 7\cdot8\equiv11\pmod{15}, also a nonresidue.
