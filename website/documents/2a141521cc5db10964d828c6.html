A presentation for the universal group of a semigroup

Statement

Let S be a semigroup, a set with an associative operation xy; a monoid is a semigroup with a two-sided identity. A homomorphism satisfies f(xy)=f(x)f(y). Let G(S) be the group presented with one generator [s] for each s\in S and relations [x][y]=[xy] for all x,y\in S. Then j(s)=[s] is a semigroup homomorphism. For every semigroup homomorphism k:S\to H to a group there is a unique group homomorphism F:G(S)\to H with F\circ j=k.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Semigroups are explicitly defined; group presentations add generators, relations and their extension property to undergraduate group algebra.

Proof 1: Use the multiplication equations as relations

Use the presentation with the elements of S as generators and all its multiplication equations as relations. Send each element of S to its corresponding generator. The relations make this a semigroup homomorphism. The universal property of a group presentation gives exactly the asserted factorization for any k, since k preserves those multiplication relations.

Level justification

Verify the multiplication relations and invoke the stated universal property of a group presentation. Undergraduate group theory suffices after learning generators-and-relations factorization; no categorical machinery or structural semigroup theorem is required.
