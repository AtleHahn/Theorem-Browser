Statement

Let g be C^3 on an open subset of \mathbb R^d and f be C^3 on an open real interval containing its range. For coordinate indices i,j,k, allowing repetition, write g_i=\partial_i g, etc. Then \partial_i\partial_j\partial_k f(g)=f'(g)g_{ijk}+f''(g)(g_i g_{jk}+g_j g_{ik}+g_k g_{ij})+f'''(g)g_i g_j g_k.

Proof 1: Enumerate partitions of three derivative positions

Use the multivariate partition formula

D_{i_1}\cdots D_{i_n}f(g) =\sum_{\pi\in\Pi}f^{(|\pi|)}(g)\prod_{B\in\pi}D_Bg,

where \Pi is the set of all partitions of \{1,\ldots,n\}, B runs through the blocks of \pi, and D_B=\prod_{j\in B}\partial_{i_j}. The number |\pi| is the number of blocks in the partition.

The five terms in the following expression correspond to the five partitions of the set \{1,2,3\}, and in each case the order of the derivative of f is the number of parts in the partition:

\begin{aligned} \partial_i\partial_j\partial_k f(g) ={}&f'(g)g_{ijk}\\ &+f''(g)(g_i g_{jk}+g_j g_{ik}+g_k g_{ij})\\ &+f'''(g)g_i g_j g_k. \end{aligned}
