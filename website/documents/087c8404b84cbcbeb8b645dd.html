Statement

Let U\subseteq\mathbb C be simply connected and open, let a_1,\ldots,a_n be distinct points of U, and let f be holomorphic on U\setminus\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}. If \gamma is a positively oriented rectifiable simple closed curve in that punctured domain, then \oint_\gamma f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_{a_k\text{ inside }\gamma}\operatorname{Res}(f,a_k), where the residue is the coefficient of (z-a_k)^{-1} in the local Laurent expansion.

Proof 1: Substitute the simple-contour winding numbers

The winding number of a positively oriented simple closed curve is one at an interior point and zero at an exterior point. Substitution into the winding-number form of the residue theorem gives the result.
