Statement

The real projective plane \mathbb{RP}^2 is not orientable.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns orientability of a quotient topological surface; no knowledge of Möbius strips is needed merely to understand it.

Mathematical Field

Review notes

Proof 1: Use the Möbius strip in the punctured projective plane

The real projective plane with one point removed is homeomorphic to the open Möbius strip. It therefore contains a Möbius strip, whereas an orientable surface contains no homeomorphic copy of a Möbius strip. Hence the projective plane is nonorientable.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the punctured projective-plane model and the Möbius-strip obstruction. Projective quotients and surface orientability are not defined locally, and their combined topological meaning cannot confidently be learned as one small extension of point-set topology; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
