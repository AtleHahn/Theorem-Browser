Some topological four-manifolds admit no compatible piecewise-linear structure. This shows that continuous space need not support the additional geometric organization suggested by assembly from linear pieces, distinguishing genuinely different mathematical conceptions of space.

Statement

A topological 4-manifold here is a Hausdorff second-countable space locally homeomorphic to \mathbb R^4. A compatible piecewise-linear (PL) structure is a maximal atlas of homeomorphisms from open subsets of the manifold to open subsets of \mathbb R^4, with transition maps piecewise linear: locally they are affine on the simplices of suitable triangulations. A PL homeomorphism between two such structured manifolds is a homeomorphism whose coordinate expressions, and those of its inverse, are piecewise linear.

There exists a topological 4-manifold that admits no compatible PL structure.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The distinction between a topological atlas and a compatible PL structure requires manifold and triangulation concepts whose cumulative burden exceeds the conservative undergraduate reading allowance.
