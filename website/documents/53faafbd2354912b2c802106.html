Statement

Let x_1<x_2 and y_0\in\mathbb R, and let \Omega=\{x+iy:x_1\le x\le x_2,\ y\ge y_0\}. Let f be continuous on \Omega and holomorphic in its interior. Suppose M>0, B>0, and A\in\mathbb R satisfy |f(z)|\le M on \partial\Omega and |f(x+iy)|\le By^A for x_1\le x\le x_2 and y\ge\max(1,y_0). Then |f(z)|\le M throughout \Omega.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Real analysis supplies bounds, uniform convergence and derivatives. Holomorphic means complex differentiable, and entire means so on all of C. These direct extensions suffice for the full bound, image or convergence claim; no Riemann surfaces or complex-geometric structures occur.

Proof 1: Divide by a high power and exhaust by rectangles

Fix a point {\displaystyle \xi =\sigma +i\tau } inside {\displaystyle \Omega }. Choose {\displaystyle \lambda >\max(0,-y_{0})}, an integer {\displaystyle N>\max(A,0)} and {\displaystyle y_{1}>\max(\tau,1,y_0) } large enough such that {\displaystyle {\frac {By_{1}^{A}}{(y_{1}+\lambda )^{N}}}\leq {\frac {M}{(y_{0}+\lambda )^{N}}}}. The denominator has no zero on the rectangle below. The lower and vertical edges are controlled by the boundary bound, and the upper edge by the displayed growth estimate. Applying the maximum modulus principle to the function {\displaystyle g(z)={\frac {f(z)}{(z+i\lambda )^{N}}}} and the rectangular area {\displaystyle \{z\in \mathbb {C} \mid x_{1}\leq \mathrm {Re} (z)\leq x_{2}\ {\text{and}}\ y_{0}\leq \mathrm {Im} (z)\leq y_{1}\}} we obtain {\displaystyle |g(\xi )|\leq {\frac {M}{(y_{0}+\lambda )^{N}}}}, that is, {\displaystyle |f(\xi )|\leq M\left({\frac {|\xi +i\lambda |}{y_{0}+\lambda }}\right)^{N}}. Letting {\displaystyle \lambda \to +\infty } yields {\displaystyle |f(\xi )|\leq M} as required.

The boundary case is already an assumption.
