Statement

Let a,b be the two standard based generator loops of \mathbb T^2=\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2. Let T_a be the positive Dehn twist about a, given on an annular neighborhood by (s,t)\mapsto(s e^{2\pi it},t) and by the identity outside. Choose the basepoint outside the annulus interior and orient b to cross it in the increasing-t direction, with a oriented by increasing circle angle. Then (T_a)_* is an automorphism of \pi_1(\mathbb T^2) satisfying (T_a)_*([a])=[a] and (T_a)_*([b])=[b*a], where b*a traverses b and then a.

Proof 1: Trace the extra winding through the annulus — outline

Consider the torus represented by a fundamental polygon with edges a and b,

\mathbb T^2\cong\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2.

Let the closed curve along the edge a be called \gamma_a. A tubular neighborhood of the curve \gamma_a looks like a band linked around a doughnut. This neighborhood is an annulus. Extending the twisting map

(e^{i\theta},t)\longmapsto(e^{i(\theta+2\pi t)},t)

of the annulus by the identity outside gives a Dehn twist of the torus by a,

T_a:\mathbb T^2\longrightarrow\mathbb T^2.

This self-homeomorphism acts on the closed curve along b. In the tubular neighborhood it takes the curve of b once along the curve of a.

A homeomorphism fixing the basepoint induces an isomorphism of fundamental groups. Therefore one has an automorphism

(T_a)_*:\pi_1(\mathbb T^2)\longrightarrow\pi_1(\mathbb T^2), \qquad[x]\longmapsto[T_a\circ x],

where [x] denotes the homotopy class of the based closed curve x in the torus. With the orientations in the statement, notice (T_a)_*([a])=[a] and (T_a)_*([b])=[b*a], where b*a is the path travelled around b then a.

Level justification

The annular winding is explicit, but interpreting it as the specified induced automorphism requires based homotopy classes, loop multiplication and surface twisting. The combined prerequisite burden is not confidently a small extension of basic topology.
