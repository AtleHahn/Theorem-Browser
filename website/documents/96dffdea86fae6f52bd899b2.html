Statement

Let \mathrm P=\bigcup_{k\ge1}\mathrm{DTIME}(n^k) and let \mathrm{DSPACE}(n) denote linear deterministic space. Then \mathrm{DSPACE}(n)\ne\mathrm P.

Proof 1: Polynomial padding and the space hierarchy — outline

Suppose \mathrm{DSPACE}(n)=\mathrm P. For a language decidable in space O(n^b), the standard polynomial-padding transfer makes its linear-space padded version decidable in time O(m^c) for some constant c, with padded length m of order n^b. The original language is therefore decidable in time O(n^{bc}), hence belongs to \mathrm P. Thus all polynomial-space languages belong to \mathrm P=\mathrm{DSPACE}(n). This contradicts the space hierarchy theorem, which puts a language in \mathrm{DSPACE}(n^2) outside \mathrm{DSPACE}(n).
