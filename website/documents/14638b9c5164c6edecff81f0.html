Proof 1: Bound sine partial sums and apply Dirichlet’s test — outline

By Dirichlet's test, a monotone real sequence tending to zero times a sequence with bounded partial sums has a convergent series. To see that \sum_{n=1}^{N}\sin n is bounded, we can use the summation formula

\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sin n =\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{e^{in}-e^{-in}}{2i} =\frac{\sum_{n=1}^{N}(e^i)^n-\sum_{n=1}^{N}(e^{-i})^n}{2i} =\frac{\sin1+\sin N-\sin(N+1)}{2-2\cos1}.
