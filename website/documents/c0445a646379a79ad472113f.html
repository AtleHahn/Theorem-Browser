Statement

Let k\ge1 and let \mu,\nu be Borel probability measures on \mathbb R^k. If, for every t\in\mathbb R^k, the pushforward measures under x\mapsto t\cdot x agree, then \mu=\nu. Explicitly, equality is assumed for \mu\{x:t\cdot x\in E\} and \nu\{x:t\cdot x\in E\} for every Borel set E\subseteq\mathbb R and every t.
