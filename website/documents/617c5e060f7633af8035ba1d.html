Statement

Fix an integer n\geq1. A symplectic 2n-manifold (M,\omega) is a smooth manifold of dimension 2n equipped with a smooth differential 2-form \omega that is closed (d\omega=0) and nondegenerate: \omega_p(v,w)=0 for every tangent vector w implies v=0. A symplectic embedding f:(M,\omega)\to(N,\eta) is a smooth embedding satisfying f^*\eta=\omega.

On \mathbb R^{2n} with coordinates (x_1,\ldots,x_n,y_1,\ldots,y_n), put

\omega_0=\sum_{j=1}^{n}dx_j\wedge dy_j,\qquad B^{2n}(r)=\{z:\|z\|<r\},\qquad Z^{2n}(R)=\{z:x_1^2+y_1^2<R^2\}.

For any symplectic 2n-manifold define its Gromov width by

w_G(M,\omega)=\sup\left(\{0\}\cup\{\pi r^2:r>0,\ (B^{2n}(r),\omega_0)\text{ admits a symplectic embedding into }(M,\omega)\}\right)\in[0,\infty].

Then:

  1. If (M,\omega) symplectically embeds into (N,\eta), then w_G(M,\omega)\leq w_G(N,\eta).
  2. For every real \lambda>0, w_G(M,\lambda\omega)=\lambda w_G(M,\omega).
  3. w_G(B^{2n}(1),\omega_0)=w_G(Z^{2n}(1),\omega_0)=\pi.

Thus w_G is a normalized symplectic capacity: a function on symplectic manifolds of fixed dimension satisfying monotonicity, positive conformality and the displayed ball/cylinder normalization.
