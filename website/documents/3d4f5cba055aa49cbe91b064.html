Statement

Let A be an abelian group of bounded exponent: there is a positive integer N such that Na=0 for every a\in A. Then A is isomorphic to a direct sum of cyclic groups. A cyclic group is generated by one element. A direct sum \bigoplus_{j\in J}C_j consists of tuples (c_j), with c_j\in C_j and only finitely many nonzero coordinates, under coordinatewise addition; the index set J may be arbitrary. The trivial group is allowed as the empty direct sum.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory abstract algebra suffices; arbitrary direct sums are explained as finite-support tuples, a small extension of finite direct products.
