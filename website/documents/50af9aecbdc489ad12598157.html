Statement

For the discrete two-point smooth manifold M, identify C^\infty(M) with R=\mathbb R\times\mathbb R. With coordinatewise action, P=\mathbb R\oplus0 is a finitely generated projective R-module but is not free. It is therefore not the section module of any constant-rank real vector bundle over M.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns projectivity and freeness over a product ring and section modules of bundles; these are abstract module-theoretic notions.

Proof 1: Two-coordinate module construction — outline

Take P=\mathbb R\oplus0 over \mathbb R\times\mathbb R. It is finitely generated and projective, but not free. Every constant-rank vector bundle on the two-point space is trivial, and hence has a free module of sections. Thus none has section module P.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The product-ring example is concrete, but the conclusion compares projective modules with section modules of constant-rank bundles. Understanding projectivity, freeness and bundle sections together is not confidently a small extension, even on the two-point base.
