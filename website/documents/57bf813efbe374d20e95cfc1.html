Statement

Let f:\{0,1\}^2\to\{0,1\}. The operations + and multiplication mean OR and AND, respectively, and x^{\prime}=1-x is Boolean complementation. For x_1,x_2\in\{0,1\}, f(x_1,x_2)=(x_1+x_2+f(0,0))(x_1+x_2^{\prime}+f(0,1))(x_1^{\prime}+x_2+f(1,0))(x_1^{\prime}+x_2^{\prime}+f(1,1)).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The finite binary function and each logical operation are defined locally, so elementary finite logic suffices.

Proof 1: Iterate Shannon decomposition

Use the dual Shannon expansion f(x_1,x_2)=(x_1+f(0,x_2))(x_1^{\prime}+f(1,x_2)). Apply the dual form to each remaining cofactor and distribute OR over AND:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}f(x_{1},x_{2})&=(x_{1}+f(0,x_{2}))\cdot (x_{1}'+f(1,x_{2}))\\&=(x_{1}+x_{2}+f(0,0))\cdot (x_{1}+x_{2}'+f(0,1))\cdot (x_{1}'+x_{2}+f(1,0))\cdot (x_{1}'+x_{2}'+f(1,1))\end{aligned}}}
Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply the dual Boolean split to both variables and distribute. At each input three factors equal one and the remaining factor equals the corresponding truth-table value. Only elementary Boolean operations are needed.
