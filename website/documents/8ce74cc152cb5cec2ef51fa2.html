Statement

Let an n-vertex graph contain a vertex adjacent to every other vertex. If V=A\sqcup B and |A|,|B|\le2n/3, then at least n/3 edges join A to B. In particular stars and wheel graphs admit no sublinear balanced edge separator.

High school only

Proof 1: A dominating vertex forces a linear balanced edge cut — source argument

The side not containing the dominating vertex has at least n/3 vertices, because the other side has at most 2n/3. Each vertex of that side has its edge to the dominating vertex crossing the cut. These are distinct edges.

Level justification

The side opposite the dominating vertex has at least n/3 vertices, each contributing a distinct crossing edge. Elementary finite counting proves the lower bound.
