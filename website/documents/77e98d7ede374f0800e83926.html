Statement

Let k be algebraically closed, let G be a connected algebraic group acting transitively on a variety X, and let f_i:V_i\to X be morphisms of nonempty varieties, i=1,2. Set \Gamma=(G\times V_1)\times_X V_2, using (g,v)\mapsto g\cdot f_1(v) on the first factor; its closed points are triples (g,v,w) with g\cdot f_1(v)=f_2(w). Then \dim\Gamma=\dim V_1+\dim V_2+\dim G-\dim X.

Proof 1: Project to the pair of source varieties — outline

The projection p:\Gamma\to V_1\times V_2 is surjective by transitivity. Each fiber is a transporter coset of a stabilizer in G, of dimension \dim G-\dim X. The dimension theorem for a surjective morphism with constant fiber dimension gives \dim\Gamma=\dim V_1+\dim V_2+\dim G-\dim X.
