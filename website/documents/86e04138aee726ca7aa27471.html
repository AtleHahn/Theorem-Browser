Statement

For sets X,Y, the collection of binary relations from X to Y, ordered by inclusion, is a lattice: for relations R,S\subseteq X\times Y, their greatest lower bound is R\cap S and their least upper bound is R\cup S.

Proof 1: Identification with a power-set lattice

A binary relation over sets X and Y can be identified with an element of the power set of the Cartesian product X\times Y. Since a power set is a lattice for set inclusion (\subseteq), relations can be manipulated using set operations (union, intersection, and complementation) and algebra of sets.

Here the power-set lattice fact identifies intersection as the greatest lower bound and union as the least upper bound.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Binary relations are subsets of a Cartesian product. The new term lattice means that pairs have greatest lower and least upper bounds; for inclusion these are intersection and union. These definitions and the imported power-set fact are a small extension of introductory undergraduate set theory, without general lattice theory.
