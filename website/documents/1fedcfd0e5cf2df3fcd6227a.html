Statement

Work in ZFC. The constructible universe is L=\bigcup_{\alpha\in\mathrm{Ord}}L_\alpha, where L_0=\varnothing, L_{\alpha+1}=\operatorname{Def}(L_\alpha), and L_\lambda=\bigcup_{\alpha<\lambda}L_\alpha at limit ordinals. Here \operatorname{Def}(Y) consists of subsets of Y definable over (Y,\in) by first-order formulas with parameters in Y. At least one of the following holds: (i) every singular cardinal \lambda of the ambient universe is singular in L and (\lambda^+)^L=\lambda^+; (ii) every uncountable cardinal of the ambient universe is inaccessible in L. A cardinal is singular if it has a cofinal subset of smaller cardinality; \lambda^+ is the least cardinal strictly larger than \lambda. Inaccessible means uncountable, regular and strong limit: it has no smaller cofinal subset and 2^\kappa<\lambda for every cardinal \kappa<\lambda. Superscript L means these notions are evaluated within L.
