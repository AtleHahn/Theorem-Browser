Statement

For f:X\to Y, write f[A]=\{f(x):x\in A\} and f^{-1}[B]=\{x\in X:f(x)\in B\}. Let f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R, f(x)=x^2, A=[-4,2], and B=[-2,4]. Then f[A\cap B]\subsetneq f[A]\cap f[B].

Level
High school only
Level justification

Images and preimages are explicitly defined, and the example involves only squaring and real intervals.
