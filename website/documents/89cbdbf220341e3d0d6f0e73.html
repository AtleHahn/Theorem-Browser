Statement

Let V be a real vector space. A subset C\subseteq V is convex if (1-\lambda)x+\lambda y\in C for every x,y\in C and 0\le\lambda\le1. For any family (C_i)_{i\in J} of convex subsets of V, the intersection \bigcap_{i\in J}C_i is convex, with empty intersection interpreted as V.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Binary operations, identities, elementary quantifiers and convex combinations are undergraduate algebra/set concepts. The full conditions are explicitly stated equations or set membership, requiring no additional theory.

Proof 1: Apply the convexity condition in every intersected set — outline

The defining segment condition holds in every set C_i. Consequently the segment joining any two points of their intersection lies in every C_i, hence in the intersection. The empty set is convex vacuously, and the intersection of an empty family is V, also convex.
