Statement

No integer congruent to 7 modulo 8 is a sum of three integer squares.

Level
High school only

Proof 1: Square residues modulo eight

Since squares are congruent to 0, 1, or 4\pmod8, no integer congruent to 7\pmod8 can be represented as a sum of three squares. The finite check of sums of three square residues is implicit in the source.

Level
High school only
Level justification

List square residues modulo eight and check the finite sums of three entries. Elementary modular arithmetic suffices.
