Statement

For every real number \alpha and every real number N\geq1, there exist integers p and q such that

1\leq q\leq N\qquad\text{and}\qquad |q\alpha-p|\leq\frac{1}{\lfloor N\rfloor+1}<\frac1N.

Here \lfloor N\rfloor is the greatest integer not exceeding N.

High school only
