Statement

For the first-order fragment with at most the variable names x,y,z, satisfiability and finite satisfiability are both undecidable.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding models, formal deduction and algorithmic decision problems requires mathematical logic.

Proof 1: Use RE and co-RE completeness

The satisfiability problem for this fragment is co-RE-complete, while finite satisfiability is RE-complete. Thus neither problem is decidable.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The reduction from completeness to undecidability is short, but applying it requires first-order model satisfiability, finite models, recursive enumerability and completeness reductions together. This cumulative logic and computability background is not confidently a small extension.
