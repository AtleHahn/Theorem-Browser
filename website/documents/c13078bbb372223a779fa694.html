Statement

A smooth projective complex surface that is unirational is rational, that is, birational to the projective plane.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Unirationality and birational classification of projective complex surfaces require substantial algebraic geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Vanishing invariants and Castelnuovo’s criterion

For a unirational complex surface, irregularity and plurigenera are bounded above by those of a rational surface, and hence are all zero. In particular its irregularity q and second plurigenus P_2 vanish. Castelnuovo’s criterion then shows that the surface is rational.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Transfer vanishing irregularity and plurigenera from a dominating rational surface and apply Castelnuovo. Birational surface theory, cohomological invariants and pluricanonical sections require algebraic geometry.
