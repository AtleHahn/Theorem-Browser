Statement

If U\subseteq\mathbb C is a domain (a nonempty connected open set), f:U\to\mathbb C is holomorphic, and f(U) lies in a real affine line in the complex plane, then f is constant.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Real analysis and calculus supply limits, derivatives, series and integration. Complex differentiability, primitives and logarithms are direct extensions, and circle integrals are parametrized ordinary integrals. The full claims use no Riemann surfaces, residues or abstract differential forms.

Proof 1: Open-image obstruction

A nonconstant holomorphic function on a domain has open image by the open mapping theorem. A subset of a line in the plane contains no open disk, so such an image cannot lie in a line.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the supplied open mapping theorem and note that a planar line contains no open disk. Undergraduate point-set topology and complex differentiability suffice for the application.
