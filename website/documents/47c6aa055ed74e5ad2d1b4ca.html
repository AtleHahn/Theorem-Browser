Statement

Let a>0 and let f:(0,2a)\to\mathbb R be three times differentiable, with f^{(3)}(x)\geq0 for every x\in(0,2a). Let n\geq1 be an integer, 0<x_i\leq a and p_i>0 for 1\leq i\leq n, and put P=\sum_{i=1}^np_i. Then

\frac{1}{P}\sum_{i=1}^np_i f(x_i)-f\left(\frac{1}{P}\sum_{i=1}^np_ix_i\right) \leq \frac{1}{P}\sum_{i=1}^np_i f(2a-x_i)-f\left(\frac{1}{P}\sum_{i=1}^np_i(2a-x_i)\right).
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Differentiability and weighted arithmetic sums are standard undergraduate calculus; no convexity theorem is required to understand the formula.
