Statement

For a finite family of positive-radius circles with disjoint open interiors in the Euclidean plane, place one vertex at each circle center and join centers of tangent circles by line segments. This is a planar straight-line drawing of the tangency graph: edges have no crossings except shared graph endpoints.

High school only

Proof 1: Use the centers-and-segments recipe — outline

By placing vertices at the centers of the circles and drawing straight edges between tangent pairs, a straight-line planar embedding is obtained.

Level justification

Each half of an edge lies in its own circle interior until the tangency point. Disjoint interiors prevent crossings; positive-radius disjoint circles cannot create a third edge through that contact. These are elementary facts about disks and straight segments underlying the supplied outline.
