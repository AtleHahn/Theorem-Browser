Statement

For a real or complex normed space X, let X^* be its bounded linear dual with operator norm, and define J:X\to X^{**} by (Jx)(f)=f(x). Then \|Jx\|=\|x\| for every x\in X.

Proof 1: The canonical bidual embedding is isometric — outline

By the norming-functional consequence of the Hahn–Banach theorem, if z is an element of X, then there exists a continuous linear map f:X\to\mathbf K such that f(z)=\|z\| and \|f\|\le1, where \mathbf K is the scalar field. This implies that the natural injection J from the normed space X into its double dual X^{**} is isometric.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The inequalities are short, but the full argument iterates the bounded dual construction and its operator norm to form the bidual and canonical embedding, then applies a norming functional. This linked normed-space framework exceeds the allowed small extension of finite-dimensional linear algebra; the conservative classification is beyond undergraduate.
