Statement

Let W be a compact (n+1)-dimensional h-cobordism between closed n-manifolds M,N, with n\ge5, in the smooth, piecewise-linear or topological category of the source’s s-cobordism theorem. If M\hookrightarrow W is a simple homotopy equivalence, then N\hookrightarrow W is also a simple homotopy equivalence.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Cobordisms and simple homotopy equivalence require substantial high-dimensional topology. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: One simple boundary inclusion forces the other to be simple (outline)

The assumed simple equivalence makes the Whitehead torsion \tau(W,M) vanish. By the s-cobordism theorem, W is a cylinder. Either boundary inclusion of a cylinder is a simple homotopy equivalence, so the inclusion of N is simple as well.

Level

Level justification

The short argument requires h-cobordisms, simple homotopy equivalence and Whitehead torsion to apply the cylinder criterion. These linked manifold and algebraic-topology concepts exceed a small extension of ordinary undergraduate preparation.
