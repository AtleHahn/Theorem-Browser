Statement

A nonconstant polynomial f\in\mathbb Z[x] is irreducible in \mathbb Z[x] if and only if its coefficients have gcd 1 and f is irreducible in \mathbb Q[x].

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Irreducibility in two different polynomial rings requires undergraduate abstract algebra and the distinction between units and nontrivial factors. A bright US college student should be able to understand the statement after about 30 minutes of focused background research; this rating concerns understanding the claim, not proving it.
