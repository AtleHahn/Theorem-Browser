Itô divisibility of irreducible representation degrees

Statement

Let G be a finite group and let A\leq G be maximal by inclusion among its normal abelian subgroups. If \rho:G\to\mathrm{GL}(V) is an irreducible representation on a nonzero finite-dimensional complex vector space V, then

\dim_{\mathbb C}V\ \mid\ [G:A].

Here [G:A]=|G|/|A| is the number of cosets of A. Normal means gAg^{-1}=A for every g\in G, and abelian means its elements commute. A representation is a group homomorphism to the invertible complex linear maps of V; irreducible means that the only subspaces invariant under every \rho(g) are 0 and V. Divisibility means that [G:A] is an integer multiple of the dimension.
