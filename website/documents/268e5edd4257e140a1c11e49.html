Statement

Let K be a field of characteristic different from 2 and 3. Let x_0,x_1,x_2 lie in an extension containing a primitive cube root of unity \xi. Define s_1=x_0+\xi x_1+\xi^2x_2, s_2=x_0+\xi^2x_1+\xi x_2, e_1=x_0+x_1+x_2, e_2=x_0x_1+x_1x_2+x_2x_0, e_3=x_0x_1x_2. Then s_1s_2=e_1^2-3e_2 and s_1^3+s_2^3=2e_1^3-9e_1e_2+27e_3.

Proof 1: Expand the two resolvents — outline

Put P=s_1s_2 and S=s_1^3+s_2^3.

A straightforward computation using the relations ξ3 = 1 and ξ2 + ξ + 1 = 0 gives

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}P&=s_{1}s_{2}=x_{0}^{2}+x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}-(x_{0}x_{1}+x_{1}x_{2}+x_{2}x_{0}),\\S&=s_{1}^{3}+s_{2}^{3}=2(x_{0}^{3}+x_{1}^{3}+x_{2}^{3})-3(x_{0}^{2}x_{1}+x_{1}^{2}x_{2}+x_{2}^{2}x_{0}+x_{0}x_{1}^{2}+x_{1}x_{2}^{2}+x_{2}x_{0}^{2})+12x_{0}x_{1}x_{2}.\end{aligned}}}

This shows that P and S are symmetric functions of x_0,x_1,x_2. Using Newton's identities, it is straightforward to express them in terms of the elementary symmetric functions of the roots, giving

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}P&=e_{1}^{2}-3e_{2},\\S&=2e_{1}^{3}-9e_{1}e_{2}+27e_{3},\end{aligned}}}
