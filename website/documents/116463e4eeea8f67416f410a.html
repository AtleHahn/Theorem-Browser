Statement

Fix \tau=x+iy with y>0 and real u,v not both integers. For \operatorname{Re}s>1 put

E_{u,v}(\tau,s)=\sum_{(m,n)\in\mathbb Z^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}}e^{2\pi i(mu+nv)}\frac{y^s}{|m\tau+n|^{2s}}.

Set q=e^{2\pi i\tau}, and for a complex z put p=e^{2\pi iz} and

f(z;\tau)=q^{1/12}(p^{1/2}-p^{-1/2})\prod_{n=1}^\infty(1-q^np)(1-q^n/p),

where q^a=e^{2\pi ia\tau} and p^a=e^{2\pi iaz}. Then the value at s=1 of the analytic continuation is

E_{u,v}(\tau,1)=-2\pi\log\left|f(u-v\tau;\tau)q^{v^2/2}\right|.
