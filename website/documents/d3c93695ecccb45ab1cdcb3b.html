Statement

Let X be a compact Kähler manifold and L a holomorphic line bundle on X. Here a Kähler manifold is a complex manifold with a Hermitian metric whose associated real (1,1)-form is closed. A holomorphic line bundle has one-dimensional complex vector-space fibers and holomorphic local trivializations.

The bundle L is positive if and only if there exist integers N\geq0 and m\geq1 and a holomorphic embedding \varphi:X\to\mathbb{CP}^N such that

L^{\otimes m}\cong\varphi^*\mathcal O_{\mathbb{CP}^N}(1).

Positivity means that L admits a smooth Hermitian metric with positive curvature form; in a local nonvanishing holomorphic frame of squared norm h, this form is -i\partial\bar\partial\log h and is positive definite on nonzero complex tangent vectors. The notation L^{\otimes m} means the m-fold tensor power, \varphi^* denotes pullback of a bundle, and \mathcal O(1) is the hyperplane line bundle on complex projective space (the dual of its tautological line bundle).
