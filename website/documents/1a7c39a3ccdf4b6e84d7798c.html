Statement

For an odd prime p\ne5, -5 is a quadratic residue modulo p if and only if p\equiv1,3,7,9\pmod{20}.

Proof 1: Combine residue conditions modulo four and five

For -5 to be a residue modulo p, either both 5 and -1 have to be residues modulo p or they both have to be non-residues: i.e., p\equiv\pm1\pmod5 and p\equiv1\pmod4, or p\equiv\pm2\pmod5 and p\equiv3\pmod4. Here we use multiplicativity of quadratic character, the criterion for 5, and the first supplement (-1/p)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}. Using the Chinese remainder theorem these are equivalent to p\equiv1,9\pmod{20} or p\equiv3,7\pmod{20}.
