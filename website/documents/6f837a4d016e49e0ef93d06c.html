Statement

Let X_1,\ldots,X_d be jointly distributed discrete random variables, where d\geq1, and let S_1,\ldots,S_n be subsets of \{1,\ldots,d\}. Repeated subsets are allowed. Suppose r\geq1 is an integer such that each index j belongs to at least r of these subsets. Then

H(X_1,\ldots,X_d)\leq\frac1r\sum_{i=1}^n H\bigl((X_j)_{j\in S_i}\bigr).

Here H(Y)=-\sum_y\mathbb P(Y=y)\log\mathbb P(Y=y) is Shannon entropy, using the same logarithm base greater than 1 throughout and the convention 0\log0=0. The sum ranges over the finite or countable values of the discrete random vector Y; entropy may be +\infty. The empty subvector has entropy zero. No independence of the variables is required.
