Statement

Let K be a number field and C a smooth projective geometrically connected curve of genus g≥2, embedded over K in its Jacobian A. Then C∩A(K)=C(K) is finite.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Smooth projective curves; Number fields; Jacobians; Rational points. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply Faltings to the embedded curve

Apply the Faltings theorem invoked in the source: a smooth projective curve of genus at least two over a number field has finitely many K-rational points. A K-defined embedding identifies the points of C lying in A(K) with C(K). Thus the indicated intersection is finite.
