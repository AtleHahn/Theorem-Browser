Statement

Let X be a complex manifold, with \mathcal O_X its sheaf of holomorphic functions. If X is Stein, then for every coherent \mathcal O_X-module sheaf F and every integer p>0, H^p(X,F)=0. Conversely, if H^1(X,F)=0 for every coherent \mathcal O_X-module sheaf F, then X is Stein. Here H^p denotes sheaf cohomology, the right derived functors of global sections. An \mathcal O_X-module sheaf is coherent if it is locally finitely generated and on every open subset the kernel of every morphism from a finite-rank free module sheaf into its restriction is locally finitely generated. A Stein manifold is a complex manifold admitting a proper holomorphic embedding into some \mathbb C^N; proper means that inverse images of compact sets are compact.
