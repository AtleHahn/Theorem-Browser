Statement

Let g\ge0,n\ge1 be integers with 2g-2+n>0. On the moduli stack \overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n} of stable genus-g curves with n marked points, of complex dimension D=3g-3+n, let E be the rank-g Hodge bundle, \lambda_j=c_j(E) with \lambda_0=1, and \psi_i the first Chern class of the cotangent line at the ith marking. The formal expression I(k_1,\ldots,k_n)=\int_{\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}}\frac{1-\lambda_1+\cdots+(-1)^g\lambda_g}{\prod_i(1-k_i\psi_i)} is a symmetric polynomial in k_1,\ldots,k_n. Nonzero monomials have total degree between D-g and D. The coefficient of \prod_i k_i^{d_i} is (-1)^j\int\lambda_j\prod_i\psi_i^{d_i} when j=D-\sum_i d_i lies in \{0,\ldots,g\}, and is zero otherwise.

Proof 1: Expand the geometric factors and select top degree — outline

Here c(E^*)=1-\lambda_1+\cdots+(-1)^g\lambda_g.

The fraction {\displaystyle 1/(1-k_{i}\psi _{i})} is interpreted as {\displaystyle 1+k_{i}\psi _{i}+k_{i}^{2}\psi _{i}^{2}+\cdots }, where the sum can be cut at degree 3g − 3 + n (the dimension of the moduli space). Thus the integrand is a product of n + 1 factors. We expand this product, extract from it the part of degree 3g − 3 + n and integrate it over the moduli space.

It follows that the integral

{\displaystyle \int _{{\overline {\mathcal {M}}}_{g,n}}{\frac {c(E^{*})}{(1-k_{1}\psi _{1})\cdots (1-k_{n}\psi _{n})}}}

is a symmetric polynomial in variables k1, ..., kn, whose monomials have degrees between 3g − 3 + n and 2g − 3 + n. The coefficient of the monomial {\displaystyle k_{1}^{d_{1}}\cdots k_{n}^{d_{n}}} equals

{\displaystyle \int _{{\overline {\mathcal {M}}}_{g,n}}(-1)^{j}\lambda _{j}\psi _{1}^{d_{1}}\cdots \psi _{n}^{d_{n}},}

where

{\displaystyle j=3g-3+n-\sum d_{i}.}

The coefficient is zero unless 0\le j\le g. Relabeling the marked points gives the symmetry.
