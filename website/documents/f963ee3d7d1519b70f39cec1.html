Statement

Let X_i be iid real random variables with distribution function F, and F_n(x)=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n1_{\{X_i\le x\}}. For each fixed real x, \sqrt n(F_n(x)-F(x)) converges in distribution to a centered Gaussian with variance F(x)(1-F(x)), including the degenerate Gaussian when that variance is zero.

Proof 1: Apply the central limit theorem to threshold indicators — outline

Apply the classical central limit theorem to the empirical distribution count at the fixed argument x. Its summands are iid Bernoulli variables with parameter F(x), hence have mean F(x) and variance F(x)(1-F(x)). The centered empirical count has the stated Gaussian limit.
