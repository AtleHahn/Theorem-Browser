Statement

Let (\lambda_n) be finite complex measures on a measurable space with K=\sup_n\|\lambda_n\|_{TV}<\infty. Then \lambda_n(B) converges for every measurable B if and only if \int g\,d\lambda_n converges for every bounded complex-valued measurable g.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement compares two measure-theoretic convergence notions under a uniform variation bound.

Proof 1: Simple approximation and a uniform Cauchy estimate

Write (S,\mathcal B) for the underlying measurable space.

The implication {\displaystyle \Leftarrow } is obvious, since, for every {\displaystyle B\in {\mathcal {B}}}, the indicator function {\displaystyle \mathbf {1} _{B}} with

{\displaystyle \mathbf {1} _{B}(x)={\begin{cases}1,&{\text{if }}x\in B,\\0,&{\text{if }}x\notin B,\end{cases}}}

is bounded and measurable and {\displaystyle \textstyle \lambda _{n}(B)=\int \mathbf {1} _{B}\,d\lambda _{n}}. For the other implication {\displaystyle \Rightarrow }, we first note that, by linearity of limits, the sequence {\displaystyle \textstyle (\int s\,d\lambda _{n})_{n\geq 1}} converges for all measurable simple functions {\displaystyle s\colon S\to \mathbb {C} }. Write {\displaystyle K} for the bound of total variations:

{\displaystyle K=\sup _{n\geq 1}\lVert \lambda _{n}\rVert _{TV}.}

Given a bounded measurable function {\displaystyle g\colon S\to \mathbb {C} } and an {\displaystyle \varepsilon >0} there is a measurable simple function {\displaystyle s\colon S\to \mathbb {C} } with {\displaystyle \lVert g-s\rVert _{\infty }<\varepsilon }. Furthermore, {\displaystyle \textstyle (\int s\,d\lambda _{n})_{n\geq 1}} converges, thus there exists {\displaystyle N} such that

{\displaystyle \left|\int s\,d\lambda _{k}-\int s\,d\lambda _{n}\right|<\varepsilon }

for all {\displaystyle k,n>N}. Then,

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\left|\int g\,d\lambda _{k}-\int g\,d\lambda _{n}\right|&\leq \left|\int g\,d\lambda _{k}-\int s\,d\lambda _{k}\right|+\left|\int s\,d\lambda _{k}-\int s\,d\lambda _{n}\right|+\left|\int s\,d\lambda _{n}-\int g\,d\lambda _{n}\right|\\&\leq \int |g-s|\,d|\lambda _{k}|+\left|\int s\,d\lambda _{k}-\int s\,d\lambda _{n}\right|+\int |g-s|\,d|\lambda _{n}|\\&<\varepsilon (2K+1)\end{aligned}}}

for all {\displaystyle k,n>N}. Consequently, the sequence {\displaystyle \textstyle (\int g\,d\lambda _{n})_{n\geq 1}} converges.
