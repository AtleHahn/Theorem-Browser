Statement

Fix a prime p, and let f(n,p) be the number of isomorphism classes of groups of order p^n. As the positive integer n tends to infinity, f(n,p)=p^{(2/27)n^3+O(n^{8/3})}. Explicitly, there are constants C>0 and N, possibly depending on p, such that |\log_p f(n,p)-(2/27)n^3|\le Cn^{8/3} for all n\ge N. Two groups are isomorphic if there is a bijection between them preserving multiplication.
