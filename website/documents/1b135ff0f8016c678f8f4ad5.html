Statement

Let (X,\Sigma,\mu) be a measure space and let f,g:X\to\mathbb R be measurable with finite L^p norm, where \|u\|_p=(\int_X|u|^p\,d\mu)^{1/p}. If 1<p<2 and q=p/(p-1), then \left\|\frac{f+g}{2}\right\|_p^q+\left\|\frac{f-g}{2}\right\|_p^q\le\left(\frac{\|f\|_p^p+\|g\|_p^p}{2}\right)^{q/p}.
