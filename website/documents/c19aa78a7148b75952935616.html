Statement

No finite tree has automorphism group isomorphic to the cyclic group C_3 of order three.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory algebra supplies groups, cosets, quotients, homomorphisms and linear independence. Complement or composition-series conditions and monoid vocabulary are small algebraic extensions; finite-tree automorphisms are adjacency-preserving permutations. No additional structural theory is required.

Proof 1: The cyclic group of order three is not a tree automorphism group — outline

Use Jordan’s characterization: tree symmetry groups are precisely the smallest class containing the trivial group and closed under direct products and wreath products with symmetric groups. In particular the cyclic group of order three is not a tree symmetry group.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The supplied characterization uses iterated wreath products with symmetric groups, and the exclusion argument is left implicit. Learning that construction together with the tree-group classification is not confidently a small extension of the baseline; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
