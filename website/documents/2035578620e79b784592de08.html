Statement

Let f(x)=a_0x^n+a_1x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_n be a real polynomial, n\ge1. If f has a local maximum or minimum at x=t\in\mathbb R, then na_0t^n+(n-1)a_1t^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{n-1}t=0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Real polynomials; Local extrema; Polynomial derivatives. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Hudde’s polynomial extremum rule — proof

Fermat’s stationary-point theorem gives f'(t)=0. The polynomial on the left of the asserted equation is t f'(t), so it vanishes.

Level justification

Apply Fermat to get f prime(t)=0 and multiply by t. Undergraduate single-variable calculus suffices.
