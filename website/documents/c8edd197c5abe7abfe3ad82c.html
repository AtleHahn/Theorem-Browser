Statement

Let (M,g) be a complete, connected, noncompact Riemannian manifold without boundary whose sectional curvature is nonnegative on every tangent two-plane. Then M contains a closed (compact and without boundary), embedded submanifold S that is totally convex and totally geodesic, and M is diffeomorphic to the total space of its normal bundle

\nu S=\{(p,v):p\in S,\ v\in T_pM,\ g_p(v,w)=0\text{ for all }w\in T_pS\}.

Here completeness refers to the distance induced by g. Total convexity means that every geodesic segment in M whose endpoints lie in S lies entirely in S. Total geodesicity means that every geodesic for the induced metric on S is also an ambient geodesic. Such an S is called a soul of M.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The cumulative Riemannian curvature, geodesic and bundle framework exceeds a short undergraduate reading allowance, even with the normal bundle and convexity defined.
