Statement

Let n\ge1, let U\subset\mathbb C^n be open, and let f:U\to\mathbb C be continuous. Suppose that f is holomorphic in each variable separately: on each open slice obtained by fixing all other coordinates, it is a holomorphic function of the remaining complex coordinate. Then f is jointly holomorphic on U, meaning that near each point it has a convergent power-series expansion in the n complex coordinates.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The distinction between separate and joint analyticity is explicitly defined; introductory complex power series and finite-dimensional continuity suffice for understanding, within the conservative undergraduate reading allowance.
