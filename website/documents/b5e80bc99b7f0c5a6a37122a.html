Proof 1: Specialize the strong Dirichlet theorem

The strong form of Dirichlet’s theorem says that the reciprocal-prime series in any coprime arithmetic progression diverges. Apply it with a=3,d=4.

The strong form of Dirichlet's theorem implies that

{\displaystyle {\frac {1}{3}}+{\frac {1}{7}}+{\frac {1}{11}}+{\frac {1}{19}}+{\frac {1}{23}}+{\frac {1}{31}}+{\frac {1}{43}}+{\frac {1}{47}}+{\frac {1}{59}}+{\frac {1}{67}}+\cdots }

is a divergent series.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Substitute the coprime integers three and four into the supplied divergence theorem. Undergraduate real series suffice to understand and apply the claim; the analytic proof of Dirichlet theorem is not used.
