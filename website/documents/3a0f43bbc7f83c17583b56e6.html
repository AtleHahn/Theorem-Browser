Statement

Let X be a real or complex reflexive Banach space and let A\subseteq X be norm bounded. Then the closure of A in the weak topology \sigma(X,X^*) is compact in that topology.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding weak compactness and reflexivity requires infinite-dimensional functional analysis.

Mathematical Field
Functional analysis
Required topics
Reflexive real or complex Banach spacesNorm boundednessWeak topology and compact closure
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Apply Alaoglu through reflexivity — outline

In a reflexive Banach space, bounded subsets are weakly precompact by Banach–Alaoglu. Here weak precompactness means that the weak closure is weakly compact.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Identify the weak topology with the bidual weak-star topology through reflexivity and apply Alaoglu. General Banach duals and weak topologies exceed the undergraduate baseline.
