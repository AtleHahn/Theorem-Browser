Statement

The closed unit ball of the continuous dual of a separable real or complex normed space is sequentially compact in the weak-star topology.

Proof 1: Compactness together with weak-star metrizability

Use the stated weak-star metrization theorem: for a separable normed space X with dense sequence (x_n), the weak-star topology on its dual closed unit ball B is induced by

\rho(f,g)=\sum_{n\ge1}2^{-n}\frac{|(f-g)(x_n)|}{1+|(f-g)(x_n)|}.

By Banach–Alaoglu this ball is compact. In the metric topology, compactness and sequential compactness are equivalent, so B is weak-star sequentially compact.
