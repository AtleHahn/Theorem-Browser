Statement

If A is a nonnegative square matrix with every row sum equal to one, then A\mathbf1=\mathbf1 and \rho(A)=1.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves finite-dimensional matrices; eigenvalues and spectral radius. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Source matrix argument

The vector \mathbf1=(1,\ldots,1)^T is an eigenvector with eigenvalue 1 because the rows sum to one. The bound |\lambda|\leq\|A\|_\infty=1 for every eigenvalue gives the reverse inequality for the spectral radius.
