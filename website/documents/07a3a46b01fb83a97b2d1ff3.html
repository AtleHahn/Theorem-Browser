Statement

Let X be a real Hausdorff locally convex topological vector space and X^* its continuous linear dual. For f:X\to\mathbb R\cup\{-\infty,+\infty\} define f^*(p)=\sup_{x\in X}(p(x)-f(x)),\qquad f^{**}(x)=\sup_{p\in X^*}(p(x)-f^*(p)), with a-(+\infty)=-\infty and a-(-\infty)=+\infty for finite a. Then f=f^{**} if and only if one of the following holds: f is proper, lower semicontinuous and convex; f\equiv+\infty; or f\equiv-\infty. Proper means never -\infty and finite somewhere, lower semicontinuity means closed sublevel sets, and convexity means that the epigraph \{(x,t)\in X\times\mathbb R:f(x)\le t\} is convex.
