Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer. For a Hermitian matrix X=\sum_j\lambda_jP_j, scalar functions act spectrally: h(X)=\sum_j h(\lambda_j)P_j. Here P_j are the orthogonal eigenprojections and \operatorname{Tr} is the matrix trace. On pairs of positive-definite Hermitian n\times n matrices define R(A\Vert B)=\operatorname{Tr}(A\log A)-\operatorname{Tr}(A\log B). It is jointly convex: for such pairs (A_1,B_1),(A_2,B_2) and 0\le t\le1, R(tA_1+(1-t)A_2\Vert tB_1+(1-t)B_2)\le tR(A_1\Vert B_1)+(1-t)R(A_2\Vert B_2).

Proof 1: A limit of Lieb-concavity difference quotients — outline

For all {\displaystyle 0<p<1}, {\displaystyle (A,B)\mapsto \operatorname {Tr} (B^{1-p}A^{p})} is jointly concave, by Lieb's concavity theorem, and thus

{\displaystyle (A,B)\mapsto {\frac {1}{p-1}}(\operatorname {Tr} (B^{1-p}A^{p})-\operatorname {Tr} A)}

is convex. But

{\displaystyle \lim _{p\uparrow 1}{\frac {1}{p-1}}(\operatorname {Tr} (B^{1-p}A^{p})-\operatorname {Tr} A)=R(A\parallel B),}

and convexity is preserved in the limit.

The proof is due to G. Lindblad.
