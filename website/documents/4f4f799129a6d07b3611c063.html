Statement

Let Ω⊂C be open and f_n:Ω→C holomorphic. If f_n→f uniformly on every compact subset of Ω, then f is holomorphic on Ω.

Level

Level justification

Proof 1: Pass contour integrals through the uniform limit

On each compact subdisk of \Omega, uniform convergence makes f continuous. By Cauchy's theorem, we know that

\oint_C f_n(z)\,dz=0

for every n, along any closed piecewise smooth curve C in a disk contained in \Omega. Then uniform convergence on the compact curve implies that

\oint_C f(z)\,dz =\oint_C\lim_{n\to\infty}f_n(z)\,dz =\lim_{n\to\infty}\oint_C f_n(z)\,dz=0

for every such closed curve C, and therefore by Morera's theorem f must be holomorphic on the disk. Since holomorphy is local, f is holomorphic on \Omega.

This is the source's disk argument applied locally, as in its stated generalization to convergence uniformly on compact subsets.
