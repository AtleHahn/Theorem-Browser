Statement

In ZFC, let \mathfrak c=|\mathbb R| and \aleph_\omega=\sup_{n<\omega}\aleph_n. Then \mathfrak c\ne\aleph_\omega.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement compares an infinite real cardinal with a limit of aleph cardinals in ZFC; cofinality is used only in its proof.

Proof 1: The König cofinality obstruction — outline

Equality can be ruled out by König's theorem on the grounds of cofinality: \mathfrak c\ne\aleph_\omega.

The consequence of König's theorem used here is \operatorname{cf}(2^{\aleph_0})>\aleph_0. Since \mathfrak c=2^{\aleph_0} and \operatorname{cf}(\aleph_\omega)=\omega, this excludes the equality.
