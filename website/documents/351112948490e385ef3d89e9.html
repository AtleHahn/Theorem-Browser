Statement

For a measure space (\Omega,\mathcal F,\mu), L^1(\mu) is the space of integrable scalar functions modulo equality almost everywhere, with norm \|f\|_1=\int|f|\,d\mu, and L^\infty(\mu) uses the essential-supremum norm. For an operator defined on L^1, the condition on its L^\infty norm means that it maps L^1\cap L^\infty into L^\infty with the stated bound on that intersection. Let T:L^1(\mu)\to L^1(\mu) be linear, with

\|Tf\|_1\le\|f\|_1\quad(f\in L^1),\qquad \|Tf\|_\infty\le\|f\|_\infty\quad(f\in L^1\cap L^\infty).

Then for every f\in L^1(\mu) the averages

\frac1n\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}T^jf

converge to a finite limit almost everywhere as n\to\infty, where T^0 is the identity. No positivity assumption on T is imposed.
