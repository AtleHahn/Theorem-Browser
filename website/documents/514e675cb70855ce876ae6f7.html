Statement

Take two copies of \mathbb{CP}^1 with the standard toric structures whose moment maps are [z_0:z_1]\mapsto |z_1|^2/(|z_0|^2+|z_1|^2). Their product with its product symplectic form and product two-torus action is a four-dimensional symplectic toric manifold, and its moment image is [0,1]\times[0,1]\subset\mathbb R^2.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The claim includes the symplectic form, Hamiltonian torus action and moment map of a toric manifold, requiring substantial symplectic geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Take the product of the two interval images

The standard toric projective line is the two-dimensional toric sphere and has moment image a segment, here [0,1] under the displayed normalization. The Delzant correspondence is compatible with products: the product toric structure has the product moment image. Two copies therefore give a four-dimensional toric manifold with rectangular image [0,1]^2.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply product compatibility to two toric projective lines. Symplectic manifolds, Hamiltonian torus actions and moment maps remain substantial graduate prerequisites even though the final image is a square.
