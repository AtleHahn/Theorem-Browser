Statement

Let F(x)=\sum_{k=1}^n a_ke^{\lambda_kx} with a_k,\lambda_k\in\mathbb C, n\ge2 an integer, on a bounded interval J\subset\mathbb R of positive length L. Put A=\max_k|\operatorname{Re}\lambda_k| and M=\sup_J|F|>0. Let C>0 be the absolute constant in Nazarov’s inequality M\le e^{AL}(CL/|B|)^{n-1}\sup_B|F| for measurable B\subset J of positive Lebesgue measure. For every measurable E\subset J of positive measure and every r>0, \int_E|F|^r\ge\frac{|E|}{2L}\left(\frac{|E|}{2CL}\right)^{r(n-1)}e^{-rAL}\int_J|F|^r.

Proof 1: Choose a half-measure threshold and integrate

Applying Nazarov's inequality to E_t=\{x\in J:|F(x)|\leq t\}, t>0, leads to

M\leq e^{AL}\left(\frac{CL}{|E_t|}\right)^{n-1} \sup_{x\in E_t}|F(x)| \leq e^{AL}\left(\frac{CL}{|E_t|}\right)^{n-1}t,

thus

|E_t|\leq CL\left(\frac{te^{AL}}{M}\right)^{1/(n-1)}.

If |E_t|=0, the last estimate holds directly.

Now fix a set E and choose t such that |E_t|\leq\frac12|E|, namely

t=\left(\frac{|E|}{2CL}\right)^{n-1}e^{-AL}M.

Note that this implies:

  1. |E\setminus E_t|\geq\frac12|E|.
  2. For all x\in E\setminus E_t, |F(x)|>t.

Now

\begin{aligned} \int_E|F(x)|^r\,dx &\geq\int_{E\setminus E_t}|F(x)|^r\,dx\\ &\geq t^r\frac12|E|\\ &=\frac12|E|\left(\frac{|E|}{2CL}\right)^{r(n-1)}e^{-rAL}M^r\\ &\geq\frac12\frac{|E|}{L} \left(\frac{|E|}{2CL}\right)^{r(n-1)}e^{-rAL} \int_J|F(x)|^r\,dx, \end{aligned}

which completes the proof.
