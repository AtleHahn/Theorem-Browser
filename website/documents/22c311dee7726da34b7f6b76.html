Proof 1: Exponent-one substitution and expansion — outline

Put t=1 in the Schur inequality x^t(x-y)(x-z)+y^t(y-z)(y-x)+z^t(z-x)(z-y)\ge0. Expanding the three products and gathering the cubic and mixed terms gives the displayed inequality.
