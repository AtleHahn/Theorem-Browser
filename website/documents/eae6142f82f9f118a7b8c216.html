Statement

Let A,B be Hermitian d\times d complex matrices, d\ge1, and let e^A denote the matrix exponential. \operatorname{tr}(e^{A+B})\le\operatorname{tr}(e^Ae^B).

Proof 1: Apply Golden’s bound along the Lie product limit

Use Golden’s dyadic trace-power inequality as an explicit prerequisite: for positive semidefinite Hermitian matrices X,Y of the same finite size, integers n\ge0 and 0\le j\le n, \operatorname{tr}((XY)^{2^n})\le\operatorname{tr}((X^{2^j}Y^{2^j})^{2^{n-j}}).

The Lie product formula and continuity of trace give \operatorname{tr}(e^{A+B})=\lim_{n\to\infty}\operatorname{tr}((e^{A/2^n}e^{B/2^n})^{2^n}). The factors are positive definite. Golden’s trace-power inequality bounds each displayed trace by \operatorname{tr}(e^Ae^B). Taking the limit proves the result.
