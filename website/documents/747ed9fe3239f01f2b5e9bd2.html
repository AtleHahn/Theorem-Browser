Statement

Let M be a connected smooth n-manifold without boundary, let 0\le k\le n, and let e_0,e_1:D^k\to M be smooth embeddings of the closed unit disc D^k=\{x\in\mathbb R^k:\|x\|\le1\}. If k=n, suppose the embeddings are equioriented. Then there is a smooth ambient isotopy H_t:M\to M, 0\le t\le1, with

H_0=\operatorname{id}_M,\qquad H_1\circ e_0=e_1.

A smooth embedding of the closed disc is smooth up to its boundary, is an immersion, and is a homeomorphism onto its image. An ambient isotopy is a smoothly varying family of diffeomorphisms of the whole manifold. For k=n, each derivative de_j at the disc center transports the standard orientation of \mathbb R^n to a local orientation at e_j(0). Equioriented means that these two local orientations are joined by continuous transport along some path in M from e_0(0) to e_1(0). When M is orientable this is equivalently that the two embeddings have the same orientation sign for a chosen orientation of M. No orientation condition is imposed when k<n.
