Statement

Let n\ge1 and let \mu be a finite nonnegative Borel measure on \mathbb R^n. Suppose that for every direction v\in\mathbb R^n there is a finite constant C(v) such that

\left|\int_{\mathbb R^n}\nabla\varphi(y)\cdot v\,d\mu(y)\right|\le C(v)\sup_{y\in\mathbb R^n}|\varphi(y)|

for every smooth real function \varphi with compact support. Then \mu is absolutely continuous with respect to Lebesgue measure: every Borel set of Lebesgue measure zero has \mu-measure zero. Borel sets form the sigma-algebra generated by open sets, and finite means \mu(\mathbb R^n)<\infty. The constant depends on the direction but not on the test function.
