Statement

Let X be a compact smooth manifold without boundary, let E,F be smooth complex vector bundles on X, and let D:C^\infty(X,E)\to C^\infty(X,F) be an elliptic differential operator. Ellipticity means that its principal symbol \sigma_D(x,\xi):E_x\to F_x, obtained from its highest-order terms, is invertible whenever \xi\in T_x^*X is nonzero. Then

\operatorname{ind}_{\mathrm{an}}D=\operatorname{ind}_{\mathrm{top}}[\sigma_D].

The analytical index is the finite integer \dim\ker D-\dim\operatorname{coker}D, equivalently \dim\ker D-\dim\ker D^* after choosing metrics to define the adjoint.

The topological index is defined as follows. The symbol determines a compactly supported complex K-theory class [\sigma_D]\in K_c^0(T^*X): the pullbacks of E,F are identified by the symbol away from the zero section. Choose an embedding i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^N. The K-theory pushforward for the induced cotangent embedding sends this class to i_![\sigma_D]\in K_c^0(T^*\mathbb R^N). This pushforward uses the Thom class of the normal bundle, whose doubled real normal directions have their canonical complex structure. Bott periodicity identifies K_c^0(T^*\mathbb R^N)=K_c^0(\mathbb R^{2N}) with \mathbb Z, with the Bott class sent to 1. The resulting integer is \operatorname{ind}_{\mathrm{top}}[\sigma_D] and is independent of the embedding. A metric identifies tangent and cotangent bundles if the tangent-bundle convention is used.

Proof availability

Proof sketches and limitations

The source describes three approaches. The cobordism approach interprets both indices as homomorphisms and compares them on generators. The K-theory approach uses an index-preserving pushforward to reduce to a sphere and then a point. The heat-equation approach pairs the nonzero spectra of D^*D and DD^* and, for positive-order operators, obtains

\operatorname{ind}D=\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tD^*D})-\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tDD^*}),\qquad t>0.

It then proposes evaluating the small-time expansion.

These are source outlines, not a complete proof here. The cobordism compatibility and generator computations, the precise K-theory reduction, and the heat-kernel cancellation identifying the topological class are not supplied. In particular, the source's assertion that every operator on a sphere is a pushforward from a point suppresses the necessary symbol-class formulation. The heat-trace identity alone does not establish the index theorem.
