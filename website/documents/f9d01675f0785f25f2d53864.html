Statement

Let X be a real or complex vector space, M\subseteq X a subspace, and let p on M and q on X be seminorms with p\le q|_M. There exists a seminorm P on X with P|_M=p and P\le q. One construction is the Minkowski functional P(x)=\inf\{t>0:x\in tS\} of S=\operatorname{conv}(\{m\in M:p(m)\le1\}\cup\{x\in X:q(x)\le1\}).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A seminorm satisfies the norm inequalities but may vanish on nonzero vectors. Convex hull and the displayed infimum gauge use linear combinations and real infima, so these are direct extensions of linear algebra/analysis without topological duality.

Proof 1: Extending a seminorm below an ambient seminorm — outline

Let {\displaystyle S} be the convex hull of {\displaystyle \{m\in M:p(m)\leq 1\}\cup \{x\in X:q(x)\leq 1\}.} Because {\displaystyle S} is an absorbing disk in {\displaystyle X,} its Minkowski functional {\displaystyle P} is a seminorm. Then {\displaystyle p=P} on {\displaystyle M} and {\displaystyle P\leq q} on {\displaystyle X.}

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Form a convex hull of the two seminorm balls and apply the stated gauge criterion. Undergraduate real analysis and linear algebra suffice after the related definitions of seminorm, balanced absorbing set and its explicitly given infimum gauge; no locally convex topology or duality is used.
