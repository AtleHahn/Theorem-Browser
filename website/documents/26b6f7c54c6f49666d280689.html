Statement

Let A be a unital Banach *-algebra and a\in A. If a unital *-homomorphism from the commutative polynomial *-algebra \mathbb C[z,\overline z] to A sends z to a and \overline z to a^*, then aa^*=a^*a.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses a general Banach star-algebra, abstract polynomial-algebra homomorphisms and operator normality.

Proof 1: Commutativity of the polynomial domain

The polynomial algebra \mathbb C[z,\overline z] is commutative. Thus its images a and a^* under a homomorphism commute, which is exactly normality.
