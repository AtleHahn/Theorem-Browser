Statement

The groups A_5 and \operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_5) are isomorphic, where \mathbb F_5=\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z with modular arithmetic.

Here A_n is the group of even permutations of \{1,\ldots,n\} under composition. For a field F, \operatorname{SL}_2(F) is the group of 2\times2 matrices of determinant 1, and

\operatorname{PSL}_2(F)=\operatorname{SL}_2(F)/\{aI:a\in F,\ a^2=1\}.

Here I is the 2\times2 identity matrix. An isomorphism is a bijection preserving group multiplication.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory and linear algebra suffice; the scalar quotient and arithmetic modulo five are explicit and require no extension-field theory.

Proof 1: Matching faithful six-point actions (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Theorem 3.34, pp. 86–87; Corollary 3.35, p. 88.).

The kernel of the action on lines is scalar: fixing the two coordinate lines makes a matrix diagonal, and fixing their sum makes its diagonal entries equal. Corollary 3.35 then identifies the index-two determinant-square subgroup with A_5.
