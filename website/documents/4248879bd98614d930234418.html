Statement

For any two prescribed positive volumes v_1,v_2 in Euclidean three-space, the least total surface area enclosing and separating two regions of those volumes is attained by the standard double bubble. Every minimizing enclosure is a standard double bubble, up to Euclidean motions and relabeling the regions when their volumes agree.

The standard double bubble consists of three spherical caps meeting along one circle at dihedral angles 120^\circ: two outer caps separate the regions from the exterior, and the third separates the two regions. When v_1=v_2, the separating cap is a flat disk.

The minimization allows nonsmooth and disconnected competitors in the rectifiable-surface formulation of enclosures; an interface shared by the two regions is counted once. Equivalently, using sets of finite perimeter, take disjoint measurable regions E_1,E_2\subset\mathbb R^3 with Lebesgue volumes v_1,v_2, and put E_0=\mathbb R^3\setminus(E_1\cup E_2). Minimize

\frac12\bigl(P(E_0)+P(E_1)+P(E_2)\bigr).

Here P(E) is the total variation of the distributional gradient of the indicator of E (finite perimeter); this measures boundary area in the nonsmooth setting. Regions and uniqueness are understood up to sets of volume zero.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The full competitor class includes nonsmooth measurable enclosures. Finite perimeter, distributional derivatives and rectifiability require substantial extra analysis; the elementary spherical picture alone does not justify an undergraduate rating.

Proof availability

Source proof outline and limitations

The article describes existence of a minimizer by compactness of rectifiable currents. A reflection-and-smoothing argument gives rotational symmetry, and structural results reduce nonstandard candidates to bounded collections of toroidal layers. The final step is to show that every such nonstandard candidate is unstable under suitable volume-preserving perturbations made from rotations. Existence and exclusion of all nonstandard minimizers then leave the standard double bubble.

The compactness, regularity and classification results are invoked without proof. The required perturbations and their area estimates are not constructed in the article. This is a roadmap of the source argument, not a complete minimization proof.
