Statement

Let a pinned planar framework have its free coordinates parametrized locally by z\in\mathbb R^m, and let \rho(z) be its vector of squared edge lengths. At a framework q, select k<m edges E' and k coordinates y so that the k\times k partial derivative matrix of \rho_{E'} in y is invertible. Write the other coordinates as x. Near q, the equations \rho_{E'}(x,y)=\rho_{E'}(q) can be solved as y=g(x) with g continuously differentiable.

Proof 1: Apply the implicit function theorem to selected edges

Our continuously differentiable function is the squared-distance map \rho:\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R^{|E|}. Its Jacobian is the rigidity matrix. Consider the subset of edges E'\subset E corresponding to the selected k independent rows of this matrix at q, yielding the submatrix for \rho_{E'}.

The selected k columns are independent. Denote the coordinates in these columns by y=(y_1,\ldots,y_k), and denote the remaining coordinates by x=(x_1,\ldots,x_{m-k}). The k\times k submatrix induced by y_1,\ldots,y_k is invertible, so by the implicit function theorem there exists a continuously differentiable function g such that, near q,

y=g(x),\qquad \rho(x,y)_{E'}=\rho(q)_{E'}.
