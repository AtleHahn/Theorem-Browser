Statement

Let (S,\mu,\eta) and (S',\mu',\eta') be monads on a category C. Thus S,S' are endofunctors, with multiplications \mu:S^2\Rightarrow S, \mu':S'^2\Rightarrow S' and units \eta:1_C\Rightarrow S, \eta':1_C\Rightarrow S'. Let \lambda:SS'\Rightarrow S'S be a distributive law: it is compatible with both monad units and multiplications, meaning

\lambda\circ\eta S'=S'\eta,\qquad \lambda\circ S\eta'=\eta'S,\lambda\circ\mu S'=S'\mu\circ\lambda S\circ S\lambda,\qquad \lambda\circ S\mu'=\mu'S\circ S'\lambda\circ\lambda S'.

Here juxtaposition of functors denotes composition, juxtaposition of a functor and natural transformation denotes whiskering, and \circ composes transformations right to left. Then S'S has monad multiplication and unit

(S'S)^2\xRightarrow{\,S'\lambda S\,}S'^2S^2\xRightarrow{\,\mu'S^2\,}S'S^2\xRightarrow{\,S'\mu\,}S'S, \qquad 1_C\xRightarrow{\eta}S\xRightarrow{\eta'S}S'S.
