Statement

Let X be a connected Hausdorff topological space equipped with an atlas of complex coordinate charts: each chart is a homeomorphism from an open subset of X onto an open subset of \mathbb C, the chart domains cover X, and every transition map is holomorphic. No countability axiom on the topology is assumed.

Then X is second-countable: there is a countable collection \mathcal B of open subsets of X such that every open subset of X is a union of members of \mathcal B.

Review notes

  • The complement of two disjoint closed coordinate disks is connected: paths can be detoured around each disk within the chart. The two boundary values make the harmonic function nonconstant.
