Statement

Let A be the adjacency matrix of a finite simple undirected graph: A_{ij}=1 if vertices i,j are adjacent and A_{ij}=0 otherwise. Then A is orthogonally diagonalizable over \mathbb R, and all its eigenvalues are real.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Adjacency matrices of finite graphs; Real eigenvalues; Orthogonal diagonalization. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Apply the real symmetric spectral theorem

Because the graph is undirected, its adjacency matrix is real symmetric. The spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices therefore gives an orthonormal eigenbasis with real eigenvalues, equivalently an orthogonal diagonalization.
