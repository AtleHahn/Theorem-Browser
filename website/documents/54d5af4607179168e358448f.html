Statement

Every rational root of a monic polynomial of positive degree with integer coefficients is an integer.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement concerns rational solutions of a polynomial with integer coefficients and leading coefficient one.

Mathematical Field
High School Mathematics
Required topics
Monic integer polynomialsRational and integer roots
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Apply the monic denominator condition — outline

Write the root in lowest terms as p/q with q>0. The rational root theorem makes q a divisor of the leading coefficient 1, so q=1.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The rational-root denominator divides the leading coefficient one, hence is one. High-school polynomial algebra suffices.

Routine omissions

If the constant term vanishes, a zero root is already integral; factors x may be removed for any nonzero root. This routine boundary case is implicit in the monic corollary.
