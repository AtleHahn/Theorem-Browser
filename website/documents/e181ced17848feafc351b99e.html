Statement

Let a convex equilateral polygon have area A and common side length a>0. For every interior point P, let d_i be the perpendicular distance to the supporting line of side i. Then \sum_i d_i=2A/a, independent of P.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement relates polygon area, a common side length and perpendicular distances.

Proof 1: Partition into equal-base triangles

The result follows from the triangle-area formula, since the equilateral n-gon can be divided up into n triangles all with equal base, say a, but with different heights. But the sum of all the areas of these triangles equal the area A of the polygon, so A=\tfrac12 a\sum_i d_i. Hence \sum_i d_i=2A/a.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Join the interior point to each vertex and add the resulting equal-base triangle areas. Only elementary Euclidean area and finite sums are needed.
