Statement

Let g be injective and holomorphic on |z|>1, with Laurent expansion g(z)=z+\sum_{j\ge1}b_jz^{-j}. For distinct \zeta,\eta with moduli greater than one, let \ell(u,v) denote the analytic logarithm of (g(u)-g(v))/(u-v) normalized to zero at infinity, with diagonal value \ell(u,u)=\log g'(u). Then

|\ell(\zeta,\zeta)+\ell(\eta,\eta)-2\ell(\zeta,\eta)|\le\log\frac{|1-\zeta\bar\eta|^2}{(|\zeta|^2-1)(|\eta|^2-1)}.

Proof 1: Two-point distortion from Goluzin’s inequalities — outline

Use the source Goluzin inequality

\left|\sum_{i,j}\lambda_i\lambda_j\ell(z_i,z_j)\right|\le\sum_{i,j}\lambda_i\overline{\lambda_j}\log\frac{z_i\overline{z_j}}{z_i\overline{z_j}-1}.

Take n=2, z_1=\zeta, z_2=\eta, and \lambda_1=-\lambda_2=1. The left side is the expression in the statement, which the source writes as the logarithm of g'(\zeta)g'(\eta)(\zeta-\eta)^2/(g(\zeta)-g(\eta))^2. Combining the two diagonal and two conjugate cross terms on the right gives the stated real logarithm.
