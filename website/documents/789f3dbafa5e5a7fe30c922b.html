Statement

There is an absolute constant C>0 such that for every integer n\ge1 and centrally symmetric convex bodies K,L\subseteq\mathbb R^n, there are volume-preserving invertible linear maps \phi,\psi for which, simultaneously for every s,t>0,

\operatorname{vol}_n(s\phi K+t\psi L)^{1/n}\le C\bigl(s\operatorname{vol}_n(K)^{1/n}+t\operatorname{vol}_n(L)^{1/n}\bigr).

One of \phi,\psi can be chosen to be the identity. A convex body is a compact convex set with nonempty interior; central symmetry means symmetry about some point. Volume is n-dimensional Lebesgue measure, volume preserving means absolute determinant 1, and sA+tB=\{sa+tb:a\in A,b\in B\}. The maps may depend on the bodies but not on s,t; C is independent of dimension and bodies.
