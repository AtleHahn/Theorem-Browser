Statement

Let B=(b_{ij})\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}, n\ge1, satisfy b_{ij}\le0 whenever i\ne j. The following conditions are equivalent: there exists a vector x\in\mathbb R^n with x_i\ge0 for all i and (Bx)_i>0 for all i; and \det(b_{ij})_{1\le i,j\le k}>0\qquad(k=1,\ldots,n). Under these conditions, for every d\in\mathbb R^n with nonnegative coordinates, the equation Bx=d has a solution with nonnegative coordinates.
