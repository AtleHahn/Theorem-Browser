Statement

Let n be a positive integer. Let R be a commutative ring and suppose a_0x^n+a_1x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_n=a_0\prod_{j=1}^n(x-r_j) in R[x]. For 1\leq k\leq n, let E_k be the sum of all products of k distinct indexed elements of (r_1,\ldots,r_n). Then a_k=(-1)^ka_0E_k. No division by a_0 is required.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory ring/group theory and linear algebra supply polynomial coefficients, ideals, determinants, bases and matrix products. Elementary transvections are explicitly given elementary row-operation matrices, and division ring means every nonzero element has an inverse. These direct definitions suffice without specialist theory.

Proof 1: Coefficient expansion without division

The factorization is assumed in the statement; no division by a_0 is needed.

Vieta's formulas can be proved by considering the equality

a_0x^n+a_1x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_n =a_0(x-r_1)(x-r_2)\cdots(x-r_n),

expanding the products in the right-hand side, and equating the coefficients of each power of x between the two members of the equation.

Formally, if one expands (x-r_1)(x-r_2)\cdots(x-r_n) and regroups the terms by their degree in x, one gets

\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^{n-k}x^k \left(\sum_{\substack{(\forall i)\ b_i\in\{0,1\}\\b_1+\cdots+b_n=n-k}} r_1^{b_1}\cdots r_n^{b_n}\right),

where the inner sum is exactly the (n-k)th elementary symmetric function (with E_0=1). Thus, comparison of the coefficient of x^{n-k} gives a_k=(-1)^ka_0E_k.
