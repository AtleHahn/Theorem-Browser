Statement

Let M be a connected Riemannian manifold of dimension n\ge2 with sectional curvature at most -1 and with \operatorname{inj}_M(p)=\infty at some point p. Then

\lambda^*(M)\ge\lambda^*(\mathbb H^n(-1)),

where \mathbb H^n(-1) is complete simply connected hyperbolic n-space of constant curvature -1, and for any Riemannian manifold Z,

\lambda^*(Z)=\inf_{0\ne f\in C_c^\infty(Z)}\frac{\int_Z|\nabla f|^2\,dV}{\int_Z|f|^2\,dV}.

Here C_c^\infty(Z) denotes compactly supported smooth real functions, \nabla is the metric gradient and dV is volume. Infinite injectivity radius at p means the exponential map at p gives geodesic normal coordinates on balls of every finite radius.
