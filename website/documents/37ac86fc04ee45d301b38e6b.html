Statement

A Platonic solid is a convex polyhedron in Euclidean three-space whose faces are congruent regular p-gons and at each vertex of which q faces meet. For circumradius R and inradius r, R/r=\tan(\pi/p)\tan(\pi/q). In particular the ratio is symmetric in p,q.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Regular-polyhedron terminology, trigonometric functions and the displayed algebra lie within elementary geometry.

Proof 1: Divide the circumradius and inradius formulas

Use the stated radius formulas for edge length a and interior dihedral angle \theta: R=(a/2)\tan(\pi/q)\tan(\theta/2),\qquad r=(a/2)\cot(\pi/p)\tan(\theta/2). Dividing cancels the common factors and gives the product of tangents, which is unchanged by interchanging p,q.
