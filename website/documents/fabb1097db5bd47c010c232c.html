Statement

Let \zeta denote the meromorphic continuation of \sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s} from \operatorname{Re}s>1. Suppose there exists a self-adjoint operator on a complex Hilbert space such that each nontrivial zero \rho of \zeta can be written \rho=1/2+i\lambda with \lambda an eigenvalue of that operator. Then \operatorname{Re}\rho=1/2 for all such zeros.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The conditional representation uses a self-adjoint Hilbert-space operator and nontrivial zeros of a meromorphic function.

Proof 1: A self-adjoint spectral realization suffices for the Riemann hypothesis

Eigenvalues of a self-adjoint operator are real. Apply this to the assumed spectral representation of each zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use reality of self-adjoint eigenvalues in the assumed representation of zeta zeros. Meromorphic zeta continuation together with potentially unbounded self-adjoint Hilbert operators exceeds the small-extension allowance; retain conservatively despite the immediate conclusion.
