Statement

Every nontrivial knot K in the 3-sphere has Property P: every nontrivial Dehn surgery on K produces a 3-manifold with nontrivial fundamental group. A knot is a smoothly embedded circle, and nontrivial means not ambient isotopic to the standard round circle (the unknot). Dehn surgery removes an open solid-torus tubular neighborhood of K and glues in a solid torus along the resulting boundary torus. It is nontrivial when the meridian of the new solid torus is glued to a slope other than the original meridional slope. A slope is an unoriented isotopy class of essential simple closed curves on that boundary torus; the original meridian bounds a disk in the removed tubular neighborhood. Nontrivial fundamental group means that some loop cannot be contracted to a point, so the resulting manifold is not simply connected.
