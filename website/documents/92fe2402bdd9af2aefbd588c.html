Statement

Let V be a holomorphic vector bundle of rank r on a smooth connected compact complex curve X of genus g. Set h^i(V)=\dim_{\mathbb C}H^i(X,V) and \deg V=\int_Xc_1(V). Then h^0(V)-h^1(V)=\deg V+r(1-g).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Holomorphic vector bundles; Chern classes; Sheaf cohomology on curves. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Multiply the curve Chern character and Todd class

On a curve \operatorname{ch}(V)=r+c_1(V) and \operatorname{td}(TX)=1+c_1(TX)/2. In the Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch product only degree two contributes, giving h^0(V)-h^1(V)=\int_Xc_1(V)+(r/2)\int_Xc_1(TX). Since \int_Xc_1(TX)=2-2g, the stated expression follows.
