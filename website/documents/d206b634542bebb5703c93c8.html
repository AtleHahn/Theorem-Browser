Statement

Let M be a connected complete Riemannian manifold of dimension n with nonpositive sectional curvature. For every p\in M, the exponential map

\exp_p:T_pM\longrightarrow M

is a covering map. Consequently, the universal Riemannian covering manifold \widetilde M is diffeomorphic to \mathbb R^n: for every \widetilde p\in\widetilde M, its exponential map \exp_{\widetilde p}:T_{\widetilde p}\widetilde M\to\widetilde M is a diffeomorphism. In particular, if M is simply connected, \exp_p is a diffeomorphism for every p\in M.
