Statement

For a smooth variety X over a field, its Picard group of line bundles satisfies

\operatorname{Pic}(X)\cong H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^*),

where \mathcal O_X^* is the sheaf of invertible regular functions and cohomology is in the Zariski topology.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The Picard group, line bundles and Zariski sheaf cohomology require substantial algebraic geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Specialize Bloch’s formula to codimension one — outline

Use Bloch’s formula

\operatorname{CH}^q(X)\cong H^q(X,K_q(\mathcal O_X)),

where \operatorname{CH}^q is the codimension-q Chow group and K_q(\mathcal O_X) is the sheaf associated to U\mapsto K_q(U) on Zariski open subsets. At q=1, the standard identifications \operatorname{CH}^1(X)\cong\operatorname{Pic}(X) and K_1(\mathcal O_X)\cong\mathcal O_X^* give the stated formula.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Specialize Bloch in codimension one and identify Chow and K-theory sheaves with the Picard and unit objects. Smooth varieties, line bundles, algebraic K-theory and Zariski sheaf cohomology require extensive graduate algebraic geometry.
