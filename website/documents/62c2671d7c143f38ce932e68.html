Statement

For every finite simple group G, its outer automorphism group \operatorname{Out}(G)=\operatorname{Aut}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G) is solvable. A simple group is a nontrivial group whose only normal subgroups are itself and the identity subgroup. The group \operatorname{Aut}(G) consists of automorphisms under composition; \operatorname{Inn}(G) consists of maps x\mapsto gxg^{-1}. A group H is solvable if its derived series H^{(0)}=H, H^{(j+1)}=[H^{(j)},H^{(j)}] eventually becomes the identity subgroup, where [K,K] is generated by aba^{-1}b^{-1} with a,b\in K.

The statement uses finite groups, inner automorphisms and derived series. The selected proof requires the classification of finite simple groups and the structure of automorphisms of Lie-type groups. The supporting argument uses root subgroups, Bruhat decomposition, Chevalley bases and Dynkin-diagram automorphisms before reducing to elementary solvable extensions.
