Statement

Let X be a real or complex Banach space and K\subseteq X weakly compact. Then the norm-closed convex hull of K is weakly compact. The convex hull consists of finite sums \sum_{j=1}^m a_jx_j with x_j\in K, a_j\ge0 and \sum_j a_j=1. The weak topology is the coarsest topology making every bounded linear functional X\to\mathbb R (or \mathbb C) continuous; weak compactness is compactness in that topology. A Banach space is a complete normed vector space.

Level justification

Weak topology generated by a dual space and its compactness differ substantially from norm topology; cumulative concepts warrant beyond-undergraduate classification.
