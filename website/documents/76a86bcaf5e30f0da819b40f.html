Statement

Let K be a number field, that is, a finite extension of \mathbb Q. Let r_1 be the number of field embeddings K\hookrightarrow\mathbb R, and let r_2 be the number of complex-conjugate pairs of nonreal embeddings K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C.

Let O be any order in K: a subring containing 1, finitely generated as an additive abelian group, whose \mathbb Q-linear span is K. Its unit group

O^\times=\{u\in O:\text{there is }v\in O\text{ with }uv=1\}

is finitely generated and has rank r_1+r_2-1. More explicitly,

O^\times\cong\mu(O)\times\mathbb Z^{\,r_1+r_2-1},

where \mu(O) is the finite cyclic group of roots of unity belonging to O (elements \zeta\in O with \zeta^m=1 for some integer m\geq1).

In particular this holds for the maximal order O_K, the ring of all elements of K satisfying a monic polynomial with integer coefficients.
