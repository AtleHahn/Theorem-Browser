Statement

For a finite sequence x_1,\ldots,x_N of distinct real numbers, let a_i and b_i be the respective maximum lengths of increasing and decreasing subsequences ending at index i. Then all pairs (a_i,b_i) are distinct.

Level

Proof 1: Compare two sequence values

If i<j and x_i<x_j, then a_i<a_j. If i<j and x_i>x_j, then b_i<b_j. The values are distinct, so one of these cases applies and the two labels differ.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Append the later value to either the earlier increasing or decreasing subsequence, according to their comparison. At least one of the two integer lengths strictly increases.
