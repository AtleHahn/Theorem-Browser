Statement

For 1<p<\infty, H denotes the Hilbert transform on L^p(\mathbb R), with sign convention Hf(t)=\pi^{-1}\operatorname{p.v.}\int_{\mathbb R}f(\tau)/(t-\tau)\,d\tau. There is C_p<\infty such that \|Hf\|_p\le C_p\|f\|_p for every f\in L^p(\mathbb R).

Proof 1: Lp boundedness of the Hilbert transform — outline

The Hilbert transform is of weak type (1,1) and is bounded on L^2 by its bounded Fourier multiplier. Marcinkiewicz interpolation gives boundedness for 1<p<2. The duality relation \langle Hu,v\rangle=\langle u,-Hv\rangle then gives boundedness for the conjugate exponents p>2.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Interpolate the weak-L1 and L2 bounds, then use duality and antiselfadjointness for p>2. Weak type estimates, interpolation and Lp duality require graduate harmonic analysis.
