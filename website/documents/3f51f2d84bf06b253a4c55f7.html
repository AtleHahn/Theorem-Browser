Statement

Contexts are finite multisets of formulas. Weakening adds any formula to either side of a sequent and contraction replaces two copies by one. In the presence of these structural rules, the two left-disjunction rules

\frac{\Gamma,A\vdash\Delta\quad\Gamma,B\vdash\Delta}{\Gamma,A\lor B\vdash\Delta}

and

\frac{\Gamma,A\vdash\Delta\quad\Sigma,B\vdash\Pi}{\Gamma,\Sigma,A\lor B\vdash\Delta,\Pi}

are interderivable: either can simulate an application of the other.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The two inference patterns are displayed, and contexts, weakening and contraction are described as finite multiset operations. Simulating either pattern is elementary symbolic manipulation.

Proof 1: Weaken contexts and contract repetitions — outline

Use weakening and contraction to pass between the two forms. Weakening supplies shared contexts when starting with distinct contexts; contraction removes duplicated contexts when starting with a shared one. Thus both presentations derive the same disjunction inference.
