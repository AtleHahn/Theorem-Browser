Statement

For real \varphi, \tan(\varphi/2)=\frac{\sin\varphi}{1+\cos\varphi}=\frac{1-\cos\varphi}{\sin\varphi}, with each equality restricted to its nonzero denominators.

Level

Proof 1: Stereographic similarity and rationalization — outline

Draw the line through (\cos\varphi,\sin\varphi) on the unit circle and (-1,0), meeting the y-axis at ordinate t. The elementary angle relation in this stereographic construction gives t=\tan(\varphi/2). By similarity of triangles,

\frac{t}{\sin\varphi}=\frac1{1+\cos\varphi}.

It follows that

t=\frac{\sin\varphi}{1+\cos\varphi} =\frac{\sin\varphi(1-\cos\varphi)} {(1+\cos\varphi)(1-\cos\varphi)} =\frac{1-\cos\varphi}{\sin\varphi}.

These divisions apply when their denominators are nonzero. At the nonsingular zero-sine case of the first expression, both sides are zero.

Level justification

Use the chord half-angle and similar triangles, then rationalize the quotient with the sine-square identity. School geometry and trigonometry suffice.
