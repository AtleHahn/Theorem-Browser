Statement

Let C_1,C_2,C_3,C_4 be circles in the Euclidean plane passing through a point M. Assume each pair meets at a second point P_{ij}, that these six second intersection points are distinct and each belongs to exactly two of the circles, and that no three of them are collinear. For each three-element subset I\subset\{1,2,3,4\} let K_I be the circle through the three points P_{ij} with i,j\in I. Then all four circles K_I pass through a common point.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Finite circle and point constructions are elementary once the explicitly stated incidence conditions are understood.
