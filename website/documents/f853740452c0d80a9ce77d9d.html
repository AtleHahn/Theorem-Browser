Statement

Let A,B be bounded linear operators on a real or complex Hilbert space H, and suppose \operatorname{ran}A\subseteq\operatorname{ran}B. There is a unique bounded linear operator C satisfying

A=BC,\qquad \operatorname{ran}C\subseteq\overline{\operatorname{ran}B^*}.

This operator also satisfies

\ker C=\ker A,\qquad \|C\|^2=\inf\{\lambda^2:\lambda\ge0,\ AA^*\le\lambda^2BB^*\}.

Here B^* and A^* are adjoints, the bar denotes norm closure, \|C\|=\sup_{\|x\|\le1}\|Cx\|, and S\le T for self-adjoint operators means \langle(T-S)x,x\rangle\ge0 for every x\in H. The infimum is over the nonnegative majorization constants appearing in the factorization criterion; this convention includes A=B=0, when the infimum and C are zero.
