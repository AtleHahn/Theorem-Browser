Statement

Let G be a finite simple graph drawn in the plane without edge crossings, and let C be a Hamiltonian cycle of G, meaning a cycle visiting every vertex exactly once. The faces are the connected components of the complement of the drawing, including its unbounded component. For each k\geq3, let f_k and g_k count the faces with k boundary edges that lie respectively inside and outside the simple closed curve C.

Then

\sum_{k\geq3}(k-2)(f_k-g_k)=0.
Level
High school only
Level justification

Vertices, edges, a cycle visiting all vertices, inside/outside regions and the finite weighted sum are elementary concepts made explicit locally. No topological proof or graph theory course is needed to understand the identity.
