Statement

Let U=\varinjlim U(N), O=\varinjlim O(N) and \mathrm{Sp}=\varinjlim\mathrm{Sp}(N) be the stable classical groups under the standard inclusions. Use identity basepoints, based loop spaces \Omega, and the standard based homotopy types for classifying spaces BU,BO,B\mathrm{Sp}. For every integer k\ge1,

\pi_k(U)\cong\pi_{k+2}(U),\quad\pi_k(O)\cong\pi_{k+8}(O),\quad\pi_k(\mathrm{Sp})\cong\pi_{k+8}(\mathrm{Sp}).

The corresponding degree-zero statements hold as bijections of path-component sets.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Stable classical groups; Homotopy groups; Path components. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Loop shifting and composition of four-degree relations

For the complex case, use the Bott loop equivalences

\Omega U\simeq\mathbb Z\times BU,\qquad \Omega(\mathbb Z\times BU)\simeq U.

These give \Omega^2U\simeq U and hence \pi_k(U)\cong\pi_{k+2}(U) by the homotopy-group shift for loop spaces.

For the real and quaternionic cases, use the Bott isomorphisms

\pi_k(O)\cong\pi_{k+4}(\mathrm{Sp}),\qquad \pi_k(\mathrm{Sp})\cong\pi_{k+4}(O).

These isomorphisms intertwine to give the 8-fold periodicity results:

\begin{aligned} \pi_k(O)&\cong\pi_{k+8}(O),\\ \pi_k(\mathrm{Sp})&\cong\pi_{k+8}(\mathrm{Sp}). \end{aligned}

At k=0, use the corresponding bijections of path-component sets.
