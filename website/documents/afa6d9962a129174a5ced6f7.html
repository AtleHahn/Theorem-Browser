Statement

Let p be an odd prime and let a,b,c be positive integers satisfying a^p+b^p=c^p. The affine curve y^2=x(x-a^p)(x+b^p) is nonsingular over \mathbb Q, and its projective completion is a genus-one elliptic curve over \mathbb Q.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The integer inputs are elementary, but the constructed curve and its projective genus and smoothness are algebraic-geometry objects.

Proof 1: Construct the displayed cubic — outline

Associate to the triple the curve y^2=x(x-a^p)(x+b^p). This is the Frey cubic; its nonsingular genus-one projective completion is an elliptic curve over \mathbb Q. The standard squarefree-cubic criterion supplies these properties once the roots 0,a^p,-b^p are distinct.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Distinct roots are an elementary check, but the conclusion uses nonsingular affine curves, smooth projective completion, genus and elliptic curves over the rationals. These are cumulative algebraic-geometric prerequisites.
