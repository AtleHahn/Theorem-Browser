Statement

Let (A,\mathfrak m) be a commutative Noetherian local ring with identity, complete in its maximal-ideal topology, and put k=A/\mathfrak m. Noetherian means all ideals are finitely generated, local means there is a unique maximal ideal, and complete means A\to\varprojlim_r A/\mathfrak m^r is an isomorphism. If A contains a field, then A\cong k[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]]/I for some integer n\ge0 and ideal I. Here k[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]] is the ring of formal power series; no analytic convergence is required.
