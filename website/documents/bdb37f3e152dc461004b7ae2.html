Statement

For n\ge1, let \gamma_n be the probability measure on \mathbb R^n with density (2\pi)^{-n/2}e^{-\|x\|^2/2} relative to Lebesgue measure. For a measurable A\subseteq\mathbb R^n put A_\varepsilon=\{x:\operatorname{dist}(x,A)\le\varepsilon\}, where \operatorname{dist}(x,A)=\inf_{a\in A}\|x-a\| and the infimum over the empty set is +\infty. Let \varphi(t)=(2\pi)^{-1/2}e^{-t^2/2} and \Phi(t)=\int_{-\infty}^t\varphi(u)\,du. Then \liminf_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\frac{\gamma_n(A_\varepsilon)-\gamma_n(A)}{\varepsilon}\ge\varphi\!\left(\Phi^{-1}(\gamma_n(A))\right). At Gaussian measures 0 and 1, the expression on the right is defined to be 0 by continuity. For each prescribed measure strictly between 0 and 1, half-spaces attain the bound, and thus minimize this Gaussian boundary measure.
