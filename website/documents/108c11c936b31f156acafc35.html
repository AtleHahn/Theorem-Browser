Statement

On \{(x,r):r>0\} and \{(x,y,r):r>0\} define \Delta_2=-r^2(\partial_x^2+\partial_r^2),\quad\Delta_3=-r^2(\partial_x^2+\partial_y^2+\partial_r^2)+r\partial_r. For smooth f(x,r) set M_1f(x,y,r)=r^{1/2}f(x,r). Then \Delta_3M_1f=M_1(\Delta_2+3/4)f.

Proof 1: Hadamard lifting intertwines hyperbolic Laplacians (outline)

Insert the source’s lift M_1f=r^{1/2}f into the displayed differential operator. It is independent of y; differentiating in r and combining the first-derivative terms cancels the terms involving f_r that are not in \Delta_2. The remaining zero-order term is (3/4)r^{1/2}f, giving the asserted intertwining identity.

Level justification

Insert the explicit r^(1/2) lift into the two explicitly defined coordinate operators and cancel derivatives by the product rule. Undergraduate multivariable calculus suffices; no abstract hyperbolic geometry is used.
