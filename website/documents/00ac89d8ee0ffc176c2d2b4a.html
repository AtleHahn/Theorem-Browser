Statement

For a smooth projective complex variety X and a vector bundle E,

\chi(X,E)=\int_X\operatorname{ch}(E)\operatorname{td}(TX),

where \chi(X,E)=\sum_i(-1)^i\dim_{\mathbb C}H^i(X,E) and the integral denotes the degree of the codimension-\dim X component of the rational characteristic-class product.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Complex projective varieties; Coherent cohomology; Chern character; Todd class. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch by pushforward to a point — outline

Specialize the source Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch formula \operatorname{ch}(f_!E)\operatorname{td}(TY)=f_*(\operatorname{ch}(E)\operatorname{td}(TX)) to the map from X to a point over \mathbb C. The left side is the alternating dimension of cohomology, since the Todd class of a point is one; pushforward on the right takes the degree.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Specialize GRR to the map to a point and interpret the pushforwards. Vector bundles, coherent cohomology, Chern characters and Todd classes require graduate algebraic geometry.
