Statement

Let n\geq2, let A be a real symmetric positive-definite n-by-n matrix, let a\in\mathbb R^n, and let c\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}. Use the shape-matrix convention

E(A,a)=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:(x-a)^TA^{-1}(x-a)\leq1\},

and retain the central half

E^-=E(A,a)\cap\{x:c^T(x-a)\leq0\}.

The unique minimum-volume ellipsoid containing E^- is E(A',a'), where

b=\frac{Ac}{\sqrt{c^TAc}},\qquad a'=a-\frac{b}{n+1},\qquad A'=\frac{n^2}{n^2-1}\left(A-\frac{2}{n+1}bb^T\right).

The square root is positive, superscript T denotes transpose, and positive definite means v^TAv>0 for every nonzero real vector v. Volume is n-dimensional Euclidean volume. The minimization is over all full-dimensional ellipsoids containing the retained half.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and multivariable calculus suffice; the shape convention and half-space are explicit quadratic and linear inequalities with a finite matrix formula.
