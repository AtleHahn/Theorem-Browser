Statement

Let \pi(x) be the number of primes at most the real number x. For every fixed real \lambda>1,

\limsup_{x\to\infty}\frac{\pi(x+(\log x)^\lambda)-\pi(x)}{(\log x)^{\lambda-1}}>1,\qquad \liminf_{x\to\infty}\frac{\pi(x+(\log x)^\lambda)-\pi(x)}{(\log x)^{\lambda-1}}<1.

Here x>1 is real and \log is the natural logarithm. In particular, this normalized count of primes in short intervals has no limit.
