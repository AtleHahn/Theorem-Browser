Statement

A real or complex Banach space X is reflexive if and only if every continuous linear functional f∈X′ attains its norm on the closed unit ball: some a with ||a||≤1 satisfies |f(a)|=||f||. Equivalently a may be chosen with f(a)=||f||.

Proof 1: Apply the compactness criterion to the closed unit ball — outline

Let B={a∈X:||a||≤1}. Use the source-stated characterization that X is reflexive exactly when B is weakly compact, and James’s compactness criterion on this closed unit ball: B is weakly compact exactly when each f∈X′ attains the supremum of |f| on B. Since \|f\|=\sup_{a\in B}|f(a)|, these conditions give the norm-attainment equivalence. If f(a) has modulus ||f|| and is nonzero, multiplying a by a scalar of modulus one makes f(a)=||f||; if f=0 there is nothing to adjust.
