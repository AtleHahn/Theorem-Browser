Let G be a simply connected simple algebraic group over a number field, with simply connected in the algebraic-group sense (no proper algebraic covering). Its Tamagawa number \tau(G) equals 1.

Proof 1: Kottwitz reduction and the final Hasse-principle case (outline)

Kottwitz proved the Tamagawa-number-one conclusion for all groups in the stated class satisfying the Hasse principle, which was known for all such groups without E_8 factors. Chernousov removed this restriction by proving the Hasse principle for the E_8 case, thus completing the proof.
