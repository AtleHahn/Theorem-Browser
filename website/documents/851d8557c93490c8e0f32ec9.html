Statement

Assume Wilkie’s existential normal-form theorem: every first-order formula in the real ordered exponential field \mathbb R_{\mathrm{exp}}=(\mathbb R,+,-,\cdot,<,0,1,\exp) is equivalent, in its complete theory, to an existential formula. Then \operatorname{Th}(\mathbb R_{\mathrm{exp}}) is model complete, meaning that every embedding between its models is elementary.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The conclusion concerns elementary embeddings and the first-order theory of the real exponential structure.

Proof 1: Existential-form criterion

The normal-form theorem supplies an existential equivalent for every formula. Apply the model-completeness criterion that a theory is model complete if every formula is equivalent modulo that theory to an existential formula. This is the model-completeness consequence stated in the source.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the existential-form criterion to the complete theory of the exponential field. Understanding equivalence modulo a theory, elementary embeddings and model completeness requires graduate model theory even though the implication is immediate.
