Statement

Let X be a smooth projective complex surface, K a canonical divisor and D a divisor. Write \chi(D)=\sum_{i=0}^2(-1)^i\dim H^i(X,\mathcal O(D)), and use a dot for intersection numbers. For a line bundle L, write h^j(L)=\dim_{\mathbb C}H^j(X,L). Put r=h^0(\mathcal O(D))-1, n=D\cdot D, \pi=1+(D\cdot D+K\cdot D)/2, p_a=\chi(0)-1 and i=h^0(\mathcal O(K-D)). Then s=r-(n-\pi+p_a+1-i)=h^1(\mathcal O(D))\ge0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Compare the classical parameters using Serre duality — outline

Compare the classical expression with \chi(D)=\chi(0)+(D\cdot D-D\cdot K)/2 and use Serre duality to replace h^2(\mathcal O(D)) with h^0(\mathcal O(K-D))=i. The difference of the two sides is h^1(\mathcal O(D)).
