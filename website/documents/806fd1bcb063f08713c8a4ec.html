Statement

Let (M,g) be a smooth pseudo-Riemannian manifold and X a smooth vector field. Identify fields and one-forms using g, let \nabla be the Levi-Civita connection, and set (\Delta X)_i=\nabla^p\nabla_pX_i, \operatorname{div}X=\nabla^pX_p, and (\operatorname{div}T)_i=\nabla^pT_{ip}. With the curvature convention

\nabla^p\nabla_iX_p-\nabla_i\nabla^pX_p=\operatorname{Ric}_{ip}X^p,

one has

\Delta X=-\nabla(\operatorname{div}X)+\operatorname{div}(\mathcal L_Xg)-\operatorname{Ric}(X,\cdot).

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Contracting the Ricci commutation formula (outline)

As an application of the Ricci commutation identities, the formula

\Delta X=-\nabla(\operatorname{div}X)+\operatorname{div}(\mathcal L_Xg)-\operatorname{Ric}(X,\cdot)

holds for any vector field X on a pseudo-Riemannian manifold.

In an alternative notation, this says that

\nabla^p\nabla_pX_i=-\nabla_i\nabla^pX_p+\nabla^p(\nabla_iX_p+\nabla_pX_i)-\operatorname{Ric}_{ip}X^p,

where (\mathcal L_Xg)_{ip}=\nabla_iX_p+\nabla_pX_i and the contracted Ricci commutation convention is the one specified in the statement.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The indexed rearrangement is short, but its meaning depends on a pseudo-Riemannian metric, Levi-Civita differentiation, index contraction and curvature commutation. These are graduate differential-geometric prerequisites.
