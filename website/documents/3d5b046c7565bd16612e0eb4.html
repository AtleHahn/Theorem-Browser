Statement

Let T>0 be a fixed rotation time and r>0 a constant discount rate. If each harvest at times T,2T,\ldots yields the same value pf(T), where p is the constant price and f(T) the timber stock, its discounted present value is PV=pf(T)/(e^{rT}-1).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Geometric series; Exponential discounting. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Sum the discounted harvest geometric series

The source sums the discounted harvests as PV=pf(T)e^{-rT}(1+e^{-rT}+e^{-2rT}+\cdots)=\frac{pf(T)}{e^{rT}-1}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The specified discounted payments form a geometric series with ratio exp(-rT) strictly between zero and one. Sum it and simplify; standard infinite-series calculus suffices.
