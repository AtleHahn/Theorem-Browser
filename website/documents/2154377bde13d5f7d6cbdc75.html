Statement

Let C be a smooth projective curve of genus g\geq2 over a number field K, embedded in its Jacobian J over an algebraic closure \overline K. Let L be a symmetric ample line bundle on J, and let \widehat h_L be its associated Néron–Tate height. There exists \varepsilon>0 such that

\{x\in C(\overline K):\widehat h_L(x)<\varepsilon\}

is finite, where the height of x is taken after the chosen embedding into J.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Jacobians, ample line bundles and canonical heights require substantial algebraic and arithmetic geometry beyond the undergraduate baseline, cumulatively far beyond brief reading.
