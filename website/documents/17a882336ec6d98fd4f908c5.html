Statement

If every prime number is a sum of four integer squares, then every nonnegative integer is a sum of four integer squares.

Proof 1: Reduction of the four-square theorem to primes — outline

The four-square identity expresses a product of two four-square sums as another four-square sum. Hence it suffices to prove representability for primes; the general theorem follows by multiplying their representations.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Factor a positive integer into primes and repeatedly apply the supplied bilinear identity; zero and one have immediate four-square representations. Integer products and finite iteration suffice.
