Statement

Every projective morphism of schemes f:X\to S is proper. Thus f is separated and of finite type, and is universally closed: for every morphism of schemes S'\to S, the projection

X\times_S S'\longrightarrow S'

sends closed subsets to closed subsets in the Zariski topology. Here projective means admitting a closed immersion into a projective space over the base, followed by its structural projection; proper means separated, of finite type and universally closed.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement depends on scheme structures, relative projective space, fiber products and separatedness in addition to topology; these cumulative prerequisites exceed the undergraduate reading allowance.

Proof 1: Projective coordinates and the valuative criterion (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Lemma29.44.5, tag01WC, and its linked prerequisites).

The definition in this statement is called “H-projective” in the Stacks Project and is a special case of its locally projective hypothesis. The proof works over arbitrary schemes: \mathbb P^n_S\to S is obtained from \mathbb P^n_{\mathbb Z}\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z by base change, and properness is preserved under base change and composition with a closed immersion.
