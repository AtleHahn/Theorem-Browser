Statement

If ABCD is a convex quadrilateral and E,F,G,H are its consecutive side midpoints, then \operatorname{area}(EFGH)=\tfrac12\operatorname{area}(ABCD).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement compares areas of explicitly defined convex plane figures.

Proof 1: Subtracting four corner triangles

The four small corner triangles are AEH,BEF,CFG,DGH; their corresponding large triangles are ABD,BAC,CBD,DAC, respectively. Write A_q for the area of ABCD, A_l for the sum of the four large triangle areas, and A_s for the sum of the four small triangle areas.

The midpoint base pairs form a parallelogram with half the area of the quadrilateral, A_q, as the sum of the areas of the four large triangles, A_l, is 2A_q (each of the two pairs reconstructs the quadrilateral) while that of the small triangles, A_s, is a quarter of A_l (half linear dimensions yields quarter area), and the area of the parallelogram is A_q minus A_s.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Subtract four similar corner triangles and use the squared scale factor for area. Elementary Euclidean geometry suffices.
