Statement

On an oriented Riemannian spin manifold M, the spin Dirac operator D satisfies

D^2=\nabla^*\nabla+\frac{\operatorname{Scal}}4\operatorname{Id}

on smooth spinor fields. Here a spin structure is a lift of the oriented orthonormal frame bundle through \operatorname{Spin}(n)\to\operatorname{SO}(n); the spinor bundle is its associated bundle for the complex spin representation. The Levi-Civita connection induces \nabla on this bundle, and locally D=\sum_j c(e_j)\nabla_{e_j} in an orthonormal frame, where Clifford multiplication satisfies c(v)c(w)+c(w)c(v)=-2\langle v,w\rangle\operatorname{Id}. The formal adjoint \nabla^* uses the Riemannian volume and spinor inner product, and \operatorname{Scal} is scalar curvature.
