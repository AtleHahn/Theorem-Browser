Statement

Let n\ge1 and let L=\mathbb Zb_1+\cdots+\mathbb Zb_n\subset\mathbb R^n, where b_1,\ldots,b_n are linearly independent. Suppose the translates [0,1]^n+\ell, \ell\in L, cover \mathbb R^n and have pairwise disjoint interiors. Then two distinct translates share a whole (n-1)-dimensional face of each cube.

Proof 1: Finite abelian factorization of a cube tiling (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Szabo (2004) §§2–3 Lemmas 1–2 and Theorem 3 pp.2–5; Zong (2005) §6 Lemma 6.1 p.201.).

Supporting passages: Zong, What is known about unit cubes, Lemma 6.1.

Schmidt’s rational counterexample reduction (Zong §6 Lemma 6.1) is a named dependency: a twin-free real lattice tiling would yield a rational one. For a rational lattice L choose common grid Lbar containing L. Half-open cube grid points give the finite-group factorization Lbar/L by coordinate intervals of lengths q_i. Split each interval into prime-length cyclic digit sets. Szabo Theorem 3 proves one prime-length factor is a subgroup, via minimal counterexample/height, replacement by periodicity-preventing subsets, and a subgroup chain forcing the complementary product both periodic and nonperiodic; its p-group case is explicitly a named Stein–Szabo dependency. A prime digit subgroup generated by g_i^d, of length p, gives g_i^(dp)=1, hence g_i^q_i=1 since dp divides q_i. Therefore e_i belongs to L and the cubes at 0,e_i share a whole face. This contradicts the rational twin-free assumption. n=1 is immediate.
