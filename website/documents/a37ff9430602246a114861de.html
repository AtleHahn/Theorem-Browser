Statement

Let n\ge1, X\ge0, and let f_i:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R be differentiable, with a finite right derivative at 0. Suppose x^0=(x_1^0,\ldots,x_n^0) maximizes \sum_{i=1}^n f_i(x_i) subject to x_i\ge0 and \sum_{i=1}^n x_i=X. Then there is \lambda\in\mathbb R such that f_i^{\prime}(x_i^0)=\lambda\quad\text{if }x_i^0>0,\qquad f_i^{\prime}(0)\le\lambda\quad\text{if }x_i^0=0.
