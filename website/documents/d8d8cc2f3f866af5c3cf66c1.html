Statement

Let A:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty) be nondecreasing. Suppose

f(s)=\int_0^\infty A(x)e^{-sx}\,dx

converges for all complex s with \operatorname{Re}s>1. If for some c\ge0 the function f(s)-c/(s-1) extends continuously to the closed half-plane \operatorname{Re}s\ge1, then

\lim_{x\to\infty}e^{-x}A(x)=c.

The integral is with respect to real x, and \operatorname{Re}s denotes the real part of s.
