Statement

Let n\geq1 be an integer, and let V\subset\mathbb R^n be a compact region with piecewise smooth boundary \partial V. Let F=(F_1,\ldots,F_n) be a continuously differentiable vector field on an open neighborhood of V. With \nu the outward unit normal, defined at almost every boundary point, the divergence theorem states

\int_V\operatorname{div}F\,dV=\int_{\partial V}F\cdot\nu\,dS,\qquad \operatorname{div}F=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\partial F_i}{\partial x_i}.

Here dV is n-dimensional volume measure and dS is (n-1)-dimensional surface measure. The right side is the outward flux of F through the boundary.
