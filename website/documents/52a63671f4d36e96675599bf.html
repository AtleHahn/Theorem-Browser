Statement

Let two distinct secant rays from a point P outside a Euclidean circle meet it in the orders P,A,D and P,B,C, respectively. Then |PA|\,|PD|=|PB|\,|PC|.

Level

Level justification

Proof 1: Common angle and equal inscribed angles

The triangles PAC and PBD have the common angle \angle DPC at P. Also \angle ADB=\angle ACB, since these inscribed angles subtend the same chord AB. Thus \triangle PAC\sim\triangle PBD. Their corresponding sides give |PA|/|PC|=|PB|/|PD|, which is equivalent to |PA|\,|PD|=|PB|\,|PC|.
