Statement

Let K be a number field and \mathcal O_K its ring of integers. Let C\subset\mathbb A_K^n be a smooth algebraic curve in a fixed affine embedding, of genus g>0. Then

C(K)\cap\mathcal O_K^n

is finite. A number field is a finite extension of \mathbb Q, and \mathcal O_K consists of its elements satisfying monic polynomials over \mathbb Z. An algebraic curve here is a geometrically integral variety of dimension one; smooth means nonsingular. Its genus is the dimension of the space of regular differential 1-forms on its smooth projective completion. The set displayed consists of the points of the curve whose coordinates in the specified embedding are algebraic integers.
