Statement

Let g:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb R and suppose F(t)=t g(t) is convex, meaning F(\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v)\le\lambda F(u)+(1-\lambda)F(v) for u,v>0 and 0\le\lambda\le1. If a_i,b_i>0, a=\sum_i a_i, and b=\sum_i b_i, then \sum_i a_i g(a_i/b_i)\ge a g(a/b).

Level
High school only
Level justification

Convexity is explicitly a real two-point inequality; the claim involves positive numbers and finite sums.

Proof 1: Use the same weighted Jensen substitution

Put w_i=b_i/b and t_i=a_i/b_i. The finite Jensen inequality for the convex function F gives

\sum_i a_i g(a_i/b_i)=b\sum_i w_iF(t_i)\ge bF\left(\sum_i w_it_i\right)=bF(a/b)=a g(a/b).
Level
High school only
Level justification

Insert the explicitly given weights and ratios into finite Jensen and multiply by the positive total b. Convexity is defined in the statement and only finite inequalities and algebra are used.
