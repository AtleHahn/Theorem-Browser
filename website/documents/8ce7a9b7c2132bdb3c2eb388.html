Statement

For connected oriented smooth n-manifolds M,N without boundary, n\ge2, the oriented diffeomorphism type of their connected sum M\#N is independent of the disc embeddings used in the following construction. Choose smooth closed-unit-disc embeddings e:D^n\to M and f:D^n\to N, with e orientation-preserving and f orientation-reversing. From

(M\setminus\{e(0)\})\sqcup(N\setminus\{f(0)\})

identify e(tu) with f((1-t)u) for every u\in S^{n-1} and 0<t<1. Use the smooth structure obtained by gluing these open punctured disc neighborhoods and the orientation agreeing with those of M and N. Any other such pair of embeddings gives an orientation-preserving diffeomorphic manifold. Here D^n=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:\|x\|\le1\}, S^{n-1} is its boundary, and a diffeomorphism is a smooth bijection with smooth inverse. The conclusion concerns this specified radial gluing construction.
