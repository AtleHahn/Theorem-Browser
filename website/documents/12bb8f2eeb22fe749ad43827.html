Statement

A compact Kähler manifold M whose Kähler form represents an integral cohomology class admits an embedding as a nonsingular projective algebraic variety.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Kähler metrics, integral cohomology classes and projective embeddings require substantial complex geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply Chow to the Kodaira embedding

Kodaira’s embedding theorem gives a complex-analytic embedding of M into a complex projective space. The image is compact, and Chow’s theorem (a closed complex-analytic subvariety of projective space is algebraic) makes this image algebraic.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use Kodaira for an analytic projective embedding and Chow for algebraicity. Kähler forms, integral cohomology and complex analytic versus algebraic subvarieties require graduate geometry.
