Statement

Let R be a commutative unital ring, M a finitely generated R-module, and J(R) the Jacobson radical of R. If the images of m_1,\ldots,m_n generate M/J(R)M over R/J(R), then m_1,\ldots,m_n generate M over R.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The assertion relates generators over a ring and its radical quotient.

Proof 1: Use the submodule generated by the lifts

Apply the superfluous-submodule form of Nakayama's lemma to

N=\sum_i Rm_i.

That form says that if a finitely generated module M satisfies M=N+J(R)M, then M=N.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the application requires module quotients over quotient rings, ideal-module products and the Jacobson radical. The supplied Nakayama assertion removes its proof, but not this combined commutative-algebra prerequisite.
