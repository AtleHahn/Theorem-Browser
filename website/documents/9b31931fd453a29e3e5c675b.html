Proof 1: Use that a product of odd integers is odd

Although a direct proof can be given, we choose to prove this statement by contraposition. The contrapositive of the statement is:

If {\displaystyle x} is not even, then {\displaystyle x^{2}} is not even.

This latter statement can be proven as follows: suppose that x is not even, then x is odd. The product of two odd numbers is odd, hence {\displaystyle x^{2}=x\cdot x} is odd. Thus {\displaystyle x^{2}} is not even.

Having proved the contrapositive, we can then infer that the original statement is true.
