Statement

Let a,b be elements of a division ring with ab\ne0,1. Then all the inverses below exist and a-\bigl(a^{-1}+(b^{-1}-a)^{-1}\bigr)^{-1}=aba.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Ring multiplication, units and matrix inverses are introductory algebra. Division rings drop commutativity but retain nonzero inverses; the expressions specify multiplication order and invertibility. No new structural theory is needed.

Proof 1: Hua’s identity — proof

One has

{\displaystyle (a-aba)\left(a^{-1}+\left(b^{-1}-a\right)^{-1}\right)=1-ab+ab\left(b^{-1}-a\right)\left(b^{-1}-a\right)^{-1}=1.}
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Multiply the two displayed factors to obtain one and use the division-ring inverse. Undergraduate abstract algebra suffices for the noncommutative calculation.
