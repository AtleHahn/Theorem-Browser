Statement

For integers n\ge1 and k\ge0, put M_n(k)=\frac1n\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(n/d)k^d, where \mu(1)=1, \mu(m)=0 if a prime square divides m, and \mu(m)=(-1)^r if m is a product of r distinct primes. Products below are identities of formal power series in z: each coefficient is determined by finitely many factors. For every pair of nonnegative integers k,\ell, \prod_{j=1}^{\infty}(1-kz^j)^{-M_j(\ell)}=\prod_{j=1}^{\infty}(1-\ell z^j)^{-M_j(k)}.
