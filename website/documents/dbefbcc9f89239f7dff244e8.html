Statement

Let a compact Lie group G act smoothly on a nonempty connected smooth manifold M. There is a unique conjugacy class of stabilizer subgroups such that the set of points having a stabilizer in that class is open and dense in M. For x\in M, its stabilizer is G_x=\{g\in G:g\cdot x=x\}. Two subgroups are conjugate if one equals gHg^{-1} for some g\in G. If H represents the distinguished class, the associated homogeneous space G/H is called the principal orbit type. A Lie group is a finite-dimensional smooth manifold with smooth multiplication and inversion; a smooth action is a smooth map G\times M\to M satisfying the group-action laws.
