Statement

Let \mathfrak g be a finite-dimensional real Lie algebra and M a compact smooth manifold without boundary. Every smooth infinitesimal action of \mathfrak g on M integrates to a smooth action of a finite-dimensional Lie group G whose Lie algebra is identified with \mathfrak g.

Explicitly, the infinitesimal action assigns to each \alpha\in\mathfrak g a smooth vector field a(\alpha), linearly and compatibly with the Lie brackets using the convention for infinitesimal fields of the chosen action. There is a smooth group action \Phi:G\times M\to M whose infinitesimal fields are exactly the prescribed ones:

a(\alpha)_x=\left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{t=0}\Phi(\exp(t\alpha),x)\qquad(\alpha\in\mathfrak g,\ x\in M).

For a left action as displayed, compatibility means a([\alpha,\beta])=-[a(\alpha),a(\beta)] with the usual vector-field bracket; equivalently one may use right actions and bracket-preserving infinitesimal actions.

The conclusion also holds for a compact smooth manifold with boundary, provided every vector field a(\alpha) is tangent to its boundary.
