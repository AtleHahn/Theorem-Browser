Statement

Let n=\prod_{i=1}^r p_i^{a_i} be the prime factorization of an integer n>1. Then the residue map gives an isomorphism of additive groups \mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\cong\prod_i\mathbb Z/p_i^{a_i}\mathbb Z.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory algebra supplies groups, cosets, quotients, homomorphisms and linear independence. Complement or composition-series conditions and monoid vocabulary are small algebraic extensions; finite-tree automorphisms are adjacency-preserving permutations. No additional structural theory is required.

Proof 1: Apply CRT to the prime-power moduli — outline

Apply the integer Chinese remainder theorem to the pairwise coprime prime powers in the factorization of n. It gives the displayed residue decomposition; addition is preserved componentwise.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply integer CRT to the coprime prime powers and observe componentwise preservation of addition. Understanding the group-isomorphism conclusion requires only basic cyclic groups.
