Statement

For every continuous function f:S^2\to\mathbb R, where S^2=\{x\in\mathbb R^3:\|x\|_2=1\}, there are pairwise perpendicular unit vectors u,v,w forming a basis of \mathbb R^3 such that f(u)=f(v)=f(w).

Level justification

Basic multivariable continuity and linear algebra completely describe the assertion; fundamental groups concern proof only.

Level justification

For the three-dimensional statement, the separation argument takes place in a planar annulus. Polygonal approximation and crossing parity supply its separating curve; continuity, compactness and orthogonal rotations then give an equal-value frame. These use undergraduate analysis, linear algebra and basic topology, with only a short introduction to polygonal crossing parity.
