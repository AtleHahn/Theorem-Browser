Statement

In a propositional calculus with modus ponens, the deduction theorem and Peirce’s law, if \Gamma,Z\to P\vdash Z, then \Gamma\vdash Z.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The unnamed propositional calculus requires the meaning of derivability and the deduction theorem, a limited formal-logic extension of ordinary undergraduate proof reasoning.

Proof 1: Deduction theorem followed by Peirce

The deduction theorem gives \Gamma\vdash(Z\to P)\to Z. Peirce’s law gives ((Z\to P)\to Z)\to Z. Modus ponens yields \Gamma\vdash Z.

Level justification

Discharge the extra implication premise and apply a single Peirce instance by modus ponens. Undergraduate introductory formal logic suffices.
