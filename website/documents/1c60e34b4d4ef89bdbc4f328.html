Statement

Let a,b,c,d,e,f,g be positive integers with a^2+b^2=d^2, a^2+c^2=e^2, b^2+c^2=f^2, and a^2+b^2+c^2=g^2; these are the edges, face diagonals and space diagonal of a perfect cuboid. A Heronian triangle has integer side lengths and integer area. The lengths (d^2,e^2,f^2) are the sides of a Heronian triangle with area abcg and rational internal angle-bisector lengths.
