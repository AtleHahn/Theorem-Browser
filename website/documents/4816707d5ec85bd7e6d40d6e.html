Statement

Let A be a commutative ring and f\in A a non-zero-divisor. Write A_f=A[1/f], \widehat A=\varprojlim_{m\geq1}A/f^mA, and \widehat A_f=\widehat A[1/f]. A module is called f-regular if multiplication by f is injective on it.

The base-change functor from f-regular A-modules to triples (F,G,\phi) is an equivalence of categories, where F is an A_f-module, G is an f-regular \widehat A-module, and

\phi:G_f\xrightarrow{\sim}F\otimes_{A_f}\widehat A_f

is an isomorphism. Morphisms of triples are pairs of module homomorphisms commuting with \phi. The functor sends M to F=M_f, G=M\otimes_A\widehat A and their canonical identification after inverting f.

In particular, every such triple comes from an f-regular A-module M with isomorphisms \alpha:M_f\xrightarrow{\sim}F and \beta:M\otimes_A\widehat A\xrightarrow{\sim}G such that \phi=(\alpha\otimes1)\circ(\beta^{-1}\otimes1) after the canonical identifications. This gluing is unique up to the unique isomorphism respecting the given identifications.
