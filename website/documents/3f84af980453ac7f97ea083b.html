Statement

Let G be a topological group whose underlying space is a finite-dimensional topological manifold. Thus multiplication and inversion are continuous, and the space is Hausdorff, second countable and locally homeomorphic to \mathbb R^n for some finite n.

Then G is isomorphic as a topological group to a Lie group. Equivalently, its underlying manifold admits a compatible smooth structure for which multiplication G\times G\to G and inversion G\to G are smooth. A topological-group isomorphism is a group isomorphism that is also a homeomorphism; a Lie group is a finite-dimensional smooth manifold with smooth group operations.
