Statement

Let A be a commutative ring, I an ideal and M an A-module. Put A_0=A/I, M_0=M/IM, \operatorname{gr}_I A=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^n/I^{n+1} and \operatorname{gr}_I M=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^nM/I^{n+1}M. If \operatorname{Tor}_1^A(A/I^n,M)=0 for every n>0, then the natural graded map \operatorname{gr}_I A\otimes_{A_0}M_0\to\operatorname{gr}_I M is an isomorphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level

These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Reduce each ideal-power tensor identity modulo the ideal

The assumption implies that I^n\otimes_A M=I^nM (by the Tor exact sequence of 0\to I^n\to A\to A/I^n\to0). Write N_0=N/IN for any A-module N. Since tensor product commutes with base extension,

\begin{aligned} \operatorname{gr}_I(A)\otimes_{A_0}M_0 &=\bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty}(I^n)_0\otimes_{A_0}M_0\\ &=\bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty}(I^n\otimes_A M)_0\\ &=\bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty}(I^nM)_0\\ &=\operatorname{gr}_I M. \end{aligned}

Here the equalities involving tensor products are the natural isomorphisms.
