Statement

Let \mathcal H be the homotopy category of pointed connected CW complexes: its morphisms are based homotopy classes of based continuous maps. A contravariant functor F:\mathcal H^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set} is naturally isomorphic to $[,{-},C,]_* $ for some pointed connected CW complex C if and only if it satisfies both conditions below.

  1. For every set-indexed family (X_\alpha), the canonical map induced by the wedge inclusions is a bijection
F\left(\bigvee_\alpha X_\alpha\right)\longrightarrow\prod_\alpha F(X_\alpha).
  1. It sends homotopy pushout squares to weak pullback squares: for a homotopy pushout W of a diagram U\leftarrow A\to V in this setting, the induced map
F(W)\longrightarrow F(U)\times_{F(A)}F(V)

is surjective. Thus every compatible pair of elements on U,V comes from an element on W, without requiring uniqueness.

Here [Z,C]_* is the set of based homotopy classes of maps Z\to C. Naturality means that the bijections F(Z)\cong[Z,C]_* commute with the maps induced by every based map between the source complexes.

The empty wedge forces F(*) to be a singleton, providing the canonical basepoints. The stated homotopy-pushout axiom implies Putman's subcomplex gluing axiom.
