Statement

Let M be a compact oriented smooth Riemannian manifold without boundary of dimension 2n, where n\geq1. Let \Omega=(\Omega_{ij}) be the skew-symmetric matrix of curvature two-forms of its Levi-Civita connection in a local oriented orthonormal frame. Then

\chi(M)=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^n}\int_M\operatorname{Pf}(\Omega).

Here \chi(M)=\sum_{j=0}^{2n}(-1)^j\dim H^j(M;\mathbb R) is the Euler characteristic, and the Pfaffian is the differential 2n-form given locally by

\operatorname{Pf}(\Omega)=\frac{1}{2^n n!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_{2n}}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\, \Omega_{\sigma(1)\sigma(2)}\wedge\cdots\wedge\Omega_{\sigma(2n-1)\sigma(2n)}.

The local forms agree to define the global integrand. The same equality holds with the curvature of any metric-compatible connection on TM in place of the Levi-Civita curvature.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Manifolds, tangent-bundle connections, curvature forms and cohomology impose several interdependent prerequisites well beyond the undergraduate reading allowance. An explicit Pfaffian formula does not remove that burden.
