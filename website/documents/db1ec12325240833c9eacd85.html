Statement

For a based path-connected space X, write \pi_j(X) for based homotopy classes of maps S^j\to X, and H_j(X;R) for singular homology with coefficients R. The Hurewicz map sends [f] to f_*[S^j], where [S^j] is the fundamental homology class of the oriented sphere. Let n\ge2 and assume \pi_i(X)=0 for 1\le i<n. Then reduced integral homology \widetilde H_i(X;\mathbb Z) vanishes for 0\le i<n, the Hurewicz map \pi_n(X)\to H_n(X;\mathbb Z) is an isomorphism, and \pi_{n+1}(X)\to H_{n+1}(X;\mathbb Z) is surjective. Reduced homology differs from ordinary homology only in degree zero, where it is the kernel of the augmentation to \mathbb Z.

For every based path-connected space X, without the preceding higher-connectivity hypothesis, the degree-one map induces an isomorphism

\pi_1(X)/[\pi_1(X),\pi_1(X)]\cong H_1(X;\mathbb Z).

Here [G,G] is the subgroup generated by commutators aba^{-1}b^{-1}, so the quotient is the abelianization of G.
