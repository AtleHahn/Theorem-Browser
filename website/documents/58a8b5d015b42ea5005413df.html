Proof 1: Counting p-cycles with Sylow theory

Let p be a prime. One may easily prove this theorem by Sylow's third theorem. Indeed, observe that the number np of Sylow's p-subgroups in the symmetric group Sp is 1/(p-1) times the number of p-cycles in Sp, ie. (p − 2)!. On the other hand, np ≡ 1 (mod p). Hence, (p − 2)! ≡ 1 (mod p). So, (p − 1)! ≡ −1 (mod p).
