Proof 1: Weak compactness and Eberlein–Šmulian — outline

The closed unit ball of a reflexive Banach space is weakly compact, by the reflexive consequence of Banach–Alaoglu. Consequently every bounded subset has weakly compact weak closure. By the Eberlein–Šmulian theorem, every sequence in such a subset has a subsequence converging weakly to an element of the space. Apply this to the given bounded sequence.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Combine reflexive weak compactness with Eberlein–Šmulian. Banach duality, reflexivity and weak topology are multiple advanced functional-analytic prerequisites.
