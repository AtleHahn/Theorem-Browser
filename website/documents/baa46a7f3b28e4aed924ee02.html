Statement

Two nondegenerate Euclidean right triangles with equal hypotenuse lengths and one corresponding pair of equal leg lengths are congruent.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The hypotheses specify the hypotenuse and one leg of two right triangles, and the conclusion is ordinary Euclidean congruence.

Proof 1: Pythagoras reduces hypotenuse-leg to SSS

The Pythagorean theorem calculates the remaining leg length from the common hypotenuse and the equal given leg. Thus all three corresponding side lengths are equal, and SSS gives congruence.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Use Pythagoras to recover the equal remaining leg and apply SSS. High-school triangle geometry suffices.
