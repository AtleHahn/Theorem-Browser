Statement

On \ell^\infty, the bounded real sequences, with the supremum norm, the bounded linear operator (Tx)_j=x_j/j is not surjective.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and analysis supply bilinearity, norms, derivatives, sequences and closure. Banach completeness or boundedness is a direct metric condition, and the maps are explicitly given. No weak topology, duality or spectral theory is needed to understand these assertions.

Proof 1: A vector outside the range — outline

The map T is not onto in \ell^\infty: for example,

x=(1,1,1,\dots)

is not in the range of T. Its only possible coordinate preimage is (1,2,3,\dots), which is unbounded.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The constant target sequence one is bounded, but its only coordinatewise preimage is j, which is unbounded. The explicitly described bounded-sequence space needs only elementary sequence bounds and linear maps.
