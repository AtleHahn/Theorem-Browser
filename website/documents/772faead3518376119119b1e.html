Statement

Let (A,\mathfrak m) and (B,\mathfrak n) be complete Noetherian regular local commutative rings of mixed characteristic (0,p), where p is prime. Suppose they are unramified, meaning p\notin\mathfrak m^2 and p\notin\mathfrak n^2. If their residue fields A/\mathfrak m and B/\mathfrak n are isomorphic and their Krull dimensions are equal, then A and B are isomorphic as rings. Mixed characteristic (0,p) means the ring has characteristic zero and its residue field has characteristic p. Local means having one maximal ideal; Noetherian means every ideal is finitely generated. Complete means the natural map A\to\varprojlim_j A/\mathfrak m^j (respectively for B) is an isomorphism. Regular means the minimal number of generators of the maximal ideal equals the Krull dimension, the supremum of lengths of strict chains of prime ideals. No particular residue-field isomorphism or canonical ring isomorphism is asserted.
