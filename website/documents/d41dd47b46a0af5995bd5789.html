Statement

If f is analytic on a neighborhood of |z|\le r, r>0, and has no zeros there, then \log|f(0)|=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log|f(re^{i\theta})|\,d\theta.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex differentiation and exponential functions extend calculus directly; real analysis supplies series, compact convergence and growth limits. All special functions or growth parameters used here are explicitly given by formulas, so no meromorphic continuation or abstract complex geometry is needed.

Proof 1: Empty zero sum

Namely, if {\displaystyle f} has no zeros in {\displaystyle \{z:|z|\leq r\}}, then Jensen's formula reduces to

{\displaystyle \log |f(0)|={\frac {1}{2\pi }}\int _{0}^{2\pi }\log |f(re^{i\theta })|\,d\theta ,}

which is the mean-value property of the harmonic function {\displaystyle \log |f(z)|}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Jensen formula with an empty zero contribution. Introductory complex analysis and the explicit logarithmic circle integral suffice.
