Statement

Let p be a real polynomial of degree d with p(0)\ne0, and let Q(x)=x^dp(1/x). If U>0 is an upper bound for every positive root of Q, then every positive root of p is at least 1/U.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The reciprocal polynomial and numerical bound use polynomial algebra and positive real reciprocals.

Proof 1: Reciprocate a positive root

If r>0 is a root of p, then Q(1/r)=r^{-d}p(r)=0. Thus 1/r\leq U, so r\geq1/U.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Evaluate the reciprocal polynomial at 1/r and reverse the positive reciprocal inequality. School polynomial substitution suffices.
