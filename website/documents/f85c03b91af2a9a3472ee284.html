Simpler proof assessment

Likely alternative prerequisite level: Undergraduate level; lowest recorded proof level: Beyond undergraduate level. A commuting family preserves each eigenspace of each member. If all members are scalar, any vector is common; otherwise restrict to a proper nonzero eigenspace of a nonscalar member and induct on dimension to find a common eigenvector. Pass to its quotient and repeat for a common invariant flag. This covers arbitrary, even infinite, families in finite dimension and all dimensions including zero. Algebraic closedness supplies eigenvalues. Ordinary linear algebra replaces Lie algebras and their representations.

Statement

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, any family of pairwise commuting endomorphisms of a finite-dimensional vector space has a basis in which every member is upper triangular.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra supplies commuting endomorphisms and triangular matrices. An algebraically closed field is one in which every nonconstant polynomial has a root, and characteristic zero is familiar field vocabulary. These definitions suffice without field-extension or representation theory.

Proof 1: Commuting matrices are simultaneously triangularizable — source deduction

Lie's theorem says that, over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, for a finite-dimensional representation \pi:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak{gl}(V) of a solvable Lie algebra, there is a basis for V such that all linear transformations in \pi(\mathfrak g) are represented by upper triangular matrices. This is a generalization of the result that commuting matrices are simultaneously upper triangularizable, as commuting matrices generate an abelian Lie algebra, which is a fortiori solvable.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

This particular proof passes through a representation of a solvable Lie algebra and Lie triangularization. Defining Lie algebras, solvability and representations together is not confidently a small extension, even though an elementary matrix proof exists. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
