Statement

Let n\ge1 and let u:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R be twice continuously differentiable with \sum_{j=1}^n\partial_j^2u=0. Write \mathbb S^{n-1}=\{x:|x|=1\} and let \sigma be its surface measure (counting measure on \mathbb S^0). With exactly the normalization

M(\rho)=\left(\rho^{1-n}\int_{\mathbb S^{n-1}}|u(\rho\omega)|^2\,d\sigma(\omega)\right)^{1/2},

for 0<r_1<r<r_2 one has

M(r)\le M(r_1)^\alpha M(r_2)^{1-\alpha},\qquad \alpha=\frac{\log(r_2/r)}{\log(r_2/r_1)}.

Here |x| is Euclidean length and logarithms are natural.
