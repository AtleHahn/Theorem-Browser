Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer. Let K=\mathbb F_p, p prime, and let H_n(K) consist of the upper unitriangular block matrices M(a,b,c)=\begin{pmatrix}1&a&c\\0&I_n&b\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix} (with a a row and b a column) with a,b\in K^n,c\in K. Fix an injective additive character \omega:K\to\mathbb T. For 0\ne h\in K, define (U_h(M(a,b,c))\psi)(x)=\omega(b\cdot x+hc)\psi(x+ha) on \ell^2(K^n). These representations are irreducible and pairwise inequivalent for distinct h.

Proof 1: Compute the character norm and compare central values — outline

Use the following external character formula:

\chi_h(M(a,b,c))=\begin{cases}|K|^n\omega(hc)&a=b=0,\\0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}

Since |H_n(K)|=|K|^{2n+1}, its character norm is

\frac1{|H_n(K)|}\sum_g|\chi_h(g)|^2=\frac{|K|^{2n}|K|}{|K|^{2n+1}}=1.

Character orthogonality therefore implies irreducibility. For distinct h, the central values |K|^n\omega(hc) differ, giving inequivalent characters and hence inequivalent representations.
