Statement

Let X,Y be topological spaces and k a commutative ring with identity. Write C_*(X;k) for singular chains and C^*(X;k)=\operatorname{Hom}_k(C_*(X;k),k). The tensor chain complex has differential \partial(\sigma\otimes\tau)=\partial\sigma\otimes\tau+(-1)^p\sigma\otimes\partial\tau for \sigma\in C_p(X;k). For degrees p,q\ge0, the prescription \alpha\otimes\beta\mapsto(\sigma\otimes\tau\mapsto\alpha(\sigma)\beta(\tau)) defines a k-linear map C^p(X;k)\otimes_k C^q(Y;k)\to\operatorname{Hom}_k(C_p(X;k)\otimes_k C_q(Y;k),k).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Singular chain and cochain complexes, their tensor differential and dual evaluation require substantial algebraic topology and homological algebra. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.
