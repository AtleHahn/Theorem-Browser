Statement

Let K be a number field and p a prime. For m\geq1 set K_m=K(\zeta_{p^m}). There exist \lambda\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}, \mu\in\mathbb Q_{\geq0}, \kappa\in\mathbb Z and m_0\geq1 such that

v_p(h(K_m))=\lambda m+\mu p^m+\kappa\qquad(m\geq m_0).

Here \zeta_{p^m} is a primitive p^m-th root of unity, h(F) is the order of the group of nonzero fractional ideals of the ring of integers of F modulo principal ideals, and v_p(h) is the largest nonnegative integer r for which p^r divides h.

The rational coefficient convention here refers to the displayed root-of-unity indexing.
