Statement

In a spherical right triangle of radius R>0 with legs a,b and opposite side c, \sin^2\frac{c}{2R}=\sin^2\frac{a}{2R}+\sin^2\frac{b}{2R}-2\sin^2\frac{a}{2R}\sin^2\frac{b}{2R}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Spherical side lengths and the displayed half-angle identity require elementary sphere geometry and trigonometry.

Proof 1: Apply the cosine double-angle identity

Substitute \cos(2t)=1-2\sin^2t into \cos(c/R)=\cos(a/R)\cos(b/R) and expand the product. Cancel the constant terms and divide by -2.
