Statement

Let p(n) count the partitions of the nonnegative integer n into positive integer summands, with their order ignored. For every integer k\ge0, p(7k+5)\equiv0\pmod{7}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

An integer partition is a sum of positive integers with order ignored; the congruences are elementary counting statements.

Mathematical Field
Combinatorics
Required topics
Integer partitionsDivisibility
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Extract coefficients of the q-product identity — outline

Write (q^d)_\infty=\prod_{j\ge1}(1-q^{dj}). Use the source identity \sum_{k\ge0}p(7k+5)q^k=7\frac{(q^7)_\infty^3}{(q)_\infty^4}+49q\frac{(q^7)_\infty^7}{(q)_\infty^8}. The product quotients have integer coefficients as formal power series, and every term on the right has a prefactor divisible by 7. Comparing coefficients gives the congruence.

Level justification

Use the supplied dissection identity, expand its product quotients coefficientwise over the integers and retain the factors seven and forty-nine. Undergraduate generating-function arithmetic suffices; the identity proof and modular forms are not required.
