Statement

Let A be a reduced Noetherian commutative ring with identity and let K be its total ring of fractions, obtained by inverting all non-zero-divisors. Its integral closure

\overline A=\{x\in K:x\text{ satisfies a monic polynomial with coefficients in }A\}

is a direct product of r Krull domains, where r is the number of minimal prime ideals of A. Reduced means having no nonzero nilpotent elements; Noetherian means every ideal is finitely generated. A minimal prime is a prime ideal minimal under inclusion. A Krull domain is an integral domain R which, inside its fraction field, equals the intersection of its localizations R_{\mathfrak p} at height-one prime ideals, each such localization being a discrete valuation ring, and each nonzero element of R lies in only finitely many height-one primes. Height one means the longest prime-ideal chain below that prime has length one. A discrete valuation ring is \{0\}\cup\{x:v(x)\ge0\} in a field F, where v:F^\times\to\mathbb Z is surjective, v(xy)=v(x)+v(y) and v(x+y)\ge\min(v(x),v(y)) when x+y\ne0. For the zero ring, r=0 and the product is the empty product of rings.
