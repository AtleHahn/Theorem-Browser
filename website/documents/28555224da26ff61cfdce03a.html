Statement

Let G,G_1,\ldots,G_m be finitely generated abelian groups, \phi_j:G\to G_j homomorphisms, and s_j\in[0,1]. Suppose \operatorname{rank}H\le\sum_j s_j\operatorname{rank}\phi_j(H) for every subgroup H\le G. If T(G) is the finite torsion subgroup, then every nonempty finite E\subseteq G satisfies |E|\le |T(G)|\prod_j|\phi_j(E)|^{s_j}. For s_j=0, the corresponding factor is one.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Finitely generated abelian groups, homomorphisms and subgroup rank/torsion fit introductory abstract algebra with the structure definition rank = number of infinite cyclic factors. The remaining assertion concerns finite subsets and real powers of their cardinalities. No specialist group theory is required to read the all-subgroup rank hypothesis.

Proof 1: Indicator specialization of the discrete norm bound — outline

Use the discrete Brascamp–Lieb norm bound under the stated rank assumptions:

\left\|\prod_j f_j\circ\phi_j\right\|_1\le |T(G)|\prod_j\|f_j\|_{1/s_j}.

Here the norms use counting measure and 1/0=\infty. Its indicator specialization gives, in particular,

|E|\le |T(G)|\prod_j|\phi_j(E)|^{s_j}.
