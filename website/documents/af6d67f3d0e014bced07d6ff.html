Statement

For every prime number p, the number r_4(p) of ordered integer quadruples whose squares sum to p, counting signs and order separately, is 8(p+1).

Level

Proof 1: Prime specialization of the divisor-sum formula

Apply the stated Jacobi divisor-sum formula. If p is odd, its divisors are 1,p, giving 8(1+p). For p=2, the even-input formula gives 24 times the sum of the odd divisors, namely 24=8(2+1).
