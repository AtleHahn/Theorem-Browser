Statement

For every definite integral symmetric unimodular form Q that is not diagonalizable over the integers, there exists a closed oriented topological four-manifold with intersection form Q admitting no smooth structure.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Topological and smooth four-manifolds; Integral unimodular intersection forms; Smoothability. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Realization followed by the smooth diagonalization obstruction

Freedman’s realization theorem supplies a closed oriented topological four-manifold with the given unimodular form. If it had a smooth structure, Donaldson’s definite-form theorem would make that form diagonalizable over the integers, contradicting the hypothesis.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The theorem applications require topological versus smooth four-manifolds and their integral intersection forms. This is substantial geometric topology; the contradiction does not remove the prerequisite burden.
