Statement

For an isometry S on a Hilbert space G, the operator U=\begin{bmatrix}S&I-SS^*\\0&-S^*\end{bmatrix} on G\oplus G is unitary and satisfies S^n=P_GU^n|_G for n\ge0, with G the first summand. Here P_G is the orthogonal projection onto that summand and n is a nonnegative integer.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The block formula acts on arbitrary Hilbert spaces and uses Hilbert-space adjoints and unitary power compression.

Proof 1: Use the complementary range projection (outline)

For a contraction A, write D_A=(I-A^*A)^{1/2}. For an isometry S on G, its defect operators satisfy D_S=0 and D_{S^*}=I-SS^*, an orthogonal projection. The block operator

U=\begin{bmatrix}S&D_{S^*}\\0&-S^*\end{bmatrix}\quad\text{on }G\oplus G

is unitary, and S^n=P_GU^n|_{G} for n\ge0, with G embedded in the first summand.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use Hilbert-space adjoints, defect-operator square roots and orthogonal range projections to form the unitary dilation and its compressions. This operator-theoretic framework exceeds elementary block matrix algebra.
