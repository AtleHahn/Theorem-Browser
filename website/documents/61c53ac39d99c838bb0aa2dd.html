Proof 1: 341 is a counterexample to the base-two Fermat converse — outline

For example, 2^{341}\equiv2\pmod{341}, but 341=11\times31 is a pseudoprime to base 2 (a composite number satisfying the congruence).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The factorization 341=11 times 31 shows compositeness. To check the congruence, 2^10=1024=3 times 341+1, so 2^340=(2^10)^34 is congruent to 1 and 2^341 to 2. Finite integer arithmetic and modular multiplication suffice; no Fermat theorem is needed.
