Statement

Let G be a finite group and \operatorname{Char}(G) the integer span of its irreducible complex characters inside the complex-valued class functions, with pointwise addition and multiplication. Then \operatorname{Char}(G) is a ring.

Proof 1: Closure from products of characters — outline

\operatorname{Char}(G) is a ring by virtue of the fact that the product of characters of G is again a character of G. Its multiplication is given by the elementwise product of class functions.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use character products and irreducible decomposition to establish closure of the integer span. Representations, irreducibility and character decomposition form a specialized algebraic framework not confidently a small addition to introductory group theory.
