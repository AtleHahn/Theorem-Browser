Statement

Let a>0 and 0\le t\le1. A random variable X taking the value a with probability t and 0 with probability 1-t satisfies \mathbb P(X\ge a)=\mathbb E[X]/a=t, where its expectation is the weighted sum of its two values.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Both probabilities and the expectation are explicitly finite weights on two real values.

Proof 1: Evaluate the two-point expectation and tail

Markov’s inequality holds with equality for distributions supported on \{0,a\}.

The source states this equality case without writing out the two-point expectation calculation.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Compute the two-value weighted average at and divide by a; the tail probability is t. Elementary finite probability and arithmetic suffice.
