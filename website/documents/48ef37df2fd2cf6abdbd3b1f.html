Statement

Let M be a complete n-dimensional Riemannian manifold and let K\in\mathbb R. Assume \operatorname{Ric}_M(v,v)\geq(n-1)K\langle v,v\rangle for every tangent vector v. Let M_K^n be the complete simply connected n-dimensional space of constant sectional curvature K. Write B(p,r) for the open ball in the Riemannian distance and \operatorname{Vol} for Riemannian volume. For any p\in M and p_K\in M_K^n, the function

r\longmapsto\frac{\operatorname{Vol}B(p,r)}{\operatorname{Vol}B(p_K,r)}

is non-increasing for r>0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Metrics on tangent bundles, Ricci curvature and model spaces require differential geometry well beyond basic point-set topology; definitions do not reduce this cumulative framework to a short undergraduate extension.
