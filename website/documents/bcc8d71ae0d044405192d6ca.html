Statement

For the Euclidean plane S=\mathbb R^2, \int_S K\,dA=0<2\pi\chi(S)=2\pi, where K is Gaussian curvature and \chi is Euler characteristic.

Proof 1: Substitute zero curvature and Euler characteristic one

If S is the plane \mathbb R^2, then the curvature of S is zero, and \chi(S)=1, so the inequality is strict: 0<2\pi.
