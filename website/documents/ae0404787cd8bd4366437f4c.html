Statement

Let R be a real closed ordered field (every positive element is a square and every odd-degree polynomial has a root). Let F=\{f_1,\ldots,f_m\} and G=\{g_1,\ldots,g_r\} be finite subsets of R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]. Write W=\{x\in R^n:f_j(x)\ge0\text{ for all }j,\ g_\ell(x)=0\text{ for all }\ell\} and

P(F,G)=\left\{\sum_{\alpha\in\{0,1\}^m}\sigma_\alpha\prod_{j=1}^m f_j^{\alpha_j}+\sum_{\ell=1}^r h_\ell g_\ell\right\},

where each \sigma_\alpha is a finite sum of squares of polynomials over R and each h_\ell is any polynomial over R. Empty products are one and empty sums zero. For every polynomial p\in R[x_1,\ldots,x_n], one has p(x)>0 for every x\in W if and only if there exist q_1,q_2\in P(F,G) such that q_1p=1+q_2 as a polynomial identity.
