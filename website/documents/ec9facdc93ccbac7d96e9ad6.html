Statement

Let H be a complex Banach space of dimension greater than one and T a bounded operator. If a polynomial p satisfies that p(T) is nonzero and compact, then T has a nonzero proper closed invariant subspace.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compact operators and infinite-dimensional invariant subspaces require functional analysis.

Proof 1: Commute with the compact polynomial and apply Lomonosov

An operator commutes with every polynomial in itself, hence T commutes with the nonzero compact operator p(T). Lomonosov’s theorem for operators commuting with a nonzero compact operator yields a nontrivial closed invariant subspace.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Observe that T commutes with its polynomial and invoke the compact-commutant theorem. Banach-space compact operators and invariant-subspace theory require graduate functional analysis even for this short application.
