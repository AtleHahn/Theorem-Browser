Statement

Let X be a closed oriented smooth four-manifold with intersection pairing Q. If a complex rank-two bundle E splits as L\oplus L^{-1} for a complex line bundle L, then \langle c_2(E),[X]\rangle=-Q(c_1(L),c_1(L)). In particular, Chern number one implies Q(c_1(L),c_1(L))=-1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Complex vector bundles; Chern classes; Cohomological intersection pairing. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Chern number of a split SU(2) bundle

For the splitting, the Chern-class calculation is c_2(L\oplus L^{-1})=-c_1(L)^2. Evaluating on the fundamental class gives \langle c_2(E),[X]\rangle=-Q(c_1(L),c_1(L)); for Chern number one this is the claimed norm equation.

Level justification

Compute Chern classes of a split bundle and evaluate their cup square on the fundamental class. Bundles, characteristic classes and manifold intersection pairings require advanced topology.
