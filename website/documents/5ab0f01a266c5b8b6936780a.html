Statement

Let M be a smooth manifold. Smooth chains are finite integer linear combinations of smooth singular simplices; \partial denotes their boundary and integration extends linearly. For k\ge1, let \omega=d\sigma be a smooth exact k-form and let c be a smooth k-cycle, meaning \partial c=0. Then \int_c\omega=0.

Proof 1: Apply chain Stokes to a primitive

By the chain Stokes theorem, \int_c\omega=\int_c d\sigma=\int_{\partial c}\sigma=0.
