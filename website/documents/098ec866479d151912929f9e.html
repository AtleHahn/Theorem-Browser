Statement

Let R be a commutative Noetherian ring and M an R-module. The set of elements r\in R for which multiplication by r on M is not injective is \bigcup_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Ass}(M)}\mathfrak p. Here \operatorname{Ass}(L) consists of the prime ideals \operatorname{Ann}(u)=\{r:ru=0\} for nonzero u\in L.

Proof 1: Take the union of maximal annihilators

The set of zero-divisors on M is the union of the set of annihilators of nonzero elements, the maximal elements of which are associated primes.

The source uses this maximal-annihilator fact without repeating its proof here.

Level justification

Express zero divisors as annihilators and pass to maximal annihilators using Noetherianity. Modules over Noetherian rings and associated-prime structure are not confidently a small extension of elementary ring theory.
