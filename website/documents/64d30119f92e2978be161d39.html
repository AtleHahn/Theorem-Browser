Statement

Let X and Y be smooth manifolds without boundary and f:X\to Y a smooth map. A point y\in Y is a regular value if df_x:T_xX\to T_yY is surjective for every x\in f^{-1}(y). Then f^{-1}(y) is a smooth submanifold of X (possibly empty). If nonempty it has codimension \dim Y, and for every x\in f^{-1}(y) its tangent space is \ker df_x.

For the holomorphic version, let X,Y be complex manifolds of complex dimensions n>m, let g:X\to Y be holomorphic, and let y\in g(X) satisfy \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb C}(dg_x)=m at every x\in g^{-1}(y). Then g^{-1}(y) is a complex submanifold of complex dimension n-m.

A smooth (respectively complex) manifold is locally described by open Euclidean (respectively complex Euclidean) coordinate charts with smooth (respectively holomorphic) transition maps. A submanifold is locally a coordinate linear subspace in such charts. The differential acts between tangent spaces; codimension is ambient dimension minus submanifold dimension.
