Statement

Let X,Y be topological spaces and k a commutative ring with identity. Write C_*(X;k) for singular chains and C^*(X;k)=\operatorname{Hom}_k(C_*(X;k),k). The tensor chain complex has differential \partial(\sigma\otimes\tau)=\partial\sigma\otimes\tau+(-1)^p\sigma\otimes\partial\tau for \sigma\in C_p(X;k). Put D_*=C_*(X;k)\otimes_k C_*(Y;k). Let F:C_*(X\times Y;k)\to D_* be the Alexander–Whitney map and G:D_*\to C_*(X\times Y;k) the Eilenberg–Zilber shuffle map. The dual maps G^*:C^*(X\times Y;k)\to D^* and F^*:D^*\to C^*(X\times Y;k) are cochain-homotopy inverses.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Dualize the chain-homotopy identities — outline

The Alexander–Whitney and Eilenberg–Zilber maps dualize (over any choice of commutative coefficient ring {\displaystyle k} with unity) to a pair of maps

{\displaystyle G^{*}\colon C^{*}(X\times Y)\rightarrow {\big (}C_{*}(X)\otimes C_{*}(Y){\big )}^{*},\quad F^{*}\colon {\big (}C_{*}(X)\otimes C_{*}(Y){\big )}^{*}\rightarrow C^{*}(X\times Y)}

which are also homotopy equivalences, as witnessed by the duals of the chain-homotopy identities for the Alexander–Whitney and shuffle maps, using the dual chain homotopies.
