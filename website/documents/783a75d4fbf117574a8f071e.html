Statement

Let (c_n)_{n\in\mathbb Z} be complex numbers such that \sum_{n\ne0}|nc_n|^2<\infty. Then \sum_{n\ne0}|c_n|<\infty and (\sum_{n\ne0}|c_n|)^2\le(\sum_{n\ne0}n^{-2})(\sum_{n\ne0}|nc_n|^2).

Proof 1: Apply Cauchy–Schwarz to the weighted factors — outline

By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality,

\left(\sum_{n\ne0}|c_n|\right)^2 \leq\sum_{n\ne0}\frac1{n^2}\cdot\sum_{n\ne0}|nc_n|^2.

This means that the coefficient sequence is absolutely summable.

Level justification

Apply finite Cauchy-Schwarz to the weighted factors and pass through monotone partial sums using the convergent reciprocal-square series. Standard series analysis suffices; no Fourier theory is actually used.
