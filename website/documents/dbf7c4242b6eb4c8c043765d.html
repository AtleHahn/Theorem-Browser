Statement

For a field K and integer d\ge1, the projective space of nonzero homogeneous degree-d polynomials in three variables, modulo scalar, has dimension \binom{d+2}{2}-1=(d^2+3d)/2.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Homogeneous polynomials form a finite-dimensional coefficient vector space over the given field. Projective space here is explicitly nonzero vectors modulo common scalar; its dimension convention adds a short definition to undergraduate linear and abstract algebra, without a general theory of algebraic-variety dimension.

Proof 1: Count monomials, then projectivize — outline

The parameter space of curves is projective space. Its dimension is the number of monomials of degree d in three variables, minus 1 from projectivization:

{\displaystyle {\frac {(d+1)(d+2)}{2}}-1={\frac {d^{2}+3d}{2}}.}
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Count homogeneous monomials as a vector-space basis and pass to nonzero vectors modulo scalar. Projective dimension as one less than vector dimension is a small extension of linear algebra; no general algebraic-variety dimension theory is used.
