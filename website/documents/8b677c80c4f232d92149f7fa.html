Statement

Let k be any field, V a finite-dimensional k-vector space, and p,q nonnegative integers. The ring of polynomial invariants of the algebraic group \mathrm{GL}(V) on (V^*)^p\oplus V^q is generated as a k-algebra by the pq pairings

c_{ij}(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_p,v_1,\ldots,v_q)=\alpha_i(v_j),\qquad 1\leq i\leq p,\quad1\leq j\leq q.

Here V^*=\operatorname{Hom}_k(V,k), and the coordinate ring is the polynomial ring in the coordinates of all covectors and vectors in any basis of V. Invariance means that after extending scalars to an algebraic closure \bar k, the polynomial is unchanged by every g\in\mathrm{GL}(V\otimes_k\bar k) acting by

(\alpha_i,v_j)\longmapsto(\alpha_i\circ g^{-1},gv_j).

Thus every invariant polynomial is a polynomial with coefficients in k in the functions c_{ij}. Invariance refers to this algebraic-group action, including when k is finite; it is not defined merely by testing the finite set of k-rational matrices.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The finite-field distinction requires algebraic closure and base extension as well as polynomial representations; the cumulative burden is beyond the conservative undergraduate allowance.
