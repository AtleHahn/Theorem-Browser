Statement

In the Erdős–Rényi graph G(n,p), where each possible edge on n vertices is present independently with probability p\in[0,1], let H be existence of a Hamiltonian cycle and C proper three-colorability. Then \Pr(H\cap C)\le\Pr(H)\Pr(C).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The finite random graph is explicitly defined by independent edge choices, and the claim compares probabilities of a spanning cycle and a three-coloring. These finite combinatorial and probability concepts are accessible at school level. This rating concerns understanding the statement, independently of the difficulty of its proof.

Proof 1: Apply Harris to opposite edge-monotonicities — outline

Existence of a Hamiltonian cycle is increasing when edges are added, while three-colorability is decreasing. The Harris inequality for an increasing and a decreasing event in a product measure therefore gives the stated negative correlation.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification
