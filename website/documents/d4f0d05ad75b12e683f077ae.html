Statement

If a smooth differential form \alpha on a smooth manifold is exact, meaning \alpha=d\omega for a smooth form \omega, then it is closed: d\alpha=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns exterior differentiation on arbitrary smooth manifolds.

Proof 1: Substitute an exact form into d²=0

The identity d^2=0 gives d\alpha=d(d\omega)=0.

Level

Level justification

The algebraic substitution is immediate, but its objects are general smooth manifold differential forms and their exterior derivative. That differential-geometric framework is not merely a scalar calculus notation.
