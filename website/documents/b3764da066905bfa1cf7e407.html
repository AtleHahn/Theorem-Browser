Statement

Let X,Y be Hausdorff topological spaces and T:X\to Y continuous. Suppose Borel probability measures (\mu_\varepsilon)_{\varepsilon>0} on X satisfy a large-deviation principle with good rate function I:X\to[0,\infty]. Here good means I is lower semicontinuous, not identically infinite, and every finite sublevel set is compact; the principle means, for every closed F and open G in X, \limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\mu_\varepsilon(F)\le-\inf_F I,\qquad\liminf_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\mu_\varepsilon(G)\ge-\inf_G I. Put \nu_\varepsilon(E)=\mu_\varepsilon(T^{-1}(E)) for Borel E\subseteq Y, and J(y)=\inf\{I(x):T(x)=y\}, with \inf\varnothing=\infty and \log0=-\infty. Then J is a good rate function and (\nu_\varepsilon) satisfies the same large-deviation bounds on Y with rate J.
