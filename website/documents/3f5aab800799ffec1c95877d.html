Statement

Define the Lucas numbers by L_0=2, L_1=1, and L_{n+2}=L_{n+1}+L_n for every integer n\geq0. For every prime p\geq5, there is a prime q satisfying

q\equiv1\pmod p,\qquad q\leq L_p.

The congruence means that p divides q-1; equivalently, the least prime of the form 1+np, with an integer n\geq1, is at most L_p.
