Statement

Every topological 3-manifold admits a compatible piecewise-linear structure and a compatible smooth structure. These are unique up to piecewise-linear homeomorphism and diffeomorphism, respectively: two such structures on the same underlying manifold give isomorphic manifolds in the corresponding category. A topological manifold is a Hausdorff second-countable space locally homeomorphic to Euclidean space. A smooth structure is an atlas with smooth coordinate changes, compatible with the given topology. A piecewise-linear structure is an atlas with coordinate changes piecewise linear after subdivisions, equivalently a compatible triangulation giving a piecewise-linear manifold. A diffeomorphism is a smooth bijection with smooth inverse.
