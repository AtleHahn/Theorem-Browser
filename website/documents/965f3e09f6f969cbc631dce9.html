Statement

Let k be an algebraically closed field and K the field of rational functions of an integral algebraic curve over k. Then K is C_1: every homogeneous polynomial f\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_N] of positive degree d in N>d variables has a zero in K^N other than (0,\ldots,0). An integral algebraic curve is an irreducible reduced algebraic variety of dimension one; its rational-function field consists of quotients of regular functions on nonempty affine open subsets. Homogeneous of degree d means every monomial has total degree d, and algebraically closed means every nonconstant one-variable polynomial has a root.
