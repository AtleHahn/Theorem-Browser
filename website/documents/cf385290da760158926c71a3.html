Statement

Let (M,g) be a smooth pseudo-Riemannian manifold and X a smooth Killing vector field, meaning \mathcal L_Xg=0. With \nabla the Levi-Civita connection, \Delta the trace-of-Hessian Laplacian and \operatorname{Ric} the Ricci tensor, \frac12\Delta\langle X,X\rangle=\langle\nabla X,\nabla X\rangle-\operatorname{Ric}(X,X). Here the brackets denote contractions with the metric; no positivity is assumed.

Beyond undergraduate level

Proof 1: Specialize the vector-length formula to a Killing field

Write \nabla for the Levi-Civita connection, \Delta for the trace-of-Hessian Laplacian (rough Laplacian on fields), \operatorname{div} for divergence, and \mathcal L_Xg for the metric Lie derivative, identifying vectors and covectors by the metric.

As an application of the Ricci commutation identities, the formula

{\displaystyle \Delta X=-\nabla (\operatorname {div} X)+\operatorname {div} ({\mathcal {L}}_{X}g)-\operatorname {Ric} (X,\cdot )}

holds for any vector field X on a pseudo-Riemannian manifold. As a consequence, there is

{\displaystyle {\frac {1}{2}}\Delta \langle X,X\rangle =\langle \nabla X,\nabla X\rangle -\nabla _{X}\operatorname {div} X+\langle X,\operatorname {div} ({\mathcal {L}}_{X}g)\rangle -\operatorname {Ric} (X,X).}

In the case that X is a Killing vector field, this simplifies to

{\displaystyle {\frac {1}{2}}\Delta \langle X,X\rangle =\langle \nabla X,\nabla X\rangle -\operatorname {Ric} (X,X).}

Level justification

Specializing the vector-length formula requires the Killing equation, its metric trace, the Levi-Civita connection and pseudo-Riemannian tensor contractions. This is a substantial differential-geometric framework, not a small calculus extension.
