Statement

In a convex tangential 2n-gon, n\ge2, the sum of the odd-numbered side lengths equals the sum of the even-numbered side lengths. A tangential polygon has one inscribed circle tangent to all its sides.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Only polygon side lengths and elementary Euclidean circle geometry are required.

Proof 1: One tangent segment from each vertex

Divide each side at its point of tangency. The two tangent segments from any vertex have equal lengths. Each sum of alternate side lengths contains exactly one segment from each vertex pair, so the sums are equal.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Split sides into tangent segments and observe each alternating sum contains one member from each equal pair. School Euclidean geometry and finite addition suffice.
