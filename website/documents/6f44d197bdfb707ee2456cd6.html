Proof 1: Symmetry of isogeny for elliptic curves — proof

The dual-isogeny theorem supplies an isogeny in the reverse direction, which proves symmetry.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof invokes dual isogenies of elliptic curves over a field. Elliptic algebraic groups and their isogeny morphisms are substantial arithmetic-geometric prerequisites despite the immediate symmetry deduction.
