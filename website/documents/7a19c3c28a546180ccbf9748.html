Statement

Let (A,\mathfrak m) be a commutative complete Noetherian local ring and let k\ge0 be an integer. Completeness means A\cong\varprojlim_n A/\mathfrak m^n, local means \mathfrak m is the unique maximal ideal, and Noetherian means every ideal is finitely generated. Write \operatorname{Spec}A for its prime ideals with the Zariski topology, whose closed sets are V(I)=\{\mathfrak p:I\subseteq\mathfrak p\}. A Noetherian scheme Y is called j-connected here if \dim Y>j and Y\setminus Z is connected for every closed subset Z with \dim Z<j; dimension is the supremum of lengths of chains of irreducible closed subsets, with \dim\varnothing=-1. If \operatorname{Spec}A is k-connected and f\in\mathfrak m, then \operatorname{Spec}(A/fA) is (k-1)-connected.
