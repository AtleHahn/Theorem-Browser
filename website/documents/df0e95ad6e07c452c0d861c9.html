Proof 1: Pull back by an affine map

Use Monsky's theorem: a square cannot be dissected into an odd number of triangles of equal area. Because invertible affine transformations preserve equidissections, it follows more generally that parallelograms (the affine images of squares) also do not have odd equidissections.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Pull a putative dissection back by an invertible affine map to the square, using uniform determinant area scaling and the supplied Monsky theorem. Undergraduate affine linear algebra suffices; the valuation proof of Monsky is not used.
