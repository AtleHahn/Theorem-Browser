For a prime p, integers x,y, and integer m\ge0, (x+y)^{p^m}\equiv x^{p^m}+y^{p^m}\pmod p.

Proof 1: Specialize Fermat’s congruence and iterate

Fermat’s little theorem gives a^p\equiv a\pmod p for every integer a. Apply it to x+y,x,y: (x+y)^p\equiv x+y\equiv x^p+y^p\pmod p. Iteration using the power-of-a-power identity yields the same congruence for p^m, with m=0 immediate.
