Statement

Let A be a commutative ring complete with respect to an ideal \mathfrak m, let f\in A[X], and let a\in A. If f(a)\in f'(a)^2\mathfrak m, then there exists b\in A such that f(b)=0 and b-a\in f'(a)\mathfrak m. Here f' is the formal polynomial derivative.

Proof 1: Set the number of variables to one

Apply the multivariable Hensel theorem with one polynomial in one variable. Its Jacobian matrix is the one-by-one matrix (f'(a)), whose determinant is f'(a). Thus the theorem's approximation condition f(a)\in(\det J_f(a))^2\mathfrak m is exactly the stated hypothesis, and its solution and correction conclusions give f(b)=0 and b-a\in f'(a)\mathfrak m.
