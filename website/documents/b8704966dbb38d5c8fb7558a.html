Statement

Put M=49598666989151226098104244512918. Every polynomial f\in\mathbb Z[x] with coefficients between 0 and M and with f(10) prime is irreducible in \mathbb Z[x]. The bound is sharp: replacing M by M+1 makes this universal assertion false. Irreducible means nonzero and not 1 or -1, with no factorization into two integer polynomials both different from 1 and -1.
