Statement

A compact connected one-dimensional cobordism between two nonempty connected zero-dimensional manifolds is isomorphic to the product of a point with [0,1], in the smooth, piecewise-linear or topological category under consideration.

Proof 1: A connected cobordism between connected zero-manifolds is an interval — source argument

The interval is the only connected cobordism between connected 0-manifolds. Each nonempty connected 0-manifold is a point, so this is the required product.

The classification of connected compact one-manifolds used here is not proved in the source.
