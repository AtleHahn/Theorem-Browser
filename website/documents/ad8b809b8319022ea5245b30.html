Statement

Let F have prime characteristic p and let E/F be an inseparable extension of degree p. Every \alpha\in E\setminus F generates E over F.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Extension degrees are vector-space dimensions; F(alpha) is the generated subfield. Characteristic p and inseparability add the definitions of vanishing p times one and repeated roots of a minimal polynomial, a small polynomial-algebra extension rather than a full separability theory.

Proof 1: Use prime degree in the tower law

The tower law gives p=[E:F]=[E:F(\alpha)][F(\alpha):F]. Since \alpha\notin F, the second factor is greater than one, hence equals the prime p. The first factor is therefore one, so F(\alpha)=E.
