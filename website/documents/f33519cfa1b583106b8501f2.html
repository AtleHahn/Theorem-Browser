Statement

Fix an integer n\ge1. Let X=\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb R)/\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb Z) with the quotient topology, identified with full-rank lattices \Lambda=A\mathbb Z^n in \mathbb R^n. A subset Y\subseteq X has compact closure if and only if there exist C,\epsilon>0 such that for every \Lambda=A\mathbb Z^n in Y,

|\det A|\le C\quad\text{and}\quad\|v\|_2\ge\epsilon\ \text{for every }0\ne v\in\Lambda.

Here \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb R) is the group of invertible real matrices, and \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb Z) the integer matrices with integer inverses. The quotient topology comes from the usual matrix topology. The number |\det A| is the covolume of \Lambda.

Level justification

Lattices and matrix-group quotients are concrete extensions of undergraduate linear algebra and point-set quotient topology; all bounds and objects are defined.
