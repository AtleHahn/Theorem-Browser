Statement

Let V be a finite-dimensional vector space over a field K, and let G\leq\operatorname{GL}(V) be finite. Assume \operatorname{char}K=0 or that its positive characteristic does not divide |G|. Let S=K[V]=\operatorname{Sym}(V^*) be the polynomial algebra on the dual space, with action (g\cdot f)(v)=f(g^{-1}v), and let A=S^G be its invariant subalgebra. The following conditions are equivalent:

  1. G is generated by pseudoreflections, meaning nonidentity elements s\in G for which \ker(s-I) has codimension one in V.
  2. A is a polynomial algebra over K.
  3. A is a regular ring.
  4. S is a free module over A.
  5. S is a projective module over A.

Here regular means Noetherian with every localization at a prime ideal a regular local ring; a Noetherian local ring is regular when its maximal ideal can be generated by as many elements as its Krull dimension. A free module has a basis, and a projective module is a direct summand of a free module.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The full five-way equivalence requires invariant rings, local regularity/Krull dimension and projective modules; these interdependent commutative-algebra notions substantially exceed the undergraduate baseline even with short definitions.
