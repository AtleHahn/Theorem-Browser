Statement

For a quadratic number field K, the unit group O_K^\times has rank 1 when K is real quadratic and rank 0 when it is imaginary quadratic. Rank means the maximal number of multiplicatively independent units.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The result is about units in quadratic number fields, not merely real or complex vector spaces.

Proof 1: Specialize the signature rank formula — outline

Use Dirichlet’s rank formula r=r_1+r_2-1, with r_1 real embeddings and r_2 nonreal conjugate pairs. It gives rank 1 for a real quadratic field and 0 for an imaginary quadratic field.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Insert the quadratic signatures into Dirichlet unit rank. Number-field integer rings, their units, field embeddings and multiplicative rank together exceed a confidently small extension of introductory algebra, despite the short arithmetic; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
