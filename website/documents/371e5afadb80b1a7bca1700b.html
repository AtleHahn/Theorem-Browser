Statement

For an integer d\ge1, the compatible L^1(\mathbb R^d) and L^2(\mathbb R^d) Fourier transforms extend to a well-defined linear map from L^1+L^2 into L^\infty+L^2, by \mathcal F(f_1+f_2)=\mathcal F_1f_1+\mathcal F_2f_2.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns compatibility and extension of functional-analytic Fourier operators.

Proof 1: Add the compatible endpoint transforms — outline

The two transforms agree on L^1\cap L^2. Therefore taking the sum of their values defines the same result for any decomposition f=f_1+f_2: the difference of two first components is the opposite difference of the second components and lies in the intersection.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Check independence of decomposition using agreement on L1 intersection L2. The algebra is simple but understanding the two Fourier extensions and sums of Lebesgue spaces requires measure-theoretic functional analysis.
