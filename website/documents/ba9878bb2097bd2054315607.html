Statement

Let p be prime and G a finite p-solvable group: it has a subnormal series whose factors are p-groups or groups of order prime to p. Assume G has no nontrivial normal p-subgroup and acts faithfully by linear transformations on a vector space over a field of characteristic p. If x\in G has order p^n, n\ge1, its minimal polynomial is (X-1)^r with r\le p^n, and at least one of the following holds: (i) r=p^n; (ii) p is a Fermat prime, the Sylow 2-subgroups of G are nonabelian, and r\ge p^n-p^{n-1}; (iii) p=2 and for some Mersenne prime q=2^m-1<2^n the Sylow q-subgroups of G are nonabelian and r\ge2^n-2^{n-m}. Here a Fermat prime is a prime of the form 2^{2^a}+1, a\ge0, a Mersenne prime is a prime 2^m-1, and a Sylow q-subgroup has order the largest power of q dividing |G|. Faithful means that only the identity acts as the identity transformation.
