Statement

Let f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C be entire. Suppose there are constants C>0, \tau\in\mathbb R, and c<\pi such that

|f(z)|\leq C e^{\tau|z|}\quad(z\in\mathbb C),\qquad |f(iy)|\leq C e^{c|y|}\quad(y\in\mathbb R).

If f(n)=0 for every integer n\geq0, then f(z)=0 for every z\in\mathbb C. Here entire means complex differentiable at every point of the complex plane.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate calculus and complex numbers suffice to learn the single definition of complex differentiability and entire function; the remaining hypotheses are explicit exponential inequalities. No specialized entire-function growth theory is required for understanding this fully specified statement.
