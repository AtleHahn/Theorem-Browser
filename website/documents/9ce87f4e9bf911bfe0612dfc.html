Statement

As even n\to\infty, the probability that a simple symmetric length-n random walk stays nonnegative and returns to zero is asymptotic to 2\sqrt{2/\pi}\,n^{-3/2}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Apply Stirling to the Catalan probability — outline

The exact probability for even n is 2^{-n}\binom n{n/2}/(n/2+1). By Stirling’s formula k!\sim\sqrt{2\pi k}(k/e)^k, this is asymptotic to 2\sqrt{2/\pi}\,n^{-3/2}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Insert Stirling asymptotics into the exact Catalan probability and simplify the powers and square-root factors. Undergraduate asymptotic limits and finite probability suffice.
