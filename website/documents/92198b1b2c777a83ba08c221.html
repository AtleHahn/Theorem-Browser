Proof 1: Use the M-test before integrating the zeta series — outline

Morera's theorem can be used in conjunction with the Weierstrass M-test to show the analyticity of the Riemann zeta function

\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1{n^s},\qquad\operatorname{Re}s>1.

The M-test justifies interchanging the integral along a closed curve and the sum. Each summand is analytic, so its contour integral is zero; Morera’s theorem gives the conclusion.

The source supplies this as the series analogue of its integral argument for the gamma function; it does not write out the compact-set majorant.
