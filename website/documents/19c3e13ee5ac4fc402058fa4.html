Statement

Let E be a Fréchet space over \mathbb R or \mathbb C, without a separability assumption. Its continuous dual E' with the topology \sigma(E',E) of pointwise convergence on E is K-analytic. Here a Fréchet space is a Hausdorff locally convex topological vector space with a complete translation-invariant metric inducing its topology; locally convex means zero has a basis of convex neighborhoods. The weak dual topology is generated by the seminorms \ell\mapsto|\ell(x)|, x\in E. A Hausdorff space is K-analytic if it is a continuous image of a K_{\sigma\delta} space. A K_{\sigma\delta} space is a subspace Z of a Hausdorff space H of the form Z=\bigcap_{m=1}^{\infty}\bigcup_{j=1}^{\infty}K_{mj} with each K_{mj} compact in H.
