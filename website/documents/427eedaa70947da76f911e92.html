Statement

Order the positive integers by a\le b when b divides a. Their join is gcd. Every positive integer x is the join of two strictly smaller elements in this order, so no positive integer is join-irreducible. Here join-irreducible means not expressible as the join of two strictly smaller elements.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Reverse divisibility, the gcd join and irreducibility are explicitly defined using positive integers.

Proof 1: Two distinct prime multipliers give a smaller join decomposition

Choose distinct primes p,q. The numbers xp and xq are strictly below x in reverse divisibility, and their join is \gcd(xp,xq)=x. Thus every x decomposes as a join of smaller elements.

Level

Level justification

Multiply by two distinct primes and compute their gcd as x. The reversed order and join are explicitly defined; elementary divisibility suffices.
