Statement

For every integer k\ge1 and real \delta\in(0,1] there exists an integer N\ge1 such that every A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\} with |A|\ge\delta N contains a,a+d,\ldots,a+(k-1)d for integers a,d\ge1. Equivalently, every A\subseteq\mathbb N of positive upper density contains such an arithmetic progression for every k. Positive upper density means there is \varepsilon>0 for which |A\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|\ge\varepsilon N for infinitely many integers N.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Density is expressed through elementary counts and infinitely many integers, without requiring advanced analysis.
