Statement

Let N\ge1 and let \mu be a nonnegative Borel measure on \mathbb R^N, finite on compact sets. For f\in L^1(\mu) define

f^*(x)=\sup_{r>0}\frac{1}{\mu(B(x,r))}\int_{B(x,r)}|f|\,d\mu,

where B(x,r) is the open Euclidean ball and a zero-mass ball contributes zero. There is a constant b_N depending only on N such that for all \lambda>0,

\lambda\mu(\{x:f^*(x)>\lambda\})\le b_N\int|f|\,d\mu.

Proof 1: Bounded-overlap subcover and the integral estimate

Use the Besicovitch covering theorem in the following source-stated form: a bounded set E' with one open ball centered at each of its points admits a countable subcover \mathcal G with S_{\mathcal G}=\sum_{B\in\mathcal G}\mathbf1_B\le b_N on all of \mathbb R^N. Also use the source-stated lower semicontinuity of f^*, hence measurability of its strict superlevel sets.

The set Eλ of the points x such that {\displaystyle f^{*}(x)>\lambda } clearly admits a Besicovitch cover Fλ by balls B such that

{\displaystyle \int \mathbf {1} _{B}\,|f|\ d\mu =\int _{B}|f(y)|\,d\mu (y)>\lambda \,\mu (B).}

For every bounded Borel subset E´ of Eλ, one can find a subcollection G extracted from Fλ that covers E´ and such that SG ≤ bN, hence

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\lambda \,\mu (E')&\leq \lambda \,\sum _{B\in \mathbf {G} }\mu (B)\\&\leq \sum _{B\in \mathbf {G} }\int \mathbf {1} _{B}\,|f|\,d\mu =\int S_{\mathbf {G} }\,|f|\,d\mu \leq b_{N}\,\int |f|\,d\mu ,\end{aligned}}}

The bound for bounded Borel subsets of the superlevel set implies the stated inequality.

Level justification

The argument requires general locally finite Borel measures, L1 equivalence classes, centered maximal averages, measurable superlevel sets and interchange of countable nonnegative integrals. The covering estimate is geometric, but this cumulative measure-theoretic framework is substantially beyond the undergraduate allowance. The endpoint zero-mass convention and measurability input are relevant to the actual theorem, not optional notation.
