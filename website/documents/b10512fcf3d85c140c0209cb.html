Statement

For a finitely generated group H, let \operatorname{rank}(H) be the least size of a generating set. For finitely generated groups A and B, \operatorname{rank}(A*B)\le\operatorname{rank}(A)+\operatorname{rank}(B).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A free group is described by words with adjacent inverse cancellations; a free product similarly alternates nonidentity factors. These concrete word models extend familiar group operations and make the stated subsets, rank bound or matrix-group freeness intelligible without geometric group theory.

Proof 1: Upper bound for the rank of a free product

Choose minimum generating sets X of A and Y of B. Their union generates the free product and has size at most |X|+|Y|. This gives the inequality.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The union of minimum generating sets generates the free product, so count its size. Free products add the reduced-word construction to introductory groups without requiring Grushko theorem.
