Proof 1: Apply weak Goldbach to the odd-integer cases outline

Invoke the weak Goldbach assertion: every odd integer greater than 5 is a sum of three primes, with repetitions allowed. Waring’s prime-or-three-primes assertion follows directly.

Level justification

Apply the stated three-prime theorem to odd integers above five and check five is prime. Only integer and prime definitions are required to use the theorem.
