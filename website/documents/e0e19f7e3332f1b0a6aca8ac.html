Statement

Let P:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C be a polynomial function. If P is injective, then P is bijective.

Level
High school only

Complex polynomials and one-to-one/onto functions are elementary function concepts. The full polynomial-root assertion needed is supplied in the companion.

Proof 1: Algebraic closure in one variable (outline)

The case of one complex variable follows because \mathbb C is algebraically closed: every nonconstant complex polynomial has a complex root.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Injectivity excludes a constant polynomial; apply the stated root theorem to P(z)-w for each complex w. High-school complex polynomial algebra and the explicit root assertion suffice; its proof is not invoked.
