Statement

On a pseudo-Riemannian manifold of dimension n\ge4, if the Weyl tensor vanishes, then the Schouten tensor in the normalization

S_{ij}=\frac2{n-2}R_{ij}-\frac{R}{(n-2)(n-1)}g_{ij}

is Codazzi: \nabla_kS_{il}=\nabla_lS_{ik} for all indices. Here \nabla is the Levi-Civita connection, R_{ij} the Ricci tensor and R scalar curvature.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Weyl, Schouten and Ricci tensors and covariant derivatives require substantial pseudo-Riemannian geometry. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: The contracted Bianchi relation

As can be calculated by the Bianchi identities, the Weyl and Schouten tensors satisfy the relation

\nabla^jW_{ijkl}=\frac{n-3}{2}(\nabla_kS_{il}-\nabla_lS_{ik}).

This identity is used as an external prerequisite. If n\geq4 and the Weyl tensor is zero, the Schouten derivative difference is then zero due to this identity, since n-3\ne0.

Level justification

Set the Weyl tensor to zero in the stated covariant Bianchi relation. Understanding the tensors and covariant derivative identity requires pseudo-Riemannian geometry despite the scalar cancellation.
