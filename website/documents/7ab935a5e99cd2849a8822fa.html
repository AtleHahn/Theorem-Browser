Statement

An integer n\ge3 is prime if and only if

\prod_{k=1}^{n-1}(2^k-1)\equiv n\pmod{2^n-1}.

A congruence a\equiv b\pmod m means that m divides a-b.

High school only

Level justification

The statement and this prime-index proof use integer divisibility, induction, trigonometric addition and finite polynomial algebra. The roots are explicitly cos(2πj/p)+i sin(2πj/p); repeated multiplication and the polynomial root bound prove the required factorization. Division by a monic integer polynomial preserves integer coefficients. General cyclotomic irreducibility, field extensions and the fundamental theorem of algebra are unnecessary.
