Statement

Let X be a scheme and L_0,\ldots,L_N line bundles with sections s_i whose nonvanishing opens X_i cover X. Put \mathcal E=\bigoplus_{i=0}^N L_i and let s:\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal E send 1 to (s_0,\ldots,s_N). Then s is a locally split injection and \mathcal F=\operatorname{coker}(s) is a vector bundle of rank N.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Line bundles; Sections; Vector-bundle cokernels. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Split using a nonvanishing component

On X_i, s is a split monomorphism since it is inverted by projection \mathcal E\to L_i followed by the inverse of the trivialization s_i:\mathcal O_{X_i}\to L_i|_{X_i}. Therefore \mathcal F is a vector bundle over X_i, of rank N, and because these open sets cover X, \mathcal F is a vector bundle.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The argument requires schemes, invertible sheaves, local trivializations, sections and locally free cokernels. A short local splitting computation does not make those objects a small extension of undergraduate linear algebra.
