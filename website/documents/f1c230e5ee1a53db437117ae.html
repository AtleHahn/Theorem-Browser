Statement

Let B,C be groups and \alpha,\omega:C\to B injective homomorphisms. For the graph of groups with a single vertex group B and a single geometric loop-edge group C, the fundamental group is

G=\langle B,e\mid e^{-1}\alpha(c)e=\omega(c),\ c\in C\rangle.

Writing H=\alpha(C), K=\omega(C) and \phi=\omega\circ\alpha^{-1}:H\to K, the map \phi is an isomorphism and this presentation is equivalently \langle B,e\mid e^{-1}he=\phi(h),h\in H\rangle, an HNN extension.

Proof 1: Source presentation calculation

Let \mathbf A be a graph of groups on a connected graph A with vertex set V and oriented edge set E. Edge reversal e\mapsto\bar e is a fixed-point-free involution, o(e) and t(e)=o(\bar e) are the endpoints, and E^+ chooses one orientation of each geometric edge. Each vertex has a group A_v, each edge has a group A_e=A_{\bar e}, and there are injective boundary maps \alpha_e:A_e\to A_{o(e)}, \omega_e=\alpha_{\bar e}:A_e\to A_{t(e)}. Choose a spanning tree T. Write F(S) for the free group on a set S and \langle\!\langle R\rangle\!\rangle for the normal subgroup generated by relators R. Define G=\pi_1(\mathbf A,T) as the quotient of (\ast_{v\in V}A_v)\ast F(E) by \bar e\alpha_e(c)e=\omega_e(c), e\bar e=1 for all e\in E,c\in A_e, and e=1 for edges of T.

Let A be a graph of groups consisting of a single loop-edge e (together with its formal inverse \bar e), a single vertex v = o(e) = t(e), a vertex group B = Av, an edge group C = Ae and the boundary monomorphisms {\displaystyle \alpha =\alpha _{e}:C\to B,\omega =\omega _{e}:C\to B}. Then T = v is a spanning tree in A and the fundamental group π1(A, T) is isomorphic to the HNN extension

{\displaystyle G=\langle B,e|e^{-1}\alpha (c)e=\omega (c),c\in C\rangle .}

with the base group B, stable letter e and the associated subgroups H = α(C), K = ω(C) in B. The composition {\displaystyle \phi =\omega \circ \alpha ^{-1}:H\to K} is an isomorphism and this HNN-extension presentation of G can be rewritten as

{\displaystyle G=\langle B,e|e^{-1}he=\phi (h),h\in H\rangle .\,}
