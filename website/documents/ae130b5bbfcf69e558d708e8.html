Statement

Let g:D\to U be a conformal isomorphism from the unit disk to a domain in the Riemann sphere. Then g extends continuously to \overline D with values in the sphere if and only if \partial U is locally connected.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic topology supplies boundary, homeomorphism and local connectedness; the Riemann sphere is the plane with its point at infinity. A conformal isomorphism here is a holomorphic bijection, a limited extension of complex differentiability. The assertion concerns continuous extension to a boundary and does not require Riemann-surface or branched-cover theory.

Proof 1: Local connectivity characterizes continuous conformal extension — outline

Torhorst proved that local connectivity of \partial U is equivalent to the domain having only prime ends of the first kind. By the theory of prime ends, the latter property, in turn, is equivalent to g having a continuous extension.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Both imported equivalences require prime ends and their classification for conformal domains on the Riemann sphere. This substantial complex boundary theory cannot be acquired as a small extension of point-set topology.
