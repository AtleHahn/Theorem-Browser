Statement

Let K be a field, G any group and H\le G. For a left K[H]-module M and a left K[G]-module N, restriction along K[H]\hookrightarrow K[G] is denoted \operatorname{Res}N. There is a natural bijection

\operatorname{Hom}_{K[G]}(K[G]\otimes_{K[H]}M,N)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{K[H]}(M,\operatorname{Res}N).
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The argument is a specialization of abstract module adjunction over group rings.

Proof 1: Group-algebra specialization of scalar adjunction

Use the general extension/restriction of scalars adjunction stated by the source: for a unital ring homomorphism R\to S, extension S\otimes_R- is left adjoint to restriction of scalars, giving natural bijections \operatorname{Hom}_S(S\otimes_R M,N)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,\operatorname{Res}N). Apply it to the inclusion K[H]\hookrightarrow K[G]. The source identifies extension here with induction and restriction with the restricted group representation, giving the displayed correspondence.

Level justification

Specialize scalar extension/restriction to a group-algebra inclusion. General modules, balanced tensor products, group algebras and natural adjunction bijections require substantial algebra beyond the baseline.
