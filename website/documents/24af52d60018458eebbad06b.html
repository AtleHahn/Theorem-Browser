Statement

Let f:X\to Y be a proper morphism of complex algebraic varieties. For the rational intersection cohomology complex IC_X with the perverse normalization, there is an isomorphism

Rf_*IC_X\cong\bigoplus_{i\in\mathbb Z}{}^pH^i(Rf_*IC_X)[-i]

in the bounded derived category D_c^b(Y,\mathbb Q) of sheaves with constructible cohomology. Each perverse cohomology sheaf {}^pH^i(Rf_*IC_X) is semisimple: it is a finite direct sum of simple perverse sheaves, equivalently of intersection cohomology complexes with irreducible local-system coefficients on subvarieties of Y, pushed forward to Y.

Here Rf_* is the derived direct-image functor; [-i] denotes cohomological shift, and {}^pH^i is the cohomology functor for the middle-perversity t-structure. Constructible cohomology means that the cohomology sheaves restrict to finite-dimensional local systems on the strata of a finite algebraic stratification. A local system is a locally constant sheaf of vector spaces; irreducibility means it has no nonzero proper local subsystem. A simple object has no nonzero proper subobject.

A perverse sheaf is a constructible complex K for which, for every integer i, the set of points y with H^{-i}(j_y^*K)\ne0 or H^i(j_y^!K)\ne0 has real dimension at most 2i. Here j_y includes the point, and j_y^* and j_y^! give the derived stalk and costalk. The intersection complex extends the constant sheaf on the smooth dense stratum by the middle-perversity extension-and-truncation construction. Its normalization restricts to \mathbb Q[d] on a smooth component of complex dimension d; on a variety with several components, take the direct sum of the component intersection complexes. Properness is the algebraic condition of being separated, of finite type and universally closed.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The complete decomposition requires derived sheaf theory, perverse cohomology and intersection complexes on singular varieties. These are substantial interdependent graduate-level constructions, not a short vocabulary extension of undergraduate topology and algebra.
