Statement

For a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group G, write \widehat G for its continuous homomorphisms to \mathbb T=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=1\}, with pointwise multiplication and the compact-open topology. Give the underlying group of \widehat G the discrete topology, calling it H. Then \widehat H is compact and the map b:G\to\widehat H given by b(x)(\chi)=\chi(x) is a continuous homomorphism.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The stated objects require locally compact abelian groups, discrete and compact groups, dual homomorphisms and pontryagin evaluation, beyond the calculus and finite-dimensional linear-algebra baseline.

Proof 1: Dualize the discrete identity map

The identity-on-elements map \iota:H\to\widehat G is a continuous homomorphism, since its domain is discrete. Duality supplies the continuous dual morphism \widehat\iota:\widehat{\widehat G}\to\widehat H. Compose it with the evaluation isomorphism G\to\widehat{\widehat G} to obtain b. Its displayed evaluation formula follows by precomposition. Since H is discrete, its dual is compact.
