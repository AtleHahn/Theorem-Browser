Statement

Let p_i\ge0 for integers i\ge0 with \sum_{i=0}^\infty p_i=1. The nonnegative integer random variable with probabilities \mathbb P(X=i)=p_i has expectation m=\sum_{i=0}^\infty i p_i, possibly infinite. For a>0, \mathbb P(X\ge a):=\sum_{i\ge a}p_i\le m/a.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Nonnegative real series; Integer-indexed probabilities. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Compare the nonnegative tail series

All terms are nonnegative, so

m=\sum_{i\ge0}i p_i\ge\sum_{i\ge a}i p_i\ge a\sum_{i\ge a}p_i=a\mathbb P(X\ge a).

Divide by a>0.
