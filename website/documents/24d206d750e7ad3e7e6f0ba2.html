Statement

Under the model of independent uniform symbols in a countably infinite family of infinite strings W_k over a finite alphabet of q\ge2 symbols, every specified nonempty finite word w is a prefix of infinitely many W_k, almost surely.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The independent coloring or symbol experiment is explicit. Elementary probability gives products for finitely specified choices, and almost surely means probability one. These concrete sequence/path events need no martingale or general stochastic-process machinery to understand.

Proof 1: Apply Borel–Cantelli to the independent prefix events

Use the second Borel–Cantelli lemma: independent events whose probabilities have divergent sum occur infinitely often with probability one.

Let Ek be the event that the kth string begins with the fixed word w. Because this has the fixed nonzero probability p=q^{-|w|} of occurring, the Ek are independent, and the below sum diverges,

{\displaystyle \sum _{k=1}^{\infty }P(E_{k})=\sum _{k=1}^{\infty }p=\infty ,}

the probability that infinitely many of the Ek occur is 1.
