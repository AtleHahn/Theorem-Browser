Statement

Let X_1,X_2,\ldots be independent identically distributed real random variables with \Lambda(t)=\log\mathbb E e^{tX_1}<\infty for every real t. Put S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i and I(x)=\sup_{t\in\mathbb R}(tx-\Lambda(t)). The distributions \mu_n of S_n/n satisfy a large-deviation principle with rate I: for every closed F\subseteq\mathbb R and open G\subseteq\mathbb R, \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log\mu_n(F)\le-\inf_F I,\qquad\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log\mu_n(G)\ge-\inf_G I, where \log0=-\infty and \inf\varnothing=\infty.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Setwise probability asymptotics combined with rate functions exceed the conservative baseline; the tail-only formula is simpler.
