Statement

Let G be a finite simple graph on n>0 vertices. For disjoint nonempty vertex sets A,B, put d(A,B)=e(A,B)/(|A||B|), where e(A,B) counts edges between the sets, and put q(A,B)=|A||B|d(A,B)^2/n^2. For partitions \mathcal A,\mathcal B of disjoint nonempty sets, put q(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)=\sum_{A\in\mathcal A,B\in\mathcal B}q(A,B). Partition cells are nonempty; empty cells arising from a split are omitted. Let U,W be disjoint nonempty vertex sets and let 0<\varepsilon<1, and suppose U_1\subseteq U,W_1\subseteq W satisfy |U_1|\ge\varepsilon|U|, |W_1|\ge\varepsilon|W|, and |d(U_1,W_1)-d(U,W)|>\varepsilon. Then

q(\{U_1,U\setminus U_1\},\{W_1,W\setminus W_1\})>q(U,W)+\varepsilon^4\frac{|U||W|}{n^2}.

Proof 1: Variance contribution of the witnessing subpair

Let \mathcal P_U=\{U_1,U\setminus U_1\} and \mathcal P_W=\{W_1,W\setminus W_1\}, omitting empty cells. Choose vertices x uniformly from U and y independently and uniformly from W, with x in part U_i and y in part W_j. Define the random variable Z=d(U_i,W_j). Finite summation gives \mathbb E[Z]=d(U,W) and \mathbb E[Z^2]=n^2q(\mathcal P_U,\mathcal P_W)/(|U||W|). Then,

{\displaystyle \operatorname{Var}(Z)=\mathbb {E} [Z^{2}]-\mathbb {E} [Z]^{2}={\frac {n^{2}}{|U||W|}}\left(q\left(\{U_{1},U\backslash U_{1}\},\{W_{1},W\backslash W_{1}\}\right)-q(U,W)\right)}

But observe that {\displaystyle |Z-\mathbb {E} [Z]|=|d(U_{1},W_{1})-d(U,W)|} on an event of probability {\displaystyle {\frac {|U_{1}|}{|U|}}{\frac {|W_{1}|}{|W|}}}(corresponding to {\displaystyle x\in U_{1}} and {\displaystyle y\in W_{1}}), so

{\displaystyle \operatorname{Var}(Z)=\mathbb {E} [(Z-\mathbb {E} [Z])^{2}]\geq {\frac {|U_{1}|}{|U|}}{\frac {|W_{1}|}{|W|}}(d(U_{1},W_{1})-d(U,W))^{2}>\varepsilon \cdot \varepsilon \cdot \varepsilon ^{2}}

Rearranging gives the asserted energy boost.
