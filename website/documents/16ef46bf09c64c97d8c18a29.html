Statement

For a closed oriented n-manifold M, write T_i=\operatorname{torsion}H_i(M;\mathbb Z) and F_i=H_i(M;\mathbb Z)/T_i. There are abstract group isomorphisms F_i\cong F_{n-i} and T_i\cong T_{n-i-1}; no canonical choice is asserted.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Integral homology; Finitely generated abelian groups; Torsion subgroups and quotients; Abstract group isomorphisms. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Pass from perfect duality to abstract group isomorphisms (outline)

Poincaré duality and universal coefficients give F_i\cong\operatorname{Hom}(F_{n-i},\mathbb Z) and T_i\cong\operatorname{Hom}(T_{n-i-1},\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z). Use the standard algebraic facts that a finitely generated free abelian group is abstractly isomorphic to its integral dual and a finite abelian group to its character group in \mathbb Q/\mathbb Z. Homology of a closed manifold is finitely generated, so composing gives the two abstract isomorphisms. These identifications need not be canonical.
