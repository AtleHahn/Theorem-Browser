Statement

Let G be a finite group, p a prime, and P a Sylow p-subgroup (a subgroup of order the largest power of p dividing |G|). Write [H,H] for the subgroup generated by x^{-1}y^{-1}xy with x,y\in H, x^g=g^{-1}xg, and N_G(Q)=\{g\in G:g^{-1}Qg=Q\}. Let O^p(G)=\bigcap\{K\trianglelefteq G:G/K\text{ has order a power of }p\}. Let \gamma_1(G)=G and \gamma_{j+1}(G)=[\gamma_j(G),G], where [H,G] is generated by h^{-1}g^{-1}hg; set \gamma_\infty(G)=\bigcap_{j\ge1}\gamma_j(G). Then P\cap\gamma_\infty(G)=P\cap O^p(G)=\langle x^{-1}y:x,y\in P,\ y=x^g,\ g\in G,\ p\nmid |g|\rangle, where |g| is the order of g. The same subgroup is \langle x^{-1}x^g:Q\le P,\ x\in Q,\ g\in N_G(Q),\ p\nmid |g|\rangle.
