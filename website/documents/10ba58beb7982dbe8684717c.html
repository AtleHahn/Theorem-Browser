Statement

Let T_j:E\to F be bounded operators between Hilbert spaces, indexed by positive integers. If

A=\sup_j\sum_k\|T_jT_k^*\|^{1/2}<\infty,\qquad B=\sup_j\sum_k\|T_j^*T_k\|^{1/2}<\infty,

then \sum_jT_j converges in the strong operator topology to a bounded operator T:E\to F, and \|T\|\leq\sqrt{AB}. Strong convergence means that \sum_{j=1}^nT_jv\to Tv in F for every v\in E.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement specifies convergence of arbitrary Hilbert-space operators in the strong topology and an operator-norm bound.

Proof 1: Finite Gram estimate and Cauchy tails — outline

Finite estimate

For a finite collection T_1,\ldots,T_n, we first prove

\sum_{i,j}|(T_iv,T_jv)| \leq\left(\max_i\sum_j\|T_i^*T_j\|^{1/2}\right) \left(\max_i\sum_j\|T_iT_j^*\|^{1/2}\right)\|v\|^2.

Fix v\in E. To prove the finite estimate set

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {R=\sum c_{ij}T_{i}^{*}T_{j}}}

with |c_{ij}|\leq1 chosen so that

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {(Rv,v)=|(Rv,v)|=\sum |(T_{i}v,T_{j}v)|.}}

Then

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\|R\|^{2m}=\|(R^{*}R)^{m}\|\leq \sum \|T_{i_{1}}^{*}T_{i_{2}}T_{i_{3}}^{*}T_{i_{4}}\cdots T_{i_{4m}}\|\leq \sum \left(\|T_{i_{1}}^{*}\|\|T_{i_{1}}^{*}T_{i_{2}}\|\|T_{i_{2}}T_{i_{3}}^{*}\|\cdots \|T_{i_{4m-1}}^{*}T_{i_{4m}}\|\|T_{i_{4m}}\|\right)^{1 \over 2}.}}

Hence

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\|R\|^{2m}\leq n\cdot \max \|T_{i}\|\left(\max _{i}\sum _{j}\|T_{i}^{*}T_{j}\|^{1 \over 2}\right)^{2m}\left(\max _{i}\sum _{j}\|T_{i}T_{j}^{*}\|^{1 \over 2}\right)^{2m-1}.}}

Taking 2mth roots and letting m tend to ∞,

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\|R\|\leq \left(\max _{i}\sum _{j}\|T_{i}^{*}T_{j}\|^{1 \over 2}\right)\left(\max _{i}\sum _{j}\|T_{i}T_{j}^{*}\|^{1 \over 2}\right),}}

which immediately implies the inequality.

All sums here have indices in \{1,\ldots,n\}. If all operators are zero, the result is immediate; otherwise the two row-sum constants are positive.

Strong convergence

So under the hypotheses of the lemma,

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\sum _{i,j}|(T_{i}v,T_{j}v)|\leq AB\|v\|^{2}.}}

It follows that

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\|\sum _{i=1}^{n}T_{i}v\|^{2}\leq AB\|v\|^{2},}}

and that

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {\|\sum _{i=m}^{n}T_{i}v\|^{2}\leq \sum _{i,j\geq m}|(T_{i}v,T_{j}v)|.}}

Hence, the partial sums

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {s_{n}=\sum _{i=1}^{n}T_{i}v}}

form a Cauchy sequence.

The sum therefore converges in the Hilbert space F with the limit satisfying the stated inequality.
