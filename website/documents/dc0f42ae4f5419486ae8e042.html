Statement

Let M be a compact oriented smooth manifold of dimension 2n without boundary. For each Riemannian metric g, let \Omega_g be its Levi–Civita curvature matrix and put e(\Omega_g)=(2\pi)^{-n}\operatorname{Pf}(\Omega_g). Then \int_M e(\Omega_g) is independent of g.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Integrating the Euler curvature form and comparing metrics requires differential geometry and characteristic classes. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use the metric-independent Euler cohomology class

The naturality of the Euler class means that when changing the Riemannian metric, one stays in the same cohomology class. That means that the integral of the Euler class remains constant as the metric is varied and is thus a global invariant of the smooth structure.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The argument identifies curvature representatives of an Euler class and integrates their de Rham classes. Characteristic classes, connections and de Rham cohomology are cumulative graduate-level prerequisites.
