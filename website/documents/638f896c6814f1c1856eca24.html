Statement

Let K be an algebraically closed field of characteristic p>0, let C be a smooth integral projective curve over K of genus g, and let S\subset C(K) be a nonempty finite set with |S|=s+1. For a finite group G, let p(G) be the subgroup generated by all its Sylow p-subgroups. Then a finite Galois extension of the rational-function field K(C) with group isomorphic to G, unramified outside S, exists if and only if G/p(G) can be generated by at most s+2g elements. Unramified outside S means that on the smooth projective curve corresponding to the extension the finite map to C has ramification index one at all points above C\setminus S; equivalently its restriction there is étale. The genus is the dimension over K of the space of regular differential one-forms on C. A Sylow p-subgroup has the largest p-power order dividing |G|. For C=\mathbb P^1_K, the criterion becomes at most s generators.
