Statement

Let i:H\to E be a real abstract Wiener space, with H a separable Hilbert space, E a separable Banach space, i a continuous injective linear map with dense image, and \gamma its abstract Wiener Borel probability measure. Write j:E^*\to H for the adjoint of i, using the Hilbert-space identification of H with its dual. Let W:H\to L^2(E,\gamma) be the Paley–Wiener map, the continuous linear extension of j(f)\mapsto f for f\in E^*, and put W_h=W(h).

For every h\in H, the pushforward under T_{i(h)}(x)=x+i(h) is equivalent to \gamma, with

\frac{d(T_{i(h)})_*\gamma}{d\gamma}(x)=\exp\left(W_h(x)-\tfrac12\|h\|_H^2\right)\qquad\text{for }\gamma\text{-almost every }x.

More generally, for v\in E, the translated measure (T_v)_*\gamma is equivalent to \gamma if and only if v\in i(H). Here T_v(x)=x+v, (T_v)_*\gamma(A)=\gamma(A-v) for Borel sets A, and equivalence means that the measures have the same null sets.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full statement requires Gaussian measures on Banach spaces, Cameron–Martin geometry, L2 extensions and Radon–Nikodym derivatives; the cumulative functional-analytic and measure-theoretic background is substantially beyond introductory analysis.
