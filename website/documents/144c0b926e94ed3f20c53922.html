Statement

Let K be a field and a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4\in K, with a_4 not a square in K (there is no b\in K with b^2=a_4). If the equation y^2=a_4x^4+a_3x^3+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0 has a solution (x,y)\in L^2 for a finite extension L/K of odd degree, then it has a solution in K^2. Here the degree [L:K] is the dimension of L as a K-vector space.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic undergraduate field theory suffices; no elliptic-curve geometry is needed to understand the affine equation.
