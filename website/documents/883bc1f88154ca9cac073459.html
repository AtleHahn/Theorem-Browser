Statement

Fix T>0. Let C_0 be the Banach space of continuous real paths on [0,T] starting at 0, with the supremum norm. Equip it with Wiener measure \gamma, the law of standard Brownian motion, and write B_t(\sigma)=\sigma(t) and \mathcal F_t=\sigma(B_s:0\le s\le t). Let H consist of absolutely continuous paths h with h(0)=0 and \int_0^T|\dot h(t)|^2dt<\infty, with inner product \langle h,k\rangle_H=\int_0^T\dot h\dot k\,dt. Let F:C_0\to\mathbb R be bounded and Fréchet differentiable with bounded derivative DF:C_0\to C_0^*. Define \nabla_HF(\sigma)\in H by \langle\nabla_HF(\sigma),h\rangle_H=DF(\sigma)[h], and put D_tF(\sigma)=\partial_t\nabla_HF(\sigma)(t). Then, \gamma-almost surely, F=\mathbb E_\gamma F+\int_0^T\mathbb E_\gamma[D_tF\mid\mathcal F_t]\,dB_t. The stochastic integral is the Itô integral and the expectations are with respect to Wiener measure.
