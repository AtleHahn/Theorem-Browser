Statement

Let \tau=x+iy with y>0. For \operatorname{Re}s>1 define

E(\tau,s)=\sum_{(m,n)\in\mathbb Z^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}}\frac{y^s}{|m\tau+n|^{2s}},\qquad \eta(\tau)=e^{\pi i\tau/12}\prod_{n=1}^\infty(1-e^{2\pi i n\tau}).

The meromorphic continuation of E(\tau,s) has, as s\to1 with \tau fixed, the expansion

E(\tau,s)=\frac\pi{s-1}+2\pi\left(\gamma-\log2-\log(\sqrt y\,|\eta(\tau)|^2)\right)+O(s-1),

where \gamma=\lim_{N\to\infty}(\sum_{k=1}^N1/k-\log N) is Euler's constant. The error is bounded in modulus by a constant depending on \tau times |s-1| near 1.
