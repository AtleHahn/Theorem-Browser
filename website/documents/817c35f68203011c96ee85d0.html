Statement

Let \mathbf{sAb} be the category of simplicial abelian groups and simplicial homomorphisms, and let \mathbf{Ch}_{\geq0}(\mathbf{Ab}) be the category of chain complexes of abelian groups concentrated in degrees 0,1,2,\ldots, with chain maps. A simplicial abelian group is a functor \Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Ab}, where \Delta has the finite nonempty ordered sets [n]=\{0,\ldots,n\} as objects and order-preserving maps as morphisms.

For a simplicial abelian group A, write d_i:A_n\to A_{n-1} for its face maps. The normalized chain-complex functor is

N(A)_0=A_0,\qquad N(A)_n=\bigcap_{i=0}^{n-1}\ker d_i\ (n\geq1),\qquad \partial_n=(-1)^n d_n|_{N(A)_n},

with \partial_0=0; on morphisms it acts by restriction.

This functor is an equivalence of categories. Thus there is a functor \Gamma:\mathbf{Ch}_{\geq0}(\mathbf{Ab})\to\mathbf{sAb} and natural isomorphisms N\Gamma\cong\mathrm{id} and \Gamma N\cong\mathrm{id}. Under the equivalence,

H_n(N(A))\cong\pi_n(A)\qquad(n\geq0),

where H_n is chain-complex homology and \pi_n(A) is the homotopy group of the underlying simplicial set, based at zero (with \pi_0 its group of components). Chain homotopies correspond to simplicial homotopies.
