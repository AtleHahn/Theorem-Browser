Statement

Let T be a bounded operator on a complex Hilbert space H with \|T\|\le1, and let p be a complex polynomial. Then \|p(T)\|\le\sup_{|z|\le1}|p(z)|.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A Hilbert/Banach setting adds completeness to normed linear algebra. Operator powers, orthogonal projection, continuous scalar functionals and unit-ball norm bounds are direct extensions. The statements use only these explicit identities/convergence conditions, without weak topology or spectrum.

Proof 1: Unitary dilation reduction (outline)

The inequality can be proved by considering the unitary dilation of T, for which the inequality follows from the unitary spectral theorem.

Here the unitary dilation theorem supplies a Hilbert space K\supseteq H and a unitary U with T^j=P_HU^j|_H for every integer j\geq0, where P_H is orthogonal projection. The unitary spectral theorem gives \|p(U)\|\leq\sup_{|z|=1}|p(z)|, and orthogonal compression cannot increase the norm. These are the external prerequisites for the source's dilation outline; the source does not expand the reduction.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Dilate the contraction to a unitary, apply infinite-dimensional spectral functional calculus and compress. Hilbert operator dilation and the unitary spectral theorem require functional analysis.
