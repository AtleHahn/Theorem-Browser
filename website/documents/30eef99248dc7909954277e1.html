Statement

Fix n\ge3. Let S:\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb C^n be S(a_1,\ldots,a_n)=(a_2,\ldots,a_n,a_1), and let I denote the identity. For \alpha\in\mathbb C with |\alpha|=1 and \alpha\ne1 put T_\alpha=(S-\alpha I)/(1-\alpha). The vertex centroid c(A)=\frac1n\sum_{r=1}^na_r satisfies c(T_\alpha A)=c(A). Hence every stage of any sequence of these transformations has the original vertex centroid.

Proof 1: Apply the cyclically invariant averaging functional

To show that all the centroids are equal, recall that the vertex centroid c(A) of any n-gon A is obtained by taking the average of all its vertices. Viewing A as a vector, this is

c(A)=(E,A),\qquad E=\frac1n(1,1,\ldots,1),

where (E,A)=\sum_{r=1}^n E_r a_r denotes the averaging functional.

Applying this functional to both sides of the equation

T_\alpha A=(1-\alpha)^{-1}(S-\alpha I)A,

and noting that averaging is invariant under the cyclic permutation operator S, we obtain

c(T_\alpha A)=(E,T_\alpha A) =(1-\alpha)^{-1}(1-\alpha)(E,A) =(E,A)=c(A),

so all the centroids are equal.
