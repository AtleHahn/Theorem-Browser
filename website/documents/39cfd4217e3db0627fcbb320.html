Statement

Let n,q,t,i be integers with q≥1,t≥1,i≥0 and t+2i≤n. Use an alphabet of q symbols. Fix symbols at t+2i chosen positions of length-n strings. The family of strings agreeing with at least t+i of those specified symbols has the property that any two strings agree at at least t positions.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The recipe only compares finite sets of agreeing positions.

Proof 1: Impose a template agreement threshold — outline

Choose t+2i positions and their symbols, and construct the family of strings that have at least t+i of those chosen symbols.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Two agreement sets of size at least t+i within t+2i positions overlap in at least t positions. School finite-set counting suffices.
