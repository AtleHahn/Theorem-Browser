Statement

For every integer n\ge1, every x,y\in\mathbb R^n and every real t,

\left(\frac{1+|x|^2}{1+|y|^2}\right)^t\le2^{|t|}(1+|x-y|^2)^{|t|},

where |x|=(x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2)^{1/2} is Euclidean length and |t| is absolute value.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Explicit coordinate length uses only elementary sums, roots and real powers; no Sobolev or operator-theory background is needed to understand the statement.
