Statement

For each n\ge1, let S_n be the family of subsets of \mathbb R^n defined by finite Boolean combinations of real polynomial equalities and inequalities. These families form an o-minimal structure: they are closed under Boolean operations, Cartesian products and coordinate projections, contain the coordinate equality and order relations, and each set in S_1 is a finite union of points and intervals.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Polynomial inequalities, Boolean set operations, products and projections are familiar undergraduate algebra/sets. Semialgebraic and o-minimal are explicitly defined by those operations and the interval condition, so no model-theoretic background is needed for this real-coordinate formulation.

Proof 1: Check the structure conditions using projection closure — outline

Finite unions and complements remain expressible by finite Boolean combinations of the same polynomial conditions. The Tarski–Seidenberg projection theorem gives closure under dropping a coordinate, and hence under successive coordinate projections. The standard one-variable semialgebraic decomposition says that every member of S_1 is a finite union of points and intervals. Together with the defining polynomial descriptions of the elementary coordinate relations and Cartesian products, these are the structure and o-minimality conditions.
