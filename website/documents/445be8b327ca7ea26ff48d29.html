Statement

Let f:X\to Y be a function and L,R arbitrary sets. For any set A, use f(A)=\{f(x):x\in A\cap X\} , f^{-1}(B)=\{x\in X:f(x)\in B\}, and F_y=f^{-1}(\{y\})=\{x\in X:f(x)=y\}. Then f(L\cap R)=f(L)\cap f(R)\setminus\{y\in f(L)\cap f(R):L\cap R\cap F_y=\varnothing\}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The complete formula uses only locally defined fibers, intersections and images, understandable by elementary membership tests.

Proof 1: Apply the image formula to the intersection

Apply the stated empty-fiber-section image formula to L\cap R. Its fiber section is L\cap R\cap F_y. Since f(L\cap R)\subseteq f(L)\cap f(R), this intersection of images is an allowed outer superset in that formula, giving the result.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply the image formula to L intersect R and choose the intersection of images as the outer superset. Elementary set operations suffice.
