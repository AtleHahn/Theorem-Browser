Statement

Fix an admissible numbering e\mapsto\varphi_e of the partial recursive functions on \mathbb N. Equality \simeq means equality of partial functions, including their domains. There is no total computable F such that \varphi_e\not\simeq\varphi_{F(e)} for every e.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Computability-theoretic indices and partial recursive functions are outside undergraduate calculus and linear algebra.

Mathematical logic

Proof 1: Contradict the fixed point guaranteed by Rogers

For any fixed total computable F, Rogers’s fixed-point theorem gives an index e with \varphi_e\simeq\varphi_{F(e)}. This contradicts the defining condition for a fixed-point-free map.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The contradiction is immediate from the supplied theorem, but the full setup requires admissible numberings of partial recursive functions and extensional equality, beyond just executable algorithms. This recursion-theoretic framework is not confidently a small undergraduate extension; retain beyond undergraduate in doubt.
