Statement

Let K be a totally imaginary number field, \mathcal O_K its ring of algebraic integers, and X=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_K. Let F be a constructible abelian étale sheaf in the finitely generated sense: on each member of a finite stratification of X by locally closed subschemes it is étale locally constant with finitely generated abelian stalks, which need not be torsion. For an abelian group A put A^*=\operatorname{Hom}(A,\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z). The Yoneda pairing with target H^3(X,\mathbb G_m)=\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z induces isomorphisms

H^r(X,F)^*\cong\operatorname{Ext}^{3-r}_X(F,\mathbb G_m)\quad(r=0,1),H^r(X,F)\cong\operatorname{Ext}^{3-r}_X(F,\mathbb G_m)^*\quad(r=2,3).

Totally imaginary means having no real field embedding; number fields are finite extensions of \mathbb Q. The étale site of X has étale morphisms U\to X as objects and jointly surjective étale families as coverings; an abelian sheaf assigns abelian groups with compatible restriction and gluing on that site. Write H^r(X,F) for the right derived functors of global sections, and \operatorname{Ext}^j_X(F,G) for the right derived functors of \operatorname{Hom}_X(F,G) in abelian étale sheaves. Both are zero in negative degrees. The sheaf \mathbb G_m assigns \Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)^\times to U. The Yoneda pairing is composition of extensions, using H^r(X,F)=\operatorname{Ext}^r_X(\mathbb Z,F), followed by the invariant identification H^3(X,\mathbb G_m)=\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z.
