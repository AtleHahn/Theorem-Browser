Statement

A global field is a finite extension of \mathbb Q or of \mathbb F_q(T), where \mathbb F_q is a finite field and T an indeterminate. An absolute value on K is a map |\cdot|:K\to[0,\infty) that vanishes exactly at zero, is multiplicative, and satisfies the triangle inequality; it is nontrivial if it is not identically 1 on K\setminus\{0\}. A place v means an equivalence class of nontrivial absolute values (equivalent when they induce the same topology), and K_v is the metric completion of K for that place. A quadratic form in n variables is a homogeneous degree-two polynomial over the field. Let K be a global field and let Q_1,Q_2 be quadratic forms in n\ge1 variables over K. There is an invertible K-linear change of variables A with Q_1(x)=Q_2(Ax) if and only if for every place v there is an invertible K_v-linear change of variables A_v with Q_1(x)=Q_2(A_vx) over K_v.
