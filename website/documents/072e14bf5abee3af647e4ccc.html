Statement

Let G be a finite group, p a prime, and P a Sylow p-subgroup (a subgroup of order the largest power of p dividing |G|). Write [H,H] for the subgroup generated by x^{-1}y^{-1}xy with x,y\in H, x^g=g^{-1}xg, and N_G(Q)=\{g\in G:g^{-1}Qg=Q\}. Define A^p(G) as the intersection of all normal subgroups K for which G/K is an abelian group of order a power of p. Then P\cap[G,G]=P\cap A^p(G)=\langle x^{-1}y:x,y\in P,\ y=x^g\text{ for some }g\in G\rangle. This subgroup also equals \langle x^{-1}x^g:Q\le P,\ x\in Q,\ g\in N_G(Q)\rangle. Angle brackets denote the subgroup generated by the indicated elements.
