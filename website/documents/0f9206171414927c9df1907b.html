Statement

Let K be a field of characteristic different from 2. A polynomial f\in K[x,y] is invariant under (x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y) if f(-x,-y)=f(x,y) as polynomials. The invariant subalgebra is K[x^2,xy,y^2].

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Polynomial substitution, ring centers, simple rings and rational partial fractions are introductory algebra. Invariance is explicitly a polynomial equality, and simple means no nontrivial two-sided ideals; no further structure is needed.

Proof 1: Use the three quadratic invariant generators — outline

For the group acting by \{I,-I\} on K^2, the invariant subalgebra of K[x,y] is generated by the three homogeneous quadratic elements x^2, xy, and y^2.
