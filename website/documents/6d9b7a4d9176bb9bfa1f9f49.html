Statement

A standard 52-card deck has 13 ranks, each appearing 4 times. If its cards are dealt into 13 piles of 4 cards each, it is possible to choose one card from each pile so that every rank occurs exactly once.

Proof 1: Selecting one card of each rank from thirteen piles — outline

Construct the source’s bipartite graph with piles on one side and ranks on the other, joining a pile to each rank appearing in it. For any k piles, their 4k cards must contain at least k distinct ranks since each rank occurs at most 4 times. Thus Hall’s condition holds. A pile-saturating matching pairs the 13 piles with distinct ranks; choose a card of the assigned rank from each pile.
