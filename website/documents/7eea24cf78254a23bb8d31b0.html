Statement

A Reuleaux triangle of width w>0 is bounded by three circular arcs of radius w, each joining two vertices of an equilateral triangle of side w and centered at the third vertex. Its perimeter is \pi w.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The result and arc calculation use elementary Euclidean geometry and radians.

Proof 1: Sum the three circular arcs

A Reuleaux triangle of width w consists of three arcs of circles of radius w. Each of these arcs has central angle π/3, so the perimeter of the Reuleaux triangle of width w is equal to half the perimeter of a circle of radius w and therefore is equal to πw.

High school only
Level justification

Sum three radius-w arcs of angle pi/3. School equilateral-triangle geometry and circle arc lengths suffice.
