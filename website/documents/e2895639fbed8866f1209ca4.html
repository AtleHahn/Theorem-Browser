Statement

Let n be a positive integer. If A,B\in\mathbb C^{n\times n} and B=U^*AU for a unitary matrix U, then \operatorname{tr}(BB^*)=\operatorname{tr}(AA^*). Equivalently, \|B\|_F=\|A\|_F, where \|A\|_F^2=\operatorname{tr}(AA^*).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Complex matrices; Unitary similarity; Trace and adjoint. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Cancel unitary factors inside the cyclic trace

Using UU^*=I and cyclic invariance of trace gives \operatorname{tr}(BB^*)=\operatorname{tr}(U^*AUU^*A^*U)=\operatorname{tr}(AUU^*A^*UU^*)=\operatorname{tr}(AA^*). Taking the nonnegative square roots yields equality of Frobenius norms.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Expand the adjoint product, cancel unitary factors and rotate factors in the trace. This is standard undergraduate complex linear algebra.
