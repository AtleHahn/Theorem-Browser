Statement

There is an absolute constant C>0 such that, for every positive integer N, the number of subsets S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\} with no x,y,z\in S satisfying x+y=z is at most C\,2^{N/2}. In this definition x and y may be equal; such an S is called sum-free.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement is elementary counting with a single quantified constant; no asymptotic notation or advanced additive-combinatorial prerequisites remain.
