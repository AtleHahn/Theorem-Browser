Statement

Let S be a nonempty collinear set of points in the Euclidean plane with integer pairwise distances and finite diameter \delta. Then |S|\le\delta+1.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement concerns points on a line, integer distances and a bound on their number. Diameter is understood as the least upper bound of pairwise distances; this concrete distance-and-counting assertion is treated as elementary even though finiteness is a conclusion rather than an initial assumption.

Proof 1: Count the separated positions along the line — outline

A collinear set with integer distances and diameter \delta has at most \delta+1 points.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Distinct points are spaced by at least one unit along a bounded segment. Any k of them span length at least k-1, so k<=delta+1; arbitrarily large finite subsets would violate this too. Only ordering and elementary counting are used.
