Statement

Let f be a smooth real function near 0\in\mathbb R^n with f(0)=0 and Df(0)=0. Let \mathbb R^n=V\oplus W be a direct-sum decomposition such that the Hessian of f|_V at 0 is nonsingular, and let B be its matrix in a chosen basis of V. There exist a smooth local change of coordinates fixing 0 of the form \Phi(x,y)=(\phi(x,y),y), for x\in V,y\in W, and a smooth real function h near 0\in W such that

f\circ\Phi(x,y)=\tfrac12x^TBx+h(y)

near (0,0). A local change of coordinates is a diffeomorphism between neighborhoods; smooth means infinitely differentiable. The Hessian is the matrix of second partial derivatives.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Multivariable calculus and linear algebra suffice; local diffeomorphism is explicitly a smooth invertible coordinate change.
