Proof 1: Use the natural numbers as a cofinal set — outline

The natural numbers are cofinal in the reals by the Archimedean property. No finite set is cofinal, since it has a largest element. Hence the least cofinal cardinal is \aleph_0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the Archimedean property to supply a countable unbounded set and finite maxima to exclude finite ones. Undergraduate cardinality and real-order basics suffice; cofinality is explicitly defined here.
