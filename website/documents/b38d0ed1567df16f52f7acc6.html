Statement

Circles A(B) and C(D) have distinct centers A,C and positive radii AB,CD. Suppose B\ne D and the midpoint M of BD does not lie on AC. With a fixed nondegenerate circle and its center, the following straightedge construction produces their radical axis.

Proof 1: Use the power center of three circles

Use the midpoint and perpendicular constructions available with the fixed circle and its center. The hypotheses ensure that A,M,C are noncollinear, so the lines j,k below are not parallel.

Suppose two circles A(B) and C(D) are implicitly given, defined only by the points A, B, C, and D in the plane, with their centers defined, but are not compass-drawn. The radical axis, line m, between the two circles may be constructed:

  1. Draw a line AC through the circle centers.
  2. Draw a line segment BD between the points on the circumference of the circles.
  3. Find the midpoint, M, of segment BD.
  4. Draw lines AM and CM, connecting the segment midpoint with each of the circle centers.
  5. Construct a line j passing through point B, and perpendicular to AM.
    • Line j is the radical axis between circle M(B) and circle A(B), as it passes through the point of intersection B and is perpendicular to the line through centers, AM.
  6. Construct a line k passing through point D, and perpendicular to CM.
    • Line k is the radical axis between circle M(B) = M(D) and circle C(D).
  7. Lines j and k intersect at point X.
    • Point X is the power center between circles A(B), C(D), and M(B), and is therefore the unique point that lies on the radical axis between any two of the three circles.
    • By transitivity, therefore, point X exists on the radical axis between circles A(B) and C(D).
  8. Construct a line m perpendicular to line AC and passing through point X.
    • Line m is the desired radical axis.

Level justification

Construct the auxiliary midpoint circle implicitly and use equal powers transitively at the two radical axes intersection. Point power is squared distance minus squared radius, a small elementary circle-geometry calculation.
