Statement

Let E be an elliptic curve over \mathbb Q, let \ell be prime and let n\ge1. After choosing a basis of E[\ell^n](\overline{\mathbb Q})\cong(\mathbb Z/\ell^n\mathbb Z)^2, the Galois action induces a group homomorphism

\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z/\ell^n\mathbb Z).

Here E[\ell^n] consists of points annihilated by multiplication by \ell^n.

Proof 1: Action on the torsion subgroup

{\displaystyle E({\bar {\mathbb {Q} }})}, the points of {\displaystyle E} over {\displaystyle {\bar {\mathbb {Q} }}}, form an abelian group on which {\displaystyle \operatorname {Gal} ({\bar {\mathbb {Q} }}/\mathbb {Q} )} acts; the subgroup of elements {\displaystyle x} such that {\displaystyle \ell ^{n}x=0} is just {\displaystyle (\mathbb {Z} /\ell ^{n}\mathbb {Z} )^{2}}, and an automorphism of this group is a matrix of the type described.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The construction uses elliptic torsion over the algebraic closure and the absolute Galois action respecting the curve group law. These arithmetic-geometric prerequisites remain substantial even though the final automorphism group is a matrix group.
