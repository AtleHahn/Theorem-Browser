Statement

Let (\alpha_k)_{k\ge1} be complex numbers and define (\beta_k)_{k\ge0} by the formal power-series identity

\sum_{k=0}^\infty\beta_kz^k=\exp\left(\sum_{k=1}^\infty\alpha_kz^k\right).

Thus \beta_0=1, and each coefficient is defined algebraically by the exponential power series. For every integer n\ge1,

\sum_{k=0}^n|\beta_k|^2\le(n+1)\exp\left(\frac1{n+1}\sum_{m=1}^n\sum_{k=1}^m\left(k|\alpha_k|^2-\frac1k\right)\right).
