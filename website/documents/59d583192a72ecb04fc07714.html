Statement

Let M be a compact differentiable manifold, possibly with boundary, and let v be a continuous nowhere-zero tangent vector field on M. If the boundary is nonempty, assume v points in the outward normal direction along it. Then the Euler characteristic of M is zero.

Proof 1: Use the empty index sum

By Poincare–Hopf the Euler characteristic equals the sum of the indices at the field’s zeros. There are no zeros, so this is the empty sum, equal to zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Poincare-Hopf and identify the empty index sum. Tangent bundles on general compact manifolds, vector-field index and Euler characteristic require graduate differential topology; an empty sum does not remove those prerequisites.
