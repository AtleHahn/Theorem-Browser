Statement

For each Hilbert polynomial P arising from an ample holomorphic line bundle on a compact complex manifold, there is an integer m(P)\ge1 with the following property. If X is a compact complex manifold and L an ample holomorphic line bundle on X with Hilbert polynomial P, then L^{\otimes n} is very ample for every integer n\ge m(P). Here a holomorphic line bundle has one-dimensional complex vector-space fibers and holomorphic local trivializations. Very ample means its global holomorphic sections give an embedding of X into complex projective space. Ample means some positive tensor power is very ample. The Hilbert polynomial is P_L(n)=\chi(X,L^{\otimes n}), the polynomial given by the Euler characteristic \sum_{q\ge0}(-1)^q\dim_{\mathbb C}H^q(X,L^{\otimes n}) of sheaf cohomology. The bound depends only on this polynomial, not on the particular X or L.
