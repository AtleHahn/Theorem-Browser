Statement

Let (X,d) be a complete metric space and let w_1,\ldots,w_N:X\to X, N\ge1, satisfy d(w_i(x),w_i(y))\le s\,d(x,y) for all x,y\in X and all i, where 0\le s<1. Let A be their attractor, the unique nonempty compact set satisfying A=\bigcup_{i=1}^N w_i(A). For every nonempty compact set L\subseteq X, h(L,A)\le\frac{h(L,\bigcup_{i=1}^Nw_i(L))}{1-s}. Here h(E,F)=\max\{\sup_{x\in E}\inf_{y\in F}d(x,y),\sup_{y\in F}\inf_{x\in E}d(x,y)\} is Hausdorff distance. In particular, residual distance at most \varepsilon>0 implies h(L,A)\le\varepsilon/(1-s).
