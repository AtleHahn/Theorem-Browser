Statement

Let \mathbb T=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z and let f:\mathbb T\to\mathbb T be an orientation-preserving homeomorphism with irrational rotation number. Choose an increasing lift F:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R satisfying F(t+1)=F(t)+1, and use the real rotation number

\alpha=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{F^n(t)-t}{n},

which is independent of t. Let p\in\mathbb Z and q\ge1 be coprime with |\alpha-p/q|<1/q^2. Let \mu be an f-invariant Borel probability measure on \mathbb T, and let \phi:\mathbb T\to\mathbb R have bounded variation. Then for every x\in\mathbb T,

\left|\sum_{i=0}^{q-1}\phi(f^i(x))-q\int_{\mathbb T}\phi\,d\mu\right|\le\operatorname{Var}(\phi).

Here f^i is the i-fold iterate, f^0 is the identity, and invariance means \mu(f^{-1}E)=\mu(E) for every Borel set E. The circular total variation is the supremum of \sum_{j=1}^m|\phi(x_{j+1})-\phi(x_j)| over finite cyclically ordered lists of distinct circle points, with x_{m+1}=x_1; bounded variation means this supremum is finite. The actual point values of \phi, not merely its almost-everywhere class, enter the orbit sum.
