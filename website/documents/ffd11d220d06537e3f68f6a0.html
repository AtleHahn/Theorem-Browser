For a finitely generated group H, let \operatorname{rank}(H) be the least size of a generating set. If A and B are finitely presented groups, then \operatorname{rank}(A*B)=\operatorname{rank}(A)+\operatorname{rank}(B).

Proof 1: Grushko formula for finitely presented factors via a free lifting

Suppose that {\displaystyle G=A*B} has a minimum generating set {\displaystyle \{g_{1},g_{2},\ldots ,g_{n}\}}. Let {\displaystyle F} be the free group with {\displaystyle n}-generators, viz. {\displaystyle \{f_{1},f_{2},\ldots ,f_{n}\}}. Consider the homomorphism {\displaystyle h:F\rightarrow G} given by {\displaystyle h(f_{i})=g_{i}}, where {\displaystyle i=1,\ldots ,n}.

By Stallings’ free-lifting lemma for finitely presented factors, there exist free groups {\displaystyle F_{1}} and {\displaystyle F_{2}} with {\displaystyle F=F_{1}\ast F_{2}} such that {\displaystyle h(F_{1})=A} and {\displaystyle h(F_{2})=B}. Therefore, {\displaystyle {\text{Rank }}(A)\leq {\text{Rank }}(F_{1})} and {\displaystyle {\text{Rank }}(B)\leq {\text{Rank }}(F_{2})}. Therefore, {\displaystyle {\text{Rank }}(A)+{\text{Rank }}(B)\leq {\text{Rank }}(F_{1})+{\text{Rank }}(F_{2})={\text{Rank }}(F)={\text{Rank }}(A\ast B).}

For the opposite inequality, if X is a finite generating set of A and Y is a finite generating set of B, then X\cup Y is a generating set for A*B and |X\cup Y|\le |X|+|Y|.
