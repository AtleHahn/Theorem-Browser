Statement

Let A=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb Z}A_n be a commutative unital Noetherian graded ring. For every n\in\mathbb Z, A_n is a finitely generated A_0-module.

Proof 1: Transfer ascending chains to homogeneous ideals — outline

Let N_i be an increasing chain of A_0-submodules of A_n. Then the chain of the ideals N_iA stabilizes in finitely many steps; so does the chain

N_i=N_iA\cap A_n.

The equality follows by taking the homogeneous component of degree n. By the ascending-chain criterion for modules, A_n is a Noetherian A_0-module, and in particular is finitely generated.
