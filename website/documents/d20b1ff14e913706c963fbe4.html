Statement

Let V,W\subseteq\mathbb P^n be irreducible projective varieties over an algebraically closed field. If \dim V+\dim W>n, then V\cap W is connected.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Irreducible projective varieties over arbitrary algebraically closed fields, their dimension and their topology require substantial algebraic-geometry foundations. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Take the product and pull back the diagonal

Use the general Fulton–Hansen theorem: for an irreducible projective variety Z and a morphism f:Z\to\mathbb P^n\times\mathbb P^n with \dim f(Z)>n, the inverse image of the diagonal is connected. Take Z=V\times W and f the natural inclusion. Its image has dimension \dim V+\dim W, and the inverse image of the diagonal identifies with V\cap W. The general theorem therefore gives the stated connectedness.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply diagonal connectedness to the product inclusion and identify its inverse diagonal with the intersection. Projective varieties, morphisms and algebraic dimension require algebraic geometry.
