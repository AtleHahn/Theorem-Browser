Statement

Let U\subset\mathbb R^3 be a bounded domain with piecewise smooth boundary and outward unit normal n. Functions are defined on a neighborhood of \overline U. Write \Delta=\nabla\cdot\nabla and \partial_n f=\nabla f\cdot n. Let \psi\in C^2 be harmonic, \Delta\psi=0. Fix \eta\in U. Let G(y,\eta) be a Laplace Green function with \Delta_yG=\delta_\eta, smooth away from its pole and with the boundary derivatives used below. If G=0 on \partial U, then \psi(\eta)=\int_{\partial U}\psi\partial_nG\,dS.

Proof 1: Eliminate the harmonic and zero-Dirichlet terms

Green’s third identity states \psi(\eta)-\int_U G\Delta\psi\,dV=\int_{\partial U}(\psi\partial_nG-G\partial_n\psi)\,dS. Harmonicity eliminates the volume term, and G=0 eliminates the term G\partial_n\psi on the boundary.

Level justification

Apply the singular Green third identity and remove its harmonic and zero-boundary terms. The distributional point source and Green-function pole require graduate PDE and distribution theory.
