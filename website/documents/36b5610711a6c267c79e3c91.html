Statement

Contexts \Gamma,\Delta,\Pi,\Lambda,\Sigma,\Theta are finite multisets of formulas (finite unordered lists, repetitions allowed). A sequent \Gamma\vdash\Delta is a pair of such multisets. A derivation is a finite rooted tree of sequents, whose child labels are premises of the rule at their parent. Concatenation of contexts denotes multiset union. The Cut rule takes \Gamma\vdash\Delta,A and A,\Pi\vdash\Lambda to \Gamma,\Pi\vdash\Delta,\Lambda. The multiplicative left-disjunction rule takes B,\Upsilon\vdash\Omega and C,\Xi\vdash\Psi to B\lor C,\Upsilon,\Xi\vdash\Omega,\Psi. Given derivations of \Gamma\vdash\Delta,A, A,B,\Pi\vdash\Lambda and C,\Sigma\vdash\Theta, a cut after combining the latter two by left disjunction can instead be made against the B premise before combining with the C premise. The endsequent remains \Gamma,B\lor C,\Pi,\Sigma\vdash\Delta,\Lambda,\Theta.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The finite-tree and multiset framework, Cut and the multiplicative disjunction rule are explicitly supplied. Tracking the branch containing A and comparing the combined contexts requires elementary symbolic bookkeeping, with no logic-course prerequisite.
