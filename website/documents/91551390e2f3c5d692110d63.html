Statement

Let R be a unital ring. Then R is semiprime and right Goldie if and only if its classical right ring of quotients exists and is semisimple Artinian.

Semiprime means that R has no nonzero nilpotent two-sided ideal. Right Goldie means that the sizes of direct sums of nonzero right ideals embedded in the right module R_R are bounded by a finite integer (finite uniform dimension), and that every ascending chain of right annihilators stabilizes. The right annihilator of S\subseteq R is \{r\in R:sr=0\text{ for every }s\in S\}.

A regular element is neither a left nor a right zero divisor. The classical right ring of quotients is a ring Q containing R, in which every regular element of R is invertible and every element of Q has the form as^{-1} with a\in R and s\in R regular. In this situation R is called a right order in Q. Semisimple means that the right module Q_Q is a direct sum of simple modules (nonzero modules with no proper nonzero submodule), and Artinian means that descending chains of right ideals stabilize.
