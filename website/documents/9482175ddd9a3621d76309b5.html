Statement

For a ring torus with major radius R and minor radius r, 0<r<R, its surface area is 4\pi^2Rr.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement describes a ring torus using two circle radii and gives an elementary algebraic area or volume formula; no calculus concepts are needed to understand it.

Proof 1: Apply the centroid theorem to the generating circle

The generating circle has arc length 2\pi r and its centroid travels distance 2\pi R. Pappus’s surface-area theorem gives the product (2\pi r)(2\pi R)=4\pi^2Rr.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification
