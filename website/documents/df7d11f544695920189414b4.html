Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer and let (v_k)_{k\ge1} be any sequence in \mathbb R^n. Form the set

S=\left\{s\in\mathbb R^n:\text{there is a bijection }\pi:\mathbb N_{>0}\to\mathbb N_{>0}\text{ with }\lim_{N\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^Nv_{\pi(k)}=s\right\}.

Then either S is empty or there are v\in\mathbb R^n and a linear subspace M\subseteq\mathbb R^n such that S=v+M=\{v+m:m\in M\}. Convergence uses the usual Euclidean norm.
