Statement

The bounds |x|<3 and 0<y<3 do not make the rational value x/y unique among integer solutions of 2y\equiv x\pmod5.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses elementary integer divisibility, size bounds and nonzero-denominator fractions.

Proof 1: Two explicit solutions

At a=2, use 2a+1\equiv-a+2\equiv0\pmod5. These yield (x,y)=(-1,2) and (2,1). Both obey the stated bounds and congruence, while their ratios are -1/2 and 2.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The pairs (-1,2) and (2,1) both satisfy the two bounds and the congruence, yet give different fractions. This is elementary modular arithmetic and direct verification.
