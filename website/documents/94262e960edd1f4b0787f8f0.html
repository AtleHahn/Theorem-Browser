Statement

Consider the real numbers with their usual operations, order, constants 0,1, and exponential function. Let \phi(x_1,\ldots,x_m) be any first-order formula in this language: it is built from equalities and inequalities between expressions using these operations, Boolean connectives, and quantifiers over real numbers.

There exist integers n\geq m, r\geq1, and polynomials P_1,\ldots,P_r with integer coefficients in 2n variables such that, for every (x_1,\ldots,x_m)\in\mathbb R^m,

\phi(x_1,\ldots,x_m)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists x_{m+1},\ldots,x_n\in\mathbb R\; \bigwedge_{j=1}^{r}P_j(x_1,\ldots,x_n,e^{x_1},\ldots,e^{x_n})=0.

Thus every set specified by such a formula is the coordinate projection of the common zero set of finitely many such exponential polynomials. Additional real parameters can be treated as additional free variables before specializing their values.

Level justification

The complete claim quantifies over arbitrary first-order formulas and their uniform equivalence. Familiar polynomial and exponential expressions alone do not supply the formal-language and definability background; conservatively beyond undergraduate because the cumulative logical preparation is not confidently within the reading allowance.
