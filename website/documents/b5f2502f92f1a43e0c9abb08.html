Statement

Any finite polygonal shape in the Euclidean plane can be cut from a single idealized sheet of paper by folding the sheet flat and making one complete straight cut through the folded paper. The shape may be nonconvex, have holes, or consist of several disjoint polygonal pieces. Here the paper is idealized as having zero thickness, and the cut is along one straight line through all layers; unfolding produces the prescribed straight-sided boundary.

High school only
