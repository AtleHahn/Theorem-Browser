CH and choice are relatively consistent with ZF

The relative consistency result shows that choice and the continuum hypothesis introduce no contradiction if ZF itself is consistent. It supports the study of alternative set-theoretic foundations while carefully distinguishing relative consistency from an unconditional proof that the axioms are true or consistent.

Proof 1: Pass from the constructible model to consistency

Gödel's proof shows that both CH and AC hold in the constructible universe {\displaystyle L}, an inner model of ZF set theory, assuming only the axioms of ZF. The existence of an inner model of ZF in which additional axioms hold shows that the additional axioms are (relatively) consistent with ZF, provided ZF itself is consistent.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof relies on the constructible universe as an inner-model interpretation and the passage from that interpretation to consistency of a formal theory. These are substantial axiomatic set-theory and logic prerequisites.
