Statement

In the orthogonal reflection realization of the Weyl group on a Cartan space of rank r, using orthonormal coordinates h_1,\ldots,h_r, the polynomial f_2=h_1^2+\cdots+h_r^2 is invariant.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Weyl groups and Cartan spaces require their specialized algebraic meaning even when the orthogonal action is stipulated. That full preparation cannot confidently be reduced to finite orthogonal matrices. The cumulative prerequisite preparation cannot confidently fit approximately 30 minutes beyond the stipulated undergraduate baseline. Under the conservative threshold rule, the classification is therefore assessed as Beyond undergraduate level. Uncertainty about the exact preparation time is retained in this justification but no longer leaves the educational assessment provisional.

Proof 1: The quadratic polynomial is Weyl-invariant

Since the Weyl group acts by reflections on the Cartan space, they are isometries, so the degree 2 invariant polynomial is

f_2(\mathbf h)=h_1^2+\cdots+h_r^2,

where r is the dimension of the Cartan space, also known as the rank.
