Statement

Let A be a compact self-adjoint operator on a complex Hilbert space H. List its positive eigenvalues, with multiplicity, in decreasing order \lambda_1\ge\lambda_2\ge\cdots>0. For every index k for which \lambda_k exists,

\lambda_k=\max_{\dim S=k}\ \min_{x\in S,\ \|x\|=1}\langle Ax,x\rangle =\min_{\dim S=k-1}\ \max_{x\in S^\perp,\ \|x\|=1}\langle Ax,x\rangle.

Here S ranges over linear subspaces of H. If the negative eigenvalues are listed in increasing order \mu_1\le\mu_2\le\cdots<0, then for every existing \mu_k,

\mu_k=\min_{\dim S=k}\ \max_{x\in S,\ \|x\|=1}\langle Ax,x\rangle =\max_{\dim S=k-1}\ \min_{x\in S^\perp,\ \|x\|=1}\langle Ax,x\rangle.

Only indices of existing positive or negative eigenvalues are asserted; zero eigenvalues are not inserted into either list.
