Statement

An Euler brick is a rectangular cuboid with positive integer edges and positive integer face diagonals. If (a,b,c) are the edges of an Euler brick, then (bc,ac,ab) are the edges of an Euler brick.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The new three integer edge lengths are explicit products of the original ones.

Proof 1: Apply the prescribed pair-product transformation — outline

From the given Euler brick with edges (a,b,c), form the new edge triple (bc,ac,ab).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The prescribed transformation is understood using the Pythagorean equations: each new face diagonal is an original integer diagonal multiplied by the remaining edge. Elementary integer products and squares suffice.
