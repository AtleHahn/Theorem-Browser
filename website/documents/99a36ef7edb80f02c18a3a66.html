Statement

Let n\geq1 and A,B\in M_n(\mathbb C). Write A^* for the conjugate transpose and \operatorname{tr}A for the sum of diagonal entries. Then there exists a unitary matrix U (so U^*U=UU^*=I_n) with B=U^*AU if and only if

\operatorname{tr}W(A,A^*)=\operatorname{tr}W(B,B^*)

for every word W in two noncommuting variables. A word is a finite ordered product of copies of the two variables; evaluation substitutes the two matrices in that order, and the empty word evaluates to I_n.

Level justification

Standard undergraduate complex linear algebra suffices; words are explicitly finite products.
