Proof 1: Parallel slicing through vertices (outline)

Choose a line direction not parallel to any polygon side and draw a parallel line through each polygon vertex. These cuts divide the region into triangles and trapezoids. A diagonal divides each trapezoid into triangles, producing a finite triangulation.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Slice through the finitely many vertices into triangles and trapezoids and cut the trapezoids by diagonals. School plane geometry suffices.
