Proof 1: Use the Spin–unitary chain and Bott periodicity

The covering and classical-group identifications give \pi_6(SO(6))\cong\pi_6(\operatorname{Spin}(6))\cong\pi_6(SU(4))\cong\pi_6(U(4))=0, with the last equality supplied by Bott periodicity in the stable range.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Transfer higher homotopy through the spin cover and classical-group identifications, then use stable Bott periodicity. This requires advanced Lie-group topology and homotopy theory.
