Statement

There is an absolute constant C such that for every real a<b and u\in C([a,b]) twice differentiable on (a,b) with bounded u^{\prime\prime}, there is an affine polynomial v with |u(x)-v(x)|\le C(b-a)^2\sup_{(a,b)}|u^{\prime\prime}| for every x\in(a,b).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves continuous and twice differentiable functions; affine polynomials; supremum norm. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Set p infinity, m two and k zero outline

Use the interval Bramble–Hilbert approximation theorem: for u\in W_\infty^2(a,b) an affine polynomial v satisfies \|u-v\|_\infty\le C(b-a)^2\|u^{\prime\prime}\|_\infty.

In the case when {\displaystyle \textstyle p=\infty }, {\displaystyle \textstyle m=2}, {\displaystyle \textstyle k=0}, and {\displaystyle \textstyle u} is twice differentiable, this means that there exists a polynomial {\displaystyle \textstyle v} of degree at most one such that for all {\displaystyle \textstyle x\in \left(a,b\right)},

{\displaystyle \left\vert u\left(x\right)-v\left(x\right)\right\vert \leq C\left(b-a\right)^{2}\sup _{\left(a,b\right)}\left\vert u^{\prime \prime }\right\vert .}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Place the function in W infinity two and specialize the Sobolev approximation theorem. Weak derivatives and the Sobolev embedding of the classical function are substantial prerequisites despite the elementary final estimate.
