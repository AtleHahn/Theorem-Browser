Statement

Let E,F be Fréchet spaces (complete metrizable locally convex real or complex topological vector spaces), and let T:E\to F be a continuous linear surjection. Then T is open. In particular this holds for Banach spaces.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Complete metrizable locally convex spaces and open linear maps require functional analysis.

Proof 1: Reduction to the bounded inverse theorem

A surjective continuous linear operator T:E\to F factors as

T:E\overset{p}{\longrightarrow}E/\ker T \overset{T_0}{\longrightarrow}F.

The kernel is closed, and the quotient by a closed subspace of a Fréchet space is again Fréchet. Here, T_0 is continuous and bijective and thus is a homeomorphism by the bounded inverse theorem for Fréchet spaces; in particular, it is an open mapping. As a quotient map for topological groups is open, T is open then.
