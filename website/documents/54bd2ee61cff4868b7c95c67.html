Statement

For a smooth real function f on a smooth manifold and a critical point p, the singularity of the Hessian and the dimension of its largest negative-definite subspace are independent of local coordinates.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Apply Sylvester’s law to the coordinate Hessians

The degeneracy and index of a critical point are independent of the choice of the local coordinate system used, as shown by Sylvester's law of inertia. Here one uses the Hessian coordinate-change rule at a critical point: the two Hessian matrices are related by congruence with the invertible derivative of the coordinate change. Sylvester's law preserves the numbers of positive, negative and zero directions under such congruence.

Level justification

Use congruence of critical-point Hessians and Sylvester inertia. The matrix theorem is undergraduate, but Hessians and coordinate changes on arbitrary smooth manifolds require a substantial geometric extension; retain conservatively.
