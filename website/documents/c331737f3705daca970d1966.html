Statement

On deterministic multitape Turing machines, let \mathrm{DTIME}(h(n)) denote languages decidable in time O(h(n)), let \mathrm P=\bigcup_{k\geq1}\mathrm{DTIME}(n^k) and \mathrm{EXPTIME}=\bigcup_{k\geq1}\mathrm{DTIME}(2^{n^k}). Then \mathrm P\subsetneq\mathrm{EXPTIME}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The time classes are defined but their multitape-machine and constructibility setting adds computational prerequisites whose cumulative preparation is uncertain. The cumulative additional preparation cannot confidently fit approximately 30 minutes beyond the stipulated undergraduate baseline. Under the conservative threshold rule, this is assessed as Beyond undergraduate level; uncertainty about the exact preparation time does not leave the classification provisional.

Proof 1: Exponential specialization of the hierarchy theorem

Use the external deterministic time hierarchy theorem in this form: for time-constructible f,g with f(n)\log f(n)=o(g(n)),

\mathrm{DTIME}(f(n))\subsetneq\mathrm{DTIME}(g(n)).

The source's exponential specialization gives

\mathrm P\subseteq\mathrm{DTIME}(2^n)\subsetneq\mathrm{DTIME}(2^{2n})\subseteq\mathrm{EXPTIME}.

The strict middle inclusion is the hierarchy theorem applied to the time-constructible exponential bounds; the outer inclusions follow from the definitions and domination of every fixed polynomial by 2^n.
