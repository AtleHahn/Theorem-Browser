Statement

If n>1 is an integer and positive integers a_1,\ldots,a_n,b satisfy a_1^3+\cdots+a_n^3=b^3, then n\ge3.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement counts positive summands in a cubic integer equation.

Proof 1: Apply Fermat’s cubic case

Fermat’s theorem for third powers excludes a_1^3+a_2^3=b^3 in positive integers. Thus the case n=2 is impossible and n\ge3.

Level

Level justification

Invoke the explicitly stated impossibility of a positive-integer sum of two cubes being a cube. Since n is already greater than one, exclusion of two proves the bound. The proof of Fermat cubic case is not invoked.
