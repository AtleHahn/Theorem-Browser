Statement

Work in \mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^2, with |0\rangle=(1,0)^T, |1\rangle=(0,1)^T, and

|\psi\rangle=(|0\rangle\otimes|1\rangle-|1\rangle\otimes|0\rangle)/\sqrt2.

Let \sigma_z=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix} and \sigma_x=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}. Put A_0=\sigma_z, A_1=\sigma_x, B_0=-(\sigma_x+\sigma_z)/\sqrt2, and B_1=(\sigma_x-\sigma_z)/\sqrt2. For an operator M write \langle M\rangle=\langle\psi|M|\psi\rangle (the quantum expectation). Then \langle A_0\otimes B_0\rangle+\langle A_0\otimes B_1\rangle+\langle A_1\otimes B_0\rangle-\langle A_1\otimes B_1\rangle=2\sqrt2>2. These four pair correlations cannot be the pair averages of one joint distribution of four signs.

Proof 1: Explicit singlet expectation values and the local sign bound

Local CHSH lemma

Let (A_0,A_1,B_0,B_1) take values in \{-1,1\}^4 with a joint probability distribution. For any function of these four signs, \langle f\rangle means its finite weighted average over the sixteen possible sign tuples. Then |\langle A_0B_0\rangle+\langle A_0B_1\rangle+\langle A_1B_0\rangle-\langle A_1B_1\rangle|\leq2.

For one sign tuple write its entries as a_0,a_1,b_0,b_1. Consider

{\displaystyle a_{0}b_{0}+a_{0}b_{1}+a_{1}b_{0}-a_{1}b_{1}=(a_{0}+a_{1})b_{0}+(a_{0}-a_{1})b_{1}\,.}

Because both {\displaystyle a_{0}} and {\displaystyle a_{1}} take the values {\displaystyle \pm 1}, then either {\displaystyle a_{0}=a_{1}} or {\displaystyle a_{0}=-a_{1}}. In the former case, the quantity {\displaystyle (a_{0}-a_{1})b_{1}} must equal 0, while in the latter case, {\displaystyle (a_{0}+a_{1})b_{0}=0}. So, one of the terms on the right-hand side of the above expression will vanish, and the other will equal {\displaystyle \pm 2}. Consequently the absolute value of the average of this combination is at most 2. The average of the sum is the sum of the averages. Using angle brackets to denote averages

{\displaystyle |\langle A_{0}B_{0}\rangle +\langle A_{0}B_{1}\rangle +\langle A_{1}B_{0}\rangle -\langle A_{1}B_{1}\rangle |\leq 2\,.}

Singlet expectation calculation

Using the Born-rule expectation specified in the statement gives

{\displaystyle \langle A_{0}\otimes B_{0}\rangle ={\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}}},\langle A_{0}\otimes B_{1}\rangle ={\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}}},\langle A_{1}\otimes B_{0}\rangle ={\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}}},\langle A_{1}\otimes B_{1}\rangle =-{\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}}}\,.}

Summing with the displayed signs yields

{\displaystyle \langle A_{0}\otimes B_{0}\rangle +\langle A_{0}\otimes B_{1}\rangle +\langle A_{1}\otimes B_{0}\rangle -\langle A_{1}\otimes B_{1}\rangle =2{\sqrt {2}}}

This exceeds the bound 2 in the local CHSH lemma.
