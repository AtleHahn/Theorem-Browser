Statement

Let f_1,\ldots,f_m\in\mathbb R[x_1,\ldots,x_n], and suppose W=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:f_j(x)\ge0\text{ for all }j\} is compact. If p\in\mathbb R[x_1,\ldots,x_n] is strictly positive at every point of W, then

p=\sum_{\alpha\in\{0,1\}^m}\sigma_\alpha f_1^{\alpha_1}\cdots f_m^{\alpha_m},

where each \sigma_\alpha is a finite sum of squares of real polynomials. The equality is an identity of polynomials.

Level justification

Multivariate polynomial algebra and compactness from undergraduate analysis suffice; the explicit certificate needs no prior real algebraic geometry.

Level justification

The compactness certificate depends on the classical Positivstellensatz. Its proof uses ordered polynomial rings, prime cones and transfer over real closed fields; this cumulative real-algebraic background exceeds introductory undergraduate algebra, even though the subsequent coefficient construction is elementary.
