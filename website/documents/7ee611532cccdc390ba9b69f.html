Statement

Let M and N be pointed finite CW complexes with basepoints 0-cells. Write χ as the alternating cell count and χhat(X)=χ(X)−1. Set M∨N=(M×{n0})∪({m0}×N) and M∧N=(M×N)/(M∨N). Then χhat(M∧N)=χhat(M)χhat(N).

Proof 1: Combine the product and quotient formulas — outline

Using the product formula \chi(M\times N)=\chi(M)\chi(N) and the formula \chi(X/A)=\chi(X)-\chi(A)+1 for a nonempty subcomplex A of a finite CW complex X, one obtains

{\displaystyle {\hat {\chi }}(M\wedge N)={\hat {\chi }}(M)\cdot {\hat {\chi }}(N),}

where {\displaystyle {\hat {\chi }}} is the reduced Euler characteristic, and {\displaystyle M\wedge N} is the smash product.

Level justification

Finite CW complexes, their subcomplex quotients and the smash construction introduce several linked algebraic-topological concepts. The scalar formulas alone do not establish undergraduate accessibility of the actual proof.
