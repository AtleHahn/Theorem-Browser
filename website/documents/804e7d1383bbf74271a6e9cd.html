Statement

A generalized arithmetic progression (GAP) of dimension d is a set \{x_0+\sum_{j=1}^d n_jx_j:0\le n_j<L_j\} with positive integer lengths L_j; it is proper when these parameter tuples give distinct values. For sets, A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,b\in B\}. For every real K\ge1, there are finite constants d(K),f(K) such that every nonempty finite A\subseteq\mathbb Z with |A+A|\le K|A| lies in a GAP of dimension at most d(K) and cardinality at most f(K)|A|.

Proof 1: Model in a cyclic group, find a progression, and cover — outline

Use the following source-stated prerequisites. Plünnecke–Ruzsa gives |mA-nA|\le K^{m+n}|A| for nonnegative integers m,n with m+n>0. Ruzsa modeling gives, whenever N\ge|sA-sA|, a subset A'\subseteq A of size at least |A|/s Freiman s-isomorphic to a subset of \mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z; this means a bijection preserving, in both directions, equalities between sums of s terms. Bogolyubov’s lemma says a set of density \alpha in the prime cyclic group has 2B-2B containing a Bohr set of dimension at most \alpha^{-2} and width 1/4. A Bohr set of dimension d and width \varepsilon contains a proper GAP of dimension at most d and size at least (\varepsilon/d)^dN. Here a Bohr set consists of residues x satisfying \|rx/N\|\le\varepsilon for every frequency r in a set of d residues, where \|t\| is distance to the nearest integer.

By the Plünnecke–Ruzsa inequality, {\displaystyle |8A-8A|\leq K^{16}|A|}. By Bertrand's postulate, there exists a prime {\displaystyle N} such that {\displaystyle |8A-8A|\leq N\leq 2K^{16}|A|}. By the Ruzsa modeling lemma, there exists a subset {\displaystyle A'} of {\displaystyle A} of cardinality at least {\displaystyle |A|/8} such that {\displaystyle A'} is Freiman 8-isomorphic to a subset {\displaystyle B\subseteq \mathbb {Z} /N\mathbb {Z} }.

By the generalization of Bogolyubov's lemma and the Bohr-set progression proposition, {\displaystyle 2B-2B} contains a proper generalized arithmetic progression of dimension {\displaystyle d} at most {\displaystyle (1/(8\cdot 2K^{16}))^{-2}=256K^{32}} and size at least {\displaystyle (1/(4d))^{d}N}. Because {\displaystyle A'} and {\displaystyle B} are Freiman 8-isomorphic, {\displaystyle 2A'-2A'} and {\displaystyle 2B-2B} are Freiman 2-isomorphic. Then the image under the 2-isomorphism of the proper generalized arithmetic progression in {\displaystyle 2B-2B} is a proper generalized arithmetic progression in {\displaystyle 2A'-2A'\subseteq 2A-2A} called {\displaystyle P}.

But {\displaystyle P+A\subseteq 3A-2A}, since {\displaystyle P\subseteq 2A-2A}. Thus

{\displaystyle |P+A|\leq |3A-2A|\leq |8A-8A|\leq N\leq (4d)^{d}|P|}

so by the Ruzsa covering lemma {\displaystyle A\subseteq X+P-P} for some {\displaystyle X\subseteq A} of cardinality at most {\displaystyle (4d)^{d}}. Then {\displaystyle X+P-P} is contained in a generalized arithmetic progression of dimension {\displaystyle |X|+d} and size at most {\displaystyle 2^{|X|}2^{d}|P|\leq 2^{|X|+d}|2A-2A|\leq 2^{|X|+d}K^{4}|A|}, completing the proof.

Here one may pad a progression by a length-one coordinate so that d\ge1. The comparison |3A-2A|\le|8A-8A| uses a translation: for fixed a_0\in A, (3A-2A)-a_0\subseteq8A-8A by padding with five positive and six negative copies of a_0. The enclosing progression uses the difference ranges of P and the elements of X as additional generators with binary coefficients. The source leaves the relation-preservation and progression-transfer checks implicit.
