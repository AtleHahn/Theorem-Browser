Statement

Let F(X) be the free group on a set X, and let H,K\leq F(X) be nontrivial finitely generated subgroups. Then H\cap K is finitely generated and

\operatorname{rank}(H\cap K)-1\leq\bigl(\operatorname{rank}(H)-1\bigr)\bigl(\operatorname{rank}(K)-1\bigr).

Here the rank of a group is the smallest size of a generating set; the trivial group has rank zero. The free group F(X) consists of finite reduced words in letters from X and their formal inverses, with multiplication by concatenation followed by cancellation of adjacent inverse pairs.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory group theory supplies subgroups and generators; the reduced-word construction of a free group is a small focused extension. Rank is defined directly, and the full numerical intersection bound needs no geometric group theory background.
