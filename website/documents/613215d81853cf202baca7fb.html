Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer and let H be a set of invertible real n\times n matrices that contains I, is closed under multiplication and inversion, and is closed in GL(n,\mathbb R) with its usual matrix topology. Suppose nonzero real matrices S_i\to0 satisfy \exp(S_i)\in H and S_i/\|S_i\|\to Y, where \|\cdot\| is the Hilbert–Schmidt norm. Then \exp(tY)\in H for every real t.

Proof 1: Approximate a real parameter by integer powers

Put Y_i=S_i/\|S_i\|.

It will be shown that etY ∈ H, ∀t ∈ R. Fix t and choose a sequence mi of integers such that mi ||Si|| → t as i → ∞. For example, mi such that mi ||Si|| ≤ t ≤ (mi + 1) ||Si|| will do, as Si → 0. Then

{\displaystyle (e^{S_{i}})^{m_{i}}=e^{m_{i}S_{i}}=e^{m_{i}\|S_{i}\|Y_{i}}\rightarrow e^{tY}.}

Since H is a group, the left hand side is in H for all i. Since H is closed, etY ∈ H, ∀t.
