Statement

Let S be a semigroup, a set with an associative binary operation. A subsemigroup is a subset closed under this operation; the empty subset is allowed. The collection of all subsemigroups of S, ordered by inclusion, is a complete lattice: every family has a greatest lower bound and a least upper bound in this collection.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A semigroup is explicitly an associative operation. Its closed subsets are ordered by inclusion, and complete lattice is explicitly existence of greatest lower and least upper bounds. Sets and elementary algebra suffice.

Proof 1: Apply the complete closure-system lattice theorem

Use the stated intersection-closure fact: an arbitrary intersection of subsemigroups of S is a subsemigroup; the empty-family intersection is S itself. Apply the general closure-system theorem: a family of subsets of a set that contains the whole set and is closed under arbitrary intersections is a complete lattice under inclusion. This gives the claimed complete lattice of subsemigroups.
