Statement

Let K be a compact subset of \mathbb C with connected complement \mathbb C\setminus K. If f:K\to\mathbb C is continuous and holomorphic on the interior of K, then for every \epsilon>0 there exists a complex polynomial p such that

|f(z)-p(z)|<\epsilon\qquad\text{for every }z\in K.

Holomorphic means complex differentiable at every point of the open interior. If the interior is empty, that condition is vacuous. The estimate is uniform over K.

Level justification

Complex differentiability is a small definitional extension of undergraduate calculus; compactness, connectedness and uniform approximation are baseline analysis/topology.
