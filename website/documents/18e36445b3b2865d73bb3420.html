Statement

Let X be a real or complex normed space linearly isometrically isomorphic to the continuous dual Y^* of a Banach space Y. Then X admits a Hausdorff locally convex vector topology \tau in which its closed norm unit ball is compact.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The conclusion concerns locally convex vector topologies and compactness on a possibly infinite-dimensional dual.

Proof 1: Weak-star topology and Banach–Alaoglu

Identify X with Y^* by the given isometry and take \tau to be the weak-star topology \sigma(Y^*,Y). This is a Hausdorff locally convex topology, and the Banach–Alaoglu theorem makes the closed unit ball compact in it.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Transporting the weak-star topology requires continuous duals and Hausdorff locally convex vector topologies, then Banach-Alaoglu. This combination is substantial functional analysis.
