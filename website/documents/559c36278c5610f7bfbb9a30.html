Statement

Let T:V\to W be linear over a field F, with V finite dimensional. Then V is isomorphic, as an F-vector space, to \ker T\oplus\operatorname{im}T.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Linear algebra and real analysis supply kernels, quotients, projections, sup norms and completeness. The index or operator bound is explicitly defined, and sequence-space examples use specified coordinate formulas. No dual or weak-topology theory is required.

Proof 1: Split the kernel quotient

Since T induces an isomorphism from V/\operatorname{Ker}(T) to \operatorname{Im}(T), the existence of a basis for V that extends any given basis of \operatorname{Ker}(T) implies, via the splitting lemma, that \operatorname{Im}(T)\oplus\operatorname{Ker}(T)\cong V.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Extend a kernel basis to a domain basis, split off its complementary span and identify the complement with the image through the quotient. All supplied theorems are standard finite-dimensional linear algebra.
