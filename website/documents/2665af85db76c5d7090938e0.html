Proof 1: Compare common divisors

Since n and a are coprime, it follows that a-n and n must be coprime. If not, their greatest common divisor d>1 would divide their sum a as well as n, contradicting the assumption.

Level

Level justification

A common divisor of n and a-n also divides their sum a. Thus it must be one when gcd(n,a)=1.
