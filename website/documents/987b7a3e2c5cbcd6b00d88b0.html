Statement

For 0\le k<n, every based continuous map S^k\to S^n is based null-homotopic; thus \pi_k(S^n)=0 (with the usual pointed-component interpretation for k=0).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement concerns based homotopy classes of maps between spheres, beyond elementary real/complex calculus and linear algebra.

Proof 1: Simplicial dimension and a missing point

Given a based map f:S^k\to S^n, it is based homotopic to a simplicial map g:S^k\to S^n. Now, for dimension reasons, g cannot map onto an n-simplex; i.e., g is not surjective and thus the image lies in a contractible subset, namely a sphere with one point removed, and g is based homotopic to a constant map. Here the based version of the simplicial approximation theorem is used.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use based simplicial approximation, dimension of simplicial images, and contraction of a punctured sphere. The combined based homotopy, triangulation and simplicial machinery exceeds a small extension of ordinary point-set topology.
