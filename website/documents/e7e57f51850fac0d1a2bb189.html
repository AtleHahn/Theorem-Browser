Statement

In a real or complex reflexive Banach space, every norm-closed bounded convex subset is weakly compact; in particular this holds in Hilbert spaces.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement combines convexity with compactness for the weak topology of a reflexive Banach space.

Proof 1: Hahn–Banach closedness inside a weakly compact ball

A reflexive Banach space has a weakly compact closed unit ball, so every bounded subset is weakly relatively compact. Hilbert spaces are reflexive.

As norm-closed, convex sets are weakly closed (Hahn–Banach theorem), norm-closures of convex bounded sets in Hilbert spaces or reflexive Banach spaces are weakly compact.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The proof relies on reflexivity, weak topology defined by the continuous dual, and separation in Banach spaces. Compactness alone is undergraduate, but these functional-analysis prerequisites are not a small extension.
