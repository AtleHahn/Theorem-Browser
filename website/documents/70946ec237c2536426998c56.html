Statement

Let f:X\to Y be a quasi-projective morphism of Noetherian schemes. For y\in Y let X_y=X\times_Y\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(y) be the fiber over its residue field. Let U\subseteq X consist of the points isolated in their fiber, meaning that the corresponding singleton is open in that fiber’s underlying space. Then U is open in X, and the restricted morphism factors as

U\xrightarrow{\ j\ }Z\xrightarrow{\ g\ }Y,

where j is an open immersion and g is finite.

Here a Noetherian scheme has a finite affine cover by Noetherian rings. Quasi-projective means admitting an immersion into projective space over Y. An open immersion identifies the source with an open subscheme. A finite morphism has affine inverse images of affine opens: if an affine open of the target is \operatorname{Spec}A, its inverse image is \operatorname{Spec}B with B a finite A-module.
