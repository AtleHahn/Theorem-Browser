Statement

Let E/F be an algebraic field extension and let \phi:F\to F^{\prime} be a field isomorphism. Fix an algebraic closure \overline{F^{\prime}} of F^{\prime}. There are a subfield E^{\prime} with F^{\prime}\subseteq E^{\prime}\subseteq\overline{F^{\prime}} and a field isomorphism \tau:E\to E^{\prime} satisfying \tau|_F=\phi. The extension E^{\prime}/F^{\prime} is algebraic. Algebraic means each element satisfies a nonzero polynomial over the base field; an algebraic closure is an algebraic extension in which every nonconstant polynomial has a root. No finite-degree assumption on E/F is required.
