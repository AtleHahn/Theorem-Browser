Statement

Let (R,\mathfrak m) be a commutative Noetherian local ring and let M be a nonzero finitely generated R-module of finite projective dimension. Then

\operatorname{pd}_R M+\operatorname{depth}_R M=\operatorname{depth}R.

Here \operatorname{pd}_R M is the least length of a projective resolution of M. The depth of M is the maximum length of an M-regular sequence in \mathfrak m: a sequence a_1,\ldots,a_s such that multiplication by a_i is injective on M/(a_1,\ldots,a_{i-1})M for each i, and the final quotient is nonzero. The depth of R means its depth as an R-module.
