Statement

Let A,B\subseteq X be subspaces of a topological space with X=\operatorname{int}_X A\cup\operatorname{int}_X B, and set C=A\cap B\ne\varnothing. Suppose (A,C) is (m-1)-connected for an integer m\ge2 and (B,C) is (n-1)-connected for an integer n\ge1. A pair (Y,Z) is r-connected here if every path component of Y meets Z and \pi_j(Y,Z,c) is trivial for every c\in Z and 1\le j\le r. The relative homotopy set \pi_j(Y,Z,c) consists of homotopy classes of maps (D^j,S^{j-1},*)\to(Y,Z,c), where D^j is the closed unit ball, S^{j-1}=\partial D^j, and * is a chosen boundary point, with homotopies respecting these conditions; it is a group for j\ge2. For every basepoint c\in C, inclusion induces \pi_q(A,C,c)\longrightarrow\pi_q(X,B,c), which is bijective for 1\le q<m+n-2 and surjective for q=m+n-2.
