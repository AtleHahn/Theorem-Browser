Statement

Let M be a connected Riemannian manifold such that at every point p there is a global isometry fixing p and reversing geodesics through p. Then M is geodesically complete.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement asks whether geodesics on a Riemannian manifold extend for all real times.

Proof 1: Extend geodesics by reflection (outline)

Any geodesic segment can be extended by reflecting it with the symmetry about an endpoint. The global symmetries allow this extension to be repeated indefinitely. Thus every geodesic extends for all real times.

Level

Level justification

Extend geodesics by global point symmetries. Riemannian manifolds, geodesic continuation and global isometries require substantial differential geometry even though the reflection idea is simple.
