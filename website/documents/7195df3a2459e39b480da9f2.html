Statement

Let A be an associative ring with identity. Form the stable general linear group

\operatorname{GL}(A)=\varinjlim_n\operatorname{GL}_n(A)

using the embeddings g\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(g,1), where \operatorname{GL}_n(A) consists of the invertible n\times n matrices. Let E(A) be the subgroup generated by all elementary matrices I_n+aE_{ij}, with a\in A and i\ne j, viewed in this stable group. Here E_{ij} is the matrix with a single 1 in position (i,j).

Then

E(A)=[\operatorname{GL}(A),\operatorname{GL}(A)],

where the right side is the subgroup generated by all commutators ghg^{-1}h^{-1}. The assertion is about the stable group, not a fixed matrix size.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

From undergraduate ring and group theory, the stable group can be understood concretely by identifying matrices after adjoining identity blocks. Together with the explicitly defined elementary matrices and commutators, this is a small extension within the reading allowance.
