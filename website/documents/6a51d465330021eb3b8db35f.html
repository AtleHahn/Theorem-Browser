Proof 1: Condensing to a series of ones

Cauchy condensation says that for a non-increasing nonnegative sequence a_n, the series \sum_{n\ge1}a_n converges if and only if \sum_{j\ge0}2^j a_{2^j} converges. For a_n=1/n, the condensed terms are 2^j/2^j=1. Thus the condensed series is \sum_{j\ge0}1, which diverges, and so does the harmonic series.
