Statement

Let 1\le M<N be integers, and let A=(a_{ij}) be an M\times N integer matrix, not identically zero, with |a_{ij}|\le B. There is a nonzero integer vector X=(X_1,\ldots,X_N) satisfying AX=0 and

\max_{1\le j\le N}|X_j|\le (NB)^{M/(N-M)}.

The equation AX=0 means \sum_{j=1}^Na_{ij}X_j=0 for each 1\le i\le M.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Matrix notation is expanded into elementary linear equations; only integer arithmetic and powers needed.
