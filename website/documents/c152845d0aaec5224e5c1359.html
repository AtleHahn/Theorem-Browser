Proof 1: Constancy of inscribed angles on a fixed arc

Both inscribed angles intercept the same opposite arc from A to B. By the inscribed angle theorem each is half its central angle, so they are equal.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Both angles intercept the same arc, so the supplied half-central-angle theorem makes them equal. Elementary circle geometry suffices.
