Statement

Let L>0 and let \gamma:\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z\to\mathbb R^3 be a twice continuously differentiable closed curve parametrized by arc length, so \|\gamma'(s)\|=1 for every s. Its total absolute curvature satisfies

\int_0^L\|\gamma''(s)\|\,ds\geq2\pi.

Here \mathbb R/L\mathbb Z identifies parameters differing by an integer multiple of L, so the parametrization and its derivatives match at the endpoints. Self-intersections are allowed. The norm is the Euclidean norm, and the curvature of a unit-speed curve is \|\gamma''(s)\|.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Multivariable calculus and vector norms suffice; the periodic parameter notation is defined locally. No knot theory or proof geometry is needed.
