Statement

Let G be a group with identity e acting on a set X from the left, so ex=x and g(hx)=(gh)x. If |X|=m and the action is transitive on n-element subsets, where 0\le n\le m, it is transitive on (m-n)-element subsets.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A group action is transitive on a family of subsets when any member can be moved to any other. Introductory actions and complement cardinalities suffice for the stated complementary-size equivalence.

Proof 1: Complementary homogeneity for a finite action

For subsets S,T the source uses gS=T\implies g(X\setminus S)=X\setminus T, since g permutes X. Apply the assumed transitivity to the complements of the desired (m-n)-subsets.
