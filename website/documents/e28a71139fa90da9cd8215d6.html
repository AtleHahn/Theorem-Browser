Let G^{\mathrm{op}} have multiplication g\circ h=hg. A right G-action on X defines a left G^{\mathrm{op}}-action by g*x=xg.

Proof 1: Right actions as actions of the opposite group

The source regards a right action as a left action of the opposite group. Its right-action identity gives g*(h*x)=(xh)g=x(hg)=(g\circ h)*x, with the same identity element.
