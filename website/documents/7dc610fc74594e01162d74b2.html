Statement

If compact boundaryless Riemannian surfaces M,N are homeomorphic, then \int_MK_M\,dA_M=\int_NK_N\,dA_N.

Proof 1: Total curvature is invariant under homeomorphism of closed surfaces — proof

For a compact surface without boundary, Gauss–Bonnet gives \int_M K\,dA=2\pi\chi(M).

If one bends and deforms the surface M, its Euler characteristic, being a topological invariant, will not change, while the curvatures at some points will. Gauss–Bonnet states, somewhat surprisingly, that the total integral of all curvatures will remain the same, no matter how the deforming is done.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The application combines intrinsic Riemannian surface curvature, area integration, and topological Euler characteristic through Gauss-Bonnet. These geometric objects are beyond the agreed baseline even though equality follows immediately.
