Statement

For infinite sets A,B, an injection A\to B gives |\mathcal P(A)|\le|\mathcal P(B)|. Thus for infinite cardinals \kappa<\lambda one has 2^\kappa\le2^\lambda.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Power sets, injections, cardinal comparisons and group actions are undergraduate set/algebra concepts. The induced action or comparison is explicitly given, without ordinal arithmetic or model-theoretic foundations.

Proof 1: Induce an injection on power sets — outline

An injection from A to B induces an injection from the subsets of A to the subsets of B. This gives the power-set cardinal inequality, and hence the stated exponential monotonicity.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Send each subset to its direct image under the injection; injectivity follows elementwise. Undergraduate sets and functions suffice, with cardinal comparison defined by injections and power-set cardinal notation as a small extension.
