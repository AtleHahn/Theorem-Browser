Statement

Let H be a real Hilbert space of functions on a set X whose point evaluations are bounded. For each x\in X there is a unique K_x\in H with f(x)=\langle f,K_x\rangle for every f\in H.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding bounded point evaluations and their representing vectors requires a Hilbert space of functions, not merely finite-dimensional vectors.

Mathematical Field
Functional analysis

Proof 1: Represent point evaluation by Riesz

For a fixed x, evaluation L_x(f)=f(x) is a bounded linear functional on H. The Riesz representation theorem supplies its unique representing vector K_x, giving the reproducing identity.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply Hilbert Riesz representation to each bounded evaluation functional. Hilbert function spaces and continuous dual representation together remain a doubtful small extension; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally under the conservative standard.
