Statement

For a finite field \mathbb F_q, let \mathbb G_m be the multiplicative group scheme. Then the algebraic stack B\mathbb G_m=[\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_q/\mathbb G_m] has dimension -1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves algebraic stacks; quotient stacks; dimension. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Quotient dimension calculation

For the quotient stack, use the dimension rule for a smooth algebraic group G \dim[X/G]=\dim X-\dim G. Then

{\displaystyle \dim B\mathbb {G} _{m}=\dim \operatorname {Spec} \mathbb {F} _{q}-\dim \mathbb {G} _{m}=-1.}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the quotient-stack dimension rule to the multiplicative group scheme. Algebraic stacks, group schemes and their dimensions require graduate algebraic geometry; the final subtraction does not remove that prerequisite.
