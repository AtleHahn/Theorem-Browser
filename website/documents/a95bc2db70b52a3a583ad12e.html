Statement

Let R be a ring with identity and let M be a unital right R-module. There is a natural isomorphism of abelian groups M\cong\operatorname{Hom}_R(R,M), where R on the right is its regular right module.

Level

Level justification

The statement uses modules over arbitrary rings and their homomorphism groups.

Proof 1: Specialization of additive Yoneda

Use the additive Yoneda lemma as an explicit external dependency: for a preadditive category \mathcal C, an object A, and an additive functor F:\mathcal C^{op}\to\mathbf{Ab}, there is a natural abelian-group isomorphism

\operatorname{Nat}(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(-,A),F)\cong F(A).

Rings are preadditive categories with one object, and the category of additive contravariant functors into abelian groups is the category of right modules. In the case of the ring R, the Yoneda lemma therefore reduces to M\cong\operatorname{Hom}_R(R,M).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Interpret the ring as a preadditive category and modules as additive contravariant functors before applying Yoneda. These categorical prerequisites exceed a small extension, even though the statement has an elementary direct proof.
