Statement

Fix an integer n\ge1. Let \mathcal K^n consist of all compact convex subsets of \mathbb R^n, including the empty set. A valuation is a function v:\mathcal K^n\to\mathbb R with v(\varnothing)=0 and v(S)+v(T)=v(S\cap T)+v(S\cup T) whenever S\cup T is convex. Suppose v is invariant under translations and rotations and continuous on nonempty sets for the Hausdorff distance d_H(S,T)=\max\{\sup_{s\in S}\inf_{t\in T}\|s-t\|,\sup_{t\in T}\inf_{s\in S}\|s-t\|\}. Let B be the closed Euclidean unit ball, and define W_j(K) by the polynomial identity \operatorname{Vol}_n(K+tB)=\sum_{j=0}^n\binom nj W_j(K)t^j\quad(t\ge0), where K+tB=\{x+ty:x\in K,y\in B\} and \operatorname{Vol}_n is Euclidean volume; set W_j(\varnothing)=0. There are real constants c_0,\ldots,c_n such that v(K)=\sum_{j=0}^n c_jW_j(K)\quad(K\in\mathcal K^n). In particular, if 0\le q\le n is an integer and also v(tK)=t^qv(K) for every t>0, then v is a constant multiple of W_{n-q}.
