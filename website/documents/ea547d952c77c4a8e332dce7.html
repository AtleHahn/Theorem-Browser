Statement

Let ABC be an equilateral triangle of side length a>0 and altitude h. For every interior point P, let s,t,u be its perpendicular distances to the three sides. Then s+t+u=h.

Proof 1: Partition into three equal-base triangles

Let ABC be an equilateral triangle whose height is h and whose side is a.

Let P be any point inside the triangle, and let u,s,t be the perpendicular distances of P from AB,BC,CA, respectively. Draw a line from P to each of A, B, and C, forming three triangles PAB, PBC, and PCA.

Now, the areas of these triangles are {\displaystyle {\frac {u\cdot a}{2}}}, {\displaystyle {\frac {s\cdot a}{2}}}, and {\displaystyle {\frac {t\cdot a}{2}}}. They exactly fill the enclosing triangle, so the sum of these areas is equal to the area of the enclosing triangle. So we can write:

{\displaystyle {\frac {u\cdot a}{2}}+{\frac {s\cdot a}{2}}+{\frac {t\cdot a}{2}}={\frac {h\cdot a}{2}}}

and thus

{\displaystyle u+s+t=h}
