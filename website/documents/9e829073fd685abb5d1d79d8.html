Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer and let f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb C be Lebesgue measurable with \int|f(x)|\,dx<\infty. Then \widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R^n}f(x)e^{-ix\cdot\xi}\,dx belongs to C_0(\mathbb R^n), the space of continuous functions tending to zero as |\xi|\to\infty.

Proof 1: Combine Fourier continuity with Riemann–Lebesgue decay

The Fourier transform of an integrable function is continuous. The Riemann–Lebesgue lemma says that it tends to zero at infinity. Thus it is a continuous function vanishing at infinity, which is exactly membership in C_0(\mathbb R^n).
