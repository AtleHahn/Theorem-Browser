Statement

Let G be a finite group, let EG be a contractible free G-CW complex, and put BG=EG/G. Let X be a finite-dimensional CW complex. Give the space F(BG,X) of continuous maps the compact-open topology. The map

c:X\longrightarrow F(BG,X),\qquad c(x)(b)=x,

is a weak homotopy equivalence: it induces a bijection on path components and isomorphisms on all based homotopy groups of positive degree, at every basepoint.

Equivalently, for each chosen basepoint of X, the space F_*(BG,X) of basepoint-preserving maps is weakly contractible: it has one path component and all its positive-degree homotopy groups vanish. Here a CW complex is finite-dimensional when its cell dimensions have a finite upper bound; it need not have finitely many cells. This is the trivial-action formulation of Sullivan’s conjecture proved by Miller.
