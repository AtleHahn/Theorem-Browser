Statement

Let A be an associative Euclidean Hurwitz algebra (\mathbb R, \mathbb C or \mathbb H), with standard involution, and n\ge3. Write X=(x_{ij})\in M_n(A). Let H_n(A)=\{X:(x_{ji}^*)=X\}, define X\circ Y=(XY+YX)/2 and (X,Y)=\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(XY). Then \circ is a commutative bilinear product satisfying the Jordan identity [L(X),L(X^2)]=0, where L(X)Y=X\circ Y. The displayed form is positive-definite and satisfies (Z\circ X,Y)=(X,Z\circ Y), so H_n(A) is a Euclidean Jordan algebra.

Proof 1: Real trace and associative symmetrization

The trace Tr(X) is defined as the sum of the diagonal elements of X and the real-valued trace by TrR(X ) = Re Tr(X ). The real-valued trace satisfies:

{\displaystyle \operatorname {Tr} _{\mathbf {R} }XY=\operatorname {Tr} _{\mathbf {R} }YX,\qquad \operatorname {Tr} _{\mathbf {R} }(XY)Z=\operatorname {Tr} _{\mathbf {R} }X(YZ).}

These are immediate consequences of the known identities for n = 1.

To check that Hn(A) satisfies the axioms for a Euclidean Jordan algebra, the real trace defines a symmetric bilinear form with (X, X) = Σ ‖xij‖2. So it is an inner product. It satisfies the associativity property (Z∘X, Y ) = (X, Z∘Y ) because of the properties of the real trace. The main axiom to check is the Jordan condition for the operators L(X) defined by L(X)Y = X ∘Y:

{\displaystyle \displaystyle {[L(X),L(X^{2})]=0.}}

This is easy to check when A is associative, since Mn(A) is an associative algebra so a Jordan algebra with X ∘Y = ⁠1/2⁠(X Y + Y X).

Level justification

Retain Beyond undergraduate provisionally. The symmetrized associativity identity is elementary algebra, but the full proof also treats quaternionic Hermitian matrices, their involution and norm, real rather than quaternion-valued traces, and the compatibility of this inner product with the Jordan product. Without prior quaternionic algebra, the cumulative preparation is not confidently a small extension of the baseline. This judgment concerns the complete R,C,H formulation, not merely its real-matrix case.
