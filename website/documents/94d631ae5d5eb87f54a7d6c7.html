Statement

Cut a disk through an interior point p into n equal-angle sectors, where n\ge8 is divisible by four. If each topping occupies a disk contained in the pizza and containing p in its interior, then alternating sector classes receive equal areas of each topping, even when its disk is not concentric with the pizza.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses disk sectors, equal angles and ordinary planar area.

Mathematical Field
High School Mathematics
Required topics

Review notes
none

Proof 1: Apply the disk theorem to each topping

Use the pizza theorem as an external prerequisite: if p lies inside a disk and n\ge8 is divisible by four, the n equal-angle sectors made by n/2 lines through p have equal total areas in the two alternating classes.

Apply the same pizza theorem to each topping disk, using the same point and sector lines. It gives equal areas for the two alternating classes for each topping separately.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply the stated disk-sector theorem independently to each topping disk through the same interior point. School plane geometry suffices.
