Statement

Let \Gamma be a countable discrete group and let H=\ell^2(\Gamma). Define \lambda(g)f(x)=f(g^{-1}x) and \rho(g)f(x)=f(xg). Then \lambda(\Gamma)^{\prime\prime}=\rho(\Gamma)^{\prime} and \rho(\Gamma)^{\prime\prime}=\lambda(\Gamma)^{\prime}, where primes denote commutants in B(H).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Countable-group regular representations on ℓ² and their operator commutants require infinite-dimensional operator theory.

Proof 1: Conjugating the regular generators — outline

For the regular representation the modular conjugation is Jf(g)=\overline{f(g^{-1})}. The finite-trace commutation theorem applies and identifies the conjugate of the left regular von Neumann algebra with its commutant. This J interchanges left and right regular operators, giving the two stated commutant identities.
