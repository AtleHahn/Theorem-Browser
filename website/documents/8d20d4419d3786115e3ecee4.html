Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer, let G be a closed subgroup of GL(n,\mathbb R), and let H be a subgroup closed in the relative topology of G. Then H is closed in GL(n,\mathbb R) and, with its relative topology, is an embedded real-analytic submanifold of GL(n,\mathbb R) and a real-analytic Lie group.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The hypotheses use matrix subgroup topology and the conclusion asserts an embedded analytic Lie-group structure.

Topological groups, Lie groups

Proof 1: Use transitivity of closedness before the matrix theorem

If H is closed in G, with G closed in GL(n, R), then H is closed in GL(n, R), so the specialization to GL(n, R) instead of arbitrary G ⊂ GL(n, R) matters little.

Apply the closed matrix-subgroup theorem to H in GL(n,\mathbb R) to obtain the asserted embedded real-analytic Lie-group structure.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Transitivity of closedness is elementary topology, but the conclusion invokes an embedded analytic manifold and Lie-group structure. Understanding that imported conclusion requires the full differential-geometric framework.
