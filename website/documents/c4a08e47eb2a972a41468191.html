Statement

A positive integer N is a congruum if N=y^2-x^2=z^2-y^2 for integers x,y,z. If a,b,c are positive integers with a^2+b^2=c^2, then (a-b)^2,c^2,(a+b)^2 form an arithmetic progression of squares with positive common difference 2ab, four times the area of the right triangle with legs a,b.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses an explicitly given Pythagorean relation, square roots of three squares, and elementary triangle area.

Proof 1: Difference and sum of the legs — outline

Use the hypotenuse as the middle square root and the difference and sum of the two legs as the outer square roots. The resulting congruum is four times the area of the same right triangle.

Level justification

Use the squared difference, hypotenuse and squared sum of the legs and expand to obtain common difference 2ab, four times the area. School algebra and right-triangle area suffice; the source gives the recipe as an outline.
