Statement

For nonnegative integers m,n,p,q with gcd one and odd sum, set d=m^2+n^2+p^2+q^2 and let E be the matrix displayed in the proof. Then E/d is a rational 3\times3 orthogonal matrix of determinant one.

Proof 1: Normalize the explicit polynomial matrix — outline

Use the source’s explicit matrix E=\begin{pmatrix}m^2+n^2-p^2-q^2&2np-2mq&2mp+2nq\\2mq+2np&m^2-n^2+p^2-q^2&2pq-2mn\\2nq-2mp&2mn+2pq&m^2-n^2-p^2+q^2\end{pmatrix}. Its columns have pairwise zero dot products and common squared length d^2, where d=m^2+n^2+p^2+q^2. The coordinate checks give E^TE=d^2I and \det E=d^3. Thus E/d is rational orthogonal with determinant one. These routine polynomial checks are implicit in the source matrix recipe.
