Statement

Let (\Omega,\mathcal F,P) be a probability space, and let \mathcal F_1,\mathcal F_2,\ldots be mutually independent sub-sigma-algebras of \mathcal F. Independence means that for every finite set of distinct indices i_1,\ldots,i_m and events A_j\in\mathcal F_{i_j},

P\left(\bigcap_{j=1}^m A_j\right)=\prod_{j=1}^mP(A_j).

Define the terminal (or tail) sigma-algebra by

\mathcal T=\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}\sigma\left(\bigcup_{k=n}^{\infty}\mathcal F_k\right),

where \sigma(\mathcal A) is the smallest sigma-algebra containing a collection \mathcal A of subsets of \Omega. A sigma-algebra contains \Omega and is closed under complements and countable unions. Then every E\in\mathcal T satisfies P(E)\in\{0,1\}.
