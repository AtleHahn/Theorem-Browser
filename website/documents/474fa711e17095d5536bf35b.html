Statement

Let \varphi be holomorphic on \mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}. If \sup_{z\in\mathbb D}|\varphi(z)|\le1 and |\varphi(z_0)|<1 at some z_0\in\mathbb D, then \varphi(\mathbb D)\subseteq\mathbb D.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Real analysis supplies derivatives, maxima, uniform convergence and completeness; complex arithmetic supplies the plane. Holomorphic means complex differentiable, and entire means so throughout the plane. With that short extension, the displayed bound or convergence assertion uses familiar analytic concepts without contour integrals or specialist function-space theory.

Proof 1: Apply open mapping after the supremum bound — outline

The supremum bound puts the image in the closed unit disc. Apply the open mapping theorem to obtain the open-disc conclusion in the nonconstant case; the prescribed interior value handles the constant case.

Level justification

The image argument is elementary topology once open mapping is given, but the application is to general holomorphic functions on a complex domain. Under the conservative baseline, retain the complex-analysis prerequisite rather than treating holomorphy as ordinary real differentiability.
