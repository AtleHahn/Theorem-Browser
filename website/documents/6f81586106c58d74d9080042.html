Statement

Let K be a field of characteristic zero, meaning that n\cdot1_K\ne0 for every integer n\geq1. Let d\geq1, let c_0,\ldots,c_{d-1}\in K, and let (u_n)_{n\geq0} be a sequence in K satisfying

u_{n+d}=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}c_j u_{n+j}\qquad(n\geq0).

There exist a finite set F\subseteq\mathbb Z_{\geq0}, an integer r\geq0, and integers a_i\geq0, b_i\geq1 for 1\leq i\leq r such that

\{n\geq0:u_n=0\}=F\ \cup\ \bigcup_{i=1}^{r}\{a_i+b_i k:k\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\}.

The union indexed by i is empty if r=0.

Level justification

Introductory abstract algebra supplies fields; characteristic zero is explicitly defined. Recurrences and arithmetic progressions require only elementary algebra, so the general field-valued statement needs no p-adic proof background.
