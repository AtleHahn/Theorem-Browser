Statement

The convergent real series

\zeta(3)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^3}

has an irrational sum: there are no integers p,q with q\ne0 for which \zeta(3)=p/q.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding a convergent infinite series and its sum belongs to undergraduate calculus/analysis; irrationality and the summands require elementary arithmetic. No zeta-function theory or proof methods are needed.

  • A landmark irrationality theorem for an odd zeta value; it has independent significance, unlike a routine even-zeta specialization.
