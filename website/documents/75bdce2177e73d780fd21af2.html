Statement

Let k be a field and A=k[x_1,\ldots,x_m] a nonzero commutative unital finitely generated k-algebra. There are 0\le d\le m elements y_1,\ldots,y_d\in A, algebraically independent over k, such that A is a finitely generated module over the polynomial subalgebra k[y_1,\ldots,y_d]. Moreover,

d=\dim A,

where the Krull dimension is the supremum of lengths of strict chains of prime ideals. If A is an integral domain, then also

d=\operatorname{trdeg}_k\operatorname{Frac}(A),

the transcendence degree of its fraction field over k.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although each definition relates to undergraduate algebra, understanding module-finite polynomial subalgebras, prime-ideal chains and transcendence degree together exceeds the conservative short-reading allowance.
