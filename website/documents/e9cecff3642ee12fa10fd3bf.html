Statement

Define \varphi:[0,1]\to2^{[0,1]} by \varphi(x)=\{3/4\} for 0\leq x<1/2 and \varphi(x)=\{1/4\} for 1/2\leq x\leq1. Its graph \{(x,y):y\in\varphi(x)\} is not closed in \mathbb R^2.

Proof 1: A graph sequence whose limit leaves the graph

For integers n\geq2, take x_n=1/2-1/n and y_n=3/4. Then y_n\in\varphi(x_n), and (x_n,y_n)\to(1/2,3/4). But 3/4\notin\varphi(1/2)=\{1/4\}, so this graph limit is outside the graph.
