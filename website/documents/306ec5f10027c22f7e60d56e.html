Statement

Let W be an h-cobordism from M to N, meaning both boundary inclusions are homotopy equivalences. Then \pi_k(W,M)=0 for every relative homotopy group degree k\ge1 (in degree one, interpreted as the trivial pointed set where necessary).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Relative homotopy groups and h-cobordisms require substantial algebraic-topology background. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Relative homotopy groups of an h-cobordism vanish — source argument

The inclusion M\hookrightarrow W is a homotopy equivalence by the definition of an h-cobordism. The standard relative-homotopy consequence of a homotopy equivalence therefore gives \pi_k(W,M)=0, as used in the source’s handle-trading discussion.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the relative-homotopy consequence of the boundary inclusion being a homotopy equivalence. Cobordisms, higher relative homotopy groups and pointed-set degree-one behavior require graduate topology.
