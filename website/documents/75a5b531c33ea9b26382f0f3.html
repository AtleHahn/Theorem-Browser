Statement

Let n be a positive square-free integer, meaning no square of a prime divides n. Define

\begin{aligned}A_n&=\#\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb Z^3:n=2x^2+y^2+32z^2\},\\B_n&=\#\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb Z^3:n=2x^2+y^2+8z^2\},\\C_n&=\#\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb Z^3:n=8x^2+2y^2+64z^2\},\\D_n&=\#\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb Z^3:n=8x^2+2y^2+16z^2\}.\end{aligned}

If there are positive rational numbers a,b,c with a^2+b^2=c^2 and ab/2=n, then 2A_n=B_n when n is odd, and 2C_n=D_n when n is even. The symbol \# counts integer triples, including all sign choices.

Proof 1: Theta counts and unconditional L-value vanishing (external)

A link to the proof outline can be found here (Kataoka (2015), §8 Proposition 8.16, Theorems 8.21 and 8.38, pp.42–62; §4 Theorem 4.17; original Tunnell Theorem 3 p.328.).

Supporting passages: Yukiya Kataoka, exposition of Tunnell, Section 8.

This is an unconditional proof outline. For odd squarefree d, the theta-series identities give a_d=A_d-B_d/2 and b_d=C_(2d)-D_(2d)/2, with all integer sign choices included. Kataoka’s Proposition 8.16 and Theorems 8.21 and 8.38 express L(E_d,1) and L(E_(2d),1) as positive constants times these squared coefficients, using Waldspurger’s theorem. A rational right triangle of area n gives positive rank on E_n. Coates–Wiles then forces the L-value to vanish, giving the required count equality. The elliptic-curve correspondence, Waldspurger and Coates–Wiles are substantive dependencies; BSD is not assumed.
