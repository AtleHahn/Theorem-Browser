Statement

Let \pi(x;n,m) count primes p\le x with p\equiv m\pmod n. As x\to\infty, \pi(x;4,1)\sim\pi(x;4,3)\sim x/(2\log x), where f\sim g means f/g\to1.

Proof 1: Specialize the progression prime number theorem

The prime number theorem for arithmetic progressions gives \pi(x;n,m)\sim x/(\varphi(n)\log x) for coprime m,n, where \varphi(n) is the number of residue classes coprime to n. Apply it with n=4 and m=1,3; both residues are coprime to four and \varphi(4)=2. This gives the two displayed asymptotics.
