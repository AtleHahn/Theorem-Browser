Statement

For a smooth vector field F defined on a neighborhood of a compact oriented smooth surface \Sigma without boundary, with compatible unit normal n, \iint_\Sigma(\nabla\times F)\cdot n\,dS=0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Vector calculus supplies curl, flux integrals and oriented smooth surfaces in three-dimensional space. The statement is a standard closed-surface flux assertion, without abstract manifold differential forms.

Proof 1: Cancellation of boundary contributions — outline

Stokes' theorem expresses the surface integral of the curl as the line integral along the oriented boundary. In an orientable manifold, the line integrals over the interior edges of a parametrized decomposition cancel out: the sum of the patch boundary integrals coincides with the line integral over the true boundary.

This also applies to manifolds without a boundary, such as the sphere or the torus. In such cases, the resulting line integrals completely cancel each other out. This allows us to assert that the surface integral of the curl of a vector field on a manifold without a boundary is zero.
