Statement

Let V be a two-dimensional real normed space. Suppose that for any unit vectors x,y a surjective linear isometry sends x to y. Then the unit circle is an ellipse, and the norm is induced by an inner product.

Proof 1: Move a contact point of the unique John ellipse — outline

Use John's ellipse theorem: the unit ball has a unique maximal-area inscribed ellipse, which touches its boundary. Since the internal John ellipse E is unique, for any bijective linear map T that preserves the unit circle, it must have TE=E. Since E must touch the circle at some point x, we may, by the assumed transitivity, map x to any other point y on the circle, thus every point y touches the ellipse E. Thus the circle is the ellipse.

The identification of an origin-centered ellipse with the unit circle of an inner-product norm is implicit in the source.
