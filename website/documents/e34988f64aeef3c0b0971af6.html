Statement

On the positive hyperboloid a^2=1+b^2+x^2+y^2, let g_s act by (a,y)\mapsto(a\cosh2s+y\sinh2s,y\cosh2s+a\sinh2s) and fix b,x. For compactly supported continuous F, define QF(A)=\int_{\mathbb R}F(g_sA)\,ds. Then QF depends only on b,x. If F is also invariant under rotations of (b,x), then so is QF.

Proof 1: Boost orbit integrals are independent of the transverse coordinate (outline)

With b,x fixed, the boosts move transitively along the positive hyperbola a^2-y^2=1+b^2+x^2. Integrating over the boost group is invariant under translating its parameter, so the orbit integral is constant as y varies. The rotations of (b,x) commute with the boosts. Therefore their invariance also passes through the integral.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Parametrize each positive hyperbola by hyperbolic functions and shift the real integration parameter; rotations commute with the explicit boost. Undergraduate multivariable calculus and ordinary parameter integration suffice.
