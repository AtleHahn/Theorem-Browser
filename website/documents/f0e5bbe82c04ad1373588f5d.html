Statement

Let G be a group, E a field and n\ge1. Let \rho_1,\rho_2:G\to\mathrm{GL}_n(E) be homomorphisms whose representations on E^n are semisimple, meaning direct sums of invariant subspaces on which no nonzero proper invariant subspace exists. If \det(TI-\rho_1(g))=\det(TI-\rho_2(g)) in E[T] for every g\in G, there exists S\in\mathrm{GL}_n(E) with S\rho_1(g)S^{-1}=\rho_2(g) for every g\in G. If \operatorname{char}E=0 or \operatorname{char}E>n, the same conclusion follows from the weaker condition \operatorname{tr}\rho_1(g)=\operatorname{tr}\rho_2(g) for every g\in G.

Proof 1: Simple characters and semisimple multiplicities (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Theorem 2.4.6 and Remark 2.4.7(iii–iv), printed pp. 36–37.).

Apply the group-algebra formulation in Remark 2.4.7(iii). Since the representations are semisimple, equality of composition factors gives an isomorphism, represented by a single invertible matrix. For the trace refinement, apply Newton identities to the powers of each g; their denominators are invertible under the stated characteristic restriction.
