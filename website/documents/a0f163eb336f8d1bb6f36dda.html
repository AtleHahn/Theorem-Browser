Statement

Let A,B,C,D be four distinct points, in that cyclic order, on a circle in the Euclidean plane. Let M_1,M_2,M_3,M_4 be the incenters of triangles ABD,ABC,BCD,ACD, respectively. Then M_1M_2M_3M_4 is a rectangle. The incenter of a triangle is the center of its inscribed circle, equivalently the common intersection of its internal angle bisectors.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement uses a circle, four ordered points and triangle incircles. Incenter is defined by elementary angle bisectors; the conclusion is a rectangle. No prerequisite beyond high-school Euclidean geometry is needed.
