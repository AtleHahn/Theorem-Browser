Statement

Let A\to B be a homomorphism of commutative rings such that B is faithfully flat as an A-module. The extension-of-scalars functor T:A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}\to B\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}, defined by T(M)=B\otimes_A M, is comonadic.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Faithfully flat descent expressed through comonadicity requires both categorical descent and commutative algebra. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Flatness and reflection of isomorphisms (outline)

Apply the dual of Beck's theorem: a functor with a right adjoint which reflects isomorphisms and preserves the relevant equalizers in its domain is comonadic. Extension of scalars has restriction of scalars as right adjoint, and module categories have equalizers. Flatness of B implies that T preserves finite limits, while faithful flatness implies that T reflects isomorphisms. Thus the dual Beck criterion applies.
