Statement

If a,b,c,d\in\mathbb R and a\ne0, then ax^3+bx^2+cx+d has at least one real root.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The conclusion concerns only existence of a real zero of an explicit cubic.

Mathematical Field

Required topics

Review notes
none

Proof 1: Pair the nonreal conjugate roots

First, if r is a root of a polynomial with real coefficients, then its complex conjugate is also a root. So the non-real roots, if any, occur as pairs of complex conjugate roots. As a cubic polynomial has three roots (not necessarily distinct) by the fundamental theorem of algebra, at least one root must be real.

Level

Level justification

Use the stated count of three roots and pair nonreal roots with equal multiplicity. Elementary complex polynomial arithmetic and parity suffice.
