Statement

Let A,B,C\in\mathbb R^2 be noncollinear. Define S=(S_x,S_y) and scalars a,b by

{{\begin{aligned}S_{x}&={\frac {1}{2}}\det {\begin{bmatrix}|\mathbf {A} |^{2}&A_{y}&1\\|\mathbf {B} |^{2}&B_{y}&1\\|\mathbf {C} |^{2}&C_{y}&1\end{bmatrix}},\\[5pt]S_{y}&={\frac {1}{2}}\det {\begin{bmatrix}A_{x}&|\mathbf {A} |^{2}&1\\B_{x}&|\mathbf {B} |^{2}&1\\C_{x}&|\mathbf {C} |^{2}&1\end{bmatrix}},\\[5pt]a&=\det {\begin{bmatrix}A_{x}&A_{y}&1\\B_{x}&B_{y}&1\\C_{x}&C_{y}&1\end{bmatrix}},\\[5pt]b&=\det {\begin{bmatrix}A_{x}&A_{y}&|\mathbf {A} |^{2}\\B_{x}&B_{y}&|\mathbf {B} |^{2}\\C_{x}&C_{y}&|\mathbf {C} |^{2}\end{bmatrix}}\end{aligned}}}

Then a\ne0, the circumcenter is S/a, and the circumradius is \sqrt{b/a+|S|^2/a^2}.

Proof 1: Expand cofactors and complete the square

Use the circumcircle determinant equation

{\displaystyle \det {\begin{bmatrix}|\mathbf {v} |^{2}&v_{x}&v_{y}&1\\|\mathbf {A} |^{2}&A_{x}&A_{y}&1\\|\mathbf {B} |^{2}&B_{x}&B_{y}&1\\|\mathbf {C} |^{2}&C_{x}&C_{y}&1\end{bmatrix}}=0.}

Using cofactor expansion, let

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}S_{x}&={\frac {1}{2}}\det {\begin{bmatrix}|\mathbf {A} |^{2}&A_{y}&1\\|\mathbf {B} |^{2}&B_{y}&1\\|\mathbf {C} |^{2}&C_{y}&1\end{bmatrix}},\\[5pt]S_{y}&={\frac {1}{2}}\det {\begin{bmatrix}A_{x}&|\mathbf {A} |^{2}&1\\B_{x}&|\mathbf {B} |^{2}&1\\C_{x}&|\mathbf {C} |^{2}&1\end{bmatrix}},\\[5pt]a&=\det {\begin{bmatrix}A_{x}&A_{y}&1\\B_{x}&B_{y}&1\\C_{x}&C_{y}&1\end{bmatrix}},\\[5pt]b&=\det {\begin{bmatrix}A_{x}&A_{y}&|\mathbf {A} |^{2}\\B_{x}&B_{y}&|\mathbf {B} |^{2}\\C_{x}&C_{y}&|\mathbf {C} |^{2}\end{bmatrix}}\end{aligned}}}

we then have {\displaystyle a|\mathbf {v} |^{2}-2\mathbf {S}\cdot\mathbf {v} -b=0} where {\displaystyle \mathbf {S} =(S_{x},S_{y}),} and – since the three points are not in a line, so a\ne0 – {\displaystyle \left|\mathbf {v} -{\tfrac {\mathbf {S} }{a}}\right|^{2}={\tfrac {b}{a}}+{\tfrac {|\mathbf {S} |^{2}}{a^{2}}},} giving the circumcenter {\displaystyle {\tfrac {\mathbf {S} }{a}}} and the circumradius {\displaystyle {\sqrt {{\tfrac {b}{a}}+{\tfrac {|\mathbf {S} |^{2}}{a^{2}}}}}.}
