Proof 1: Append three to the six-prime theorem — outline

Use Ramaré’s theorem that every even integer at least 4 is a sum of at most six primes. For odd n\ge7, apply it to n-3\ge4 and append the prime 3. For n=5, the prime 5 itself is a one-term sum.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Apply the explicitly given six-prime theorem to n-3, append three, and check five separately. School integer arithmetic suffices; the deep analytic proof of the supplied theorem is not invoked.
