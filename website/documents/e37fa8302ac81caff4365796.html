Statement

Let (X,d) be a locally compact length metric space. Locally compact means every point has a compact neighborhood. A length metric means d(x,y)=\inf_{\gamma(0)=x,\,\gamma(1)=y}L(\gamma),\qquad L(\gamma)=\sup_{0=t_0<\cdots<t_k=1}\sum_{i=1}^k d(\gamma(t_{i-1}),\gamma(t_i)), where \gamma:[0,1]\to X is continuous and the supremum is over all finite partitions. Then X is complete if and only if every closed bounded subset is compact. When these conditions hold, any two points x,y can be joined by a continuous curve \gamma of length d(x,y). Complete means every Cauchy sequence converges in X.
