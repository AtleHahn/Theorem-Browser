Statement

For C^3 real functions g:I\to J and f:J\to\mathbb R on open intervals, D^3(f\circ g)=f'(g)g'''+3f''(g)g'g''+f'''(g)(g')^3.

Proof 1: Identify the three derivative directions

The third mixed-derivative formula is

\begin{aligned} \partial_i\partial_j\partial_k f(g) ={}&f'(g)g_{ijk}\\ &+f''(g)(g_i g_{jk}+g_j g_{ik}+g_k g_{ij})\\ &+f'''(g)g_i g_j g_k, \end{aligned}

where subscripts denote partial derivatives. If the three derivative directions are indistinguishable from each other, then three of the five terms above are also indistinguishable from each other, and then we have the classic one-variable formula in the statement.
