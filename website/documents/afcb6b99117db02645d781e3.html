Statement

Let a finite group G act continuously on X, and let EG be its contractible free classifying G-space. With conjugation action (g\cdot f)(e)=g\cdot f(g^{-1}\cdot e) on the mapping space F(EG,X), there is a natural map X^G\to F(EG,X)^G sending a fixed point to its constant map. Mapping spaces use the compact-open topology.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Classifying spaces, homotopy fixed points and mapping-space actions introduce several connected algebraic-topology constructions. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Precomposing with the equivariant map to a point (outline)

The G-equivariant map from EG to a single point * induces by precomposition a natural map

\eta:X^G=F(*,X)^G\longrightarrow F(EG,X)^G

from the fixed points to the homotopy fixed points of G acting on X.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Precompose with EG to a point and take equivariant maps. Contractible free classifying spaces, equivariant mapping spaces and homotopy fixed points require algebraic topology beyond point-set topology.
