Statement

Let (M,g) be a closed Riemannian manifold of dimension m\ge1, where closed means compact without boundary. Let \operatorname{vol}_g(M) be its Riemannian volume and \operatorname{inj}(M)=\inf_{p\in M}\operatorname{inj}_p(M) its injectivity radius, where \operatorname{inj}_p(M) is the supremum of radii r for which the exponential map at p is a diffeomorphism from the open radius-r ball in T_pM onto its image. If c_m is the volume of the unit round sphere S^m, then

\operatorname{vol}_g(M)\ge c_m\left(\frac{\operatorname{inj}(M)}{\pi}\right)^m.

Equality holds if and only if (M,g) is isometric to a round sphere, with its radius allowed to be any positive number.

Level justification
