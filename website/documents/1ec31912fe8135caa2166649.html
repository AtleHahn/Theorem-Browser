Statement

Let H be a complex Hilbert space and B(H) its bounded operators. For every M\subseteq B(H), \overline M^{\mathrm{SOT}}\subseteq\overline M^{\mathrm{WOT}}. SOT is pointwise norm convergence on H; WOT is convergence of all scalar coefficients \langle Tx,y\rangle.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

General Hilbert space and bounded operators add completeness and continuous-linear-map definitions; SOT/WOT are explicitly pointwise norm/coefficient convergence. These linked definitions extend undergraduate metric and point-set topology.

Proof 1: Compare neighborhoods in the two operator topologies

This follows directly from the weak operator topology being coarser than the strong operator topology: for every point x in \overline M^{\mathrm{SOT}}, every open neighborhood of x in the weak operator topology is also open in the strong operator topology and therefore contains a member of M; therefore x is also a member of \overline M^{\mathrm{WOT}}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The full Hilbert-space setting includes completeness and bounded linear maps. With these limited extensions of undergraduate inner products/topology, the explicitly defined pointwise norm and scalar-coefficient topologies can be compared by Cauchy–Schwarz. A coarser neighborhood then meets M whenever the finer one does.
