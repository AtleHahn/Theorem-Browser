Statement

Let a<b be real. Let f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R be nonnegative, nonincreasing and integrable, and let g:[a,b]\to[0,1] be integrable. Put k=\int_a^bg(x)\,dx. Then

\int_{b-k}^{b}f(x)\,dx\le\int_a^bf(x)g(x)\,dx\le\int_a^{a+k}f(x)\,dx.

Integrals may be interpreted as Lebesgue integrals (and the assertion applies in particular when the functions are Riemann integrable). Nonincreasing means f(x)\ge f(y) whenever x\le y.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The full formulation allows measurable integrable weights beyond Riemann integration; Lebesgue integration is outside the baseline, so conservative classification retains beyond undergraduate.
