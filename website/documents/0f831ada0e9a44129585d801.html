The triangle (7,15,20) has integer area 42 and side gcd one, but its sides are not pairwise coprime.

Proof 1: Source calculation — outline

The Heronian triple (a,b,c) is primitive provided a,b,c are setwise coprime. With primitive Heronian triangles the stronger statement that they are pairwise coprime does not always hold true, such as with (7,15,20).

The source lists (7,15,20) with area 42 as a primitive Heronian triple, without expanding the area or gcd calculations.
