Statement

Let (a_n),(b_n),(c_n) be real sequences, with a_n,c_n\to\ell\in\mathbb R. If a_n\le b_n\le c_n for all n\ge N for some N\in\mathbb N, then b_n\to\ell.

Proof 1: Epsilon bounds for sequences — outline

Use the epsilon definition of convergence in a bounding argument: choose indices beyond which a_n and c_n differ from \ell by less than \varepsilon, and take n beyond both indices and N. Then -\varepsilon<a_n-\ell\le b_n-\ell\le c_n-\ell<\varepsilon.
