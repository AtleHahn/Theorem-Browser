Statement

Let M be a smooth regular surface in \mathbb R^3, let p\in M, and choose a unit normal n at p. For a unit tangent vector v, let \kappa(v) be the signed curvature at p of the section of M by the plane spanned by v and n through p, positive when the curve bends toward n. If \kappa is not constant on unit tangent directions, its maximum k_1 and minimum k_2 are attained in perpendicular unit directions v_1,v_2. For any unit tangent vector v making angle \theta with v_1, \kappa(v)=k_1\cos^2\theta+k_2\sin^2\theta. The extrema are called principal curvatures and their directions principal directions.
