Statement

Let A be a commutative ring complete with respect to an ideal \mathfrak m, and \mathbf f=(f_1,\ldots,f_n) with f_i\in A[X_1,\ldots,X_n]. Suppose \mathbf a\in A^n, f_i(\mathbf a)\in\mathfrak m for every i, and \det J_{\mathbf f}(\mathbf a) is a unit. Then there is \mathbf b\in A^n with \mathbf f(\mathbf b)=0 and \mathbf b\equiv\mathbf a\pmod{\mathfrak m}. Here J_{\mathbf f}=(\partial f_i/\partial X_j)_{i,j} is the matrix of formal polynomial partial derivatives.

Proof 1: A unit-Jacobian specialization of multivariable Hensel lifting — proof

In the multivariable Hensel theorem, the approximate-solution condition is f_i(\mathbf a)\in(\det J_{\mathbf f}(\mathbf a))^2\mathfrak m, and the conclusion has \mathbf b-\mathbf a\in\det J_{\mathbf f}(\mathbf a)\mathfrak m coordinatewise. When the determinant is a unit, both ideals are \mathfrak m, giving the stated specialization.

Use that multiplying an ideal by a unit leaves it unchanged in the supplied multivariable theorem. The algebraic simplification is easy but the full ideal-adic completion and root-existence framework exceed the small-extension allowance; retain conservatively.
