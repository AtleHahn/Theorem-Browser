Statement

Let (a_n)_{n\geq1} and (b_n)_{n\geq1} be real sequences with |b_n|\geq|a_n|+1 for every n\geq1. Define A_0=0, A_1=a_1, B_0=1, B_1=b_1, and A_n=b_nA_{n-1}+a_nA_{n-2}, B_n=b_nB_{n-1}+a_nB_{n-2} for n\geq2. The continued fraction with successive numerators a_n and denominators b_n converges absolutely: \sum_{n\geq1}|A_n/B_n-A_{n-1}/B_{n-1}|<\infty. Its limit x satisfies |x|\leq1.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves real sequences and series; continued fractions and convergent recurrence; absolute convergence. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Determinant recurrence and telescoping bound

Recall that the {\displaystyle n}th convergents of {\displaystyle x}, which will be denoted by {\displaystyle {\tfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}} here, can be computed from the following recurrence relation:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}A_{n}&:=b_{n}A_{n-1}+a_{n}A_{n-2}\\B_{n}&:=b_{n}B_{n-1}+a_{n}B_{n-2},\qquad n\geq 2\end{aligned}}}

where {\displaystyle A_{0}=0}, {\displaystyle A_{1}=a_{1}}, {\displaystyle B_{0}=1}, and {\displaystyle B_{1}=b_{1}}.

nth convergent as a series

First, we will prove the following claim via mathematical induction

Claim—For all positive integers {\displaystyle n}, then

{\displaystyle A_{n}B_{n-1}-A_{n-1}B_{n}=(-1)^{n-1}a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}

Proof

The case {\displaystyle n=1} is trivial.

Suppose the claim is true for {\displaystyle n=k}. By using the two recurrence relation above, it follows that

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}A_{k+1}B_{k}-A_{k}B_{k+1}&=\left(b_{k+1}A_{k}+a_{k+1}A_{k-1}\right)B_{k}-A_{k}\left(b_{k+1}B_{k}+a_{k+1}B_{k-1}\right)\\&=b_{k+1}A_{k}B_{k}+a_{k+1}A_{k-1}B_{k}-b_{k+1}A_{k}B_{k}-a_{k+1}A_{k}B_{k-1}\\&=a_{k+1}A_{k-1}B_{k}-a_{k+1}A_{k}B_{k-1}\\&=-a_{k+1}\left(A_{k}B_{k-1}-A_{k-1}B_{k}\right)\\&=-a_{k+1}\cdot (-1)^{k-1}a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{k}\\&=(-1)^{k}a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{k}a_{k+1}\end{aligned}}}

which finishes the induction step.

By dividing both sides of the claim by {\displaystyle B_{n}\cdot B_{n-1}}, the equation becomes

{\displaystyle {\dfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}-{\dfrac {A_{n-1}}{B_{n-1}}}=(-1)^{n-1}{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}{B_{n-1}\cdot B_{n}}}}

Thus,

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\sum _{i\,=\,1}^{n}\left({\dfrac {A_{i}}{B_{i}}}-{\dfrac {A_{i-1}}{B_{i-1}}}\right)&=\sum _{i\,=\,1}^{n}(-1)^{i-1}{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{i}}{B_{i-1}B_{i}}}\\{\dfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}-{\dfrac {A_{0}}{B_{0}}}&={\dfrac {a_{1}}{B_{0}B_{1}}}-{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}}{B_{1}B_{2}}}+{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}a_{3}}{B_{2}B_{3}}}-\ldots +(-1)^{n-1}{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}{B_{n-1}B_{n}}}\\{\dfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}&={\dfrac {a_{1}}{B_{0}B_{1}}}-{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}}{B_{1}B_{2}}}+{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}a_{3}}{B_{2}B_{3}}}-\ldots +(-1)^{n-1}{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}{B_{n-1}B_{n}}}\end{aligned}}}

Absolute value of nth convergent as a series

Now suppose that {\displaystyle |b_{n}|\geq |a_{n}|+1} for all {\displaystyle n}. Using the recurrence relation of {\displaystyle (B_{n})}, note that

{\displaystyle \left|b_{n}B_{n-1}\right|=\left|B_{n}-a_{n}B_{n-2}\right|\leq \left|B_{n}\right|+\left|a_{n}B_{n-2}\right|.}

Thus,

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\left|B_{n}\right|&\geq \left|b_{n}\right|\left|B_{n-1}\right|-\left|a_{n}\right|\left|B_{n-2}\right|\\&\geq \left(\left|a_{n}\right|+1\right)\left|B_{n-1}\right|-\left|a_{n}\right|\left|B_{n-2}\right|\\\left|B_{n}\right|-\left|B_{n-1}\right|&\geq \left|a_{n}\right|\left(\left|B_{n-1}\right|-\left|B_{n-2}\right|\right)\end{aligned}}}

Since {\displaystyle \left|B_{1}\right|-\left|B_{0}\right|=\left|b_{1}\right|-1\geq \left|a_{1}\right|} by assumption, then by using mathematical induction, one can show that

{\displaystyle \left|B_{n}\right|-\left|B_{n-1}\right|\geq \prod _{i\,=\,1}^{n}\left|a_{i}\right|.}

Consequently, the sequence of {\displaystyle \left|B_{n}\right|} is monotone nondecreasing and bounded from below by {\displaystyle \left|B_{0}\right|=1}. Moreover,

{\displaystyle {\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}B_{n-1}\right|}}\prod _{i\,=\,1}^{n}\left|a_{i}\right|\leq {\dfrac {\left|B_{n}\right|-\left|B_{n-1}\right|}{\left|B_{n}B_{n-1}\right|}}={\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n-1}\right|}}-{\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}}

Since the right-hand side forms a telescoping series, it is easy to see that

{\displaystyle {\dfrac {\left|a_{1}\right|}{\left|B_{0}B_{1}\right|}}+{\dfrac {\left|a_{1}a_{2}\right|}{\left|B_{1}B_{2}\right|}}+\ldots +{\dfrac {\left|a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}\right|}{\left|B_{n-1}B_{n}\right|}}\leq {\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{0}\right|}}-{\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}=1-{\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}<1}

for all values of {\displaystyle n}. Furthermore, the nondecreasing property of the sequence {\displaystyle \left|B_{n}\right|} also implies the nondecreasing property of the sequence {\displaystyle 1-{\tfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}}. Thus, the sequence {\displaystyle 1-{\tfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}} must converge, by the monotone convergence theorem.

Note that the left-hand side is the upper bound of series representation of {\displaystyle \left|{\tfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}\right|} after applying triangle inequality, which completes the proof.

Statement

Let (a_n)_{n\geq1} and (b_n)_{n\geq1} be real sequences with |b_n|\geq|a_n|+1 for every n\geq1. Define A_0=0, A_1=a_1, B_0=1, B_1=b_1, and A_n=b_nA_{n-1}+a_nA_{n-2}, B_n=b_nB_{n-1}+a_nB_{n-2} for n\geq2. The continued fraction with successive numerators a_n and denominators b_n converges absolutely: \sum_{n\geq1}|A_n/B_n-A_{n-1}/B_{n-1}|<\infty. Its limit x satisfies |x|\leq1.

Proof 1 — Determinant recurrence and telescoping bound

Recall that the {\displaystyle n}th convergents of {\displaystyle x}, which will be denoted by {\displaystyle {\tfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}} here, can be computed from the following recurrence relation:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}A_{n}&:=b_{n}A_{n-1}+a_{n}A_{n-2}\\B_{n}&:=b_{n}B_{n-1}+a_{n}B_{n-2},\qquad n\geq 2\end{aligned}}}

where {\displaystyle A_{0}=0}, {\displaystyle A_{1}=a_{1}}, {\displaystyle B_{0}=1}, and {\displaystyle B_{1}=b_{1}}.

nth convergent as a series

First, we will prove the following claim via mathematical induction

Claim—For all positive integers {\displaystyle n}, then

{\displaystyle A_{n}B_{n-1}-A_{n-1}B_{n}=(-1)^{n-1}a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}

Proof

The case {\displaystyle n=1} is trivial.

Suppose the claim is true for {\displaystyle n=k}. By using the two recurrence relation above, it follows that

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}A_{k+1}B_{k}-A_{k}B_{k+1}&=\left(b_{k+1}A_{k}+a_{k+1}A_{k-1}\right)B_{k}-A_{k}\left(b_{k+1}B_{k}+a_{k+1}B_{k-1}\right)\\&=b_{k+1}A_{k}B_{k}+a_{k+1}A_{k-1}B_{k}-b_{k+1}A_{k}B_{k}-a_{k+1}A_{k}B_{k-1}\\&=a_{k+1}A_{k-1}B_{k}-a_{k+1}A_{k}B_{k-1}\\&=-a_{k+1}\left(A_{k}B_{k-1}-A_{k-1}B_{k}\right)\\&=-a_{k+1}\cdot (-1)^{k-1}a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{k}\\&=(-1)^{k}a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{k}a_{k+1}\end{aligned}}}

which finishes the induction step.

By dividing both sides of the claim by {\displaystyle B_{n}\cdot B_{n-1}}, the equation becomes

{\displaystyle {\dfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}-{\dfrac {A_{n-1}}{B_{n-1}}}=(-1)^{n-1}{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}{B_{n-1}\cdot B_{n}}}}

Thus,

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\sum _{i\,=\,1}^{n}\left({\dfrac {A_{i}}{B_{i}}}-{\dfrac {A_{i-1}}{B_{i-1}}}\right)&=\sum _{i\,=\,1}^{n}(-1)^{i-1}{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{i}}{B_{i-1}B_{i}}}\\{\dfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}-{\dfrac {A_{0}}{B_{0}}}&={\dfrac {a_{1}}{B_{0}B_{1}}}-{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}}{B_{1}B_{2}}}+{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}a_{3}}{B_{2}B_{3}}}-\ldots +(-1)^{n-1}{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}{B_{n-1}B_{n}}}\\{\dfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}&={\dfrac {a_{1}}{B_{0}B_{1}}}-{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}}{B_{1}B_{2}}}+{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}a_{3}}{B_{2}B_{3}}}-\ldots +(-1)^{n-1}{\dfrac {a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}{B_{n-1}B_{n}}}\end{aligned}}}

Absolute value of nth convergent as a series

Now suppose that {\displaystyle |b_{n}|\geq |a_{n}|+1} for all {\displaystyle n}. Using the recurrence relation of {\displaystyle (B_{n})}, note that

{\displaystyle \left|b_{n}B_{n-1}\right|=\left|B_{n}-a_{n}B_{n-2}\right|\leq \left|B_{n}\right|+\left|a_{n}B_{n-2}\right|.}

Thus,

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\left|B_{n}\right|&\geq \left|b_{n}\right|\left|B_{n-1}\right|-\left|a_{n}\right|\left|B_{n-2}\right|\\&\geq \left(\left|a_{n}\right|+1\right)\left|B_{n-1}\right|-\left|a_{n}\right|\left|B_{n-2}\right|\\\left|B_{n}\right|-\left|B_{n-1}\right|&\geq \left|a_{n}\right|\left(\left|B_{n-1}\right|-\left|B_{n-2}\right|\right)\end{aligned}}}

Since {\displaystyle \left|B_{1}\right|-\left|B_{0}\right|=\left|b_{1}\right|-1\geq \left|a_{1}\right|} by assumption, then by using mathematical induction, one can show that

{\displaystyle \left|B_{n}\right|-\left|B_{n-1}\right|\geq \prod _{i\,=\,1}^{n}\left|a_{i}\right|.}

Consequently, the sequence of {\displaystyle \left|B_{n}\right|} is monotone nondecreasing and bounded from below by {\displaystyle \left|B_{0}\right|=1}. Moreover,

{\displaystyle {\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}B_{n-1}\right|}}\prod _{i\,=\,1}^{n}\left|a_{i}\right|\leq {\dfrac {\left|B_{n}\right|-\left|B_{n-1}\right|}{\left|B_{n}B_{n-1}\right|}}={\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n-1}\right|}}-{\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}}

Since the right-hand side forms a telescoping series, it is easy to see that

{\displaystyle {\dfrac {\left|a_{1}\right|}{\left|B_{0}B_{1}\right|}}+{\dfrac {\left|a_{1}a_{2}\right|}{\left|B_{1}B_{2}\right|}}+\ldots +{\dfrac {\left|a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}\right|}{\left|B_{n-1}B_{n}\right|}}\leq {\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{0}\right|}}-{\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}=1-{\dfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}<1}

for all values of {\displaystyle n}. Furthermore, the nondecreasing property of the sequence {\displaystyle \left|B_{n}\right|} also implies the nondecreasing property of the sequence {\displaystyle 1-{\tfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}}. Thus, the sequence {\displaystyle 1-{\tfrac {1}{\left|B_{n}\right|}}} must converge, by the monotone convergence theorem.

Note that the left-hand side is the upper bound of series representation of {\displaystyle \left|{\tfrac {A_{n}}{B_{n}}}\right|} after applying triangle inequality, which completes the proof.
