Statement

If ABCD is a rectangle and P is an interior point, then AP^2+CP^2=BP^2+DP^2, where juxtaposed points denote Euclidean distances.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The rectangle and its interior-point squared distances use elementary geometry.

Proof 1: Equal-base substitution in the trapezoid theorem

Use the isosceles-trapezoid identity as an external theorem: if ABCD is a convex isosceles trapezoid with parallel bases AB,CD and P is interior, then

AP^2+\frac{AB}{CD}CP^2=BP^2+\frac{AB}{CD}DP^2.

In the case of a rectangle the fraction AB/CD evaluates to 1 and hence yields the British flag identity.

Level
High school only
Level justification

A rectangle is an isosceles trapezoid in the inclusive sense required here, and its two opposite bases have equal lengths. Substituting their ratio one in the explicitly stated identity yields the result. Only elementary geometry and algebra are used.
