Statement

For u,v\in C^n(I) on a real interval I and integer n\geq1, \int uv^{(n)}\,dx=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}(-1)^ju^{(j)}v^{(n-1-j)}+(-1)^n\int u^{(n)}v\,dx, up to an additive constant.

Proof 1: Repeated integration by parts — outline

Considering a second derivative of {\displaystyle v} in the integral on the left-hand side of the integration-by-parts formula suggests a repeated application to the integral on the right-hand side:

{\displaystyle \int uv''\,dx=uv'-\int u'v'\,dx=uv'-\left(u'v-\int u''v\,dx\right).}

Extending this concept of repeated partial integration to derivatives of degree n leads to

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\int u^{(0)}v^{(n)}\,dx&=u^{(0)}v^{(n-1)}-u^{(1)}v^{(n-2)}+u^{(2)}v^{(n-3)}-\cdots +(-1)^{n-1}u^{(n-1)}v^{(0)}+(-1)^{n}\int u^{(n)}v^{(0)}\,dx.\\[5pt]&=\sum _{k=0}^{n-1}(-1)^{k}u^{(k)}v^{(n-1-k)}+(-1)^{n}\int u^{(n)}v^{(0)}\,dx.\end{aligned}}}

Level justification
