Statement

Let G be a connected complex semisimple Lie group, let \mathfrak g be its Lie algebra, let \mathfrak h\subseteq\mathfrak g be a Cartan subalgebra, and let W be its Weyl group acting on \mathfrak h. Write \mathbb C[\mathfrak g]^G for the algebra of complex polynomial functions F on \mathfrak g satisfying F(\operatorname{Ad}(g)x)=F(x) for all g\in G and x\in\mathfrak g, and write \mathbb C[\mathfrak h]^W for the algebra of polynomial functions on \mathfrak h invariant under W.

Restriction induces an isomorphism of complex algebras

\mathbb C[\mathfrak g]^G\longrightarrow\mathbb C[\mathfrak h]^W,\qquad F\longmapsto F|_{\mathfrak h}.

Thus every W-invariant polynomial on \mathfrak h is the restriction of a unique adjoint-invariant polynomial on \mathfrak g.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Semisimple Lie groups, their adjoint representations, Cartan subalgebras and Weyl actions form a cumulative prerequisite chain well beyond standard undergraduate algebra and the conservative reading allowance. Understanding polynomial rings alone does not suffice.
