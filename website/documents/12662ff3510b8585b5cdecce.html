Statement

For an integer k\ge3, put D=-(4^k-3\cdot2^{k+1}+1). Then x^2+D=2^n has at least the four positive integer solutions (x,n)=(2^k-3,3),(2^k-1,k+2),(2^k+1,k+3),(3\cdot2^k-1,2k+3).

Proof 1: Four explicit parameterized pairs — outline

If D=-(4^k-3\cdot2^{k+1}+1) for an integer k\geq3, there are at least the four solutions

(x,n)= \begin{cases} (2^k-3,3),\\ (2^k-1,k+2),\\ (2^k+1,k+3),\\ (3\cdot2^k-1,2k+3). \end{cases}

The source gives these pairs without expanding their substitutions into x^2+D=2^n.
