Statement

The moduli space of smooth complex cubic surfaces is a rational variety of dimension 4, where rational means birational to \mathbf P^4_{\mathbb C}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves moduli of complex cubic surfaces; rational varieties; dimension. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Proof 1: Use the weighted-projective open embedding

The Salmon–Clebsch description identifies this moduli space with a nonempty open subset of the weighted projective space \mathbb P(1,2,3,4,5). This weighted projective space is a rational fourfold, so its nonempty open subset is also a rational fourfold.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Identify the moduli space with a weighted-projective open subset and transfer birational type. Moduli, weighted projective varieties and birational dimension require substantial algebraic geometry.
