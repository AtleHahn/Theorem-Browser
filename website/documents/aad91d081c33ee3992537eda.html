Statement

Let G be a connected semisimple real Lie group with finite center and compact Cartan subgroup T contained in a maximal compact subgroup K. Let W_G,W_K be their Weyl groups relative to T. For any fixed infinitesimal character, there are at most |W_G|/|W_K| equivalence classes of limit of discrete series representations with that infinitesimal character.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Limit discrete series, infinitesimal characters, Cartan subgroups and Weyl groups require substantial semisimple representation theory. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Count admissible chamber pairs modulo the compact Weyl group — outline

Use the limit-series classification: representations are parametrized by admissible pairs (v,C) modulo W_K, where v\in L+\rho is singular for the roots of G, C is a Weyl chamber with v in its closure, and the nonvanishing condition excludes orthogonality to the roots of K corresponding to walls of C. The infinitesimal character is the W_G-orbit of v. Here L is the weight lattice of T and \rho the Weyl vector.

These pairs give at most |W_G|/|W_K| representations with a fixed infinitesimal character.
