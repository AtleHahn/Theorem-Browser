Statement

For A\subseteq\mathbb N_{>0} put A(n)=|A\cap\{1,\ldots,n\}| and \sigma(A)=\inf_{n\geq1}A(n)/n. For the positive even integers E and positive odd integers O, \sigma(E)=0 and \sigma(O)=1/2.

Proof 1: Use parity counts and the first missing value — outline

The Schnirelmann density is sensitive to the first values of a set:

{\displaystyle \forall k\ k\notin A\Rightarrow \sigma A\leq 1-1/k}

In particular,

{\displaystyle 1\notin A\Rightarrow \sigma A=0}

and

{\displaystyle 2\notin A\Rightarrow \sigma A\leq {\frac {1}{2}}.}

Consequently, the Schnirelmann densities of the even numbers and the odd numbers, which one might expect to agree, are 0 and 1/2 respectively.
