Statement

Let b\ge2 and n\ge0 be integers satisfying n\equiv4\pmod6. Then (b-1)b^n-1 is composite.

Proof 1: Use the stated polynomial divisor — outline

The exponent of Williams primes of the first kind cannot be congruent to 4 modulo 6, since the corresponding polynomial in b is reducible. If n\equiv 4\pmod6, then (b-1)b^n-1 is divisible by b^2-b+1.
