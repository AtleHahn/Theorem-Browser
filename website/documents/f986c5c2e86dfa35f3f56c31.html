Statement

Let V be a real Hilbert space with norm \|\cdot\|, and let a:V\times V\to\mathbb R be bilinear with |a(v,w)|\le\gamma\|v\|\|w\| and a(v,v)\ge\alpha\|v\|^2 for all v,w\in V, where \gamma,\alpha>0. Let L:V\to\mathbb R be bounded and linear, and let V_h\subseteq V be finite-dimensional. Assume a is symmetric. Let u\in V and u_h\in V_h satisfy a(u,v)=L(v) for all v\in V and a(u_h,v)=L(v) for all v\in V_h. Write \|w\|_a=\sqrt{a(w,w)}. Then \|u-u_h\|\le\sqrt{\gamma/\alpha}\,\|u-v\| for every v\in V_h.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Hilbert means complete inner-product space. Bilinear forms, linear functionals and finite-dimensional subspaces are linear algebra; all continuity/coercivity bounds and the two variational identities are explicitly given. These norm inequalities need no Sobolev or PDE foundations for comprehension.

Proof 1: Energy best approximation and norm comparison

The a-orthogonality of u-u_h and V_h,

a(u-u_h,w)=0,\qquad w\in V_h,

follows directly from V_h\subseteq V and the two variational equations:

a(u,w)=L(w)=a(u_h,w)\qquad\text{for all }w\in V_h.

The bilinear form a is symmetric. This, together with continuity and coercivity, implies that a(\cdot,\cdot) is an inner product on V. The resulting norm

\|v\|_a=\sqrt{a(v,v)}

is called the energy norm. This norm is equivalent to the original norm \|\cdot\|.

Using the a-orthogonality of u-u_h and V_h and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality,

\|u-u_h\|_a^2 =a(u-u_h,u-u_h) =a(u-u_h,u-v) \le\|u-u_h\|_a\cdot\|u-v\|_a \qquad(v\in V_h).

Hence, in the energy norm, the inequality becomes

\|u-u_h\|_a\le\|u-v\|_a\qquad(v\in V_h).

If u=u_h this is immediate; otherwise divide the preceding inequality by \|u-u_h\|_a.

This states that the subspace solution u_h is the best approximation to the full-space solution u in respect to the energy norm. Geometrically, this means that u_h is the projection of the solution u onto the subspace V_h in respect to the inner product a(\cdot,\cdot).

Using this result, one can also derive a sharper estimate in the norm \|\cdot\|. Since

\alpha\|u-u_h\|^2 \le a(u-u_h,u-u_h) =\|u-u_h\|_a^2 \le\|u-v\|_a^2 \le\gamma\|u-v\|^2 \qquad(v\in V_h),

it follows that

\|u-u_h\|\le\sqrt{\frac\gamma\alpha}\,\|u-v\| \qquad\text{for all }v\in V_h.
