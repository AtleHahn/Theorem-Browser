Statement

Let X_k be independent centered real random variables with finite variances, s_n^2=\sum_{k=1}^n\mathbb E X_k^2>0, and S_n=\sum_{k=1}^nX_k. Assume for every \varepsilon>0 that \max_{k\le n}\mathbb P(|X_k|>\varepsilon s_n)\to0. If for some \varepsilon>0 the quantities s_n^{-2}\sum_{k=1}^n\mathbb E[X_k^2\mathbf1_{\{|X_k|>\varepsilon s_n\}}] do not tend to zero, then S_n/s_n does not converge in distribution to a standard normal variable.
