Statement

Every nonabelian simple group G is perfect, meaning [G,G]=G.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Finite groups, normal subgroups and simplicity are introductory algebra. Perfect means equal to the subgroup generated by commutators, one short group-theoretic definition. No classification or character theory is needed to understand these claims.

Proof 1: Normality of the commutator subgroup

The commutator subgroup [G,G] is normal and its quotient is abelian. Simplicity leaves only [G,G]=1 or G; the first would make G abelian, so [G,G]=G.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply normality of the commutator subgroup and exclude the trivial case using noncommutativity. Standard undergraduate abstract algebra suffices.
