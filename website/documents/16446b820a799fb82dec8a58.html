Statement

Let M be the Meissel–Mertens constant. For each fixed integer k\ge0, \sum_{p\le x}1/p-\log\log x-M=o((\log x)^{-k}) as x\to\infty.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves Prime numbers; Logarithms; Asymptotic estimates; Real sequences and series. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Compare the stated exponential error with powers

Use Landau's estimate

\sum_{p\leq x}\frac1p=\log\log x+M+O\!\left(e^{-(\log x)^{1/14}}\right).

In particular the error term is smaller than 1/(\log x)^k for any fixed integer k.

Level justification
