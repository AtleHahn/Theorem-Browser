Statement

Let f:C\to D be a functor between small categories. For each object d of D, let (d\downarrow f) have objects (c,u) with c\in C and u:d\to f(c); a morphism (c,u)\to(c',u') is a morphism v:c\to c' satisfying f(v)\circ u=u'. If B(d\downarrow f) is contractible for every d, then

Bf:BC\longrightarrow BD

is a homotopy equivalence. For a small category E, its classifying space BE is the geometric realization of its nerve: the simplicial set whose n-simplices are strings of n composable morphisms, with faces given by composition or deletion and degeneracies by inserting identities. Realization glues a standard topological simplex for each such string along its faces. Contractible means homotopy equivalent to a point; homotopy equivalence means having an inverse up to continuous homotopy.
