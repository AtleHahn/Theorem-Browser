Statement

On \mathbb R^n, let \mathcal D=C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n) and let K:\mathcal D\to\mathcal D' send v to the regular distribution [v], defined by \langle[v],u\rangle=\int v(x)u(x)\,dx. Its distribution kernel is k(x,y)=\delta(x-y): for test functions u,v, \langle k,u\otimes v\rangle=\langle Kv,u\rangle, where (u\otimes v)(x,y)=u(x)v(y).

Proof 1: Evaluate the diagonal delta on a product test function — outline

Take the diagonal delta distribution, using its standard action \langle\delta(x-y),\Phi(x,y)\rangle=\int\Phi(x,x)\,dx. On the product test function this gives \langle\delta(x-y),u\otimes v\rangle=\int u(x)v(x)\,dx=\langle[v],u\rangle. Thus the prescribed kernel gives the embedding v\mapsto[v].

Level justification

Evaluating the prescribed diagonal delta is short, but the statement concerns the test-function topology, distributions and distribution kernels as maps into D prime. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because those linked functional-analytic objects require more than an isolated integral formula.
