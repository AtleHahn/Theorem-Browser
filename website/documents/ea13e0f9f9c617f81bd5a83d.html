Statement

If positive integers p,q satisfy p^2-2q^2=\pm1, then \left|\sqrt2-\frac pq\right|=\frac1{q^2(\sqrt2+p/q)}.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The Pell relation is an explicit elementary integer equation, not an assumed theory of its solutions.

Proof 1: Rationalize the Pell error

Rationalize the difference to obtain \left|\sqrt2-\frac pq\right|=\frac{|2q^2-p^2|}{q^2(\sqrt2+p/q)}. The assumed Pell relation makes the numerator one.
