Proof 1: A square among two, minus two and minus one modulo an odd prime

Let p be any odd prime. It follows from the multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol that 2, -2 or -1 is a square modulo p.

Here the Legendre symbol of a nonzero residue modulo p is 1 if it is a square and -1 otherwise; its multiplicativity is used without proof.

Level

Level justification

Use the supplied multiplicative square/non-square signs: if both two and minus one are nonsquares, their product minus two has sign one. School modular arithmetic and multiplication of signs suffice; Legendre notation is explicitly defined.
