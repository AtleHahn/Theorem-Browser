Statement

For a smooth closed spin manifold X of dimension divisible by four, \widehat A(X) is an integer, and is even when \dim X\equiv4\pmod8.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Smooth closed spin manifolds; A-hat characteristic genus; Dimension congruences. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Identify the genus with the integral even spin index

This can be deduced from the Atiyah–Singer index theorem: Michael Atiyah and Isadore Singer showed that the Â genus is the index of the Atiyah–Singer operator, which is always integral, and is even in dimensions 4 mod 8. Here the operator is the spin Dirac operator, and its index is the complex kernel dimension minus the cokernel dimension.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the spin Dirac index identity and its parity property. Understanding the spin bundle, characteristic genus and elliptic index requires advanced geometry even though integrality follows immediately.
