Statement

For a finite hypergraph H with nonempty edges, let \mathcal M(H) have pairwise disjoint edge families as faces, and let L(H) join distinct intersecting edges. Then \mathcal M(H) is the independent-set complex \mathcal I(L(H)), and \eta_H(\mathcal M(H))\ge\Psi(L(H)). Here \eta_H denotes reduced homological connectivity plus two, with the same fixed coefficient convention throughout. In Meshulam’s game, CON offers an edge; NON either deletes it or explodes it, deleting its endpoints and all their neighbors. The score is the number of explosions when all vertices disappear, and infinity if isolated vertices remain. CON maximizes and NON minimizes; write \Psi for the value.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Reduced homological connectivity of simplicial complexes adds substantial algebraic-topology prerequisites to the explicitly defined graph game. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Identify the complexes and apply the game bound

Independent sets of L(H) are exactly disjoint edge families of H, giving equality of the complexes. Apply the bound \eta_H(\mathcal I(G))\ge\Psi(G) to G=L(H).
