Statement

Let K\subseteq\mathbb C be a subfield and let \varphi\in\mathbb R. Interpret a pole of tangent, cotangent, secant or cosecant as the symbol \infty. If \tan(\varphi/2)\in K\cup\{\infty\}, then all six values \sin\varphi,\cos\varphi,\tan\varphi,\cot\varphi,\sec\varphi,\csc\varphi belong to K\cup\{\infty\}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Subfields are closed under arithmetic operations, and the six trigonometric functions are familiar calculus. The pole convention is explicitly supplied, so the closure assertion adds no further algebraic structure.

Proof 1: Use closure under the rational half-angle formulas — outline

The circular half-angle formulas express sine, cosine, tangent and their reciprocals as rational functions of t=\tan(\varphi/2) with integer coefficients:

\frac{2t}{1+t^2},\quad\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2},\quad\frac{2t}{1-t^2},\quad\frac{1-t^2}{2t},\quad\frac{1+t^2}{1-t^2},\quad\frac{1+t^2}{2t}.

A subfield is closed under these rational operations whenever the denominator is nonzero. Thus the finite values lie in K, with the pole cases represented by \infty.
