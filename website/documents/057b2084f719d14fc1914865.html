Statement

For a finite explicitly represented graph G=(V,E), a proper three-coloring has a certificate consisting of |V| labels from \{1,2,3\}, verifiable in polynomial time in the graph input size.

Proof 1: Check a color at every vertex and edge — outline

Supply the color of every vertex. Check that each label lies in \{1,2,3\} and that the endpoints of every edge have different labels. These checks establish precisely a proper three-coloring, using one label per vertex and one check per edge.

Level justification
