Statement

Between two parallel planes at distance 2r, a central sphere of radius r>0 admits a closed ring of six spheres of radius r, each tangent to both planes, the central sphere and its two neighbors. The ring can be rotated continuously about the central sphere.

Level

Proof 1: Equal-radius ball-bearing construction — outline

The annular Soddy's hexlet is a special case, in which the three mutually tangent spheres consist of a single sphere of radius r sandwiched between two parallel planes separated by a perpendicular distance 2r. In this case, Soddy's hexlet consists of six spheres of radius r packed like ball bearings around the central sphere and likewise sandwiched.

The chain of six spheres can be rotated about the central sphere without affecting their tangencies, showing that there is an infinite family of solutions for this case. The regular-hexagon distance checks in this ball-bearing construction are implicit in the source.
