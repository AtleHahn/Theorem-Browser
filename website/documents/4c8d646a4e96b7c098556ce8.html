Statement

For f,g∈Q[[x]], write D=d/dx for formal differentiation. In Q[[x]][[y]],

\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{y^n}{n!}D^n\bigl(f^ng(1-yf')\bigr) =g+\sum_{k\geq1}\frac{y^k}{k!}D^{k-1}(f^kg').
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Calculus and power series supply composition, coefficients, differentiation and local inversion. Formal series retain the same coefficient operations without analytic convergence; Lambert W is specified by its inverse equation. These direct definitions suffice for the displayed coefficient identities.

Proof 1: Apply the product rule and cancel successive coefficients

The formal product rule gives

f^ngf'=\frac{(gf^{n+1})'-g'f^{n+1}}{n+1}.

Consequently the left side equals

\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{y^n}{n!}D^n(f^ng)-\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{y^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}D^{n+1}(gf^{n+1})+\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{y^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}D^n(g'f^{n+1}).

Reindex the second sum by k=n+1. It cancels every positive-degree term of the first sum, leaving g. Reindexing the third sum gives the claimed remainder. Every coefficient uses only finitely many contributions, so this is a formal cancellation with no convergence assertion.
