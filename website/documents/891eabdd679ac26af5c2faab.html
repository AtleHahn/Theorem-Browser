Statement

A trigonometric polynomial of degree at most d is a function T(x)=\sum_{j=-d}^d a_je^{ijx} with complex coefficients. Let r\ge1 be an integer and let f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb C be 2\pi-periodic and r times differentiable with |f^{(r)}(x)|\le1 for every x. For every integer n\ge1, there is a trigonometric polynomial T of degree at most n-1 such that \sup_{x\in\mathbb R}|f(x)-T(x)|\le\frac{C_r}{n^r},\qquad C_r=\frac4\pi\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k(r+1)}}{(2k+1)^{r+1}}. This value of C_r is sharp as a constant valid for all such functions and all n.
