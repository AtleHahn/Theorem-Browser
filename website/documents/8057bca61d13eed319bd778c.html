Statement

Let F be a probability distribution on a finite or countable support I, with masses p_i>0, and let H be counting measure on I. Its entropy relative to H, S[F\mid H]=-\int (dF/dH)\log(dF/dH)\,dH, is -\sum_{i\in I}p_i\log p_i, allowing the value +\infty.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

On a countable support, counting measure assigns unit mass to each point and the density is the point-mass function. These concrete definitions extend undergraduate nonnegative series; general Radon–Nikodym existence is not needed.

Proof 1: Choose counting measure in the relative entropy integral — outline

The ordinary discrete entropy expression -\sum_i p_i\log p_i is obtained by choosing H to be counting measure in the relative entropy definition.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

In this concrete countable space the density is the point mass and integration against counting measure is summation. Undergraduate discrete probability and nonnegative series suffice with those two elementary discrete interpretations; no general density-existence theorem is invoked.
