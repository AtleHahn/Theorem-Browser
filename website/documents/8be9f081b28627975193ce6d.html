Statement

Let C_2=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z, let S_3 be the permutation group on three letters, let A_3 be its alternating subgroup, and put G=C_2\times S_3. Write 1 for a trivial subgroup. The subnormal series 1\times1\triangleleft C_2\times1\triangleleft G and 1\times1\triangleleft1\times A_3\triangleleft G are not equivalent, but have equivalent refinements. Here series are equivalent when their successive quotient groups can be paired by isomorphisms.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The displayed definitions of orbits, centralizers, solvability or series equivalence are direct uses or small extensions of these familiar structures; no representation theory is needed for comprehension.
