Statement

Let X be a compact manifold or finite CW complex embedded as a subspace of the realization |K| of a geometric simplicial complex. Suppose a continuous retraction r:|K|\to X is given, meaning r(x)=x for all x\in X. For a continuous f:X\to X, let i:X\hookrightarrow|K| be inclusion and put F=i\circ f\circ r. Then \{y\in|K|:F(y)=y\}=\{x\in X:f(x)=x\}, where the right side is viewed as a subset of |K|.

Proof 1: Compose the map with the given retraction — outline

Use the composite |K|\xrightarrow{r}X\xrightarrow{f}X\xhookrightarrow{i}|K|. With the given retraction, this map has exactly the same fixed points as f in the included subspace X.
