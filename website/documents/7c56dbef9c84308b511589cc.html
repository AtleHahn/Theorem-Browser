Statement

There is an infinite sequence of pairwise inequivalent tame links L_1,L_2,\ldots\subset S^3 such that every complement S^3\setminus L_j is homeomorphic to S^3\setminus W, where W is the Whitehead link.

A link is a finite disjoint union of embedded circles; tame means ambient equivalent to a union of polygonal circles. Two links are equivalent here if a homeomorphism of the ambient three-sphere takes one onto the other, allowing permutation of components and reversal of orientation. The three-sphere may be viewed as \mathbb R^3 with one point at infinity. A homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse.

For an explicit specification, W is the closure of the three-strand braid

\sigma_1^2\sigma_2^2\sigma_1^{-1}\sigma_2^{-2}.

The generator \sigma_i crosses strand i over strand i+1, interchanging their positions; its inverse crosses it under instead. Products concatenate these crossings in the displayed order, with other strands straight. Closure joins each bottom endpoint to the corresponding top endpoint outside the braid in the usual disjoint closing arcs.

Level justification
