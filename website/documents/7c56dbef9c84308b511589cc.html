Statement

There is an infinite sequence of pairwise inequivalent tame links L_1,L_2,\ldots\subset S^3 such that every complement S^3\setminus L_j is homeomorphic to S^3\setminus W, where W is the Whitehead link.

A link is a finite disjoint union of embedded circles; tame means ambient equivalent to a union of polygonal circles. Two links are equivalent here if a homeomorphism of the ambient three-sphere takes one onto the other, allowing permutation of components and reversal of orientation. The three-sphere may be viewed as \mathbb R^3 with one point at infinity. A homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse.

For an explicit specification, W is the closure of the three-strand braid

\sigma_1^2\sigma_2^2\sigma_1^{-1}\sigma_2^{-2}.

The generator \sigma_i crosses strand i over strand i+1, interchanging their positions; its inverse crosses it under instead. Products concatenate these crossings in the displayed order, with other strands straight. Closure joins each bottom endpoint to the corresponding top endpoint outside the braid in the usual disjoint closing arcs.

Proof 1: Disk twists and component determinants (external)

A link to the proof can be found here (Purcell, Hyperbolic Knot Theory, §7.1 Proposition 7.2 proof, pp.128–129, Figures 7.3–7.5. Iltgen §4.1, pp.46–47, displayed Seifert matrix and Alexander polynomial; §4.3 Proposition 4.4.).

Supporting sources: Iltgen, On the Slice Genus of Twist Knots, Section 4.1.

Take the even twist-knot family L_j=J(2,2j)\cup U, j\ge1, with crossing-circle unknot U. The disk twists in Purcell's proof give homeomorphic complements. The knot component has Seifert matrix

A_j=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\0&j\end{pmatrix},\qquad |\det(A_j+A_j^T)|=4j+1.

Thus the unordered component-determinant multiset is \{1,4j+1\}, invariant under mirrors, orientation reversal and component permutation. It distinguishes all these links.

For the explicit braid, put a=\sigma_1, b=\sigma_2, A=a^{-1}, B=b^{-1}. The following word chain uses braid relations and adjacent inverse insertions or cancellations:

\begin{gathered} aabbABB=aabABaB=aBabBaB=aBaaB=AaaBaaB\\ =AaBbaBaaB=AaBAbaaaB=ABAbbaaaB=ABAbAabaaaB\\ =ABAbAbabaaB=ABAbAbbabaB=ABAbAbbbabB=ABAbAbbba. \end{gathered}

The last word is (AB)(AbAbb)(ba), conjugate to AbAbb, the mirror of the usual five-crossing Whitehead braid aBaBB. Mirror ambiguity does not affect the allowed unoriented ambient-homeomorphism convention.
