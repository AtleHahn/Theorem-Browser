Statement

Every set of natural numbers definable by a \Sigma^0_1 formula is computably enumerable. Here a \Sigma^0_1 formula consists of a finite existential prefix followed by a formula with only bounded quantifiers.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The formula shape is explicit; computable enumeration and halting semidecision add a limited algorithmic interpretation to finite quantified arithmetic.

Proof 1: Witness-search construction

For every formula {\displaystyle \varphi (n)} in {\displaystyle \Sigma _{1}^{0}} with k existential quantifiers, we may enumerate the {\displaystyle k}-tuples of natural numbers and run a Turing machine that goes through all of them until it finds the formula is satisfied. This Turing machine halts on precisely the set of natural numbers satisfying {\displaystyle \varphi (n)}, and thus enumerates its corresponding set.

The bounded-quantifier part is decidable by finite search for each tuple.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Enumerate finite witness tuples and decide the bounded part by finite search. Undergraduate discrete mathematics and introductory computability suffice; no recursion theorem or arithmetical-hierarchy machinery is required beyond the supplied formula shape.
