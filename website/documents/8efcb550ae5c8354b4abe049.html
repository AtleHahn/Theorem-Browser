Statement

Let P be an integer-valued polynomial of degree k\ge1 (so P(x)\in\mathbb Z for every integer x), and let \varepsilon>0. For positive integers N put f_N(\alpha)=\sum_{x=1}^N e^{2\pi iP(x)\alpha}\qquad(\alpha\in\mathbb R). For 2\le\lambda\le2^k, let \mu(\lambda) be the piecewise linear function whose successive vertices are (2^\nu,2^\nu-\nu+\varepsilon), \nu=1,\ldots,k (for k=1, only \lambda=2 occurs). There is a constant C_{P,\varepsilon}>0, independent of N and \lambda, such that \int_0^1|f_N(\alpha)|^\lambda\,d\alpha\le C_{P,\varepsilon}N^{\mu(\lambda)}.
