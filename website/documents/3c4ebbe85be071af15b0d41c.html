Statement

Let X be a real or complex Banach space, X^* its continuous dual with the operator norm, and X^{**}=(X^*)^* its bidual. Write B_X=\{x\in X:\|x\|\le1\} and B_{X^{**}}=\{x^{**}\in X^{**}:\|x^{**}\|\le1\}. Under the canonical embedding J:X\to X^{**} defined by J(x)(\varphi)=\varphi(x), the set J(B_X) is weak-star dense in B_{X^{**}}.

Explicitly, for every x^{**}\in B_{X^{**}}, every finite family \varphi_1,\ldots,\varphi_n\in X^* and every \epsilon>0, there is an x\in B_X such that |\varphi_i(x)-x^{**}(\varphi_i)|<\epsilon for all 1\le i\le n. The weak-star topology on X^{**} is the topology of pointwise convergence on X^*.
