Statement

Let X be a complex projective manifold and L a semi-ample invertible sheaf on X with Iitaka dimension at least 2. Then

H^0(X,L^{-1})=H^1(X,L^{-1})=0.

Here an invertible sheaf is a locally free module of rank one over the sheaf \mathcal O_X of holomorphic functions, equivalently the sheaf of holomorphic sections of a line bundle. Semi-ampleness means that some positive tensor power of L is generated by its global sections. The Iitaka dimension is the maximum dimension of the images of the rational maps given by the global sections of positive tensor powers with nonzero sections. The notation L^{-1} denotes the dual line bundle and H^i denotes sheaf cohomology.
