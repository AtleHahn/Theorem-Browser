Statement

For each integer j\ge1, writing i^2=-1 and \zeta(s)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k^{-s},

\frac1{2j+1}=2\sum_{m=1}^j\sum_{r=1}^j\frac{(j!)^2}{(j+1-m)!(j+1-r)!}\frac{(-1)^r}{i^{m+r}}\frac{\zeta(m+r)}{(2\pi)^{m+r}}+\frac1{(j+1)^2}.

Proof 1: Parseval applied to a monomial

For integers j\ge1 and k\in\mathbb Z, let I_{j,k}=\int_0^1\theta^j e^{-2\pi i k\theta}\,d\theta, where i^2=-1. For the recurrence take I_{0,0}=1 and I_{0,k}=0 for k\ne0. Also write f_j(\theta)=\theta^j, \|f_j\|^2=\int_0^1|f_j(\theta)|^2d\theta, and \zeta(s)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k^{-s} for integers s\ge2.

Coefficient calculation

Integration by parts yields the recurrence relation that

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}I_{j,k}&={\begin{cases}{\frac {1}{j+1}},&k=0;\\[4pt]-{\frac {1}{2\pi \imath \cdot k}}+{\frac {j}{2\pi \imath \cdot k}}I_{j-1,k},&k\neq 0\end{cases}}\\[6pt]&={\begin{cases}{\frac {1}{j+1}},&k=0;\\[4pt]-\sum \limits _{m=1}^{j}{\frac {j!}{(j+1-m)!}}\cdot {\frac {1}{(2\pi \imath \cdot k)^{m}}},&k\neq 0.\end{cases}}\end{aligned}}}

Parseval identity

Then by applying Parseval's identity for the exponential orthonormal basis e_k(\theta)=e^{2\pi i k\theta} on (0,1) along with the linearity of the inner product yields that

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\|f_{j}\|^{2}={\frac {1}{2j+1}}&=2\sum _{k\geq 1}I_{j,k}{\bar {I}}_{j,k}+{\frac {1}{(j+1)^{2}}}\\[6pt]&=2\sum _{m=1}^{j}\sum _{r=1}^{j}{\frac {j!^{2}}{(j+1-m)!(j+1-r)!}}{\frac {(-1)^{r}}{\imath ^{m+r}}}{\frac {\zeta (m+r)}{(2\pi )^{m+r}}}+{\frac {1}{(j+1)^{2}}}.\end{aligned}}}

Level justification

Integrate polynomial Fourier coefficients by parts and apply the explicitly stated squared-coefficient Parseval identity to a monomial. Calculus, complex numbers and ordinary series suffice for this concrete application.
