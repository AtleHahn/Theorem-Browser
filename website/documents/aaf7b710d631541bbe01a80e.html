Statement

Let A be a commutative ring, B,A^{\prime} commutative A-algebras, B^{\prime}=B\otimes_A A^{\prime}, and M a B-module. There is a natural isomorphism of A^{\prime}-modules (M\otimes_B B^{\prime})_{A^{\prime}}\cong M_A\otimes_A A^{\prime}, where subscripts denote restriction of scalars.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Tensor products over general rings require abstract algebra.

Commutative algebra

Proof 1: Associativity of tensor products

Here the subscript indicates the forgetful functor, i.e., {\displaystyle M_{A}} is M, but regarded as an A-module. Indeed, such an isomorphism is obtained by observing

{\displaystyle M\otimes _{B}B'=M\otimes _{B}B\otimes _{A}A'\cong M\otimes _{A}A'.}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Associativity is short only after understanding modules over several rings, balanced tensor products, algebra base change and scalar restriction. This cumulative algebra background is not confidently a small extension.
