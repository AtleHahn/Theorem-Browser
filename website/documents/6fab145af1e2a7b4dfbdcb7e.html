Statement

Let n,r be positive integers. On the round sphere S^{n-1}\subseteq\mathbb R^n, suppose r continuous (respectively smooth) tangent vector fields are linearly independent at every point. Then there are r continuous (respectively smooth) tangent fields that are orthonormal at every point. Conversely, pointwise orthonormal fields are pointwise linearly independent. Here a tangent vector at x is perpendicular to x.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves sphere as a Euclidean level set; continuous tangent vector fields; pointwise linear independence and orthonormality. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Pointwise Gram–Schmidt outline

By the Gram–Schmidt process, it is the same to ask for (pointwise) linear independence or fields that give an orthonormal family at each point.

The source invokes the process without writing its pointwise tangency, nonzero-denominator or regularity checks; these remain implicit.

Apply finite Gram-Schmidt pointwise. Tangency is explicitly perpendicularity to x, so projections preserve it; independence keeps denominators nonzero and calculus preserves regularity. Linear algebra and multivariable calculus suffice without general tangent bundles.
