Quadratic character of two for primes congruent to one modulo four

Statement

If a prime p\equiv1\pmod4, then two is a quadratic residue modulo p if and only if p\equiv1\pmod8.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All residue conditions are explicit integer divisibility conditions.

Mathematical Field
High School Mathematics

Proof 1: Intersect the two residue conditions

The supplementary law gives p\equiv1 or 7\pmod8. Combining this with p\equiv1\pmod4 leaves only p\equiv1\pmod8.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Intersect the explicitly stated plus-or-minus-one condition modulo eight with the given condition modulo four. School integer congruences suffice.
