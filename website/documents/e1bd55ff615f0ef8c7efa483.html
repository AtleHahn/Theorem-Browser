Statement

For a contraction T on a Hilbert space H, set D_T=(I-T^*T)^{1/2} and H'=\bigoplus_{j\ge0}H. The operator S(h_0,h_1,\ldots)=(Th_0,D_Th_0,h_1,\ldots) is an isometry with T^n=P_HS^n|_H for n\ge0. Here H is the first coordinate summand, P_H is its orthogonal projection, and n ranges over nonnegative integers.

Proof 1: Shift by one coordinate after the defect component (outline)

For a contraction T (i.e., \|T\|\leq1), its defect operator D_T is defined to be the (unique) positive square root D_T=(I-T^*T)^{1/2}.

Every contraction T on a Hilbert space H has an isometric dilation, with the polynomial calculus property, on

H'=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}H

given by

S=\begin{bmatrix} T&0&0&\cdots\\ D_T&0&0&\cdots\\ 0&I&0&\cdots\\ 0&0&I&\ddots\\ \vdots&&\ddots&\ddots \end{bmatrix}.

Here the calculus property means T^n=P_HS^n|_H for n\geq0, with H the first coordinate summand. The source supplies this construction without expanding the norm and power-compression checks.
