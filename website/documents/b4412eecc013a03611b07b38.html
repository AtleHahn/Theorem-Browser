Statement

If a,b,c\in\mathbb R and b\ne0, then u^4+au^2+bu+c factors as two real monic quadratics. More precisely, the cubic y^3+\tfrac52ay^2+(2a^2-c)y+a^3/2-ac/2-b^2/8=0 has a real root y>-a/2, and W=\sqrt{a+2y}>0 gives factors u^2-Wu+a+y+b/(2W) and u^2+Wu+a+y-b/(2W).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The statement concerns real polynomial factors only.

Proof 1: Choose a positive square parameter by continuity

The cubic function

{\displaystyle C(v)=v^{3}+Pv+Q,}

where P=-a^2/12-c and Q=-a^3/108+ac/3-b^2/8, has the properties that

{\displaystyle C\left({a \over 3}\right)={-b^{2} \over 8}<0} and

{\displaystyle \lim _{v\to \infty }C(v)=\infty ,} where a,b are the quartic coefficients in the statement.

This means that the depressed cubic C(v)=0 has a real root greater than {\displaystyle a \over 3}, and therefore that the cubic in y=v-5a/6 stated above has a real root greater than {\displaystyle -a \over 2}.

Using this root the term {\displaystyle {\sqrt {a+2y}}} is always real, which ensures that the two quadratic factors in the statement have real coefficients.

Put W=\sqrt{a+2y}>0. The cubic gives

(a+2y)((a+y)^2-c)=b^2/4,

so the source's difference of squares is

(u^2+a+y)^2-(Wu-b/(2W))^2=u^4+au^2+bu+c.

Its two factors are u^2-Wu+a+y+b/(2W) and u^2+Wu+a+y-b/(2W).
