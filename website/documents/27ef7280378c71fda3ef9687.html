Statement

Let K be a convex subset of a real Banach space V, and let f:K\to\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\} be Fréchet differentiable at every point, with bounded linear derivatives Df(x):V\to\mathbb R. Differentiability entails that f(x) is finite; on K the derivative satisfies

f(y)-f(x)-Df(x)(y-x)=o(\|y-x\|)\quad(y\to x,\ y\in K).

The following are equivalent:

  1. f((1-t)x+ty)\le(1-t)f(x)+tf(y) for all x,y\in K and 0\le t\le1.
  2. Df(x)(y-x)\le f(y)-f(x) for all x,y\in K.
  3. (Df(x)-Df(y))(x-y)\ge0 for all x,y\in K.

A Banach space is a complete normed vector space.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The complete formulation combines infinite-dimensional differentiability and dual-valued monotonicity; the cumulative extension beyond multivariable calculus is conservatively beyond undergraduate.
