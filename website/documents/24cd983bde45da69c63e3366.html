Statement

Let \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n be a bounded open set of positive measure, 0<s<1, and 1\le p<\infty. For u\in W^{s,p}(\Omega), write u_\Omega=|\Omega|^{-1}\int_\Omega u and [u]_{s,p}^p=\int_\Omega\int_\Omega |u(x)-u(y)|^p/|x-y|^{n+sp}\,dx\,dy. Then \|u-u_\Omega\|_p\le\frac{\operatorname{diam}(\Omega)^{n/p+s}}{|\Omega|^{1/p}}[u]_{s,p}.

Proof 1: Jensen and the diameter bound

Let u_\Omega=\frac1{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega u(x)\,dx. By applying Jensen's inequality, we obtain:

\begin{aligned} \|u-u_\Omega\|_{L^p(\Omega)}^p &=\left\|\frac1{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega(u(y)-u(x))\,dx\right\|_{L^p(dy)}^p\\ &=\int_\Omega\left|\frac1{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega(u(y)-u(x))\,dx\right|^p\,dy\\ &\le\frac1{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega\int_\Omega|u(y)-u(x)|^p\,dx\,dy. \end{aligned}

By exploiting the boundedness of \Omega and further estimates:

\begin{aligned} \frac1{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega\int_\Omega|u(y)-u(x)|^p\,dx\,dy &\le\frac{\operatorname{diam}(\Omega)^{n+sp}}{|\Omega|} \int_\Omega\int_\Omega\frac{|u(y)-u(x)|^p}{|y-x|^{n+sp}}\,dx\,dy. \end{aligned}

Taking pth roots, it follows that the constant C can be taken as

C=\frac{\operatorname{diam}(\Omega)^{n/p+s}}{|\Omega|^{1/p}}.
