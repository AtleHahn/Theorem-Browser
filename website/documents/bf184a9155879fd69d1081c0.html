Statement

Let \mu be a nonnegative Borel measure on \mathbb R with finite absolute moments of every nonnegative integer order, and put m_j=\int_{\mathbb R}x^j\,d\mu(x). If \sum_{n=1}^\infty m_{2n}^{-1/(2n)}=+\infty, then \mu is the unique nonnegative Borel measure on \mathbb R with the moment sequence (m_j)_{j\ge0}. A zero term m_{2n}=0 is interpreted as contributing +\infty in the criterion.

Level justification

Arbitrary measure integration and uniqueness among measures exceed the baseline real-analysis curriculum; elementary-looking series do not remove that requirement.
