Statement

For A=(a_{ij})\in\mathbb C^{n\times n} every eigenvalue belongs to at least one disk \{z:|z-a_{jj}|\le\sum_{i\ne j}|a_{ij}|\}.

Proof 1: Apply the row theorem to the transpose

Apply the row-disk theorem to A^T. A matrix and its transpose have the same characteristic polynomial and hence the same eigenvalues.
