Area of the outer Napoleon triangle

Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate plane triangle with side lengths a,b,c and area Δ. Construct the three equilateral triangles on its sides externally and join their centroids to form the outer Napoleon triangle. Its area is \Delta/2+\sqrt3(a^2+b^2+c^2)/24.

High school only

Proof 1: Substitute the common side length into the equilateral area formula

Analytically, it can be shown that each of the three sides of the outer Napoleon triangle has a length of

s=\sqrt{\frac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{6}+ \frac{\sqrt{(a+b+c)(a+b-c)(a-b+c)(-a+b+c)}}{2\sqrt3}} =\sqrt{\frac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{6}+\frac{2\Delta}{\sqrt3}}.

This side-length formula is used here as a prerequisite; its derivation is not supplied in the source. The area of an equilateral triangle equals the square of the side times \sqrt3/4. Thus the area of the outer Napoleon triangle is

A_{out}=\frac{\Delta}{2}+\frac{\sqrt3}{24}(a^2+b^2+c^2).
