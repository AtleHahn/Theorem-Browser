Statement

Let Z\subseteq\mathbb R^m be nonempty compact. For each z\in Z, let \phi(\cdot,z):\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,\infty] be proper closed convex: it is never -\infty, is finite somewhere, and has a closed convex epigraph \{(x,t)\in\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R:t\ge\phi(x,z)\}. Put f(x)=\sup_{z\in Z}\phi(x,z) and U=\operatorname{int}\{x:f(x)<\infty\}. Suppose U\ne\varnothing and \phi is continuous on U\times Z. For x\in U, put Z_0(x)=\{z:\phi(x,z)=f(x)\}. Then \partial f(x)=\operatorname{conv}\bigcup_{z\in Z_0(x)}\partial\phi(\cdot,z)(x). Here \partial g(x)=\{v\in\mathbb R^n:g(y)\ge g(x)+v\cdot(y-x)\text{ for all }y\in\mathbb R^n\}, and \operatorname{conv} denotes convex hull.
