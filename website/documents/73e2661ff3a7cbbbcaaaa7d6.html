Statement

For each integer m\geq0, define the Legendre polynomial

P_m(x)=\frac{1}{2^m m!}\frac{d^m}{dx^m}(x^2-1)^m,

where the zeroth derivative leaves the polynomial unchanged and 0!=1. For every integer n\geq1 and every real x with -1<x<1,

P_n(x)^2>P_{n-1}(x)P_{n+1}(x).
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

With Rodrigues formula supplied, understanding requires only polynomial differentiation, factorials and quantification over n and x. No orthogonal-polynomial theory or differential equations are prerequisites. Repeated derivatives place the formulation within undergraduate calculus rather than high-school-only mathematics.
