Statement

Let X_0\subseteq X\subseteq X_1 be Banach spaces with continuous inclusion maps. Suppose the inclusion X_0\to X is compact, meaning it maps bounded sets to relatively compact sets. Let 0<T<\infty and 1\le p,q\le\infty. Let W consist of the functions u\in L^p(0,T;X_0) whose distributional time derivative \dot u lies in L^q(0,T;X_1), with norm \|u\|_W=\|u\|_{L^p(0,T;X_0)}+\|\dot u\|_{L^q(0,T;X_1)}. The L^p spaces here are Bochner spaces of strongly measurable Banach-valued functions, using the essential-supremum norm for p=\infty.

If p<\infty, the inclusion W\to L^p(0,T;X) is compact. If p=\infty and q>1, every element of W has a continuous X-valued representative and the inclusion W\to C([0,T];X), with its supremum norm, is compact. Thus a bounded sequence in W has a subsequence converging in the indicated target space. No reflexivity assumption is imposed on X_0 or X_1.
