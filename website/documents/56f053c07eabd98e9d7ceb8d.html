Statement

Let H be any infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space, with no separability assumption. Let GL(H) be the set of invertible bounded complex-linear operators on H, equipped with the operator-norm topology. For every finite CW complex Y and every continuous map f:Y\to GL(H), there exist A_0\in GL(H) and a continuous map

F:Y\times[0,1]\to GL(H),\qquad F(y,0)=f(y),\quad F(y,1)=A_0.

Thus every such f is homotopic to a constant map.

A Hilbert space is an inner-product space complete in its induced norm. The operator norm is \|A\|=\sup_{\|x\|\le1}\|Ax\|, and invertibility here requires a bounded two-sided inverse. A finite CW complex is a space built by attaching finitely many closed balls as cells, identifying each boundary sphere by a continuous attaching map to the cells already attached.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although the norm and homotopy formula are explicit, finite CW attachment, infinite-dimensional operator topology and the full mapping assertion require several additional structures beyond basic point-set topology. Conservative beyond-undergraduate classification.
