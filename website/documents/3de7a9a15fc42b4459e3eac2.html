Statement

For every integer m\ge0, the following polynomial identity in x,y holds (in particular for all complex x,y):

(x+m+1)\sum_{i=0}^m(-1)^i\binom{x+y+i}{m-i}\binom{y+2i}{i}-\sum_{i=0}^m\binom{x+i}{m-i}(-4)^i=(x-m)\binom{x}{m}.

For any complex z and integer k\ge0, define \binom zk=z(z-1)\cdots(z-k+1)/k!, with \binom z0=1.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Finite products and sums explicitly defined; elementary algebra suffices.
