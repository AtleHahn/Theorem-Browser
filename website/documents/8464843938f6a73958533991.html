Statement

Let X be a Hausdorff locally convex topological vector space over \mathbb R or \mathbb C, with continuous linear dual X^*. Let (x_n)_{n\ge1} be a sequence in X. Suppose that for every strictly increasing sequence n_1<n_2<\cdots there is an element s\in X such that

\sum_{k=1}^N f(x_{n_k})\longrightarrow f(s)\quad\text{for every }f\in X^*.

Then every such subseries \sum_k x_{n_k} converges in the original topology of X. Local convexity means that zero has a neighborhood base of convex sets; vector addition and scalar multiplication are continuous. The assumption is convergence in the weak topology \sigma(X,X^*), the weakest topology making every member of X^* continuous. No completeness assumption on X is required.
