Statement

The linear span over \mathbb C of 1,\cos(nx),\sin(nx), n=1,2,\ldots, is dense in L^2([-\pi,\pi]).

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The conclusion concerns approximation in an infinite-dimensional measure-theoretic function space.

Proof 1: Apply Stone–Weierstrass to the trigonometric span — outline

The density of the trigonometric span follows from the Stone–Weierstrass theorem.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The outline combines uniform Stone-Weierstrass approximation with density of continuous functions in Lebesgue L2. The latter space, equivalence classes and norm-density framework require measure-theoretic analysis.
