Statement

A genus-g handlebody is a compact three-manifold homeomorphic to a regular neighborhood of a bouquet of g circles; a Heegaard splitting is a decomposition into two such handlebodies along their common boundary. If a closed oriented three-manifold admits a genus-zero Heegaard splitting, it is homeomorphic to S^3.

Proof 1: A genus-zero Heegaard manifold is a three-sphere — outline

The splitting glues two three-balls along a homeomorphism of their boundary two-spheres. Alexander’s trick extends the boundary homeomorphism over a ball, so this gluing is homeomorphic to the standard two-ball gluing giving S^3.
