Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer and X be the \mathbb R^n-valued Feller Itô diffusion dX_t=b(X_t)\,dt+\sigma(X_t)\,dB_t, where B is standard Brownian motion of the appropriate dimension. Suppose its generator acts on C_c^2(\mathbb R^n) by Af=\sum_i b_i\partial_i f+\tfrac12\sum_{i,j}(\sigma\sigma^\top)_{ij}\partial_i\partial_j f. For X_0=x, a stopping time \tau with \mathbb E^x\tau<\infty, and f\in C_c^2(\mathbb R^n), one has \mathbb E^x f(X_\tau)=f(x)+\mathbb E^x\int_0^\tau[\sum_i b_i(X_s)\partial_i f(X_s)+\tfrac12\sum_{i,j}(\sigma\sigma^\top)_{ij}(X_s)\partial_i\partial_j f(X_s)]\,ds.

Proof 1: Apply the Feller-process formula to the diffusion generator

The diffusion is a Feller process, so Dynkin’s formula applies. On compactly supported C^2 functions its generator is Af=\sum_i b_i\partial_i f+\frac12\sum_{i,j}(\sigma\sigma^\top)_{ij}\partial_i\partial_j f. Substituting this generator gives the stated identity.

Level justification

Substitute the diffusion generator into Dynkin at an integrable stopping time. Ito processes, Feller generators and stopping-time expectations require advanced stochastic analysis.
