Statement

Let X be a real topological vector space, X^* its continuous dual, and f:X\to\mathbb R. Define f^*(u)=\sup_{x\in X}(\langle u,x\rangle-f(x)), allowing +\infty. Then \langle u,v\rangle\leq f^*(u)+f(v) for every u\in X^* and v\in X.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

General dual spaces and convex conjugation on arbitrary real vector spaces are beyond finite-dimensional calculus/linear algebra.

Level justification

The supremum comparison is immediate, but its full setting is an arbitrary topological vector space and its continuous dual. Retain beyond undergraduate provisionally because this general dual-space framework is not confidently a small extension of finite-dimensional linear algebra.
