The positive integers 220 and 284 are amicable: the sum of the positive proper divisors of either equals the other.

Proof 1: Substitute n equals two into the amicable-pair criterion — outline

Use the Thabit criterion: when 3\cdot2^{n-1}-1, 3\cdot2^n-1 and 9\cdot2^{2n-1}-1 are prime (with n>1), the numbers 2^n(3\cdot2^{n-1}-1)(3\cdot2^n-1) and 2^n(9\cdot2^{2n-1}-1) are amicable. For n=2, these primes are 5, 11 and 71. The two products are 4\cdot5\cdot11=220 and 4\cdot71=284, giving the conclusion.
