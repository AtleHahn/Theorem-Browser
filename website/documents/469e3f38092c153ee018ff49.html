Statement

Let a,b be nonzero elements of a division ring with a+b\ne0. Then \bigl(a+ab^{-1}a\bigr)^{-1}+(a+b)^{-1}=a^{-1}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Ring multiplication, units and matrix inverses are introductory algebra. Division rings drop commutativity but retain nonzero inverses; the expressions specify multiplication order and invertibility. No new structural theory is needed.

Proof 1: Inverse-sum form of Hua’s identity — outline

In Hua’s identity replace its second variable by -b^{-1}. This gives a-\bigl(a^{-1}-(a+b)^{-1}\bigr)^{-1}=-ab^{-1}a. Rearranging and taking inverses gives the displayed identity.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Substitute -b inverse in the supplied Hua identity, rearrange and invert. Undergraduate division-ring arithmetic suffices; multiplication order is the only extra care beyond familiar field manipulations.
