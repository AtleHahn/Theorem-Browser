Statement

Two polygonal regions are scissors-congruent if they admit finite dissections into pairwise congruent polygonal pieces. If P and Q are each scissors-congruent to a polygonal region R, then P and Q are scissors-congruent.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Uses elementary plane geometry and finite dissections.

Proof 1: Overlay on a common intermediate region (outline)

Overlay the two dissections on R and cut according to both. This common refinement can be transported back to both P and Q, providing paired congruent pieces and hence a scissors congruence between them.
