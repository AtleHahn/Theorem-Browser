Statement

For a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group G, write \widehat G for its continuous homomorphisms to \mathbb T=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=1\}, with pointwise multiplication and the compact-open topology. Let \mu and \nu be mutually dual Haar measures on G and \widehat G. Writing C_c(G) for the continuous complex-valued functions of compact support, the Fourier transform on C_c(G) has a unique linear isometric extension \mathcal F:L^2_\mu(G)\to L^2_\nu(\widehat G), satisfying \|\mathcal Ff\|_2=\|f\|_2 for all f\in L^2_\mu(G).

Proof 1: Extend by density and completion (outline)

Use the Plancherel identity for continuous functions of compact support: if f:G\to\mathbb C is continuous with compact support, then \widehat f\in L^2_\nu(\widehat G) and

\int_G|f(x)|^2\,d\mu(x)=\int_{\widehat G}|\widehat f(\chi)|^2\,d\nu(\chi), \qquad \widehat f(\chi)=\int_G f(x)\overline{\chi(x)}\,d\mu(x).

In particular, the Fourier transform is an L^2 isometry from the complex-valued continuous functions of compact support on G to the L^2-functions on \widehat G (using the L^2-norm with respect to \mu for functions on G and the L^2-norm with respect to \nu for functions on \widehat G).

Since the complex-valued continuous functions of compact support on G are L^2-dense, there is a unique extension by continuity of the Fourier transform from that space to a linear isometry

\mathcal F:L^2_\mu(G)\to L^2_\nu(\widehat G),

and we have the formula

\forall f\in L^2_\mu(G):\qquad \int_G|f(x)|^2\,d\mu(x)=\int_{\widehat G}|\mathcal Ff(\chi)|^2\,d\nu(\chi).
