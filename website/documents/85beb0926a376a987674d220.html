Statement

Let X be a set with binary operations \circ,\otimes:X\times X\to X and respective two-sided identities e_\circ,e_\otimes. Thus e_\circ\circ a=a=a\circ e_\circ and e_\otimes\otimes a=a=a\otimes e_\otimes for every a\in X. Suppose (a\otimes b)\circ(c\otimes d)=(a\circ c)\otimes(b\circ d) for all a,b,c,d\in X. Then e_\circ=e_\otimes.
