Statement

For real p\ge2, define \gamma_p=\sqrt2\left(\Gamma((p+1)/2)/\sqrt\pi\right)^{1/p}. Then \gamma_p^2=p/e+O(1) as p\to\infty.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Calculus supplies improper integrals, derivatives and asymptotic notation. Gamma is defined by its integral and recurrence, extended away from its listed poles; these formulas and complex differentiability are limited additions sufficient for the stated identity or bound, without special-function theory.

Proof 1: Asymptotic growth of the Gamma moment constant — outline

Apply Stirling's formula \Gamma(z)=\sqrt{2\pi}\,z^{z-1/2}e^{-z}(1+O(1/z)) to z=(p+1)/2 in the displayed definition. The source states the resulting asymptotic \gamma_p^2=p/e+O(1).
