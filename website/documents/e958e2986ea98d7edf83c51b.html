Statement

Let ABC be a nondegenerate triangle in the Euclidean plane. Put a=|BC|, b=|CA| and c=|AB|, and let K be its area. Let \ell be any line tangent to the incircle, the circle inside the triangle tangent to all three sides, and let I be the center of that circle.

Let a',b',c' be the signed perpendicular distances from A,B,C, respectively, to \ell: the sign is positive on the side containing I, negative on the other side, and zero on \ell. Then

aa'+bb'+cc'=2K.

High school only

All geometric objects and the sign convention are explicit and use elementary plane geometry and arithmetic.
