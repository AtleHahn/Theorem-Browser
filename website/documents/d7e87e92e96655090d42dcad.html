Let A,B,C,D be four distinct points in their cyclic order on a circle. Then \angle ABC+\angle ADC=180^\circ, and likewise \angle BAD+\angle BCD=180^\circ.

Proof 1: Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary

Write the opposite inscribed angles as \theta,\phi. Their intercepted arcs make the whole circle, so the inscribed angle theorem gives 2\theta+2\phi=360^\circ, hence \theta+\phi=180^\circ. The same computation applies to the other opposite pair.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The two intercepted arcs partition the full circle. Halving their angle measures gives a total of 180 degrees.
