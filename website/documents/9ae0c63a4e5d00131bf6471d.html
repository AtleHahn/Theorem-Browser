Statement

Every unital field homomorphism h:F\to G between real closed fields preserves their order.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Real closed fields add a finite collection of related ordered-field definitions to undergraduate field algebra; their order and algebraic extension meaning is needed in full, not just the supplied square consequence.

Proof 1: Preserve the square characterization of order

In a real closed field, x\le y if and only if y=x+z^2 for some z. A field homomorphism preserves this equation, giving h(y)=h(x)+h(z)^2 and therefore h(x)\le h(y).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A real closed field must itself be understood, not merely the square-order property used by the proof. It is an ordered field with no proper algebraic ordered extension, equivalently with square roots of positives and roots of odd-degree polynomials. These linked definitions extend undergraduate field algebra; apply h to y=x+z squared.
