Statement

Let X be a uniformly convex Banach space and (x_k) a bounded sequence in X. It has a subsequence (x_{k_j}) and x\in X such that for every y\in X, \limsup_{j\to\infty}(\|x_{k_j}-x\|-\|x_{k_j}-y\|)\le0. This is Delta-convergence in the norm metric.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Uniform convexity and completeness of a general normed space are functional-analysis prerequisites.

Proof 1: Verify the space hypothesis of Delta-compactness

Uniformly convex Banach spaces satisfy the asymptotic-completeness hypothesis of the Delta-compactness theorem. That theorem states that every bounded sequence in an asymptotically complete metric space has a Delta-convergent subsequence. Applying it with the norm metric gives the assertion.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply asymptotic-completeness and Delta-compactness results in uniformly convex Banach spaces. The undeveloped asymptotic-center/completeness framework together with uniform convexity is substantial functional-analysis background.
