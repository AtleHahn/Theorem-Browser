Statement

Let A,B be groups, G=A*B their free product, and H≤G. There exist index sets I,J, subgroups A_i≤A and B_j≤B, elements g_i,f_j∈G, and a subset S⊆H freely generating a free group F(S), such that H is the internal free product

H=F(S)*\Bigl(*_{i\in I}g_iA_ig_i^{-1}\Bigr)*\Bigl(*_{j\in J}f_jB_jf_j^{-1}\Bigr).

Proof 1: Act on the Bass–Serre tree of a single edge

Let G = A∗B and consider G as the fundamental group of a graph of groups Y consisting of a single non-loop edge with the vertex groups A and B and with the trivial edge group. Let T be the Bass–Serre universal covering tree for the graph of groups Y. Since H ≤ G also acts on T, consider the quotient graph of groups Z for the action of H on T. The vertex groups of Z are subgroups of G-stabilizers of vertices of T, that is, they are conjugate in G to subgroups of A and B. The edge groups of Z are trivial since the G-stabilizers of edges of T were trivial. By the fundamental theorem of Bass–Serre theory, H is canonically isomorphic to the fundamental group of the graph of groups Z. Since the edge groups of Z are trivial, it follows that H is equal to the free product of the vertex groups of Z and a free group F(S) isomorphic to the fundamental group (in the standard topological sense) of the underlying graph Z of Z. Under the isomorphism its free generators give a subset S of H, while the vertex groups are the indicated conjugate subgroups. This implies the conclusion of the Kurosh subgroup theorem.
