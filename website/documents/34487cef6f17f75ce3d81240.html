Statement

Let R be a commutative ring with identity, I an ideal, k\ge1, and h\in R[X] have leading coefficient \alpha invertible modulo I. Suppose f,g are monic, \deg f+\deg g=\deg h, h\equiv\alpha fg\pmod {I^k}, and af+bg\equiv1\pmod {I^k}. There exist \delta_f,\delta_g,\delta_a,\delta_b\in I^kR[X], with \deg\delta_f<\deg f and \deg\delta_g<\deg g, such that h\equiv\alpha(f+\delta_f)(g+\delta_g)\pmod {I^{2k}}, (a+\delta_a)(f+\delta_f)+(b+\delta_b)(g+\delta_g)\equiv1\pmod {I^{2k}}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory ring theory supplies ideals, quotient congruences, powers and polynomial degrees. All lifting conditions are explicit finite algebraic identities modulo ideal powers; no adic completion or topology is needed to understand these formulations.

Proof 1: Quadratic Hensel lifting with a lifted Bezout identity — proof

Proof: The factorization assertion follows from one-step linear Hensel lifting (a monic coprime factorization modulo an ideal J lifts to modulo J^2 by degree-preserving corrections in J), applied to the ideal {\displaystyle I^{k}} instead of {\displaystyle I.}

Here \alpha is invertible modulo I^k, since an inverse modulo I lifts to any power by the finite geometric-series identity.

Let {\displaystyle \rho =af+bg-1\in I^{k}R[X].} One has

{\displaystyle a(f+\delta _{f})+b(g+\delta _{g})=1+\Delta ,}

where

{\displaystyle \Delta =\rho +a\delta _{f}+b\delta _{g}\in I^{k}R[X].}

Setting {\displaystyle \delta _{a}=-a\Delta } and {\displaystyle \delta _{b}=-b\Delta ,} one gets

{\displaystyle (a+\delta _{a})(f+\delta _{f})+(b+\delta _{b})(g+\delta _{g})=1-\Delta ^{2}\equiv1\pmod{I^{2k}},}

which proves the second assertion.

Level justification

Apply the explicit one-step lifting result to I^k and correct the Bezout identity by multiplying by 1-Delta. Undergraduate ring algebra and polynomial congruences suffice; ideal powers add no new theory beyond their products.
