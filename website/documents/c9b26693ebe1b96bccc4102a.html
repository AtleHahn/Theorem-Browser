Statement

Let X be a compact convex planar set with nonempty interior. For a finite nonempty family \mathcal S of translates of X, put r(\mathcal S)=\max_{\mathcal I}\operatorname{area}(\bigcup\mathcal I)/\operatorname{area}(\bigcup\mathcal S), where \mathcal I ranges over pairwise disjoint subfamilies of \mathcal S. Define f(X)=\inf_{\mathcal S}r(\mathcal S). If X is a square, f(X)=1/4.

Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Area and convexity are elementary; infimum over all finite configurations is comfortably within undergraduate analysis.
