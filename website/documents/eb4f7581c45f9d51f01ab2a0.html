Statement

If an entire function is not a polynomial, it takes every complex value infinitely often, with at most one exception.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Complex exponentials and complex differentiability extend familiar calculus. Entire means differentiable on all of C, and the value or modulus claim is directly stated without Riemann surfaces or meromorphic continuation.

Proof 1: Apply Great Picard at the essential singularity at infinity

Use the standard classification stated in the source: a nonpolynomial entire function has an essential singularity at infinity. Apply Great Picard there; every neighborhood of infinity then contains infinitely many preimages of each finite value except possibly one.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the explicit classification at infinity and apply Great Picard at the resulting essential singularity. Introductory complex analysis of entire functions and isolated singularities suffices to understand and use these exact consequences.
