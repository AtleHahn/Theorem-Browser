Statement

Let ABCD be a nondegenerate Euclidean tetrahedron with an inscribed sphere tangent to its four faces. For any edge, say AB, let P and Q be the points of tangency on its two incident faces ABC and ABD. Then the triangles ABP and ABQ are congruent, with corresponding vertices A,B,P and A,B,Q. Thus the twelve triangles obtained by joining each face contact point to that face's three vertices form six congruent pairs, one across each edge.
