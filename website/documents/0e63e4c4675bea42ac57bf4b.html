Statement

For every pair of positive integers n,r there is a finite constant K(n,r) such that every real 2\pi-periodic C^r function f with zero Fourier coefficients at frequencies |j|<n satisfies \|f\|_\infty\le K(n,r)\|f^{(r)}\|_\infty. Here \|h\|_\infty=\max_{0\le x\le2\pi}|h(x)| and the Fourier coefficient at j\in\mathbb Z is (2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}f(x)e^{-ijx}\,dx. The least such constant is \sup\{\|f\|_\infty:\|f^{(r)}\|_\infty\le1,\ f\text{ as above}\}.

Level justification

Calculus and introductory Fourier-series definitions suffice; no almost-periodic-function or width theory is needed for this statement.
