Statement

Every subset A\subset\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\} of positive upper density,

\limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac{|A\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|}{N}>0,

contains a nontrivial three-term arithmetic progression: there are integers a\ge1 and d\ge1 such that a,a+d,a+2d\in A.

In the finitary formulation, let r_3(N) be the largest cardinality of a subset of \{1,\ldots,N\} with no such progression. Then r_3(N)=o(N), meaning r_3(N)/N\to0. Equivalently, for every \delta>0 there is N_0 such that for every N\ge N_0, every A\subset\{1,\ldots,N\} with |A|\ge\delta N contains a nontrivial three-term arithmetic progression.
