Statement

For a Euclidean hexagon with six distinct vertices on a nondegenerate conic, if two pairs of opposite side lines are parallel, the third pair is also parallel.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The affine geometric conclusion is elementary, while its proof uses projective geometry.

Mathematical Field
High School Mathematics
Required topics
Euclidean conicsparallel lines
Review notes
none

Proof 1: Points on the line at infinity

The first two opposite-side intersections are points on the line at infinity. Pascal’s theorem puts the third intersection on their common line, which is the line at infinity. Thus the third pair is parallel as well.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Place the first two opposite-side intersections on the line at infinity and apply Pascal. Undergraduate linear algebra with the basic projective completion of the affine plane suffices.
