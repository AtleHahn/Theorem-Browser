Statement

For a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with side lengths a,b,c and area \Delta, a^2+b^2+c^2\ge4\sqrt3\,\Delta, with equality exactly for an equilateral triangle.

Proof 1: Heron’s formula and squares of squared-side differences

This method assumes no knowledge of inequalities except that all squares are nonnegative.

Use Heron’s area formula:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\Delta &{}={\frac {1}{4}}{\sqrt {(a+b+c)(a+b-c)(b+c-a)(c+a-b)}}\\[4pt]&{}={\frac {1}{4}}{\sqrt {2(a^{2}b^{2}+a^{2}c^{2}+b^{2}c^{2})-(a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4})}}.\end{aligned}}}{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}{}&(a^{2}-b^{2})^{2}+(b^{2}-c^{2})^{2}+(c^{2}-a^{2})^{2}\geq 0\\[5pt]{}\iff &2(a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4})-2(a^{2}b^{2}+a^{2}c^{2}+b^{2}c^{2})\geq 0\\[5pt]{}\iff &{\frac {4(a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4})}{3}}\geq {\frac {4(a^{2}b^{2}+a^{2}c^{2}+b^{2}c^{2})}{3}}\\[5pt]{}\iff &{\frac {(a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4})+2(a^{2}b^{2}+a^{2}c^{2}+b^{2}c^{2})}{3}}\geq 2(a^{2}b^{2}+a^{2}c^{2}+b^{2}c^{2})-(a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4})\\[5pt]{}\iff &{\frac {(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2})^{2}}{3}}\geq (4\Delta )^{2},\end{aligned}}}

and the result follows immediately by taking the positive square root of both sides. From the first inequality we can also see that equality occurs only when {\displaystyle a=b=c} and the triangle is equilateral.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Expand squared differences of squared side lengths and substitute Heron. Elementary real algebra and triangle area suffice.

Proof 2: Heron’s formula and arithmetic–geometric mean

Use Heron’s area formula:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\Delta &{}={\frac {1}{4}}{\sqrt {(a+b+c)(a+b-c)(b+c-a)(c+a-b)}}\\[4pt]&{}={\frac {1}{4}}{\sqrt {2(a^{2}b^{2}+a^{2}c^{2}+b^{2}c^{2})-(a^{4}+b^{4}+c^{4})}}.\end{aligned}}}

This proof assumes knowledge of the AM–GM inequality.

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}&&(a-b)^{2}+(b-c)^{2}+(c-a)^{2}&\geq &&0\\\iff &&2a^{2}+2b^{2}+2c^{2}&\geq &&2ab+2bc+2ac\\\iff &&3(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2})&\geq &&(a+b+c)^{2}\\\iff &&a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}&\geq &&{\sqrt {3(a+b+c)\left({\frac {a+b+c}{3}}\right)^{3}}}\\\Rightarrow &&a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}&\geq &&{\sqrt {3(a+b+c)(-a+b+c)(a-b+c)(a+b-c)}}\\\iff &&a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}&\geq &&4{\sqrt {3}}\Delta .\end{aligned}}}

As we have used the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality, equality only occurs when {\displaystyle a=b=c} and the triangle is equilateral.

Proof 3: Cotangents and square completion

Let A,B,C be the opposite angles. Write x=\cot A and q=\cot A+\cot B>0. Since A+B+C=\pi,

S=\cot A+\cot B+\cot C=q+\frac{1-x(q-x)}q.

Thus

qS=q^2-xq+x^2+1=\left(x-\frac q2\right)^2+\left(\frac{q\sqrt3}2-1\right)^2+q\sqrt3\ge q\sqrt3,

so S\ge\sqrt3. But \cot A=(b^2+c^2-a^2)/(4\Delta), and hence S=(a^2+b^2+c^2)/(4\Delta).

Proof 4: Inner Napoleon area formula

The inner Napoleon triangle joins the centroids of the equilateral triangles constructed inward on the three sides.

It can be shown that the area of the inner Napoleon's triangle, which must be nonnegative, is

{\displaystyle {\frac {\sqrt {3}}{24}}(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}-4{\sqrt {3}}\Delta ),}

so the expression in parentheses must be greater than or equal to 0.

The area formula is an external prerequisite. The equality characterization is supplied by Proof 1.

Proof 5: Equilateral specialization of Pedoe’s inequality

Use Pedoe's inequality: if a,b,c and A,B,C are the side lengths of triangles with areas \Delta and F, then

A^2(b^2+c^2-a^2)+B^2(a^2+c^2-b^2)+C^2(a^2+b^2-c^2)\geq16F\Delta,

with equality if and only if the two triangles are similar with corresponding sides (A,a),(B,b),(C,c).

Pedoe's inequality is a generalization of Weitzenböck's inequality, which is the case in which one of the triangles is equilateral. The source leaves the specialization of the formula implicit.
