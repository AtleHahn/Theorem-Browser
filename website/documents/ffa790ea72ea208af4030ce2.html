Statement

In the Euclidean plane let \Gamma be a circle and let C_0,C_1,C_2,C_3,C_4 be circles whose centers lie on \Gamma. Indices are read modulo 5. Suppose consecutive circles meet at two distinct points

C_i\cap C_{i+1}=\{A_i,B_i\},\qquad A_i\in\Gamma.

Form the lines \ell_i=B_{i-1}B_i. Whenever the indicated construction is nondegenerate, the five pentagram vertices

T_i=\ell_{i-1}\cap\ell_{i+1}\qquad(i=0,1,2,3,4)

satisfy T_i\in C_i.

Nondegenerate here means that the five second intersection points B_i are distinct, and each displayed pair of lines has a unique finite intersection. Thus every line and vertex in the assertion is defined. The notation UV denotes the full straight line through distinct points U,V. The sixth circle \Gamma contains both the five centers and the five selected chain intersection points A_i.
