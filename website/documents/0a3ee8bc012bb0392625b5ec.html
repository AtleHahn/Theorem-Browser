Statement

Let X be a normal complex algebraic variety and let x be a closed point of X. In the complex analytic topology, x has arbitrarily small open neighborhoods U such that the set of nonsingular points of U is connected. Equivalently, every analytic open neighborhood of x contains an analytic open neighborhood U of x with this connectedness property.

A complex algebraic variety here is integral and of finite type over \mathbb C. Normal means that its local rings are integrally closed domains. A nonsingular point is one at which the local ring is regular, that is, its Krull dimension equals the minimal number of generators of its maximal ideal. The analytic topology is the usual complex topology, rather than the Zariski topology.
