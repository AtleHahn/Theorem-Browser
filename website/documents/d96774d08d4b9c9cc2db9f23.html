Statement

Let X be a finite-dimensional Hausdorff topological vector space over \mathbb R or \mathbb C, and let K\subseteq X be compact. Then its convex hull \operatorname{conv}K is closed, and hence equals its closed convex hull.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Finite-dimensional vector spaces and Hausdorff topology are baseline concepts. A topological vector space requires continuity of addition and scalar multiplication; this single compatibility definition suffices for the finite-dimensional continuity or compact-convex-hull assertion.

Proof 1: Use compactness of the convex hull — outline

The stated compact-convex-hull theorem says that \operatorname{conv}K is compact. In a Hausdorff space a compact set is closed. Thus the convex hull equals the closed convex hull.
