Statement

For a Levi-Civita connection, write \nabla_{\partial_\mu}\partial_\sigma=\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\sigma}\partial_\rho and R^\rho{}_{\sigma\mu\nu}=dx^\rho(R(\partial_\mu,\partial_\nu)\partial_\sigma), with R(X,Y)=[\nabla_X,\nabla_Y]-\nabla_{[X,Y]}. Then R^\rho{}_{\sigma\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\Gamma^\rho{}_{\nu\sigma}-\partial_\nu\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\sigma}+\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda{}_{\nu\sigma}-\Gamma^\rho{}_{\nu\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda{}_{\mu\sigma}, summing repeated indices.

Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Proof 1: Expand the coordinate connection commutator — outline

Convert the curvature expression R(\partial_\mu,\partial_\nu)\partial_\sigma to coordinates using the Christoffel symbols for the connection; the coordinate fields commute. This gives the stated derivative and quadratic terms.
