Statement

In a category with zero morphisms, let q:Y\to Q and q':Y\to Q' be cokernels of f:X\to Y. Here a cokernel is universal among maps a:Y\to A with af=0. There is a unique isomorphism u:Q\to Q' satisfying q'=uq.

Proof 1: Specialize universal-object uniqueness

As with all universal constructions the cokernel, if it exists, is unique up to a unique isomorphism, or more precisely: if q:Y\to Q and q':Y\to Q' are two cokernels of f:X\to Y, then there exists a unique isomorphism u:Q\to Q' with q'=uq.

Level justification
