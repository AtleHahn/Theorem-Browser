Statement

Every contraction T (a bounded linear operator with \|T\|\le1) on a Hilbert space H has a unitary dilation: there are a Hilbert space K containing H and a unitary operator U on K such that T^n=P_HU^n|_H for every integer n\ge0, where P_H is orthogonal projection onto H.

Proof 1: Block-operator construction (outline)

For a contraction T (i.e. \|T\|\le1), its defect operator D_T is defined to be the unique positive square root

D_T=(I-T^*T)^{1/2}.

In the special case that S is an isometry on a Hilbert space H', D_{S^*} is a projector and D_S=0; hence the following is a Sz.-Nagy unitary dilation of S on H'\oplus H':

U=\begin{bmatrix}S&D_{S^*}\\D_S&-S^*\end{bmatrix}.

It has the required power-compression property S^n=Q_{H'}U^n|_{H'} for n\ge0, where Q_{H'} projects onto the first summand.

Returning to the general case of a contraction T, every contraction T on a Hilbert space H has an isometric dilation, again with the power-compression property, on

H'=\bigoplus_{j\ge0}H,

given by

S=\begin{bmatrix} T&0&0&\cdots\\ D_T&0&0&\cdots\\ 0&I&0&\cdots\\ 0&0&I&\ddots\\ \vdots&&\ddots&\ddots \end{bmatrix}.

Here H is embedded as the first coordinate summand. Substituting the S thus constructed into the preceding unitary dilation for an isometry, one obtains a unitary dilation for the contraction T:

T^n=P_HS^n|_H =P_H(Q_{H'}U|_{H'})^n|_H =P_HU^n|_H.

The source leaves the norm, block-unitarity and power-compression checks implicit.
