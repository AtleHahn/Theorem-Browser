Statement

On the Riemann sphere let P=\infty and let \ell(nP) be the dimension of meromorphic functions with no poles away from P and a pole of order at most n at P. For integers n\ge0, \ell(nP)=n+1.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement prescribes meromorphic poles at infinity on a complex-analytic surface.

Proof 1: Substitute the sphere canonical divisor — outline

The sphere has genus zero and canonical divisor -2P. Riemann–Roch therefore gives the sequence \ell(nP)=1,2,3,\ldots.

Level

Level justification

Apply Riemann-Roch with the canonical divisor and negative-degree vanishing. Divisors, meromorphic sections and the Riemann-Roch dimension formula require substantial Riemann-surface theory.
