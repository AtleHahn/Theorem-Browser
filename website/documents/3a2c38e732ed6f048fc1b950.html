Statement

If a right G-action on X satisfies xe=x and (xg)h=x(gh), then g*x=xg^{-1} is a left G-action.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Group actions, opposite multiplication, ring homomorphisms and subgroup complements are direct introductory algebra concepts. The action or complement conditions are explicitly given, without additional structural theory.

Proof 1: Convert a right action into a left action

The source composes the right action with group inversion, using (gh)^{-1}=h^{-1}g^{-1}. Thus g*(h*x)=(xh^{-1})g^{-1}=x(gh)^{-1}=(gh)*x, and the identity acts trivially.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the inverse product rule and right-action associativity to check the two left-action axioms. Introductory group theory suffices.
