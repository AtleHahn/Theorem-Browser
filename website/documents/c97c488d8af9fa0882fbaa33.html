Statement

Let k be any field, let \mathbb P^1_k=\operatorname{Proj}k[x_0,x_1], and let E be an algebraic vector bundle of rank r on \mathbb P^1_k, equivalently a locally free sheaf of \mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_k}-modules of rank r. There are integers a_1,\ldots,a_r, unique up to permutation, such that E\cong\bigoplus_{j=1}^r\mathcal O(a_j). Here \mathcal O(1) is the hyperplane line bundle, the dual of the tautological bundle \mathcal O(-1); \mathcal O(a) is its ath tensor power, interpreted by dual powers for a<0 and the trivial bundle for a=0.
