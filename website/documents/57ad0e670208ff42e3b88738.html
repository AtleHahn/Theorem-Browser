Statement

All function spaces below are complex-valued and use Lebesgue measure on \mathbb R. For p=1,2, L^p consists of measurable functions modulo equality almost everywhere with \|f\|_p=(\int_{\mathbb R}|f(x)|^p\,dx)^{1/p}<\infty. A span means finite complex linear combinations.

For f\in L^1(\mathbb R) put \widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-ix\xi}\,dx and f_a(x)=f(x+a). Then

\overline{\operatorname{span}\{f_a:a\in\mathbb R\}}^{\|\cdot\|_1}=L^1(\mathbb R) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \widehat f(\xi)\ne0\text{ for every real }\xi.

An equivalent Tauberian formulation is: if this nonvanishing condition holds, h\in L^\infty(\mathbb R) is essentially bounded, and A\in\mathbb C satisfies

\lim_{x\to+\infty}(f*h)(x)=A\int_{\mathbb R}f(t)\,dt,

then, for every g\in L^1(\mathbb R),

\lim_{x\to+\infty}(g*h)(x)=A\int_{\mathbb R}g(t)\,dt.

Here (g*h)(x)=\int_{\mathbb R}g(t)h(x-t)\,dt.
