Statement

Let a<b and f\in L^1([a,b]). Its Fourier coefficients c_k=\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)e^{-2\pi ik(x-a)/(b-a)}\,dx, k\in\mathbb Z, satisfy c_k\to0 as k\to\pm\infty.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Integrability is Lebesgue L1; the complex Fourier coefficient integral is defined in the statement.

Mathematical Field
Harmonic analysis on Euclidean spaces

Proof 1: Zero extension and real-line Fourier decay (outline)

Extend f by zero outside [a,b], obtaining an integrable function on \mathbb R. Apply the real-line Riemann–Lebesgue lemma: the Fourier transform of an L^1(\mathbb R) function tends to zero at infinite frequency. This gives the assertion for the Fourier coefficients.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Extend the L1 function by zero and apply Fourier-transform decay at the discrete frequencies. Lebesgue integrability and the real-line Fourier transform require advanced analysis beyond ordinary numerical Fourier integrals.
