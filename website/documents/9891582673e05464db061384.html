Statement

Let \gamma:\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z\to\mathbb R^2, L>0, be a C^2 simple closed curve parametrized by arc length, so |\gamma'(s)|=1. Suppose its curvature satisfies |\gamma''(s)|\le1 for every s. Then the bounded region enclosed by the curve, together with its boundary, contains a closed Euclidean disk of radius 1. Simple means that \gamma is injective on the parameter circle. The enclosed region is the bounded component of \mathbb R^2\setminus\gamma(\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z).
