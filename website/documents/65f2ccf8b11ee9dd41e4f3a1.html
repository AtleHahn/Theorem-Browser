Statement

Let f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C be a nonconstant entire function, meaning that it is holomorphic on the whole complex plane. Write

M_f(r)=\sup_{|z|\leq r}|f(z)|,\qquad \rho=\limsup_{r\to\infty}\frac{\log\log M_f(r)}{\log r},

and assume its order \rho is finite.

A finite asymptotic value of f is a number a\in\mathbb C for which there exists a continuous curve \gamma:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb C with |\gamma(t)|\to\infty and f(\gamma(t))\to a as t\to\infty. The number of distinct finite asymptotic values of f is at most 2\rho.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic complex analysis together with supremum/limsup and curve limits suffices; growth order and asymptotic value are explicitly defined. These related additions fit the conservative undergraduate allowance without value-distribution theory.
