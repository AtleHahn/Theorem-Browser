Statement

Let V\subset\mathbb R^3 be a bounded region with piecewise smooth boundary S, with outward unit normal n. All fields below are C^1 on an open neighborhood of \overline V. For a scalar g and vector field F, \int_V(F\cdot\nabla g+g\operatorname{div}F)\,dV=\int_SgF\cdot n\,dS. Equivalently, \int_V F\cdot\nabla g=\int_SgF\cdot n-\int_Vg\operatorname{div}F.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves C¹ scalar and vector fields; Volume and surface integrals; Outward normals; Gradient and divergence. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Integration by parts for a scalar and a vector field

Apply the divergence theorem to gF and use \operatorname{div}(gF)=F\cdot\nabla g+g\operatorname{div}F. Rearranging gives the integration-by-parts form.

Level justification

Apply the standard three-dimensional divergence theorem to gF, expand by the product rule and rearrange. Ordinary multivariable calculus supplies all objects and hypotheses.
