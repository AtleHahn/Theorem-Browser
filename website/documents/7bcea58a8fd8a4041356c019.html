Statement

Identify \mathbb R^3 with the purely imaginary quaternions. The conjugation action of the unit quaternions, z*x=zx\bar z, is not faithful.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Unit quaternions form a concrete multiplication group, and faithfulness means distinct group elements induce distinct maps; these are a short extension of introductory algebra.

Proof 1: Unit-quaternion conjugation is not faithful

This is not a faithful action because the quaternion -1 leaves all points where they were, as does the quaternion 1.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The central scalars one and minus one both act identically by the displayed conjugation. Introductory group actions plus concrete quaternion multiplication as a small extension suffice; no Lie-group theory is used.
