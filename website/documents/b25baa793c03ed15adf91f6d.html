Statement

Let G be a finite group, let O(G) be its largest normal subgroup of odd order, and let T be a Sylow 2-subgroup of G, so |T| is the largest power of 2 dividing |G|. Write

Z^*(G)=\{x\in G:xO(G)\in Z(G/O(G))\},

where Z(H) denotes the center of a group H, consisting of the elements that commute with every element of H.

For any t\in T, the condition t\notin Z^*(G) is equivalent to the existence of an abelian subgroup U\leq T and an element g\in G with the following properties. Put

C_T(U)=\{x\in T:xu=ux\text{ for every }u\in U\},\qquad N=N_G(U)\cap N_G(C_T(U)),

where N_G(B)=\{x\in G:xBx^{-1}=B\} is the normalizer of B. Then:

  1. g\in N.
  2. t\in U and tg\ne gt.
  3. U=\langle hth^{-1}:h\in N\rangle, the subgroup generated by these conjugates.
  4. The exponent of U equals the order of t. Here the exponent is the least positive integer e such that u^e=1 for every u\in U, and the order of t is the least positive integer d such that t^d=1.

When t\notin Z^*(G) and t\in Z(T), the witnesses may be chosen with g of prime-power order. When t\notin Z^*(G) and t\notin Z(T), they may be chosen with g\in T.
