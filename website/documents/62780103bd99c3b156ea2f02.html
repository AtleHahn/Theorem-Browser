Statement

Let U\subseteq\mathbb R^3 be open and simply connected, and let F be smooth with \nabla\times F=0. Then every piecewise smooth loop c in U has \int_cF\cdot dc=0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Multivariable calculus supplies curl and line integrals. Simply connected means every loop can be continuously contracted within the domain, one geometric-topological extension sufficient for the circulation claim.

Proof 1: Smoothing a contraction — outline

Simple connectedness supplies a continuous contraction of the loop to its base point, keeping that base point fixed. Whitney’s approximation theorem, in its relative form for the prescribed boundary data, supplies a piecewise smooth contraction with the same boundary conditions. Apply the zero-circulation theorem for such a contraction.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the supplied relative smoothing consequence for a Euclidean loop contraction and then the stated curl-free circulation theorem. Undergraduate multivariable calculus and elementary homotopy of loops suffice to apply these particular consequences; no smooth-manifold approximation theory is developed.
