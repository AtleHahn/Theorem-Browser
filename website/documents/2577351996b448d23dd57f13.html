Statement

Work over a field of characteristic zero. For integers m,n\ge3, the point blowup of x^2=y^m z^n has charts with equations x^2=y^{m+n-2}z^n and x^2=y^m z^{m+n-2}. Thus one exponent strictly increases in each chart while multiplicity stays two.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Point blowups, their charts and strict transforms require substantial algebraic geometry despite the explicit exponents. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Compare the exponents in the two transforms — outline

For the singularity x^2=y^mz^n, which is singular along x=yz=0, blowing up the singularity at the origin produces the singularities

x^2=y^{m+n-2}z^n \qquad\text{and}\qquad x^2=y^mz^{m+n-2}.

The exponents increase if m and n are both at least 3, while the x^2 term still gives multiplicity two at the origin in each displayed chart.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Interpret the two chart equations as strict transforms and compare multiplicities. Blowups, saturation and singularity multiplicity require graduate algebraic geometry beyond exponent arithmetic.
