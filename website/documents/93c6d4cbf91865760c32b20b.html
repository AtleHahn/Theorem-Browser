Statement

Let n\ge1 be an integer. Let A,B be positive-semidefinite Hermitian n\times n complex matrices, with eigenvalues a_1\ge\cdots\ge a_n\ge0 and b_1\ge\cdots\ge b_n\ge0. Then \operatorname{Tr}(AB)\le\sum_{i=1}^n a_i b_i.

Level justification

Understanding the statement involves complex Hermitian matrices; positive semidefiniteness; sorted eigenvalues; trace. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. Proof 1: Specialize von Neumann’s singular-value bound — outline

Use von Neumann’s trace inequality: for complex matrices X,Y with decreasing singular values s_i(X),s_i(Y), |\operatorname{Tr}(XY)|\le\sum_i s_i(X)s_i(Y). For positive-semidefinite Hermitian matrices the singular values are exactly the eigenvalues. Applying this to A,B, and using that their product has real trace, gives \operatorname{Tr}(AB)\le|\operatorname{Tr}(AB)|\le\sum_i a_i b_i.
