Statement

Let integers n,m,d,e satisfy n^2+m^2=de, \gcd(n,d,e)=1, and -n\le0\le2m\le d\le e. Then the quadruple (a,b,c,f)=(-n,d+n,e+n,d+e+n-2m) satisfies (a+b+c+f)^2=2(a^2+b^2+c^2+f^2).
