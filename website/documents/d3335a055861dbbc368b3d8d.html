Statement

For each finite group G other than the cyclic groups of orders 3,4,5, there is a finite graph H with \operatorname{Aut}(H)\cong G and at most 2|G| vertices.

Level

Level justification

Proof 1: Two-orbit graph realizations have at most twice the group order — outline

Use the stated two-orbit realization theorem: outside the three exceptions, G is the full symmetry group of a graph whose vertices have two orbits. Consequently the number of vertices is at most twice the order of G.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the given two-orbit realization and bound each orbit by group order. Introductory finite groups and actions suffice to apply the existence theorem.
