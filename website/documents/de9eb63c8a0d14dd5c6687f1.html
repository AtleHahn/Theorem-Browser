Statement

Let R be a commutative ring and n\in R. The set \{x^2+ny^2:x,y\in R\} is closed under multiplication.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Inner-product identities and polynomial/ring arithmetic are undergraduate material. The quadratic field expression or finite quotient is concrete, and the displayed relation defines algebraic dependence. No general field norm or Galois theory is involved.

Proof 1: Read the new coordinates in the product identity outline

Let a,b,c,d\in R. The generalized identity (a^2+nb^2)(c^2+nd^2)=(ac-nbd)^2+n(ad+bc)^2, verified by expanding each side, has the same form with new coordinates ac-nbd and ad+bc.
