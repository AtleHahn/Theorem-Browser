Statement

Let T be a rooted tree with finitely many immediate successors at each node and with every maximal branch infinite. Nodes have finite levels, their distances from the root; write T(j) for level j. The ancestor order is denoted by \leq.

For h a positive integer or h=\omega (infinitely many levels indexed by the nonnegative integers), a strongly embedded subtree S of height h is a rooted subset of T with the induced ancestor order and with every maximal branch of length h, satisfying these conditions:

  • There is a strictly increasing sequence of ambient levels (\ell_j)_{j<h} such that S(j)\subseteq T(\ell_j).
  • If s is not terminal in S, then for each immediate successor t of s in T, exactly one immediate successor u of s in S satisfies t\leq u.

The root of S need not be the root of T. Write \mathbb S_T^n for the family of its strongly embedded subtrees of height n (with n levels).

For every pair of positive integers n,r and every coloring of \mathbb S_T^n with r colors, there is a strongly embedded subtree R\subseteq T of height \omega such that all members of \mathbb S_R^n have the same color.
