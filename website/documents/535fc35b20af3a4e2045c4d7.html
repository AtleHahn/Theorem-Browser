Statement

If M is a closed oriented (2k+1)-manifold, its middle torsion homology T_k=\operatorname{torsion}H_k(M;\mathbb Z) carries a perfect linking pairing T_k\otimes T_k\to\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Torsion homology and its perfect linking pairing require substantial manifold algebraic topology. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Specialize the torsion dimension shift

The torsion pairing couples dimensions i and n-i-1. Put n=2k+1 and i=k; then n-i-1=k, so the pairing is a perfect bilinear form on T_k.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Substitute n=2k+1 and i=k into the torsion linking duality. Torsion homology, linking pairings and manifold duality require graduate algebraic topology.
