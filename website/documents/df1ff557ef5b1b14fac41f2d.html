Statement

For positive real numbers p,q, q-p-p\ln(q/p)\ge0, with equality if and only if p=q.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Only positive real numbers, natural logarithms and elementary algebra are needed to understand the scalar statement.

Proof 1: Substitute the positive ratio in the logarithmic bound

The source uses \ln x\le x-1 for x>0, with equality exactly at x=1. Its substitution x=q/p and multiplication by p>0 give p\ln(q/p)\le q-p, the displayed scalar inequality. Equality is equivalent to q/p=1.

Level

Level justification

Substitute q/p in the supplied logarithm inequality and multiply by positive p; its equality condition gives p=q. School logarithms and inequalities suffice for this application.
