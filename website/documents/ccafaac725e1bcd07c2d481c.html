Statement

On smooth real functions on an open subset of \mathbb R^n, any two differential operators formed as finite constant real linear combinations of compositions of coordinate partial derivatives commute.

Proof 1: Commuting generators (outline)

In terms of composition of the differential operator D_i which takes the partial derivative with respect to x_i, symmetry of mixed partial derivatives gives

D_i\circ D_j=D_j\circ D_i.

From this relation it follows that the ring of differential operators with constant coefficients, generated by the D_i, is commutative; but this is only true as operators over a domain of sufficiently differentiable functions. Smooth functions are a valid domain.
