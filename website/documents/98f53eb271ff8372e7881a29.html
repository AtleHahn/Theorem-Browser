Statement

Let a group G act by isometries on a nonempty proper length space (X,d). A length space has distance equal to the infimum of lengths of curves joining the two points, and proper means that closed balls are compact. Suppose the action is properly discontinuous and cocompact: for every compact K\subseteq X the set \{g\in G:gK\cap K\ne\varnothing\} is finite, and the quotient X/G is compact.

Then G is finitely generated. For every finite generating set S and every p\in X, the orbit map

(G,d_S)\longrightarrow X,\qquad g\longmapsto gp

is a quasi-isometry. Here d_S(g,h) is the shortest word length of g^{-1}h in S\cup S^{-1}. Quasi-isometry means that there are constants L\ge1, C\ge0 such that

L^{-1}d_S(g,h)-C\le d(gp,hp)\le Ld_S(g,h)+C

for all g,h\in G, and every point of X is within distance C of the orbit.
