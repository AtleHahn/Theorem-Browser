Statement

Let U\subseteq\mathbb C be open, let f:U\to\mathbb C be holomorphic, and suppose the closed disk \overline D=\{z:|z-z_0|\le r\}, with r>0, lies in U. Let \gamma be its counterclockwise boundary circle. For every a in the open disk,

f(a)=\frac1{2\pi i}\oint_\gamma\frac{f(z)}{z-a}\,dz.
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Multivariable calculus supplies parametrized line integrals, and real analysis supplies derivatives. A complex contour integral is the integral of f(gamma(t))gamma-prime(t); holomorphic means complex differentiable. For the explicitly given circles or smooth curves, these are closely related extensions of familiar calculus, with no residue or Laurent-series machinery needed.

Proof 1: Shrink the contour and estimate the continuous remainder — outline

By using the Cauchy integral theorem, one can show that the integral over \gamma is equal to the same integral taken over an arbitrarily small counterclockwise circle C_\varepsilon contained in the disk and centered at ⁠{\displaystyle a}⁠. Since {\displaystyle f(z)} is continuous, we can choose a circle small enough on which {\displaystyle f(z)} is arbitrarily close to ⁠{\displaystyle f(a)}⁠. On the other hand, the integral

{\displaystyle \oint _{C_\varepsilon}{\frac {1}{z-a}}\,dz=2\pi i,}

over any circle {\displaystyle C_\varepsilon} centered at ⁠{\displaystyle a}⁠. This can be calculated directly via a parametrization (integration by substitution) {\displaystyle z(t)=a+\varepsilon e^{it}} where {\displaystyle 0\leq t\leq 2\pi } and {\displaystyle \varepsilon } is the radius of the circle.

Letting {\displaystyle \varepsilon \to 0} gives the desired estimate

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\left|{\frac {1}{2\pi i}}\oint _{\gamma}{\frac {f(z)}{z-a}}\,dz-f(a)\right|&=\left|{\frac {1}{2\pi i}}\oint _{C_\varepsilon}{\frac {f(z)-f(a)}{z-a}}\,dz\right|\\[1ex]&=\left|{\frac {1}{2\pi i}}\int _{0}^{2\pi }\left({\frac {f{\bigl (}z(t){\bigr )}-f(a)}{\varepsilon e^{it}}}\cdot \varepsilon e^{it}i\right)\,dt\right|\\[1ex]&\leq {\frac {1}{2\pi }}\int _{0}^{2\pi }{\frac {\left|f{\bigl (}z(t){\bigr )}-f(a)\right|}{\varepsilon }}\,\varepsilon \,dt\\[1ex]&\leq \max _{|z-a|=\varepsilon }\left|f(z)-f(a)\right|~~{\xrightarrow[{\varepsilon \to 0}]{}}~~0.\end{aligned}}}
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The limiting estimate is ordinary continuity, but first deforming a complex contour through a punctured holomorphic domain uses Cauchy’s theorem. The full contour and holomorphic framework exceeds the baseline.
