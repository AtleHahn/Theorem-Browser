Statement

Let n\ge1 and u\in C_c^1(\mathbb R^n,\mathbb R). For each i=1,\ldots,n, \int_{\mathbb R^n}\partial_i u\,dx=0.

Proof 1: A compactly supported partial derivative has zero integral

Integrate first in the ith variable. By the fundamental theorem of calculus and compact support, \int_{\mathbb R^n}\partial_i u\,dx=\int_{\mathbb R^{n-1}}\left(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\partial_i u\,dx_i\right)dx'=0.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Integrate first in the differentiated coordinate, where compact support makes both endpoint values zero. Iterated integration for continuous compactly supported functions is standard multivariable calculus.
