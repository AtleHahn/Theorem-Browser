Statement

A first-order formula \varphi(\bar x) is preserved under embeddings between all structures for its language if and only if it is logically equivalent to an existential formula \exists y_1\cdots\exists y_m\,\psi(\bar x,\bar y) with quantifier-free \psi. Preserved means that for any embedding e:A\to B and tuple \bar a from A, A\models\varphi(\bar a) implies B\models\varphi(e\bar a). An embedding is an injective map preserving constants and functions and both preserving and reflecting relations. Logical equivalence means equal truth for all structures and all assignments.
