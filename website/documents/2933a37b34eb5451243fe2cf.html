Statement

For a continuous complex-valued function f on \mathbb R and real a<0<b, \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\int_a^b\left(\frac{f(x)}{x-i\varepsilon}-\frac{f(x)}{x+i\varepsilon}\right)\,dx=2\pi i f(0).

Proof 1: Cancel to the Cauchy approximate identity

The difference of the kernels is 2i\varepsilon/(x^2+\varepsilon^2). Use the Cauchy approximate-identity property: \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\int_a^b\frac{\varepsilon}{\pi(x^2+\varepsilon^2)}f(x)\,dx=f(0). Multiplying by 2\pi i yields the jump formula.
