Statement

Independently draw with replacement from n\geq1 coupon types, each with probability 1/n, and let T be the number of draws until every type has appeared. Here n is an integer. Write H_m=\sum_{j=1}^m1/j, H_m^{(r)}=\sum_{j=1}^m j^{-r} and H_0=0. For every real c>0, \Pr(T\geq cnH_n)\leq1/c.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The conclusion concerns a finite-threshold event in repeated uniform drawing and an elementary numerical bound.

Proof 1: Apply Markov at the expected-time scale

Markov’s inequality for the nonnegative time T gives \Pr(T\geq cnH_n)\leq\mathbb ET/(cnH_n)=1/c, using \mathbb ET=nH_n.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Apply the supplied Markov inequality to the nonnegative discrete collection time and insert its mean. Basic discrete expectation suffices.
