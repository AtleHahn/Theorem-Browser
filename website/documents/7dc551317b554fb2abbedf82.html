Statement

Let (M,\omega) be a smooth symplectic manifold of dimension 2n, n\ge1, and let H:M\to\mathbb R be smooth. For p\in M, if dH(p)\ne0, there is a symplectic coordinate chart (f_1,\ldots,f_n,g_1,\ldots,g_n) near p with f_1=H and \omega=\sum_{i=1}^n df_i\wedge dg_i.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves smooth symplectic manifolds; differential of a function; symplectic coordinate charts. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: One-function specialization — outline

Apply the Carathéodory–Jacobi–Lie theorem with one prescribed function, f_1=H. The theorem extends smooth functions with independent differentials and pairwise zero Poisson brackets to a symplectic chart. Here dH(p)\ne0 supplies the one-function independence condition, and \{H,H\}=0. The resulting chart includes H as a coordinate.

Level justification

Apply the one-function Carathéodory–Jacobi–Lie theorem using the nonzero differential and vanishing self-bracket. Symplectic forms, Poisson brackets and adapted smooth charts require substantial differential geometry.
