Statement

Let R be a commutative ring, \mathfrak m a maximal ideal, and \widehat R=\varprojlim_n R/\mathfrak m^n. Let h\in R[X] have leading coefficient \alpha\notin\mathfrak m. A factorization h\equiv\alpha fg\pmod{\mathfrak m} into monic coprime polynomials lifts uniquely, with the same degrees and reductions, to h=\alpha FG in \widehat R[X]. Likewise every simple root of h\bmod\mathfrak m has a unique lift to a root in \widehat R.

Proof 1: Hensel lifting to the adic completion — outline

Finite Hensel lifting gives unique factors modulo every \mathfrak m^n. Uniqueness makes these factorizations compatible under reduction. Their coefficient sequences therefore define factors in the inverse limit \widehat R, and the factorization identities hold there. Uniqueness holds coefficientwise at every finite quotient and hence in the inverse limit. The simple-root case is the factorization case with the monic factor X-r.
