Statement

Let v:M\to\mathbb R^3 be a smooth immersion of a torus M (a surface diffeomorphic to S^1\times S^1), with its induced Riemannian metric and area element dA. Let \kappa_1,\kappa_2 be its principal curvatures and set H=(\kappa_1+\kappa_2)/2, K=\kappa_1\kappa_2. With the source’s energy convention

W(M)=\int_M H^2\,dA-\int_M K\,dA,

one has W(M)\ge2\pi^2. An immersion has injective differential at every point; it may have self-intersections. The principal curvatures are the eigenvalues of the shape operator.
