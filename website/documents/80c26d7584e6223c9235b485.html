Statement

For probability measures P_n,Q_n on the same measurable space, D_{\rm KL}(P_n\Vert Q_n)\to0 implies \delta(P_n,Q_n)\to0, where \delta=\sup_A|P_n(A)-Q_n(A)| and D_{\rm KL} is the natural-logarithm relative entropy. Explicitly, D_{\rm KL}(P\Vert Q)=\int\log(dP/dQ)\,dP for P\ll Q, and +\infty otherwise; zero-mass terms are zero.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The general statement and expectation proof require probability measures and Radon–Nikodym derivatives.

Proof 1: Apply the Pinsker bound

Pinsker’s inequality gives 0\le\delta(P_n,Q_n)\le\sqrt{D_{\rm KL}(P_n\Vert Q_n)/2}. Its right side tends to zero, giving the claimed convergence.

Level justification

Apply Pinsker and squeeze, but in the full measurable-space setting the relative entropy is defined through Radon–Nikodym densities and integration. Those measure-theoretic prerequisites remain advanced despite the elementary limiting step.
