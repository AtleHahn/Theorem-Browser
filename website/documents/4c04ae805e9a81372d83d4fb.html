Statement

Let K_1,K_2\subset S^3 be tame knots, where S^3 is the three-sphere, a knot is an embedded circle, and tame means ambient isotopic to a polygonal knot. If S^3\setminus K_1 and S^3\setminus K_2 are homeomorphic, then there is a homeomorphism S^3\to S^3 carrying K_1 onto K_2. If the complements are homeomorphic by an orientation-preserving map, the knots are ambient isotopic. Ambient isotopy means a continuous family of ambient homeomorphisms starting from the identity.
