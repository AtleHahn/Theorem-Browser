Statement

Let \mathcal I be the groupoid with two objects and exactly one morphism between each ordered pair of objects. Identifying its two objects in the category of groupoids gives the one-object groupoid whose automorphism group is infinite cyclic.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The conclusion is a categorical quotient of a groupoid, beyond ordinary sets or finite-dimensional linear algebra.

Proof 1: Categorical identification recipe — outline

The algebraic recipe identifies the two vertices of \mathcal I in the category of groupoids and obtains the group of integers. In terms of the standard presentation of the interval groupoid, its invertible arrow becomes a single loop generator under this categorical identification; the resulting group is the free group on one generator, \mathbb Z.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use categorical identification of objects in a presented groupoid to turn the invertible arrow into a free loop. The categorical quotient and groupoid presentation are not confidently a small extension of group theory; retain beyond undergraduate conservatively.
