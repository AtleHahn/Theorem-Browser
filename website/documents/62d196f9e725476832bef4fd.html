Statement

Let \zeta(s) be the meromorphic continuation of \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s} from \operatorname{Re}s>1. Let N(T) be its number of zeros, counted with multiplicity, whose imaginary parts lie in (0,T]. For every real T\ge e,

\left|N(T)-\left(\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi}-\frac{T}{2\pi}\right)\right|\le 0.1038\log T+0.2573\log\log T+9.3675.

All logarithms are natural.
