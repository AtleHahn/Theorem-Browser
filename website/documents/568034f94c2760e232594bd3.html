Statement

For a positive integer n, let \varphi(n) count the integers 1\le k\le n with \gcd(k,n)=1. For every positive integer m, \varphi(2m)=2\varphi(m) if m is even, and \varphi(2m)=\varphi(m) if m is odd.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The two cases compare finite integer counts.

Proof 1: Specialize the gcd-corrected product formula at the factor two — outline

The general formula \varphi(mn)=\varphi(m)\varphi(n)d/\varphi(d), where d=\gcd(m,n), specializes at one factor equal to 2 to \varphi(2m)=2\varphi(m) for even m, and \varphi(2m)=\varphi(m) for odd m.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Insert the factor two in the given product formula and distinguish gcd equal to one or two. Elementary arithmetic suffices.
