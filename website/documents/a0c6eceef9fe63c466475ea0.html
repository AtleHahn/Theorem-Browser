Statement

For every integer n>1, there exists a full-rank lattice \Lambda\subseteq\mathbb R^n whose packing by equal Euclidean balls centered at lattice points has density at least

\frac{\zeta(n)}{2^{n-1}},\qquad \zeta(n)=\sum_{j=1}^\infty j^{-n}.

A full-rank lattice is A\mathbb Z^n for an invertible real matrix A, with covolume |\det A|. For the largest admissible ball radius r=\tfrac12\min_{0\ne v\in\Lambda}\|v\|_2, density is the volume of the radius-r ball divided by the covolume; the interiors of these translated balls are disjoint.
