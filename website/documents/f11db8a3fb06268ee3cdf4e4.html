Statement

A real closed field admits exactly one compatible field ordering: its positive elements are precisely the squares of nonzero elements.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Compatible orders and real closed fields add linked ordered-field definitions to undergraduate abstract algebra. The assertion is about the unique positive cone, not model theory.

Proof 1: Identify the positive cone with nonzero squares

In any ordered field a nonzero square is positive, while in a real closed field every positive element has a square root. Thus the positive cone is exactly the set of nonzero squares and determines the ordering uniquely.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding real closed includes its algebraic-extension or positive-square/odd-degree-root characterization. This is a limited extension of undergraduate field algebra. Every compatible order makes nonzero squares positive, and the supplied real-closed property forces the same positive cone in every order.
