Statement

For an integer n\ge1, form F_n from n disjoint triangles by identifying one vertex from each triangle to a single vertex v, with no other identifications or added edges.

For every integer q\ge0, the number of proper vertex colorings of F_n with q labeled colors is q(q-1)^n(q-2)^n.

Level
High school only
Level justification

The graph and its construction are explicitly finite; only elementary counting is used.

Proof 1: Common-vertex triangle construction (outline)

The source deduces the formula from the chromatic polynomial of a triangle and the common-vertex construction. There are q choices at the common vertex; for each attached triangle the remaining two vertices have (q-1)(q-2) ordered choices. These choices for different triangles are independent once the common color is fixed, giving q(q-1)^n(q-2)^n. For q=0,1,2 no proper coloring exists, in agreement with the formula.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Choose the common color and independently choose two ordered distinct remaining colors on each triangle. Handle zero, one and two colors directly. This is elementary product counting.
