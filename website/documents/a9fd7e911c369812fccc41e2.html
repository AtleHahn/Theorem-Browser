Statement

For integers k\ge0 and n\ge k+2, suspension gives an isomorphism \pi_{n+k}(S^n)\cong\pi_{n+k+1}(S^{n+1}). These groups therefore stabilize as n increases and represent the kth stable homotopy group of spheres.

Proof 1: Insert the connectivity of a sphere into Freudenthal’s range

The sphere S^n is (n-1)-connected. Freudenthal suspension is an isomorphism in degree n+k when n+k\le2(n-1), equivalently n\ge k+2. This inequality remains true on increasing n, so all subsequent suspension maps are isomorphisms.

Level justification

Insert sphere connectivity into Freudenthal and iterate suspension isomorphisms. Homotopy groups, suspension and stable homotopy remain advanced prerequisites even though the range inequality is elementary.
