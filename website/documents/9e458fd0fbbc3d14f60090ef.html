Proof 1: Compare quantifier-free closed sentences — outline

Quantifier elimination reduces a sentence to a Boolean combination of comparisons between closed arithmetic terms. Those terms and comparisons have the same truth value in every ordered field. Thus every real closed field agrees on the original sentence.

Level justification

Pass from quantifier elimination to agreement of all closed sentences in every real closed field. First-order theories, semantic completeness and real closed fields form a combined prerequisite burden not developed locally; retain beyond undergraduate provisionally.
