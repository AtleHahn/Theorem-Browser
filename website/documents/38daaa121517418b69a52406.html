Statement

Let p be prime and A a countable abelian p-group, meaning every element has order a power of p. Suppose every nonzero a\in A has finite p-height: there is an integer h\ge0 such that a\in p^hA but a\notin p^{h+1}A, where p^hA=\{p^h b:b\in A\}. Then A is isomorphic to a direct sum of finite cyclic groups of p-power order. The direct sum consists of tuples in the cyclic groups with only finitely many nonzero coordinates, with coordinatewise addition. Countable includes finite groups.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Finite height is an explicit solvability condition for integer multiples; with finite-support direct sums this is a realistically small extension of introductory group theory.
