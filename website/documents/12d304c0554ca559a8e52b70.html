Statement

Let M be a closed connected nonorientable 3-manifold, where closed means compact without boundary. Put

N=(S^2\times[0,1])/((x,1)\sim(-x,0)),

where S^2=\{x\in\mathbb R^3:|x|=1\}. Thus N is the nonorientable 2-sphere bundle over the circle, obtained as the mapping torus of the antipodal map.

There is a finite link in N such that Dehn surgery along that link produces a manifold homeomorphic to M. Here a link is a finite disjoint collection of smoothly embedded circles with disjoint tubular neighborhoods homeomorphic to S^1\times D^2, where D^2=\{y\in\mathbb R^2:|y|\leq1\}. Dehn surgery removes the interiors of these solid-torus neighborhoods and glues solid tori back to their boundary tori by chosen boundary homeomorphisms. No restriction to surgery coefficients +1 or -1 is asserted in this nonorientable formulation.
