Statement

Let K\subseteq\mathbb R^2 be compact with connected complement \mathbb R^2\setminus K. For every continuous f:\partial K\to\mathbb R and every \varepsilon>0, there exists a polynomial P(x,y) with real coefficients such that

\frac{\partial^2P}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2P}{\partial y^2}=0,\qquad |f(x,y)-P(x,y)|<\varepsilon\quad\text{for all }(x,y)\in\partial K.

Here \partial K is the topological boundary, and the differential equation defines a harmonic polynomial.
