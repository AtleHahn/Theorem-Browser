Statement

Let M,\widetilde M be Riemannian manifolds, and let \gamma:[0,T]\to M, \widetilde\gamma:[0,T]\to\widetilde M, T>0, be unit-speed geodesics. Suppose no point \widetilde\gamma(t), 0<t\le T, is conjugate to \widetilde\gamma(0) along this segment. Let J,\widetilde J be normal Jacobi fields along the respective geodesics with

J(0)=\widetilde J(0)=0,\qquad |D_tJ(0)|=|\widetilde D_t\widetilde J(0)|.

Suppose for each t and every pair of tangent 2-planes \Pi\subset T_{\gamma(t)}M, \widetilde\Pi\subset T_{\widetilde\gamma(t)}\widetilde M containing the respective velocity vectors,

K_M(\Pi)\le K_{\widetilde M}(\widetilde\Pi).

Then |J(t)|\ge|\widetilde J(t)| for all t\in[0,T]. Here D_t is covariant differentiation for the metric Levi-Civita connection; a geodesic has D_t\dot\gamma=0. A Jacobi field satisfies D_t^2J+R(J,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma=0, where R is the curvature tensor, and normal means perpendicular to \dot\gamma. Conjugacy along the segment means existence of a nonzero Jacobi field vanishing at both endpoint parameters. Sectional curvature of an orthonormal pair v,w is \langle R(w,v)v,w\rangle.
