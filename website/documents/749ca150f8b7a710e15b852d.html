Statement

Let A be a Noetherian local G-ring, and let \widehat A be its completion at the maximal ideal. Then \widehat A is a direct limit of smooth A-algebras.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

G-rings, completion and smooth algebra approximations require substantial local algebra. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Apply regularity of the completion map

The G-ring condition gives that A\to\widehat A is a regular homomorphism. The completion is Noetherian. Apply the stated Popescu theorem: a regular map of Noetherian rings expresses its target as a direct limit of smooth algebras over its source.
