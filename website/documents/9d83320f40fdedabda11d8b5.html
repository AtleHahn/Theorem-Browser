Statement

Let t_0\in\mathbb R, \tau\ge0, and \alpha>\beta>0. Let v:[t_0-\tau,\infty)\to(0,\infty) be differentiable for t\ge t_0 (with the right derivative at t_0). Suppose v^{\prime}(t)\le-\alpha v(t)+\beta\sup_{s\in[t-\tau,t]}v(s)\qquad(t\ge t_0). Then there exist constants k>0 and \eta>0 such that v(t)\le k e^{-\eta(t-t_0)}\qquad(t\ge t_0).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate real analysis and differential equations suffice; no delay-equation theory is needed to understand the inequality.
