Statement

Every second-countable Hausdorff topological manifold is metrizable. Here a manifold is locally homeomorphic to Euclidean space; second-countable means having a countable base of open sets.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic topology supplies Hausdorff spaces, countable bases, covers and metrizability. A topological manifold here adds only locally Euclidean neighborhoods, explicitly defined; no smooth structure or tangent bundles occur.

Proof 1: Apply Urysohn to manifold regularity

Use that a Hausdorff locally Euclidean space is regular, together with Urysohn’s metrization theorem: a Hausdorff, regular, second-countable space is metrizable. The manifold meets these three conditions, so the theorem applies.
