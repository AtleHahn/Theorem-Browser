Statement

Let N\ge1 be an integer and let a,b be complex N-periodic sequences. Define c[n]=\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}a[m]b[n-m] and \widehat a[k]=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}a[n]e^{-2\pi ikn/N}, with analogous definitions for b,c. Then \widehat c[k]=\widehat a[k]\widehat b[k] for every integer k, and c[n]=N^{-1}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\widehat a[k]\widehat b[k]e^{2\pi ikn/N} for every integer n.

Proof 1: Finite double-sum rearrangement and inverse DFT

A time-domain derivation proceeds as follows:

{\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}\widehat c[k]&\triangleq \sum _{n=0}^{N-1}\left(\sum _{m=0}^{N-1}a[m]\cdot b[n-m]\right)e^{-i2\pi kn/N}\\&=\sum _{m=0}^{N-1}a[m]\left(\sum _{n=0}^{N-1}b[n-m]\cdot e^{-i2\pi kn/N}\right)\\&=\sum _{m=0}^{N-1}a[m]\cdot e^{-i2\pi km/N}\underbrace {\left(\sum _{n=0}^{N-1}b[n-m]\cdot e^{-i2\pi k(n-m)/N}\right)} _{\widehat b[k]\quad \scriptstyle {\text{due to periodicity}}}\\&=\underbrace {\left(\sum _{m=0}^{N-1}a[m]\cdot e^{-i2\pi km/N}\right)} _{\widehat a[k]}\left(\widehat b[k]\right).\end{aligned}}}

And therefore, by the finite inverse DFT formula,

c[n]=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\widehat c[k]e^{2\pi ikn/N} =\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\widehat a[k]\widehat b[k]e^{2\pi ikn/N}.
