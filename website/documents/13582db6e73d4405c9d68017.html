Statement

Let D be an elliptic differential operator between smooth complex vector bundles E,F on a compact oriented smooth manifold X without boundary, of real dimension n. Let \operatorname{ch}(D) denote the standard rational cohomology class obtained from its symbol class by the Chern character and inverse Thom isomorphism, and let \operatorname{Td}(X) be the Todd class of the complexified tangent bundle. Then the topological index

(-1)^n\langle\operatorname{ch}(D)\operatorname{Td}(X),[X]\rangle

is an integer. Here [X] is the fundamental class and the pairing evaluates the component of cohomological degree n. The standard symbol-class construction of \operatorname{ch}(D) is assumed.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Elliptic symbol classes, Chern characters, Thom isomorphisms and Todd classes require substantial index theory and algebraic topology. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Equality with the analytical index

The analytical index is \dim\ker D-\dim\ker D^*, a difference of finite integers. The Atiyah–Singer index theorem states that this equals the topological index.

Although the analytical index is usually hard to evaluate directly, it is at least obviously an integer. The topological index is by definition a rational number, but it is usually not at all obvious from the definition that it is also integral. So the Atiyah–Singer index theorem implies some deep integrality properties, as it implies that the topological index is integral.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Although the final step is a difference of integers, applying the index theorem requires elliptic operators on bundles and the symbol, Chern, Thom and Todd constructions in cohomology. These are substantial advanced prerequisites.
