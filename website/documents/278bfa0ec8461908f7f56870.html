Statement

Let C be a smooth connected projective curve of genus g\ge2 over an algebraically closed field, with canonical line bundle \omega_C. For a positive integer m, the Hilbert polynomial of \omega_C^{\otimes m} is H(t)=m(2g-2)t+1-g. In particular for m=3 it is (6t-1)(g-1).

Proof 1: Substitute tensor-power degrees

Write h^j(C,L)=\dim_kH^j(C,L) and \chi(L)=h^0(C,L)-h^1(C,L). The canonical sheaf \omega_C has degree 2g-2, which gives an ample line bundle for genus g\geq2. If we set \omega_C(n)=\omega_C^{\otimes n} then the Riemann–Roch formula reads

\begin{aligned} \chi(\omega_C(n)) &=\deg(\omega_C^{\otimes n})-g+1\\ &=n(2g-2)-g+1\\ &=2ng-2n-g+1\\ &=(2n-1)(g-1). \end{aligned}

This gives the degree 1 Hilbert polynomial of \omega_C,

H_{\omega_C}(t)=2(g-1)t-g+1.

For \omega_C^{\otimes m}, substitute n=mt, giving m(2g-2)t+1-g. In particular, the tri-canonical polynomial is

\begin{aligned} H_{\omega_C^{\otimes3}}(t)&=(6t-1)(g-1)\\ &=6(g-1)t+(1-g). \end{aligned}
