Statement

Let R\to S be a homomorphism of unital rings, let M be a left S-module and N a left R-module, and let {}_RM denote restriction of scalars. For every integer n\ge0:

  • If S is projective as a right R-module, then
\operatorname{Ext}_R^n(N,{}_RM) \cong\operatorname{Ext}_S^n(S\otimes_RN,M).
  • If S is projective as a left R-module, then
\operatorname{Ext}_R^n({}_RM,N) \cong\operatorname{Ext}_S^n(M,\operatorname{Hom}_R(S,N)).

The tensor product carries the left S-action on its first factor; the left S-action on \operatorname{Hom}_R(S,N) is (s\cdot h)(x)=h(xs). Here \operatorname{Ext}^n denotes the usual derived extension group for left modules.
