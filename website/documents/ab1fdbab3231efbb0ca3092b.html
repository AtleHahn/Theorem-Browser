Statement

Let G be a finite group, p a prime, and P a Sylow p-subgroup (a subgroup of order the largest power of p dividing |G|). Write [H,H] for the subgroup generated by x^{-1}y^{-1}xy with x,y\in H, x^g=g^{-1}xg, and N_G(Q)=\{g\in G:g^{-1}Qg=Q\}. Put N=N_G(P). Then P\cap[G,G] is generated by P\cap[N,N] together with all subgroups P\cap[Q,Q] as Q ranges over the Sylow p-subgroups of G.
