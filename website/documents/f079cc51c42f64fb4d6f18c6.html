Statement

Let G=(V,E) be a finite graph with n vertices. A partition V=A\sqcup S\sqcup B with no A–B edges and |A|,|B|\le2n/3 gives edge-disjoint subgraphs G_1,G_2 whose edge sets cover E, whose vertex sets cover V, whose intersection is S, and each of which has at least n/3 vertices. Conversely every such pair of subgraphs yields a separator S=V(G_1)\cap V(G_2) with the stated bounds on the exclusive vertex sets.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All subgraph overlap, edge coverage and size bounds are finite set/graph conditions.

Proof 1: Vertex separators and overlapping subgraph separations — source argument

Given the partition, take V(G_1)=A\cup S and V(G_2)=B\cup S. Assign every edge meeting A to G_1, every edge meeting B to G_2, and distribute edges within S arbitrarily. No edge assignment conflicts because there are no A–B edges. Also |V(G_1)|=n-|B|\ge n/3, and likewise for G_2. Conversely, take the intersection as S and the two set differences as A,B. An edge from A to B could belong to neither subgraph, contradicting edge coverage. The lower bound on each subgraph’s vertex count gives the upper bound on the opposite exclusive set.
