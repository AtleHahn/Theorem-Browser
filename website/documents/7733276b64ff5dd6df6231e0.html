Statement

For a>0, b\in\mathbb R, C\in\mathbb C, let f(x)=Ce^{-\pi a(x-b)^2} and \widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-2\pi ix\xi}\,dx. Then \int|f|^2=\int|\widehat f|^2.

Proof 1: Plancherel identity for translated and rescaled Gaussians (outline)

A standard Gaussian h(x)=e^{-\pi x^2} is transformed to itself under the Fourier transformation, and the theorem is trivial in that case. Finally, the standard transformation properties of the Fourier transform (translation, scaling, and scalar multiplication) then imply Plancherel for all Gaussians of the stated form. The source leaves this reduction as an outline.
