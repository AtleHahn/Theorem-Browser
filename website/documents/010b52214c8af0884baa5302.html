Statement

Let C be a nonempty closed convex subset of a Banach space V. If f:C\to C is continuous and its image f(C) is compact, then there is x\in C with f(x)=x.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A Banach space is a complete normed vector space; completeness, compactness and convexity fit undergraduate analysis and topology.

Level justification

Linear algebra, metric-space compactness and finite convex combinations suffice. Brouwer’s theorem follows from Sperner’s finite labeling argument and a convergent subsequence; finite approximations and completeness then give the Banach-space conclusion.
