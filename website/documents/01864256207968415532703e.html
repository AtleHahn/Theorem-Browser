Statement

Let S be a smooth regular surface patch in \mathbb R^3, with a smooth choice of unit normal near p\in S. At x\in S, let k(x)\ge m(x) be the greatest and least signed curvatures of the normal sections, the plane curves obtained by intersecting the surface with planes containing the normal at x. Signs are positive for bending toward the chosen normal. The Gaussian curvature is K(x)=k(x)m(x). If k has a local maximum at p, m has a local minimum at p, and k(p)>m(p), then K(p)\le0.

Level justification

Multivariable calculus and elementary curve curvature suffice with the related normal-section definitions supplied.

Level justification

Multivariable calculus and linear algebra suffice. Principal directions are eigenvectors of the shape operator; their local coordinates come from smooth ordinary differential equations and the inverse function theorem. The Gauss–Codazzi identities follow by differentiating the coordinate vectors, and the conclusion uses the second-derivative test.
