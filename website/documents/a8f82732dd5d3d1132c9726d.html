Statement

Let n\ge1 and equip the cube Q=[0,1]^n with a smooth Riemannian metric g (a smoothly varying positive-definite quadratic form on tangent vectors). Let d_g be the intrinsic distance obtained by taking infima of g-lengths of curves in Q. For 1\le i\le n, put

d_i=\inf\{d_g(x,y):x,y\in Q,\ x_i=0,\ y_i=1\}.

Then \prod_{i=1}^n d_i\le\operatorname{vol}_g(Q), where in cube coordinates \operatorname{vol}_g(Q)=\int_Q\sqrt{\det(g_{ij}(x))}\,dx.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The variable metric, induced path distance and invariant volume must be understood together, exceeding the conservative short undergraduate extension.
