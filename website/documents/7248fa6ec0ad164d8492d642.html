Statement

For any commutative ring R and a,b,c,d,n\in R, (a^2+nb^2)(c^2+nd^2)=(ac+nbd)^2+n(ad-bc)^2=(ac-nbd)^2+n(ad+bc)^2.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The full statement is an explicit polynomial identity in a commutative ring. Introductory abstract algebra supplies that generality; no further concepts or specialist research are needed.

Proof 1: Set h equal to one in the two-parameter identity outline

Use the two-parameter identity

{\displaystyle (ha^{2}+kb^{2})(hc^{2}+kd^{2})=(hac\pm kbd)^{2}+hk(ad\mp bc)^{2}.}

Set h=1 and k=n. This is the stated special case.
