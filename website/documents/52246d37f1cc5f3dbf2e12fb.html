Statement

Let g be a nondegenerate pseudo-Riemannian metric of class C^k, for an integer k\ge1, on a smooth manifold. In every smooth chart its Levi-Civita connection coefficients are of class C^{k-1}.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Smooth manifolds; Pseudo-Riemannian metrics; Affine connections; Lie brackets of vector fields. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Read the derivative order from the Christoffel formula — outline

In local coordinates use the source Christoffel formula \Gamma^k_{ij}=\tfrac12g^{kl}(\partial_ig_{jl}+\partial_jg_{il}-\partial_lg_{ij}),

where g_{ij} are the metric coefficients, (g^{ij})=(g_{ij})^{-1}, and repeated indices are summed. The inverse matrix coefficients are C^k where the metric is nondegenerate, while the first derivatives of g_{ij} are C^{k-1}. The formula therefore gives C^{k-1} coefficients.
