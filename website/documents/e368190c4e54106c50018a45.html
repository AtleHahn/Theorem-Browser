Statement

Let a,p\in\mathbb Z and b,q\in\mathbb Z_{>0}. If a/b\ne p/q, then |a/b-p/q|\ge1/(bq).

Level
High school only
Level justification

The bound concerns two explicitly given fractions and a nonzero integer numerator.

Proof 1: Bound the nonzero cross product

One has \left|\frac ab-\frac pq\right|=\frac{|aq-bp|}{bq}\ge\frac1{bq}, because |aq-bp| is a positive integer and therefore is at least 1.
