Statement

Let X be a based finite CW complex, let \Sigma^{8}X denote its 8-fold reduced suspension, and let \widetilde{KO}(X) denote its reduced topological real K-theory classes. There is a natural bijection of these classes

\widetilde{KO}(\Sigma^{8}X)\longleftrightarrow \widetilde{KO}(X).
Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Based finite CW complexes; Reduced suspensions; Reduced topological real K-theory classes. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Suspension–loop adjunction (outline)

For the infinite orthogonal group, the space BO is the classifying space for stable real vector bundles. Bott periodicity states that

\Omega^{8}BO\simeq\mathbb Z\times BO.

This yields the consequence that KO-theory is an 8-fold periodic theory.

For the stated reduced classes on based finite CW complexes, the prerequisites for this consequence are their standard representation by E=\mathbb Z\times BO, namely \widetilde{KO}(X)=[X,E]_*, and the suspension–loop adjunction [\Sigma^8X,E]_*\cong[X,\Omega^8E]_*. Here brackets denote based homotopy classes, and based loops in E stay in its zero integer component.
