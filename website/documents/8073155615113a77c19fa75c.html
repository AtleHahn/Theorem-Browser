Statement

For positive integers a<b<c, a triangle with those sides is Heronian if and only if E=(a^2+b^2+c^2)^2-2(a^4+b^4+c^4) is a nonzero perfect square divisible by 16.

Proof 1: Source calculation — outline

Heron’s formula gives 16K^2=E=2(a^2b^2+a^2c^2+b^2c^2)-(a^4+b^4+c^4). Thus integer area is equivalent to the stated square condition. Positivity of this Heron expression gives the nondegenerate triangle condition for positive ordered sides. The source leaves this routine algebraic form of Heron’s formula implicit.

Level justification

Expand Heron to 16K^2=E and use the positive factorization to identify the triangle inequality. High-school polynomial arithmetic, integer squares and divisibility suffice.
