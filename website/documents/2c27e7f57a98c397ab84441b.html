Statement

The real quaternion algebra \mathbb H has no unital associative \mathbb C-algebra structure extending its given real scalar multiplication.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Quaternions are a concrete four-dimensional real algebra; a compatible complex scalar action and the algebra center add related definitions to undergraduate rings and linear algebra.

Mathematical Field

Proof 1: Use the real quaternion center

A complex-algebra structure would embed \mathbb C in the center of \mathbb H. But the source uses that the center of \mathbb H is \mathbb R, which cannot contain \mathbb C as an extension of its real scalars. Thus no such structure exists.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

A compatible complex scalar action would place a central square root of minus one in the real quaternion center, impossible over the reals. Undergraduate rings and real linear algebra suffice; quaternions and central scalar multiplication are a small explicit algebra extension.
