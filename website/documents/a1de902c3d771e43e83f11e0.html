Statement

Let R be a commutative unital ring, A\in M_n(R) with n\ge1, p(t)=\det(tI_n-A) and B(t)=\operatorname{adj}(tI_n-A). With formal derivative p', t p'(t)-np(t)=\operatorname{tr}(AB(t)).

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Undergraduate linear algebra supplies inner products, determinants, characteristic polynomials and traces. Introductory ring theory supplies the general scalar setting where needed, and the displayed formulas define all remaining notation. No specialist extension is required for understanding.

Proof 1: A traced adjugate identity for the characteristic polynomial

Use Jacobi's formula in its formal characteristic-polynomial form,

p'(t)=\operatorname{tr}B(t).

Specifically, (tI_n-A)B(t)=I_np(t), and this identity together with the derivative of p, when one takes the trace, yields

t p'(t)-np(t)=\operatorname{tr}(AB(t)).
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Take the trace of (tI-A)B=pI and substitute the supplied formal derivative identity p prime=tr B. This is matrix algebra and polynomial differentiation over a commutative ring; no analytic differentiation is needed.
