Statement

Let n\ge2 be an integer and let \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n be a bounded open Lipschitz domain, 1\le p<n, and p^*=np/(n-p). For 1\le q<p^*, every sequence bounded in W^{1,p}(\Omega;\mathbb R) has a subsequence converging in L^q(\Omega;\mathbb R).

Sobolev weak-derivative spaces and Lebesgue-space convergence require measure theory and functional analysis.

Proof 1: Interpret the compact inclusion sequentially

Since an embedding is compact if and only if the inclusion (identity) operator is a compact operator, the Rellich–Kondrachov theorem implies that any uniformly bounded sequence in W1,p(Ω; R) has a subsequence that converges in Lq(Ω; R).
