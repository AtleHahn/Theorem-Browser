Statement

Let K be a finite simplicial complex and f:K\to K a simplicial map whose geometric realization has no fixed point. Then \sum_k(-1)^k\operatorname{tr}(f_*:H_k(K;\mathbb Q)\to H_k(K;\mathbb Q))=0.

Proof 1: Zero diagonal and the chain–homology trace identity (outline)

Since f is fixed-point-free, it sends each simplex to a different simplex: a simplex mapped onto itself would have its vertices permuted and its barycenter fixed. This means that the diagonal values of the matrices of the linear maps induced on the simplicial chain complex of K must all be zero. Here the bases are oriented simplices; a simplex collapsed to lower dimension contributes zero.

Then one notes that, in general, the Lefschetz number can also be computed using the alternating sum of the matrix traces of the aforementioned linear maps, by the chain–homology trace identity. In the particular case of a fixed-point-free simplicial map, all of the diagonal values are zero, and thus the traces are all zero.

Level justification

Show chain-map diagonal entries vanish and transfer the alternating trace to homology. Simplicial chains, induced homology maps and Lefschetz numbers require algebraic topology despite the final matrix calculation.
