Statement

For a commutative Noetherian ring R and n\ge0, R[X_0,\ldots,X_{n-1}] is Noetherian.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Introductory ring theory supplies polynomial rings and quotient ideals. Noetherian means every ideal is finitely generated; a finitely generated algebra is a polynomial-ring quotient, and the finite presentation is explicitly stated. These closely related definitions are within the calibrated Hilbert-basis allowance.

Proof 1: Induction by adjoining one variable at a time — outline

Hilbert's basis theorem says that adjoining one variable to a Noetherian ring gives a Noetherian ring. By induction we see that R[X_0,\dots,X_{n-1}] will also be Noetherian.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Induct on the number of variables using the supplied one-variable theorem. Noetherian adds finite generation of ideals to undergraduate commutative rings, and the iteration requires no proof of the basis theorem.
