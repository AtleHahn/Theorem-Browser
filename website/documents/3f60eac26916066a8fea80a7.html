Proof 1: Identification of first homology with abelianization

The standard identification H_1(G,\mathbb Z)\cong G/[G,G] identifies first homology with abelianization. Its vanishing is exactly [G,G]=G, the definition of perfectness.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the standard identification with abelianization, but understanding the full statement still requires integral group homology. That construction is not defined locally and exceeds a small extension of undergraduate group theory.
