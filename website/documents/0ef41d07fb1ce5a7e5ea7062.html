Statement

On a compact interval I, suppose the continuous Picard iterates y_{k,j} satisfy |y_{k,j}|\leq b and |y_{k,j}(t)-y_{k,j}(s)|\leq2C|t-s| for fixed b,C. Then the family is relatively compact in C(I) with uniform norm; limits y_k drawn from its closure also have a uniformly convergent subsequence.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Basic topology and real analysis supply covers, continuity, compactness and uniform convergence. Local finiteness and the uniform norm are explicitly described; relative compactness means compact closure in that familiar metric function space.

Proof 1: Apply Arzelà–Ascoli to the common bounds

The common Lipschitz bound gives equicontinuity, and the common absolute bound gives boundedness. Arzelà–Ascoli therefore makes the family relatively compact. Its closure is compact in the uniform norm, so any sequence of limits y_k lying in that closure has a uniformly convergent subsequence.
