Statement

Let MU_*(-) denote complex cobordism homology and let

MU_*=MU_*(\mathrm{point})\cong\mathbb Z[x_1,x_2,\ldots],\qquad |x_i|=2i,

be its graded coefficient ring. For each prime p there is a sequence of elements v_1^{(p)},v_2^{(p)},\ldots of MU_* with the following property.

If M_* is a graded MU_*-module such that, for every prime p, multiplication by p on M_* is injective and, for every n\geq1, multiplication by v_n^{(p)} is injective on

M_* /(p,v_1^{(p)},\ldots,v_{n-1}^{(p)})M_*,

then

E_*(X)=MU_*(X)\otimes_{MU_*}M_*

defines a generalized homology theory on CW complexes, with the corresponding formula for CW pairs. The injectivity conditions express regularity of every finite initial sequence (p,v_1^{(p)},\ldots,v_n^{(p)}) on M_*; zero quotient modules are allowed.

Here a graded module is a direct sum of groups indexed by degree with the ring action respecting degrees; the tensor product is taken over the displayed coefficient ring. A CW complex is a space built by attaching cells, and a generalized homology theory assigns graded groups to such spaces and pairs with natural boundary maps satisfying homotopy invariance, the long exact sequence of a pair, excision, and the disjoint-union axiom, without imposing the ordinary dimension axiom.
