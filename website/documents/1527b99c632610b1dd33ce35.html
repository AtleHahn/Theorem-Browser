Statement

Let X be a real topological vector space and X^* its continuous real dual. For f:X\to\overline{\mathbb R}=\mathbb R\cup\{-\infty,+\infty\} define f^*(p)=\sup_{x\in X}(p(x)-f(x)), p\in X^*. Let (f_\alpha)_{\alpha\in A} be a family of such functions, with pointwise infima and suprema. Supremum and infimum are taken in the extended real order, including \sup\varnothing=-\infty and \inf\varnothing=+\infty. Then (\inf_{\alpha\in A}f_\alpha)^*(p)=\sup_{\alpha\in A}f_\alpha^*(p) for every p\in X^*.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Continuous vector operations and the continuous dual are a short extension of undergraduate topology and linear algebra. Pointwise infima of arbitrary function families and extended-real suprema use undergraduate order and function notation, with empty-family conventions and the conjugate formula supplied.

Proof 1: Interchange the two suprema — outline

For a family of functions (f_\alpha)_\alpha it follows from the fact that supremums may be interchanged that

\left(\inf_\alpha f_\alpha\right)^*(p)=\sup_\alpha f_\alpha^*(p).
Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Subtract the pointwise infimum from finite p(x), then interchange the two suprema; the supplied empty-family conventions cover an empty index set. Understanding the complete statement also includes the topological-vector-space compatibility definition and its continuous dual; only evaluation of those functionals is needed, with no topology on the dual.
