Statement

For a nonconstant real polynomial P, its nonzero negative-remainder Sturm chain starts with P_0=P, P_1=P', and continues by P_{i+1}=-\operatorname{rem}(P_{i-1},P_i) until the first zero remainder. Even when P has integer coefficients, this chain need not have only integer coefficients.

Proof 1: A quartic whose first negative remainder has rational coefficients

Take the polynomial p(x)=x^4+x^3-x-1. So

\begin{aligned} p_0(x)&=p(x)=x^4+x^3-x-1,\\ p_1(x)&=p'(x)=4x^3+3x^2-1. \end{aligned}

The remainder of the Euclidean division of p_0 by p_1 is

-\frac3{16}x^2-\frac34x-\frac{15}{16};

multiplying it by -1 we obtain

p_2(x)=\frac3{16}x^2+\frac34x+\frac{15}{16}.

Thus the Sturm sequence of this integer polynomial contains a polynomial whose coefficients are not integers.
