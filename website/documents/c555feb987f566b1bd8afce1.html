Statement

Let T\ge1 be an integer and let H be a real Hilbert space of functions f:X\to\mathbb R^T with bounded evaluation E_x:H\to\mathbb R^T at each x. For every x and c\in\mathbb R^T there is a unique h_{x,c}\in H satisfying \langle f,h_{x,c}\rangle=f(x)^\mathsf{T}c for every f.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Bounded evaluations are operators from a Hilbert function space into a finite-dimensional target.

Functional analysis

Proof 1: Apply Riesz to each scalarized evaluation

For fixed x and c, the scalar evaluation functional f\mapsto f(x)^\mathsf Tc is bounded. Apply the Hilbert-space Riesz representation theorem to obtain its unique representer.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Compose a bounded evaluation map with a finite scalar product and apply Hilbert-space Riesz representation. The continuous-dual representation framework is not confidently a small addition to ordinary linear algebra.
