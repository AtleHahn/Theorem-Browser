Statement

Let n\geq1 be an integer and let a_1,\ldots,a_n be nonnegative real numbers. For each k=1,\ldots,n, define

S_k=\frac{1}{\binom nk}\sum_{1\leq i_1<\cdots<i_k\leq n}a_{i_1}\cdots a_{i_k}.

Thus S_k is the average of all products of k distinct indexed numbers from the list, and \binom nk is the number of such products. Then

S_1\geq S_2^{1/2}\geq S_3^{1/3}\geq\cdots\geq S_n^{1/n}.

Equality throughout holds if and only if a_1=\cdots=a_n.

Level
High school only
Level justification

All means are explicitly defined by finite arithmetic; no symmetric-function theory is needed.
