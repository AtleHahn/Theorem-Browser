Proof 1: Apply the two-term inequality twice

Use the following external form of Titu's lemma: for an integer n\ge1, real x_1,\ldots,x_n and positive y_1,\ldots,y_n,

\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{x_i^2}{y_i}\geq\frac{(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n y_i}.

We have {\displaystyle {\frac {a^{2}}{1}}+{\frac {b^{2}}{1}}\geq {\frac {(a+b)^{2}}{2}}} so that {\displaystyle a^{4}+b^{4}={\frac {\left(a^{2}\right)^{2}}{1}}+{\frac {\left(b^{2}\right)^{2}}{1}}\geq {\frac {\left(a^{2}+b^{2}\right)^{2}}{2}}\geq {\frac {\left({\frac {(a+b)^{2}}{2}}\right)^{2}}{2}}={\frac {(a+b)^{4}}{8}}.}
