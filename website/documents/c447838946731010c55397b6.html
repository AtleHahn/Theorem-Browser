Statement

The space of smooth Riemannian metrics of constant sectional curvature on the standard smooth 3-sphere, endowed with the C^\infty topology, is contractible. A Riemannian metric is a smoothly varying positive definite inner product on tangent spaces. Constant sectional curvature means the curvature of every tangent 2-plane at every point is one constant for the given metric; this constant may vary between metrics. The C^\infty topology requires convergence of metric coefficients and all their derivatives in local charts. Contractibility means the identity map of this space is homotopic to a constant map.
