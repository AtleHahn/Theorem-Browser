Statement

Let X be a smooth complex projective variety of dimension n, let D be a reduced simple normal crossing divisor, and let p be an integer with 0\le p\le n. Simple normal crossings means that locally in holomorphic coordinates z_1,\ldots,z_n the divisor is z_1\cdots z_s=0 for some s. The sheaf \Omega_X^p(\log D) is the pth exterior power of the locally free sheaf generated in such coordinates by dz_1/z_1,\ldots,dz_s/z_s,dz_{s+1},\ldots,dz_n. For every invertible subsheaf A\subseteq\Omega_X^p(\log D) one has \kappa(A)\le p.

Here an invertible sheaf is a locally free sheaf of rank one, and \kappa(A) is its Iitaka dimension: the maximum dimension of the images of rational maps defined by the complete linear systems of positive tensor powers A^{\otimes m}, or -\infty if all these spaces of sections vanish. Equivalently, for every invertible sheaf A with \kappa(A)>p, H^0(X,A^{-1}\otimes\Omega_X^p(\log D))=0, where H^0 denotes global sections and A^{-1} the dual line bundle.
