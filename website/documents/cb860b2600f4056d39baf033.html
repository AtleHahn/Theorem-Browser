Statement

Let k be algebraically closed and let K be the rational-function field of an integral algebraic curve over k. Every finite-dimensional central simple K-algebra is isomorphic, as a K-algebra, to a matrix algebra M_r(K) for some r\ge1; equivalently, \operatorname{Br}(K)=0. Here an integral curve is an irreducible reduced variety of dimension one, and its rational-function field is the field of fractions of the coordinate ring of any nonempty affine open subset. A central simple algebra is a nonzero associative unital algebra with center exactly K and no nonzero proper two-sided ideals. The Brauer group consists of such algebras modulo matrix stabilization, with tensor product as group operation.
