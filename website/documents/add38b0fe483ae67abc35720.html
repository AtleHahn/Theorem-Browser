Statement

Let \operatorname{Map}_0(S^1,S^1) be the space of continuous basepoint-preserving maps of degree zero, with the uniform (equivalently compact-open) topology. Then H_1(\operatorname{Map}_0(S^1,S^1);\mathbb Z)=0.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves based mapping spaces; circle; singular homology; Degree of a circle map; Uniform and compact-open topologies. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Contractibility of the mapping-space component

It follows from the theory of covering spaces that the mapping space {\displaystyle \operatorname {Map} _{0}(S^{1},S^{1})} of the circle {\displaystyle S^{1}} is contractible, so {\displaystyle H_{1}(\operatorname {Map} _{0}(S^{1},S^{1}))=0}.
