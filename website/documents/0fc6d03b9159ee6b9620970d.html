Statement

On the Borel sets of X=[0,1], let \nu be length and \mu=\nu+\delta_0, where \delta_0(A)=1 if 0\in A and zero otherwise. Then \nu\ll\mu and d\nu/d\mu=1_{X\setminus\{0\}}.
