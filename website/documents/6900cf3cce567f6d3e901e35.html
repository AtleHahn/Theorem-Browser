Proof 1: A monochromatic progression among eight-digit ternary-digit numbers — outline

View the digits as words in [3]^8, the length-eight words over \{1,2,3\}. A variable-word line replaces a fixed nonempty set of positions by one common variable and keeps every other position fixed. The source’s special Hales–Jewett result gives a monochromatic variable-word line w(1),w(2),w(3). In decimal notation these three numbers form an arithmetic progression: replacing the variable digit by its successor changes each number by the same positive sum of the corresponding powers of 10.
