Statement

Let k\geq1 and n\geq1 be integers and let \Omega\subset\mathbb R^n be a bounded open domain. Let H^k(\Omega) be the real Sobolev space of functions with all weak derivatives of orders at most k in L^2(\Omega), with

\|u\|_{H^k(\Omega)}^2=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq k}\|D^\alpha u\|_{L^2(\Omega)}^2.

Let H_0^k(\Omega) be the closure of the smooth compactly supported functions in this norm. Suppose there is a bounded linear extension E:H^k(\Omega)\to H^k(\mathbb R^n) such that (Eu)|_\Omega=u.

For multiindices \alpha,\beta\in\mathbb N_0^n with |\alpha|,|\beta|\leq k, let A_{\alpha\beta} be real essentially bounded coefficients on \Omega. Suppose those with |\alpha|=|\beta|=k extend continuously to \overline\Omega, and suppose there is \theta>0 such that

\sum_{|\alpha|=|\beta|=k}\xi^\alpha A_{\alpha\beta}(x)\xi^\beta>\theta|\xi|^{2k}

for every x\in\Omega and 0\ne\xi\in\mathbb R^n. Here |\alpha|=\alpha_1+\cdots+\alpha_n, D^\alpha denotes the corresponding weak derivative, and \xi^\alpha=\prod_j\xi_j^{\alpha_j}.

Define

B[v,u]=\sum_{|\alpha|,|\beta|\leq k}\int_\Omega A_{\alpha\beta}(x)D^\alpha u(x)D^\beta v(x)\,dx.

Then there exist constants C>0 and G\geq0, independent of u, such that

B[u,u]+G\|u\|_{L^2(\Omega)}^2\geq C\|u\|_{H^k(\Omega)}^2\qquad(u\in H_0^k(\Omega)).
