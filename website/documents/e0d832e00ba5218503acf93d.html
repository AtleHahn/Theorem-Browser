Statement

Let M^d be the smooth double of a manifold with boundary \bar M, with natural mirror involution i interchanging its two copies and fixing \partial M. Suppose a smooth Lorentzian metric g^d on M^d is invariant under i, and a smooth Cauchy temporal function \tau^d for g^d satisfies \tau^d\circ i=\tau^d. Then \nabla_{g^d}\tau^d is tangent to \partial M along that hypersurface.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the full hypotheses involves Smooth doubles of manifolds with boundary; Lorentzian metric gradients; Isometries and temporal functions. These require substantial specialist preparation beyond a brief introduction from undergraduate mathematics. Under the statement-understanding criterion, a bright US college student would need more than approximately 30 minutes of focused background research to understand the full claim.

Proof 1: Use invariance under the double mirror — outline

The source supplies the mirror-invariant metric and mirror-invariant temporal function as hypotheses of this local step and concludes that the gradient is tangent to the common boundary. The routine symmetry check uses covariance of the metric gradient under an isometry: the invariant function has an invariant gradient. Along the fixed hypersurface the mirror fixes tangent directions and reverses the transverse direction, so its invariant vectors are tangent.
