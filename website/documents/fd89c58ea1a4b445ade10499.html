Statement

For every complex elliptic curve E, there exists an exact sequence of complex Lie groups

0\longrightarrow (\mathbb C,+)\xrightarrow{\iota}(\mathbb C^\times)^2\xrightarrow{\pi}E\longrightarrow0

which does not split holomorphically. Here \mathbb C^\times=\mathbb C\setminus\{0\} with multiplication, exactness means that \iota is a holomorphic group isomorphism onto the closed kernel of the surjective holomorphic group homomorphism \pi, and nonsplitting means that no holomorphic group homomorphism s:E\to(\mathbb C^\times)^2 satisfies \pi\circ s=\operatorname{id}_E. The sequence is analytic, not algebraic: the two-dimensional multiplicative algebraic group cannot be such an extension by algebraic group morphisms. A complex elliptic curve is a smooth connected projective genus-one curve with a chosen identity and its group law.
