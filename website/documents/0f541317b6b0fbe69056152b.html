Statement

Let p be a prime, \mathbb Q_p the completion of \mathbb Q for the absolute value |p^ku/v|_p=p^{-k} when u,v are integers not divisible by p, and \mathbb Z_p=\{x\in\mathbb Q_p:|x|_p\le1\}. Set |0|_p=0. A function f:\mathbb Z_p\to\mathbb Q_p is continuous if and only if for every x\in\mathbb Z_p its Newton series converges to f(x):

f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n\binom xn,\qquad a_n=\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^{n-k}\binom nk f(k),

where \binom x0=1 and \binom xn=x(x-1)\cdots(x-n+1)/n! for n\ge1. In that case a_n\to0 in \mathbb Q_p. Conversely, any sequence (a_n) in \mathbb Q_p tending to zero gives a continuous function by this series, and its coefficients equal the displayed finite differences. Convergence and continuity use the p-adic metric.
