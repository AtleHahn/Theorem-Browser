Statement

Let M be a closed smooth n-manifold and f:M→R a Morse function with C^k critical points of index k. For each 0≤k≤n, the Betti number b_k(M)=dim_Q H_k(M;Q) satisfies b_k(M)≤C^k.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement relates Morse critical-point indices on a closed smooth manifold to its homological invariants.

Proof 1: Count cells using the Morse cell theorem

By the Morse cell theorem, M has the homotopy type of a finite CW complex with one \gamma-cell for each critical point of index \gamma of f.

By cellular homology, the rank of the nth homology group of a CW complex is less than or equal to the number of n-cells. Therefore, the rank of the \gammath homology group of M, that is, the Betti number b_\gamma(M), is less than or equal to the number of critical points of index \gamma of a Morse function on M. In particular, for any \gamma\in\{0,\ldots,n=\dim M\},

C^\gamma\geq b_\gamma(M).
