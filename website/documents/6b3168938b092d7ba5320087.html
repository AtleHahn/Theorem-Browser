Statement

Let A,B,C,D be the consecutive vertices of a convex quadrilateral, let E and F be the midpoints of its diagonals AC and BD, and let L be an interior point. Write [PQR] for the ordinary nonnegative area of triangle PQR. If the quadrilateral is not a parallelogram, then E\ne F and

[BCL]+[DAL]=[LAB]+[DLC]\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad L\text{ lies on the line }EF.

If the quadrilateral is a parallelogram, then E=F and the area identity holds for every interior point L.

Level
High school only
Level justification

Every object and relation belongs to elementary Euclidean geometry; both the locus and degenerate case are explicit.
