Statement

If A is an irreducible row-stochastic matrix, its Perron spectral projection is row-stochastic and all its rows are equal.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Understanding the statement involves finite-dimensional matrices; eigenvalues and spectral radius; spectral projections. These prerequisites fit undergraduate coursework or a brief extension of it. A bright US college student should be able to understand this statement after approximately 30 minutes of focused background research; this assesses understanding the claim, not its proof.

Proof 1: Source spectral argument outline

The right Perron vector can be taken as \mathbf1. The standard Perron projection formula is P=vw^T with w^Tv=1; consequently P=\mathbf1 w^T, w>0, and \sum_i w_i=1. Thus every row equals w^T and has sum one. This is a projection assertion, not a claim that powers of a periodic matrix converge.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

Insert the all-ones right eigenvector into the explicit normalized outer-product projection formula. Undergraduate matrix eigenvectors and rank-one projections suffice; no convergence of powers is assumed.
