Statement

If p is a nondegenerate critical point of a smooth f:M→R on a finite-dimensional smooth manifold, then p has a neighborhood containing no other critical point of f.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

The statement uses nondegenerate Hessians and neighborhoods on smooth manifolds.

Proof 1: Differentiate the Morse normal form

By the Morse lemma, there exists a chart (x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n) in a neighborhood U of p such that x_i(p)=0 for all i and

f(x)=f(p)-x_1^2-\cdots-x_\gamma^2+x_{\gamma+1}^2+\cdots+x_n^2

throughout U. Here \gamma is equal to the index of f at p. As a corollary of the Morse lemma, one sees that nondegenerate critical points are isolated.
