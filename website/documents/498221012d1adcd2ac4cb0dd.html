Statement

Let G be a finite group, p a prime, and P a Sylow p-subgroup (a subgroup of order the largest power of p dividing |G|). Write [H,H] for the subgroup generated by x^{-1}y^{-1}xy with x,y\in H, x^g=g^{-1}xg, and N_G(Q)=\{g\in G:g^{-1}Qg=Q\}. Choose representatives t_1,\ldots,t_m of the left cosets G/P. For g\in G write gt_i=t_{\sigma_g(i)}h_i with h_i\in P and define the transfer v(g)=\prod_i h_i\,[P,P]\in P/[P,P]. Then \ker v=\bigcap\{K\trianglelefteq G:G/K\text{ is an abelian }p\text{-group}\}.

Level
Within undergraduate level
Level justification

The transfer is explicitly defined using coset representatives and an abelian quotient, a small extension of undergraduate group theory.
