Statement

For every integer n\ge1, an orientation-preserving homeomorphism of \mathbb R^n is stable: it is a finite composite of homeomorphisms, each of which is the identity on some nonempty open set.

Proof 1: Consequence of the annulus theorem

The annulus theorem states that if a homeomorphism h of \mathbb R^n sends the closed unit ball B into its interior, then B\setminus h(\operatorname{int}B) is homeomorphic to S^{n-1}\times[0,1].

Brown & Gluck (1964) previously showed that the stable homeomorphism conjecture is equivalent to the annulus conjecture, so it is true.

Level
Beyond undergraduate level
Level justification

Use the annulus theorem and its equivalence with the stable-homeomorphism assertion. Arbitrary-dimensional topological orientation, annuli and homeomorphism decomposition require substantial manifold topology.
